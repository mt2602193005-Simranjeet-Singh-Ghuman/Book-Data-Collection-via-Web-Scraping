Back in the 1990s I read and enjoyed many of Joanna Trollope’s ‘Aga Saga’ novels and, thirty years on, they still display a certain timelessness in their exploration of English life and manners. But they’re also a little twee, as this reread unfortunately confirmed - or perhaps I’ve just heard enough about the domestic woes of the gentrified middle classes by now. This is a comforting and comfortable read though, with Trollope’s reliable narration and no-nonsense style a positive force throughout.
