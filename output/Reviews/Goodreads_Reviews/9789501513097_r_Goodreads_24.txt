As I read this compulsively for the 437th time... (I may be exaggerating there), I'm reminded why it's on my keeper shelf. There's humor - offbeat, there's self-awareness - the h is the impulsive sort and well aware that it's a failing but she can't seem to help herself, and the conflicts are non-threatening - at least, beyond the loose cannon who got what he deserved. Really, the only stupid moment was the h charging into town at night, alone and unarmed, thus presenting the loose cannon's brother with the perfect opportunity to grab her. As for the panther... I don't think it works like that, but for the story...yeah; the idea of a leopard who behaves more like a big dog...we'll let it slide.
