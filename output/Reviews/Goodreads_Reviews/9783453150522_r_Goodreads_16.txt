It's been years since I read this book, but I distinctly remember being immediately drawn into this real-life account of the tragic events of the 1996 climbing expeditions on Mt. Everest, told by Anatoli Boukreev, a guide responsible for leading some of those climbers to safety. It's a great companion book to Into Thin Air: A Personal Account of the Mount Everest Disaster , by Jon Krakauer, to get a different perspective on the same events. ★★★★ ½ Stars
