THE CHRISTMAS WITCH by Joanne Oppenheim interested me because it was a different version of the La Befana Italian Christmas Witch who visits children's homes on the eve of the Epiphany, not unlike Santa Clause. It's one of 3 such Befana stories for children that I read just after Thanksgiving. This one is actually a "Level 3" Bank Street Ready to read book, which gives parents an idea of which kids might be able to read it on their own. However, I have to say, as cute as the storyline was, I almost gave it 3 stars instead of 4. Then decided that I was possibly rating it that way because I'd just re-read Tomie de Paola's THE LEGEND OF OLD BEFANA - and how could anyone even begin to come near the masters of the illustrations by him, as well as storytelling! Still, if you're looking for books for kids that reflect on holidays in other cultures, this wouldn't be a bad choice. Nice illustrations as well. I happen to love most Befana stories I've come across. And I'm hoping to read several holiday-themed books during this season!
