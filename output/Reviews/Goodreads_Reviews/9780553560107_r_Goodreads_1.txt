Despite having a human family with no indication that the point of view character is adopted or at some point murdered and replaced a human child, You Are An (amnesiac) Alien sent to Earth to spy on humanity. Now, you might think that you're a cool and highly original alien, but no, You Are A Crab , because we can't have nice things with Edward Packard around. Other aliens in the book disappointingly include monkeys, cockroaches, and slightly different crabs, with just one mildly exotic sea serpent. If you stay as a human you can use your crab powers to stop time, yet if you go back to the crab homeworld you inexplicably lack that ability (and it would've been nice to have, since there are multiple ways to die there). Nothing interesting happens in the whole book and while Edward Packard clearly likes the crabs, they come off like jerks.
