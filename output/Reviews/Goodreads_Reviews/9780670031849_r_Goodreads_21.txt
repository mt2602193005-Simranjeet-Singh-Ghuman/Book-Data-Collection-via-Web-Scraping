I've learned babies, late nights and cycling into work make reading much harder than it used to be. This was probably quite a good book - and it's one I've been waiting to read for aaaages , but a combination of the above kept interrupting my flow. Possibly a shame because it is a sad story. The background research is obviously impeccable and for once William Dalrymple is a background figure, yet yet yet... A good third of the book is taken up with scene-setting which made going even slower, and over-detailing every incident only confused matters. However this amazing source stands out: A LADIES MONITOR Being a series of letters first published in Bengal on the subject of FEMALE APPAREL. Tending to favour a regulated adoption of Indian Costume; and a rejection of SUPERFLUOUS VESTURE by the ladies of this country: with Incidental remarks on Hindoo beauty; whale bone stays; iron busks; Indian corsets; man-milliners; idle bachelors, hair powder, side saddles, waiting-maids; and footmen. By the author of A VINDICATION OF THE HINDOOS I'm still looking on Amazon.
