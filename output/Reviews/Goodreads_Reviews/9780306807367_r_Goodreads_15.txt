With the amount of time that I spend reading Seuss books to my children, I was interested in finding out more about the literary legend himself. This book does a good job of following his life, although it is dry and slow at times, particularly the beginning. The authors, being friends with Geisel, tend to be a little too uncritical of him, especially during times of his life that deserve more scrutiny, particularly after the death of his first wife. Overall, it's a good read on the man who remade children's literature.
