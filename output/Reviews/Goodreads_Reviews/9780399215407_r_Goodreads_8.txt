Reading level: All elementary grades This book of poems is a huge collection of very traditional and happy poems from a variety of poets. Some poems are written by very famous poets such as Robert Frost and Langston Hughes. dePaola also uses some more modern poets that may not be as familiar. The illustrations in the story are very light and simple. However, they do add a good picture to go with the poem. For the most part, the poems are short and written about happy things. The variety of the kinds of poems would be a good collection to have in your classroom because there are so many different poets and different kinds of writing styles. There are also some multicultural poems in the collection that adds to the author’s variety. I would use this book as a read aloud on various occasions and also as a book to have as an individual read and something to look up various poem types. Content Connections: Language arts: looking up and discussing various styles of poetry. Science: studying the poems on page 46-47 about the moon as a content connection to the moon/space study.
