I bought this book up at a library in the Adirondacks. It was written in 1977. I was born in ‘76 and was amused that the state of the country and world in 1977 was very similar to the world of 2023 (China and Russia threats, terrorism, murder in cities, etc). I remember watching Billy Graham specials when they were live on television. It was a big deal when he would do addresses. I always admired that he was non-denominational. His message was always crystal clear and he peppered it with fascinating anecdotes from people he met during his crusade. I was not raised “Born Again”. I was raised Catholic. As a teenager and young adult, I read the New Testament. I have read it a few times. It is an easy read and the term “born again” needs to considered in how it is used in the Bible. The Bible also uses the term “saved” which I appreciate because we all need saving. This book is a step by step understanding of being a Christian written to be understood by all.
