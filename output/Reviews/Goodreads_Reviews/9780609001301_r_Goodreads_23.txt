Slow-burn, edge-of-your-seat, suspense science thriller about biological weapons and warfare!! It’s *supposedly* only fiction (as opposed to being published as nonfiction) because the author’s sources refused to be listed as sources in the back of the book. So the front disclaimer will say: “Any names and places in this book are made up and purely coincidental and fictional” etc. (By the way I looked up many names and places and things that happened-and from reading his previous nonfiction science books with actual sources-most of this book is completely real) This novel felt like watching a really REALLY intense episode of NCIS/ Criminal Minds about biological warfare, like when you need to use the bathroom you can’t because you have to keep watching. Or if you’re really hungry but you can’t eat because you have to keep watching… and getting up to do ANYTHING would interrupt finding out what happens faster. It was terrifying and really makes you think “holy ****”. A little slower than I thought or wanted, but wow so SUSPENSEFUL!! If you like a good horror science novel mixed with truth, FBI, police, politics, thrill, and actual science, it is definitely worth a read!
