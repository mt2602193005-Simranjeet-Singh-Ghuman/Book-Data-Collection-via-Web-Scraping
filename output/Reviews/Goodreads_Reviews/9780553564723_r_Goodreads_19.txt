This was a 5 stars story till our hero Luke cheated while his wife was depressed over losing two children! I hated how his cheating was handled and how everyone including Lettie blamed herself for neglecting his ‘manly needs’ for two years! And I hate how they compared a man sleeping with a whore to a woman befriending a man who gave her attention and entertained her children. Made me lose respect for everyone and quit the story. Lettie and Nathan deserved so much better from this author. I’m genuinely so upset because cheating is a hard limit for me and I wouldn’t have read this book had I known it contained it.
