Possessing the Gates of the Enemy-Cindy Jacobs This is basically a manual for intercession. It was originally published during the 1980's and is being republished as the author believes the spiritual principles for intercession and spiritual warfare, we learned during the 80's and 90's, are just as valid for the resent generation; who she believes have not had instruction passed down and desperately need to learn to interceed. The author, Cindy Jacobs, has had many year experience of intercession in every sphere of infouence, from local church level to international warfare. The book tells how she learned her skills from the Holy Spirit and biblical study. At each stage she points out useful warnings and advice. The author spends time discussing how to know if you are an intercessor, what the role of intercessor is and the different forms of intercessor which exist. She provides a whole chapter on spiritual intercession. She explains how to set up prayer groups within the local church and what the role of prayer leader involves. She deals with problems which can arise within prayer grous, including discussing the Absalom spirit. She talks about prayer walks, personal prayer support and praying against strongholds is given a lot of space. This is a book everyone who prays should read. We are not all called as intercessors, but we are all called to interceed. This book will provide wisdom and instruction to every christian. All the teaching is carefully supported by scripture. Important points are set out with bullet points for clarity. As a result the book is easy to read and clear in its teaching. All points are illustrated by examples from the author's personal life or from the lives' of other godly christians. The book is not a heavy instruction manual. The style is light, despite containing such a depth of wisdom. I highly recommend this book.
