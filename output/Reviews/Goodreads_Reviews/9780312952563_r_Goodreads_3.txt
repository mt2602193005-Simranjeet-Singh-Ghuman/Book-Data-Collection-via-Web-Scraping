After abandoning her husband and four children two years earlier, this mother has changed her mind. She wants her family back, especially her boys. Her ex-husband has finally managed to move on from the mess she left behind. He has full custody of the kids, although some weekend visits with their mother are permitted. Her preference for the two older sons, Kester and Michael, is marked. Mother's expectations of a happy family together again are completely unrealistic, but she intends to make it happen. Kester has a cruel streak in his makeup, and Michael is only too happy to follow his cue. Warning - there is animal cruelty involved here (that business with the frog in the woods is beyond the pale. It made me want to kill that kid.) as well as the mother being too loving to her boys in all the wrong ways. Some of the dialogue seemed awkward to me, and there were several things that strained credulity, but no one can take that ending away. It was a shocker with an "after tweak".
