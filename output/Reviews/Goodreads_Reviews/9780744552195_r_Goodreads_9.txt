Sophie's Adventures were the first series I read solo as a child. More than any other fictional character, she taught me to hold my own and pursue what I wanted. This series starting with a 5 year old who wants to be a "lady farmer". Having always been small myself, I have lived my life aspiring to her determination. First book I buy any friends who have children, and I have to confess when I'm stressed and work is getting to me, the ease and joy of flicking through the 6 Sophie books relaxes and entertains me. Sophie is not flawless, these books though written for small children, have very real characters and relationships portrayed. Definitely a top book to have for your kids, and I dare your adult self not to bawl near the end of them.
