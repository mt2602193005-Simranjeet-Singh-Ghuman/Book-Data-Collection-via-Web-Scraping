Nothing like starting off with a real-life “Bang.” Kristin Gabriel’s first book, Bullets Over Boise, was selected as a Harlequin movie that premiered on The Movie Channel in December 1998 renamed for the occasion to ‘Recipe for Revenge.’” Carly Westin is a struggling caterer who honors her friend’s last-minute request to provide a meal for the friend’s dinner date with Carly’s food as something the friend made. While Carly prepares the feast in the friend’s kitchen, a knock at the door is followed by shots, and the couple is dead before they can even be seated for the meal. That’s when police investigator, Special Agent Jack Brannigan, becomes involved in protecting Carly before the killer strikes again. This is a quick and light-hearted read.
