Least interesting of the series that I’ve read in a while. very little action. Mason is never on the hot seat. Lieutenant Tragg and Hamilton Burger are not involved. Boring descriptions of what the shady import export company does. Mason’s explanation of the crime in the end is the most unbelievable I’ve seen. The “36-24-36” code is silly and also hard to believe that it would have been used as it was. It’s humorous that Gardner can use “bitch” and “pimp” as insults against characters but can’t use the word “pregnant” to describe a woman who is “…in trouble…”.
