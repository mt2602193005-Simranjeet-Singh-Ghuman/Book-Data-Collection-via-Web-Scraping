This book is written in a slightly annoying, detached style, but the story it tells is so insane (since it's true) that I couldn't put it down. It's interesting how there seems to be some kind of con-artist syndrome where people do the exact same things - if you've seen the movie Catfish, the similarities are all over the place. and I NEED to know if more ever happened with this story. It's driving me crazy!
