"Up the river", "to the big house" all phrases that originated at SingSing. This book chronicles the life of an extra ordinary warden of SingSing during the beginning of the 1900's. Through his experiences the reader is opened to a view of capital punishment/electrocution that few have seen except through the movies. I got this as a mistake and glad I did. Once you start reading it is hard to put it down. In one of the most hardened places it offers a glimpse of what true compassion and reform can look like while at the same time maintaining order and discipline.
