This 1991 potboiler has its moments. Sprinkled with stomach churning romantic drivel, Hodge does provide a couple of twists. Protagonist antihero Justin is a drunken loser who ends up on the wrong side of drug dealer Tony Mendoza.. think Pacino from Scarface.. This novel really homages film and movies from the 80s.. even porn with an absurd twist. The horror aspect is a lycanthropic twist on evolution. A jungle powder from the Amazon turns users into monstrous were animals from the past. ...The Relic meets Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde. The Tampa setting is amusing.. not the typical exotic Miami one would expect. There is silliness of course.. gunplay, high speed chases, even international travel tends to be absurd. The ending is almost anticlimactic especially the antagonists demise.. an almost Kolchak Night Stalker deus ex machina. Recommend.
