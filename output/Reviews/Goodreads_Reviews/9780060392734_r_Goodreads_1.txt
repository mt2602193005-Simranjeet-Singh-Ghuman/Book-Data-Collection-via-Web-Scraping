This is an excellent little book. A friend was giving it away and I'm glad I took the time to read it. Short, informative, incredibly readable little chapters talk about common myths and misconceptions about money and help the reader to focus on what their financial goals really are. I really liked her focus on people taking the time to make sure that their passions/dreams are included in their financial planning. She also has a very realistic view of retirement planning. Her tips for incorporating the information she gives into a workable financial plan in your life, echo those of Dave Ramsey (no relation) and make complete sense to me. This is a good book for my teenagers/young adults to read -- it would be great to start out one's adult life with this kind of perspective on money management.
