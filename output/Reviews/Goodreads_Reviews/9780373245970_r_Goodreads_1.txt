Straight-laced or, as some might put it ,stodgy, Richard Carlton is convinced his Aunt Destiny has more on her mind, than just wanting Richard to hire Melanie Hart as his PR Executive as he considers going into politics. She is as different to anyone he would consider as can be. So why is Destiny so insistent about her and why is Melanie so convinced she can do the job? When the press see them together they assume Richard and Melanie are a couple. Despite serious doubts, Melanie allows herself to be convinced into pretending they are a couple and heading towards engagement. It’s a charade aimed at trying to try to teach his aunt a lesson about not meddling in his life. But the plan ends up being fraught with danger. Especially when attraction flares between Richard and Melanie. How will things work out.? Is it destined to end up in disaster? Destiny raised Richard, Mack and Ben after their parents were killed in an accident. Destiny is, as Richard her nephew describes her, a force to be reckoned with. she is a born match maker and manipulator, the type that works well in fiction but is not someone you would like to be too involved with in real life. As long as you don’t investigate this too closely and think too deeply about it, this is a light fun rom com and an engaging read. However it did tend to go on for too long in my opinion. Not sure I will read more in this series. But still it was a good foil to the other heart wrenching book I am also reading. A read that will please those who are happy to suspend disbelief for a while.
