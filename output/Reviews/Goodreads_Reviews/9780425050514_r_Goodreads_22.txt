I am a bit confused regarding my feelings for this book. Having read it through two channels (book in Romanian and audio book in English) might have also "helped". I was intrigued by the subject of an alternative history where the Nazis won the war and that made me put it on my to read shelf. Also, I heard there is a tv-series based on it which gave me an extra nudge. For the good parts, I enjoyed the world building, the philosophical ideas and the open ending. There is not much science but I did not see that as a problem. The downside for me was the large number of main characters. I thought some of them to be uninteresting, although, in the end, I understood their role in conveying the message of the book. It was of particular interest to see how fast a country can become accustomed to a Nazi regime, taking in consideration the recent refugee crisis and how much hate it generated.
