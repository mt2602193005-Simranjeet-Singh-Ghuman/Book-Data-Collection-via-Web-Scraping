This is my second Clyde Robert Bulla early chapter book and I really enjoyed this one. It's about a girl named Sarah Ida who is sent to spend the summer with her aunt in a small town. Sarah Ida is wanting money to spend and eventually gets a job at a shoeshine stand, owned by an elderly man named Al. I found this book to really be complex for such an early read. Sarah Ida is a complex character. There is a good arch in her and with the plot. While I wish it had been longer, I'd recommend it to anyone who's interested in realistic fiction for early-chapter readers. My rating - 5/5
