I read this with an older cover, one which lowered my expectations and feelings about the story. Juvenile SF that is authentic, not parody or fantasy, is far too rare, so in that sense this is a treasure. But it's actually only a so-so book, unfortunately. And it's mostly a mystery, and I don't like mysteries, so, gosh, I honestly don't know whether I can recommend it or not, or whether or not I want to read more by the author. We'll see.
