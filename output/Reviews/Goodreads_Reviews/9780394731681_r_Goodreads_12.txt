“How To Make Yourself Miserable” made me laugh out loud, and having a wish to highlight each page and say: I know those thoughts! Even from the first promise they earned my heart: “It is our humble but earnest desire that through these pages you will be able to find for yourself the inspiration and the tools for a truly painful, meaningless and miserable life.” An amazing satirical guide that uses humor to expose the "shaming" and "intimate" thoughts we as people often have, I love how the book highlights the absurdity of our internal anxieties through observations like: * On Social Paranoia: "Everyone is looking at you, everyone knows, and everyone is judging you." * On the Trap of Comparison: "Never believe that what makes others happy wouldn’t also make you happy.", or find someone that’s nicer, more famous, richer than you and compare with them It’s a life changer to see thoughts that you believe only you have, out there, out loud being more common than anything. Made me realize, and think when I have some thoughts from the book: Do I want to make myself feel miserable? Best, funniest way to teach you a great life lesson: don’t get attached and value a destructive mindset.
