I've never read any Trixie Belden books before, but my aunt gave me several of her childhood copies. It's very reminiscent of Nancy Drew books, although maybe a little less scary, which makes sense as Trixie is supposed to be in her early teens as opposed to Nancy's late teens to early adult years. As this book was published in 1961, it is a bit outdated, but the overall story was still cute and age appropriate for my daughters. I liked that, despite their initial assumption of Dan being a bad guy, their minds are changed by the end. They even invite him to join their Bob-Whites Gang, inviting him into their fold.
