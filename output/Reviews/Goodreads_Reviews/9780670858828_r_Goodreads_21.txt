I don't often give five star reviews. I'm rather stingy with those. I wish Goodreads had more than a five star system, for one. I would also have a different rating system for different genres. I might give a four star to an easy read, but it wouldn't equate to a four star for a much heavier subject. I gave this book five stars because of the author's depth of understanding of the complex situation of post-apartheid South Africa, and of the emotional landscape of her characters that she depicts in this novel. She has a way of reflecting the societal upheavals in the intimate relationships of the main characters. She has a true artist's sense of phrasing. Having said that, I also find the structure of some of her sentences and paragraphs to be grammatically confusing. If there were 10 stars I would subtract a star for that to give this book a rating of nine.
