Not my favorite Beattie novel, and found it hard to read. Maybe shades of questions and concerns about teaching that I've had over a long time. Will not recap the plot. Suffice it to say that caring people sometimes goof up, screw up, and make some life for themselves. Teachers teaching poetry who don't write poems? Incredibly awful people who lie and keep on lying and...? And siblings trying to remember what their life was like in childhood. Memories and dreams and nightmares.
