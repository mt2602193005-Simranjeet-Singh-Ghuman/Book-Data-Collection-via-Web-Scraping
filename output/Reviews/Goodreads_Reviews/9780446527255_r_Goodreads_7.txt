Author Sabrina is approached by a reporter, Derek, for a story and turns him down. She has a severely handicapped son and is in a very unhappy marriage with a very wealthy man in nyc. A few months later, she learns that Derek has been unjustly sent to prison for murder. What follows is intrigue and love. There was just something about this book that made it hard to keep my interest. Part of it was that it was very predictable, but there’s something else I just can’t put my finger on.
