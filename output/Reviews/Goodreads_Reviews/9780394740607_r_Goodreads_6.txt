I play the chancy world like a harp tuned by a half-mad satyr on a foreign isle, finding its secrets out by feel. Modern (1971) retelling of classic Greek myth in blank verse, complete with glasses- and hat-wearing “modern” narrator. Monumental effort—to write and to read. Countless inexcusable anachronisms. Not bad, in fact well done, but a waste of time. “See it to the end. The gods require it.” Modifies Greek mythology to suit his story, changing attributes of the gods and rewriting their history, a practice as old as Greek mythology itself. And, like classic Greek tales, no trivia is too small to include. The rabbit trails have rabbit trails. Leavened with modern Western and Eastern (circa 1971) philosophy, religion, and physics. Nothing is impossible! Nothing is definite! Be calm! Be brave!
