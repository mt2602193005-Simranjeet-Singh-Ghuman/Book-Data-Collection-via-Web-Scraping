10/1/2021-I reread this book as an adult and while a lot of it was dated, given the time it was written, it was still a nice read with basic information that any teenage newbie witch should use and read about. I love how Ellen writes like she is just having a conversation with the person reading and I think it would be a nice touch for any teenager who reads it. My four star rating still stands. This is a good book by Ellen Dugan. She gives lots of info on basic things to get started. She states a few basic beliefs and then goes into telling about the Elements and candle/color magick. She also describes working with herbs and using the outdoors alot. My favorite part of the book was when she went into detail about the element of Water because that was one element I felt most connected too and it helped get to know what symbols are associated with that element. I also was interested in the Fire element too seeing as I'm a Sagittarus. This is a book I think all beginner teen will love and should read. It helps with the basic's and does provide a few spells to use.
