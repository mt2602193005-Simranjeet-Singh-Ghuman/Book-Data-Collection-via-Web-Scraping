I loved this book so much! I was a struggling reader but this book gripped and transported me. I hung over my bunk reading it out to my younger brother who was also enraptured. I remember it clearly almost 40 years later. I have never seen another copy of this book and it may no longer be in print - the author has now sadly died. As everyone knows, nothing ages faster or worse than futuristic science fiction- I expect it would be rather dated now. But how charming it would feel to re read this rare, happy moment from my childhood.
