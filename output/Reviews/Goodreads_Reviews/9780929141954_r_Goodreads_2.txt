Main character has the social graces of a garden snail - she says some terrible things to people she supposedly likes. She is selfish and occasionally, childish. That kind of character doesn't work in a cozy mystery. Plot is virtually non-existent - everything happens in the first 10 pages and the last 10 pages. The rest is full of Santa angst, knitting and yarn angst, boyfriend angst, etc. 1 star because I actually finished it. 1 star for it being unintentionally funny in spots.
