Simona helps her ad agency's client, Roberta Riddle, when the fashion designer's sample model is found murdered. Roberta asks for Simona to investigate--only partly because Simona has just moved in with the lead detective, Stan Greenhouse--but she's not prepared to tell Simona the truth about the various people who work in her design house. Meanwhile, someone seems to be targeting Simona. Fortunately, a Russian cab driver with a soft heart that's as large as his muscular frame comes to Simona's rescue.
