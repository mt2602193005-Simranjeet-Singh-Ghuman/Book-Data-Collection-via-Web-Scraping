Book 24 of the Morland Dynasty series follows Henrietta and Venetia as they finally get their happy endings. For Henrietta, it's tinged with the worry of scandal as her husband is a divorced man, a social stigma in Victorian England. Whilst I always love following the fortunes of the London contingent, it's Morland Place that I most want to hear about and there wasn't as much of the story set there in this book. However, it set the stage nicely for book 25, The Question. This was another fantastic instalment of a series I adore.
