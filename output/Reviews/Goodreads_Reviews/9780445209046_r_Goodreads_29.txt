Descends Into Nonsense. I have read many books by this author, he seems to be a very uneven writer, at times I've really enjoyed his work and at other times I did not. This book for most of the first three quarters I found enjoyable, but there came a breaking point for me when it seemed to devolve into incoherent nonsense. I ended up hating it, giving two stars is generous because I enjoyed the beginning.
