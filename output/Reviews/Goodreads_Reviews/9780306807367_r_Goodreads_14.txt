The first part of the book describing Geisel's early years were interesting. But I was disappointed in the rest. Most notable of the disappointments was how the authors brushed over the death of Ted's first wife. Clearly the authors knew that this life event could tarnish Geisel's public image. But by barely touching on the subject, the authors only made it look worse. I try not to read biographies that are written by family and friends. I was reminded again, as I read this book, that I should have stuck to this principle.
