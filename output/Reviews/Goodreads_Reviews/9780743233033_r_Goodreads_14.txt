Eat the Rich! I love this book and this series - it makes me laugh out loud, a lot. The premise is quite simple: pampas old rich guy from Imperial England comes over to the American frontier to shoot everything in sight, thinks he can order/re-order everyone and everything, including Mother Nature, according to his privileged whims and, in then end, gets what's coming to him. In the process he damn near destroys his family and everyone around him. While the rich get predicably eaten, literally and figuratively, by nature, the frontier and its inhabitants, it is the strong female characters who hold this story together and keep the book moving along with a sassy spirit. I can't wait to read what the Berrybender women will get up to in the next installment.
