I wasn't quite sure what to think of this book at first. It's a novel based on Louisa May Alcott's Little Women with the three main characters (sisters) named Meg, Joanna and Amy. However, the novel completely diverges from there. The sisters find out about their mother's affair, and although their parents are unconcerned as it has been resolved, they take great offense and Jo and Amy go with Meg to school. I'm giving this four stars because I found it to be rather witty and the ending was very clever. I also appreciated the exploration of their feelings and their relationship as sisters.
