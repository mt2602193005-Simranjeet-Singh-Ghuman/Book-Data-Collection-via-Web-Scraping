This collection of poems includes a variety of poets from William Blake to Nikki Giovanni. It also includes Native American chants, a Spanish poem by Frederico Garcia Lorca and Japanese haiku in both English and Japanese. All the poems that have been chosen (even those of classic poets) are accessible to all students from kindergarten on up. It would be hard not to find a connection in these poems! It includes both a table of contents as well as an index. This would be an easy book for a poetry go-around.
