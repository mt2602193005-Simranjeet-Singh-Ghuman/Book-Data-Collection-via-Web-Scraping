It took me a whole lot of time and dedication to get into this and the reason was that Egil, as a kid, was a total little shit. I rather wished someone would strangle him and I knew they wouldn't. Like many real life humans he improved as an adult, and when I got past the early parts of Egil it was great. By the end I was pretty much in awe of the fact this saga spanned the lifetime of a single hero, the amount of information that came through on the times, kings, battles, trading and raiding voyages. It is an incredibly information rich book and the information is enhanced by the notes and indexes I especially valued the unravelling of the poems and the kennings in them.
