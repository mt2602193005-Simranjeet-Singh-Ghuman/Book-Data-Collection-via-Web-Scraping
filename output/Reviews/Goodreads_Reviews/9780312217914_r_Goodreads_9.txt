This was an okay mystery, but interesting because of all the real people involved. I have read another series featuring Dorothy Parker that I enjoyed more. I have friends who will enjoy this more than I did as they are familiar with the personalities. People familiar with New York will also enjoy this as it visits several hot spots at the time. I spent most of the story looking up who was real - the bad guys were fictional. Hope that is not too much of a spoiler. I have ordered the next in the series as I am more familiar with Alfred Hitchcock. I bought a copy of The Dorothy Parker Murder Case.
