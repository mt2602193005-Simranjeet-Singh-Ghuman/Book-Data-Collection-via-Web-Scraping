This was an odd little book. It was kind of all over the place, nothing was explained, and weird stuff just randomly happened. I loved it. It's not a scary book, it's more eerie, and it definitely plays on someone losing their sanity. While we got some answers for why things were happening, I'm not sure what to even believe. The house was quite the character itself, the descriptions really made it something you could invision. The blurb on the cover is so laughable though, "A haunting love story in the tradition of the movie Ghost". This was nothing like ghost, it wasnt even a romance honestly (and thank god for that). I randomly picked this up not expecting a lot but I'm happy to say that I was pleasantly surprised.
