i started reading this book during a sunrise at barrington beach and finished it at nightfall back home in New York and for some reason, i feel like that’s so peoetic ^_^ thank you inari for lending me your copy and what a great read. in no way were these topics light but the narration of this story made this such an easy read and honestly played to themes i often reckon with? why do so many bad things occurs if god unconditionally loves all his children? is god really ever present? am i less of a believer for questioning my faith? why do such terrible things happen to good people? it’s all left to ponder in the plot of this book and as we track gracie’s life from childhood to adulthood, we see how religion is sometimes a burden yet also our salvation. i really commend smith for really explicitly detailing acts of deep christian faith in direct contrast with “sinful” behavior, showing the many false prophets that often walk in our midst and also showing that blind faith can be detrimental. loved this read and really made me practice grace for our titular character (no pun intended) because trauma left untreated can really lead good people down roads of hard battles. read it!!! great for those who many deal with religious trauma or just need a more nuanced discussion of god in the rural south.
