I know this was written a while ago so attitudes about dating are dated, but this book was pretty frustrating to read, characters who were clearly assholes were treated like nice guys a woman would like to date, and not with any authorial overview like the characters are making bad decisions. More like just as a matter of course, for instance, a cop who is obsessed with a teenage waitress at a diner crosses the line into creepy but in the book he ends up a hero.
