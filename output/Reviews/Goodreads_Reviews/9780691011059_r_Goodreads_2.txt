I truly did not expect to read this book in it's entirety. I brought it home from the local library just to glance through it and read the "important stuff". I ended up reading every single word. The author is so likeable and his writing is almost like reading a very excellent novel. He is a zoologist and rescues a great horned owlet and the reader follows him on this remarkable journey of how he raises it, eventually releases it, and follows "Stripey's" adventures. Totally educational and never boring.
