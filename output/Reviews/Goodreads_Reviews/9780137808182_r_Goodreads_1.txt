This was a fun read and a very honest look at sexuality. I enjoyed that sex was treated as a mostly mental exercise and the problems were examined as mental road blocks. I think both couples and single people could enjoy and benefit from this book, as the issues addressed are both personal and interpersonal in nature. As is the case with many interpersonal problems, even those of a sexual nature, the problem begins with you. I'm not sure all of the visualization techniques work, but they are a good beginning for thinking about sex as an enjoyable and playful activity between adults and not a chore or obligation. The index in the back provides reference for specific problems with the page numbers for the corresponding visualization technique.
