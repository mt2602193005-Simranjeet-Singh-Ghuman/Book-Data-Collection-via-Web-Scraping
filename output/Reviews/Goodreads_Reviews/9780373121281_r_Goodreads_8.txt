I love me some smitten hero. It's not usually something that's right in my alley, but I find this is sweet. Old college friends form a love triangle in which the odd one out finally gets to play the lead. The best thing about it is that all characters were really not so hateful at the end. Everyone wasn't really vindictive and jaded as you would think having money, drive and ambition as key but subtle element in the book. I'm glad I took a chance with this book.
