The book is about Eldridge Cleaver becoming radicalized in prison as he begins to see how America oppresses men like him. The first part was a coherent call for social change but he later rants about raping white women and some allegory about sexual politics or something. There is much that he goes on about that is still true today especially with the relationship between black men and the police. With Trump in the White House there is going to be more black men pondering the same issues as Eldridge Cleaver in prison.
