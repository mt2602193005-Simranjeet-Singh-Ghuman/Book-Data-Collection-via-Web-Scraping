I wish I'd found a different edition than the one illustrated by Melissa Manwell. The pix are adequate, but not charming as they could have been. The story is an animal fantasy, typical of King-Smith's work. I wish more of his were still avl. in libraries, rather than Babe & just a couple of others. I do appreciate the acknowledgement that one can become 'hooked' on chocolate and suffer 'severe withdrawal symptoms.' It's true!
