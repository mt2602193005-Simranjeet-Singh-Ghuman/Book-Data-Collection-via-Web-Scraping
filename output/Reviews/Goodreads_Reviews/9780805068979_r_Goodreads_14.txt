3.5 stars Tennant did a good job of describing the flowers and plants surrounding Rovinia. Also describing the food that she got to eat while there. This is definitely a train of thought memoir writing style. Sometimes I would get a bit lost. She was clearly just writing memories as the occured to her and then it all was put together in this book. It didn't insight any real laughter or tears or emotions from me as a reader. But it did encourage me more to take a trip to Greece and the islands. The ex-pat lifestyle really brings forth the idea of wanting to go live it, for sure.
