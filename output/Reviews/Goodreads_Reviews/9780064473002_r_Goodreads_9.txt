I’ve been reading some of the books that have been on my TBR list for more than a decade, and I’m glad to check this one off my list. The world building is incredible, and the descriptions of so many species living on the same planet is so fascinating. However, the characters are not as well fleshed out as the setting. I found myself skipping a lot of the last part of the book, just to see what happens and to be done. It was an okay ending, but a little flat.
