This book is exactly what a “self help” book should be. I am often disappointed by these kinds of books, because they are usually filled primarily with positive encouragement and inspiring quotes, which are both good things, but I feel like they almost always lack substance; practical action steps to help people help themselves. Not this book. 95% of this book is devoted to walking the reader through actual exercises to help them monitor and modify their own thoughts, feelings, and behaviors. I will be reading this again in the near future, and referring to this often when working with my clients.
