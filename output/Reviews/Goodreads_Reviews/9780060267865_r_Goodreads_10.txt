3.5 a collection of very brief stories, some spooky (ghosts, ghouls, poltergeists), some frightening (murderers, insanity, the bizarre), and some just gross (graveyard-bone soup, stolen toes, and body decomposition). a few were a bit too disgusting for my taste, and most were so simple and lacking in detail that they almost felt like sketches of scary stories...but maybe elaborate horror is too much to ask of a children's book.
