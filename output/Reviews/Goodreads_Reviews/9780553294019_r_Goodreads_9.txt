You are now a grown-up; you decide to grab a Choose-Your-Own-Adventure from a discount rack. Hey, your uncle Raoul is letting you drive in a car race! Do you want the Porsche or the Ferrari? It doesn't matter what car you drive. Oh, no! You see someone suspicious with a suitcase, and he says he can help you win the race IF you follow him to a restaurant where a suspicious lady is waiting. Do you follow the man, or do you tell Uncle Raoul to bring the damn car around so you can start the race? And so on. You realize that these books have hidden morals, where if you're a dick to the nice characters, or if you stupidly get involved with suspicious characters who are NOT Uncle Raoul, you will be kidnapped and the police will never find your body. The End.
