This is another book in the Little Critter series by Mercer Mayer. It's a great book for children, especially when the topic in the classroom is about going to the dentist. It explores what the experience of going to the dentist is as well as introducing new words that children may have never heard before. It explains what an X-ray is, what a dentist chair is, what a cavity is..etc. It's a great learning tool for children to learn about the dentist and why it's nothing to be afraid of. LE: In small groups, have the children discuss their experiences, if any, about going to the dentist. Was it as bad as they thought it was going to be? Was it fun? What did the dentist tell them? This way as a small group the children can share common experiences and the teacher can discuss the importance of going to the dentist and why.
