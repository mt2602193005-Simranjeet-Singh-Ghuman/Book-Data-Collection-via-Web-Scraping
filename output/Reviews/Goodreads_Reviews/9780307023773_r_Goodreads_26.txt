Out of the 16 Bantam Doc Savage series novels I've read so far, this was a chore to get through. I thought for sure someone other than Lester Dent had written it because it seemed poorly written. The prose seemed stilted and just didn't flow the way the other novels did. The novel started with Doc, Ham, and Monk and never even mentioned the other three members of the gang until half way through, and then just in passing that they were busy elsewhere.
