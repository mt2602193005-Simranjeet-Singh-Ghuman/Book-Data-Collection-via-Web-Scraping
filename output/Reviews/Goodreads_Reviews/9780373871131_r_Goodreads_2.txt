I didn't finish this one. I read 50 pages and then skimmed about 100 more. Here are the main problems i had with the book: 1. The author tried to be suspenseful by reminding readers every two pages that something terrible happened the night that Gabrielle left Drew and we don't know what it is. That's not suspenseful; that's annoying. The terrible thing, by the way, was the reason Gabrielle left Drew. Her horse injured its leg and the immediate conclusion was that it should be put down. Gabrielle wanted to try a way to heal the leg first, but Drew went out and shot it. I don't exactly blame her. I would find that very hard to forgive . 2. Throughout the book, Drew does everything possible (at the doctor's suggestion) to keep Gabrielle from knowing that she lost 9 months worth of memories. He should have told her the truth from the beginning. Instead, he chose to lie many, many times and even go so far as to buy a look-a-like horse to replace the one she loved whose death she doesn't remember . This is a Christian book, by the way. The Christian author believes it's okay to be that deceitful. 3. The characters were constantly remembering things. Going to make dinner? Memory. In the action of cooking? Memory. Putting dinner on the table? Memory. It made the plot move veeeeery sloooowly. 4. On top of all of that, there were run-on sentences and sentences that didn't make any sense. Awful book. Don't waste your time.
