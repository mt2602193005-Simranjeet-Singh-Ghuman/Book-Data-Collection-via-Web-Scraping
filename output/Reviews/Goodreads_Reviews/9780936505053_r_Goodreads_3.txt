Probably the closest thing to a biography of Jim and Marian Jordan that we'll ever get, which is unfortunate; this is the kind of book that starts from "Remember how great these guys were? They were great!" and doesn't feel very pressed to prove it. In describing the off-mic lives of the Jordans it's very reliant on breezy, in-character features from '30s and '40s magazines, and it's hard to see where those end and the in-person interviews begin since there are no citations or explanations for the material. It's pleasant for what it is, though, and it does a good job of compiling information from those sources, even if it doesn't do anything with it. Worth it, if nothing else, for all the great photos, most of which are still not on the internet anyplace I can find them. Show more
