Typical historical romance. I'm giving only 3 stars because apparently there is a long convoluted back story with the main characters' ancestors that I couldn't keep straight. Perhaps this is the second in a series that doesn't quite stand alone? Also some stilted language where I had to re-read a paragraph a few times (or even aloud) to understand what they were saying. Two typos (in a printed book!) threw me too. "Seen" rather than "seem" and "his" rather than "hers". Not something a spell check would have caught.
