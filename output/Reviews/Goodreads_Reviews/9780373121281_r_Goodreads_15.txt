The H fell in love with the h back when they were in uni together but she had a boyfriend from the same class whom she dates for the next 6 years. After that, said boyfriend periodically dumps her to be with other women then comes back. The h is a moderately attractive self made career woman while the H is the heir to a million dollar business who looks like Adonis -she resents him for this. Things come to a head when her ex being the mole rat that he is, sends her a formal wedding invite as a way of telling her its over between them. The H's occasional one-line POVs were sweet. I felt for him, having unrequited love for 10 years and wish he had taken more steps to grab the h for himself. Then again, he does tell the h that was so infatuated with her ex that she would never have considered him. Cute HEA with nice detailed love scenes, with the H occasionally angst-ing about the h only loving him for his body lol. I think the h should have begged more though - who the heck kisses their stinky ex on the mouth if they're excited?
