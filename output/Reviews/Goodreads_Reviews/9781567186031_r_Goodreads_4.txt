I've been toting this book and CD around with me since I was a teen in the nineties. Pulled it out today because my young sons are doing a mythology unit and were reading about the Mabinogion. The songs about Welsh mythology are pure gold, no matter your religious feelings about the Pagan songs in the book. All the songs on the CD are the myth retellings. The one with the chorus, "Mighty Pwyll descended into Annwn..." is a banger. I'd love to know whatever became of Hugin the Bard.
