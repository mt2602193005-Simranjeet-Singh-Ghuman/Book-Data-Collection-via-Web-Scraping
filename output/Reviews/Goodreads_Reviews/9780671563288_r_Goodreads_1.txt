I read The Last Curve , a romantic suspense, by Margaret Allison in 2007. This was my first story by this author and I wasn’t impressed. Fast forward to 2016 and I found the book Indiscretion and I thought I would give her another chance. Katherine Wells was starting over after a relationship ended badly at her former workplace. She was to meet a friend (the lady was a no-show) at a bar that evening before starting her new job the next day. This was not the type of place that Katherine would usually visit. Within a short time, a handsome man introduced himself as ‘Mike’. Before the night was over, he saved Katherine from an overbearing creep and asked if he could meet her again soon. It turned out to be her new boss. Let me just mention that the reader is constantly reminded that Katherine was beautiful. Pretty. Gorgeous. And Michael was handsome. Good-looking. With a body to die for. Then, for the next umpteen pages, Michael Benson does his best not to be attracted to her because he doesn’t believe in relationships with co-workers or marriage. This comes from a guy in his mid-thirties who had been sexually involved with one of his vendors who happened to be a family friend who worked for him. She subcontracted for his company so that was the reason it made it different. Talk about the pot calling the kettle black! Lastly, there was The Big Misunderstanding. Need I say more?
