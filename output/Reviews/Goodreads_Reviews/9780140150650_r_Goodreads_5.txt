A history of how we got to the point of having history. Herodotus, one tentacles down though he tried, I think his values are too foreign to us. Thucydides and his interminable made-up speeches, two tentacles down. Xenophon, his intelligence shows and he can write readable almost modern prose, two tentacles up. Polybius and his contempt for the made-up nonsense but not fun, one tentacle up. A very good sampling but just too much Thucydides.
