An Awesome book!!! I loved reading The Cowboy and The Cradle(Tallchief Book 1). All about Duncan Tallchief a thirty-five year old Cowboy/Rancher & Sybil White who is early thirty’s and a independent researcher/Mom. She has contacted Duncan in person to plead that he finds her thirteen year old daughter Emily, who has been kidnapped by her ex husband. During Duncan’s off hours he rescues children & fugitives in a timely matter. His code name is Mother, and he’s very good at his job. Read this story and find out what adventures await when dealing with a Tallchief!! Every emotion known to man runs thru this book, it’s gripping and will hold your attention to the last word in the book!!
