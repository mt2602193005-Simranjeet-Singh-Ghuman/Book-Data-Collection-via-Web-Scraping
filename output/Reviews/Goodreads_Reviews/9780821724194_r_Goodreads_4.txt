His vow to shame her cost him his heart! A powerful, arrogant New Orleans planter believes "Miss L. Delaney" is responsible for his uncle's death. He plots to humiliate the schemer called Lavinia Delaney. Instead, he confuses his uncle's lover with her refined cousin, Laurel Delaney. Laurel tries to help Lavinia, only to land in trouble herself with the reckless rogue bent on revenge. Sensuous, charming Tony Duvalier will stop at nothing until he makes "Miss L. Delaney" pay for her mistakes. This story whisks readers into a world of glittering steamboats, jasmine-scented breezes, and Southern-style seduction beneath shimmering moonlight. Lynette Vinet weaves a magical spell of fiery passion as bright and hot as a MIDNIGHT FLAME.
