Very interesting and illuminating look at the experiences of a US astronaut putting in a tour of duty on Mir. The book falls into several parts. The first part, where Linegar talks about his training to become an astronaut, is a fascinating "origin story" for this almost superhero. I say superhero because the man is astonishing - his physical health and stamina, medical training, the number of degrees he picks up along the way boggle the mind. The part talking about his time training in Russia, and the next part about flying on Mir, are illuminating in a different way. His view of the process is somewhat cynical, but that is the result of what happens to him. Poorly run training provided by the Russians with no real support from the Americans (who did not want to offend the Russians), and the dangers faced on Mir itself, make these parts of the book interesting as well, though in rather a different way.
