Just a toy starts out with little critter and his sister going to the toy store. His sister picks a toy easily but critter takes a little while to pick the perfect toy he ends up picking a dinosaur. He takes it home to only for the dinosaurs head to fall off. Critter is not happy so his father takes him back to the toy store the next day and he gets a toy that is already in pieces this time so he can build it.
