3.5 stars from me 4.75 stars from my son I read this with my 10 year old son for the kids bookclub he belongs to through one of our local independent bookstores. It wasn't one I had previously heard of, although it is a Dutch classic, where it was originally published in 1962. This is quaintly written old fashioned chivalric fantasy which would provide a nice entry to the genre for middle grade and young adult audiences. It's an older book, and reads that way, but it nevertheless has a simple appeal that keeps it fresh enough for modern readers. My son is not new to fantasy, and he quite enjoyed this. Aside from the story, he loved the included map and black and white illustrations done by the author. It was quite an accomplishment for him to tackle a book that comes in at just over 500 pages, but the story moves along at a nice pace that ended up making the length less daunting for him.
