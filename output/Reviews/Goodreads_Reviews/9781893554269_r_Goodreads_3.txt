Who Killed Homer? by Victor Davis Hanson and John Heath is a sharp, thought-provoking book that passionately defends the value of classical education and its relevance today. The authors write with clarity, conviction, and deep respect for the ancient world, making a strong case for why Greek language, literature, and history still matter in modern culture. It’s an engaging read that leaves you reflecting long after the last page. A solid four-star book for anyone who cares about education, culture, and the legacy of the classics.
