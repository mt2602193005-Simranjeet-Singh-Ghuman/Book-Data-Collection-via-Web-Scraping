Loved the book up until the point where Clay had slept with a Mexican prostitute to get Nina out of his system. Although this happened early on in the story not long after their meeting and Nina’s departure to Mexico, still it left a sour taste in my mouth. Wish I’d make do not knowing there was another woman in between. Sorry I shouldn’t make a big deal out of it but I am too sensitive for that🥲 Either way, this is such a beautiful love story! There’s a mix of everything in between ; romance, drama, tension, comedy, adventure, violence. I love the camel part the most I don’t know how many times I giggle over Clay’s ploy of deception. Disguising himself as an Arab riding a camel haha!
