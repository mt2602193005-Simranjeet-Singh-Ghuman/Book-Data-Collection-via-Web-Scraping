The book is what it claims to be which is told from the perspective of a serial killer. It interested me as I was trying to understand why a serial killer act the way he or she does. Therefore, this book is great in the way of understanding the mindset of the subject. What I didn’t like was the structure of the book. It jumped around a lot, which makes sense in that it is based on a recollection of someone’s memory. However, I didn’t find the format fit my reading taste. In addition, I noticed an error on one of the first few pages I saw, which stuck with me. I know that is nitpicking, but that was something basic that could have been resolved.
