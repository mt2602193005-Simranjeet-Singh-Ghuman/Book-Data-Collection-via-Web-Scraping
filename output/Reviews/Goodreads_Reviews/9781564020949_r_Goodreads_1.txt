Book Title: The Cats on Tiffany Street Author/Illustrator: Sarah Hayes Reading Level: 2 Book Level: This book would be appropriate for grades 1-3. Book Summary: "The Cats on Tiffany Street" by Sarah Hayes is a fun short picture book which introduces us to the playful cats of Tiffany Street and takes us along for the ride on their adventure outside of their neighborhood. From cats dancing in the street, to our feline friends being captured and brought outside of the city and to a small farm, "The Cats on Tiffany Street" is a well written short story that will be well liked by many of our cat loving friends - who will be kept interested not only by the great illustrations but also the AA/BB rhyme scheme of the story. Bookshelf Genre: Picture Books Bookshelf Mentor Writing Traits: Sentence Fluency - "The Cats on Tiffany Street", while also a fun read in the classroom because it includes playful cats, can be used as a useful mentor text. The book can be used as a tool by teachers to show students how they can utilize rhymes as a writing technique to catch and maintain the interest of readers.
