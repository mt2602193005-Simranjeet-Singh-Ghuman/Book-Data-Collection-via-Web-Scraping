This book is about a little girl named Rachel who gets a blister on her toe. Her mother sees the blister and gets really concerned about it. She proceeds to ask everyone she can think for advice on how to get rid of the blister. They tried everything from band-aids to lemonade and nothing made Rachel feel better. Eventually they get the idea to call the queen and ask for her advice. I thought this book was really cute. I thought the illustrations could have been better, but the story flowed really well and a had a cute concept. This book would be a great resource to use for teaching a rhyming lesson.
