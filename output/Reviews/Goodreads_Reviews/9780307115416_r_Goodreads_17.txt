Synopsis: "It's all about Elmo—as told by the little red Muppet himself—in this charmingly illustrated Sesame Street Little Golden Book. Boys and girls ages 1–4 will delight in hearing all about Elmo's favorite things—which are the same as every toddler's!" My Review: This is a great little book to get to know Elmo better. Being one of Munchkin's favorite Sesame Street characters, this book really appealed to him and kept him engaged as we read about Elmo's favorite things. Munchkin would get very excited when he found something that was his favorite too. Definitely a great book for little Sesame Street and Elmo fans.
