Well, I had to wait for this book to arrive, as I unexpectedly found that I didn't have it, what ??? So I am a little, oh ok 2 months, behind everyone else who is doing the Poirot unofficial challenge, but as you can tell from my 4 star marking, i am really enjoying this challenge. If i'm honest I didn't realise how many Poirot's I had not read. So this was a book of short stories published in"The Sketch" through 1923 and although they are not all excellent (some are better than others) they are all good fun. They all showcase Poirot's prodigious talent in a variety of differing situations. Ably "assisted" as ever by the wonderful Captain Hastings who bumbles through every case wondering what on earth Poirot is up to. A great collection of short stories for every Poirot fan
