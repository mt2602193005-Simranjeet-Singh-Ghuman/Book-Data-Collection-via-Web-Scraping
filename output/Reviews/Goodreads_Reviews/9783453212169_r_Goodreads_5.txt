Look at your circle of guy friends. Could the love of your life be standing right in front of you and you don't even know it? That is what this book is all about. It's a lighthearted novel about a girl with a group of friends and a problem with finding the guy whose right for her. She has two good girlfriends and a best guy friend, all of whom are close. Througout the book, you'll follow her and her friends as they experience situations that have most likely happened to you or your friends. It is a good book that you can relate to and you are constantly asking the question: Who will she end up with??
