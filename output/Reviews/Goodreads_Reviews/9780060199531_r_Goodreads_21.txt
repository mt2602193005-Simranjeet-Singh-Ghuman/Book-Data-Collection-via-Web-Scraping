I read this book because it was given to me. There is probably 60 pages of useful information: p40 - 100. The ideas are clearly now outdated and it is less suitable for puppy owners and more suitable for people who have a dog with one specific issue they want to address. Once you’ve read those relevant bits of the book, the rest basically says “a dog had an issue. I applied these principles. The dog didn’t have that issue again.” And in summary, if a dog does something you don’t like, ignore it. If it does something you want it to do, reward it. I read it all because I struggle to stop a book once I’ve started, even if I don’t enjoy it. This was one such case.
