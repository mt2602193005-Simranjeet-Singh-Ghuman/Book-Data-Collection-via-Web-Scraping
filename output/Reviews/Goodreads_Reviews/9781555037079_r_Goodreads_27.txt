Join Kitty as she relates her idyllic childhood in 1930's Indonesia. Her parents, both children of Indonesian mothers and Dutch fathers, were wonderful parents. Her grandparents were remarkable as well. Then, when Kitty was six years old, the invasion of the Japanese cast a dark shadow over their lives. At this point in the story I knew I wanted to share it with my family so I began reading aloud to my two teenagers from page one. This story was even more compelling to me than Corrie ten Boom's "The Hiding Place". As WWII came to an end the Indonesians made a strike for independence from the Dutch. Those of mixed parentage (Dutch and Indonesian)were now outcasts, deprived of property and shunned by former friends and family. Kitty relates her family's struggles through all these perils, their escape to Holland, their challenges all along the way, and then to her adulthood in America. If I could, I would give this book ten stars.
