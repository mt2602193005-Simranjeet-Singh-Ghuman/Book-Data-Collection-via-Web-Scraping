I think this might be King's most underrated work. I can see why the GR aggregate score is so low, though: it's not horror, it's a connection of linked short stories, not a novel, and frankly is King at his most literary. Which means the general public wishing for a haunted whatsit might bounce off of this thing. But those that don't are rewarded by King's book about the 1960's and America's involvement in the Vietnam war, while also weaving in a coming of age tale, some Dark Tower-linked magical realism, and a beautiful ending from someone known to normally struggle with that. A fantastic reread.
