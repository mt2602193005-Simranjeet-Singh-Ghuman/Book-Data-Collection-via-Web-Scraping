I really need to stop reading James Patterson novels. I didn't hate this book. It was actually pretty thrilling, but close to 400 pages, the ending really shouldn't be the pitch for another book. Also, the whole family drama subplot seemed kind of thrown in without much of a setup. The main plot also seemed recycled from the previous Alex Cross thrillers "Kiss The Girls" and "I, Alex Cross", which already did this whole kidnapping of women by some big bad guy plot before. I guess I'm getting a little sick of this easy bad guy plot. Two stars.
