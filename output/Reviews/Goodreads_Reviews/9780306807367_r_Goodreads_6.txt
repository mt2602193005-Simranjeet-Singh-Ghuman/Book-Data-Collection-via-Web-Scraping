This book is for all the old fuddy-duddies who hate Dr. Seuss and prefer Dick, Jane and Spot. It talks about some stupid things that he's done, but it also talks about the points that he was trying to prove through his books. It's not all nonsense. I like this book, because it's not the stereotypical, dull autobiography. It was written by 2 of his close friends, which probably gave the book more information, but they may have left out some truly bad details about his life. Over all, this book is humorous and satisfying. Everyone should read it. Even you, Grandpa Joe. Even you.
