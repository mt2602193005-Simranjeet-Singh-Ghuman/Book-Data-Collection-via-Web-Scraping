Summary: A little boy named Henry is lonely, so his parents buy him a dog. The dog, named Mudge, becomes Henry's best friend. They walk to school together, play together, and Mudge sleeps on Henry's bed every night. One day, Mudge goes on a walk without Henry. He goes so far that he becomes lost. Henry goes out looking for his dog and calls and calls for him until finally he finds Mudge. Mudge will never run away again, and Henry never worries about it again. My thoughts: I love this book! It has an element of poetry in the writing, and the illustrations are adorable. It is a great book for dog lovers and shows kids that animals can be your best friend. One thing I didn't like was that only Henry was out looking for Mudge when he got lost. I think it would have been meaningful if Henry's parents had helped look for Mudge too. Overall, this is a wonderful book!
