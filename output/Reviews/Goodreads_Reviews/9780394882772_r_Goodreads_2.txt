I absolutely love this book. There have been numerous books written about little boys getting a dog, and this book is the exact opposite. I love how it is told from the dog's perspective of him getting his new boy. It was heartwarming for me to read, and I am sure children would love it as well, especially those who have a pet dog. I enjoyed reading the concept of the dog explaining, from his point of view, what the boy does that he loves. I recommend this book to anyone! It was an adorable book, especially for animal lovers.
