There is one story in this collection that I love and that's Under the Kissing Bough by Mary Kingsley. I am sure the other stories are fine, but not peculiarly memorable. Under the Kissing Bough is the only story I find myself rereading every year during Christmas time. I am not sure why, it is a simple story of two people who knew each other as children reconnecting when they are snowbound together. I think I enjoy it because there is an undeniable sweetness to it. If I was rating on the this story alone I would give it 4.5 stars.
