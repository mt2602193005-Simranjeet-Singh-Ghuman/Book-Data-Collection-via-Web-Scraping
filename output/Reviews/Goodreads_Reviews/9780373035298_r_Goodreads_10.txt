This book is basically about the hero who always felt like he played second fiddle to his more charming brother. In fact, so much so that the woman he became enamored with, the heroine's sister, ditched him for that brother. Enter the heroine, who was about sixteen at the time. She was a nuisance and a thorn in the hero's side. The reason why is glaringly obvious to everyone but the hero. He remains oblivious throughout the years, and that's why he's horrified when his old flame begs him to look after her sister, who is determined to come to his neck of the woods and take it by storm. The book is told 99.9% from the hero's perspective, which I thought was an intriguing change from either being solely in the heroine's point-of-view or splitting between the hero and heroine. Other than that, I don't have much else to say. The book was entertaining in a fluffy sort of way. Good for a trip to the beach or when you need to take your mind off of a busy life, but don’t' want to embroil yourself in a serious murder mystery or a deep and intense romance with strong-willed characters.
