I listened to this audiobook. I found this series after seeing several episodes on TV of the Murder in a Small Town show based on these books. Therefore, I did know who the murderer is because of seeing a TV episode based on this book. However, there were significant differences in the TV version and this book. As usual, the book is better than any TV adaptation. But I took off 1/2 star because the coincidences in the story were too obvious. I didn't find some of the story credible. A woman is found murdered in the woods. A ex-con finds the body while walking his dog. He really doesn't want to get mixed up in a murder case, so he is less than cooperative. Meanwhile, the librarian in whom Staff Sergeant Alberg is romantically interested is having a fling with an aging actor who is visiting the Sunshine Coast. The actor lives in Los Angeles and so it is another coincidence that the murdered woman is someone he knows. I like the pace of these books and I like Alberg's personality.
