The world is so much more homogenized now, compared to when this was written. Enjoyable travelogue of 1980s Italy, mixed with a writer’s journey into her Italian heritage. The writing felt self involved at times, but the author seemed to capture an Italy in transition that I hadn’t considered prior to reading the book. Recommend as a good pre-trip read, and found myself paging through the book after we came back from our own trip. Fun NYC bookstore pick up, glad to have read.
