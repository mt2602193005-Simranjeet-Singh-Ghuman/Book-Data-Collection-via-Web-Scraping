The 2009 Wes Anderson film featuring the voice talents of Bill Murray and George Clooney is a favorite of mine and so I was compelled to visit the original work, the beloved children’s book by the inimitable Roald Dahl. First published in 1970, this short work describes the machinations of a chicken thief, Mr. Fox. Perhaps not as urbane as portrayed by Clooney and directed by Anderson, Dahl’s fox is nonetheless fantastic for his ability to outfox messers Boggis, Bunce, and Bean. Being well acquainted with the film it was fun to see what scenes from the book had been dramatized and I can report that Anderson stayed true to the book while adding some fun details for our viewing pleasure. This then, is the opposite of most novels in that the director added scenes to the film. Good fun for Dahl fans, aficionados of children’s lit and for Wes Anderson enthusiasts like me.
