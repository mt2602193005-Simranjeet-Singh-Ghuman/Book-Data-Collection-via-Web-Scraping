I feel bad rating this low, especially because it's someone story. But I hope that her writing this book was the true therapy she needed in order to release some of this trauma and move forward. I felt like the pace was very slow. I often times wondered why we were giving so much backstory, but also figured it was important in some way. I completely mistook what this book was supposed to be about- I was 1000% convinced it was going to be a kidnapping story- spoiler alert: it's 100% NOT. I do think the ending leaves an air of mystery, which if this was a thriller- I would absolutely obsess over. Overall, just not a fan though.
