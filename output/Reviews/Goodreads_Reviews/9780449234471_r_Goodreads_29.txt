Continuing to enjoy Mrs. Pollifax. Appreciated that the author was content to leave most of the violence off-screen. That works just fine, publishers and filmmakers. You don't have to be ubergraphic about everything! The only annoying thing was having something important hinge on somebody's clairvoyance. Narrators can make or break an audiobook experience for me based on how they voice characters of the opposite sex, so I continue to appreciate Barbara Rosenblat's performances.
