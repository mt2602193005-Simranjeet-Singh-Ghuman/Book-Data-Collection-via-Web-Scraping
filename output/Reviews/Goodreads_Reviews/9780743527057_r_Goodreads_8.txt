Red Dragon by Thomas Harris is the first in the Hannibal Lector series. My god what a creepy start it is. The addiction for this book starts right from the first page and keeps you gripped untill the last, we only get snippets of Hannibal and when he does show you can just feel the chills forming 😲 Francis Dolarhyde (red dragon ) is a terrible and despicable,creepy psychopath (which nobody wants to meet ) but doesn't have that lector way about him. This is a sinister, heart palpitating book which has you reaching for the next in the series as soon as possible. Do I recommend this??? 🤔 Oh definitely!!!
