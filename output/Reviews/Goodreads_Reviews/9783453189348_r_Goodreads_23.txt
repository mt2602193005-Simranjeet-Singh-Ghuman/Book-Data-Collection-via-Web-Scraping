St. Marian of fiction can really do no wrong in my eyes, well ears really. I can’t believe I had never read this title, I don’t know how it passed me by, I kept waiting to remember the story and I never did. An early Keyes (1999) and a winner! The old emotions were a bit ropey, I was on the brink of tears often, but more importantly were the friendships, and the laughs. The book covers the stigma of HIV and the trauma of cancer, but it’s funny, and kind and smart and Mariany so you know it’s worth the trauma.
