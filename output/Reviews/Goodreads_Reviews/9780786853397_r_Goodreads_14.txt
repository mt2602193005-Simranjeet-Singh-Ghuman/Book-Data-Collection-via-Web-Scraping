The first time I read a Birnbaum's guide I was an excited tween who had never been to Disney and I studied it like a religious document. Now, as an adult who's been to Disney a few times, I'm less impressed. This is a decent basic guide for people who have never been to Disney World at all, but none of the information is too exciting if you're already familiar with Disney. Frankly, most (if not all) the information contained in it can be found for free online. It's also pretty obvious that this is an "official" guide, since it tells exactly what Disney would want you to know. No insider tips or tricks here.
