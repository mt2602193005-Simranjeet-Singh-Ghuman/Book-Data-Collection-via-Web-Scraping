A solid 3.5. Herbert had an amazing imagination. The Magic Cottage is set in the New Forest. Mike and Midge a young couple buy it. The history of the cottage is mysterious Flora and elderly eccentric woman lived and died there. Strange things begin to happen with birds and animals strangely tame. A bird with a broken wing mysteriously heals overnight. A strange cult befriends them with their leader Mycroft memorizing. Mike begins to have hallucinations and the cottage seems to have a life of its own. Tension builds with the Synergist Cult bewitching Midge and a confrontation at the cottage reveals the truth.
