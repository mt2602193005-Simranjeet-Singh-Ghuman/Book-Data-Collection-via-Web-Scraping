Adorable. This jaded reader has seen the trope too many other times to be enchanted, and Sam is just too too smart & curious (and his family too too heedless) but it's highly recommended to new parents and older siblings who need help understanding the perspective of a child who, for example, doesn't know that water pipes and smoking pipes are different. Best moment is when Sam realizes his poor impulse control has gotten him into a mess and he tells his mom that he wishes he could spank himself (but neither of them want spanking to happen, so they decide to laugh instead). I also recommend Socks by Beverly Cleary if you liked the idea of seeing the world through the eyes of a small being with a limited means of expression....
