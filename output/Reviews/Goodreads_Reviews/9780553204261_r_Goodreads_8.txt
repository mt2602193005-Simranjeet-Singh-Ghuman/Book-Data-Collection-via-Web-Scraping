Just what the doctor ordered! A Medicine for melancholy is a series of short stories by Ray Bradbury. Even though it was written in 1959, there were quit a few stories readers can relate to today. A family moves to Mars to escape nuclear war on Earth, when they can not return, something very alien starts to happen to them. A Man orders a hair piece to charm his young neighbor, but will she be? A husband takes note from National Geographic Magazine and begins his passage to the next life, his wife is not so impressed. Some of these stories were a surprise. I wasn't expecting a few endings and they turned out more sci-fi or heart felt then I would have guessed. One of two felt a little out dated or under developed but the book is good enough as a whole that you wont even remember those ones.
