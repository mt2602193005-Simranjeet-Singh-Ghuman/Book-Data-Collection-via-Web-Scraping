Mom… can you come pick me up? The boys are eating each other’s shit and I’m scared… A self-conscious Marquis de Sade looking for their soul. Who wouldn’t be upset about it? That would be too embarrassing, too anachronistic. And you have much better music and drugs to contend with. The worst part is winding up with a soul you have to pretend you don’t have. Sex is a battle of permutations we are all looking to lose, bloodied and bruised. Wanting to get inside the other and when you get there all you see is guts and ripped assholes competing with the craters of the moon. Expecting to find yourself reflected back in the deformities of desire and only finding things to graze against in dark rooms. Weirdly or maybe not so, some of the teenage boy inner monologues remind me of Duras??? Hell yeah.
