I wish zero stars was an option. Imagine reading the worst and most depraved John Saul book back to back with the worst and most incesty V.C. Andrews book while extremely ill. This is that fever dream. Confusing is an understatement (at some points past events were happening written in present tense, and flashbacks were concurrently happening written in past tense). This wasn't scary because I didn't care about any of the characters, and certain elements were never fully explained (the "muses" in the forest, the random turtle that keeps appearing). If you felt anything other than revulsion at the awful tween/teen sexual content, then your hard drive needs to be checked and you need to be on a watchlist. I wish I could go back in time and erase this mess from my brain.
