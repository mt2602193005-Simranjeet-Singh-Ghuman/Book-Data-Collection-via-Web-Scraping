A world existing of male dominance is where Elizabeth Roffe, the sole heir to a multimillionaire father, lives. An unloving father as hers is, dies a mysterious death while on a vacation. Devastated as she is, its up to her to run her fathers company, but what she doesn't know that her family members on the board of directors are vying for the money put on shares, which according to the her father, shouldn't be made public. On top of that, someone's trying to kill her. Is it her father's super sexy, and trusted ex-secretory, or one of her relatives, is what she trying to find out. Failed attempts to kill her makes her think about the selling the share money. Will she able to survive and save her father's company at the same time? This Sheldon novel is very interesting, as his every novel is. Dealing with people's psychology, this might connect to personally. A very good book, even though it sometimes gets confusing. Read it slow and you might find emotions hidden.
