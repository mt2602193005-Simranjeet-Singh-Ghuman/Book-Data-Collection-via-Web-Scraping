I think this is the first L'Engle book I've ever read that I didn't love. Honestly, I'm as shocked as you are. Clearly, I have a lot of time L'Engle, even when her plots get kind of wacky. But this one was a little too wacky for me. Lasers and genies, and the worst ever name of a street gang. Plus, we spent a lot of time with Dave, and I wanted more Austins, especially more Vicky, who was kind of a pill in this book. Usually L'Engle's teen protagonists have a lot more dignity and relatability, but that just didn't come through this time. I'm glad I finally read this, because I've been wanting to finish the whole Austin series for years, but this was kind of a disappointment at the end of the day.
