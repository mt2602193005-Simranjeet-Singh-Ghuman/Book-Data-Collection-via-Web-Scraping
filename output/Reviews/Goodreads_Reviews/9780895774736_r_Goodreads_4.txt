Summary: The farmer brings home a new little puppy, which all the other animals are very curious about. They poke at him and make fun of him for his size, but the the mother hen says that she can't find her chick. The puppy uses his sniffing skills to trace the chick down and bring him home safely, proving to the other animals that he's capable of doing good things. Response: I liked reading this book. It's a short story but encourages the reader to not let what other people think about you hold you back. This would be a great book to read to younger children. It teaches about strength and confidence despite difficulties. Ideas: Since this book is short and has a lower reading level, this would be a good book to use in speech therapy for a younger child.
