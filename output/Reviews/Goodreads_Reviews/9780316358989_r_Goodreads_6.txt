A group of fifth grade struggling readers ban together to steal a thesaurus and end up having their lives changed. This is a re-read of a book I remember enjoying as a kid. I've always loved reading about anything to do with reading and this fills that sweet spot. It also brought back memories of nasty school lunches - one of my least favorite was the 'Tater Tot Surprise' which was left-over tater tots from the previous day mixed with whatever the cafeteria had too much of. This book is definitely better than tater tot surprise.
