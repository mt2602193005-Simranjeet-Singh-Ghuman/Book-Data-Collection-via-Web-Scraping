This was a fun book! The author is clearly a fan of the Holy Trilogy. She is an expert in her own field, but doesn't rely on her knowledge only when proposing how the worlds, life-forms, and technology presented in Star Wars could exist. She found help from experts in electronics, planetary science, astronomy, and dozens of other disciplines to ensure that she wasn't using bad science. This book is written for people with little or no scientific knowledge, which I appreciated. She uses clear examples to "dumb down" the theory for the common man. She does obviously want it all to be possible, so some of her theories are far-fetched, but she admits as much and provides the statistical backing... 1 chance in 31 trillion is still a decent chance since space is so big. If you're a scientist you might be able to poke holes at it, but for the intended audience, it's great.
