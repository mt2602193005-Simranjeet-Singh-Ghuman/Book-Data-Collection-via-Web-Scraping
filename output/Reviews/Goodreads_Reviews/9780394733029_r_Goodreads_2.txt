Without question, the weirdest of the many weird things I've found in outdated corners of academic libraries. I was looking for legit botany texts, and was very confused why this was shelved in a section of what appeared to be Catalán poetry (nothing else was in English). I had to read about a third of the book to even understand what I was reading and whether it was meant to be fiction. It's kind of a cross between Natural History of Dragons, the concept of peculiarity from the Miss Peregrine series, and the plants from Harry Potter. Unfortunately, I'll probably never recommend this to anyone, due to the stilted early 1930s pseudoscientific writing, likely impenetrable to most modern readers. But if this all sounds intriguing and you're not put off by old-fashioned diction, you might like this. If you can find it! There's a very long, detailed review by a big fan over on amazon, if you want to learn more.
