Conceptually, this is unlike any other Marvel comic. It’s completely original. And, it’s fucking awesome. This volume compiles the first four issues of Exiles, and boy is it a doozy. We meet our team in some random, time separated, extra-dimensional desert. From there, it’s a wild ride while they spend the remaining pages defeating evil Professor X and Jean Grey, the Dark Phoenix. The entire team is interesting and fun, but the star is Blink. The same exact Blink who battled Holocaust in Age of Apocalypse. Each one of our heroes is from a different alternate universe. They’ve been assembled by an inter dimensional cosmic entity called “The Time Broker” to correct inter dimensional wrongs. Mind. Blown. Judd Winick’s writing is smart, clever and entertaining. Mike McKone’s pencilwork is inspired. This series is a must read for anyone, but it’s an extra special treat for X-Men fanatics.
