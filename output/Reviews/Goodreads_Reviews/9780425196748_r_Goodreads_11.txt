I haven't read any Dick Francis novels for years and years. There was a time that whenever I found one in a second hand store or in the library, I would immediately acquire it knowing that I was in for an exciting thriller involving a "stiff upper lip" hero and a lot of horses. And I wasn't disappointed, although this more recent book was slightly removed from the horse world in that it involved making a film in the racing environment rather than actual racing. It was a fun read with interesting characters and the requisite strong, principled main protagonist. Although I don't think it was quite as good as Francis' earlier books, I enjoyed the read and again was amazed by Dick Francis' in depth understanding of the environment in which the book is set.
