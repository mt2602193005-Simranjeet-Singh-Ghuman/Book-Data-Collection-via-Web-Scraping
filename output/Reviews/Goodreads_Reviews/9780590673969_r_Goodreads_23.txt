It’s very clear that Stacey’s parents divorcing is plot device to get her back to Stoneybrook. Their reasons were kind of weak. If her mother shops because she’s bored then she should get a job. And her dad shouldn’t work so late to be home for dinner. And the marriage counselor said to divorce after 3 months? Not a very good therapist. Besides the weak plot, Stacey’s reaction and the way she dealt with it was well done. It’s relatable for kids with divorced parents. 3 out of 5 NYC Bagels.
