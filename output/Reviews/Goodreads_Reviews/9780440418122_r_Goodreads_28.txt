Johnny, a young boy, must power through some of the hardships of war. His father on the front, his mother helping the war effort in the factories. Johnny sits idly by waiting for the war to end. This book sheds light the true horrors of war. It shows just how much of an impact war has on the world as a whole. Trust, doubt, secrets, sorrow, and anger shine through clear as day in this book. An emotionally driven tale of those effected by the great war. Iain Lawrence has masterfully used letters to communicate ideas. He has wielded emotions with real meaning.
