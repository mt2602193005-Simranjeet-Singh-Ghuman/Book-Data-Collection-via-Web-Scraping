The mediums for this book seem to be done in a mixture of watercolor and pencil. This mixture was great for two reasons. The first was that it allowed for a lot of detail to be done with the pencil which gave the illustrations a lot more life and depth. Secondly, the watercolors provided a great range of color with many varying shades to set a difference between the foreground, mid-ground, and background. This gave the story a sense of being located in its own little world and allowed the characters to seem more real and interesting to the reader. Show more
