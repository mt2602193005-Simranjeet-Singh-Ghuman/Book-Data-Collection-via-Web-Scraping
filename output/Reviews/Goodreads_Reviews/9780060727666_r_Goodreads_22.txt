These vignettes about a fictional rural town shine mostly because Philip Gulley has a gift for taking commonplace occurrences and imbuing them with deeper meaning and insight. Sometimes delightful, sometimes poignant, but always pertinent. There were moments when I was a little troubled with how unenlightened (read that as nasty ) some of the members of the church were but then isn't that just how it is in reality? Very enjoyable, with more than a few thought-provoking quotes.
