This story is about a little boy who got an old teddy bear as a present from his grandmother. Andy did not want the bear. When he went to sleep that night he dreamed about the bear as well as several other bears. In the dream he got packaged up and shipped off to the bear's house in which he originally did not want. The bear opened the box and kicked Andy across the room as it expressed that is not what it wanted. Andy started to cry and tell the bear that he wanted to go home. The bear apologized and they began to play together. Eventually Andy's dream ended and he now loved the bear.
