This book was such a disappointment. * No point to the plot. * Characters were very unpleasant. They were all small minded religious people who give a poor representation to Christianity/Faith and what it's actually supposed to be. * The main character takes no responsibility for the Church he is in charge of. Instead he's just miserable and sad and seems to hate and regret being a Pastor. * The sarcastic humor was funny at times but got redundant pretty quick. * Themes of loneliness were VERY briefly introduced but never explored deeper. Which was frustrating to me * This book wasn't plot driven OR character driven. Back to my first point, it was literally pointless. This book had potential, but it was wasted. Just very disappointing.
