Book Title: The Story of Sitting Bull Author/Illustrator: Lisa Eisenberg/ David Rickman Reading Level: 6.5 Book Level: 4-8 Book Summary: This biography of Sitting Bull outlines important times in his life and many reasons why he is such a famous Lakota Chief. The book tells of Sitting Bull as a child becoming a child to becoming a brave warrior who fought for not only his people, but all Indian people. The writing traits of this book are voice trait and word choice. This book is written as a biography, but from a Lakota's view. The words used in the book help tell the story and the seriousness of what Sitting Bull Stood for and how it helped his people.
