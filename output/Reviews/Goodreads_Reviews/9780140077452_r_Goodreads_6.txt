Murder, mayhem, and other unladylike behavior erupt at the Third Annual Conference of American Writers of Romance where author Patience McKenna finds herself caught in an intrigue that would horrify her most devoted readers. Complete with a crime-solving cat, this Edgar-nominated whodunit is a clever behind-the-scenes look at the romance business-authors, agents, editors, publishers, and fans. Heather's Notes I did not particularly care for this book. It was all over the place, and I don't believe the police would focus so quickly on her without exploring other avenues. I did like the stealing kitten though. I doubt I will read more.
