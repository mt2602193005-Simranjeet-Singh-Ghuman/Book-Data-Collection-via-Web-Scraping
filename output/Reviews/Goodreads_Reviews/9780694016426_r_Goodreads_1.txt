In this story, Harold needs a present for his mother's birthday, and he goes to sleep wondering what to do. He imagines himself travelling through the woods to a field of flowers which are at first beautiful, but then get droopy for want of sun and rain. Eventually, Harold realizes that a picture of a flower would be the best gift. This is a good early reader and is a wonderful lesson in using your imagination and talents. It teaches problem solving and thoughtfulness at the same time.
