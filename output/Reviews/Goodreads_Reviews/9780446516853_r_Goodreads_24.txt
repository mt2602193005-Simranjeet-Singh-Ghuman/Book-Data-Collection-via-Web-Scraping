One of her best! This is one of her best efforts! The suspense level will keep you addicted! This is a book you will want to read, from cover to cover, without doing anything else. It is that good! in this book, Patricia McDonald manages to totally shock you! The wonderful thing about it is, she manages to do it more than once. A couple, their adopted child, living their lives. Things all of a sudden begin to unravel, things begin to go very very wrong! One person at their door, their worlds will never be the same!
