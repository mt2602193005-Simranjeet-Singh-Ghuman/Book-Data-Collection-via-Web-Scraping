Read for the Newbery club in Children's Books group, Sept. 2021/ Imo, this deserves the honor. It's a short picture-book, illustrated by one of my favorite artists, Peter Parnall *. Though Miles is not, apparently, Native, I could not find any controversy about her or about the book. She did live with the Navaho and so when she says that the coyote is God's dog, for example, I believe her. Poignant, wise, effective. *I especially like his work with Byrd Baylor , for example The Way to Start a Day and Everybody Needs a Rock .
