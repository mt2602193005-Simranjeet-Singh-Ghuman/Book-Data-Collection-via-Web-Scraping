Wow!!! This book was awesome! I really wish I would have known about this book before I did my mammal unit. This would have been an amazing introduction book. It went into details about what qualifies an animal to be a mammal. It also covered the heaviest and smallest mammal, omnivores, herbivores and carnivores, etc. This was a great book! I highly suggest reading to kids or read it for yourself to your re-freshened about mammals :)
