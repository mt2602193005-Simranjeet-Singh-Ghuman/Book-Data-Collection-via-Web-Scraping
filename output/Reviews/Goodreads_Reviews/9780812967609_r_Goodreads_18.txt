This one was decent for what it was. I'm not typically a non-fiction fan, but my husband read it, thought it was really good and suggested I give it a read, as well. It was okay. Not terrible, not amazing, just okay. It certainly highlighted things about the different Bloc countries I didn't know anything about, but I also felt it was a little dated having been written about her time in these countries in the 90s. Much has changed in the 20+ years since she was there. I did enjoy the different perspectives of the citizens living in each of the countries and how Griest handled herself in each country being a foreigner who came with preconceived stereotypes of how she believed citizens of a certain country would be. Overall this was an interested read with a lot of factual information about Bloc countries in the 90s. Nothing earth shattering, but definitely entertaining.
