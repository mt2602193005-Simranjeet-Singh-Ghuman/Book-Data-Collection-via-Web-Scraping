The magical realism in The Canning Season did not work for me. In the 3 other Horvath books I've read, the "magic" manifested itself in unlikely but ultimately believable events, which I found refreshing and charming. But in this book, it was outlandish. For instance, I really could not suspend disbelief enough to allow that a mere birthmark could inspire fear and loathing. I also failed to appreciate several instances of profanity from a middle-grade novel.
