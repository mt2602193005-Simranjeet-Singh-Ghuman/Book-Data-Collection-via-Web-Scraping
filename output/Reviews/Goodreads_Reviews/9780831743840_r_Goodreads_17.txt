"Sometimes life can be as bitter as dragon tears. But whether dragon tears are bitter or sweet depends entirely on how each man perceives the taste." - Chinese quote, Dragon Tears, Dean Koontz . A cop faces the wrath of a man with a God-complex... cops vs supernaturally being! I've struggled with a lot of Koontz work, but this is deffo one of his better ones, even though it's crammed with critique of the Millenium doomsday sayers (it was written in the early 1990s). 7 out of 12.
