This memoir is a treasure and a delight to read. What an amazing life William Murray had! He grew up in Rome New York and Fire Island in the homes of his Mother Natalia Danesi Murray, (a publishing executive and later, broadcaster) his live-in Maternal Grandmother, "Mammina Ester" (herself a formidable writer and founder of La Donna, an Italian magazine of "Women, Arts, and Culture.") and his mother's lovers. Among the most amazing of these women was Janet Flanner, the writer who for many years wrote The New Yorker Magazine's "Letter From Paris" column. Flanner was instrumental in shaping the style of The New Yorker. This is a loving, joyous book. Murray himself followed in their footsteps by becoming a novelist, and later a writer and editor for The New Yorker.
