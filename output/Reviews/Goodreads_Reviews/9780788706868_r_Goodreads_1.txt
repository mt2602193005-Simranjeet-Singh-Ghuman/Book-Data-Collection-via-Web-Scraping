This is a pleasant easy read of only 217 pages. It was written for a teen audience (the heroine, Puck, is 13) but an adult reader of SF could enjoy it. The relationship between the alien "Hush" and Puck is a high point of this book. Lots of other very interesting characters. I would recommend this to any reader over age 10. I do want to make a point. I am 67 so I review in a way for ME. I often pick YA novels when available to see what the kids are reading. So why only three stars? Well, as an adult reader I could see where a lot of things were going . Trust me, if I had read this at the age 0f 12---when I started SF---the rating would definitely be 4 stars. Out of print now, but worth finding and giving to your teen----and read it yourself.
