Sister Joan is on retreat to the remote rock cave in Scotland. It is located up a steep set of steps and has no electricity or water-a very rustic space. While on retreat, Sister Joan gets involved with the locals and the monks who live on the island monastery off Lake Morag. A husband who apparently ran off leaving his wife and son, the mysterious young woman, Morag. A young monk, Brother Cuthbert, gives Sister Joan rides to and from the monastery where Sunday Catholic Mass is held and where Sister Joan goes to paint pictures of the church as a gift to give the monks. When caught in a storm while she is rowing across the lake, she gets caught in a wild tide that exposes a body in the lake. It all adds up to a very twisted tale of local secrets and hidden crimes.
