Okay, another memoir, and what is my comment? The writer was a little self absorbed. Maybe that's a requirement to write one. She actually wrote about how important being popular in school was to her. Gag. But, the book was more about the relationship Margaret had with her father J.D. Salinger. I did find her memory of living with him in a secluded environment in New Hampshire very interesting. His ideas of how life is to be lived and how much everyone is a "phony". His relationship with her mother also was I would say mentally abusive. Also, many of the comments she attributed to him have explained for me much of what bothered me about "Catcher in the Rye" (not that I like it any better, but I understand it better.)
