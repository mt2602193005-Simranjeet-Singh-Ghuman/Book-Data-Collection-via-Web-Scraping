Finally, sweet release. My favorite parts of this book are the ones I mercifully cannot remember. There wasn’t a single character in this book who I felt morally clear to root for. And there wasn’t any detectable redemption for really anyone. The plot structure/direction itself was fine, I suppose, but the people were just deplorable or forgettable. And I appreciate that this was written 20 years ago, but so much of the language around culture, relationships, and neurodivergence made my skin crawl. You won’t miss anything skipping this one.
