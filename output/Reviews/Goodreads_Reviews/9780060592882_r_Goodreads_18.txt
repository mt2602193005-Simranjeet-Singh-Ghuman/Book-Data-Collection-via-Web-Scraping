These are nineteen very tense, dark, thrilling, exciting and dangerous short stories. JCO never fails me; her books are so intense and so very well written. Each and every one of these were wonderful but a few of them stood out for me. MRS. HALIFAX & RICKIE SWANN; A BALLAD..A teacher takes a troubled student under her wing with astonishing results. JORIE (&JAMIE)..Fraternal twin sisters are so very different. Jorie has intense issues that her family just can't handle. THREE GIRLS .. On a cold and snowy night two young girls discover a surprise customer in a used book store. There are plenty of surprises, twists, turns, and shocks in every one of these stories. They are shocking, bold, and dark in such a good way. Highly recommend.
