Text-to-Self Connection As a young child I remember my parents reading There's a Nightmare in My Closet to me one night before moving into my own bedroom. I was so afraid that I would have something in my new closet. My mom and dad reassured me there was nothing in my closet. I kept asking them if the nightmare was going to wear my clothes and play with my toys when I was asleep. This is a classic book that I would share with the kids in my own classroom.
