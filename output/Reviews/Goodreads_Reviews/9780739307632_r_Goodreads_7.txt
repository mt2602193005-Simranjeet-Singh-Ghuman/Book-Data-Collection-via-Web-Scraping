5/19/2019. I read this book several years ago - before I joined Goodreads - so dates etc. are a vague memory... This was the second Hickam I read after "The Rocket Boys" (later renamed to "October Sky"). I was hesitant about a real-live rocket scientist writing fiction but Hickam pulled it off! Since then, I've always looked forward to an opportunity to read his work and I've never been disappointed. I don't recall many details about this specific title but when I saw it come up in a social media feed I immediately thought "That was a great book!" I remember it being muted, with subtle character development and culminating in a very satisfying end. Pretty good for a book I read over 10 years ago!
