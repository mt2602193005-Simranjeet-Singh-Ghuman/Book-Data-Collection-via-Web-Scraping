Miss spiders ABC is a story written by David Kirk. For each letter of the alphabelt she has a different insect. Which is a lot of insects that I didn’t even know about. This book would be not good but okay for helping children out with the alphabet because, a lot of the insect names are kind of over the top and wouldn’t really work with someone just learning the alphabet, but it also wouldn’t work with someone who only knows the basics of reading. If you want kids to get a better understanding of insects, then this would be a great book to read. The illustrations for this book were colorful and very fun to look at, I mean if you are going to have a birthday party there should be quite a few colors. Also if you wanted to use this in teaching you could have a lesson about insects and going over the differences or similarities that insects have.
