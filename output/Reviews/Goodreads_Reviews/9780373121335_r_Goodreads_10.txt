Re-issue of an older Harlequin This was a good book of a trope I like (Stepbrother romance) written by an author I have read many times. Keeping in mind the time this was written is not how things are now, you can appreciate the story. In many ways it was timeless but not all. Not only a Stepbrother romance but also a second chance romance as well. As we follow the main characters journey through the drama, angst, treachery, we still get the romance and surprising some steam as they navigate to their HEA.
