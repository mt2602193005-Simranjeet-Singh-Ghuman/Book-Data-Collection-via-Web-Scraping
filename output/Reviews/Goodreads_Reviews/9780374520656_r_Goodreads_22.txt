the geologist of the hour here is by far the most interesting of the three we've followed so far, but i found this the least compelling of the three books so far. i couldn't really tell you why. David Love's story was one worth putting to paper, if nothing else. a big thread here by the end was David Love's necessity to find the truth of geological matters, even when that truth might lead to the plunder of the landscape he has lived in and loved. the sections on the rise of oil, coal, and uranium (discovered by Love himself) and the environmental effects that the gathering of those resources cause is something that you see Love grapple with -- i just wish that thread were pulled even more.
