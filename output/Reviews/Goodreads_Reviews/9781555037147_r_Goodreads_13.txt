First Love and Forever by Anita Stansfield is about Emily who gives up marriage to Michael for religious reasons. Years later they come across each other. The book relates their long conversation as they catch up on their lives since they last saw each other. When he finds out her current marriage is unhappy, Michael believes the pain of parting was a waste . He has never gotten over her and wants Emily to leave her husband. Emily has a conflict wanting to be true to her vows, but still loving Michael and knowing he is a better man. I really enjoyed First Love and Forever because the characters are brought to life so vividly, Emily Ladd and Michael Hamilton seem like real people to me. I enjoyed Michael a lot more due to the face that he is Australian. I try to imagine his accent. Emily's greatest quality is her unusually strong faith in her church. This was a lot better than I had anticipated. Sometime I think we have a chance to see what our life would have been if we had taken a different road. This book helps me question where I am & where I could have been if I had taken a different choice earlier in my life. What I liked most about this book is that it taught me that even if I didn't take the right path, I can still recover.
