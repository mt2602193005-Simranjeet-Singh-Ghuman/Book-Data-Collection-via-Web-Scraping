The book started out with a bang, with the author setting up an absolute pig of a hero with a strong, beautiful, woman who seemed prepared to make him eat his words. Unfortunately, the story almost immediately morphs into insta-love, with the hero going into stalker-mode, and the heroine doing the traitorous body bit. There is some drivel about a corporate take-over, mean stepkids, a psycho ex, and a cute ragamuffin kid put there strictly for window dressing. What left me nauseated were all the dessert metaphors. Hero compares heroine first to chocolate eclair (shiny on the outside, but fluff on the inside), then to ice cream (sweet to look at but icy to touch), then finally, get this, a reverse baked alaska (icy on the outside, but sweet meringue filling on the inside, once you can get past the ice fortress).
