I really like this book, primarily because it really makes you think about how difficult it must be to enter a place where you know nobody and nothing. This story is about a small young man that traveled on an airplane to be with his new family; his adopted family. I would use this text to show the students the hard transition that adopted children have to make, including meeting new people, adopting new beliefs, learning a brand new culture, amongst many other things. The students coudl interact with the text in many ways becuase for some it may be very new and soem may know about adoption, but they're still unsure about the process. The illustrator did a great job with the painted pictures and this would definitely keep he attntion of the children. This too was a great choice for our text set because it focuses on adoption, which was our smaller topic.
