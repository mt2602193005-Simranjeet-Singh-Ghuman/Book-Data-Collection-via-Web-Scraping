I have read and enjoyed several of Mr. Gardner's books, but this one was...different. A visiting professor meets a local professor at a cocktail party, who invites him to his home to meet his "monster" son, the titular Freddy. This happens, and the painfully shy son visits the visitor's bedroom later to leave a manuscript of the book he has written. End of framing story. The remaining 2/3 of the book is this manuscript, telling the story of a 16th C. Swedish king and his closest advisor. The book, which I'm sure reaches high literary standards, reads more like a writing assignment than a novel. I got no sense of atmosphere from it, or that any character or indeed the author himself was emotionally involved in the story. The best word I could use to describe it is bland. Maybe it's me.
