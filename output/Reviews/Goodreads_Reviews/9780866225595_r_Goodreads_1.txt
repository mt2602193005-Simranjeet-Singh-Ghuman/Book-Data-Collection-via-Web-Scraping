This is an informative book designed for children ages seven and up that explains how to choose and care for a pet turtle. The narrative is short enough to keep a child's attention, with enough information to give the basics on caring for this reptile. The book is filled with big, colorful pictures and we enjoyed the quick read. Before we had children, my husband and I caught and kept several turtles (mainly red-eared sliders), and our family all loves turtles of any kind. We often find turtles near our home and our girls usually ask to keep them as pets. For the most part, it's only for a short period of time (anywhere from a few hours to a few days), but they recently found a baby snapping turtle and even my husband wanted to keep him. So we decided to do some research and learn more about caring for him. I borrowed several books from our local library about turtles and we figured we'd peruse them and see if we could pick up any tips about caring for a snapping turtle. This one really only had basic information, but the topic certainly interests us and we'll continue to research it.
