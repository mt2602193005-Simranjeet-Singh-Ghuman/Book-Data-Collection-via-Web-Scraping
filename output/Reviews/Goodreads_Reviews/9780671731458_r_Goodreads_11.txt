I picked up this book for 10p in a charity shop, it had the cards and so I did the test. I found it to be very inaccurate only one or two of the interpretations had any similarity to my actual personality. I will probably do the test with some other family members to see if it fairs any better with them and perhaps try myself again at a later date. Other than that it is an interesting artifact of its time and there are some interesting ideas about colours and their significance and how they may affect us. Its quite expensive to buy a book with the cards online so I am glad I only paid 10p for this copy!
