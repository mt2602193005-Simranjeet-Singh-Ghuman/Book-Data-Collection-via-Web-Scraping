Lettie and Luke go through A LOT and I mean that in every sense of the word. Lol The story is well written and follows them through a span of many many years. This is a story that you want to read when you've got a good block of time to dedicate to reading it. The characters are imperfect and unique. They are interesting and complex. The story could have easily been split into 3 different books. ARC received in exchange for an honest review.
