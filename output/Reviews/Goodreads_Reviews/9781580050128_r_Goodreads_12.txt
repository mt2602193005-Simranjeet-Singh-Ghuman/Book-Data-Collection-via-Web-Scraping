FIVE STARS: I look forward to when it's time to read this one again. I WILL ADMIT, I got a little thrown by the choice of narrator and how her perspective shaped my perception -- those last hundred pages, man, I was bobbing and weaving, I was thrown wondering how it would end and that never happens to me. But this? This was charming in ever way. Gosh . I laughed, I adored, I even teared up in spots. What a gorgeous book.
