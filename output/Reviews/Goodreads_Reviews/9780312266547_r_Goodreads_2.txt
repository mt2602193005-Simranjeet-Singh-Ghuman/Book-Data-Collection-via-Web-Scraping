I didn’t realize this was the end of a trilogy and read it as a stand-alone. I enjoyed the story and found myself invested, though I was confused about who was who until about halfway through. I found myself wanting to pick it up often all the way to the end. Loved the world building. I was confused why Gavin didn’t have a bullet wound when they found his body, and there’s something creepy about Fay and Quig ending up together since they’re so far apart in age, but that’s looking at it from a modern lens so I have grace for it.
