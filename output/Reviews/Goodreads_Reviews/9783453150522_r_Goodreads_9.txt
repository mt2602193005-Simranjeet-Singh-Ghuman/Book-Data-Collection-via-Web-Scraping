A study of the 1996 disaster at Mt. Everest, when climbers were stranded and several died. In 1996, it seemed like almost anyone could climb Everest, as the guides had the whole thing down to a science. It was becoming little more than just another adventure tour. Then Mother Nature took a hand. There was too much ambition and hubris. They forgot that Everest is a very dangerous place. They paid the price. A very good examination of the whys and wherefores.
