Here's the story of how I bought this book. Picking my wife up from the RenCen and there was Ernie- autographing copies of this book. Since I didn't have a credit card, I ran back to the car, pulled all of the change out of the ashtray and added to the five bucks I had in my wallet bought his book. He autographed it to me, my wife and son. Being that it wasn't too crowded I had the opportunity to talkk with Ernie for about 20 minutes until my wife got off work.
