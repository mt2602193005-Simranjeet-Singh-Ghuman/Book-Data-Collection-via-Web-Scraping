An enjoyable story about Rialla, a former slave going undercover as her former self and Tris, a mysterious healer who comes to her aid on her mission. Vivid images and engaging story, but although there is a romantic undercurrent throughout and the relationship between the heroes evolves and they obviously fall in love, they never exchange more than a kiss. Apart from that and the obvious "twist" concerning the bad guys, I didn't have any major issues with the story, which sucked me in and kept me listening until it was over, which is why the lack of romantic development did not bother me as much as it normally would.
