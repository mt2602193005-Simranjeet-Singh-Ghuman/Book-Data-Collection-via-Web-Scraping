I enjoyed this book. Wonder is the younger sister of pop star Lucky, who was killed in a car accident before the story opens. When Lucky's former manager taps Wonder as the next pop princess, Wonder learns more about the industry and her sister, seeing beyond the persona created to the woman Lucky might have become. If you're a fan of pop culture, you'll enjoy this book. It's also the perfect read for girls who idolize the pop industry and its princesses.
