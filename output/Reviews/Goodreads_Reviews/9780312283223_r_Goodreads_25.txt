I read the 2014 revised and updated edition. I really wanted to like this more. The strength of this book was as a wide-ranging overview of the history of and politics surrounding Black hair. This was what's most helpful to me as an outsider learning about things that i didn't know that i didn't know. There are some illustrations but given the topic I think that more illustrations, especially of the historical hairstyles they mentioned, would have gone a long way. The book is weakest in its sourcing and citations, several times I wanted to find out more about a claim or its support but wasn't able to. And the book really drags at the end with the two update chapters. They have a lot of superficial analysis based on anecdotal evidence that has already not aged well from at-the-time influential bloggers and entrepreneurs.
