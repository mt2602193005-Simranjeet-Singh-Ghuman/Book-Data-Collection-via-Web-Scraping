A great introductory book for anyone who wants to enter the world of chess and not only that but a great book that reminds us to keep in mind the basic principles while playing chess at any level. Although I wasn't new to chess when I read this book, it still was a lot fun and helpful reading Yasser's guide to play winning chess. This book will surely help beginners but also to players at higher level in many ways. The Annotated games were fun to follow on the board :) Loved my first Chess Book read!
