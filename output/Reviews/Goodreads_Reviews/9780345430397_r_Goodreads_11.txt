I wouldn't call this the brightest point in the DemonWars Saga. A lot of this book feels like it only exists to bridge the gap between the previous trilogy and what is to come next. Unlike some others I actually enjoyed the political intrigues both at court and within the church. Maybe Salvatore is onto something here by showing how the world is adjusting after the culmination of the events in books 1-3, showing that just because the threat was ended doesn't mean everything is... Rosey ;) Who is this for? Well being the fourth book in a saga, it's for people who are already invested in the world of Corona as I cannot see this being a good entry point into the series
