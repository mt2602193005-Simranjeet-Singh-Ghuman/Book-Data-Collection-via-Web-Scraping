I liked it, and I can see a lot of Bukowski in it. Fante's voices are a little whinier at times, but I like the relate-able flaws in the characters. Going through this reminded me of going through Buk's novels and seeing the author age (as you are, in fact, doing with Fante). The letters at the end are really their own mini-timeline, and show a bit of the man changing, but also how an unknown got into the world of authorship. By coincidence, a day after reading this I watched the ok-but-not-great Bukowski: Born Into This documentary and learned more about Buk in that 2hr film than about Fante in the collection. If you like Buk, come see where he got it from. If you liked Winesburg, Ohio you will probably find something here, too. Everyman street-level semi-fiction. Great as a primer and how-to, acceptable but not outstanding as a work on its own (this person's opinion only).
