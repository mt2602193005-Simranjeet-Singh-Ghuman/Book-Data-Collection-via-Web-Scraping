This has been the best one yet. Always good to see Legion, and this Ultimate version did not disappoint. The best character work yet. Colossus doubting his place, and rightfully so. Beast doubting emotions, losing faith in a mentor able to mentally manipulate people. Iceman being a kid in over his head. Xavier becoming multiple shades of gray. And Wolverine, Marvel Girl, and Cyclops being their usual boring selves. Good stuff all around.
