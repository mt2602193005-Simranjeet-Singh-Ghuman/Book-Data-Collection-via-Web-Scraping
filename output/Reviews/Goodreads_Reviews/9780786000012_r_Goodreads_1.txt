Deceived by Carla Simpson This book was okay but a bit slow for me. The description of the book I am listing below is from the back jacket of the book. I'm adding it because I believe the one on the Goodreads page does not depict this story. Daring They call her China Lili—a dazzling star of the San Francisco stage whose exotic face hides a bitter past. But when a famous actress is found dead, it is exquisite Lili, flower of the Orient, who is suspected of murder… Dangerous Gray Harper has built a vast shipping empire in the waters off the California Coast. Feared by many, known by few, he conceals his secret scars behind a black silk mask—living dangerously in the night shadows of San Francisco… Deceived From the magnificent mansions of Nob Hill to the Barbary Coast and the opium dens of Chinatown, Lili and Gray are reluctant partners in a deadly game...wary lovers bound by an irresistible passion that could destroy them—or set them gloriously free…
