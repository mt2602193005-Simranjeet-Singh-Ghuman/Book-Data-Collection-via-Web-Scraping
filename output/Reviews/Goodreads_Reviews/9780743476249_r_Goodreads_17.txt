This novel sits at 3.5 stars After finishing Vengeance by Zane I was compelled to pick up Nervous. This book reads more like a mystery novel rather than erotica. It plays with the mind for sure. The story kind of reminded me of the movie Cat Woman with Hallie Barry. The main character Jon, she's a good girl by day and a bad girl by night. The writing was sexy and smooth. The only thing I had fault with was the fluffy sex scenes. They didn't propel the plot forward which I found myself zoning in and out. It's one of the reasons why I shy away from erotic novels. The romance between Jon and Manson was good. I felt Mason was an undercover freak. It seemed as if he got aroused hearing about Jon's crazy nights. The ending took me by surprise. Didn't see it coming. However it lacked foreshadowing. I felt the author threw people underneath the bus for shock value. The story is set in Atlanta and narrated by a young black woman by the name of Jon. She seeks psychological help when experiencing these random episodes of blackouts. Later on, she is encouraged to dig into her past and she discovers some shocking family secrets and much more. If you read Zane's novel Vengeance and liked it, you will like this one.
