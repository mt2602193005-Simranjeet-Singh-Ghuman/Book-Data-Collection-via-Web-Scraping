This is a beautiful story about tough subject matter. It covers a lot of subject matter about sexuality, abuse, mental health, and ultimately love. The story starts out with an elderly woman being brought to a care home and everyone is afraid of her. Through her flashbacks to the past we find out more about her life, and the events that brought her to where she is. It takes place in a fictional country in the Caribbean, but really the story could have taken place anywhere. This book is on the CBC list of the 100 Novels that make you proud to be Canadian, and that is why I sought it out.
