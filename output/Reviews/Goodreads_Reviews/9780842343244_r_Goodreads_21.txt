Tribulation Force is the second book in the Left Behind Series. The novel picks up a few days later from where Left Behind left off. The book was written very well and does a really good job portraying how events are occurring throughout the novel. The ending becomes very intense which prohibits you from being able to put the bookdown. Over all the book was extremely intense and is a excellent recommended read. Pros Character development Plot development Intense Clift hangers Cons A bit slow at times
