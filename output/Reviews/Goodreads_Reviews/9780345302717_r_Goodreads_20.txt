McGuane writes with a western tongue, a lyrical voice with a plethora of good words. The story only mustered so much luster. A little too much tragic wallowing... reminded me of Catcher in the Rye if the Rye was in Montana. I had to tattoo my bookmark while reading this. Some great words: Neurasthenic Rakish Piquant Paroxyism Hermenic Dotage Spasmodic Patina
