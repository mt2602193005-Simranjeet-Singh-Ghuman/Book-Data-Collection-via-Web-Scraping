My mother gave me a copy sometime after I left home. I think it was during grad school when I used it as stress relief to bake up a storm. Still have it and make stuff out of it from time to time. I always seem to double the herbs and spices - the amounts given make stuff a bit to bland for my taste. My sister also has a copy. And recently my 8 year old niece made a scrumptious peach pie from this. And so the book passes on to another generation. An Side - love the ring-bound binding and wonder why more cookbooks don't come like this.
