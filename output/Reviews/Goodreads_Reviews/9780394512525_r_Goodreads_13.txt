While impossible to find these days, Lionel Davidson provides an interesting novel with the plot revolving around two boys having fun. Barry was lost for 3 days in a cave near his parent's summer home and can only just remember the details through dreams. As he writes them down the plot unfolds. The fascinating thing about Under Plum Lake is that the plot arc is just "two boys have fun, have more fun, have more fun, have more fun," etc. This would seem to make for a horrible book, but because Davidson is creating an entire world in which to have fun, the reader is transported in and feels exactly like Barry does. An immensely fun, quick read if you can find it.
