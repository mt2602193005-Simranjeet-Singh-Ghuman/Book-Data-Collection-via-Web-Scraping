4.52 Excuse me, have you read this book? Have you heard of this author? I need to read this again, like five more times. Extremely difficult subject matter (incest, slavery, rape), and unlike anything else I've read. I'm tempted to say "not for the faint of heart," but I think the faint of heart should read it. Challenges my need for commas. One of the best medical chapters I've ever encountered ("The King of Hearts"), and I do like gross medical stuff in books in small doses. The more I think about it, the more I realize how incredibly smart this book is. The allusions, the language, the images, the implications. Each human body has its own system. I think myself into bodies until I understand them. I know the ways in which an individual human body intersects with all the ways of all human bodies. I know when not to fix something. I know when something that is wrong is supposed to be wrong. I cut deeply and neatly without waste. I sew bodies back up again. I can do it. I know how to read the signs. You don't know how to read the signs. You think the signs are in heaven. The signs are here from in front of you and you hate them. You hate snot and slime. Snot and slime are signs. You hate spit and pus. Spit and pus are signs. You hate putrid sores and stink. You don't understand. You don't understand anything. You hate life. You hate human life. I love human life. Soon to be re-released by McNally Editions.
