This is a book of unbelievable life. It has so many different levels in it that the reader is pulled into another world. The emotion, the pathos, the love, the hurt. Anger, bitterness, sweetness, passion. WOW. This author has always had a way of writing that pulls my heart and mind. In this particular book you will laugh and you will cry. You may hurt for all and become angry for all, however the one thing that will not happen is for you to feel or think nothing. You will enjoy this book. It is a marvelous work done by a queen of a writer! I was given this book in return for an honest review. Swedenmom
