I loved this series growing up so I wanted to revisit it with my kiddo. I'm hoping this book isn't indicative of the series (I don't remember this particular one) as it's a pretty big disappointment to me. My problems are: - Spot says "I can't sing" which is fine except it's not addressed? Which is a weird line and bad takeaway to have in this dialog sparse book as the character hides from his perceived inadequacy. - Dress-up clothes are a stereotypical Native American feather headband and a British Honor Guard hat? Obviously one is more problematic than the other but they're both really odd choices imo, even for an old book. - Wrestling during story time and absolute paint mess chaos are the icing on the cake of "no thanks." Hoping I find other Spot books are better, as I still love the dog and the flaps and have a ton of nostalgia for the series.
