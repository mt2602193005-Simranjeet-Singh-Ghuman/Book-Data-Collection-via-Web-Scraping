Absolutely Riveting First off, I LOVE the first two books in this series and I'm immediately going to start the fourth. This book three is every bit as wonderful as the others. The intriguing mystery, the cast of characters, the well described locations, all spun out in great writing is a joy for this old English major. I can't possibly find anything not to like in these Veronica Black creations. You don't have to be Catholic to enjoy them thoroughly.....I'm not. The spiritual struggles Sister Joan has are of the universal variety I believe because I've dealt with a few myself and her comments in this area are priceless. Universal mysteries as well as some very practical down to Earth entanglements are run of the mill for inquisitive, needs to know answers, of the mind of Sister Joan. I encourage you to try and then enjoy one of these books. I bet you'll read the series!
