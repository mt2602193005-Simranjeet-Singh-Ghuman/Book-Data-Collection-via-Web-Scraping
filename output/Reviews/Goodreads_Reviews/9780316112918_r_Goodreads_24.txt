Arthur’s teacher announces a contest that the President is holding to his class. Students are to write letters explaining how to make America great and the winner will get travel to Washington DC with their class to meet the president. Arthur wins the contest but is worried the entire week about forgetting his speech. When he finally gets to meet the president, D.W. helps lighten the mood and remind Arthur of his speech. This book wasn't one of my favorite. There wasn't enough details given for readers. I do not see myself using this book in my classroom to aid me in a teaching a lesson, but it still may be fun for students to read.
