My last book for 2021. It took me to finish Chapter 1 to recognize who our hero really is. He is the adorable, old soul kid in Sarah's Child who is too good to be true, now a grown-up and a successful doctor. I have mixed feelings whether it is justifiable that this installment is a novella or if the MCs deserve such a short love story (which also reminds me of a good 'ol classic novel from one of my favorite authors), but either way, it is refreshing to get to visit this series again and I think I should remind myself to read the sequel as soon as I could get. But yeah, final answer. This was too short for a love story.
