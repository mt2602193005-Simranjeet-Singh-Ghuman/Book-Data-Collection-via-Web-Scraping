This book is by far the most wonderful and heartwarming/breaking tale I have ever read. I laughed, cried, sobbed, laughed...it's amazing! It's about a family and a Beagle named Bea. Bea escaped (how we'll never know) from a research laboratory and ended up in the arms of the author. Initially the dog was terrified, and stiffened to endure touches, pets, and love from the guardians. After much patience and love we see this terrified, lonely, and sad Beagle come out of her shell and become the best friend anyone could ask for! Read it!!
