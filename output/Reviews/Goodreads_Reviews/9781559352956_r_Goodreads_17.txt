I didn't read this novel until a few years after I finished the first 4 of the series. It was odd returning to the girls after they more or less managed to work through their problems. Especially when it turned out that their problems had only just begun. In this novel they all proceed to run away from the Home they get placed in, mainly because their guardians are getting a little touchy-feely. But things on the road are not how the girls expected. And who but a group of naive girls would expect paradise?
