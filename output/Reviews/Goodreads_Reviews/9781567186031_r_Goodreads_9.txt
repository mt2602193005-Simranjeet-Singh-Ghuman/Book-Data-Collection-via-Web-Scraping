Not what I was hoping for. I dove into this book with ulterior motives. I have a Celtic trio and I'm the self-appointed tune finder, so I'm always searching archives and other odd places for old tunes for our group. This seemed like a find when I ordered it. While it started with a few tales from the Mabinogian with accompanying songs, the songs were all composed by the author, a contemporary wiccan elder from Minnesota. Soon it went from Welsh tales to "Cosmic Cowboy" and a couple of hymns for wiccan worship.
