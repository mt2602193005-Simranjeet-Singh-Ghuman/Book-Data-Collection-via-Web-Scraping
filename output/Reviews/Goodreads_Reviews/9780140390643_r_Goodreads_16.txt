One of Longfellow's more famous poems, Angeline is about the deportation of Acadians from Nova Scotia because they refused to swear allegiance to the British crown. What's interesting is that as a New Englander Longfellow chose to put his nationalism above religious considerations and wrote a very sympathetically about the plight of the Catholics who suffered under the evil Brits. And it's incredible that his Paul Revere's Ride is not better known. Whatever its poetic merits, the subject should interest even high school kids.
