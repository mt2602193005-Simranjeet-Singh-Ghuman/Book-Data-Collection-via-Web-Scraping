2.5 stars This story had a bit more excitement then the first one, but also had some good parts about working together to accomplish things. In this story Josefina’s sister, Francisca, must face the fact that life and changes happen but that isn’t a bad thing. One thing that bugged me is that Ana never seems to actually be a mother or have a husband. Her little boys are mentioned, but not her husband. I like the “A Peek into the Past” at the back of each book with photographs, and drawings about real life back then. This was a fun, light read, but I probably won’t be reading it again unless it’s in another dozen years or so for fun.
