My Review: Each year I try to find some books, especially during the holiday season, to prompt Munchkin and myself to think of others. The Berenstain Bears are always a reliable source for kindness. During the crazy holiday season, when the house really seems to be over flowing with stuff, this was a great book to read. It was nice to see that it wasn't about getting back money for the items the Bears were done with but about giving it to those in need or those who could really use the items.
