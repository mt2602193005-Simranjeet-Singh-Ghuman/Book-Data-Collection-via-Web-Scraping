I enjoyed this Nantucket-placed mystery very much. The setting has a great deal to do with the story, which begins with the captain of a fishing boat -- and father of her High School friend -- dying right at the beginning of the book. However, this is not the only death our Detective Merry Folger has to solve, and in the course of her investigation, her relationship with rich landowner Peter Mason heats up and confuses her right when she needs to think clearly. In addition to these investigations, Merry must contend with a variety of co-workers and their temperaments, a grandfather who has been pressed into babysitting services, and several possible suspects. How to sort it all out???? Foul language peppered throughout HIGHLY RECOMMEND
