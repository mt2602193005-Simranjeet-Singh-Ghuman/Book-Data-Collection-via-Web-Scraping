In my opinion this book is a HP masterpiece. The H’s don’t come better than this possessive, dominant, besotted, passionate hero. He can’t be without her. So romantic. What would have made it 5 stars, is if she was still a virgin when she married the H. She slept with another man while the H was (at first) trying to friendly court her. The H and h weren’t in a relationship at that time, but still… She lost her virginity to another man in that time when she already knew the H. It was love at first sight for him, but not for her. She was a rich privileged girl, partying and fooling around with other men while he had serious intentions with her and was mad about her.
