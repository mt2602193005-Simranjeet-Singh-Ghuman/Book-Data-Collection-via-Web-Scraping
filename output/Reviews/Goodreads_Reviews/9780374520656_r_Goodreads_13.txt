McPhee at his best. The author mixes geology, history and humans into a tightly woven story. Wyoming could not be more alive than in this story about the early 20th century settlers. John Love settled at the epicenter of Wyoming and has two sons and a daughter. His son, David Love, took up geology of Wyoming in a large way and his observations and tact are the center of McPhee's writing. A well-spun yarn.
