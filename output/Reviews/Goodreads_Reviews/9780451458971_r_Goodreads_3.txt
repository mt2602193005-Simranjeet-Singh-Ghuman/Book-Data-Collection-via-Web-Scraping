There are three (or more) possible reactions on a reread: your original thoughts are confirmed, you feel the book wasn't as good as you originally thought, or the book's esteem grows in your mind. For me, this third read of A Song for Arbonne is firmly in the latter category. I recently reread this via audiobook, but this read was a traditional read, and I loved absolutely everything about the experience. Seeing the plot threads develop, the characters' lives changed, bits of foreshadowing not noticed before, all with a beautiful and lyrical prose style. This book screams quality and is one of my favorite books I've ever read. ====================================== This was a reread via audiobook of one of my favorite books I've ever read. The book remains in my high esteems, and the audiobook is fantastic! Euan Morton's performance was remarkable at times, and he even sang all of the songs in this book. I enjoyed this so much I plan on another traditional reread very soon. My original review is below. ================== This is both one of the best books I've read this year, as well as one of the best fantasy stand alones I've read in my lifetime. The two things that stand out universally in the three books by Kay that I've read so far are his incredibly rich world-building and stupendous writing style. But everything else is fantastic as well - characters, themes, plotting, pacing - these can all be described as strengths as well. Kay writes books that make me remember how much I love to read. I realize I've said nothing concrete about this book in this review, so just let me say: go read it.
