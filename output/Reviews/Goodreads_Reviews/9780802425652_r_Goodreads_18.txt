So far, it's amazing! Three true stories of couples who made their way into debt and how, with the help of God and financial counseling, made their way out. I am learning a lot and it's a great companion to Dave Ramsey's Financial Peace University! **edit** Book finished, and I am completely satisfied. The stories were all very inspiring - and some more than a little heart-wrenching as I either A) related with what the couples were going through or B) realized how much better off I am in my own financial struggles (which is a great motivator, knowing if they could do it, so can I and with NO EXCUSES!) I definitely recommend this book to anyone on the tough but rewarding road to financial peace and (book plug here:) debt-free living!
