The problem: How do you make an aloof, sourpuss of a politician appealing to a large audience? The solution: Use all of the tricks of television to package and sell him to the American electorate. The aftermath: Change the model of presidential campaigns for generations to come. To the contemporary audience, there is nothing new or shocking in the book. However it is worthwhile to help understand how we got to where we are today as it relates to the theatrics of America's politics. Before Twitter, there was TV.
