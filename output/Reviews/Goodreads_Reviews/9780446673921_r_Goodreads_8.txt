This is an example of finding the right book at the exact right time. I had been away from yoga and got back into it in March. I bought this little book at a library book sale for 25 cents and it has been a great book for carrying around in my purse. I enjoyed learning how the author got interested in yoga (it was a bumpy road). I highly recommend this book to anyone who may be thinking about trying this form of exercise or someone who just enjoys reading more and more about yoga.
