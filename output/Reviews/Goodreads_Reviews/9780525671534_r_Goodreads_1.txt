This was one of my childhood books that I decided to read again last night. I wish my daughter took more of an interest in reading, because she is exactly the age of the girls in this book, although they seem older. Darci is a typical sixth grade girl, with a crush on a boy and resentful of a new girl who seems bossy. She and her friends are giggly and a little shy around boys they like. Darci writes about everything in her diary until it disappears one day. She's worried that a neighborhood boy took it and will show everyone. The story unfolds nicely, is well written and engaging. Darci learns that everything isn't what it seems. It's a good lesson without being preachy and everything ends well.
