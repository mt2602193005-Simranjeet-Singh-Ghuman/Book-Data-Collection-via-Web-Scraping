All Ruby does is tremble (get horny) and not stand up for herself and yet I was entertained So it’s not that it’s necessarily like a well-written story and some parts are super cringy and/or problematic as hell. BUT this is a fun, drama-full read. It’s like one of those melodramas where there’s a scandal every second and a lot of things seem unprobable, but you keep watching because you’re invested. I mean I can’t say I was bored and I love the New Orleans setting. I think this is definitely worth reading if you’re looking for something a little less serious, with plenty of villains to hate, and a heroine who progresses from a doormat to slightly less of a doormat (there’s more books in this series though, so I’m not bashing her I think she has time to grow ~ and yes I will be reading)
