I love Nora Roberts. She is one of my go -to authors for romance and chick-lit. I have been discovering her earlier work, as until recently I was just familiar with her trilogies. Second Nature revolves around a reclusive horror author and the reporter determined to get him to talk. They are both serious contenders in the game of cat and mouse. Eventually, romance becomes the new name of the game. Neither of the protagonists are particularly likable, to me at least. But Roberts is skilled at her craft, and the pages just kept turning. She requires you to keep reading. You are invested in finding out how it ends.And of course, we all know how it ends. This is Nora Roberts after all.
