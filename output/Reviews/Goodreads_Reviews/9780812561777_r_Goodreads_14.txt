Mother Ocean, Daughter Sea by Diana Marcellas was fairly interesting but I skimmed over some sections from tl;dr (too long; didn't read) and didn't feel any satisfaction in how it ended, even aside from how it's book one of a series. I don't have an urge to read the next one. Brierley thinks she may be the last shari'a with in the world and usually keeps a low profile to avoid a witch hunt. But her Callings now place her in very public places and entrap her in a web of political power struggles and deceit. To my surprise, the protagonist tripped over a moral issue of mine I didn't even know hit so hard for me. She and Melfallen start a sexual and love relationship even though Melfallen is married. His wife is a good person who loves him, which they both know, who nearly died and has delivered Melfallen's first child recently, but they do it anyway. I know Melfallen has cheated on his wife before, but Brierley doesn't have to go along with it. I don't care that they supposedly have some soul connection. I lost a lot of sympathy for both of them. Is this the "love that will last the ages" the back cover copy spoke of? If so, ew. Anyway, this affected my rating and enjoyment of this book.
