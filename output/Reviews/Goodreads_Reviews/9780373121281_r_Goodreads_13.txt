I liked the hero Tyler though I dislike his name. I dislike the girl, Michelle. She strikes me as someone flighty, blind, and childish. While I am all for pining hero story, the quick change in Michelle's feelings felt way too contrived. She really was on a rebound too. This of course is why I have started to just stick with the lengthier novels instead. If you are looking for a very fast, quick, pining romance, this book is just right.
