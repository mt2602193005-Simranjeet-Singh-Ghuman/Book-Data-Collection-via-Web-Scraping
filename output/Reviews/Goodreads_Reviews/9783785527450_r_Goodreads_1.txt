Lise Gast's German language Tiergeschichten vom Ponyhof is anecdotal, episodic and at times most definitely more than a bit too repetitive (and certainly not in any way a classic of German literature or intellectually demanding, challenging in any way). But the thirty odd short little tidbits of autobiographical personal memoirs have proven to be a most enjoyable and diverting, relaxing, in many ways laugh out loud funny, as well as at times rather sweetly nostalgic reading pleasure for me (as many of Lise Gast's remembrances about her and her children owning a stud farm, a farm where they both kept and bred Shetland Ponies and Icelandic Horses, often do somewhat remind me of my own family, of the fact that my parents also used to raise and bred horses, although especially my father certainly does NOT in any manner have Lise Gast's delightful sense of humour and totally non German laissez-faire attitude especially with regard to cleansiness, organisation and order, and would probably have conniptions and shake his head in annoyed and likely angry consternation at precisely those stories featured in Tiergeschichten vom Ponyhof which I have found the most fun, the most readable, the most approachable and yes the most entertaining). Recommended for German language savvy readers who enjoy and appreciate short episodic interludes of autobiographical information that are neither too bitter nor too sweet and which also often tend to appear with a clear and engaging sense for the hilarious, sometimes even for the ridiculous, but with the caveat that one should probably be fond horses or at least enjoy reading about them (a high three star rating, and the only reason why I have not considered four stars for Tiergeschichten vom Ponyhof is that for one Lise Gast's writing style, while certainly immediate and personable, is also at times rather a bit jumpy and flighty, read all over the place, and that for two, I do find it a tad confusing that for some of the presented anecdotes she uses the actual given names of her children, whilst for others, she obviously has been utilising pseudonyms).
