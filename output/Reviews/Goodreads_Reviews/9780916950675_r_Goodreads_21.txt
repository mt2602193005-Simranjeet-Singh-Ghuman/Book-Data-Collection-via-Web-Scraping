(53) I had difficulty entering the story, finding a shape that would allow a sustained interest. Conceptually, it's intriguing: a one-sided epistolary near-novella, to be read in various order set out by the author, though I opted for chronological by page. There is a relentlessness for a good while: abusive or disgusting male-encounter while traveling after another. But there's a turn toward the end, a grounding in something more substantial, that makes sustaining the reading worth it.
