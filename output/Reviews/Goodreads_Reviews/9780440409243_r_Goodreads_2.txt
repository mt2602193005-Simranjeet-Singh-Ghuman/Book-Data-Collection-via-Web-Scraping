Gary Soto wrote The Skirt, illustrated by Eric Velasquez, published by Yearling (1992). This book is written about a hispanic little girl named Miata. She is very excited because in the coming weekend she will be participating in a dance group to perform a Folklorico. She knows her parents are going to be very proud of her. There is only one problem; she left her beatutiful dress on the bus. The book desribes the trouble Miata goes through to rescue her folklorico dress from the bus over the weekend. The dress means a lot to Miata and her mother. Miata is afraid to get caught, but with the help of a friend, overcomes her obstacles. This book is entertaining and easily relatable. It is authentic and uses good details without over-doing it. I think students would be able to connect to Miata, regardless of their culture. Reading level is ages 6-9. I could see it being a good book for middle of second grade on average.
