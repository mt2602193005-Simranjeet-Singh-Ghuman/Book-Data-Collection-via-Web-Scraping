I must admit, the only reason I started listening to this book was because I had no other choice. I was driving to Nashville and forgot to load anything decent on my phone. I'm not a fan of Roberts' earlier stuff, especially Harlequin. It was cheesy and oversimplified and downright pathetic. But I finished it just to add another book to my 2011 goal. So there's that... if you like Harlequin, you might like this. But if you're a fan of Roberts' yearly hardbacks and her "In Death" series, don't expect much.
