3.5 I seem to have had a talent today, for rather liking romances that others have found lacking. Taste is such a personal thing. In this story, I'm fond of the mature, witty writing style and indeed, the mature, witty H Alex, a Canadian doctor from a merchant banking family. He meets our h, interior designer Agnes, when her Morgan breaks down en route to her beloved Scottish holiday home. These two seem a very good match. The H's brother Barny, turns up and the h's childhood holiday friends (the Laird and a female cousin) are also thrown into the mix.
