This book was published in 1997 Which means to those with more than a passing familiarity with the Troubles a fair amount of the stories told have been told already. I'm sure when the book was released, it had impact. Today, though, it lacks some punch. Taylor has written a series of "Irish Country Doctor" books, and those to me were far more enjoyable and worthwhile. I respect this book and the effort to write it. I wish it had delved more into why Catholics were so angry at the British. Instead it seems to try to "both sides" the conflict. To me, that leaves out some essential context.
