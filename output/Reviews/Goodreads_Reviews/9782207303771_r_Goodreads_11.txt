Member "The Brave Little Toaster"? This was written by the same guy. That could tell you all you need to know. I enjoyed this book so much, I think I am going to rethink my rating on "The MD" (another book in this 'series', and a story I've found myself thinking of often). The humor, kindness, character foibles and occasionally awful violence share a lot in common with another pair of Midwestern story-tellers, the Coen Brothers. Also, I feel the subtitle "A Tale of Terror" is kind of misleading. Though there is some gore and the ideas are "terrifying" but the whole book is written with such warmth it appeared more comedic and sweet than anything else. This is in stark contrast to "The MD", which I found to be almost desperately sad. Again...the Brave Little Toaster explains all.
