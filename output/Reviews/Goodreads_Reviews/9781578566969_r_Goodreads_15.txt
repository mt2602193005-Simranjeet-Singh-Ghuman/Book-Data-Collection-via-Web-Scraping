Excellent read! I really enjoyed Unveiling Mary Magdalene. I had the assumption she was a very “bad girl” even though I have read the passages in my Bible about her. I now realize that was erroneous teachings (and listening on my part). I have read the other Bad Girl books in this series by Liz Curtis Higgs and have laughed, cried, and learned about them as well as myself. In comparing Mary Delaney with Mary Magdalene it has helped me to see and understand to a deeper level the grace and love our Savior JESUS CHRIST has for us, regardless of our past, present or future. Overall an excellent book and I highly recommend! Five stars!⭐️
