A book I picked up at the thrift store for .50 cents. Was the only book in the sci-fi section that looked interesting on that particular day. Turns out it was on okay book. I finished it. But I wasn't enthralled with it or anything like that. It reminded me of Heinlein's The Moon Is a Harsh Mistress . It seemed like an attempt at replicating that book. The idea was there; a political battle between those on earth and those in outer space. I'll give it some slack though as it turns out it is Barnes' first novel. I've heard he has gotten way better.
