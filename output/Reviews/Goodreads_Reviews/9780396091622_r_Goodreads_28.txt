Belgian Detective Hercule Poirot is at it again and he still marvels me, and Agatha Christie gets another 5-star review from me, whether she deserves it or not. Hastings and Poirot are called to France. they are called for a murder of a rich noble man. Poirot and a French detective Monsieur Giraud are faced off to solve the mystery. Hastings the poor guy is really taken by Giraud methods. but of course, Poirot solves the case and Giraud has to give Poirot a tribute of his victory. Hastings is his normal dimwitted self. he of course is swept away with love! Poirot triumphs again!
