Theroux's long essays are clearly researched but not demanding of the reader: instead of cavernous and analytical screeds, each portion is an enthusiastic deluge and amalgam of anecdotes, history, culture which don't aim at getting to the heart of the matter but instead choose to be wide tableaux. At about the midway point of the first essay, on blue, I questioned if there was a point; midway through the second, on yellow, I realized "the point" was that it's a lot of fun.
