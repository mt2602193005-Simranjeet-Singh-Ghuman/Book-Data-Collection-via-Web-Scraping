Wow, possibly the worst thriller I've ever read... We start with an utterly unlikeable "hero", who puts his daughter into harm's way then fails to comprehend what's happening until it's too late. His ego manages to stop him noticing anything that's happening until the daughter's at death's door. He then flies completely off the rails, attacking several people and acting like a lunatic until he finally realises the obvious truth (some people in the food industry are unscrupulous, dur). He then makes a half-assed attempt at rectifying the problem. I won't give away the ending, but he causes at least one needless death. I had to keep reading this, as I wasn't sure how such an experienced novelist could get so much wrong - bad characters, actions that come out of nowhere and "revelations" that seem transparently obvious.
