Wow... My husband and I decided to read this collection of macabre tales together, and after wading through story after story, I have to say that Ms. Oates truly knows how to terrify people. My favorite stories in the book were those that were ambiguous and subtle, ending up like a Rorschach test for people who interpret the story differently. My favorite such tale was the first, a story about a deadly haunting (or perhaps not a haunting at all?). Some of the more gruesome tales involve rape or child abuse, so readers be warned!
