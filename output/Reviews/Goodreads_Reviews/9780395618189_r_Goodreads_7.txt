I remembered this one from when I was a child, but not most of the details. After reading it again, I can see why I remembered it! I love the clever turns of phrase, the wacky adventures, and the eventual unlikely friendship that develops. Also, it's kind of fun that it takes so long to even start developing. Prince Brat certainly lives up to his name for a lot longer than expected--which makes the transition even better when it starts. :) Content--one mention of Hell (as a place); a few uses of "blazes" and some invented expressions; mention of cursing; references to executions; mentions of boys being whipped
