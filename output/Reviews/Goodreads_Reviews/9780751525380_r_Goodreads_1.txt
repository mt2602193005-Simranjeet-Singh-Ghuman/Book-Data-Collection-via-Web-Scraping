Book 23 in the Morland Dynasty series. Things at Morland Place go from bad to worse as George's wife fritters away money to get her back to her perceived station in life, George's sister, Henrietta, is trapped in a loveless and stifling marriage, and Lady Venetia Fleetwood gives up everything to follow her desire to become a doctor against all the odds. As always, this is another brilliant instalment following the Morland family's fortunes.
