I just remembered this book today, after not thinking about it in years. I read it when I was kind of obsessed with suicide. This book is the novelization of a made for TV movie. As I was trying to find information on this book, I found out that the movie aired on ABC in 1985. It starred Molly Ringwald and Zach Galligan as the suicidal teenagers. Apparently River Phoenix was in the movie too. Strangely, I have never seen the movie (re-released in 1993 on VHS and renamed Tragedy ), but I am pretty sure I read the book more than once.
