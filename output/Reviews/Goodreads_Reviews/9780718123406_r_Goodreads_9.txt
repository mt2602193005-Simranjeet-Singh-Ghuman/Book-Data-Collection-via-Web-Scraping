I've always enjoyed reading Dick Francis books and I thought I'd read them all when I found this book in a second hand store. It did not disappoint, considering that it had been written over 30 years ago. I'm not one to analyse the strengths and weaknesses of a book. I rate it based on a) if it held my interest and b) if I would recommend the book to others. Yes to both, and hence the 5 star rating.
