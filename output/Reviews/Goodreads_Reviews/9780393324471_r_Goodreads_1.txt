This book was really not good. i was not impressed by the writing style, but i love el sal, so i plowed through it. the author had a nasty attitude about cultural adaptation, and she was a jealous brat when her husband wanted to hang out with his family once a year. she sat in a tree and sulked. she didnt paint a very good picture of herself. read it only if you have any interest in el salvador. dont read it if you like feeling sympathetic at all to the protagonista.
