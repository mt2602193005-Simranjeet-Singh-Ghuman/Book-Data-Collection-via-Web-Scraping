4.5 stars... Excellent book and start of my 2015 reading! This book is a re-issue of one written earlier in her career, 2001 I think. It follows a different path than most of her books, and it doesn't have a time travel element per se. Written in a modern gothic style, it is worded eloquently and wonderfully. I sound like a fangirl, and that I am! I see that many didn't enjoy it as much as I did, and I hate that. It's a great book. The mysteries in the storyline aren't that hard to figure out, but I think it was intentionally that way by the author. It didn't lessen my enjoyment of reading it in any way, but maybe it's a personal thing. It has romance as well and a little paranormal activity too:) I recommend it to SK fans and those who might enjoy a gothic feel in a book. Notation: clean read:)
