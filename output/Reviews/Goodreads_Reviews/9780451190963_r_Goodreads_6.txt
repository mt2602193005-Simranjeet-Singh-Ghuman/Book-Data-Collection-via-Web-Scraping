This was the first book that I have read by J D Christillian and I liked it, but did not love it. It was very well written, but seemed to be longer than it could have been. It seems that Olivia was left hanging and you didn't get a sense of what would happen when Harp told her husband whatever he was going to tell him. I thought this was the focus of the story, but it quickly turned into something else. The secondary story that became the main focus was an interesting one, but I personally would have liked to find out what happened to Olivia. Harp was a very easy person to like. It was the tall, dark and menacing kind of fellow. You got the sense that hardly anyone messed with him. He also had a great supply of street urchins to help in his endeavors whatever they might me. I am giving this 4 out of 5 stars.
