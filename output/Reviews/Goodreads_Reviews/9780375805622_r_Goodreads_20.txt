Elmo and Grover want kids to join them on an interactive adventure. It will be up to you to choose to open this monster filled book. Elmo is trying to convince Grover to get to the last page of the book. Readers can follow along with the help of some convincing from Elmo. On each page there is a fold or pop-up with some encouraging words from Elmo. Interactive, fun and engaging readers will learn new words along with shapes and can also increase motor skills. Parents and teachers will find this book an educational treat for beginners.
