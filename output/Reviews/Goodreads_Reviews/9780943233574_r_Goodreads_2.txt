This book was exactly what I needed. It wants to tell you the stories of other people. It really inspired me to take a few moments in the day to look around and absorb what was going on around me. Instead of gluing myself to a phone, a game, a book, or just the interior of the vehicle as I am carried toward whatever errand or responsibility, I look outside at what we're passing. Sometimes I look at the sky, and the various shades of blue there, or the shapes that the clouds make. Sometimes I look at the trees, and smile at the different shades of green and the way the leaves blow in the wind. Sometimes I look at the people we pass; the early morning joggers, the Sunday afternoon families enjoying the nice day, someone waiting at a bus stop. While I still worry and fret about many things, I found (and still find) that by thinking about the advice of this book, that time spent traveling to the destination no longer holds the same worries and annoyances that it used to, and that is one less area of my life that causes me stress. I can only hope that the message this book contains stays with me: that I continue to have those moments of peace, and that they spread to other areas of my life. What this book can really teach you is whatever you need to know. For me, that was to worry less, and to learn, at least a little bit, what living life is about. Everything will not be the same forever. These things that take over our lives, that we worry and fret to get just right, in the end don't really mean anything. What does mean something, indeed everything, is your family; your friends; and the small moments in life. If you need a book that can make you feel life a little sweeter, and whose words, not with individual meanings but with the feelings they give to you, make you appreciate life, this book will help.
