An engaging, richly described short novel set in Stockholm Archipelago, Sweden in the late 1800s. Carlsson, an experienced farmer with no idea about anything to do with the sea, begins employment at a rundown farm at Hemso. Madame Flod is a widow and her son, Gusten, a hunter and fisherman, knows little about the running of a farm. Carlsson quickly makes improvements to the farm. Gusten resents Carlsson’s influence on his mother, Madame Flod. A worthwhile, interesting read. This book was first published in 1887.
