This is book 2 in the cousin's series. Since it wasn't available on Kindle, which I hope will be remedied soon, I had to read books 1 and 3 in the series while I waited for this paperback to arrive. Yancie's story was super funny. A Feisty Fiance. This is Finnia's story, and it was super cute. Astra is the final story. A Marriage in Mind. You can read them out of order, but the characters are introduced in Yancie's story. Some spice that is easy to skip. Even though it gets spicy, Jessica Steele generally has her characters end up as chaste brides, and all three cousins kept their pact to save themselves for marriage. Cute series.
