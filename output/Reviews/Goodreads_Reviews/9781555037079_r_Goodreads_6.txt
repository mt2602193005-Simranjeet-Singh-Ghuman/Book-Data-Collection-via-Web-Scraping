I was deeply moved as I heard Kitty relate her story at a women's conference back in the mid 1990's. First chance I had, I found her book and read it. Kitty de Ruyter told her experiences as powerfully on the page as she did in person. Her mother's spiritual strength was incredible. Several years later, I had an opportunity to hear Kitty de Ruyter-Bons speak again to a much smaller group of about ten women. She told "the rest of the story," so to speak. She was really cute the way she told it. She always had a thing for men in uniform since she was rescued by the soldiers. Her grandson, I believe, once excitedly called her to see a man in uniform. She laughed because the uniformed man turned out to be a postman. After her first husband died, she met and married a retired military man (don't recall which branch) and she is very happily married to him (Paul Bons).
