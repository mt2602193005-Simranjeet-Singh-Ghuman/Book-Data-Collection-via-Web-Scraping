One to Keep on Hand This is a great little book. I read it in two days! It teaches us how important trusting God is to the Christian life. With biblical quotes and examples of people from the bible, Charles Swindoll shows us that we can have faith and don't have to be perfect to have it. He teaches us how worry is so against the Christian faith. I love the biblical quotes at the end, but he missed one, under worry, Philippians 4:6-7.
