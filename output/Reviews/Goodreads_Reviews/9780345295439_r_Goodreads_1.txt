I really wanted to give this thing 2 stars because the beginning half was so full of cheeze whiz and made me chuckle, but the second half became less amusing rather quickly, and the repetitive use of the "sensuous man/sensuous woman/sensuous couple" on every goddamn page got old. Fast. Chartham's sexcapade stories, which he consistently kept apologizing for after filling a page and a half up with them, were totally distracting and irrelevant. Boasting about how many orgies you had while you were studying in Paris and how many times you bathed your manjunk in icy champagne does not tell me anything about how to be a more sensual lover, dude. I think I will be placing this book back in the musty old box that I found it in...
