I always love reading books my friends adore because it feels like getting a little glimpse into who they are. This one was recommended to me by one of my best friends, and she’s loved it since she was young. I honestly didn’t know what to expect going in, but this book pulled me in quickly. The mystery kept my attention, and the writing was engaging. Were there a few cheesy moments? Absolutely, but let’s be honest, most romances have them 😂. I enjoyed most of the characters, and the romance was really sweet. I also appreciated the perspective it gave on the prejudice Native Americans faced. Overall, this was an enjoyable read, and I’m really glad I finally picked it up. 🪶Historical Romance 🪶Standalone 🪶Closed Door 🪶Mild Swearing (No F Words) 🪶CW: Racism, Death, Sexual Harassment
