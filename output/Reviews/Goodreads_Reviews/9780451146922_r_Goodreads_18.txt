This is a book that I will remember for the rest of my life. Truly epic, it is a novelization of the life of Michelangelo Buonnaroti. The author spent six years in Italy researching Michelangelo, the Medici, and the various popes and dukes of the era; and it shows. I learned so much about carving marble, painting frescoes, and the tumultuous nature of life in renaissance Italy. It is a very long novel, and it took me a long time to read it, but I was practically never bored by it. Creating art is a deeply spiritual experience, and the author perfectly captured the artist’s mindset in his characterization of Michelangelo. He was equally adept at immersing the reader in the politics of the Medici and the various popes of the 1500s. This was my first Irving Stone novel, and I look forward to reading more of his work.
