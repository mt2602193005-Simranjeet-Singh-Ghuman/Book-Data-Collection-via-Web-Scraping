My mom has read a lot of parenting books, and this is one of the two she recommended for me to read. One of the main ideas is that children respond better to positive reinforcement than punishment. If we do not give our children our attention unless they are acting-up, then they will continue to act-up because they want our attention. The author, Dr. Latham teaches how to teach appropriate behavior and positively reinforce good behavior. He also talks about how to deal with problem behavior in ways that are non-reinforcing. Dr. Latham was a professor at Utah State University, and his book teaches parenting skills that are based in science. The basic principles of the book are given in chapters 2 and 3 (particularly chapter 3), but the other chapters are good discussions of how to apply those principles to various situations. I enjoyed reading the book.
