I’ve been eyeing this book for a while and was quite afraid that someone else will buy it as it was the last copy in my city, acting on my fear, I’ve hidden the book in a corner where no one else would find it and had decided to wait until I’ve had the money for it. Come my birthday week I've decided to treat myself and get the book; which to my good luck, it remained in the same spot I've left it! Going home pleased with my purchase, I've started reading it while enjoying the rich content it contained. I've always had an appreciation for literature and living where I am you could not find many books that highlight key writings of the genre; so you could understand my cheerfulness when I've finally found a book like this anthology.
