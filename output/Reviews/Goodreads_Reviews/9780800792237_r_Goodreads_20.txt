Not really a training manual This book is, for the most part, filled with inspiring information. The chapter about soaking in God, although very short is like a jewel jammed between the pages. Reading this book will cause you to take a serious look at your prayer life. About two thirds in the author shells out a few paragraphs of Gay bashing. Had I know that I would not have bought the book. But for this out of place excursion into lasciviousness I got great value from reading this book.
