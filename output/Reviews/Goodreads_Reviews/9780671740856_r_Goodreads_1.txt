I am a great fan of childrens books. I have a special affection for books with unlikely animal detectives which I collect to give to a friend who also likes them. This book appeared at a library book sale this morning and is really a delight. Our hero is a rock hopper penguin who lives upstairs from a cafe in Chicago. The mysteries and their solutions all involve chocolate. It does not get a lot better than this. I will look for the others in the series.
