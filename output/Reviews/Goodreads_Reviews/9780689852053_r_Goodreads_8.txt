This book was absolutely horrible. I found this in a library book sale and read the back of the book thinking, 'This look interesting. I'm looking for a good book, this might be it.' I was SO wrong. It started out okay, kinda weird, but hey! the beginnings of books can be like that; they start out bad but end up awesome. Not very far into the book characters started dropping the F-bomb and other inappropriate words I'd like not to repeat. Then after that, it just kept getting worse and worse; continual foul language, inappropriate actions/conversations, a gay character, and immodest everything. I honestly couldn't even finish the book; I'm horrified at how many liberties were used in this 'teen' book. Adults shouldn't even read this! I'd rate this zero stars, but I'm being generous and giving it a one star. Not appropriate for any age!!
