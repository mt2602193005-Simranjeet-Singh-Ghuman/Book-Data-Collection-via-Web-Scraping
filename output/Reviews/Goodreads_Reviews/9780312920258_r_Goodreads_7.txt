One of Arkansas' op criminal attorneys, Bill McArthur lived a life of dreams: wealth, a fast-track career, a beautiful family, and influential friends in the highest circles of Little Rock's good-old-boy society. But all that bursts to pieces when on one July day two men deliver flowers in his house, and kill his wife. What follows is a horrible investigation which seems just too bizarre to have happened in the real life. DA says he is innocent. The sheriff says he's guilty. This is perhaps the most bizarre true crime story I've read (or at least can't think of anything weirder right now... at least if we exclude a few eclectic serials), or at least the most bizarre in a long time. Very well researched and written, but sad. Sad how the investigations were done... and to see all that was happening while the court proceedings were still going on. It didn't really matter if whom they wanted was guilty or not... or whom that "they" refers to - once again, in the real life, not all incompetent and and bigoted people get what they deserve (this most corrupted piece of law enforcement in this case changed career and is or was an apparently successful politician after. That is after the damage of this case was already done). And the one the back cover refers as "remorseless femme fatale" is quite a (negative) character too... BC
