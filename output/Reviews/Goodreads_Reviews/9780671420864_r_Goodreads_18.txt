I suppose that Eve Babitz keeps rewriting the same book over and over again. The problem is, I don't mind. It has become such a cultural truism -- especially for those of the Eastern persuasion -- that there is something fundamentally wrong with L.A. I used to buy into that myth, but half a century of living here has made me see different. L.A. Woman is more fictionalized than Eve's Hollywood , but it's the same basic book. Eve's power is in her positivity: And I was an L.A. woman. In fact, looking back on those one-night stands, I must have been crazy. Yet there were thousands of girls living between Sunset and Santa Monica in between La Brea and La Cienega who painted the town red like me -- and who got away with it too. I first arrived in L.A. from Cleveland (yechhhh!) at the tail end of 1966 -- right around the time the place seemed to be really getting interesting. I'm glad I stuck with it, and I'm glad Eve Babitz is there to be its muse.
