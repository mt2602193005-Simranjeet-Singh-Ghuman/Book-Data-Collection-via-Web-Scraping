This book is very insightful and kind of terrifying with over 50 years of hindsight. It concisely portrays the ins and outs of the Nixon campaign and allows the actions of the campaign managers to speak for themselves. McGinniss barely comments on his own impressions of the situation, but they are nonetheless very clear. It is predictive of lots of campaigns as I have known them in my lifetime. I do feel I should warn potential readers that there are a few instances of offensive language in this book, but it is exclusively limited to quotes from actual people in the story.
