Not the worst of these types of books. The biggest problem is a complete lack of tension. Kendra doesn't really seem all that tempted by Revell--which, on the one hand, I appreciate, since I roll my eyes at female protagonists who go all weak-kneed at the dangerous man who's threatening their entire family. At least Kendra demonstrates a few ounces of strength and willpower. But Revell doesn't seem all that powerful by comparison and spends the whole book making empty threats.
