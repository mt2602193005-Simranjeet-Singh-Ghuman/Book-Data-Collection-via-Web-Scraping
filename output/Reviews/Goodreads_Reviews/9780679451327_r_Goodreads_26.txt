I read this book about 5 years ago and I was not terribly impressed. Upon giving it a second reading, I really like it. Clearly my mood and place in life affects my judgement of what I'm reading. This is the story of one woman's faith journey. She is fortunate enough to end up in a congregation that has little money, but a big heart; a congregation actually intereted in the last, lost, least and little of the world and in supporting each other's spiritual needs. Worth the read - even twice!
