This holds up surprisingly well! There are times when the worldbuilding is a little weak -- are there still countries in this time? The kids mention Australia, e.g., but it also seems like the Government is somehow global -- but it detracts minimally from the story. The other thing that struck me rereading this after over a decade was the pacing. I'd forgotten how these big chunks of time pass. Both the thinness of the worldbuilding and the pacing can easily be put down to this being very much a kids' book, and I think the principles of it and the intrigue manage to make up for that, for the most part.
