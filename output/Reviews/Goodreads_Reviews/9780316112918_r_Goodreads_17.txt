This has been among the many books out as a February feature. I grew up with plenty of Arthur adventures, and I have several in my classroom library. This one isn't my favorite. It stays true to the series and characters, but feel it lacks details. A lot of D.C. connections are provided, but it doesn't elaborate for children any of the details or feelings of actually being in D.C. I'm looking forward to wrapping up this current trend of picture books so we can move on to a new theme!
