I wanted to read this so badly because the cover was giving a Las Vegas desert type of paradise—and guess what? I wasn't wrong! I've always been looking for a summery-feeling book where I could imagine the hot, burning sun piercing through my white-as-a-sheet skin without any umbrellas, and the sand getting into my eyes. Because let's be honest, who doesn't like the sun? To be frank, I'm only 20 pages in and I am hooked like a fat piranha in my seat! Though there are some pages that bore me—most of those are the parts where they suddenly slip their hands into women's skirts and touch each other, then suddenly, by tomorrow morning, the man is gone and a child is born. It's very tiring as a reader to encounter five scenarios of this in just two pages. But anyways, I like Susan's writing style; it's very blissful and exciting. However, I don't believe I will be finishing this book because I've got millions of things to do. Seriously? What agony could unfold with two popular, flirty couples? 600+ pages is just crazy. I've seen tons of feedback saying the ending is ass as well, and I am not wasting my time with an abomination like this! I like the concept of it, though, Susan.
