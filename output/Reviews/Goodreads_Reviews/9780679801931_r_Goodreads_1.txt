Our grandson is extremely choosy about his books these days. He will only let you read a few books and those over and over again. And while I respect that, I also think he needs to be exposed to some of the other books which are out there. I gave him a choice between this, and another book and he chose this and enjoyed it very much. Webby is cousin to Huey, Dewey and Louie who won't play with her because she's a girl. But then Webby does something which impresses everyone, and her cousins decide that she might be okay after all.
