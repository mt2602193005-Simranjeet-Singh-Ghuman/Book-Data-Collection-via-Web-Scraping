You cannot go wrong by selecting a book written by Smith Wigglesworth. His life is a life to be sought after. People often wonder why they cannot attain the results of people like Wigglesworth. But when you read his books, you get an understanding of his total commitment, relationship with God, obedience, and denial of his flesh that are all necessary to underlie faith. Why don't we have the faith of Wigglesworth? Why can we not go to a wake, pick up a body from the coffin, stand it up against a wall and tell it to live, then when it falls down again, pick it up again, and keep telling it to live... until it does? This book will show you the lifestyle that is required to sustain and grow your faith.
