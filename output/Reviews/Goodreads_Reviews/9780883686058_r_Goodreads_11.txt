I can't say my relationship with God was forever changed by this book. But I can say the way I look at my relationship with the Lord is completely changed. I always felt a heaviness of the fact that judgment day was coming. Now I feel relieved that I have already been forgiven fro my past transgressions and their is no record of them in heaven. I am free from sin. I believe every word Roland Buck said. I believe he saw angles, and I believe that the visited him in little old Boise, Idaho. I can say that this book is worth reading.
