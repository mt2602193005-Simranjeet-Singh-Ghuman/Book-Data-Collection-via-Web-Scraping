4.5 Do not look for justice in a Zola novel. The Belly of Paris (Le Ventre de Paris), the third novel in Émile Zola's twenty-volume series of Les Rougon-Macquart is a criticism leveled at the fat, well fed, self-satisfied bourgeois class. The setting is Les Halles of Paris and the story takes place over one year from 1858 to 1859.
