I wonder if Trollope had written Pride and Prejudice , how that would have turned out? I was just looking back at all the Trollopes I've read and thinking they almost all have convoluted love stories where one of the couple is considerably richer than the other. Trollope was a master at writing female characters and relationships, and also at capturing Darcy types. Elizabeth would have got her man, but what tangled path would have got her there? Finished last century, reviewed Nov 2020, except it's not a review, it's an idle hmming sort of musing.
