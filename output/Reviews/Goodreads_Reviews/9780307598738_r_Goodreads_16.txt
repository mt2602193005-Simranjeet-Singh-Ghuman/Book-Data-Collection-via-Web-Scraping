Another great Mercer Meyer book. This is about a young boy who is going to the dentist for a check up. He talks about the process of going and how he had to wait in the waiting room with other kids. He even sees someone who has braces, he now hopes he can get braces. He says he waits a long time but that at least they had toys to play with and books to read. Finally, it was his turn. So he goes to see the dentist all by himself where they go in a weird room. He talks about how funny the chair looks and the fact that he had to wear a bib. This book is funny, I like that you get to see this from a kids perspective. This is even neat for kids to read because some kids might be scared of going to the dentist. With this book being a series, the illustrations look very similar to the rest of her books. They are detailed, textured and colorful
