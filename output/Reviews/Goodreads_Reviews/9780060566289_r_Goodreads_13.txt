This is simply one of the most beautiful books. I LOVE this book and the stories in it. There is nothing preachy at all about these stories, that concentrate on the lives of black people. The stories are told creatively, with an older, some might say, dated vernacular. I like to think that Edward P. Jones is from around the time I grew up and that he has a better vocabulary than most young writers of today. The stories are so unique and intriguing, told simply and honestly. The compassion Jones feels for his characters is clear. I cannot say enough about this book. An absolute treasure, from a black man who writes. Buy it, read it, think about it. From the story about the girl who cared for pigeons, to "The Night Rhonda Ferguson was Killed," the stories show the scope of what humanity is capable of and provide you with NO answers. I love that part of it. The reader truly is challenged to make sense of it. The author doesn't make it easy, does not give it away, in any capacity. Highly recommended!
