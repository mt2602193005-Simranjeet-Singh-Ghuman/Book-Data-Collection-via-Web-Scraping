Romance trope involved: forced proximity This book is filled with stuff I'd find problematic in an actual relationship, but just love to read. For instance, a hero who knows the heroine's mind better than she does. The protagonist is so "girly" that she's practically helpless camping and that gets a little annoying. I have a feeling this was one of Roberts's earlier works because there are only two characters for the majority of it. Usually she shines in creating side characters. It's not my favorite of her works, but I did enjoy it.
