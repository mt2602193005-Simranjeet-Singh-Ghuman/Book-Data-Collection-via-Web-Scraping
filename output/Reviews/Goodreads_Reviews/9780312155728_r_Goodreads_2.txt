Read it first in 1998 and found it to be magical as I was living on the edge of the Great Basin. Read it again and loved it just as much 22 years later. A tale for lovers of stories with a cast of great characters. I would compare it favorably to Tom Robbins, another favorite of mine for the sheer creativity of story, the magical turn of phrase, and the perfect blending of the mystical and the profane.
