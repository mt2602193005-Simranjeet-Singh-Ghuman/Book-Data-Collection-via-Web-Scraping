This story is about the period of India's independence and the separation of Pakistan and India. Primarily the book is set in Punjabi, the state that was divided by the separation. The story is told from the perspective of a Sikh engineer and his two wives. I found the start of the book and the story of the two women really interesting. Oddly enough, the last part of the book where everything happens kind of lost me a bit. What I did get from the story is that this was a violent and confusing time for everyone caught up in the separation. I did like the book and I thought the characterisation was really good.
