Basically one assassin chases another, both thinking they're smarter. John Mikali, premier assassin and world-famous pianist likes to kill. I'm not sure where he gets his assignments, but there's obviously some that are his personal missions. Asa Morgan is a long-term military member with experience in several combat operations. Mikali killed his 14-year-old daughter, accidentally, as part of another assassination. This is Morgan's reason for his vengdetta. Neither character is likable and the reason for Katherine Riley's presence in this book seems pointless. My first book by Jack Higgins and likely my last.
