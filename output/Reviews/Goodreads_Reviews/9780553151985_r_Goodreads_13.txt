Bought this at a school book fair and read it decades ago. Now I have children of my own — and one of the a enthusiast of witches and Halloween to boot — I went to find a copy to read to the next generation. Sadly this appears to be out of print and my childhood copy was donated to a library book sale, it seems. Thank goodness for public libraries and inter-library loans. Managed to find a copy to read to my little one last Halloween and you know what? It's delightful. Every bit as fun as I remember. Course now I'd add sweet and charming too. I'll have to pony up for a used copy to replace my old 80s era paperback. This could be a great Halloween tradition.
