I liked the matter-of-fact approach of the book. She introduces 'New Age' ideas in a way that makes them seem not-so-weird (of course, I kind of lean toward the 'New Age' in general, so maybe others won't feel that the ideas are not-so-weird). For example, when she talks about meditation and deep relaxation, she makes it feel like something that a normal person can do rather than something that is only for monks on mountaintops. Well written, easy to understand, and backed up with research. I originally checked this out from the library a couple of years ago, then purchased it so that I can refer back to it over and over (and I do refer to it often).
