Identical Cousins Switch Places Lovely Regency Romance set in Savannah, where the rich Mr. Hyde Purchis has married the very french Josephine. Josephine married for wealth and is immensely bored and quite shallow, but she does have one plan. She's planning to use her husband's money to buy a ship and free Napoleon from St. Helena. To do this she talks her cousin who is her twin in looks, to temporarily take her place at home. The rest of the story takes off from there. Absolutely great, as poor Juliet tries to cover for her with the little information her thoughtless cousin provided. Great story and a very satisfactory ending. Show more
