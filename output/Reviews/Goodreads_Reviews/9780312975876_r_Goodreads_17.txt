Sarah was running away from everything trying to find a safe place for herself and her baby Daniel. Zack Garrett wanted his land back and finding Sarah Kincaid and her son and taking them back to Helena and he would be paid and his land would be his again. He finds them and they begin a journey together that will change them both. Sarah the deceived and shamed. Zack the orphan from the streets of New York still struggling to survive. I loved their story and recommend it.
