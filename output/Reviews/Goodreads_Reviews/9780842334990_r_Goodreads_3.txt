These two books are two books that everyone should read. They will guide your path and help you understand what is expected of you and what to except. Love them! There is a lot of repetition and that is because of the importance of the words in them; after Psalms 70 there are many chapters that have been partially made into songs of worship. I found myself singing parts of many a chapter. They are both great books to meditate on and to spend quiet time with.
