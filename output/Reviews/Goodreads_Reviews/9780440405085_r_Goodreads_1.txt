Since I was a child I have always been fascinated with the culture of the Native American tribes and would devour any book I could have found on them. But unfortunately receiving any information about specific individuals was a bit harder for me to find and so I can say I am not well-versed in knowing many of the lives of famous Native Americans as they were never taught in detail like so many other individuals in the broad class of history. I had heard about Sitting Bull and knew a bit about him but he was unfortunately one of those obscure names mentioned before falling back into darkness. So as far as historicity I cannot vouch whether the information was correct or not on him although from the cultural viewpoint it was factual from the information that I had known. At the same time with the age of this book, though, it can still be said to be a bit outdated as it does include terminology now believed to be politically incorrect for the zealous. What made this telling for me interesting was the fact that it did explore Sitting Bull from a baby although very small in comparison to his adult years before progressing. As such the reader is given an almost rounded view although it is more than likely that such a book hasn't nor cannot cover all aspects of his life, which would be best kept in mind. All in all I thought it was a pretty good book although the writing is a bit on the dry side so as a result just consider it as a stepping stone. By now there should be plenty of good books about Sitting Bull for those who are interested but for those who are looking for a brief read this one should do quite nicely.
