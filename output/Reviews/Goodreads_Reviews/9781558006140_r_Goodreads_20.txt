November of the Heart (1993) is another captivating love story by LaVyrle Spencer… full of sharp dialogue and unforgettable characters. Absolutely loved it! Minnesota, 1895.., Lorna falls for the Norwegian odd jobs man working in the family kitchen at her parent’s lake front estate. Jens is ambitious.. he actually is skilled at building racing yachts and insists he can build a winning yacht for Lorna’s father. Lorna and Jens are instantly attracted to each other… she’s bold and determined, never mind the class differences. Both are incredibly naive about how her parents will react. The story is filled with exquisite prose, sensual love scenes, heartache and the power of love…. it’s definitely a page turner. ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
