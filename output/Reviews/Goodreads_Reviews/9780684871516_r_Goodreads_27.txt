I agree with other reviews that it's a bit too 'oh isn't Conrad Anker amazing' and got a bit sickly at times with all the Conrad admiration 😂 However, he deserves credit for his achievements, it can't be argued that he has played a pivotal role in rescuing people and the fact that he and his team found Mallory 75 years after he vanished is incredible. I very much enjoyed learning more about George Mallory, Sandy Irvine and the rest of the team, it really is amazing what they managed to do back in 1924. Do I think Mallory and/or Irvine made it to the summit? In all likelihood, no. Between the primitive equipment and clothing they had, the fact that some equipment that they may have needed was left behind, the fact that they started late in the day (by modern standards) and would have been coming down in the dark (having left the flashlight and compass behind.) It all adds up to it being highly doubtful but not, I admit, impossible. As Ed Viesturs says: "getting to the top is optional, coming down is mandatory" so it almost doesn't matter if they did because they didn't make it back to tell anyone. It's very sad as Mallory, in my opinion, deserved to be the first but that's not how the world works is it?
