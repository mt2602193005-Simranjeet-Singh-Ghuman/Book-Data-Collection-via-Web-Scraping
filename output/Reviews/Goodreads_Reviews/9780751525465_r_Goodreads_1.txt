As always, I highly recommend starting this series at the beginning, in order to understand the various interrelationships between the characters. Therefore my review/synopsis is hidden. It is 1870, and a new, younger generation of Morlands takes center stage in this book. After his father's death, George assumes the role as lord of the manor and quickly marry. His new bride is quick to take over management of the house, with many changes — especially to the lives of the younger children. In London, Charlotte's daughters Venetia and Olivia have very different ambitions: despite all the obstacles in her way, Venetia wishes desperately to become a doctor; while Olivia happily settles for an appointment at court, serving the Queen for four months at a time. Set against the backdrop of high Victorian era, this is another fascinating story full of interesting, well-drawn characters. Highly recommended.
