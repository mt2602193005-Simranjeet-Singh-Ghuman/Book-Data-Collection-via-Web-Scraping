Christina's favorite book from her childhood. It is a story of a little boy who is contempuous of an old teddy bear that he recieves as a gift and literally kicks it away. He goes to sleep and dreams that he is a gift to a young bear in a town of bears: a boy with an umbrella. The young bear kicks the little boy aside and only after the boy pleads that he is really a little boy does the Teddy Bear relent and become friends with the little boy who returns to his home with the magic umbrella where the boy now appreciates the Teddy Bear. A scary tale of rejection and redemption. Christina's respect and identification with the underdog is perhaps linked to this tale.
