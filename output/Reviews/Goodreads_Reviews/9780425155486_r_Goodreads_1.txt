Charlotte Graham, an amateur sleuth, assists police to solve multiple murders in the town of Zion Hill, a quiet community on the east bank of New York's Hudson River. The book is populated with "loonies," each character, other than Charlotte and Jerry, the police chief, have some kind of serious mental problem: an artist who "recomposes the decomposed," a plastic surgeon who creates clones of his dead wife, a one-armed handy man who had been struck by lightening, and a whole community of religious cultists. The author's attempts at "creepy" unfortunately come out "kooky."
