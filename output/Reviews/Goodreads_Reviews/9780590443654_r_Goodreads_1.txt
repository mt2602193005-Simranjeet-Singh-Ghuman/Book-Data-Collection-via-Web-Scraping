I have heard mixed reviews about this book, but I have to admit that I loved it. I also loved the 1990 film version. In fact, I cannot read the book without hearing the voices of Eva Gabor, who voiced the character of Bianca, and Bob Newhart who perfectly voiced Bernard's character. The story itself is one of adventure, and loyalty to a cause. I give this Disney book 4 OUT OF 5 STARS ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Check out the movie stills at: https://m.imdb.com/title/tt0100477/
