This is a quick read filled with inspirational stories designed to encourage the reader to think twice before judging another human being who may look, act or think differently. Although the stories and quotes were thought provoking, I felt like this book was missing something. This book is great if you're looking for a story to share for a bedtime devotional at YW's camp or short inspirational message for FHE but if you're looking for something with a little more "meat" you may be disappointed.
