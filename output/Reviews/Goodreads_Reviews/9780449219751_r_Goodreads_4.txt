By the time I got to the 4th book it became nauseatingly obvious that the compiler of these quotes/books was carefully sandwiching the really good quotes between the mediocre and outright worthless ones at a rate of about 1 good per every 12 bad. All the really good quotes from all 4 volumes could've been compiled into 1 book and it still wouldn't've totaled 637. Ho hum. But I guess that's how money is made. Still, the good quotes are great, and make this book worthy of 4 stars.
