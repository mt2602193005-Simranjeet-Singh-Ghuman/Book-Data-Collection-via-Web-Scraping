I was enjoying this book until I got to the section about the Mormon pioneers. Even if you aren't LDS, it is clear that the author wrote with the intention of marring the character of early church leaders, which was hardly the point of writing about their trek west. I happen to be LDS and have read many non-member accounts of early church history that were perfectly unbiased and properly objective, as the rest of this book seems to be. However, this section made me question the validity of the other so-called facts in the book. On an unrelated note, I was also thoroughly sickened at the description of the Donner party ordeal and had nightmares for weeks. There ought to be an R-rating on this book for the gore in that section--or at least a warning at the start of the chapter.
