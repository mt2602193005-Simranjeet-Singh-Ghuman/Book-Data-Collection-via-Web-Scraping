This was actually my very first ever introduction to spell books and magic outside of fairytales, way back in around 1998 that sparked a long-standing fascination with folklore and the occult. Reading through it now, many many years later, I can acknowledge that's it's not a great read or an insight into Romani workings but it's a nice way to start on the path of research into cultures and their lore, and a good introduction to things like colour and ingredient associations, candles, and crystals.
