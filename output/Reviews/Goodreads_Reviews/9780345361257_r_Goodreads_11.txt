An oldie, but a goodie. Ernie Harwell is the voice of legends where I’m from, as the most-recognized and respected broadcaster for the Detroit Tigers of Major League Baseball. This book tracks through his life journey, with chapters focusing on players he knew and situations he encountered. It’s important to note that this book also features first-hand accounts of when baseball became integrated, as Harwell worked for the LA Dodgers when Jackie Robinson entered the scene. Told with humour, but always with kindness towards even the most abrupt of individuals, this book warmed my heart. The beauty of it is the fact that you don’t even need to be a baseball fan to enjoy this book. Sure, it may help with name recognition, but the book centres on the humanity of the people he came across throughout his storied career.
