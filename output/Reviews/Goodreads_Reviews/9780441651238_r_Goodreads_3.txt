entertaining fictional farce. i laughed out loud all the way through this jocular gem. an informed beatles fan will likely understand most of the references and appreciate the humor. i particularly enjoyed the account of a late 70s beatles reunion where the band was the opening act for peter frampton. note that this book was written before lennon's shocking death, after which "paperback writer" seemed a bit like "vaughn meader syndrome", if you know what i mean.
