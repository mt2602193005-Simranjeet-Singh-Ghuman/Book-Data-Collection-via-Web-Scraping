ORIGINAL POST 👇 Click here to watch a video review of this book on my (old) channel , From Beginning to Bookend . The truth is, I DNFd The Hobbit the first time I picked it up. All those dwarf names were confusing: Bolbo and Bungle and Dolvo and Dobby . . . or whatever. I couldn't keep them straight, so I gave up on the book. But Tolkien's iconic opening line about a hole in the ground stayed on my mind, and I eventually found myself drawn back to the book to give it another chance. Oh boy-oh-boy, am I glad I did! The Hobbit is a glorious tale spun in lyrical prose. It begs to read again and again. More importantly, it's a book that begs to be read slowly. Now that we live in an age that's always moving fast and constantly demands that we all work faster, I appreciate timeless stories like The Hobbit that encourage us to stop and slow and breathe and enjoy. If you haven't read this book yet, you're missing out on something truly special. Go read it!
