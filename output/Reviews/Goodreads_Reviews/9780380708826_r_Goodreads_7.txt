Ted Morgan, the Frenchman formerly known as the Comte de Sanche de Gramont, wrote this biography of Burroughs while wild Bill was still alive but without his cooperation. It's all here, from drug fiend in Tangiers to proud inductee into the National Academy of Arts and Sciences. What Morgan brings to Burroughs studies is a sense of his place in the twentieth century pantheon of writers, e.g. Burroughs meets Sam Beckett: Beckett: "You copied passages from my plays and pasted them into your novels! That's theft." Burroughs: "I I cut out anything and make literature from it. Words are not copyrighted."
