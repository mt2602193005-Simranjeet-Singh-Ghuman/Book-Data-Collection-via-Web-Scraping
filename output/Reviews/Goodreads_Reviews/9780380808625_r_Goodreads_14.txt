This book has probably been the most relatable and realistic book i have read in awhile. It is a true story about a teenage girl's life and all the twists and turns it has taken. She talks about a variety of issues, from her relationship with her bestfriend after she got a boyfriend to her relationship with her substitute teacher. I really thought that the author did a really good job setting the mood of this book. She really did a great job making the reader uncomfortable when talking about the relationship between Jennie and Mr. J. Overall i really liked this book and i recomend it to just about anyone.
