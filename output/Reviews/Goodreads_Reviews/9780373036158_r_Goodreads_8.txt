3 1/2 Stars ~ Two year old Lucie's parents were very late in picking her up from her day nursery, and Finnia was growing very concerned. With the day nursery's manager having left early on her own emergency and all the children's records duly locked up, Finnia had no way of searching for contact information. Finally a man shows up, tall and fair and very good looking, and claiming to be little Lucie's uncle, her father's brother. Apparently Lucie's parents have been in a terrible accident and both are in a serious way at the hospital. He's come to collect her, only when Finnia looks at his identification she voices her concern that his surname doesn't match that of his brother's. Not sure if she should release the child in his care, she says she'll have to go with them to make sure Lucie is safe. Jeger is actually relieved that Finnia insists staying with the child and agrees to her demand. With both parents in such a terrible way, and the grandparents away, Jeger convinces Finnia to stay on with him and Lucie for a few months. While Lucie adapts very well to the new living arrangements, that can't be said for either Jeger or Finnia. In The Feisty Fiancee we met Yancie's two cousins, Finnia and Astra; this is Finnia's story. After watching her mother flirt and use men, Finnia refuses to explore her sensuality with fears that she'll turn out as promiscuous as her mother. Jeger gets to her and for the first time in her life she wishes she didn't have her inhibitions. Jeger is charmed by Finnia, but is confused by the way she gets under his skin.
