Really ‘The Jasmine Farm’ but Goodreads wouldn’t let me add this edition with an English title, given that the first one added was in Italian. Again, not one of her better ones. Perhaps her later books aren’t so enjoyable for me? Anyway, the first part about the house party dragged on too long; I mean, it was only on P119 of 375 that the affair first became known to the world, and all that about gooseberries and their effect on people seemed like padding. Too many characters of no relevance too. There was little comedy until Mumsie turned up at the farm, and even that wasn’t that funny. A disappointment.
