"For Bea" a story of love and compassion Reading this book was an incredible experience of the love, caring, compassion, joys and tears and connection we can have with our pets. Imagine finding a beagle while you're out for a walk, bringing beagle home only to find out it had "Escaped" from a medical testing lab. When the author and her husband decide to keep "Bea" they never realized all the trauma the dog had been subjected to at the lab. While reading I felt I had been invited into her kitchen to experience Bea's healing and life . It is a very good read and I couldn't put it down.I learned more about testing labs, medical testing on animals than I ever thought about. Lots of thoughtful opinions, ideas,and sources to find out more about this subjext.Beagle lovers will really love the true story, too. I an so glad I read it . I enjoy the author's calming , soft words while delivering a story with a message from her heart.
