katherine swynford, you annoyed me to death but you are also one of the most well-written female characters i’ve ever read. this book wasn’t bad per say, but it was such a drag to get through. its redeeming quality was definitely its characters — hawise, blanchette, and nirac were just a few of my favorites. if you are looking for a quick easy read, DO NOT pick up this book. but if you want a long deep read that you can really savor, this might be calling your name! 3 stars
