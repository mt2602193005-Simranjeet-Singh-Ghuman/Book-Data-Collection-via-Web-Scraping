This book stretched my thinking in many ways. Some things I’ve experienced and can give a hearty recommendation and assertion to and really blessed me, and other things not so much. She gives good language to aspects of intercession I’ve not read elsewhere. It’s a book I hold as brain food and things to consider and not wholehearted recommendation, and thus the 3-star rating. It’s worth noting that Mike Bickel is spoken highly of (caveat - this book was published before his removal from ministry) and also Oral Roberts, who many accredit with the founding of the prosperity gospel movement in the US. While this is not the focus of the book, aspects of it bleed through and at best I can recommend this read to mature Christians with a discerning spirit.
