Outlaw Hearts by author Rosanne Bittner is beyond wonderful! I have been reading her books for many years. Ms. Bittner is beyond amazing as a story teller. She pulls you into her stories in such a way that you will feel as if your right there living it with the characters. With this book you get so much detail of the Old West that you can literally see every detail in your mind as you read. This is a lengthy story which just adds to how awesome it is! Just AMAZING! I found this story to flow beautifully and the characters were highly developed. If you love the Old West and a lover of wonderfully descriptive stories I highly recommend this author and book!
