I am struggling with this series. The teenage angst and stupidity over a high school crush is difficult to take seriously. It’s annoying that they use all these high and mighty sounding words and somehow Mayura, who is the victim initially, has turned into a high-level villain with better grasp of words than the two supposed lotus users. Mayura blathers about Alice never saying anything about her crush, but Mayura also never said it until right before she confessed, after a year of stalking him. I have no patience with this kind of mean girl crap. And give me a break - being afraid to say what you mean makes sense when it comes to disagreeing or feeling you will hurt someone’s feelings, but Alice just doesn’t say anything at all? None of these characters are likable so far. I find it annoying that humor is inserted randomly at serious points. I want to finish the series for the sake of completion, but it is a difficult road.
