Eight principles to have the provisions from God, a hundred fold life. 1. Want God’s best. Do not settle for less. 2. Focus on Jesus. 3. Meditate on God’s Word. 4. Make friends with the Holy Spirit. 5. Hear and promptly obey God’s voice. 6. Be careful how and what you hear. 7. Be more concerned with the eternal than the temporary. Make sure your priorities are right. 8. Let God choose for you.
