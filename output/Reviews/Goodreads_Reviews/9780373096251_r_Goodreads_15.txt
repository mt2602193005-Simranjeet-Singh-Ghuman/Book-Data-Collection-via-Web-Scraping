Each summer starting a few years back, I decided to read a few romances, and Nora Roberts tends to grab my attention. She's smart, her characters are rounded and interesting and both the settings and the jobs characters hold seem interesting and believeable. In this case, it shows it's an early story, but I can see some glimpse of the later Roberts. When I started this one, I wasn't aware it was the fourth in a series, so I read it as a stand alone and it does read like that until the very end when you find the family and see all the results of other novels. But it still, holds its ground. It's not a memorable read, but it can be read. It keeps your interest and the characters are a good fit for each other. The story is a bit out of hand, but that's the ground for Roberts to write even more out of the box stories that have catapulted her to the ceiling of the great romance writers.
