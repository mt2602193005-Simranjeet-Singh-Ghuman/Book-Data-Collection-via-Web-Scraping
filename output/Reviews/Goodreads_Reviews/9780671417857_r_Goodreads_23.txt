The Velvet Promise is the start of the Velvet Montgomery historical romance series by Jude Deveraux. Judith always thought she’d go to a nunnery later in life after being raised by her mother. But then one day, after her father loses all of his sons, he tells Judith she’s going to marry and have children instead! Enter Gavin Montgomery. It’s 1501 Tudor-era England, and this historical romance has enemies to lovers vibes, feuding families, an arranged situation, OW drama and more! There was parts of this book I liked and was invested in. Judith was an interesting, strong character. But a lot of this book was OW drama and complicated arranged marriage dynamics involving other characters/relationships that I just wasn’t as into. I’m still invested in the series and plan to binge them all. Jude Deveraux is just such an iconic author and her writing is so fun. CW: noncon/dubcon, miscarriage
