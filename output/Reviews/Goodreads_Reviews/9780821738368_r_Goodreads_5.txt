Sweet, But Needs a Little Fun… In both books and in real life, my favorite part of romance is the laughter. You know…that moment when you look into his eyes and you both laugh out of sheer delight and enjoyment. Romance should be fun and the fun is the only that's missing from this little book. I actually liked this book quite a bit. The romance moves from sweet to smoldering and back again. I absolutely loved Charley and Jess and enjoyed their romance. And that's saying a lot because Charley is kind of a mix of a Saint and a doormat. Usually, Saints don't make terribly interesting characters. But in spite of her perfection, she's sweet and under her door mat-itude lurks a backbone. As for Jess, he's far too cynical, but I enjoyed seeing Charley break down that wall of his. But it's an earnest and serious little romance and I wanted just a bit of fun mixed in with that earnestness. Jess and Charley never really seem to have fun at all. Still, a well done romance. Just needs a little lightness here and there. *ARC Provided by Netgalley for review purposes.
