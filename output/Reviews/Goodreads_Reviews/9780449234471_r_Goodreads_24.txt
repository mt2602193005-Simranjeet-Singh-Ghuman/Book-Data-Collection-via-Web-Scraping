I am a fan of that elderly widow turned CIA spy, Mrs. Pollifax. We recently took a road trip and listened to this book on CD. The reader is Barbara Rosenblat and she makes this book so much fun to listen to. This one is the 2nd book in the series so you are still getting introduced to and suprised by Mrs. Pollifax's ingenuity. The story has a good mystery and characters you enjoy. It is slightly predictable but so much fun you hardly notice. If you are OK with mysteries that are dated (this is clearly the 1970's) and before internet and cell phones, you have got to pick up a Mrs. Pollifax adventure. Listened for the second time 10 years later. Still so delightful! I love this series. It is just so much fun! I plan to re-read them all again.
