I read this lots of times when I was a child. I loved the creepy photos more than the story itself! For all its faults (and there are many) it stuck in my brain over the years, even though I couldn't remember the name. When a friend of mine recalled the book recently, we did some research and found out the title and ordered a copy each. And so, after almost 30 years, I came to read it again. It was fun for the nostalgia factor, but really it needed to be a LOT longer to properly flesh out the story. It reads more like a short story than a novel – and a rushed short story at that! Oh well, the photos are still nice and creepy!!!
