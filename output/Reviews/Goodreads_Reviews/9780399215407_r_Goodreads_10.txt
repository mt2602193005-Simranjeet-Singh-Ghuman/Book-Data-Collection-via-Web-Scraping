This is a great poetry collection! It includes poems by famous poets like Lewis Carroll, William Blake, Robert Louis Stevenson, Langston Hughes, and Robert Frost. There are also poems by other poets such as Jack Prelutsky and Nikki Giovanni. There are poems of all formats (i.e. free verse, haiku, etc.) There are a range of topics including: animals, family, loss, seasons, and nature. I found several poems that I could share with children from K-5 and they could be used for a variety of purposes. Tomie dePaolo's illustrations are wonderful as always and if the children reading this poetry collection are familiar with his other work they will love seeing his interpretation of these poems!
