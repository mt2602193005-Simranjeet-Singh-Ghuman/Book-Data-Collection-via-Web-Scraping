Alright I cave-in and gave this book an extra star due to the nightmare. He was kind of ugly yet cute enough while completely pitiful towards the end. As the little boy comes to the conclusion - how can you be scared of something that you can make cry. By this point in re-reading old childhood favorite stories I have noticed that a bunch of the books are from the same author. What a change from authors found in this time while I can understand why it may be hard for parents to find good healthy reading material for young bibliophiles. The story is basic and one that parents will enjoy reading with their children, especially for those who have unknown beasties lingering in their closets. No time is too late to start helping a child to learn how to face their fears as they start to get older.
