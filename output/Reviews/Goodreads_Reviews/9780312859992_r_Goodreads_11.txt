Not as good as I thought it was going to be. The haunting by Billy the Kid's ghost was interesting idea. It's a shame that the rest of the book didn't hold up. The plot line of Pat Garrett is really sporadic and doesn't really have a narrow theme to it. The Spanish alchemist is barely involved in the whole book, which makes me question why they bothered to mention him at all in the summary on the back cover. What surprises me is that it won two awards.
