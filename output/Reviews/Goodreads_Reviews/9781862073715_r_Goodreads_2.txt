Really loved being in this book. Ciaran Carson meanders between anecdotes, myths, legends, fairy-tales, art history, Dutch history, Irish poetry, and more. Rather than having a single through-line as such (though there are recurring characters), all the stories somehow orbit around amber, bringing us back to the precious stone before we’ve realised it was at all relevant. It’s the kind of book where I don’t remember everything having finished it, but being in it was as rewarding as collecting amber stones and gazing at each individually preserved narrative within them. Paintings and saints and mermaids I’ve read about will come to me randomly I’m sure.
