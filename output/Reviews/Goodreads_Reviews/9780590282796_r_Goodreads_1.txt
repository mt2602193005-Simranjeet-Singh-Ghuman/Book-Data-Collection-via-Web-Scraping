Kirk,David. Miss Spider’s ABC. Callaway & Kirk Company, 2000. In Miss Spider’s ABC by David Kirk, the story begins with the first letter of the alphabet and continues in sequence until the letter Z. Each page begins with a letter and a very short two word sentence or a phrase that begins on one page and then continues onto the next, such as “ J. Jumping June bugs ‘K’ kiss katydids.” Every page is supported with an illustration of an insect and the action that is described. The boldly colored and charming insects are illustrated in a way to appeal to infants, toddlers, and preschoolers without scaring them with more realistic drawings. The target audience for this book is for infants, toddlers and preschoolers. It is a board book.
