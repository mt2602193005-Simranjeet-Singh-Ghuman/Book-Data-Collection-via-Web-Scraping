As a whole the stories are witty and interesting in a way that is rare for erotica. Apart from the one suspect story that I didn't quite understand (Chester and the Conscious Cuckold)there seems to be something here for everyones taste. I found a few of them to be incredibly humerous but in a good way ie: funny ha ha not funny lame. One of my faves has to be "Jewish Sexuality, such as it is Volume 1". Also, the pointers at the end on how to write good erotica should be mandatory reading for anyone even remotely considering attempting to write about sex. Steamy, smart and wickedly funny indeed.
