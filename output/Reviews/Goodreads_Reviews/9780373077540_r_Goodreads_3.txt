Rating 3.5 I enjoyed this book, Caroline was a very strong woman whose life suddenly changed after her father died, then her first brother and then she witnessed the murder of the other, her family wiped out and finding out that her father was a book-keeper for a criminal, agreeing to testify, becoming a prisoner and being hunted, the latest being pushed into a fake marriage. But as we see throughout the book Caroline is independent and self-sufficient, her brother taught her well and after her mother's death, her father distancing himself from his kids did too. So, she isn't pleased to be forced into a marriage and being under house-arrest and proves it again and again that she needs space, she is dealing with too much and sometimes she cannot breathe. Jeff always has a plan and he worked through it to become a lawyer but when his father like figure FBI uncle asks for a favor he cannot refuse and finds himself married to a woman who doesn't seem to want his help or protection, so we see them struggle for power, along with some fun moments with Jeff's brother Mac. They soon fall into a rhythm, but keeping the chemistry between them from exploding is tough especially for Jeff. He helps Caroline through her loss and we see them grow closer. I loved the characters and enjoyed the book. Show more
