This is a creative sequel to one of the best-loved Little Golden Books, There's a Monster at the End of This Book . Playing off the charm of The Monster at the End of This Book while introducing humorous new twists, the writer-illustrator team of Jon Stone and Michael Smollin has created another memorable story. The language is noticeably updated from the first book, but I don't mind that. It's great to revisit the original classic work in this sequel.
