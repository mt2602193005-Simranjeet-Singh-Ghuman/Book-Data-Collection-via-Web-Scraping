Florina is a chef. but she is also basically a slave for her spoiled father. When she learns he could work but chooses not to and therefore she has been working for the years since her mothers death as a cook at a local hotel to pay the bills. also she does all the cooking and cleaning at home for her verbally abusive lazy father. Finally she gets fed up over it and gets a new job that she can live there and gets paid more as well. If she wasn’t such a doormat she would have left earlier. Sir William is a widower with a young child, Pauline. He is engaged to the most unsuitable woman, Wanda. Wanda loves parties, the nightlife of London and money. She must think of William as a project, but will he give up his newly renovated quiet country house for the bright life of the city? Wanda hates Pauline, doesn’t want children, and wants to put her in a boarding school. Wanda dislikes the young beautiful new cook, Florina and repeatedly tells her that as soon as she is married Florina will be chucked out. Wanda wants to get rid of Nanny as well. In other words she wants zero competition. A good clean read. slice of life style romance.
