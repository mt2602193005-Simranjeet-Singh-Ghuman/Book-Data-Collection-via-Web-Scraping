The second instalment in the War Gods story, following the adventures of Bahzell Bahnakson, Champion of Tomanak. Bahzell and his friend Barndark find themselves bringing truth of The Rage back to the Hradani people, one of the four races of the world. Adventures with Bahzell are never boring though, and this second book proves that point with Bahzell and Brandark teaching a pompous young lord the true way of Tomanak before they all create the first Hradani chapter.
