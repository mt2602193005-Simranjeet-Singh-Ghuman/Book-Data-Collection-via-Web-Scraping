A very clever book equally delightful to adults and children. The long-awaited baby—a daughter— is born to the king and queen. A wicked aunt throws some powder into the christening water and chants an incantation that the princess would be "light of spirit and light of body." She has no weight and she is unable to cry. MacDonald puns with all the meanings of light and all the meanings of gravity. This fairy-tale makes the reader think about things we take for granted. "Perhaps the best thing for the princess would have been to fall in love. But how a princess who had no gravity could fall into anything is a difficulty."
