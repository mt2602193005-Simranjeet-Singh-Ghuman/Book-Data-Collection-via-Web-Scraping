This was a nice ending to the series. Even though the heroine drove me mad thru most of the book, I still enjoyed it. She made me drop it a star. She certainly had reasons to distrust men but it just got tiresome after awhile. However, things happened to make her change her mind, dark, tragic things but it woke her up to Lucien's true nature. Lucien learned lessons too, the hard way. However, it of course, all turned out well in the end.
