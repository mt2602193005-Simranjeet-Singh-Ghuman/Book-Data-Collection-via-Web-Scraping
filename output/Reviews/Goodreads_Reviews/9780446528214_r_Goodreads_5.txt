I remember picking this book up when I was sixteen or so and reading the entire thing while I waited for my dad to get fitted for a suit. I was in tears by the end, I absolutely love this book, one of my all time favorites and I wish it was more widely available. It even got me to read Hemingway, which is nothing short of effing miraculous. There's just something I really adore about the sea as both an atmosphere and a character, which likely had a lot to do with how much I loved this, but its philosophical aspect appealed to me as well, especially in contrast with the unadorned Old Man and the Sea, of which this book is a sort of spin-off. Both emotional and, in places, humorous. If you can find it, read it.
