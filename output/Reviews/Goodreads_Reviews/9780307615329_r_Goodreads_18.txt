Diana Lynch is beautiful and rich and seemingly has it all. Why then is she so unhappy? Trixie and Honey reach out to a lonely girl and find a mystery in the form of a long-lost relative. Con man in disguise or is Trixie seeing things again? It's hard to say (maybe she should quit getting clunked over the head...ah the life of a mystery series heroine...) Another great Trixie story, involving Halloween, a new club member, and a fair bit of danger. Trixie is on the case once again. Thankfully so are the rest of the BWGs!
