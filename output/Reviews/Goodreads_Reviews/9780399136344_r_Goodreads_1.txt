This book was a gift. Not my normal read, however I found it interesting, and being Catholic Lite (C of E) I am always interested in books that include Rome and the church. Story: An Archbishop is killed, and then it is discovered he might have performed miracles. Should he be considered a Saint? A Priest, who was never a fan of the Archbishop was assigned to dig deep into his life, he is to find anything to prove or disprove the Sainthood, and he is to make sure that there are no "smoking guns" that Rome would be embarrassed about. Father Laurence McAuliffe takes the task seriously, and delves into John McGlynn's life. His travels take him all over Europe and interviews with Mossad! What Father McAuliffe finds out, about the Archbishop Johnny whom he did not like, surprises him, but sainthood is never granted over night!
