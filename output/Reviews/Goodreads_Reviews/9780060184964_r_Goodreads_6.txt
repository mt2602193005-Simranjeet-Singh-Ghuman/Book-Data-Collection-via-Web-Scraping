The spiritual forefather of Charles Bukowski. You can directly see the influence in his work. I do find Charles Bukowski's prose to be a bit less graceful however it has a somewhat sharper cleverness. Fante on the other hand tends to be more sentimental in a way that's more piercing. This collection is a smattering from his various books and a collection of letters. They vary in subject matter as a sampling of his work I think the selections are pretty good. Tantalizing without bordering into making you sick of his style. If you're a big Charles Bukowski fan it's worth taking a look at.
