I got this book in Kuala Lumpur from the Kinokuyia bookstore. I read the series of short stories over several years and have finally finished it now in Cape Town. This is a great collection as it provides an excellent summary of the lives and politics and personal histories of the authors included in the collection. Most of them were born at the turn of the century or in the 1920’s thus lived through the brutal years of war. Many were very political and their writings include descriptions of lives of poverty and hardship including experiences of destruction and devastating tragedy of the war years. In the summary of the author’s lives it is noted that so many committed suicide. It is a very diverse selection of stories and captures a range of cultural and historical images and perspectives including the lives of prisoners on death row, and a woman confronting the revelations found within her dead mother’s diary. I enjoyed these selections very much and highly recommend it.
