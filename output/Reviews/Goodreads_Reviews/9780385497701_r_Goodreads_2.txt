This is a very long compendium of facts about London that is hard for me to classify. It is not a history of London; it is vaguely in chronological order but not really. It is topical, sort of but even the topics are spaced out so the same themes are repeated throughout the book. It was a book best read over a long time, I did that, dipping in and out of it over a period of seven months. I think those who live in London and are very familiar with it will find this more enjoyable than those like me, I really wish there were some maps. The illustrations were good, and I thought the book was better at the end than at the beginning. I really enjoyed the essay at the end which gave suggestions for further reading on the topics covered in the book.
