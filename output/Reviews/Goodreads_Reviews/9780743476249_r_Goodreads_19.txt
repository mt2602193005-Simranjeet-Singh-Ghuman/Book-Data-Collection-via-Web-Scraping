Jonquinette is a sweet, nervous, and shy girl. She is not good at talking to men nor being assertive. She seeks the help of a therapist after waking up several times in slutty clothing, and with her private parts aching as if she has had sex, but she has no memory of having sex. Jonquinette has an alter ego named Jude. Jude is a bad girl. Sassy, assertive, and a bit crazy. Jude does all the things that Jonquinette cannot do. We later learn that Jude was created by Jonquinette to deal with the sexual abuse that her father was inflicting on her. I love the way Zane writes. This book was erotic without being overly sexed, and had a great plot.
