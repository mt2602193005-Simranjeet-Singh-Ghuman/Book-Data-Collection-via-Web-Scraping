Novel equivalent of "light entertainment" TV or "easy listening" music, with nothing exciting/dramatic happening, but with lots of pleasant characters bumbling around being pleasant to each other and everything ending neatly/happily . Very well written/observed and with well-drawn characters/dialogue and got increasingly humorous (but not too humorous!), so that if you like this kind of thing you might easily give it 4 or 5 stars, but for me it topped out at 3.5 rounded down.
