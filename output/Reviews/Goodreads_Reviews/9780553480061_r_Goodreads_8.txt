Caroline’s Halloween Spell Yep, it’s Halloween - for the THIRD time in 33 books! Liz can’t decide what she wants to be, but everyone else shares - except Caroline who tells Liz to mind her own business. This sets off Jess and other kids, teasing Caroline about her red hair, and she vows revenge. Everyone in the class has to read a Halloween book and give a report on it, and Liz chooses one on witches. She starts noticing behaviours of Caroline’s that match the witches in the book - Caroline predicts a spooky neighbour changes her door decoration to a ghost, the twins spy on her with a broom at home, and stirring a big pot. They even run into her at the mall after buying their costumes (Liz is a detective and Jess a cheerleader 👏🏻) and she’s bought a lizard (for a spell?). They catch her in the spooky neighbour’s yard with her cat (getting lessons from another witch with her familiar?). They’re convinced and so are their friends. Then Caroline catches them spying and vows Liz will be sorry! The twins come up with a way to defeat this witch, and at school on Halloween, they put it into action when Caroline - openly and brazenly dressed as a witch, starts chanting ‘double, double, toil and trouble...’ they throw water on her! But she does not melt. The whole thing was a misunderstanding if you can believe it! 😱😱 They make up and all go trick or treating. My rating - 4/10 - look, I hate reading about kids being ganged up on. But Caroline is a pain who even tells Ned to mind his own business. Not a fan 🤷🏼‍♀️
