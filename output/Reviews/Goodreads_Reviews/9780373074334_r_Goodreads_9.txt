This book follows the normal Nora Roberts mode. Girl meets boy, either old flame or new guy. Problems and then happy ending. This one is about Venessa, a pianist who has travels all over the world and is very famous. She comes home to try and heal old wounds with her mother and other things that she isn't even sure of herself. She meets up with an old flame and things go from there. If you are a fan of Ms Roberts you know that the path of love is never even or easy but fun to follow as she writes it.
