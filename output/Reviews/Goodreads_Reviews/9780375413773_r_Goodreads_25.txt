To Begin the World Anew was, above anything else, a 149 page testimony to the old adage of ' using a twenty five cent word when a five cent word would do '. Now, there may have been, perhaps, 8-10 pages throughout the entirety of the book when Bernard Bailyn either forgot to use his thesaurus or the editor took pity on the future readers of Bailyn's piece. In addition to the author's ENDLESS attempts to sound like the smartest person on campus, I found the material lacking in depth, on numerous levels. Bailyn fails to go into any depth on key figures in the construction of the Constitution, that is beyond their names ( well some of them at least ). He glosses over the impact America was having on Europe. Mind you, these are failures that the author specifically donated chapters to !
