6-stars [NOTE: I like to provide a 6-star rating to a book when it has been able to keep me in a state of amazement and awe almost continuously for around 80% of its content.] This was such a super-reread after nineteen years!! I think it may be amongst the best coming-of-age stories ever written in the modern times. A novella of about 140 pages. What it brought out in me was all the nostalgia (and tears: yes, never a book has brought out tears from within my self!) of my younger days with my close circle of friends, and some parts from my school days. Reading the entire story was such a pleasure, indeed!! I would only read a few pages daily, like one or two scenes, and keep reminiscing about that and relating it to my own warm memories with friends, throughout that day, sometimes even two. Steven King does such an awesome job in his writing in this book. It is something that I was not able to appreciate back in 2001, due to my lack of good understanding of his prose. But now here, he has proven himself to be a master of the coming-of-age stories. (I first heard this from Mike, whom I recently started listening to about half a year ago. One can find his BookTube blogs here if interested. He is good. Do check it out. :) King can totally get into the mindset of kids, which he does fantastically here. Absolutely no doubt about that! The four kid characters, aged around 12, are so well described. The story is a first-person narrative from one of them, who is now older, but writing his memoir. Solid prose by King here, some even better than in many of his other books. Another amazing thing about the story is, it includes two sub-stories written by the narrating character (since he is a writer) and interwoven within the story-line, which stretched my thinking further, if I had to get into King's mind, wondering the way he must have had to do that! Wow!! And for all this, I would consider it a masterpiece. Very much recommended to anyone who loves this genre. :D
