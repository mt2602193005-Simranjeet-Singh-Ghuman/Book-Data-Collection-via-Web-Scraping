Elegant lived in HK around 1996-7 (as did I) and clearly wanted to tell us all about Hong Kong as it was previosuly, as it was in 1996 when different factions were courting Beijing, particularly the rich and unscrupulous, and how the Handover in 1997 will turn out. Will the Chinese honour the 50 year promise of rule of law? It turns out Elegant was right on the money in predicting a total takeover within years. On top of that clearly thought out structure he builds a less than clearly thought out romance between Tibetan-English Hong Kong born Robbie and the clumsily named Lucretia, an American divorcee who is obsessed with being divorced from a husband she despised. The characters are stereotypes chosen to represent the political dynamics. One star for the story, 4 stars say for my special interest in his “essay” (for that is what it is) so 3 stars in all. But I can understand others reading for the story, for it does purport to be a novel, settling on 2 or even 1 star I noticed. Pity, because Elegant is an award winning writer: I’ll give his China trilogy a go. It can’t be ad bad as this. Show more
