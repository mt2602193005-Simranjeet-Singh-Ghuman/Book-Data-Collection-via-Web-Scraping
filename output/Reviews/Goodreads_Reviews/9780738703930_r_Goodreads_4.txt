I'm a new witch trying to learn all I can about witchcraft and Wicca, and so far this is my favorite book on witchcraft. It gets down to the basics in a non complicated way. It's a very relaxing yet inspiring book to read with excellent meditations and helpful information on the elements, elementals, color magick, tools, spells, seasons, holidays, crystals, candles, and just magick in general. The author does an AMAZING job at explaining the Wiccan rede, the threefold law, and the consequences magick can have! She walks the reader through everything slowly and patiently. Even though this book is very informative, you'll never feel overwhelmed with information or like there's too much to take in. If you are new to the craft , this is the book for you. Teenager or not, I highly recommend. I truly feel this book has helped me connect to the elements more, and helped with my understanding of the craft. That being said, the book is not without its problems. The main one is that the author talks down to the teenagers the book is written for. She keeps reiterating that movies like Sabrina the teenage witch are fictional, and acts like teenagers are dumb, and like the reader can't tell fiction from reality. She likes to poke fun at us and say things like "that's fictional, don't you want to know the truth? The truth then." I'm 18, not 8, I KNOW lighting won't shoot from my fingers. it's a tad insulting, but teenagers are already used to older talking to us like that so it's not a big deal. TLDR She's a mom and you can tell. other than that? FIVE STARS ✨
