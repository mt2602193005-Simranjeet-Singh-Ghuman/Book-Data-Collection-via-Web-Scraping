I love this series. This is the second of the Karl Alberg series and it does not disappoint. This story finds our main character trying to solve the mystery of an unidentified woman found in a clearing. With little to go on, Alberg gets creative in his approach and of course, gets his man in the end. You may be wondering if he also gets his woman, if you've read the first in the series. Well, that is for you to find out. Definitely worth the read. Excellent series.
