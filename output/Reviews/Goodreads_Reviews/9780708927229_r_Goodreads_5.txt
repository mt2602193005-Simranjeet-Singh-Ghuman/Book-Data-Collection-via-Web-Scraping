I really like this book — like several others by Essie Summers, the heroine, Monique Belfield, is looking for family. Her grandfather had left home as a young man and changed his name, but while Monique is on a buying trip for the antique business she works for, she visits Port Beauchamp, and realizes that her grandfather had anglicized his name. When an aunt leaves her a legacy "to bring you joy" and leave her responsibilities to her stepfather and half-siblings, she is determined to return to Port Beauchamp and help her two great-aunts (who don't know who she really is) turn it into a living museum. But when Edouard Fergusson discovers her there, he seems to be furious, despite a brief previous acquaintance (and attraction). What has changed? and will Monique ever be able to reveal her true identity? Excellent evocation of what is to me an exotic location (New Zealand). Recommended.
