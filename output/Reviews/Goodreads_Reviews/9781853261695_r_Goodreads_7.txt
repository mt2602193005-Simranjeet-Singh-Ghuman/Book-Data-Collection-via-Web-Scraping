Both Rip Van Winkle and The Legend of Sleepy Hollow are delightful short stories. They are well written and light-hearted. Overall I prefer Sleepy Hollow . It's more solid and, though the descriptions are overlong, they are rather more beautiful than anything in Rip Van Winkle. I also like that they are morally ambiguous, as in, they don't try to teach you anything, just entertain. Reading up on Irving I learned that he is considered the first writer of the United States, as in the first one to make a living from his pen. But in light of this information I have to agree with Edgar Allan Poe, who wrote of him "Irving is much over-rated, and a nice distinction might be drawn between his just and his surreptitious and adventitious reputation—between what is due to the pioneer solely, and what to the writer." Sorry Irving, you've been Edgar Allan Poewned.
