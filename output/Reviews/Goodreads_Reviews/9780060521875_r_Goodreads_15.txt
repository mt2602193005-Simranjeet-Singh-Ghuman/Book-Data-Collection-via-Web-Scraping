Such a great read. I read it in 2 days, I literally couldn't put it down. Set in England in the early 80's, 14 year olds Gemma and Tar run away from home and end up in a squat with other runaways. They are introduced to heroin aka "junk" and become rapidly addicted despite telling themselves they can quit at any time they want. Things rapidly escalate and we see the devastating effects of drugs on their individual lives. Think Trainspotting for teenagers. Loved it.
