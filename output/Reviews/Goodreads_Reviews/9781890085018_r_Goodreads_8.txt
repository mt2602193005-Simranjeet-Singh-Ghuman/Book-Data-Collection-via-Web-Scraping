Not a bad book however a lot of the stuff you can find on LiChess. I will say it is nice that it’s all organized, makes looking for a concept easy. Tao Of Chess in my opinion is a better beginner to intermediate book, but this one does get more specific with certain subjects such as the opening, middle game and tactical motifs, and EndGame strategies. Just like the Tao of Chess this is a book that I’d have to revisit over and over again to better understand and internalize the lessons. All and all not a bad book, great supplementary material to the Tao of chess.
