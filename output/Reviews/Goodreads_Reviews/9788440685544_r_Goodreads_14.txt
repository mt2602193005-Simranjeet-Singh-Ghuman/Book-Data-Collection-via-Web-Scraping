Sigh... This was good, but a very distant third to The Witching Hour and Lasher . There was nothing really wrong with it. It just didn't grab me like the first book. Except I couldn't quite square the history of the Taltos with Lasher's powers in the first book. Why did Lasher have all these abilities that the rest of the Taltos didn't have? Maybe I missed something. I liked Taltos more toward the very end. I enjoyed the world-building narration of the Taltos' history, but even still it wasn't quite the richness of the witches history from The Witching Hour .
