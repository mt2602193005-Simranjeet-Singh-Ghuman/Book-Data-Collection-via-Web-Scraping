Sir Waldo Hawkridge and Miss Trent are the main characters in this romantic comedy. The completely spoilt Tiffany Wield is well written. She is indeed a buffleheaded clunch. There is nothing like a GH to bring a smile to ones face and several chuckles. Waldo a Corinthian of the highest calibre inherits the rundown Broom Hall in the West Riding where he meets gentile governess Miss Trent. He is there to create a school for Waldo’s wretched brats. Along the way he finds romance. An enjoyable Regency read.
