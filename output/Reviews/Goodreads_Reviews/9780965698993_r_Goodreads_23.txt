An experimental sort of writing style, that switches between a stream of consciousness style third-person narration and absurdist kind of conversations. Having read Days of Abandonment prior to reading this, I felt that Nicola and Olga were going through a similar sort of crisis. Only that, one had a support system, the other did not; one was free from maternal responsibilities, the other was unflinchingly tied to it; one's partner had chosen to cheat on them, the other's had only wanted some "space". But ultimately both become able to pull themself out of the abyss that the sudden declaration of marital/relationship separation had brought upon them. And in the end, both realise that life is so much more than a man's, even if that man is the centre of their world, appreciation of them.
