Awards: N/A Grade levels: k-6 Summary: Designed for beginners and experienced collectors alike, these field guides make identification of individual specimens sure, simple and straightforward. Each entry has a full color illustration as well as color-coded bands that provide at-a-glance facts for quick reference. Easy to use and beautiful to look at, this series is an invaluable resource for every collector. Review: I like that this book is for beginners and experienced collectors because it can be used for multiple different grades and different activities within the class. Each page is full of color and descriptions to help young students with identification. 2-3 uses: (1) students can make their own fossil on paper and use the book as a layout of what else to add to their drawings, (2) teacher can bring in supplies to bring the students drawings to life and do a lesson on how to make fossils using the materials brought in.
