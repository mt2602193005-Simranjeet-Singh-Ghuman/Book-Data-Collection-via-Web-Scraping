This is my #1 recommended adoption book. I'm pretty sure it is out of print which is heartbreaking. The book goes back and forth between an adoptive mom and a birth mom's stories. The birth mother's story was very touching and very helpful in preparing me to watch the heartache a birth mom goes through. Be prepared to bawl if you ever get the opportunity to read this book. It is also a great book to help in understanding the depths of the atonement. My only complaint being that the stories weren't related. I would have loved to hear the story of the family who adopted that particular birth mom's baby.
