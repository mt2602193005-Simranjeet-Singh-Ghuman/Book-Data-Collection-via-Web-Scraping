The first time I read this book I was already a Nora Roberts fan, but had not read any J. D. Robb books. I normally don't get into futuristic or murder mysteries much. After reading this book I was hooked. I began aquiring all the JD books and promptly turned my daughter onto them. We are now in the process of completing her collection, as I already own them all. I Would recommend this series (...in death) to anyone who likes a) a good 'who done it' b) a REALLY good love story c) ongoing series stories check them out. You won't be sorry.
