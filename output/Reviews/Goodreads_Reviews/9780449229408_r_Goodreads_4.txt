Another nonpareil from Heyer, The Nonesuch, is as her readers expect from her, light and frothy, but founded on a solid romance. The two lovers are Sir Waldo Hawkridge, the society whip, and Miss Trent, a well bred governess. Each brings beauty and common sense to the match. There are side pairings as well, Sir Waldo's young protege and cousin, Julian Lindeth, and the gorgeous, but spoiled, Miss Wield. This comedy of misunderstandings, and near misses in a carriage and in marriage by inexperienced drivers, is never dull and a ton of fun.
