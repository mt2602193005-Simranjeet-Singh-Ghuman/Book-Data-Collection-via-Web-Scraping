The author did an outstanding job showing how much this couple loved each other, but they couldn’t figure it out themselves until the very end. It’s extremely frustrating when there are stupid misunderstandings that play out, but it worked. When there is such a huge lack of communication between a couple, it’s a hard sell to think this just goes away... Fortunately, this couple has a history and love for each other that is well-developed and displayed - which won me over for the most part.
