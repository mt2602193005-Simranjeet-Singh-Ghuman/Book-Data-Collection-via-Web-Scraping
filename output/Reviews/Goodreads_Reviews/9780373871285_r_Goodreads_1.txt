A Romantic Christmassy story that will warm your heart with the spirit of Christmas. Lenora Worth this is one of your best books as far as this reader is concerned. It would make a great Hallmark Christmas movie. This book was written in the year 2000 but if you haven’t read it I highly recommend it. If you have already read it years ago than you might want to reread it again this year. So grab a cup of tea or hot chocolate and snuggle up with One Golden Christmas.
