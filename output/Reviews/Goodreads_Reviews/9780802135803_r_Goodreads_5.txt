I loved the prose of this book. I did have to just power through some of the very disturbing content though. It was very disturbing and drew me into the very darkest corners of humanity. It was like watching a snuff film. I almost didn’t finish it because the first 40 pages were too intense for me but something drew me back a couple days later to finish it. I’m not used to shock literature ( if that’s what you call this?) but I also felt there was some heart in the characters and the writing. I didn’t understand any of the references cause I didn’t grow up in the 90’s but it was pretty clear to me. This is my second book from Cooper and I’m so excited to read more.
