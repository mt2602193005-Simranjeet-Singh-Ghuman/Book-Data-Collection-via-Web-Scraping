Ok, please don't judge me! We collect books and when we stumbled onto this pristine hardcover copy it seemed initially like something of interest. Reading the inner cover I discovered it was German erotica written in 1927/28 just at the point of transition of the Weimar Republic to Nazi Germany. I thought it might provide some insight to the culture of the time. However, you can think of this book as the 50 Shades of Gray in the 1920s - but probably even less restrictive. Yes, some of the characters weakly represent old and new Germany at the time but most of the book is very sexually explicit. I couldn't really see the overall point other than the wealthy decadence of the 1920s in Europe. In fact, the situations start to become comical as the story progresses. Much of the material would be considered very problematic today. Personally, I wouldn't recommend this book to anyone unless they were doing research on the rise of National Socialism and German culture in the 1920s. WARNING - if you're squeamish at all about sexual imagery, this book is NOT for you. 2.5 stars upped to 3 for historical curiosity.
