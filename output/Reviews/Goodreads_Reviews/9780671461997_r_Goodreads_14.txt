When I opened this book, a plane ticket came fluttering out. About 15 years ago, I had the opportunity to tour The Anne Frank House. What this diary doesn’t talk about is the lack of natural light. My gosh…the sense of doom in the hiding place. It was so oppressive that almost immediately upon entering, I wanted to leave. Yet Anne’s diary is so hopeful. This diary is very well done. None of the entries are overly long, and Anne isn’t trying to hit some arbitrary word count. The entries also have plenty of dialogue, and with 8 people in hiding, there are many different personalities. Hope can light up the darkest of times.
