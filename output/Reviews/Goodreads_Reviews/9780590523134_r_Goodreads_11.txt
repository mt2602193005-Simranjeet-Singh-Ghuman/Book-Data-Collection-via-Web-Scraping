Maybe it's because I read so many BSC books in a row, but I loved this book so much. I feel like the structure of the series (and the never ending repeat of the same year) have never allowed for Kristy to heal or move on from her father leaving her family when she was a kid. This book finally closes that hole a little bit, which was so satisfying. And I was so satisfied with Kristy's conclusion on her feelings about her father, because it's so realistic, and so earned for Patrick. Surprisingly, so, so, well done.
