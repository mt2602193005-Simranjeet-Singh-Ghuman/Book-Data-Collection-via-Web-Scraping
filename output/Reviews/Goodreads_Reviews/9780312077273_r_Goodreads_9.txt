This was a book club choice because it came in a library kit and was set in St. Louis. Betsey is growing up in the midst of integration, when black students were bused to white suburban schools, and that forced mixing for her is very hard. She is trying to figure out her identity as a black woman, about self, love, and her place in the world. A quote I loved from Regina, p. 136: "Betsey, honey, that's called loneliness. You're gonna be lonely sometimes, sweetie. Cause you are special. Your mama's not making that up. You are different and it's not the color of your skin, either. You have a good time the way nobody else can. There is no such thing as ordinary, Betsey. Nobody's ordinary. Each one of us is special and it's the coming together of alla that that makes the world so fine."
