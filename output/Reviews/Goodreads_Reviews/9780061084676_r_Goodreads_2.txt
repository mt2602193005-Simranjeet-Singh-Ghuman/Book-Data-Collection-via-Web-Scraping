After her father's recent death in the line of duty, Officer Kristen Ford finds herself on forced personal leave, bored out of her mind, when a strange small old women forces her way into her home, telling her that she was born in the wrong time and her soul mate needed her. Trying to placate the woman, Kristen agrees to go with her - and finds herself suddenly back in 1889 Nevada, in the middle of a train hold up. Her mission, before she can return home, is to find her soulmate and "guide him to a life of morality". Her first task is to figure out who he is. This was a very entertaining book to read on a hot summer afternoon. I won a copy form Goodreads.com.
