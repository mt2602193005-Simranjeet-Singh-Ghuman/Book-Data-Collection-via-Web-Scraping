I DID NOT FINISH THIS BOOK. As somebody that absolutely loved CwG 1-3, I was eager to read Tomorrow's God. Unfortunately, there is really not much that is new in this book. I feel like NDW elaborated on points that just didn't need elaborating on. I got through 80 pages, but this book feels almost as if a band recorded the same album twice, but the second album had worse production quality and not as good songs. I would recommend to readers that they read Conversations with God 1-3 and not this one.
