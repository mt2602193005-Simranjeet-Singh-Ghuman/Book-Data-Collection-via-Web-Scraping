This is the history of black hair in the US. It is very academic and sometimes dry. I'd recommend this as a resource for school or research but not for enjoyment. If you're early in your personal exploration of black history this has some good information in it to help keep you anti-racist when it comes to black hair but if you're deeper into your journey you're probably aware of most if not all this book has to give. For personal learning I'd recommend a shorter, visual format. Like youtube or tiktok.
