This is an important book detailing the amoral pursuit of success by IBM throughout the Nazi regime. My ranking of it is not to diminish its importance, for that it deserves full credit. I only rank it as a 4/5 for how it reads. The structure of the book is a thesis followed by hundreds of pages of undeniable proof--and to prove such a thesis, such proof is necessary. After reading, it certainly makes me reconsider any use of IBM products (however possible that is in such a corporately-powered world), but it also has me reflect on how unbridled corporations may respond to atrocity. The word "salivate" comes to mind.
