Heroine Michele discovers her on-again, off-again boyfriend Kevin is marrying another woman. Smitten hero Tyler rushes to console Michele. They agree to go to the wedding with each other and pretend that they are an item. Will Tyler finally get his chance with the lady he has loved for years? I really liked Tyler, he was a great hero. Thoughtful, sexy, handsome and rich, he has been crazy about Michele for ten years and finally has an opportunity to win over the lady he has loved. I didn't like Michele as much, though. There were times when she was too bad-tempered and sarcastic towards Tyler. (She resented him because he was rich.) And I couldn't figure out why she wasted so much of her time throughout the years on her boring boyfriend, Kevin. But she eventually fell for Tyler and realized what a wonderful person he was. An okay read by Miranda Lee that could have been better with a nicer heroine.
