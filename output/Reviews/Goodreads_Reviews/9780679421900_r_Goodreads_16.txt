This book was so interesting! I didn't know much at all about Hershey or Mars' founders, history, rivalry, etc. before reading. It took me a little while to get through, since it was extremely factual, but I enjoyed it. Sometimes it jumped around a bit, I wish it was more chronological. Overall, very well-done, though. The amount of work the author put into getting any information or interviews was amazing. I had no idea how secretive chocolate/candy companies are, how much espionage goes on, etc. It was like reading a real-life Willy Wonka story sometimes.
