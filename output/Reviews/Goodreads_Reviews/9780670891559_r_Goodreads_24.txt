What a beautiful book. I absolutely love the writing style of this author, the way he romanticizes all things food, kitchen, chef, etc. Reading this book is like listening to live music while watching professional dancing, an absolute immersion of your senses. I could picture the people, the places, the food, and imagine the smell, taste, and feel of it all in my mind. Truly fantastic writing, a thing of art and beauty in itself. I did not research any description of this book prior to reading it, so it came as a fantastic surprise that he profiled Michael Symon, his restaurant and crew, and Thomas Keller and the infamous, incomparable, French Laundry. It’s so unfortunate that it’s now intrinsically linked to the idiot hypocritical Governor Gavin Newsom flouting his own Covid rules, but I digress. I was also unfamiliar with the CMC exam, certification, etc., and found it very interesting to learn about. I also loved The Soul of a Chef by this author, and plan to read his third book, The Reach of a Chef, very soon.
