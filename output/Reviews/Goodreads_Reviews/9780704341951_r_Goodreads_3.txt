A realistic look of an unusual life from a fictional character. The entire time I was reading this book I thought Mei-Li was a real person. Even after learning the work is fiction- I still feel like I was transported in time and space to witness a complicated and yet very real life. The author seemed to capture the humanity of the dark side of American History; poverty, hunger, race, religion, immigration, disaster, betrayal, mental illness...the list goes on an on and as if a naturally unfolding life of the main character Mei-Ali. It left me full and fulfilled as well as touched. Incredibly written to make you believe it is a true story.
