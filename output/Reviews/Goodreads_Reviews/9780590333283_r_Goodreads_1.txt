UPDATE 6/1/2014: Was able to find an old used copy on Amazon.com and it arrived yesterday. I happily read then and finished it up today. Yes, it was still as good as it ever was...a classic teen love story!!! Still relevant now as it was then. I'm thrilled to have gotten a copy to read, and to pass to my teenage daughters to read This is a great teen/YA romance fiction. I loved the storyline about the good girl and the "bad" boy! One of those books from my "teen shelf" that I wish I still had and could read for the 10th time. A definite feel good book!! It all started with a Chemistry experiment gone wrong... Fate steps in as Pam gets involved with trouble-maker Bud O'Connor. Bud O'Connor with the thick dark hair and brown eyes. Bud O'Connor who may be facing suspension from school. Her parents don't approve. Her friends don't approve. Her reputation is on the line. Gossip swarms around them, but Pam is still convinced that Bud has a good heart underneath his devilish ways. Is she right? This is a sweet young adult romance that will leave a definite smile on your face.
