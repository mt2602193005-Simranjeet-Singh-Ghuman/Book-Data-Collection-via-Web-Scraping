I made it through Full House only because I remembered only having mildly liked it back then, but I'd loved this one, and it's making me cringe so hard now. He paid $500 for her. And that's where I quit. I really question my past self tbh. I'm dropping my ratings on the series and giving them to my mom who might still like them. *gets out the brain bleach*
