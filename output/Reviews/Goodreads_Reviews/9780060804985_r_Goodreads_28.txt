Mr Middleton and his wife Catherine host Lilian, stepmother to Denis and Daphne, two funny teens with unique personalities of their own. Together they mingle with villagers through various gatherings and soon enough, everyone falls in love. When Angela Thirkell is at her best, she’s truly one of my favourite authors. She’s so funny. Sadly, a lot of her books are fine but not great. I think it comes from having lines that make me laugh out loud in between long stretches of chapters with long conversation between unpleasant characters who lack any kind of spark. Before Lunch was mostly boring with flashes of genius here and there. One of the characters only eats a specific type of bread, a girl cries and because she uses a man’s shoulder for two seconds is engaged, a dog is making a fool of its master and a composer is vainly trying to write a ballet amidst bouts of illness. This is the kind of jolly eccentricity I adore so I so wish the writing had been tighter, the book less unnecessarily long and the fun more consistent. It’s so frustrating to read Angela Thirkell! She’s just short of my favourite author ever, every single time.
