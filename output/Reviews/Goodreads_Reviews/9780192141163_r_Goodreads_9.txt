Anthologies/collections of short stories are a great way to sample different authors and decide whether their style of writing inspires the reader to seek out full length offerings. Unfortunately, none of these inspired me. Those of so-called classic authors were the worst! I should have heeded my instinct to avoid any book that has been nominated/won an award or has a recommendation from a pretentious academic publication. The length of time it took me to wade through to the end shows that it was definitely not to my liking!!
