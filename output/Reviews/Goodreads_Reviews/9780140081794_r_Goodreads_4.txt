A classic of the genre: funny and wit with a very clever humour. An interesting and entertaining book on cultural diversity and British uniqueness. However, having being published decades ago it's a bit outdated and sometimes, as an expat in London, it makes me difficult to relate to it. Highly recommended to be read along with Angela Kiss's "How to be an alien in England", which provides an equally interesting and sharply hilarious, yet updated look at the English character.
