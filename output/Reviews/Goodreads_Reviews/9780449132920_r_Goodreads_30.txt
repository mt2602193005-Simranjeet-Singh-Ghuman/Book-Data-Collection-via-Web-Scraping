I guess I'm getting tired of these. I still like the Travis McGee character a lot, though he's at his best in warm tropical climes and this one takes place in Chicago. In this one he pushes his egoism about his deep knowledge of all that is woman a bit too far. And the writing in this one is overwrought. Everything gets analyzed to the nth degree; it's tiring. The mystery is OK and the unravelling of it interesting, but the pace lagged a bit in the middle and the ending was forced. All in all not one of MacDonald's better efforts. I'm going to put the rest of these on hold for awhile a look for a new series I'll like a lot more. Another Bosch, maybe.
