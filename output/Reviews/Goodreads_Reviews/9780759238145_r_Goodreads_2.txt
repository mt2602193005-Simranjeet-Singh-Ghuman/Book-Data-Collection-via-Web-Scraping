"Heart of Stone" is the story of Stephanie and Brock. An utter trainwreck..and I loved it. If you love absolute manwhore and tomcatty heroes, who glide in with their mistress, only to see the heroine and proposition her instead- then slowly chase the heroine and disappear for months- make her fall MADLY in love with him while hurting her by pretending to be with his mistress, so much so that she spends months in depression losing weight, wallowing in self pity and trying to have accidents- this would be your book. A mess, filled with angst, jealousy and crazy characters that need therapy. SWE 3/5
