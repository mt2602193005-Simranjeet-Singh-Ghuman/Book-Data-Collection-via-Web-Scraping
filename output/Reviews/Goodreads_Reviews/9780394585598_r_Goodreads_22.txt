This is a very unusual biography, my Book is about 531 pages of text, and half of it covers the Hiss-Chambers HUAC hearing of 1948 and the Hiss perjury trial of 1950. About 15 percent covers Chambers life 1951-1960. Another 15 percent details Chambers life up to age 32 when he became a Soviet spy. The remainder covers 1933-1948 when Chambers was a spy, a defector, and a journalist at Time Magazine. The focus is almost entirely on Chamber's communist spying and anti-communist activities. And his conflict with Hiss. There's little about the man himself. Very few quotes from "Witness" or from his letters or papers. It describes how Chambers became a spy, what he spied on, and why he "Defected". And later came forward to accuse Hiss and battle him in the HUAC hearing and trial. Its not a "Chamber went here and did this" biography. Or an analysis of Chamber's character and thoughts, and actions. Nor does it go into detail about his marriage or friendships. Now that I read it, I understand why WFB's biography was written the way it was. And why it took tanenhaus so long to write it.
