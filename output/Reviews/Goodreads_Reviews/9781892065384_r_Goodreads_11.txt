What a terrible, cruel, devastating and amazing book. A collection of mostly inaccessible stories that don't care at all whether they're ever accessed or by who or what, that come together in a whip-stitched panoply of truly Kiernan things: transhumanism; the lie of gender; wet, gasping blind moist things; the world beneath the bricks and the dust; drowning girls and rotting metal. If nothing else, it does what it says on the front cover: these are tales of pain and wonder, but you're never quite sure which at any given time.
