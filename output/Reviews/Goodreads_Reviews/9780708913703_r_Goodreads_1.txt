4 Stars ~ Rachel is the elder of three daughters and when her mother passes away, she takes on the task of running her doctor father's house, putting off her own career as an artist in London. She likes her role and finds time to do some free lance illustrations for children's books. Rachel particularly enjoys her excursions on to the manor estate, now vacate for three years since the old squire passed away. It's there while she's enjoying a quiet sunny morning that she meets Daniel, the grandson and heir. He startles her and unnerved she becomes defensive and sparks fly between them. Rattled Rachel is determined to dislike Daniel, but at every turn he's surprising her. While he too has a brittle façade, Rachel also sees the intelligent brave man beneath. But when she thinks he's taken an interest in her sister Carola, Rachel finally accepts the proposal of her childhood friend, Edward. This is a charming story that sweeps into Rachel's world of family joys and tribulations. She's always dreamt of a faceless man who was her Mr. Right, but when she finally meets him, she's confused and totally unnerved. Daniel's a self-made man who's worked very hard all his life. It's obvious from the start that he's completely smitten with Rachel and is only waiting for her to catch up. Lovely read!
