A really incredible biography. Leeming, a friend and companion of Baldwin’s, writes with nuance and clarity about his life and works in a beautiful manner. It truly feels like a friend walking through Baldwin’s life. While this book may not be the most analytical or exposing biography of Baldwin, in its handling of his life with careful sensibility, Leeming evokes some of Baldwin’s style for himself. For any keen Baldwin readers this biography is a must!
