This took me forever to finish just because I didn't get along with the writing style. The plot, military details, and characters were all very interesting and well written, but the contrast between the dry and vague prose style, but frantic and fast dialogue was really difficult for me to focus on. It was hard at times to follow what was happening because two characters would just get going firing random sentences at each other, and sometimes it was just hard to tell what was going on in general. When I properly sat down and only focussed on the book, and when action was going on, I could get engrossed...But picking it up to finish was off putting.
