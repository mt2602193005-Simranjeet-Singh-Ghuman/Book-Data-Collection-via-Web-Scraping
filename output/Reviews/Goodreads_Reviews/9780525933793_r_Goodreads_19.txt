It kind of reminded me of reading a coming of age girls diary; And also reminded me of real life camping and hiking experiences; And perhaps a retelling of a kidnapping experience. It did not convince me that it was during a prehistoric time period. Although it did remind me of historical and modern writings of kidnappings and the growing relationships between kidnappers and their young victims. It was still an interesting read. I know the author was writing as a "translation" into present day language but I couldn't get around the fact that Jes said about the baby "He is a small {picture} of Mar" I stopped thinking about it as being prehistoric and decided to just read it as a fictional story. Then it was delightful even through the inaccuracies. I would like to read other stories by Joan Wolf.
