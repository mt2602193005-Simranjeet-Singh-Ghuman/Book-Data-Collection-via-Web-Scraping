I love mud and mud loves me is a picture book about a boy named Sam. This is a simple story that is appropriate for preschoole aged children. Throughout the story Sam's mom askes him why he's muddy, got worms in his pocket, paint on his arms, jam on his face, sand in his shoes, and gum in his hair. Each time Sam answers that its because he loves it and it loves him. At the end of the story, Sam is taking a bath and when his mom asks why, Sam tells her its because her loves her and she loves him. In this story, small children can see that bathing, getting clean is a good thing. It also reinforces a positive parent child relationship. The illustrations are done in watercolor. This is a good book that I think small children would enjoy having read to them. Show more
