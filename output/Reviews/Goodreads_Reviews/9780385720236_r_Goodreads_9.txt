Ma Jian has come to a difficult point where he is struggling with life. His wife has left him, taking their daughter, his employers are critical and so he decides to leave everything and discover his China by bus, foot & train. This is quite a personal book about self discovery but I’m not quite sure he found exactly what he was looking for. Some parts held my attention but others I felt bogged down with. Certainly if you have a wish to learn about China it’s worth a read.
