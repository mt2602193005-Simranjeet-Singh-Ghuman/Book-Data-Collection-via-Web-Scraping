This sequel to Black Aura is another homage to and gentle ribbing of golden age mystery tropes, with members of an old WWII "murder club" being threatened by crimes some 30 years later. Once again, both funny and a good puzzler in its own right. I do think there was only one character with a clear motive in this one, so the identity of the murderer was unsurprising. On the other hand, the locked room mystery part of it is something that I couldn't have figured out in a million years, and it at least superficially satisfying even if I'm not sure that's how inflatable boats would work. I hope to learn one day that John Sladek actually tested this method out to insure authenticity.
