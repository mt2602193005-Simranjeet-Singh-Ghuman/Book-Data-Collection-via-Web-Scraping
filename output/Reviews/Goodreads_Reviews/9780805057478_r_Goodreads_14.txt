Audiobook. Not sure I'd give it a full four "I really liked it" but still. I listened to it for almost 29 hours, so it gets the benefit of the doubt. Benefits from an influx of correspondence never before seen, so there's a lot in this biography that couldn't have been in the prior ones. The first while was fairly unexciting as it dealt with his childhood and adolescence and I'm more concerned with his writing years, but, of course, his childhood and adolescence provide valuable insights into his character and authorial nature. I would recommend it to anyone else seriously interested in Chekhov.
