Everything she does is magic... this is no little book though, but an immense emptying of many notebooks into one great book. The thread,, formed by Owain Glendwr the great Prince of Wales is at times very thin. The anecdotes and sometimes tantalising glimpses onto even more werid facts and figures keep it a light read. From a guardian interview close to her death I have learnt she kept a different marmalade for every day of the week, I think a mind who derives pleasure out of this will deeply enjoy this book.
