Pretty good book, I enjoyed most of the stories, though there were some jokes I wasn’t keen on and I didn’t enjoy the last three stories as much. The Barney Gumble story didn’t quite make sense to me. I figured there was probably a pop culture reference I was missing that was integral to understanding lots of the jokes. I thought about it for a little while afterwards before remembering that in the US there are Flintstones-themed commercials for “Fruity Pebbles” cereal that the story must be parodying. That hadn’t occurred to me while reading, because I am from the UK and did not grow up seeing these commercials. I have seen stuff from/about the ads a handful of times online in the past but I had basically forgotten that they existed, so I didn’t make the connection.
