A little over a year ago, I joined a group of readers on bookstagram reading a victorian sensation novel every couple months. Lady Audley's Secret was one of my favorite reads last year and I enjoyed a collection of Mary Elizabeth Braddon's short stories last fall. So when the group decided to read Aurora Floyd this month, I was thrilled. I am happy to say that it did not disappoint. I just love Braddon's writing and she has quickly become one of my favorite victorian authors. Aurora Floyd was fast paced and well written. I loved the characters, good and bad. At 459 pages (in the Oxford edition), it's not a short story, but I was so engrossed in the story that I didn't want to put it down. I had to keep reading to find out how everything played out. Some people have complained about the intrusive narrator, but I actually loved that element. It almost made it feel like I was sitting with Mary Elizabeth Braddon while she told me her tale.
