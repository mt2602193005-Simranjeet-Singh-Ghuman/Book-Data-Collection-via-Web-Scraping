28 years ago, Linenger spent 5 months aboard the dying Mir space station. From his description, it sounded like a pretty tough 5 months. I find it appalling that NASA was still letting astronauts go up there. This book is a pretty well written memoir. You can tell that Linenger was kind of a jock type, and a very type A personality. There are aspects of what was going on that reminded me of a documentary I watched about a group of Americans trying to help out one of the Russian hockey clubs. Very similar attitudes from the Russians.
