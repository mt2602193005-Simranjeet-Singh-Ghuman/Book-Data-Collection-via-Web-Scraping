This is a powerful book about one little boy that Dorothy W. Baruch worked with when he was a boy. The boy was very backward and in a lot of trouble when he came into therapy. This book is not for the lighthearted. Though it can be read by the uninitiated to psychology some familiarity with psychology would help. A great read. J. Robert Ewbank author "John Wesley, Natural Man, and the 'Isms'"
