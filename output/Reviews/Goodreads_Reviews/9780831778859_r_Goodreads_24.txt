I would recommend this book for the primary grades, second through third grade, due to the lengthy pages. I would categorize it as traditional literature fantasy. This is a classic story that I grew up reading, and eventually watching. I always found it so interesting that she was comfortable walking into a random house. My parents always taught me the "stranger danger" phrase, so I would have never done that. I think that the dwarves are super cute, and they were always my favorite characters. It's crazy that there are so many alternative endings/versions of this story. This one is totally appropriate for children, but some are not.
