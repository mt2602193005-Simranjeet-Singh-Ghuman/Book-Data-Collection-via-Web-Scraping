Eh. I know this is supposed to be the anonymous diary of a teen, and sometimes the writing and topics were SO juvenile, it was completely believable. But other times, I just had a hard time believing this was a real diary written by a real junior high student; it felt more like a cautionary tale written by an adult behind a typewriter somewhere imagining what a teen in this situation would say and do. I am most certainly not trying to belittle the teen who went through this experience or minimize the seriousness of her victimization! This is simply an observation and meant to serve as an explanation for my rating. I did sincerely appreciate the Q&A section at the end as well as the hotline numbers for teens in crisis. Very pertinent, valuable information. If this book helps even one teen who is in such a devastating situation, then it actually deserves 5 stars, not 3!
