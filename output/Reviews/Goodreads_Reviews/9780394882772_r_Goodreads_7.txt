Such a sweet, sweet book written from a dogs point of view. This would be perfect to get for a child just getting his-her first dog. Starts out with the dog at the pet store tring to find his right child... ones pull his tail, or is hyper etc but the one that comes and pats him on his head and is nice to him he decides to 'keep'. The dog continues to 'teach' the boy different things like eat, play, clean up etc. All the special things that dogs help children do. Then dog looses his boy, he is scared and panic trying to find him. They are reunited at the end and he says to never loose each other again...
