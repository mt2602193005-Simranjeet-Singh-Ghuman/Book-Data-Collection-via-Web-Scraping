Picked this up at a flea market, not realising it was part 4 of a series. However, it doesn't bother as it is a transition book between two sets of trilogies, and deals with topics not really related to either story. It serves as a setup for the new trilogy, and a wrapup of the previous one, showing a world in shambles, scurrying to reconstruct alliances to fill up a power vacuum created at the end of the previous book. It deals with sorrow, intrigue, and fear of death. Not a lot of action in this book, but that doesn't bother. It is a refreshing read from standard fantasy books.
