This is a really good book. I thoroughly enjoyed it. Wonderfully erotic, but never overly course or vulgar. Set in 1920s Berlin, it follows the sexual encounters of a rich you man as his mixes with friends and socialites, and enjoys the delights (i.e. women) that Berlin had to offer. Probably a feminist nightmare when judged by todays standards (and morals?) but attitudes were certainly different 100 years ago. I’m certainly glad that this was written by a woman, it gives an interesting / different perspective to the motives of main male character, and a belief to the response, desires and actions of his female encounters. If you fancy a bit of erotica, then this comes highly recommended.
