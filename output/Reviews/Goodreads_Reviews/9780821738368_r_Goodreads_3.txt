My copy was given through Netgalley for an honest review. This is the first book I've read from this author and I understand this is her pen name. This was truly a sweet story with an opposites attract sort of vibe and a very happy ending. Charley saves a boy from an accident, that first chapter is very emotional, and a pair of strong arms saves her from death trying to save the others. Having to care for her burned hands by herself is a chore but she's extremely lucky to have help. Her "boyfriend" is a scientist and they are working on a cure for diabetes but he's clueless to her needs and wants. Jess is there for her and becomes the strength she needs during this difficult time. She was given a handsome reward and now has the opportunity of a lifetime. Their relationship is normal, he's the strong dependable type and she's very unselfish and shy. Charley learns to believe in herself and it's okay to look out for her own needs. Jess appears a little cynical until he spends time with Charley and she melts his heart. His guilt is wearing on him and the ulcer brewing inside is ripping him apart, will his secret deepen their love or tear them apart? The storyline was beautifully written and the characters are very developed. I happen to enjoy a conversation full of humor and healthy banter, it was missing here. There were conversations but most of the book was narrative and descriptive making it seemed heavy and less carefree. Maybe that was the whole point, it was very enjoyable nonetheless.
