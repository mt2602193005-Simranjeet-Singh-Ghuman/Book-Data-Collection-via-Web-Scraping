Ward 9 finally clicked for me. “Story Under the Ur-Bororo” is as funny as it is clever. “Waiting” feels instantly classic, and the title story might be satire at its sharpest. I don’t really understand some of the negative reviews — they seem to be judging it like conventional fiction and missing the point entirely. Is it an easy read? No. But it isn’t trying to be. It’s not about narrative — it’s about context, perception, and how meaning accumulates.
