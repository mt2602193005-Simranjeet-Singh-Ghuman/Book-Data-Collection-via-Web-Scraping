My first book by Marian Keyes and yeah, I can see myself reading her entire body of work! A gentle story and yet… plenty of drama. Three main characters. Four supporting characters. One asshat. London. Serious issues. Job difficulties. Family problems. Poignant. Light but heartfelt. Romance and wit for added zest. Unexpected mystery revealed towards the end. Oh yeah, tissues needed! A fab read and one I can definitely recommend.
