Borrowed from the book box at school, this is the debut novel of Elin Hilderbrand and her first visit to beloved Nantucket. It shows that her marriage themes would always be love, family, friendships and the legacy and pull to Nantucket's shores. Told from the perspective of several characters, the book certainly shows EH's magical writing building to have me dreaming of sunny beaches during an actual Canadian snowstorm.
