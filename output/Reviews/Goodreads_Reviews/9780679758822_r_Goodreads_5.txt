I don’t rate books I didn’t finish. I read about half of this when on a camping trip where I’d accidentally forgotten my ereader and this was in a campground little free library. It kept me entertained enough while hanging out at the campsite but I found that I didn’t care enough to bother finishing it when it was time to pack up and I put it back in the library. Plot was meh and the setting was lovely, but the male gaze came out instantly and the main character was kind of a bastard. I put it down when the love interest came on the scene. She was supposed to be a badass and she was instantly attracted to this misogynistic bozo? Nah.
