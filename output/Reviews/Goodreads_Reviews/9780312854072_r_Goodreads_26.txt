'Brightness Falls from the Air' by James Tiptree Jr. was the January pick for my online book club. 16 people gather on the planet Damiem for various reasons, some known, some hidden and some nefarious, for a chance to view an astronomical event. The planet is also host to an exploited native species. There were things I really liked about this story and things I did not. There is some difficult subject matter that goes unexplained and is left to the reader to interpret. There is also a certain aged prose style that makes the story overdramatic at times. I wanted to like this more and, frankly, didn't.
