A distant friend and I ask each other at our annual catch-up reunion every year: "If I only read one book this year, what should it be?" My answer this year will be: "Read Henry Cloud's 'Changes That Heal.'" Written for laymen, "Changes That Heal" is easy to understand and practical. I especially appreciate Cloud's many case scenarios/illustrations and his straightforward advice for simple changes you can start making today to improve emotional and relational health.
