This book has an extremely niche audience—if you aren't interested in Quebecois, Post-modern, feminist literature, you won't get much out of it. Though there are multiple characters and narrators, the novel lacks a traditional plot. I never really wanted to keep reading it...every time I picked it up, I would struggle to find meaning in the pages, even when I made a conscious attempt to read closely or re-read. The most interesting parts are the poetic language (though it often became grating), and motifs of the desert, driving, fear, and words. You could probably get a few beautiful excerpts out of this book, but 99% of it was pointless.
