Muffin was a bit overdone in terms of personification...a little too sci-fi for me in this scenario. Not that I don't want a car with AI like Muffin; it just seemed a bit over the top. One takeaway from this book: proper use of trouper versus trooper. I have my normal grammatical pet peeves, as most readers do: your/you're, its/it's, there/their/they're, etc...you know, the mundane ones that elicit groans from even non-editors. However, I also eschew lesser known errors, and trouper/trooper is a big one. Evanovich/Hughes got it right on p 261. Rather sad (and telling) that that's such a standout from this book that it takes up nearly half of my review, but so be it. Maybe I was expecting something closer to the Plum series' style of writing, but this was rather disappointing for me. Weak characters, obvious storyline, nothing to grab the reader and draw her in. Well, after browsing other reviews of this book and seeing multiple mentions that #2 was superior to #1, I doubt I'll go read #1 of this series...even "as good as #1" might turn me off reading #1, as this one wasn't fabulous.
