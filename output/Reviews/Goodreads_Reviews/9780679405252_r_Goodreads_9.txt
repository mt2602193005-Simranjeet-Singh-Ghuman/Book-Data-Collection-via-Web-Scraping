I hadn't planned to watch the Royal Wedding, but then Paul suggested there might be an anarchist outrage during the ceremony and, well, I couldn't resist. But I've been annoyed ever since. One of my favourite parts of this book is that one of the characters wants his wedding vows to contain the phrase, "With all my worldly goods I thee endow". It is, admittedly, a beautiful phrase and I can believe a character wanting this included in their vows. So, the Royals being high church and all, I expected they would have this included in their vows. But like all beautiful poetry in the church liturgy this has been destroyed or 'modernised' - it now says something like, "And all my worldly goods I will with you share". This is, obviously not nearly as modern as it ought to be. Why not, "And I'll happily share all my stuff with you". By the way - I listened to this as an audio book performed (quite literally) by three actors.
