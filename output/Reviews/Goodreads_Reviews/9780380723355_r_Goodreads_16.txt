Fishing captain Joe Duarte is killed in an accident on his boat. His estranged daughter Adelia and her daughter Sara come back to Nantucket and decide to stay, even though Joe’s first mate Jackie Alcantrara inherited Joe’s boats. A few days after Joe’s funeral, another tragedy strikes Nantucket, and ‘Del is found murdered, her young daughter sitting at her side. Complex machinations and relationships muddy the waters of the investigations of both deaths. This second in the Nantucket Island mysteries is complex and rewarding. Relationships among the returning cast evolve, the plot in intricate enough to keep the reader involved, and the conclusion is a bit of surprise. Recommended.
