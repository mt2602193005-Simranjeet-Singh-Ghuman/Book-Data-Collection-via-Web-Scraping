I bought this book for a dollar at the rabbit hole bookstore in Dayton, Ohio. A delightful little bookstore. With all these great publications for a dollar money going to various charities in the city. With that said, I expected a rather light fluffy book, but this got into the hearts of women at a time when they were supposed to be good little wives standing behind their husband’s success. So not the case interesting to note that the politics reflected in this book are an eerie reminder of what we are living right now in America. Scary to think we really didn’t pay attention to past history. Thoroughly enjoyable!
