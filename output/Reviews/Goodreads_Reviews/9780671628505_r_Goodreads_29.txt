not sexy, not spooky, just sad if you've read this book you're probably thinking what the fuck and if you haven't read this book but know anything about it you're probably also thinking what the fuck. which is TOTALLY warranted. but like... I'm disappointed. I'd heard that this book was a dark tale with some incestuous themes but I was lead to believe that these themes would be... well thought out. that our beloved V.C. Andrews would somehow find a way to introduce some grey morality and make us believe that the things that happened in this book were okay. if you don't know what this book is about: dad dies, mom takes her 4 children to live at her parent's house but she has to keep her kids hidden because her father doesn't know they exist. hence the whole attic thing. eventually the two oldest kids get 'involved' if you want to call it that. my main issue with this book were the 'twists' and the lack of justified 'issues'. it seemed like every chapter was just these kids suffering in an attic until the next shitty twist came along. and all the twist just added to the kids misfortune. like they could not catch a BREAK. you didn't root for the kids because you knew they were just going to get fucked down no matter what. and then the incest in the room. just... bad. I'm not saying that you should, as an author, condone those sorts of practices. in fact there's a fine line between justifying actions and condoning them. but Andrews did neither. she made you hate the elder brother, and feel really really sorry for his younger sister. it would have been easy to say that the two older siblings became parents to their younger siblings and took the roles too far and that's why they got together. instead we get rapey-ness. it was pretty horrible and sad. overall... this uh. sucked. I hated it. I want to bleach my brain forever.
