I will admit that this book took me a very long time to finish but that is because of how dense I found this book and how much I have found myself invested in its arguments and positions. Even with sixteen years between its publication and now the concerns discussed are highly prescient in this post-9/11, post-Enron, post-Snowden world. The book points out that we rail at government and corporate secrecy but we do so from opposite societal ends without realizing that these ends actually share a common desire, to shine the light on secrets whose sole existence can be dangerous.
