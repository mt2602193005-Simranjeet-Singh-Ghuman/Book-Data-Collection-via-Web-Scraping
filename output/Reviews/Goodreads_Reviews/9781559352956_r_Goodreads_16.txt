When I read these novels back in the day I would have given them 3-3.5 STARS and now would say about one Star I started reading VC Andrews books in the 1991 and stopped about 2003. I have read: -Dollanganger Series -Casteel Series -Cutler Series -Landry Series -Logan Series -Orphans Series -Wildflowers Series -Hudson Series -Shooting Stars Series -DeBeers Series -Broken Wings Series As a preteen reading these novels was a rebellion and the gothic theme also seemed cool. I stopped reading this author because the novelty wore off, the novels are too formulaic that I could not tell one book from another and gothic aspect was lost.
