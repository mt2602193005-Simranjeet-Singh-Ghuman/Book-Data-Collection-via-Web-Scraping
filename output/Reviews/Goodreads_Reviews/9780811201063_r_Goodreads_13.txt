Hmm, how to rate this book. There are some absolutely great chapters focusing on places he visits as well as some strange characters he meets along the way that are fascinating. Unfortunately, it’s not long before he cannot help but wank on about Paris. We get it, you had a great time. I’ve read around ten books by Miller and half of those are based on his time in Paris. We don’t need to hear more about it. So yeah, I think the main problem is that I feel cheated as it’s half travelogue, half reminiscences of Paris. Strictly for Miller fans.
