A Hare Krishna once gave me a cookbook as I walked through an airport back in the ‘80s. I still have it, but don’t remember ever using it. A big reason why is that it explained in its introduction that the sect prohibits using garlic in its cooking. I’m sorry, but a life without garlic is just unimaginable! The Garlic Lovers’ Cookbook is the antithesis of that Hare Krishna one. It is a celebration of garlic generously added to your cuisine. It contains many succulent recipes, but one stands out for me, a recipe from this book that I have made regularly for the past quarter century. Its recipe for Greek Green Beans, cooked in tomato juice till it reduces to a thick sauce, and combined with onions, lots of garlic, diced tomatoes, spices and herbs, all sautéed in olive oil became one of my favorite standards.
