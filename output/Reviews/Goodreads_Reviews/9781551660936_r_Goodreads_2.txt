The actions of 1 man affected the lives of 4 women and we follow their story. I like that each woman faced a different challenge and it was fairly realistic in some sense. Although let's be honest, Elizabeth didn't face that much difficult of a challenge compared to the other ladies, although her husband's story is quite intriguing. All of these women come together at one point or another into each other's lives. Philippa has always been daddy's little girl, doing whatever her parents wanted and being the perfect lady. But now that her husband's committed suicide, what can she do and who can she turn to? I enjoyed seeing her grow and determine to become independent after decades of dependence on other male figures in her life. Sally and her husband are going through a tough time, him with his job less and her taking on more responsibilities around the house and working full time. It is easy to see how easy it is for her to snap and crave the attention of another man...who as we slowly see over time turns out to be very controlling and even dangerous (in the psychological sense). Will they finally have a heart to heart or will their marriage crumble? Deborah thought she wanted this promotion, but with all the unwanted attention from her boss and jealousy of her lover... it is hard to say. Honestly I don't think her boyfriend deserved her after treating her like that, I feel like she should have been with the friend, who seems like such a nicer guy. But whatever people make their own choices about their lives, who are we to question. Definitely an interesting read with some fairly realistic settings and obstacles.
