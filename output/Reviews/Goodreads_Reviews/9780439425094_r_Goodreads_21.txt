Another great read by Lauren Brooke, I was speeding through the pages as I had to find out what was going to happen to Amy! 😊 Amy is struggling with all the work that has to be done at Heartland after the devastating storm, the horses that need to be trained and the endless stalls that need to be mucked out. Amy is missing Ty (the stable hand and family friend) more than ever, but she is set on continuing to stay positive while he is in hospital. When a phone call about gentling a wild mustang comes through, Amy jumps at the chance to help train him to keep her mind off the daily challenges at Heartland. Will Amy really be able to help Dazzle trust her or will she be committing to more than she is capable of? *Feel free to check out my overview for the Heartland series over on my Substack blog: https://thepaintedcaravan.substack.co...
