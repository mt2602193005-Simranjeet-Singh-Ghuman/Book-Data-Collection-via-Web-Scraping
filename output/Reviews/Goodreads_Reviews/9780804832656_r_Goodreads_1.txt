when i first picked this up, i looked at the title and said "i wish." i have since changed my mind. i do not want to be a cat. i read this out of my new(ish) found obsession with japanese literature about cats, but this is not a book about cats. this is a book narrated by a cat, and is mostly about his weird owner doing things like widely critiquing a woman's nose and fixating on schoolkids knocking baseballs into his backyard. it is, and without checking i'm pretty confident about this, one million pages long. it had its charming moments, and its extreme dedication to capturing (to put it nicely) la vie quotidienne was at first admirable, but by around the halfway mark i could not believe this book was still chugging along with its self-same commitment to having no plot. bottom line: no plot just vibes final boss.
