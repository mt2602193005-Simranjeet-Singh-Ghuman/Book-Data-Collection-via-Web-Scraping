Patrick Robinson is one of my favorites when it comes to the technical side of submarine warfare and the intrigue he brings into the world of espionage. And while I enjoyed H.M.S. Unseen overall, I did not care for how Robinson ultimately characterized Adnam, making him out to be this "perfect" spy, even when he was lost in the darkness of his own mind. I quite understand the direction Robinson took Adnam on in bringing him to the states, which was his only option to exact revenge on those who betrayed him, but he didn't deserve an honorable death (I secretly wish he never died and came back in future novels.) I wish he would've pit all three countries against each other and Adnam at the end. What a climax that would've been! As always, Arnold Morgan is one of my favorite characters ever created and I can't wait to continue the series.
