I don't cate to give this a star rating as this is a very basic informational text. I'm quite shocked by how accurate this test is! Most people think of the Rorschach inkblot test as a staple in psychoanalysis but I'm surprised the Color Test isn't as popular. (Of course, I'm speaking as someone who's not in the psychological field so, I don't know, maybe this is really popular?) As someone who has synesthesia (sounds have colours for me) I've always been curious about the psychology behind colours. The text is a bit dated and the language used is pretty complicated but I found it fascinating nonetheless.
