3.5 stars Lovely! I found this little comprehensive guide at my local library. I nice little reference guide of information. Vegetable soaps, transparent soaps, liquid soap, laundry soap, oil guide, natural plant colorant guide, minerals and clays, troubleshooting guide, some essential oil combos, etc. There's also a very helpful section on some of the legal aspects of starting a business (and a laughable section about the internet with categories like "What does "online" mean?") I don't think that I'd choose to purchase this one over some other, newer guides, but it's great if you have cheap or free access! Still relevant 20+ years later.
