Of Kinkajous, Capybaras, Horned Beetles, Seladangs is a collection of nature-based entries that focuses on providing readers with easy-to-read and understand nature entries with a sprinkle of pop culture. If you have been around for a while than most of it will be stuff that you at least know pieces of and since the book was also originally published in the early 90s than parts are definitely going to be outdated with more modern discoveries. Even so I found the book to be interesting still and worthy of a read-through. The book itself is rather random in organization with the reader being able to read entries on evolution, zoology, botany and abiogenesis although not in that particular order. As a result readers can easily flip through and read selections that interest them more instead doing a cover-to-cover read. There are a few black-and-white illustrations although they are the exception and not the rule. All in all it was a decent nature read that is more rounded than many other science reads of the now and as a result it still has a place on science shelves of the curious nature-lover. Show more
