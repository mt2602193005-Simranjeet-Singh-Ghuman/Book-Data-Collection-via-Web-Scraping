This is the second Mrs. Pollifax adventure. This time we see her make a few simple mistakes that complicate her mission for the CIA to Turkey, but of course in the end her irrepressible spirit saves the day. Along the way she meets some wonderful people including "gypsies" (as they were called, and treated with respect), a travelogue maker, and a young fashion model; visits a Turkish love-in; and generally creates havoc all over Turkey. The plot of this book is excellent. Every scene requires actions that lead inexorably to more trouble, and just when the reader starts to relax things get tense again - but always in a way that feels quite natural and logical. I always forget how much I like this book until I reread it, and then I get delighted all over again.
