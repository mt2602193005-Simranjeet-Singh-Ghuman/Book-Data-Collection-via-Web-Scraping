A nice study in brinksmanship. The afterword in particular is really good, highly analytical. It tries to resolve the nuclear paradox, which is that with mutually assured destruction, neither nation can win, but each nation has to be willing to lose. The paradox is a useful platform for explaining why Kennedy was willing to compromise on the Jupiter missiles in Turkey (interestingly, he had ordered them to be slowly removed a while before, but generals simply didn’t carry out the orders). The book itself is pretty short and is a fast read, but the afterword is more lengthy. However, I think it’s worth the time.
