I read this off of the recommendation of Thomas Sowell. While it’s a hard read (or listen, as I did), it’s definitely eye opening and incredibly thought provoking. And to me, the thought provoking aspects are those in which I found the mentality that makes the underclass the very thoughts prevalent in the Church. I’d encourage you to read this and truly question your conscience or subconscious stances. Is all sin really equal? Is judgment really so bad? Is staying quiet and just being “supportive” really helping anyone to a joyful and free life? Are we just fated to our paths? Is our life our responsibility or are we the victim of circumstances or other’s choices? Is authority and law merely guidelines to be dismissed when it suits? Is the system rigged against striving for betterment; for goodness, purity, even holiness? Is continual sin just to be expected this side of heaven?
