What stays with me is how the novel uses enslavement not to diminish her but to reveal the full architecture of her power. Estri stripped of her office and her freedom is still a woman who thinks in complete sentences, who reads every room she enters, who understands that survival on Silistra requires a kind of intelligence that force alone cannot provide. The sixty bones of the Yris-tera foretelling her fate while she systematically refuses to be defined by it is the oldest tension in serious fiction, and you found a way to make it feel entirely new.
