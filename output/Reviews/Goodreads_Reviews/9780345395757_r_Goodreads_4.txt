Lily is currently living in her aunt Janie Belle's house after her property was foreclosed. While there, she discovers an old letter from her mother to her father that mentions valuable jewelry. She finds the jewelry when she sneaks into her old house to search for it. Meanwhile, the mystery surrounding her father's death remains unresolved. The story has a slow pace until the end, which feels rushed. The resolutions come in the last few pages, leaving little room for explanation.
