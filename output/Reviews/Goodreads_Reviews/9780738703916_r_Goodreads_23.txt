This was far more YA than I anticipated it being, and I suppose that is on me for not doing my research prior to starting the reading. On the other hand, I have read a lot of YA that was perfectly enjoyable for older audiences, and this one was just not well written. The dialogue is AWFUL. So bad. Very “Oh my God! That’s like, so TOTALLY whatever. Like, totally.” It was painful and made the characters very unlikable, especially Amber. When characters didn’t talk like this, like Donovan, they spoke so angsty and very tortured poet. Overall, the dialogue was just painfully bad. When all the characters were not speaking and we were just getting action or description, such as the nightmare scenes, the writing got decent. I enjoyed the nightmares and the way they blended with reality, and actually thought the writing was pretty eerie in these sections! They created a good ambience, which is why it was so shocking when the dialogue was so cringey. But the characters were irrational, awful, underdeveloped, and impossible to root for. Mix that in with the way the killer was painfully easy to predict, and this book just did not have enough going for it. I will not be continuing the series, but I do think I’d be willing to read more from this author if it was adult fiction instead of YA. It was all the teenager angst and romance and outdated slang that made this novel difficult.
