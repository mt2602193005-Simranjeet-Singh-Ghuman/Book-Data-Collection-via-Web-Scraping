Hatred and bigotry are in our DNA, at the very core of our nature, and that nature is easily manipulated by others, as we find in this third Inspector Morrissey novel by Kay Mitchel. When members of the local council secretly cultivate racial hatred where there previously had been none, they accumulate power and wealth; but there are also unintended consequences, some deaths and arson. As the Inspector goes about his investigation, not only are there forces working against him from the shadows, there are the complexities of his own life and the people around him to contend with. A village cozy of a novel, but also a good police procedural. Show more
