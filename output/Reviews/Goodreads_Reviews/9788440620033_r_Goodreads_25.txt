Not a soap opera… So much of what is written, and read, Is retreaded soap operas and romance novels, couched in perhaps less obvious language and structure. Blended with the adventure tales from the stone age campfires of our ancestors, they still move us, and I quite like them. This is not one of those stories. Nor is it a piece one might call mainstream literature, the millions of copies of which flood the shelves of used book stores. Think of a piece written by early Carlos Castaneda blended with early JD Salinger, and a bit of Aldous Huxley thrown in. Perhaps Harold Lamb contributed as well. Have I mentioned a tincture of James Joyce in the mix? It is best to read with no knowledge of the plot or the ending. The Kindle version has fewer errors and anomalies than usual. If you are over 40, read it now. If you are under 40, read it now, and then again later.
