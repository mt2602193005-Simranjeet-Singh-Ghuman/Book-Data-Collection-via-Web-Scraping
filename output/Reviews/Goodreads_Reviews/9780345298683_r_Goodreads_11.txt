A foundational text in robot theology! When most of the humans on earth suddenly disappear, what do their robot servants do? Create a religion of course. I’m always slightly baffled when a science fiction author, even one as philosophical as Simak, writes a book with a strong anti technological theme. If we just get rid of all the tech we’ll almost certainly develop telepathy and the ability to teleport around the universe at a whim. So enjoy your social media time because it’s costing you the ability to get out of the rat race through teleportation.
