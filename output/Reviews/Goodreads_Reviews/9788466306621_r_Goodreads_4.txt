Nora Roberts can always be counted on to make me cry happy tears at some point in every book she writes and this one was no exception. Maybe her books are little bit formulaic and her characters are interchangeable, but she writes them all so brilliantly that each one seems original when you are reading it. In Jewels of the Sun American Jude Murray and Irishman Aidan Gallagher reminded me very much of Eve Dallas and Roarke, and that is not a bad thing! The Irish setting was delightful, the characters were good fun, and I enjoyed the traditional Irish folk tales which were a strong feature of the whole story. Five stars for good, honest, well written entertainment.
