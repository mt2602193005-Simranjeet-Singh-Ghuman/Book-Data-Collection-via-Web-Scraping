I thought this was a great set of stories. Most of them are connected in their ideas. I was doing some reading about these stories, and many people are quite disturbed by the ideas presented in this collection. There is a lot about cloning, incest, childhood sex, age-regression, and the main one being switching from one sex to another many times during ones life. The stories themselves I feel are well constructed and explore these topics in an interesting way. Some people seem to think he had some mental illness issues or something, but I think he was actually pushing the boundaries of the time period and exploring ideas that many would consider taboo, especially in far removed societies where these taboos may have disappeared with the advance of technology. I feel that is what good writers do. They can go beyond their own culture and explore the "what ifs". He also seems to have been highly influenced by Robert Heinlein, who was into writing about these ideas, too. Varley still was a product of his time, but this is a pretty solid set of stories.
