This is the book that started it all for me. My grandmother bought this book in hopes that it would get me to read, and read I did. This is a great book for any kids who don't want to read, 'normal stuff' like I did. I learned to read on some of the scarier Mercer Mayer books when I was three or so, and from there I read Scary Stories to Tell in the Dark and Goosebumps novels. I will be teaching my children how to read with this book.
