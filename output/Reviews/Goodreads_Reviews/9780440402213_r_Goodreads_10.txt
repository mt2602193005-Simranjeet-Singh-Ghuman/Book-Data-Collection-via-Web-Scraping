Sam is a newborn and he's mad that he can't communicate to his family that he wants them to take his hat off. Later he's a bigger baby and he explains how he learns how to roll over for the first time. Then he becomes scared of the "terrible two's" his mom is always talking about and he's constantly looking over his shoulder to search for those scary "twos." His perspective is adorable and relatable to my kiddos. Loved this book! Very cute chapter book for young kids. My 4 year old doesn't usually sit still very long to listen to his big brother's chapter books. But he loved this one, as it's told from the perspective of, first a newborn, and by the end of the book, Sam is probably about 5. Lowry has always been one of my favorite children's authors and will continue to be so. We're looking forward to more of this series.
