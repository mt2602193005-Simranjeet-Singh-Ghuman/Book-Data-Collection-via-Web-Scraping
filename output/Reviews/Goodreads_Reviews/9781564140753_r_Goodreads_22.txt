“How to Study” is a great resource for students who want to use their time more efficiently when they are studying. Author Ron Fry breaks down his advice into eight chapters: 1. How to Start Out Right 2. How to Organize Your Studying 3. How to Read to Remember 4. How to Organize Your Time 5. How to Excel in Class 6. How to Research 7. How to Write 8. How to Study for Tests These tips can be applied to students from middle school to graduate school.
