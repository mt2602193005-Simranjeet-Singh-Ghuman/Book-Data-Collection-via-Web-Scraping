The Bookshop (1978) Set in 1959 - Florence Green buys old, haunted, decrepit, property to set up bookshop in small Suffolk town. Interesting read. Project ends badly, partly because Florence "values kindness above everything else" and is no match for powerful and malicious adversaries. Good, convincing portrayal of all characters, including minor ones. 4.5* The Gate of Angels (1990) Set in 1912, largely around small Cambridge college. Interesting, but I couldn't relate to characters so was disappointing after The Bookshop. 3* The Blue Flower (1995) Gave up on soon after the beginning. 1*
