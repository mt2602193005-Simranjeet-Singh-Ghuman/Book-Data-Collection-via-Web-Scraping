Rating 4.5 This was a much more enjoyable read than I expected. The first third of the book immediately pulled me in with its unusual and engaging premise. The way the story set up Alex and Giana's relationship was unique, particularly through Giana's unconventional "education" about the differences between men and women and the realities of a woman's place under her husband's authority during that time. It made for an interesting foundation that was different from the typical historical romance. The final two-thirds of the novel is where the story truly shined for me. I loved the reunion between Alex and Giana and the confrontation over Alex's mistaken beliefs about her and the events of the past. I was worried that misunderstanding would drag on for the entire book, so I was relieved it was resolved fairly quickly. Instead, the focus shifted to watching them build a genuine relationship based on trust, understanding, and love, which was much more satisfying. Alex was a wonderful hero, and I especially appreciated Giana's growth throughout the story. She matured emotionally and gradually came to understand that Alex was very different from the majority of men she had known or been taught to expect. Their romance felt believable, and I found myself fully invested in their journey toward a well-earned happily ever after. Overall, this was a very pleasant surprise—engaging, entertaining, and a historical romance I enjoyed far more than I anticipated.
