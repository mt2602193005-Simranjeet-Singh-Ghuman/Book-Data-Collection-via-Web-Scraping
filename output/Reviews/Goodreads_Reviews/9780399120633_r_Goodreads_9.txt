A bit scary, a bit wozy novel of the sort that DK is famous for. It dwells on how our memories are fickle and unreliable features, what lurks in the depths of (some) humans' psyche and how this all could be made into a fun-to-read book. Here we meet paranormalicious, a cartoonish love story, creepy family members getting creepier by the second and a ton of other things that we might have been better never setting our minds upon. Just like that. Not stellar but a very decent read.
