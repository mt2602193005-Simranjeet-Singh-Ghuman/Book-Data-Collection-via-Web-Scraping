5 Mesmerizing Well researched- the best biography I have ever read stars! This is the best biography I believe ever to be written or this is the best biography I have ever read. I have never cared much about Frida Kahlo except the movie which I had been planning to watch for the past 5 years but never did. But now I really want to watch the movie. I saw this book laying next to Marlon by Peter Manso in the library and I have been reading it during the lunch and tea breaks and I finally finished it yesterday. For a biography to work this well, the subject has to be interesting and the biographer or writer's skills have to be really good to bring out the essence of the subject and Hayden Herrera did such an amazing job. This is one of those books which will never be released on Kindle(along with catcher in the rye) Review coming up.........although I am at a loss as how to review this book that made me so feel so much and connected with me on so many levels and yet it falls under non fiction!
