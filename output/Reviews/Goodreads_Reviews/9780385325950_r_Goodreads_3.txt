I was 12 when I read this and it resonated with me immensely because of my own alcoholic mother and the struggles I was facing at that time. This book made me feel seen in a time when I truly needed it. I am 24 now and have since cut ties with my toxic mother and am the happiest I’ve ever been but this book stuck with me after all these years. It is a heartbreaking, honest portrayal of children forced to become adults.
