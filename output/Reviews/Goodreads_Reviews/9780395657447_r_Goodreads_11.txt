Wonderful rhyming book: "Rachel Fister found a blister on her little left-hand toe. Just a tiny little blister, but she thought her mom should know." Mrs. Fister sent for Mister, who calls... until eventually the Queen is consulted. Whose advice is to "Just use your lips." Which has everyone stumped except Rachel's mother. The children loved the absurdity. 2/1/12 Used again for a couple programs
