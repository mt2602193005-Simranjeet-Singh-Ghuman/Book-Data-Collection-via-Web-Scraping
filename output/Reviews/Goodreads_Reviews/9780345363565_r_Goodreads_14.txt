The Metrognome is a good collection of fifteen well-crafted short stories by Foster. It's a pleasant mix of science fiction, fantasy, and horror, and showcases his range quite well, with a mix of humor, drama, and irony. He creates likable characters and gives them realistic dialog and interesting settings, and each story is prefaced by introductory comments.
