I love a well written book with beautiful language but I felt Janny Wurtz tried too hard with her writing. Similes were often overblown and so numerous as to obscure what she was trying to say. I didn’t need quite as many descriptive similes of minor scenery and her frequent use of passive voice made it sometimes difficult to follow along The story itself is interesting and complicated enough to keep a reader engaged.
