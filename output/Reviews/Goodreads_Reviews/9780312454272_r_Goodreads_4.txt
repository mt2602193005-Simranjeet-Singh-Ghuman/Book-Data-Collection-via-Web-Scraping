Overview of Arthur from a scholar who read everything Arthur. Great example of how history and legend were not distinct categories for pre-moderns. And how legend moves history: Edward III was inspired by the tales to found The Order of the Garter and build a round table. Elizabeth I had a genealogy tracing her lineage back to Arthur. Most generations have their own Arthur retellings: I'm pumped for Malcolm Guite's upcoming "Merlin’s Isle: An Arthuriad".
