I enjoyed this story from Alexis Harrington about Sarah and Zach, I thought it was sweet. I must admit that Sarah got on my last nerves at times with her crying and placing blame on Zach when, stuff wasnt always his fault. Likewise, Zach got on my nerves with is constant misplaced concern for things that weren't really important. I liked watching the attraction grow between the two, and how in the end they worked together to get to a common goal. This isn't one of my favorites by the author, I much prefer Harper's Bride and the Bridal Veil, but this was still a nice romantic read, which was just what I was looking for, so if you enjoy timepiece romance books, then you will enjoy this one as well.
