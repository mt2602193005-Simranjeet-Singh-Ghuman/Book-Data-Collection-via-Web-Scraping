Elspeth McGillicuddy is travelling by train. When another train rides alongside her train, she can’t help her curiosity from taking control as she glances inside the other train. But what she sees takes her by surprise as a man strangles a woman to death. Elspeth wants to do the right thing, but nobody believes her as she’s the only witness and no body has been found. Nobody but her friend, Miss Marple. Fantastic start. This story just has a very cool premise that immediately grabs you. The trouble with starting off your story so strong, is that it gives you certain expectations. And it’s hard for the rest of the story to keep you hooked until the end of the book. While the story does slow down after the beginning, I expected that the murder mystery would manage to intrigue me until the end. Unfortunately, I started to lose interest after a while to be honest. Will admit, a pretty cool ending though. The story definitely started and ended on a high note. Overall, a solid cozy mystery with a cool premise.
