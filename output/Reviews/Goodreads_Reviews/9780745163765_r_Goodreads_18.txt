An oldie but a goodie. Dystopian fiction at it's best from John Wyndham. The main character David appears at first to be 'normal'. Anyone with a birth defect is a deviant and either killed outright or sent off to The fringes to live with the other mutant. As he grows up David becomes aware of others like him who can communicate in thought patterns. (Telepathy). This if discovered would be classed as deviant and they and he would be in grave danger. David has to protect his friends and especially his younger sister so when danger threatens he takes them away to find a home on the Fringes. Lots of interesting scenarios and overall a really good story. I did feel the end was a little too wrapped up for my liking but still well worth a read. 3 1/2 * rounded up to 4
