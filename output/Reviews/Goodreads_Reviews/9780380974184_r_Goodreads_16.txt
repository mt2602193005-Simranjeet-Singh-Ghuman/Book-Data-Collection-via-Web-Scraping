I mean, at times it was entertaining, I'll give it that. I feel a little bad giving it one star, but I really didn't enjoy this. Campbell exists in a weird space of making very generalising and sweeping statements about different genres of music and how they can be used for healing, and very specific anecdotes about people he met and worked with by using "toning" to heal (in one example he claimed to cure a breast tumour). Still, kind of a funny thing to look at as an example of new age 90s "medicine."
