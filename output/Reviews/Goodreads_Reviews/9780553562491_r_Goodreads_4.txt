This has been on my "want to reread" list for a long while. But I was always aware it was a long book and I kept putting it off. Clearly that wasn't necessary as I read it in about 3 days. I was kind of surprised at just how much I loved it and ended up giving it a 10/10 because the reading experience was so good. There are some things about it that had dated a little, but as a whole the story is surprisingly good in 2018. There's one more after this, written much more recently. I'm not sure if I'm going to go on to that, or if I'll wait and see if any further books are forthcoming.
