In my English class junior year, we had a choose an author and write a critical paper about several of their books. My 8th grade English teacher highly recommended Joyce Carol Oates, so I decided to read some of hers for this paper. Why I chose this tome is beyond me... maybe the story sounded interesting from the blurb on the back? The writing style she adopted for this novel was so long-winded--I'm talking parathetical thoughts that go on for three pages. The time frame of the novel was weird too--for some characters the novel took place over a couple of months, for others it was decades, and the landscape seemed to change like millenia had passed--which, once I finished reading this, was how much time I felt had passed as well.
