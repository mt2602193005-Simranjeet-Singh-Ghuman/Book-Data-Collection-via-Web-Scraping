Misleading. Disappointing. It's not about books that the book class read and only once is a book read correlated with a member's life. The Book Class was formed by 12 16-year-old NYC debutantes in the 20s. The novel takes a brief look at the lives of some of these high-society women in the 50s and some children and one tangentially related person, one member's husband's secretary. I'm just not that interested in these women's lives. Though it's well written, I found it boring. I had to make myself finish it for a reading challenge.
