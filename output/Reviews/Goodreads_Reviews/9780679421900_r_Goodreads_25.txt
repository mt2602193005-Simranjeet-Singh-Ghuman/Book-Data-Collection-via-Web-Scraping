What a wonderful journey into the world of chocolate this was! The author focuses on two big giants in the candy world, Hershey and Mars, and she does a marvelous job telling their stories with lots of delectable details. She takes you into the jungles where chocolate starts, back in time with the founders, into the Willy Wonka-like factories, and into the cutthroat corporate world of these competitive companies. I will never be able to look at a Snickers bar, M&Ms, or Hershey bar the same. Warning: there are side effects to reading this book - you will be tempted to buy and eat lots of chocolate. Content issues: A few of the people interviewed in this book used some pretty nasty language, but thankfully it was brief.
