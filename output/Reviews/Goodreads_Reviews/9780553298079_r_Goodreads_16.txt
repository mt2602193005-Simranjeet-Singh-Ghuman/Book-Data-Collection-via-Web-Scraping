The Queen of Western Historical Romance, Rosanne Bittner, never ceases to amaze her readers with her picturesque ways of telling a story. We are so blessed to be living during a time when the greatest western story teller, graces us with her majestic works of written art!!!!!! Outlaw Hearts is a fabulous piece of extraordinary work. It is filled with regrets, revenge, romance, and reunion. Jake and Miranda's (Randy's) love and courage remind us of what true devotion and love can conquer. 5 stars just isn't enough for this wonderful book. Rosanne, you have done it again. Excellent book!!!!
