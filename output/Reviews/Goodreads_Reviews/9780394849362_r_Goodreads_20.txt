I just love Richard Scarry books. Although this tome is aimed at small children (as I was when I first read it) I still find it charming today. As an illustrator, I really appreciate the cute, fun artwork, especially the Swiss influences in some of the architecture and clothing, and the stories themselves are well-written, happy, and funny. This book is also great for Christmas because it includes some songs, gift projects, and games that pretty much anyone can do. The idea of the apple pomander has always stuck with me since I first read this book as a little kid and one of these Christmases, I'm going to actually try it!
