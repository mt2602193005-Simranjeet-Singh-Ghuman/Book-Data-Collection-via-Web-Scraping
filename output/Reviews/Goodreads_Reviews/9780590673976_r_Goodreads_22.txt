I really enjoyed this book. I thought there was just the right amount of baby sitting vs outside drama. The mystery jumped the shark a bit but what do you expect? I really enjoyed this quote from Mallory. “As I read I thought, how lucky I was. I mean, just to be reading. When you read you can sit in your room and travel back and forth in time,…”. Definitely felt that reading this book about the 80s. Speaking of, if you need a fashion fix, p78 of this book gives a description of what each member of the club is wearing at the meeting. Have to laugh at Mallory’s “I ❤️ kids” shirt. The girl is only 11!!
