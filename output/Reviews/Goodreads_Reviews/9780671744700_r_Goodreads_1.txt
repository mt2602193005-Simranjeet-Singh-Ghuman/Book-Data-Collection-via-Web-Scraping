This book started with some serious promise. I was immediately hooked but about a third of the way in, I started to feel like the writing almost got lazy. The calculated killer all of a sudden wasn’t so calculated anymore. This may be naivety on my part, but the fact that his pattern of killing families because of their own secret language was an unbelievable pattern and anticlimactic. The detective’s wife became an increasing more annoying character as the book went on. Did I overall enjoy it? I guess. I don’t regret reading it but I probably won’t read it again.
