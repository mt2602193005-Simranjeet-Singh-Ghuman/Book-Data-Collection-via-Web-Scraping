This was a good book but seemed a little more heavy on the drama. I'm thinking closer to 3 1/2 stars cuz it was really good, but it also had me wanting to throw the book a few times. I liked the characters and their personalities (mostly). This was written well and flowed, it pulled me in some but it didn't quite immerse me in the story. I got aggravated countless times with the main characters and just wanted to stuff them in a room together and throw away the key until they communicated ! I did like the the plot and there was a little twist that I saw coming, but still didn't quite expect. There were definitely some really touching moments though, and I really enjoyed those to the fullest. And although the couple did get on my nerves, I still really felt for them and what they had went through and what was going on at the same time. This was definitely a book that got me going back and forth between sympathy and understanding to exasperation. A good read and I enjoyed reading it. Good for reading for something to read, snuggling in, or for the waiting room. Have at it!
