I loaned this book to a friend with dog troubles. She gave it back to me explaining that she couldn't finish it. The book was just too large. She was just too busy to process it all. This is ironic as Fennel's methods easily blend into ordinary daily person <-> dog interactions and are very simple. The problem is that the book is 4 times larger than it needs to be. Her methods could have been explained in about 50 - 100 pages with plenty of room left over for some illustrative anecdotal accounts. A good editor could have saved this book. The same goes for her video, which I found terribly disjointed. If I had not read the book I would not have understood what she was trying to communicate. Fennel's methods are truly innovative, cruelty free, cheap, and practical. I hope she decides to hire an editor for her books and her videos to make her ideas easily accessible. It could make a difference in the lives of many dogs
