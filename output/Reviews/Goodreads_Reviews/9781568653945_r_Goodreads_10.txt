Captivating Storyline, interesting characters It's been a while since I read a Belva Plain book. I hope to read another of her books real soon. Charlotte is a young teenage girl when the story first starts. She has a very traumatic experience that continues to shape her relationships, family and really her life. The storyline kept me not wanting to put it down. Some of the characters, l know. Both of those "attributes" made this a great book.
