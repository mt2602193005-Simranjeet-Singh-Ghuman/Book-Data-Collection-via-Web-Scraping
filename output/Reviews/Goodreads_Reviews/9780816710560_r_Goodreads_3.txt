Heinel really had one hell of an adventure. His father unwittingly sold him to a dwarf for gold, he outwitted the dwarf by faking his death, he was abused by people and then rescued a princess, married her and became king, did not listen to his queen, got betrayed by her, outwitted giants, got revenge on his wife, killed the royal guests who mocked him. That was a lot for one story and one lifetime, well, in a fairy tale. I liked this story well enough but I thought it was not as good as some of the other fairy tales.
