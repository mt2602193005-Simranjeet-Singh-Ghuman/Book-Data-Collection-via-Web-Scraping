There is much darkness in the heart of some who walk among us, who wear the trappings of a luxurious life, who view others as mere objects to destroy and own. Whose pomposity erased any humanity, any empathy or compassion, for satisfying their own greedy needs and get away with it due to their connections, bribes, and heartless disposal of their victims once done. This book is about those soulless brutes who turn to the dark web with their shopping list of desire and purchase humans. The specifications of their 'toy' is matched by various teams who snatch victims, and at the heart of the organization is a Russian who goes by "Wolf." ...and this book is about our MC's move to his fast-track training at Quantico. Due to his uncanny success, he is treated like a senior agent, pulled out from norm FBI protocols and team building to provide insight on cases. And there's little Alex...his son whose mother makes an appearance and seems to be looking to be ready to resume full time care. Lots of violent action, lots of dark nightmarish stuff...small pockets of sunshine. Hug your humanity.
