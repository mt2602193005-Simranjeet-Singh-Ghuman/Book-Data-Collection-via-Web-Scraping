I have enjoyed studying the history of Joseph Smith. Rough Stone Rolling by Richard Bushman was a great read. The Saints volumes published by the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (primarily Vol. 1) are also good. I have also read material critical of Joseph Smith, including the CES letter and portions of No Man Knows My History by Faun Brodie. This book is a recollection of Joseph's life by his mother, Lucy Mack Smith. I had never heard about some of the accounts in this book, like Joseph's difficult trip from Harmony, PA to Palmyra, NY to check on Martin Harris with the early translation, the early persecution of Joseph's father, and the escape of Samuel Smith from the mob in Missouri to Quincy, IL. I recommend this book to anybody who earnestly would like to know more about Joseph Smith.
