This is a fascinating novel that is written a series of letters from Chinese emigrant, Tan Suang U, to his mother back in his home village in China. He has come to Thailand just after WWII in hopes of finding a way to make a fortune or at least a good living. He ends up staying, marrying, having children, operating a successful business in Bangkok's Chinatown. The book does a great job of comparing life in China to life in Thailand - noting cultural attitudes, activities, and food among other things. While some might be under the impression that all Southeast Asians are alike, this book points out both similarities and major differences. The book is a classic in Thailand. Written by Botan (a pseudonym for Supa Sirisingh) in 1971 and translated to English in 2002. It has a slow-moving plot, but a multitude of interesting facts and opinions.
