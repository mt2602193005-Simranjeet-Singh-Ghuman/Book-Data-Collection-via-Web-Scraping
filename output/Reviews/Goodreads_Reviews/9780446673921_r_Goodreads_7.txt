Handy Little book you can throw in your bag for some yoga at work or on the go. Erika Dillman was an active runner before developing such a severe case of Chronic Fatigue Syndrome that she was unable to do literally anything for years, until she discovered yoga. It helped her build up strength and stamina (initially, she was exhausted after five minutes of class, but she persevered, and after several months, could participate in a full class). Dillman spends a little time explaining what yoga is (and isn't), demystifying some myths commonly believed regarding yoga - it's not a religion, but a health practice. Then she goes on to describe some basic asanas (poses), offering tips to gain greater benefits from the poses, as well as listing several sample workouts to try at home. Includes a grouping of recommended poses according to health problems (poses listed can help alleviate said ailments), sports/activities (poses to perform before or after physical activity), and body parts (poses that will focus on a particular body part). Essential for anyone wanting to practice yoga, whether they are a beginner, or a life-long practitioner. Show more
