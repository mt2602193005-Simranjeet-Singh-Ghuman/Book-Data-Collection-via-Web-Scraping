This book has been slowly eating me alive for like 8 months. I think it could be good to someone who’s not me, but I just could not get into it. The only thing I will say in its favour, is that there was exactly one plot twist (and no it was not the main plot twist) that I genuinely did not see coming, and I blame it mostly on the fact that I was reading this between studying for an exam and wasn’t thinking that hard about where it was going.
