Sci fi/anthropology/religion/thriller with a message: sacrifice judgement, accept faith, acknowledge and return God's love, which we don't deserve. It's somewhat entertaining in a Clive Cussler-ish sort of way, with some pretty gory death scenes in the jaws of genetically engineered Pleistocene predators, but not enough so that I would recommend reading it. The author's anti-Arab bias is particularly irritating.
