The first Amélie Nothomb work that I’ve read and now I’m hooked. The dialogue brims with character similar to that of a Howard Hawks classic. The plot is simplistic which makes it so accessible. Some of the ideas that made me laugh or ponder included the need for marriages to be renewed every couple of years (great idea), that the south no longer exists, the new age caste system, as well as the use of crosswords to keep certain classes busy, and the M.I.M - moi, ici, maintenant (otherwise known as the power of now)…. A superb book. Perfect for a long train journey. Enjoy!
