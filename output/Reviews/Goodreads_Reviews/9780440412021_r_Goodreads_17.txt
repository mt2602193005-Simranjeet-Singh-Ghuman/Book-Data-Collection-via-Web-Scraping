During the annual spring break carnival, Cam and Eric become curious about the dime toss game. It’s very hard to toss a dime into the center of a circle, yet eight times various children get a dime in the exact center of the middle circle. The winners are four girls and four boys. As Cam reviews her mind pictures she realizes that the four boys are really one boy. The same is true of the girl. Cam and Eric saw the boy leaving the carnival just before it started. He was carrying a roll of tape. Cam stood behind the girl at the refreshment booth and remembers that she talked about her lucky dime. When Cam examines the dime toss board and looks through the dimes used to play the game, she solves the mystery. ©2024 Kathy Maxwell at https://bookskidslike.com
