Many people misquote the Apostle Paul in saying, "money is the root of all evil." Others grasp for it and pursue fame and fortune as if their very lives depended on it. The Bible has more to say about money than practically any other topic. Derek Prince systematically reviews biblical principles, explaining how money can be part of our worship to God, how our use of it expresses the condition of our hearts, principles of sowing and reaping and putting God first. Not a large or difficult read, it is convicting. In his usual spirit of grace and humility, Derek Prince won't beat you over the head or lay a guilt trip on you. He lets the Word of God and His Holy Spirit gently convict you, so that you can make the changes necessary to be a true steward of God--amply provided for, but freely giving of the resources God gives you to manage for Him. Every follower of Christ should read this!
