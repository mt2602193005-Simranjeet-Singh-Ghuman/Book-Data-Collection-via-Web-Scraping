After seeing 'The Green Knight', I decided to re-read 'Sir Gawain and the Green Knight', forty-five years after the first time. I remember not liking it then, but I actually loved it this time. Mature tastes, or just more patience? Certainly a lot more understanding of the symbolisms beyond the obvious Christian ones that long-ago professor focused on. Of note: I re-read it in the same book I first read it in. Surprised to find I lugged it around all these years.
