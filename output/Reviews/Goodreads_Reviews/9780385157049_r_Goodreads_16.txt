Read similarly to how I remember reading the juvenile short-story anthologies of mysteries, except Asimov doesn't always play fair; some of the solutions relied upon only the most tenuous of logic or the most esoteric of trivial knowledge. Others (probably in part because they've been ripped off and repurposed by other authors since) were too obvious. But some were perfect. Happy to have read it. (This is my standard review for Isaac Asimov short-stories outside the sci-fi genre.)
