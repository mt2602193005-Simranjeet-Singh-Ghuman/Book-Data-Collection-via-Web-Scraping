As a GenXer, quicksand and the Bermuda Triangle were both fairly predominant topics during my childhood. So when I spotted this in a used book store, I had to pick it up. Author Kusche debunks a lot of the so-called "mystery" of the Bermuda Triangle. He outlines many maritime & airplane disasters/accidents that were attributed to the Bermuda Triangle but which, in reality, were sometimes nowhere close to the geographic area, were explainable by weather, mechanical failure, or other issues, & also includes the unknown/unsolved disappearances. He discusses how he (a librarian at Arizona State University) researched the "disappearances", including extensive records searches, contacting Lloyd's of London, reports from agencies in both the US & other countries, & sometimes even talking with a few survivors of the accidents. Considering the book is approximately 50 years old, it was an interesting refresher to see the amount of work needed to research these topics (vs. the relative ease of doing so today). Some disappearances have more info than others (for example, when a military inquiry was completed & he was able to get extensive records from the investigation vs. some vague stories that seem to be completely fabricated). I learned a lot about multiple tragic losses (from the Mary Celeste & the Atalanta to Flight 19 & the Star Tiger) & think how hard all of these instances must have been for those meeting their fates this way & for the loved ones left behind. I found it fascinating yet sobering.
