Don't tell my grandmother, but this was pretty meh. I didn’t hate it, but I didn’t love it either—it was just... okay. Nothing extraordinary happens, which is a shame because the premise sounded so intriguing. The romance? Total snooze-fest. I couldn’t get invested in it at all. With cheating as one of the themes, I expected strong emotions, tension, something , but it was mostly just crying and a lot of repetitive drama. Honestly, it left me feeling pretty indifferent.
