There's just no accounting for certain reading choices that I made around the turn of the century. I happened to be thinking about Scientology today (don't ask), wondering why they've never fielded a candidate for president and whether or not John Travolta could win California, when I remembered that I had actually read a book of L. Ron Hubbard's once, a long time ago- July 2000, according to my records, when I was fourteen- about which I remember nothing, except for a vague impression of a subpar Twilight Zone episode. But why, and under what circumstances, did I read it? Well, one of the reviews here implores readers not to be lured in by the Stephen King blurb on the cover. And since I liked Stephen King when I was a teenager, I guess that must have been the reason. Case closed.
