This was my first Danielle Steel experience and wow this lady sure can write. I do not regret reading this book, but I’m grateful for how short it is. If you’re thinking about reading this one I say absolutely go for it for the countless nightgown descriptions and the elusive quadruple flip. The Klone and I is a perfect group beach read- we had a lot of fun reading lines out loud to each other, but god I am glad to be done this book.
