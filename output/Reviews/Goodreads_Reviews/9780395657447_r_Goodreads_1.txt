A book that must be read-aloud to children, Rachel Fister's Blister by Amy McDonald is a darling tale about the many suggested cures for a little girl's blistered toe. The illustrations are scribbled sketches that perfectly fit the chaotic storyline. For coming up with the ultimate solution, the Queen is the smarest in the land, but mom will always be the best at making a boo-boo go away. It's one for my F.C.L.P.(Future Classroom Library Pile) for sure.
