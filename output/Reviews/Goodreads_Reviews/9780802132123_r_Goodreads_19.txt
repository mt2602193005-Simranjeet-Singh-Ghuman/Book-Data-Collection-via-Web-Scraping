Dennis Cooper Closer is about the closeness between people, especially lonely and broken people. I feel like, despite the amount of sexual relationships in this book, nobody ever gets any closer, especially not to George. George seems to be looking for love, but at the same time he doesn't. The men around him like the idea of him, his beauty, but nothing else. They don't really want to know him. As someone who lived through the death of her mother under similar circumstances, the self-destruction and the grieving before the inevitability of death felt very real to me. I felt like nobody had a real connection in this entire book, and that's why the title Closer felt so powerful. Dennis Cooper is a lot more than an outrageous author. His quotes are memorable, and so are his characters.
