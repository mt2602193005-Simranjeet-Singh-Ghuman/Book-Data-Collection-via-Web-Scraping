This German version of the Star Wars cookbook contains the translations of all recipes from both of the original versions. Even for someone who has little experience in cooking and baking, the recipes are fully comprehensible and easy to follow. However, a number of the recipes in this book are far from innovative: Although the Wookiee Cookies were as good as I had hoped they would be, they were hardly any different from the cookies I usually bake. Some of the recipes therefore seem quite unimaginative, whereas others in fact gave me new ideas especially for drinks and desserts. For German die-hard Star Wars fans (especially those with little cooking experience) this book is definitely a must-have, but certainly not very useful for people looking for completely new ideas and challenges.
