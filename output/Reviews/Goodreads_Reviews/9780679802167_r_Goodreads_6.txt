A beginning reader featuring folk tale stars, Brer Rabbit and Brer Fox. In this trickster tale, Brer Fox is trying to trap Brer Rabbit, but Brer Rabbit has his own set of tricks to foil the conniving fox. You may need to explain to young readers what briars are before reading this. Other than that term, the rest of the book is a quick and easy read featuring two tricksters. The illustrations are attractive, and make this a simple book to include in a trickster tale unit.
