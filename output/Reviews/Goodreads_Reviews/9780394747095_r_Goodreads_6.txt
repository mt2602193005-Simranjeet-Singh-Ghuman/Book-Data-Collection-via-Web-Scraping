This work is one of the best autobiographies I know. It's true; you must love Sartre and adore him even a little so as not to tire. However, understanding a writer's journey from his childhood in books allows you to dream and idealize the author. He tells his life through episodes and anecdotes and analyzes it, not always tender with himself, even though we sense a peaceful, calm tone. However, he did not write it just before his death but long before, in full glory. The text is a tribute to those who inspire us, to those who make us discover a passion, and finally, a hymn to beautiful letters.
