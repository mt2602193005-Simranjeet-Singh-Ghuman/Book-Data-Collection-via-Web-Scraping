I am really very tired of the number of authors who feel the need to constantly specify when a character is black every time they're mentioned. That peeve aside, this book waan't the best I've read. The writing was lackluster and awkward, the story wandered all over the place (random sex scene that could have been hot and kinky but was a) awkward, too and b) written weirdly) as if the author kept forgetting what story they were trying to tell, the villain was too incompetent to be believable at all and I'm not sure if it was my copy or if all copies are like that but the book is full of typos. The ending, too, was rushed and anticlimactic to me. I don't think there can even be said to have been any kind of suspense as I read...at all. I liked the idea, but it could have and should have been executed far better.
