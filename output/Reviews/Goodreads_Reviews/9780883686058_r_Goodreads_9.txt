"Do not seek angels. Seek Jesus! He is far greater than any angel!" I enjoyed this book more than I expected. In fact, I read half of it in a single day. Honestly, it is not the kind of book I would normally pick up, but a pastor I trust recommended it. At first, I thought it would be just another book focused on angels, but what I appreciated most was that the author spent less time emphasizing his experiences with angels, even though the accounts he shares are truly remarkable. I am sure he could have focused much more on those encounters if he had wanted to, but instead he remained grounded in God's Word and devoted more attention to sharing the revelations he received. Throughout the book, the focus remains on God's love for us and the power of Christ's atonement. They were beautiful experiences that God allowed this pastor to have. “Some of these teachers dissect and categorize the Bible until it loses its life. Gabriel said, "Read the Word, feed on it, let it become the Living Word to you, not just columns of truths and opinions of men."” “Jesus wants to be known as the Living Christ, coming out the pages of the Bible to us! He added that though study sounds spiritual, some people would always be learning, and never doing, because by the time they acquired knowledge of one type of a study or doctrine, it was out-of-date and they had to start all over again!” "Feed on the Word." He repeated, "Feed on the Word!" Don't settle for the dissected Word torn into little bits and analyzed by sections. Keep it the Living Word!” “God showed his keen interest in families by forming the home before he made the church. He wants that same wonderful unity, and that recognition of himself, which people expect to feel only in the church, to also be in the home.”
