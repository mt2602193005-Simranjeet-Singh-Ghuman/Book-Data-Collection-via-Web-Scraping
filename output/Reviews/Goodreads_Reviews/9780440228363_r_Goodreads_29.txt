The Watson's Go To Birmingham was a very typical 60's book with segregation and all that junk. This book is really nothing special. In general this was a very slow pace book, which I honestly did not like. I prefer books that keep you on the edge of your seat and that get to the point. I'm not saying that the author shouldn't give the reader some background information, but you shouldn't have to wait until the last 4 chapters of a 20 chapter book to finally reach the main event of the story (I'm just using this as an example I don't know how many chapters were in this book and exactly when the Watson's actually went to Birmingham). I have also experienced this with some of this author's other books. Overall, this book was not very interesting with cliché characters and a boring plot.
