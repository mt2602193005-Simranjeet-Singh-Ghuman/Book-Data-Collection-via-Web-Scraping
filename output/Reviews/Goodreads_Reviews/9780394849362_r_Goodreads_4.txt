I might be a bit biased with this book, even reading it as an almost-28 year old, because I LOVED these back when I was a kid... everyone around was living on Richard Scarry's Busytown books in my country in that era (and later on the oh so many hilarious parodies which were about criminals, college students or 21th century people). And I still find these adorable. I still find myself loving nice children's books and cartoons, but I rarely find one which are cute, appropriate for kids (complexity and quality of language, stories etc.) but not sickening headache-inducing saccharine sweet which regards kids as braindeads, not as kids... and this book was exactly the type I love. I could still read it without thinking "Oh this is dumb" "This is fakely cutesy" and so on... The stories were nice, fun, cute, could be read by anyone in any era, taught a lot of nice things to kids and it also had a nice set of words to learn at the end, a built-in board game (fun, but easy enough to understand for everyone) and even a great home-made gift tip. It described a really nice town with all its funny and cute citizens.
