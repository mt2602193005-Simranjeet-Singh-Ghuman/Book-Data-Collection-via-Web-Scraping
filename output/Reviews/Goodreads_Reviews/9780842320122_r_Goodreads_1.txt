I really wanted this to be an epic saga that drew me in and became a new favorite series but...sadly, it fell far below expectations. Though I enjoyed the beginning of the story, I was glad to finish it by the end. The pace is very, VERY slow...except when randomly 8 years go by. The characters were enjoyable in the beginning but by page 200 or so, you're thinking "oh my gosh...300 more pages like this?!" But you keep reading because surely it will get better...right? {spoiler alert!} WRONG. Thomas becomes a crazy person! I mean, I was glad that in the end Thomas and Jocelyn are FINALLY happy...but it was for like 5 minutes before the end of the book. I felt seriously jipped.
