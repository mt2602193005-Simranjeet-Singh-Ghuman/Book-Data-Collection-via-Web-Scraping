This is a middle-grade read. I really enjoyed it. I love a good ghost story and this was exciting and well written, especially towards the younger crowd. Hope it wouldn't be too scary for them. I enjoyed it though. Staying in an old mansion which had been abandoned after a dual murder 3 years prior, the ghosts of the dead are unsettled. Ten year old Christina is determined to find out why the ghosts are there and to solve the mystery of the murders. Then the ghosts start acting up forcing her and her uncle to leave the house. This is my kind of story.
