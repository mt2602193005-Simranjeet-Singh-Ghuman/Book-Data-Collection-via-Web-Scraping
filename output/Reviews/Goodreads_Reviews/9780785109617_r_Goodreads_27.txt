Starting out with a quick two-shot standalone introducing Gambit to the Ultimate universe (and also some of the most enh line art by Esad Ribic), the "World Tour" arc takes us a little deeper into Colossus' character and also reminds us what a dick Xavier can be by making the X-Men's next adventure be to track down his abandoned son, David, before he destroys half the world in an "Ultimate" take on Claremont's Proteus arc. Thankfully, outside of introducing Betsy "Psylocke" Braddock to the storyline, Millar keeps the X-Men roster the same to allow for better character development. Adam Kubert's pencil work really hits its stride between issues 15 and 17 with some really awesome art: just in time to be replaced with Chris Bachalo who makes the X-Men look like they belong in an episode of Undergrads. Three stars for an engaging storyline that improves on the previous volumes, but the art takes a hit halfway through and a less attractive Gambit I've never seen.
