Charles Eastman (Ohiyesa) in his own words. Wonderful, simple. The story arc is beautiful and instructive. Eastman describes being pulled from his native tribe shortly before going on warpath. He hates it at first but is later stunned by the things that his culture was lacking. However, by the end of the novel, the degree of corruption and abuse he sees among the "Christian" culture he's adopted leaves him wondering if he's made the right choice.
