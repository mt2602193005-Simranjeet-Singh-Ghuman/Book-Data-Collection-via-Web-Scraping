I am disappointed in this book. I have read many of Ms. Woods books and was thoroughly delihted with them, but not this one. It is not typical of her writing ability. One of the main characters, Richard, is an egotistical snob, his Aunt Destiny is a control feak, and Melanie is as spineless as they come, letting the other two characters walk all over her. The story is repetitious with little action and becomes boring. It just misses the mark!
