I hate when we lack sources. Alexander does the best she can with what we have, and I think she makes a solid revisionist claim on Bligh not being as terrible as the skewed historical record would suggest. She also does a good job unteasing the threads of the trial and explaining contradictory statements to get at the "most likely" truth of things. But damn. We have next to no information on what happened with Christian and the mutineers afterward, our sole source is basically one surviving mutineer decades after the fact who had an obviously vested interest in lying about various things (and lied in different ways over the years) so at the end of the day we just don't know. I was also surprised that Bligh's ultra-competent navigation in an open boat to Coupang received such short shrift. I mean I guess there's not much to say when it's just day after day of sailing in a small boat with no change and little food, but she could've done a better job of building a picture, making me invested in it, etc.
