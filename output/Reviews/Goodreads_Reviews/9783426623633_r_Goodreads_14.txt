I'm probably the last person under the sun not to have seen Julie Taymor's Frida , based on this biography (which is changing as I write these words). I find rating biographies difficult - do I rate a book, or a life? - but I think I can safely give it 3.5-4 stars, with the disclaimer that my ratings are more lenient for biographies than for fiction. The description of the birth of creativity, and subsequent relations between Frida and Diego, and other people in her life, were interesting, thorough, and touching. The image of Frida and Diego locating each other in the crowd by means of whistling The International will stay with me. This book has its faults, pointed out by other reviewers, but I find them minor, not enough to spoil my reading experience.
