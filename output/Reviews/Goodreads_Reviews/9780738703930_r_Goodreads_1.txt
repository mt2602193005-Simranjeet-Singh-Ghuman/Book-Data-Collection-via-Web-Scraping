I first read this book back in 2003. It was the first book of Ellen's that I'd ever read and I've been hooked on her books ever since. And don't let the "for Teens" in the title fool you; this book is PERFECT for any beginner to the Craft. Ellen is funny, personable, and gives several personal anecdotes to compliment the information she gives. Her information is quite accurate, she gives healthy emphasis on learning the ethics, not being a dabbler, and not doing any magick that screws with anyone's free will, etc. She also places emphasis on the fact that you don't have to have fancy altar tools to be a witch. Naturally she acknowledges that there's nothing wrong with having altar tools, just that a Witch is not crippled from doing magick if they do not have all the thingamabobs. She tells about the beauty of simplicity and the magick that is done when we get close with Nature. So whether you are a beginner seeking a good foundation from which to begin practicing or you're a veteran witch just looking for a down-to-earth refresher, this book is pure delight!
