I grabbed this book during my last visit to the local library. I soon discovered it was among the earliest books published by Lisa Scottoline, dated 1995. What is truly hard to miss are the telltale signs that Scottoline was a very talented writer who cleverly inserts comedic comments into an otherwise thrilling storyline. I couldn't put this book down. Smart-talking lawyer Rita Morrone takes on the case of the father of her live-in boyfriend. He's a judge charged with murdering his mistress. However, the best parts of this book involve the ongoing commentary of Rita's own father and his elderly friends who she comes to heavily rely on to prove her client's innocence.
