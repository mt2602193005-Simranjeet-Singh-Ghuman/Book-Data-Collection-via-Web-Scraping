1997: An amusing one-shot mystery where the detective is a trial attorney—not adverse to a few tricks—who investigates a case she has taken to defend her future father-in-law [bad move] and winds up investigating murder. Along the way there are some esp. funny parts involving her father’s cronies. Set in Philadelphia—L.S. Is a U. Penn Law grad. 2020: I doubt I would enjoy the book as much now because I have become averse to mysteries where the detective is a lawyer.
