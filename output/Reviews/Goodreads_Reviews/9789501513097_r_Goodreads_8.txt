Johanna Lindsey knows how to write an entertaining love story. I laughed out loud several times. Angel's reactions to Cassie's "pet" were amusing. A panther! I went with it because the wild animal turned domesticated could also be a metaphor for Angel's ultimate taming by Cassie, being pretty wild himself. I wasn't expecting to like Angel as much as I did. His brief appearances in the preceding books combined with his shady background didn't make me think he'd be hero material. Well... I was wrong! He was a great hero: dangerous yet noble, tender yet tough, etc. As for Cassie she's sweet and strong-willed, exactly the way I like my heroines. Her tendency to meddle, although well-intentioned, initially created more trouble than solutions but they all panned out in the end. I liked seeing these two get together. Poor Angel couldn't stay away from Cassie in spite of himself! LOL Their romance was as fun and cute as they were.
