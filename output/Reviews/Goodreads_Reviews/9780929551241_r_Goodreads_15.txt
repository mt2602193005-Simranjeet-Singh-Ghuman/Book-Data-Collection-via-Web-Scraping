Many of the things I read in this book remind me of the students I’ve taught who had dyslexia. It’s eye-opening for me as I have been bothered often by the niggling thought that my dyslexic student is bright and would be easy to teach if I could just find the right way. This book confirms that thought. It does seem to lean a little too heavily on the author’s experiences and not enough on scientific research. Having said that, how can we ever know the science of the brain’s many and multilevel functions. Im going to give it a try in the classroom before I race too much though. Meet you back here.
