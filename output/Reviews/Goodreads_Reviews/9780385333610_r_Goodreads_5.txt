This series is a bit of a mess. Or at least the two I’ve read. Nice enough heroines with heroes that just don’t deserve them. Temper tantrums, meanness and lack of communication are not patched over by good hair. Some of the lines Patrick used early on in their relationship put me in mind of a high school boy. “Come on, babe. Just the tip...” Definitely a bit skeezy and light on the consent. I wanted something insubstantial after the Novik. Boy did I get it.
