I can safely say one of the strangest books I have ever read, but in a good way. It is a rather interesting spin on a First Contact scenario and one that I never actually thought about. How would the arrival and availability of advanced alien technology affect the businesses/economy of the world? That is the premises of the book and I think Greg Costikyan did a good job of examining that issue in a fun way. The book is an easy read and a slight bit of a page turn as you wonder what obstacle will be throw in the main character's path. The book's end is nice and funny surprise. It makes for a nice easy read and it was a welcome break from all the serious, real world topic books I am also reading.
