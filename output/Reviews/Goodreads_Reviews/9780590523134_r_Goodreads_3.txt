So apparently I'm experiencing a bit of a Baby-Sitters Club redux right now. This is the first book in the "Friends Forever" series, which came out right after the original series ended with Mary Anne's house burning down. This series is a bit different as it focuses on the four original characters. Also, the new series doesn't really focus on baby-sitting. In fact, in this book there is no mention of anyone ever even having a baby-sitting job. There are two mentions of a BSC meeting, but they barely talk about the actual baby-sitting and it seems like the meetings are mostly just there for the social aspect. Kristy even mentions that the BSC "doesn't meet as often as they used to." Weird. I guess this was something that was covered in "Everything Changes," the first super special of the series, which I haven't been able to get my hands on yet. Anyway, the premise of this book is that Kristy's deadbeat father is getting re-married, and he wants Kristy and her older brothers Charlie and Sam to come to his wedding in California. He doesn't invite David Michael, her younger brother, for some inexplicable reason. People keep saying it's because he's too young, but I'm not buying it. Anyway, after much fighting and arguing and pouting, all three of them end up flying out to California to spend a week with their dad and his fiancee before the wedding. I actually felt like this book covered a pretty sensitive issue, and I thought it was handled well. Certainly having your father abandon you when you are young is traumatic enough, but then to have him suddenly reappear and want to be part of your life years later would be extremely difficult. Especially as a teenager, as Charlie so eloquently demonstrates. I thought the writer did a nice job of portraying Kristy's conflicting emotions about her father and the whole event. Anyway, a good, quick read. I read the entire book in one sitting. Just like old times. :)
