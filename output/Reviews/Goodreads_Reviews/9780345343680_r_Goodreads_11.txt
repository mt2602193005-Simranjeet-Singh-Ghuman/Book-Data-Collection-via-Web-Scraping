#2 In the Elizabethan England mystery series mostly in 1602 with 40 something clothier Matthew Stock and Essex county constable and his wife Joan living in the town of Chelmsford. Matthew is a bit wishy washy physically, so does poorly in confrontations. Joan is a real force in the investigation as she is thoughtful, assertive an perceptive and can be characterized as the brains behind the scenes. Matthew Stock’s young relation, Thomas, leaves home to apprentice himself in London. Then he disappears, the craftsman writes to say Thomas has gone to sea, but Stock is suspicious. He leaves to search for the boy, who meanwhile returns to Essex and tells Joan of the danger he’s in. Show more
