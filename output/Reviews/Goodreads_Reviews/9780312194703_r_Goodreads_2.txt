I LOVED this book. An accessible, readable, demystifying guide to ballet for those who are new to it or returning after many years away, the Joffrey's approach is realistic and extremely helpful. When you start ballet as an adult, you often aren't told what to expect, what to wear, what you'll be able to do, or what a typical class even looks like. This book is an incredible resource for adult beginners, and it's the rare reference book that I actually sat down and read cover-to-cover and plan to refer back to often as I continue taking classes. For ballet nerds who've been considering going back to class... do it. This book will help you get there.
