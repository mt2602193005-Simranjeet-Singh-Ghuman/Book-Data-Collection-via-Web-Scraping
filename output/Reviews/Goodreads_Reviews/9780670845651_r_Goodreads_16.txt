More like a vignette Less a murder mystery and more vignettes of people in a small town, although half of this book is taking place in Vancouver and then moves to a small town for the bigger scene/ finale. Maybe it's a statement about small towns not having crimes. Even the other mystery centered around a man who worked in Vancouver. So, while the author has an interesting writing style, this isn't really much of a mystery book with a mystery to solve, although there is a missing man. And the police are only tangential. Might be a good episode on the show, but not so good for a book.
