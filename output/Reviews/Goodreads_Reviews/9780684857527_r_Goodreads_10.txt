3.5* rounded up because it deserves the better rating. It is probably the best known western novel of all times and deservedly so. It is an epic book, in size and scope. It can be funny and sad at the same time. You get to really care about the characters in 1000+ pages. It is a huge adventure ride with many types of characters, rangers, whores, cowboys, bandits. I loved most of it but I think that the characters deserved a better fate than the misery they got. I probably read it at the wrong time and the ending affected me too much.
