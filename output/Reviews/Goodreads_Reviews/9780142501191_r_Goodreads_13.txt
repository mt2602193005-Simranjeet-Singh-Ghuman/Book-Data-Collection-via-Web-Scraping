Oh my god(dess) This finale was amazing. The first 20 or so pages had me so annoyed and ticked off but this was a truly awesome ending. Not many YA books show the characters as adults but it really tied in with the Wiccan archetype of maiden, mother and crone. The cycle of people and ideas being renewed and the passage of time made this series stand out tremendously to me. Would absolutely recommend giving this series a go
