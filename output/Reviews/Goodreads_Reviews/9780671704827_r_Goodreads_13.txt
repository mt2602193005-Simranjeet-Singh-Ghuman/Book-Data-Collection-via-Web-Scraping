Quite a good novel about a hapless, sodden Englishman who is compelled to find out what happened to a young woman who disappears while in his company; resembles Ian McEwen. Nothing is as it seems as the themes are betrayal and deception. Harry turns out to be a dogged, skillful investigator but he’s unaware of how he’s being manipulated. As in other recent mysteries, adoption is the key although I’m not sure that the revelation that precipitates all the events is quite that damning. The ending is a bit drawn out but there are two twists/reveals to get through. I liked the Greek policeman. Also: the kids in the 80s wear big basketball shoes, not baseball shoes (which have spikes).
