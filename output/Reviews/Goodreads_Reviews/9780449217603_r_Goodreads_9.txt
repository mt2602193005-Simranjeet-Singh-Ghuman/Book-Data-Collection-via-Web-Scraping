Where’s that trapdoor when you need it? Cupboard Kisses was my fifth Metzger book, and I think I just keep trying to relive the charming uniqueness of Miss Lockharte’s Letters , and that’s not going to happen. I need to either throw in the towel or distinctly lower my expectations. My main issue with this book was that the entire misunderstanding could have been remedied by one person recognizing the other or asking the right question. You’re telling me that you didn’t even recognize that this was the same voice as the man you’d had such a recent—and memorable—interaction with? Cristabel and her beau fell in love without deigning to show the reader anything that would’ve caused them to do so, aside from being physically attracted to each other. I don’t expect every romance to be a slow burn, but could they at least be, ya know, romantic? I’ll think twice about picking up another Metzger book after this one, unfortunately.
