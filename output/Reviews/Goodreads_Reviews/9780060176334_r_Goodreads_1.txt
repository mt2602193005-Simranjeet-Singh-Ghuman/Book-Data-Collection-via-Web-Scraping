An attractive little book which tells the the tale of a wolf (man) and a raven (woman) and their relationship. It is set in the natural glory of the north west pacific - native Indian lands and latterly home to the white hunter. In Indian lore the raven assists the wolf in hunting. In this tale Helen, the raven, is partly Indian and partly Scottish; the wolf, Dane, of white European settler stock. The setting for the tale is magnificent and the observation of nature throughout is a joy to read. The story line is good, if predictable. The dialogue however I found wooden and stilted at times and this stopped me reaching for a fourth star.The constant repetition of the main character's name, Dane, I found very irritating and could have been relieved by a few more "he's" sprinkled here and there.
