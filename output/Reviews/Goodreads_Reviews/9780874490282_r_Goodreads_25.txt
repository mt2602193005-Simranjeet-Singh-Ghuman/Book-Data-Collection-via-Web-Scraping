This is the ideal Christmas Eve read! I love Clement C. Moore's classic Christmas poem and Duvoisin's illustrations are perfect. The primary colours work well and the pages with red white and black are even better. This tall size format means the page size is ideal for looking down chimneys and scenes of reindeers landing on rooftops. I enjoyed the foreword and was surprised to learn that this poem was originally written in 1822! Great things are timeless! I would highly recommend this to anyone looking for an addition to their Christmas book collection.
