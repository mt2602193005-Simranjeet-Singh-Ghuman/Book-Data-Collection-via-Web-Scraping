I am really annoyed at having to give this novel a 3-star, but I had to find a way to state my disgust with the publisher of these reprints of Gardner's books. This is yet another one in the series that has missing words, missing letters, wrong or no punctuation, and wrong words (unless they are just misspelled. There is so much of this it takes the pleasure out of reading what is a really good novel, although the who-did-it is telegraphed. I can only say I'm glad I have bought these books at a very low price because in this state, they are not worth more. And yet the story is fun and cute and Perry Mason makes a couple of mistakes that he fixes but that's fun because we see him as more human, less of a lawyer-god. I hope the publisher sees this review and fixes these novels. It's only having someone read them and fix the errors, but then that might cost something. Honestly, it's discouraging how fiction is treated sometimes.
