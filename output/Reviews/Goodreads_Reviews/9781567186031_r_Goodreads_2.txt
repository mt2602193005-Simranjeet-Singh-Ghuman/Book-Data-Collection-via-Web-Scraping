This book of "Pagan songs" is actually partly a specific set of Welsh tales set to music and partly a group of ritual/festival songs. The sheet music is minimalist--it's just a basic idea so you can get the tune--and there are notes and comments from the artist throughout. It's a real treat to see something authentic and old school here (I feared it would just be nursery rhyme tunes with replaced Pagan lyrics about the Goddess or something), but it's firmly rooted in folklore and a lot of fun. Plus it comes with a CD for those who can't read music or just want to hear it!
