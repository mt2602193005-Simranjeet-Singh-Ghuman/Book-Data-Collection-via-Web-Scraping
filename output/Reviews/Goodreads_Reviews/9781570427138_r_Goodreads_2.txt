Well, that was a weird ending. Another excellent Alex Cross addition to the series with a brilliant and sick psycho killer and Alex distraught most of the book. It was great to see a support system come through to assist him on this one. I hope we get to see more of a resolution to events in this book. Overall this was another wonderful adventure, but it felt less "wrapped up" in MANY ways with many unanswered questions and a weird ending that was somewhat strange for sure. Looking forward to continuing the journey of Cross. 4 Stars
