This was going along fine and I was enjoying it, but I did not like the ending at all. Sarah Ida's transformation felt rushed and large chunks of it seemed to take place offstage. I was expecting her experience at the shoeshine stand to change her, but her friendship with Rossi was renewed in a way that did not feel realistic to me, and suddenly offering to do chores for Aunt Claudia felt off to me too. And after maturing and changing, why did the story have to punish Sarah Ida with the bad news about her mother? Why couldn't she go home because her mother is much better and eager to have her back? I feel like now she is supposed to go be a stand-in housekeeper for her dad. :(
