"To Bring You Joy" is the story of Monique and Edourad/ Edward. Hero and heroine have a meet cute- where he saves her from a disastrous engagement. She then inherits a wonderful home from an aunt, and meets the hero again.. who is unpleasant! The book was wonderfully written with a strong heroine and an eventual smitten hero, but there were too many distractions and OW drama came out of nowhere. I wish the romance was more interesting but the writing is superb. Safe 2/5
