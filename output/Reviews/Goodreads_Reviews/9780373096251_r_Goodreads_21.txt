“I love you, Trace, with all my heart, and I want nothing more than to spend my life with you. It doesn't matter where.” - Gillian Fitzpatrick What happens when a seasoned yet cynical intelligence agent is forced to work with an idealistic scientist? Dr. Gillian Fitzpatrick is in a pickle. Her brother and niece have been abducted by a terrorist group that is after a weapon she and her brother designed. She can't free them herself - she's gonna need the help of Trace O'Hurley, a man whose morals are ambiguous at best, and whose interest is not entirely on this mission. Can these two completely different people work together to stop the terrorists and free the hostages in time? Honestly, I don't remember who joked about Trace being a spy in the previous books, but damn! Right on the money, huh? Though, this is obviously not a spy story - it's not even a love story, to be honest. This book was a chemical reaction! Slow-building and volatile! The action and suspense? Top of the top. The tension between Gillian and Trace? Over the roof. The dialogues and the love scenes? Every bit the masterpieces Nora Roberts is familiar with writing. Not gonna lie, I had high expectations for Trace - and he didn't disappoint. He was gruff but in that sexy way, and there was still enough softness inside him to keep him humane in the grumpiest way - I was all for it. Gillian was also a delight. Smart and spunky, a woman who would sacrifice herself for her loved ones and what's right. She was way too pure for some of the stuff the world might throw at someone in her shoes, but Trace's cynicism was there to balance things nicely, in my opinion. And with this, the O'Hurley series is over for me. I loved it from start to finish, and it was something I needed to get back on my "NR feel good" mood.
