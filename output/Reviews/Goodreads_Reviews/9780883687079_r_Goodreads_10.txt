This book is meant for people who struggles with tithings, or people who would like to have a biblical overview on the subject matter. The author laid out all the relevant bible verses regarding tithing, addressed the biblical concept of tithings and offerings, and again and again assured his readers of the necessity of tithings and the blessings that come from it. The author had made straightforward and compelling reasons for tithings, and I believe his readers would be challenged to tithe willingly and expectantly, though I fear uncareful Christians would be motivated to do so with a wrong motive picked up from the arguments.
