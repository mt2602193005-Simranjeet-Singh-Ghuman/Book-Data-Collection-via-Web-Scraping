Awards: n/a Grade: K-1 Summary: David Kirk does it again with another Miss. Spider adventure. This story is about her friend's preparing for a birthday party for Miss. Spider. The author goes reveals each of Miss. Spider's friends through all twenty-six letters of the alphabet. Everyone is excited and waiting for the arrival of Miss. Spider these beautiful illustrations that children would sure love. Review: I am all about visual aides to help reinforce what you are trying to say and these illustrations are bright and fun that any student would be able to remember. In Class Use: (1) Reinforcing Alphabet Recognition- I would use this a way to show the alphabet in a different format. To help students recognize a letter I would say "Remember jumping june bugs from Miss. Spider birthday party?, What letter does june bug start with?".
