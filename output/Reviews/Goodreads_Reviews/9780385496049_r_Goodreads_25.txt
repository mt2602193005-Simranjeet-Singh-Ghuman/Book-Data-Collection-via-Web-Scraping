I love anything related to India, historical fiction, travel books, mythology, you name it. But this book was pretty hard to digest. I loved the storyline and even the writing style but somehow I couldn’t like it the way I wanted to. It revolves around Roop and Satya, wives of a rich Sardarji. Roop is taken as his second wife because Satya failed to give him kids, so throughout the book we see Roop as just being taken as a baby-producing vessel. I couldn’t help but empathize with the two women, who were fighting their own inner battles while also trying to “please” their man. The role of women during those times is well presented in this book; which mostly constituted of obeying their husband, never be angry, endure, etc etc. it was frustrating to read about the patriarchal society, which is still persistent in india. Then the partition happens, and we read the gore details of the atrocities that took place. I was particularly sad reading about Kusum(Roop’s sis-in-law). The ending was satisfying.
