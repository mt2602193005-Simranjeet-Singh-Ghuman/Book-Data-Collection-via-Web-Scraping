This is one of my favorite collections of nursery rhymes, second perhaps only to one - The Read-Aloud Treasury by Joanna Cole. Beautifully illustrated, with only a few of the rhymes containing cruelty (like throwing the old man down the stairs), The Orchard Book includes fun ditties I've not seen (so far) in other anthologies, such as "Moses Supposes." It also features some tongue-twisters, songs, and all the beloved classic nursery rhymes and poems you'd expect to see, as well as a few Christmas songs at the end. My little kiddo enjoyed this with me over several sittings. She was very interested in the colorful, lovingly detailed illustrations.
