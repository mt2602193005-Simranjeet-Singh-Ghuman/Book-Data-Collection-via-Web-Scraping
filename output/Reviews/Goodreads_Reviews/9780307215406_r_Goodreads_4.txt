5+ stars (6/10 hearts). Um... wow. I really liked this book!! For once Trixie didn't get herself into any dumb messes (okay, so she kinda did, as usual, butttt… It’s Trixie. And I still love her). Mart, Honey, Brian, and Jim were all as awesome as usual; and I ended up liking Tad a lot, along with Spider. Also, I really liked how kind Mrs. Wheeler was (we already know how awesome the Beldens & Mr. Wheeler are, so it’s nice to see Mrs. Wheeler at last). Oh, and I loved the Japanese brothers!! So sweet. As for the mystery, it was pretty cool, though not what I expected. And was the climax ever tense?! Um, YES! I loved that the kids were doing so much to help with the UNICEF and really meant it, not just doing it for show. And did I mention the humour?? Because it’s really good. Per the usual. Overall, one of my favourite Trixie books! Content: euphemisms A Favourite Humorous Quote: "'Moms will be even more worried[.] Why couldn't Mr. Maypenny have a telephone in this [old shack]?' "'So he could talk to the animals?' Jim inquired. "Be yourself, Trixie. Let's play Twenty Questions.'"
