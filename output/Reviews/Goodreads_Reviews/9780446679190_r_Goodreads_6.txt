This was another mind blower by P.D. James. I saw the film years ago, but forgot the end. So the end was a surprise for me. The only reason why I gave this one four instead of five stars, is because this was supposed to be a Dalgliesh book, but it seemed more like a cameo role instead of a full on Dalgliesh story. Maybe James was trying something new with this one. I am looking forward to reading Number 9 in this series.
