A perfect read to get my mind off of the cold! This is probably one of the liveliest books I've ever read, bar none. I could envision the characters, interactions, and settings so perfectly! It had a clever and determined heroine, a hero you grew to like, and a great supporting cast of characters, as well! I just had a lot of fun reading this book; finished it in just a few days, I was so hooked. I will very likely reread this one again sometime in the future! 5/5 stars
