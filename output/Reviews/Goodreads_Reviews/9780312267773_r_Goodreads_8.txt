Definitely not a book for everyone, and probably past its sell-by date as far as cultural relevance goes, but for the right reader, this is an essential book. If you are around 20, disaffected, bourgie but open-minded, and idle enough to have time to contemplate the "point of it all," this is your manifesto. I am none of those things--well, idle--but I loved it anyway. I like the way Shiner puts sentences together.
