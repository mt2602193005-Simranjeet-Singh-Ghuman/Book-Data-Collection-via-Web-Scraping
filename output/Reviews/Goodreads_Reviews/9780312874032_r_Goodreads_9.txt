This book was so weird. In the distant future, artists are deleted, and all their works must be destroyed to make room for new art. Pick up artists go out to gather the books, music, and art to turn in for deletion. There are lots of designer drugs that allow you to do all sorts of things. Time has no meaning? Maybe? With every turn of the path, it gets weirder and harder to describe. It's like a mash up of Pushing Daisies (the TV show), Fahrenheit 451, and Arthur Dent (Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy) plus it's totally own unique thing. It was fascinating for sure. If you're interested in a mind bender, give it a whirl.
