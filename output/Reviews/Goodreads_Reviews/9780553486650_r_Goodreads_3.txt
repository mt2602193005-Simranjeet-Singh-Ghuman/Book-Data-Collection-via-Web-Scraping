After Lacey's boyfriend dumps her she puts her sights on someone else - someone who likes Jessica Wakefield. Trouble abounds. Does Lacey actually like this boy or is she just trying to get her old boyfriend jealous??? Drama abounds. These books are silly but I do like that they have a little bit of depth to them. This version of Sweet Valley is fast becoming my favorite. It's too bad I can't find many of these to read.
