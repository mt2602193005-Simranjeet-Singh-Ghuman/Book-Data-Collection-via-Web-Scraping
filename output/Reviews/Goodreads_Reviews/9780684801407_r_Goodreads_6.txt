This is a very detailed family genealogy told chronologically. A family tree early in the book helps a reader to identify family members. More attention is given to FDR and Teddy Roosevelt and their family members. This is a thoroughly researched and carefully written presentation. Keep a dictionary handy; there are several vocabulary words in the book. There are exhaustive discussions about politics and upward employment changes and disputes among the Oyster Bay and Hyde Park Roosevelts. The family details at times are overwhelming and generally not that critical to an undrstanding of the Roosevelts' story.
