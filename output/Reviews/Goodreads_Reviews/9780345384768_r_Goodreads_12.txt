Hard times have come upon the land, and the local lord sends his Wizard and his two elder sons to find out what’s going on; back over the mountains to where the boys’ grandmother hailed from. The younger son trails along, but in the rear. They encounter witches and goblins and trolls and a fragment of an evil mirror. And a cute friendly witch, who is in over her head (as are they). Cherryh always provides imagination, a fantastical plot, and intriguing characters. But she has a tendency to spend too long insider her people’s heads, and here it slows down the action, sometimes to a crawl. Overall entertaining, tho.
