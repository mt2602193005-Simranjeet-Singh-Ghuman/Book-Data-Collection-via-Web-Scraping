Washington D.C. stories like mini novels (most of them). Sometimes there seemed a little too much going on, but all are engaging, complicated tales set amongst the black community in the USA capital - the part tourists don't see - in the 60s/70s/80s. A very sympathetic, well tuned ear. Single mothers, drugs, music, church, family life, how to run a store for years. The best one though was a story from his next collection (All Aunt Hagar's Children) - this is a quite recent edition - must get that.
