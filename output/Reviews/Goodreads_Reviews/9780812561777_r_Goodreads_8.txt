3/08. Quite good fantasy about one of the few "witches" who've survived extermination over the centuries in this created world. Conflict comes as she tries to keep to her calling to heal while remaining anonymous and discovering the history of her people. Re-read, 2/24. Dropping a star on re-read. Most of this was good, although not 4 stars-worth, but the romance that dominated the last 1/4 or so was pretty badly done and I did a lot of skimming. Not really that interested in re-reading the rest of the series, as I assume there's a good deal more of that there. Eh.
