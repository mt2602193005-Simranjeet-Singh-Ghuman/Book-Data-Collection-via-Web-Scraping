Reading a “Jedi Apprentice” book shortly after “Kenobi” provides some… interesting whiplash, I think. This story is a direct sequel to the previous one, in which our two Jedi heroes appear on Gala right before they’re about to transition from a hereditary monarchy to a representative government. The prince of this planet is, understandably, quite upset about the switch and is trying to get himself elected so he can restore the monarchy. And while the other candidates sound good on paper, they might not be as squeaky clean as they present themselves. Also, the prince isn’t even actually the true heir (dun dun DUN!!!!!). The titular ‘Mark of the Crown’ feels unnecessary to the Plot. There’s enough going on in this short book that you don’t really need to have the whole, “There’s another heir!!” subplot going on. I’m unclear how it even *works*, too–the true heir will have some kind of distinguishing mark, but it can be read with a machine doohickey in the palace? Also, I remember the description Obi-Wan has of a sword from the first time I read this: “it was like a lightsaber, but made of metal”. Just… oi. There’s some excitement, it’s not bad, but it feels a little overstuffed, and there’s a minor tie-in to the overall Plot of the series which seems a little tacked-on. Could be better, I think.
