After all these years, and never having known her personally, I still miss Princess Diana terribly. She was a bright light in the world, yet all too human, so it was easy to love her. I wish that Charles could have loved her and given her a happy home life. And I wish the Monarchy could have realized what a treasure she was. It breaks my heart that such a beautiful women led such a tragic life. As for this book, there was a glaring omission regarding the public’s outcry toward the Queen when Diana died. I wish there had been more like the chapter titled “Passport to Parachinar” that recounted the good Diana’s memory and her memorial fund is doing. It was so heartwarming, and just the thing that would have made her heart glad. Of course, Diana’s biggest legacy is her boys. And they are doing her proud in carrying on her charity work, and spreading kindness & goodwill throughout the world. Well done, Diana.
