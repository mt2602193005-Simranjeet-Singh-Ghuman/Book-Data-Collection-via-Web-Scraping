Meh. I love Briggs' Mercy Thompson series and this one pales in comparison. Know it's from earlier on in Briggs' writing career, and it shows. The characters have less depth (the villain practically mwa-ha-has from the corner), you could see the twist way early on, the romance doesn't feel like it comes from a place of maturity and growth, and the main character is kinda...well...dull. I didn't dislike it. The world is interesting, the magic is kinda neat, and the story of the prophet is thought provoking. The main character gets help but also helps herself, and she's not swooning over some dude for 200 out of the 300 pages. But I really didn't need another beautiful heroine who is scarred but doesn't realize she's beautiful, and a quiet healer that's really a dreamboat. Briggs also makes the mistake of getting rid of the main character's interesting friends way too soon. Rialla's friendship with Laeth starts so great, and then he inexplicably vanishes 100 pages in. Good start, but definitely not her best.
