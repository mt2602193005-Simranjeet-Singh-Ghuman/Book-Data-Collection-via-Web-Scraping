This was a book I chose for my book club to read. I chose it because earlier in the year I met and had dinner with the author, Jay Nussbaum. The category for the months book was humor. I thought the book was funny and very entertaining but for some reason about 20% of the club did not share my enthusiasm. The book is about a big fish and life under the sea and fishing from a fishes perspective. In the first chapter the main character introduced himself by saying "Call me Fishmael". That is funny. It is like a cross between Moby Dick, The Old Man and the Sea and Finding Nemo. I still think it is a very good read despite what the minority of my book club members think. It is a quick read so enjoy it.
