Bonnie Bumble eats FIVE of the SIX turn-overs she made and finds when she tries to stand up that she is now upside down and can't get right-side-up again. Children get a kick out of her wearing her hat on her feet and her shoes on her hands as she goes out to do her farm chores. Milk spills, eggs break, her hair gets nibbled and her ears get tickled. Whatever will she do? A simple solution presents itself, with a slight twist. Have fun with this story!
