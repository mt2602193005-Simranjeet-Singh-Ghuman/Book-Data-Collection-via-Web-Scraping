What a lovely story. On the face of it it’s a standard quest. Mark Cornwall discovers an old reference in a library to something hidden in the wasteland, and decides to follow it. A rafter goblin sees him and, saving him from a malicious school official, joins his quest. There is a lost girl, a woodsman, a talking animal, a shifty old witch, and a tin man. A magic sword and a shining steed. But they add up to something very different.
