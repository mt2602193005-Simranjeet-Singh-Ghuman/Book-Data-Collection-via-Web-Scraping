3.5* This is my third Essie Summers book and I think her heroines tend to have interesting jobs, are independent and with pleasant characters. The heroes are nice, but in no way pushovers; they get their way but it's all done so charmingly most give in without realising it! This book is a pleasant read. It's all about our couple and their fascination and deep knowledge of their French roots and culture, brought over from France generations ago. I was glad at the end when Monique's heritage and parentage was revealed, Edouard did not get his knickers in a twist and thereby dragging the story!
