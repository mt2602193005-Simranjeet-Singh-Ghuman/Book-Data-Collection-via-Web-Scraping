Welcome to a collection where plot doesn't matter, but words flicker like stars. Cartesian Sonata, a collection of 4 novelas, is more a trip though the beauty and power of the word, than any set a/b/c plot. Infact, if clear cut formulaic books are your thing, then I'd suggest you stear clear of Cartesian Sonata. However, if floating in a sea of images, humorous proes, and well-crafted turns of phraze interest you, then I highly recommend this. Even if you don't think it's your cup of tea, it's not overly long, and new experiences are the spice of life. I liked my first review I wrote, but FF crashed.
