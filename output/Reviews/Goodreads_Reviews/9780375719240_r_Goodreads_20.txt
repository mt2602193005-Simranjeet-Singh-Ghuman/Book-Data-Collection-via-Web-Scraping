I found this a well-crafted period mystery that made effective use of its late 1960s setting in rural Australia. Its theme of the two boys going missing and one returning with no memory did have echoes for me of Joan Lindsay's 'Picnic at Hanging Rock' and this also heightened by the presence of dreams and spiritual elements, which were not overplayed but felt very much a part of the essence of the landscape. It was well received by our reading group generating discussion about various themes such as the role in such tales of a catalyst, in this case the presence of Sydney police detective Gibson.
