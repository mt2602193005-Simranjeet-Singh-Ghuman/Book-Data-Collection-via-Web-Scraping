I found out about Shadowrun through the (really good, in my opinion) Sega game. I was a huge fan of that, so when I found out that it was a tabletop RPG I had to grab it. The setting is a mashup of cyberpunk and fantasy, and it did it pretty well. In fact, Shadowrun has survived so long because it has so much style. The rules (for all editions) are bloated and clunky at best and contradictory nonsense at worst.
