Ultimately, it doesn't matter what scripting language you use. Perl, Python, R, Javascript, Ruby (ok, maybe not Ruby), whatever. Having your code be lucid, literate and readable is important no matter what language. If you work in Perl, this is a great book to help make your code more idiomatic (or, as Perl coders say, "Perlish"). This is one of the two books (along with The Camel) I have at my elbow every time I code in Perl.
