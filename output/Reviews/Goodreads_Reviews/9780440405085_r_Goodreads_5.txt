Grade: 5th Grade Lexile Measure: 800L Genre: Biography Characters: Sitting Bull Setting: Varies throughout the biography POV: The story’s point of view is told from the author. This book is a biography about Sitting Bull. He was born in the United States in South Dakota. At the age of 10 he killed his first buffalo. At the age of 14 he proved himself as a brave warrior. He was a famous medicine person who surrendered in the US Government on July 20, 1881 after being chosen to lead his people against the U.S government troops. He wanted to keep his people off the reservations. He fought and protected the Indians. It is also stated in this book that he lost a battle when he fought for Lakota Sioux to continue hunting buffalo. It tells his life from I would use this book in 4th grade or 5th. I would use this book for a social studies lesson on hunters and famous leader war chiefs. I would also use this book to introduce other Dakota Indians, Kings and rulers, wars, and other biographies. I would do a compare and contrast activity with my students or have them write about this certain individual and how me made history in the United States.
