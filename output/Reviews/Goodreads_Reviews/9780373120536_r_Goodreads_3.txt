An entertaining book with lots of misunderstandings and lots of angst. The h hired a man escort to accompany her to her ex-boyfriend's wedding. He is marrying her cousin. The one he cheated on her with. The man she assumes is the escort is actually the nephew of her recently deceased boss. The H thinks the h screwed her way into the position she had with his uncle. He decided to accompany her to the wedding so he can find out more about the real her. A decent read. A one night stand turned into a pregnancy and into ... HEA. The constant bickering between the main characters gets tired fast. And so does their denial of their attraction for each other. A bit long winded at times but still worth it.
