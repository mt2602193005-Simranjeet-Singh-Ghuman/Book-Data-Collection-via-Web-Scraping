I can relate to Lachlan in a few ways as, while I don’t have Cerebral Palsy, I am legally blind, hearing impaired, and living with a life-threatening medical condition. Like him, I’ve had my fair share of being bullied at school by other students and, at times, even teachers. That’s just one of the many ways I can relate to him. I enjoyed this book a great deal and found it so inspiring. This true story is written by Greg Jones, and it is told through his eyes as the father of Lachlan Jones, OAM, who is a world champion, world record holder and gold medalist. Lachlan was born with Cerebral Palsy and is legally blind. The book covers a time span from the time Lachlan was born, up to and including his time at the 1996 Atlanta Paralympics. Written in a down-to-earth manner, it covers many ups and downs, not just for Lachlan, but his father and family too. You feel like you are along for the ride and there will be moments where you’ll laugh, worry, cry and cheer. It’s a book I would definitely recommend.
