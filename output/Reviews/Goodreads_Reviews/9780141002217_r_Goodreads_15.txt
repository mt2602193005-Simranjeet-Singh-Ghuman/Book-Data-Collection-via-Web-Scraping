I love this book! Full disclosure: the author, Dennis Overbye, is a friend of mine from a long time ago. This book explores Einsteins's life, but what I find fascinating is the exploration of the science along with the life, and the personal interaction between the two. I got a lot out of reading about the intellectual context: his peers, his rivals, and their personal interaction. Einstein is revealed as an alive, and flawed, person. His treatment of the various women in his life is not what one would call exemplary. Still, anyone who is interested in Einstein in particular, or interested in general in the world of scientists as human beings, should enjoy this book. I also recommend Overbye's other book, "Lonely Hearts of the Cosmos."
