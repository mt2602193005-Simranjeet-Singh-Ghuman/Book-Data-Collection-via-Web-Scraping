An interesting take on the first contact idea, except it's an alien business takeover of Earth rather than a military invasion. The aliens have technology so far advanced that humanity is reduced to making mud pies and yet somehow have translators that don't quite have mastery of English, although the translation of 'trade show' as 'industrial carnival' will seem accurate to anyone who has ever been to one. A little bit slow in the protagonist's down-and-out period, but otherwise, a highly entertaining and easy read written stylistically along the lines of Douglas Adams. Highly recommended.
