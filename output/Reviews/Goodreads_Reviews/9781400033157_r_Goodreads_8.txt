This is an essential volume in the story of women soldiers in the Civil War. The authors take a great first step in uncovering the story in a well researched way giving the soldiers their due. 20 years later more work could stand to be done through the lens of sexual identity and gender studies which the authors almost go out of their way to avoid or minimize. Lastly, the writing was at times a bit dreary and the organization was sometimes confusing (I would have put the second to last chapter first).
