One of the Intro books I'd recommend if anyone was to ask. Curott encourages you on nearly every page to *do*, rather than read, which I love and completely agree with. Go witch, witches! We also have a very similar idea about what the spiritual path is for: to connect with the Divine. Curott's definition of a witch is one I gel with. A bit gender essentialist to my eyes now, I'd love to see a rewritten version of this slightly updated. Overall still a book I refer to and enjoy.
