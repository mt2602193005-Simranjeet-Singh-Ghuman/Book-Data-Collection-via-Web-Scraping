Frank Peretti is a very good writer, and this book is no exception. I was a bit more eager to get to the problem-solving part later on, but the intro stories were dramatically well done, and drew us into the topic. He has some key pieces of wisdom for the wounded, such as "If you are wounded, you are not alone," "It was not your fault," "You don't have to put up with it," and "Speak up. You really don't have to tolerate the abuse any longer." Peretti discusses the positive things that happen when you speak up, versus the negative things that people think will happen when they speak up. I think that is a key point - getting the courage to speak up, and ditching the idea that speaking up is somehow tattling.
