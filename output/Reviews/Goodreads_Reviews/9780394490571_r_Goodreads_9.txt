This was an interesting take on the criminal underworld, especially for the time period. I had never heard of it, but came across it on the bookshelf and just decided it would be entertaining. And it was. Nothing terribly thought provoking, but I really liked Higgins's style. He assumes the reader will keep up and does not overly explain ANYTHING. He is also very sexist and misogynistic; hard to know if that is the time period or the writer's personal thoughts or some extra layer to "bad guy", but it was slightly offensive at times. Overall nothing special, but a good ambiance piece.
