A charming book--it is the story of Hemingway's Old Man and the Sea told from the point of view of the marlin and the remora (the fish who lives on the Marlin). It is filled with allegory (as these books are), but very well done. It follows the Hemingway book carefully as the Marlin is snagged, dragged, and finally captured. A fun thoughtful book--good to read with the Hemingway.
