A friend's cat ran away. It was quite a big deal, his mother was a mess. They were calling me every day for days because I had been at their house when the cat went missing. I didn't know what to do! They had me all stressed out. I think they thought I was somehow responsible. I remembered this book had a spell to return a missing pet. I followed the spell, lit the candle and asked my roommate not to blow the candle out when I left for work. I came home and my roommate told me she happened to walk past my room right as the candle burned out. I didn't think anything of it. The next day my friend called me and told me the cat came home the night before and looked a fright! She had clearly been though an ordeal. They were so glad to have her back safe and sound. I asked what time the cat returned and I called my roommate to ask what time the candle had burned out. It was the same time!
