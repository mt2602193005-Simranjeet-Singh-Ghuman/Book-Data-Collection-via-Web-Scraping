Some poems are hilarious, some are wistful, a few are sad, but all are engaging, and the illustrations for well. My nephew especially liked "Bananas and Cream" by David McCord, while I enjoyed the Robert Louis Stevenson poems, especially "Bed in Summer," which my nephews can identify with, in particular the lines "And does it not seem hard to you, / When all the sky is clear and blue, / And I should like so much to play, / To have to go to bed by day? "
