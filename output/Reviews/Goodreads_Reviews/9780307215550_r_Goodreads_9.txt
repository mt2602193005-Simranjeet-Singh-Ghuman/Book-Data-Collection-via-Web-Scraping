I didn’t read this one as a kid but it might be my favorite in the series. I’ve recently started thrifting Trixie books and the 1980s ones are my favorite. It’s interesting how they try to preserve the innocence of the 1960s but at the same time aren’t quite able to capture it so they read more nostalgic. I don’t mind that tho. The characters seem more realistic in the more recent books, and the mysteries are more advanced with less emphasis on gender roles for Honey and Trixie.
