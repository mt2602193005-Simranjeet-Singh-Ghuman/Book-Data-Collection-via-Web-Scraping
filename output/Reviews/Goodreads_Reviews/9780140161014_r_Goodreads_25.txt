Nasty, shallow little book this. Only picked it up from the covid-lockdown re-read shelf as I remember from other works Molly Keane can be a laugh-out-loud funny writer. Not here. While there are some moments of Irish lyricism in the descriptive writing, some sentences are so wordy they need reading three or four times to get the drift. The characters are almost without exception detestable, waspish, green with sickening jealousy and evil intentions, all proved as this tedious novel gallops towards one of its two denouements: the near-entire obnoxious cast getting off on the pursuit and slaughter of a tired fox cub. Devoted Ladies? Demented more like.
