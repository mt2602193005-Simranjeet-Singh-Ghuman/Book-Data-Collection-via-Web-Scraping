The final and grimmest book in the series. At last, Kel is finally a knight. And Tortall is at war. At the end of Squire , The Chamber of The Ordeal gave Kelly a secret task--to find and stop the "Nothing Man" who has been sacrificing children to create magical killing machines. Kelly has no idea where this man is or how to find him, just that their paths will cross and, when they do, she must end his evil. In the meantime, Kel has to follow orders. In an unprecedented move, her superiors have designated 18-year-old Kelly as a commander in the war against the Scanrans. And Kel is an excellent commander, though still faces an uphill battle to prove herself to those at her command. Lady Knight is a somber book. Kel witnesses many people she cares about die horrific deaths. She discovers that sometimes it is impossible to prevent all suffering, to protect all those for whom she is responsible--it's the bleak nature of war. Though Kel is excellent at commanding those assigned roles beneath hers, what is truly endearing is seeing her friends and supporters rally around her in her time of need even when not commanded. Appropriate for a middle grade/young teen audience. CW: death, violence, war
