Simply put, this book does an amazing job of personalizing the people/characters in the book. When you live in a town of 30-40 people, you can't help but get to know everyone on a very intimate level. I often thought of my father's mother and father, how much they meant to me, and how, as a family, we always worked towards a collective goal. I found the book to be slow at first, but as I got to know he cast, I was often smiling and reminiscing on my own life. Quick easy read, especially if you read multiple books at a time like myself
