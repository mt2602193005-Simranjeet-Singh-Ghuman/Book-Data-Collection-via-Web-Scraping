You can stream this album on Spotify which we do a lot of mornings while cooking breakfast, or while the kids do some artwork or play with shapes etc. I love this album because it strings together nursery rhymes into a larger narrative of a group of friends going to London for King Cole's birthday party. The voices and sound effects are entertaining and on top of that I think it's important for kids to get exposed to classic nursery rhymes and lullabies.
