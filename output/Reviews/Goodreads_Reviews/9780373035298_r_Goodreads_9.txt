One of my favorite light-hearted reads from Lucy Gordon Harlequin Romance. The guy is so oblivious but the girl sure knows how to push all his buttons. An up-and-coming banker, Nick was asked by his sister-in-law (a nice girl for whom he always carried the torch but she chose to love his ordinary brother instead) to take care of her younger sis, Katie. All Nick could remember of her is she was an annoying teenager who had managed to ruin his courtship of the big sister. So, when he sees her again five years later he was totally unprepared to deal with the new and much-improved Katie. There's neither big misunderstanding here nor big dilemma. But the sparring scenes were delightful. The hero considers her the "poison pixie" and tries to avoid her but she never fails to nettle him with her quick repartees and silly antics. She even manages to make his heart twinge with jealousy with her budding relationship with his good friend. Of course, all the characters are in on her secret anyway: that she’s in love with him. But he is so impervious until he almost successfully drives her away.
