While out for a walk, Henry and Mudge see a sign for a snowman building contest. When they go home to tell Dad, they see him covered in green paint from trying to paint a chair. When they arrive at the contest, there are all sorts of different snowmen like dogs, Abraham Lincoln, aliens, but only one snowman of Henry's dad covered in the green paint! This book is a quick and easy read for readers in the 1st-3rd grade level. It shows the bonds between Henry, Mudge, and Dad in a cute and relatable way. This book would be a good read aloud book to do during the winter and discuss different activities you could do in the snow. It would also be a good book for readers to read individually to transition into early chapter books because the vocabulary is simple and easy to follow.
