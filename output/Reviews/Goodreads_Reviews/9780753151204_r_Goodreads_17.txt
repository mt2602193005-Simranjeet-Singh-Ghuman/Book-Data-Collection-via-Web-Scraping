You can tell the author has spent time working in theatre, and I really enjoyed the wry wit of this novella and how it navigates all the usual backstage shenanigans, frustrations, obsessions, and heartbreaks. I found Stella navigating class in post-war Liverpool, and particularly class dynamics within the arts, really compelling, but felt so genuinely sorry for her in her naiveté and the repeated abuses she suffers. This novella really takes a dark turn towards the end, juxtaposed with a fantastical production of Peter Pan, and overall, it kind of just left me sad. The McNally edition has a great foreword by YiYun Li that really added a lot of heart and context, and I definitely look forward to reading more Baibridge.
