I learnt a great deal from this book, and the history is all very intriguing, but for a newcomer to most of the names and chronology, the book did get a little bogged down with details. I'm super glad Caroline Alexander knows what the weather was like in Portsmouth for every single day of the court-martial, but, y'know, I maybe didn't need to know. So this is somehow more than you will ever need to know about the mutiny on the Bounty, and at the same time, I'm still not sure why there was a mutiny on the Bounty. Incredible. Guess I'll blame it on the coconuts.
