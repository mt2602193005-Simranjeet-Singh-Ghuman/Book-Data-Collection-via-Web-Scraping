Jim DeRogatis is a true rock critic in the classic sense. There's no point in being mediocre; he either loves it or its terrible. (And "the terrible" is often a lot more fun to read.) Anyways, this "best-of" collection highlights the best of the 90s alternative explosion. The first chapter is probably the best chapter in the book, where he goes into detail about a 10-hour interview with Kurt Cobain at his Seattle home. Follow that up with excellent chapters on Courtney Love, Smashing Pumpkins, rock dinosaurs U2 and REM, a whole slew of lesser known hidden gems, and a collection of wannabes; you've got an entertaining book.
