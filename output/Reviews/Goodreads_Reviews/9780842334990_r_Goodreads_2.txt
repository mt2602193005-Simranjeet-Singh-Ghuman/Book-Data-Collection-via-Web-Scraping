You can not read this book and remain the same for it’s the only book that’s alive and active (Heb 4:12). I read this book with a group of ladies and watched us all experience greater depths of Christ’s love, peace, and joy in our lives. We will all face Jesus one day and I pray if you don’t believe that or think it matters, just read the New Testament with an open heart and mind and then decide. I pray that from his glorious, unlimited resources he will empower you with inner strength through his Spirit. And may you have the power to understand, as all God’s people should, how wide, how long, how high, and how deep his love is. Then Christ will make his home in your hearts as you trust in him. Your roots will grow down into God’s love and keep you strong. May you experience the love of Christ, though it is too great to understand fully. Then you will be made complete with all the fullness of life and power that comes from God. Now all glory to God, who is able, through his mighty power at work within us, to accomplish infinitely more than we might ask or think. Ephesians 3 This is the first time I read the NLT translation of the New Testament. I have always used NASB and I find for an easy to read version, this one holds quite true to the thoughts and themes of more complicated translations.
