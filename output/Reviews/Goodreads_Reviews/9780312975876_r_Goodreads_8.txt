This book is so well written it was hard to read at times, as the emotions it evoked in me were painful. There is no love that compares to that between a mother and her child and just the thought of someone maliciously trying to sever that bond was difficult to read about. I could feel Sarah's desperation, terror and love for sweet little Danny. Zach was certainly a tough nut to crack in the beginning, but when he finally let love into his heart it was a beautiful thing to behold. I loved these characters and although the big climax was a tad anti-climactic and the bad guys caved a bit easily, I was just glad and relieved that Sarah got her baby boy back safely and that she got the HEA she so deserved. Zach ended up being a better man than I could have imagined in the beginning and he too deserved to finally have a family and home of his own. I would have loved an extended epilogue that followed them later down the road to see how their family expanded and how their love grew.
