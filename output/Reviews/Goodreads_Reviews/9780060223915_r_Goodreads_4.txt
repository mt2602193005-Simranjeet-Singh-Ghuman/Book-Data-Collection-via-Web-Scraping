Arthur the monkey books have always been a favorite of mine from when I was a little girl, and am now sharing them with my granddaughter. In this one the kids are getting ready for school and dressing up for Halloween. Arthur wants to be scarey but his sister and her friends keep telling him he's not. And so he uses his imagination and comes up with a costume and in the end wins the school's prize at being the most unique. Teaching readers it's okay to be creative.
