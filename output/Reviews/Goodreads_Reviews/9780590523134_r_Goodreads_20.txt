I liked that this book didn't bore me with long descriptions of all the characters and their respective roles in the BSC (although these descriptions are helpful to readers who are not reading the books in order). Instead, this series follows the interpersonal relationships and the experiences that the four main characters (Kristy, Claudia, Stacey and Mary Anne) have as they grow up. This book centers around family relationships- namely Kristy and her brothers relationship with their estranged father. I really liked the fact that the author showcases the diverse emotions that a person can feel and the different ways in which they can come to terms with their situation(s). This story made me wish that there was a book (told through the POV of Kristy's mother Elizabeth) which would give us Kristy's backstory (her early childhood and life immediately after Patrick left). I liked this book better than some of the books in the original series.
