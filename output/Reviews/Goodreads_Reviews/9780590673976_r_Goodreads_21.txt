I really enjoy when I read the BSC books. It reminds me of when I first started reading plus some of these books I don’t even think I have read so it’s nice to read them for the first time. Mallory is helping Stacy and Claudia move boxes up to Stacy’s attic when they come across and old trunk. Stacy’s mum gives Mallory permission to take it home however it’s locked and no one can seem to break the locks. Mallory enlists her brothers to break them and when they do she finds an old diary from the 1800s. Then the mystery unravels of whom this person is. She also teaches one of the kids to babysit how to fall in love with reading. Definitely a fun story.
