A fascinating look at a time in history that a lot of us were here for but most of us did not think to document. This is a fascinating little slice of time that goes far beyond eBay. The world was changing, and as the book goes on it becomes sad in a way, because the frontier that the internet was at the time did morph and shift into the internet as we know it today. The colorful characters that emerged from the early days of both the internet and eBay did eventually phase themselves out into "a business major" and "someone from the legal team" over time. Gradually things went from an exciting, community driven, organic thing of something special, into business as usual. Anyway, the problem is capitalism.
