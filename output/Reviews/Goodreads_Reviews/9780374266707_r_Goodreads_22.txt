Everything can change at the mercy of a girl who had pearl in her ear an ice in hair I wish I’d read this book when it came out! This was a spectacular piece of Scottish fiction, I felt the characters were so realistic and the author really captured the dialogue perfectly. The nostalgia was real I found this to be a heartwarming and emotional read. The book also included characters facing real issues such as terminal illness, poverty, fear of sharing their sexuality as well as fears of what happens after high school. This book just felt so real and at times I was reading it and remembering conversations with friends. This book is a new favourite There is swearing, excessive alcohol consumption, wearing clothes that would make a nun faint, and a lot of talking about sex. This felt authentic to being a Scottish teenager in the 90s (and 00s!). This may not be to everyone’s taste, so please take that into account There is also a hospital scene that is definitely assault, that should be acknowledged
