Capturing her heart I remember reading this book years ago, quickly followed by the rest in this series. Cait London brought a story filled with a strong history, legends and family. The bonds I found within the pages still to this day capture me. This was one of the first series I’d ever read. The premise of the story of the orphaned Tallchief children had me invested. This story was well paced and engaging. The character development was superb and the chemistry between our leads Duncan and Sybil sizzled. It’s well worth the reread. It’s a big yes from me
