I got really lazy with my readings during the holidays so it took me much longer than I planned to read this book. Solid book. Takes more of a straight forward and linear approach to the development of Jacksonian democracy. The book starts with a brief recap of Jackson's presidency then focuses on the ideas of Jacksonian democracy. I learned the Jacksonian democracy can be traced back to Jeffersonian democracy. Schlesigner painted Jackson as a Jeffersonian democrat and not the first "Jacksonian." Jacksonians were suspicious of big business and corporations, banking, and overlarge (or tyrannical as some would put it) government. They supported expanded suffrage, for white men still, and shifted the course of the country to be more democratic than it was originally founded. I'm too lazy to write more.
