A basic standard Berenstain Bears book. Not something you'd want to read over and over. But a way to have a conversation. In this book it's really on having too much stuff. And maybe figuring out what to do with some of it. Papa Bear isn't all that foolish in this one, which is a plus. And they do a whole bunch of different things with the stuff, but in the end they are still themselves. 3.5 of 5.
