I don’t know if I can give this a proper rating. As a primary document of slums of the Lower East Side, I found it to be very fascinating. Many of the streets Riis mentions, I walk down almost every week so it gives me a greater sense of how much things have changed in the 135 years since this book’s publication. Riis’ writing is quite offensive and relies on many offensive stereotypical notions of the groups he writes about so that is something to also be aware of. However, I found his visceral descriptions of people living in complete squalor to be quite disturbing and make this well worth a read. If you are interested in urban planning at all I would say this is an essential text.
