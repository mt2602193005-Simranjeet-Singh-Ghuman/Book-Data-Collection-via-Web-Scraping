This was really cute. Heroine is beautiful, bold, and kind to young children and animals. A dancer by profession, she is the polar opposite in temperament to the hero. She has loved the hero since she was sixteen and the hero was courting her sister. Hero is a high-powered London banker, who has worked his whole life to overcome his jealousy of his older, more charming brother who married the love of his life (heroine's sister). Told almost entirely from the POV of the hero, we have a tale of unrequited love from the heroine finally coming to a satisfying conclusion as the hero eventually clues in that he can't live without her. (That's really not a spoiler - any romance reader worth her salt could see where this animosity was going from page one - page three at that most if you weren't really paying attention.) The spoiler is in *how* the author got them together: 1. By having them live together (temporarily - until the OW has the hero kick her out) 2. By having every secondary character (including the older sister) in on the heroine's campaign to win the hero over. 3. By having two OMs making the hero jealous. 4. By having the heroine charm the hero's boss. 5. By showing the heroine's kindness to children. 6. By giving them an idyllic weekend in the country, culminating with a trip to bed. 7. By the traditional morning-after misunderstanding. 8. The wrong letter in the wrong envelope. This is a lovely souffle of a story. Lot's of banter between the H/h.
