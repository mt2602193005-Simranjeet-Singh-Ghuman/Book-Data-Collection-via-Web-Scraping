Rubicon is an excellent history book, I enjoyed the way it was written and I really enjoyed the subject matter. The later days of the Roman Republic is a time period that is among my favorites in history, such interesting stories, so many powers are at work, so many captivating characters. Sulla, Marius, Crassus, Cicero, Cato, Pompey, and of course Julius Caesar! Tom Holland is a very good storyteller. He cautiously maps out the route from the Roman Republic to that famous moment when Julius Caesar crosses the Rubicon and what happens after that, the end of the Republic and the start of the Roman Empire. Very captivating and entertaining at the same time.
