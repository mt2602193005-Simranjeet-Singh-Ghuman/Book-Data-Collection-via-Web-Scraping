Not a bad book if you are trying to find out basic facts about TMJ and what to do about them. I was hoping for more "secret" kind of fixes but, alas, there does not seem to be any. What I learned? Chewing gum, sleeping on my stomach, and how I relax to read are probably my main culprits in exacerbating symptoms. If you want a reminder how to change things up holistically and naturally, this is the book to check out.
