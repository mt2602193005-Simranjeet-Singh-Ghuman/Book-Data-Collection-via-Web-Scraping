I love Moebius' work and some of Jiro Taniguchi's works have been excellent. This is....fine? The characters are kind of flat, some of the artwork is great, some is off-putting, especially how close together some of the characters' eyes are spaced. There is also a lot of gratuitous nudity which distracts from the story and seems unnecessary. Those pages could have been used to instead give the characters more depth.
