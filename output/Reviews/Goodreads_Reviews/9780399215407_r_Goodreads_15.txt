"Tomie dePaola's Book of Poems" is a wonderful anthology of classics as well as modern poems. The poems are poems that children can relate too and are great to read as a family. The collection includes poems from Emily Dickinson, Robert Frost, Lewis Carroll, Byrd Baylor and many others. The book also has wonderful illustrations that incorporate a lot of color and detail. This is a fun book that every student will find a poem they will enjoy. I would use this with the 7-12 age range.
