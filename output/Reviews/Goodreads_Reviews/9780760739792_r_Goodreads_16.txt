Once upon a time I was into Weird New Jersey. The magazine highlights interesting (and weird) places in New Jersey. The interest grew over the years to expand beyond the state. That is when things got weird (pardon the pun) for Mark and Mark. The franchise lost its appeal as they went national, had a television show, and began publishing books. This first book about New Jersey was basically a re-print of articles from the magazine put together in an anthology. Overall, for longtime readers, this was disappointing. For those who had never read the magazines, I imagine it was thrilling to gain insight into the weirdness of the Garden State.
