The stories are very short, most around 4 pages. But, some are quite good, making good use of their space to tell quick snappy stories and vignettes. Some are not as good, feeling like they don't really tell a full story, or that they are wasting their space. It runs the gamut of comedy to drama to horror. The horror ones seem odd, it's hard to build up a good horror tone in such a short space. The big problem with the book is that such short stories are like pieces of candy, and reading the whole book through is kind of like eating a plateful. It works better a few at a time, to give them time to percolate.
