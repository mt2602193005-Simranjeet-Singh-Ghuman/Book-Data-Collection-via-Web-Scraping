Absolutely love this classic! I love how the world is portrayed, and oneself, through the characters, actually believes The Game is just that, a game, until it isn't. Until it is more than that, and one is on another planet, the full-face of it astounding . This is something that is not seen in many books today as many books do not give the reader a chance to be confused with the characters, and while that is not always a bad thing, it definitely is a stronger element of writing when one finds themself grasping at straws and learning with the characters in a slow, less-informed plot. It is sad to see that Hughes's works are becoming obsolete for classics, when they are strong reads, especially for children. Hopefully, one day, Hughes's works become more known again. There is so much potential. So much theme . And just beautiful old writing!
