This book has been recommended to me countless times and cited as one of my favourite authors inspiration, but because it's not my usual style and on the longer side, I put it off for a long time. I'm so glad I finally gave in. I can't do justice to the quality of the writing, the characters or the emotions. There is so much adversity, brutality but also so much heart and kindness in it. It had been years since a book both made me both laugh and cry, and since I found so weel written characters, especially the feminine ones. I'm so sad to have to let this characters go, but so grateful I got to spend time with them. What a beautiful book. Now it's my time to recommend it.
