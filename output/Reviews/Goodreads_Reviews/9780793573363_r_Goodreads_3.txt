One of my favourite books ever. Learning what people have done to help others around the worl in the name of Jesus makes me feel insignificant at times but this book inspires me to no end. I was and am moved by this collection of stories, I experienced tears of joy, laments of heart ache and moments of complete awe. It is a lot to take in and it will be a while before I read it again but I WILL read it again!
