Ridiculous title but decent book. I liked Annie's steady determination to turn her life around and Maddox was a good fit for her and her kid. If their abortive high school romance was rekindled a little too quickly, so it goes in series romance. The weakest part of the story was the lingering resentment from Annie regarding events of high school past- while everyone should have certainly used their words I cannot blame Maddox for pulling out so quickly given the very real legal and ethical issues at stake. Incidentally, this reads like a PSA on the role that guns play in domestic violence.
