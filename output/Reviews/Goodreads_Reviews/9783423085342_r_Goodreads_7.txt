3.6 I ordered this book because I've recently read some of Scottoline's newer works, and picked this up on sale. It's really old, but it was kind of fun to read a story with no mentions of social media or AI 🤣 I find Scottoline to be a very diverse writer, there's always an element of mystery (at least in her books that I've read so far) but she uses many different settings and time periods, which is fun. This one started a tiny bit slow, but the writing is sharp, the MC amusing and sarcastic, and it quickly picked up speed. I really enjoyed the ride - don't get me wrong, this isn't a deep meaningful story that leaves you thinking about the characters for weeks, but it is a fun, surprisingly engaging mystery. If you want a light read that isn't too light, this one is very readable and with just the right amount of twists - not over the top, and believable.
