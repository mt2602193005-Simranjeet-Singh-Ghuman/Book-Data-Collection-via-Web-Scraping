This book tries to be many things: A book about Bob Dylan and the Band’s “Basement Tapes”, a book about classic folk and blues musicians from the 1920s, a book about Harry Smith’s role in the folk revival with his “Anthology of American Folk Music”, a book about the mythologizing of America’s past. Unfortunately, Greil Marcus failed to thread the needle on this very ambitious concept. Marcus’s prose is very impressionistic and stream of consciousness which just left me feeling very lost and adrift amongst the various concepts he was tying together. You can feel his passion for the subject and there were some truly interesting parts of the book (particularly the sections about Harry Smith’s anthology and Dock Boggs’ life), but sadly it did not all some together in the end.
