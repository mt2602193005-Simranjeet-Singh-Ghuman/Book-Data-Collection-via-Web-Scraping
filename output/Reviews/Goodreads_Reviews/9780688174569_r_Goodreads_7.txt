Another find at The ArtsXchange little free library. The cover is misleading. The van Porter buys on impulse isn't a caravan/trailer like that. No, it's a Grateful Dead truck/van emblazoned with a vivid, psychedelic mural. Much hilarity ensues admidst some heart breaking grief as he and his family navigate the recent death of Lucy, his wife. Published in 2001, back when cell phones weren't as sophisticated as they are now there was a refreshing off the grid simpleness to the story.
