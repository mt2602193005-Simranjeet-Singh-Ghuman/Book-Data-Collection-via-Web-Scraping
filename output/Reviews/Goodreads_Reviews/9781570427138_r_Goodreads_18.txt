Book 5 in Alex Cross series. An entertaining read but my least favorite of the series so far. Alex Cross is one of my favorite characters ever! I love following his work and life throughout all of James Patterson’s Alex Cross novels. This one was entertaining and kept me invested but didn’t hold me quite like the rest of the series had. Personally I’m not a fan of Cross’ love interest and their relationship so I think because this one was centered along that in the second half of the book it kind of lost me! I’m excited to read on with this series to see what Alex Cross does next! Alex Cross is back at it again hunting down a British diplomat serial killer who’s playing a deadly game. Lines cross when Alex’s personal life gets intertwined with his current investigation.
