We don't usually go for books with TV characters, but the subject was enough to get me to pick it up for the preschool at the library's by-the-bag book sale. It's not too bad. It covers a variety of ways to recycle and reuse things, which is good. I was disappointed in Piggy's conclusion that with the money from her yard sale she will go shopping for new clothes. Arg. Ending on that note pretty much kills the positive points.
