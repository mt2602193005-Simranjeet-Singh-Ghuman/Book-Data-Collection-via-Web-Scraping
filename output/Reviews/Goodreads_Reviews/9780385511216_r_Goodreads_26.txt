Nobody writes historical fiction,particularly regarding London,than Peter Ackroyd. Ackroyd deftly weaves a tale of murder,intrigue,secret societies and a power struggle for the English crown. You can smell the rivers,the streets,the people of the period(a lot of it,which makes you appreciate Air Fresheners). A well written,pacey tale that captures so much in it's short length. Another Ackroyd gem.
