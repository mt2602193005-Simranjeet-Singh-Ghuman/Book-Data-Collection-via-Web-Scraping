Cute and frivolous frenemies to lovers where the heroine is light years smarter than the hero. I've seen the reviews more than once, so a must read because GR reviewers say so. It was cute and would make a nice little Rom-com as the hero is led around by his nose. Much has been made of the plot where she's the bratty little sister while he was in love with the elder sister that his brother wins. Hmm. Sir Clueless has put the older pale pastel sister on a pedestal, enshrined in his heart and all that crap, while the bright and vibrant heroine buzzes around creating havoc in the past and present. The older sister calls him and asks if the h can stay with him in London. He hasn't seen the h in several years. When the H sees her again he doesn't recognize the little sis and confesses to her he has to pick up a poisonous young charge. Laughs on him more than once when she points out that she's the gorgeous grown up version of her bratty self. The h is the flower and all them men are bees to her loveliness. There is a mildly bitchy OW who is another pale pastel creature and mirrors the blandness of his original love that doesn't make the cut. She goes from one job and one escapade to the next while the hero pants after her trying to rescue her from bad jobs, lecherous men including his friends and a bad apartment selected by his current girlfriend. She kind of reminds me of a more clothed version of Goldie Hawn's character in Butterflies Are Free. The big question is does he really fall in love with her or is she second best to her older sister? I think yes. He may be clueless, but he's not a total idiot, and the heroine has sass and charm to spare. Plus, the big misunderstanding is his regret in failing to take care of the h as he promised he would rather than regret he lost the older sister. My only real complaint is the cover. Yikes. He's downright scary looking.
