I just love the Berenstain Bear's stories! They are all full of love and imagination with a great intake of moral character to teach children everywhere! I highly recommend these wonderful books to everyone, everywhere because you can never go wrong with a lovely family of bears! "The Berenstain Bears: Think of Those in Need" is a wonderful tale about giving back to those around who might be less fortunate than you. I have found that this is a hard moral trait to teach small children, but I believe that this children's story did a beautiful job at it. If you find yourself having a hard time of explaining giving back to your small children, I highly recommend this book.
