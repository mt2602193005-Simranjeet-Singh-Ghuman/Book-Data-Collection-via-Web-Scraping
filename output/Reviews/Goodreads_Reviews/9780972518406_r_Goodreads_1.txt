Sometimes I come across an obscure book that intrigues me. This book is one of those books. I retained the book from a selection of books that a family member was clearing out from their shelves for me to donate - or read and then donate to a local book sale. So, the Music of Heartohopia is a rather spiritual book, particularly regarding music and life in general. The book is comprised of a collection of notes (pun not intended, but maybe it is!) from a visitor to our planet by the name of Onaip Springwell. Onaip originates from the planet Heartohopia. The notes cover topics such as the muse, ears, notes, rests, and dynamics. I was able to determine that Onaip Springwell is a simple anagram for Piano and Wellspring. Piano is both an instrument and a dynamic mark - I'm not a very advanced musician. A Wellspring is a source of continual supply. George Trier compiled the book. I was unable to find any information on George Trier. The book was published in 2003, and I read it in 2025. My family member most likely acquired the book at a music retreat. Another interesting thing I uncovered while searching for additional information about the book was that there was a famous and baffling Vaudeville act that premiered in May 1910 at the East End in Memphis. The act featured a magician named ONAIP, who began his act by playing the piano. As the piece continued, the piano and player began rising in the air, and then the entire collection began to "whirl and revolve like some maddened thing." The man's identity was never revealed. I will be passing my copy on to a friend of mine who holds a degree in music education. I hope you will treat yourself to a read of this book if you are able to find a copy.
