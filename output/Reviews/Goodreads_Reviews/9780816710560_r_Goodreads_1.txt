Now this is everything I like in a book. A good guy who gets betrayed but manages to come back to everything he's worth. This was a really good story and I really enjoyed it. I don't think there are any faults with this story other than I wish that it had been longer. Still you can't really fault a faerie tale for that. Longer than most, enjoyable, and I had a smile on my face the whole story. Really well done, and I recommend this one.
