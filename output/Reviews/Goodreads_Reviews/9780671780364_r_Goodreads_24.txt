This is an absolutely excellent book. It's where modern politics begins. It describes how Nixon, having failed to secure the presidency in the previous election, left nothing to chance. how he entrusted his fate to an advertising man, how he crafted an image which was sold like soap powder. It really explains so much about all the politics that followed, both in the United States and elsewhere. It's historical and it sounds very basic and very crude by modern standards, but it's the start of a revolution in democracy and one that really doesn't end well.
