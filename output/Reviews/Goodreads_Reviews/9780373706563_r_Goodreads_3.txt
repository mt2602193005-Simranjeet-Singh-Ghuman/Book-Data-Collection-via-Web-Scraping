This was a pretty good book. There were plenty of emotions and good personalities overall. I was a little disappointed at the end, sometimes the craziness takes away from the feeling of closure. I mostly liked the characters but it seemed like mostly I just wanted to knock heads. I did like the plot, and the side characters were about 50/50 with me. Half I really liked an the others I really was aggravated then exasperated with. I loved the mythology and the NA connections though and I wished there was a little more of that included. Overall a good book for just reading, make sure it can take a little wear an tear though.
