Disappointing This is the second book I have read in the Sophie Rivera series and I find I do not like her at all. Although she is a principled and moralistic tutor in the first book she throws herself at a handsome conductor and then between the two books she had an affair with a married colleague. in the second book she homes in on a visiting poet and has sex with him several times only to find at the end of the book that he is married with children. Not my type of character at all.
