I love this book and if you’re traveling the Great Lakes to see lighthouses, it’s a great book to take with you on your travels. It’s out of print, so you’ll have to buy a used one, but I found one on Amazon that didn’t have any writing in it. This is my 2nd copy. I bought my first in the late 1990’s and had documented over 50 lighthouses I’d seen in person, but I guess I lost it in a move. So now I can start over again. Yay for me. I get to take more trips to see lighthouses.
