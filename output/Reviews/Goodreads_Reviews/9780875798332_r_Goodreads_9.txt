I re-read this book recently and enjoyed it just as much as I did the first time. Genealogy and murder mystery are a brilliant combination. Alex is a fun, but not too perfect, protagonist, with plenty of realistic (and a few less realistic) problems. Although it's an LDS novel, the religious themes weren't overpowering to the story, which I appreciated. A fun, light read for anyone who likes genealogy, mysteries, or especially a combination of the two. Note: for some reason the books in this series aren't officially numbered, but this is the first book.
