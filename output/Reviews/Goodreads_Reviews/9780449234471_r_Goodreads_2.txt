Since the first book Mrs Pollifax has been learning Kung Fu so watch out world - anything could happen next. And it does! Mrs.P is called upon again to do a simple courier job which turns into a series of disasters. Luckily she has a talent for collecting useful and trustworthy strangers who help her along the way and she comes up trumps again. The world of spies seems to be a very convoluted place. Everyone appears to be a double or even a triple agent. The only person you can really rely on in these books is Mrs Pollifax herself. So if you feel like reading something quick, easy and completely fun jump into this series. I am off to find the next one.
