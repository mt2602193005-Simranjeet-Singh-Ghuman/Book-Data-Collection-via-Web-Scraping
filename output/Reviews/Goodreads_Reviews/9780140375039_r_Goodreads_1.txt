Okay, I must admit, I didn't finish the book. As a rule, if, after the first five chapters I still find the book boring, etc., I can put it down without feeling guilty. I read 8 chapters. I still couldn't get into it. The plot-line: A teenage aspiring author discovers that the story she is writing is coming true. But instead of going on cool adventures, and doing amazing things, she creates a poorly written, non-sensical, and jerky "love affair", which by the way, is very inappropriate. Now, I don't like writing bad stuff about books, but I reallydidn't like this book. I'm 14, and I don't really recommend this book.
