As usual, an outstanding read by Charles Swindoll. He reminds us of God’s sovereignty in our lives and in all things. Life is not without trials and difficulties. In fact, Jesus clearly tells us that. However, we don’t have to walk through them alone. We can also be sure that there are reasons for tough times. We may or may not discover these reasons this side of heaven, but we can be sure as children of God that they are for our good and His glory. This little book is a good reference to revisit from time to time as a reminder of who God is and why we can lay all our burdens at the cross of Jesus.
