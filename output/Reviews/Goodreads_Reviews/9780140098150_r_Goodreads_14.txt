Edward secretly gives his friend an LSD tab and leaves him alone while he has a liaison with Sarah a neighboring woman. When he returns Mark has fallen out a window and been killed. The rest of the story is Edward dealing with his guilt and his dysfunctional upper middle class family. Midge and her husband the worst psychiatrist in London. Harry, Edwards stepfather who Midge has been having an affair with for two years. Stuart, Edwards brother who is celibate and mystical. Edward leaves London to visit his father Jesse who he has never met and is an artist recluse who lives with his wife and two daughters in an architectural monstrosity. Alas, Jesse has dementia and the redemption Edward is looking for appears impossible. The story has a lot of coincidences which are farfetched but do bring everything together. Murdoch writes wonderfully and uses philosophy well. Moral choices, conflict, passion and moving forward after a tragedy are the crux of the story.
