Surprisingly good read of the exploits of Ian Hunter and Mott the Hoople as they try to make the breakthrough in the USA. It highlights the occasional boredom, the lack of soundchecks for support bands, the vagaries of weather in the largeness of the USA and the cheap guitars (much more expensive back home in the UK) at pawn shops. Tbh they all got more excited about the bargains to be had in the pawn shops than anything else. Ray Smillie
