Delightful. I can’t wait to reread Oliver Twist. Biography of Charles Dickens. Poor family. Worked as court reporter to earn money. “One day, Charles got tired of writing down only what other people said. He decided to use his imagination and make up a story of his own.” “Whole crowds of characters were already there in his mind, just waiting for him to put them on paper.” I love thinking about him reading aloud to audiences. His books were highly demanded and his reading of them was too. People gathered, not if or movies or parties, but for group “read aloud.” How amazing is that?? Let’s continue reading aloud all our days. Counter cultural today, but who cares?? Magic is released from the pages….
