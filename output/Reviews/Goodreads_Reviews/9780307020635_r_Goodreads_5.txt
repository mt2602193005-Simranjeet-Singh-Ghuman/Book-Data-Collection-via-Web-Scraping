The Animals' Christmas Eve: I saw this at a local bookshop and swore it was the book my grandma had since I was a kid and put out every Christmas. The first page wasn't familiar. I like the rhymes. It's cool how the donkeys remembered one of their kind who was part of the nativity. '5 gray donkeys speak with pride, Remembering one who gave a ride: "Our brother donkey went with them From Nazareth to Bethlehem." & 'six spotted calves now nibble hay Like that on which the baby lay.' Cool to tie modern times to olden ones. The page with the star in the sky is pretty. I love that color blue, and the yellow star with the white circles around it. Very pretty! They also tie in the modern sheep with the long ago shepherds, which I liked. I like the scene outside the barn behind the sheep; the blue night sky. &the green and blue on the shepherds page. I didnt like that they had used the baby lambs, after having already used sheep until I read the line "He was the Lamb of God who came." So it ended up really fitting. That's sweet that the puppies were listening well, so that they'd be able to tell the Christmas story next year. That's makes me wonder how the kittens knew it if the puppies didn't.
