Murder mystery set in the Birmingham area - OK but ... You really have to suspend your sense f reality when reading about Sophie and her investigation into murder, kidnapping and other nefarious goings-on. Why ? Would the police officers (several of which she knows from a previous story), really share so much information with a teacher of English - without much hesitation. We learn about her previous romantic entanglements while contemplating another. This all takes place in a writer's workshop in Eyre Hall. I feel that there's too much going on in this shortish novel although it's still quite enjoyable.
