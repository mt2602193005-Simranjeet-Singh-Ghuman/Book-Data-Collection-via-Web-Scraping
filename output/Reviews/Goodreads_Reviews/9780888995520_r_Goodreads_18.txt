The Canning Season confused me. It flowed like a children's book, and the main character was young, but it had some things in it that I consider inappropriate for younger readers. There was language worthy of a PG-13 rating, a reference to homosexuality, gory details, and a veiw on life, marriage, and death that creeps me out. While people could find that the endearing personalities of the characters, and the ridiculous storyline make the book worth it, I did not.
