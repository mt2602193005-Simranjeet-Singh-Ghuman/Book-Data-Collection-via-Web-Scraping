We're getting closer and closer to Peck's niche. He's warming up to writing ever more eccentric and wonderful grandmothers in this book, on his way to creating the Grandmother in A Year Down Yonder and A Long Way From Chicago. This book takes place on a cruise ship. And it's a lovely way to tell a story. The grandma doesn't bake cookies. She's too busy staying the pink limelight. This is a great story about teens learning about family in a modern world that's sorely lacking what the old world gave us: intergenerational bonds that mean so much to teens. Love it!
