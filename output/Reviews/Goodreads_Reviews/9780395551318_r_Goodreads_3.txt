This book would make a good litmus test of whether you (the reader of this review) and I would enjoy each other and have anything to talk about. Reading Italian Days is like being in the company of a remarkably well-read and well-traveled friend who can sit down without notice for a long conversation about Dante, or modern publishing, or why Milan is a fashion capital, or why certain cities are essentially female or male. I have a library copy but will need to buy my own because there are so many passages I wanted to underline. I also need to get everything else Harrison has ever written. Love.
