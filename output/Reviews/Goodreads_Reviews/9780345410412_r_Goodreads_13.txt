Second book in the Staggerford Series (although the books aren’t numbered I’m reading in order of publication date). This story follows Agatha the now retired school teacher who takes a trip to Ireland where she meets her pen-pal James, a man to whom she feels romantically inclined. Agatha, who suffers no fools and devoutly Catholic, she lives a life according to her rigid moral interpretations. Once in Ireland Agatha realizes all is not what she imagined. I’m enjoying the quiet imperfect humanity of Hassler’s books.
