⭐⭐⭐ A Decent Read This one felt stronger than The Hidden Past (Book 3). It actually digs into some decent philosophical ideas and political themes, which made parts of it surprisingly interesting. The story has a nice balance between action and reflection, and it starts to show more depth in the Jedi way of thinking. What I liked most is how it expands on the relationship between Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan. You can really see their bond developing and the tension between their different views of the Force. The writing is still simple, and not every moment lands, but overall it feels like the series is starting to mature a little. It’s not great, but it’s definitely a step up and worth the read for fans of the series.
