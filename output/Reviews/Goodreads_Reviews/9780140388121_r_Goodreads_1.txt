I’m not a fan of biographies/band stories that are not official and approved by the band. A nostalgic look into world of the biggest girl group in the world, whose story lasted a shockingly short time which always manages to surprise me. Next if I read something about Spice Girls it should be an official book about them. This had nothing new and it was based on interview quotes and comments by people somewhat close-ish to the girls. Lazy journalism/authoring if you ask me.
