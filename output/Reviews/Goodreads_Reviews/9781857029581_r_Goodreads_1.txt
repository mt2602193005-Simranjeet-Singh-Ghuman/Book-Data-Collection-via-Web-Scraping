Although the story was fairly predictable, the presentation is fresh and the writing brisk and clever. The narrator, self-deprecating and self-assured at the same time, never leaves the facts only to his imagination but involves himself in the dramatic transformation of an associate in the Tokoyo branch of an English law firm that provides the bland frame for their existence. Without resorting to polemics, this book is a clear indictement of the superficial way of life that knows no allegiance to the past and relies on fairy tale visions of possible futures.
