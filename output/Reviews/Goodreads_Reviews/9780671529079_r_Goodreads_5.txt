Just as good as the first edition of The German Aces Speak Audiobook edition (tantor) of this work Most of those considering reading this book have probably already read The German Aces Speak Volume 1 and are seeking more of the same. The good news is that this book fulfills these expectations. It has the same content but with different players. There are many first hand accounts of aircraft handling characteristics, combat in a very wide variety of aircraft against a wide variety of aircraft, etc. In addition there are a large number of stories at a level over and beyond individual accounts of combat. These include high level examinations of strategies and strengths and weaknesses of the luftwafe, administrative issues. Also, again with respect to first hand accounts, there are a large number of observations, experiences and insights involving high level Luftwaffe officials (such as Goering) as well as high level Nazi officials such as Hitler (in particular) and, to a lesser degree, characters such as Himmler. Many of these are dynamic, over time, hence the reader gets an idea of how these characters have involved over time. For example, Goering was not a bad Luftwaffe leader during the 1930s but became quite bad, progressively, with the onset and during the war. All and all a very interesting read (just like volume 1). With respect to the audio performance, this edition is so and so. Considering the high action of much of the narrative (i.e., exciting air to air combat) the voice over is quite empty of any emotion and robotic. Not very appropriate for the material. In short, the book is a four star but the audio performance is worthy of only a two star. Show more
