This is a weird book about a dude named Hasan bumble-fucking through his fate. Hasan is too bored and horny and gets kidnap whisked away into a journey of adventure and peeping at hot girls. While on his quest he kidnaps a bird-woman princess, forces her into housework and child-bearing, then when she runs away from him, he tracks her down to the far side of the globe, where he annihilates the kingdom that she's from, almost deposed the Queen, her sister. And then at the end he looks like the good guy. Pretty par for the course for Anthony books. I didn't dislike it, but it was definitely an early career book. The characters aren't very flushed out, kind of just acting as tokens to get us through the plot, which is fine, as the adventurous plot really is the most interesting part of the story.
