This book was a good ABC book especially if your class has enjoyed hearing the other Miss Spider books. I thought it was one of the better ABC books I have read because it actually tells a story with the creatures that it introduces with each letter. At the end of the story it tells that each of the insects are throwing Miss Spider a surprise birthday party. I would read this to my class that is learning letters and have them pick a letter of the alphabet and color and spell the insect represented in the book by that letter.
