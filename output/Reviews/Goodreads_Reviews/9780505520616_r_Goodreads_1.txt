Lauren Hamilton, an architect, buys an old house and tries to renovate it. But somehow the past comes haunting her. The wife of its former owner died here. Was it murder? Who murdered her, her husband or someone else? Here we can read a very uncanny tale of Lauren becoming more and more obsessed with the house and the dead wife. Her whole personality and appearance changes. Can her friends help her? What about her fiance Robert? How will the story end? Creepy novel with Ouija-board insight and all the right elements of horror you'd expect. A slow burner of hair raising tension you can't put down. Highly recommended blast from the past. This is the way you want to read horror!
