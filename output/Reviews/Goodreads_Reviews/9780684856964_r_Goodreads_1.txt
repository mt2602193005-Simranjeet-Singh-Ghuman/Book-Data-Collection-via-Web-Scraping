Frankly, I'm puzzled. How can this book be published by the same people who put out the excellent Simon & Schuster MEGA crossword Puzzle Books? No puzzle maker worth their salt would accept the same word for an answer and a clue. For example, on page 103: booster: 11 booster dose, booster shot, booster unit, 12 booster stage 13 booster rocket, 14 takeoff booster. The whole book is redundant. And instead of including page 729 (Abbreviations and Acronyms) through page855 (Weights and Measures) in the body of the book, they make you conduct a whole new search for answers. Save your money for The Million Word Crossword Dictionary.
