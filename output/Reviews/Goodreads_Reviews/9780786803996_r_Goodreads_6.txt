CIP: Three warthogs count to ten as they bake cupcakes. Review: Colorful illustrations, easy to read rhyming scheme with good sense of meter, plus a dash of humor and also culinary tips (including recipes at the end) make this a fabulous and entertaining book for children. Publishers Weekly says "this is lightweight, irresistible fun kids can count on." Parents Choice says "the book is something of a muddle, but the pictures are winning."
