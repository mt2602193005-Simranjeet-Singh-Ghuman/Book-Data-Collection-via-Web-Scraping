In the state that this world is in, it really feels like we go 1 step forward to go 2 steps back. So I’m really trying to lean on history to inspire me about the change that can happen. I think this novel about the underground abortion service “Jane” in Chicago during the late 60s-early 70s is particularly relevant right now. It’s a bit of a brutal story in many ways because of the jarring measures taken to provide abortions, like unlicensed doctors giving them, the uncertainty in how far along a women was in her pregnancy, the experimental, “new” practices in doing abortions, etc. However, the overwhelming success of Jane in Pre Roe v Wade era to help thousands of women safely receive the reproductive care they deserve, reminds me that groups run by women for women will always exist and persist.
