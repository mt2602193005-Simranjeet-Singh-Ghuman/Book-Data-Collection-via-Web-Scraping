Wholesome family vacation story with cozy 90s nostalgia— a winning combo. Arthur feels a bit anxious about spending a week with his family on vacation, especially because that means lots of time with his annoying kid sister, D.W. He’d much rather be at camp with his best friend Buster! Indeed, the family vacation seems off to a rocky start, as many unexpected challenges arise. Can it be salvaged? Or is Arthur destined to have the Worst Summer Vacation Ever?
