What a sensuous, evocative historical novel! The Tale of Genji is set in ancient Japan, and it largely follows the love life of Genji, a favorite son of the emperor. This is like the old school version of Married at First Sight where women were largely kept out of view, whispering to potential suitors through paper walls or curtains. And ordinary words would never do. The couple must speak to each other by quoting poetry! Reading this book transports the reader by describing the beautiful scents of perfume and the magnificent color of the robes, all with perfect control. A beautiful journey to a time when things were a little simpler and where attention was paid to the details Because details matter.
