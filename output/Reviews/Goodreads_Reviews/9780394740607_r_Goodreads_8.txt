This is a brilliant epic poem written very much in the style of the Homeric Epics. It is amazing the way Gardner is able to adapt his style - from the style of Ian Fleming when he continued the James Bond series, to himself, and to Grendel in which he successfully captured the spirit of the Middle English work he was responding to. A brilliant writer and a great shame that this work is so difficult to buy due to it being out of print. It will appeal to a small audience of those interested in the classics.
