This was my third book from Dickens,after A Christmas Carol and Great Expectations. And well , despite Dickens is a champion of his genre, it wasn't my book. Dickens ghosts are not the shocking and terrifying creatures. Their functions, while no less mysterious and occassionally unnerving, usually have to do with intervening in human affairs to right or prevent a wrong. His stories are more odd rather than frightenig. But I wasn't as much entertained as I expected. Nice to read, but for me, no page turner.
