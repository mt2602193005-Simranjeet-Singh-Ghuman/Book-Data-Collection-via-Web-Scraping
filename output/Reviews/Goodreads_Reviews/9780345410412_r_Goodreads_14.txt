A true blind read for me. I picked this up for free off the discount rack at a used bookstore in Sleepy Hollow last autumn and am only just getting around to it now. Hassler has an admirable command of atmosphere and mood, pleasingly crafting mental images of the parallel beauties of Ireland and rural Minnesota, but his characters don't really inspire, and I wasn't very fond of the conclusion of Randy's arc. That said, I might recommend it to my grandmother. The themes around religion and the cultural evolution of faith could be up her alley.
