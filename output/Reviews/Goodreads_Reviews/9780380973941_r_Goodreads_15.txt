Man…. I am sad to say that I do typically love JA Vance but this book just changed my perspective. While I recognize that it was written a significant longer time ago, the derogatory term used in this book on multiple occasions when discussed undocumented immigrants was out of pocket and to use it so easily like it was the norm was definitely shocking to me when the same character frequently has talked about how she stood up for her friend when being mistreated. That left a sour taste in my mouth and I sadly do not think I’ll continue reading this series.
