The Watsons Go to Birmingham – 1963 by Christopher Paul Curtis coming of age and historical novel that follows the Watson family from Flint, Michigan, as they take a trip to Birmingham, Alabama, during the Civil Rights Movement. Kenny Watson, a 10 yo , his mom Wilona, his father Daniel, his little sister, Joetta and his older brother, Byron. Due Byron’s rebellious behavior prompts the family to to take a trip to Birmingham. Byron will spend the summer with their strict grandmother hoping to straighten him out. The author writes a novel that gives the reader many giggles as long as moments to draw out the family drama. The family arrives in Birmingham just before the 16th Street Baptist Church bombing that killed four young Black girls. This is a sad moment of reality with racism and the violence during the fight for Civil Rights. This is a Historical Fiction novel written in a way to reach the audience for young adults to understand the harsh reality of this sad era in the United States history.
