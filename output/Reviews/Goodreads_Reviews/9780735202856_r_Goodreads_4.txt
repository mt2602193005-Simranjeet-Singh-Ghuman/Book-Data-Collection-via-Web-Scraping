I am rating this for originality and heft of ideas, not writing. This book may come across as a little cheesy, as it did for me, until I considered that it may be the origin, or at least a key 20th Century popularization, of important performance enhancing techniques, and that from my layman's point of view, said techniques and concepts remain as sound as ever against current neuro- and cognitive science. Why was Lindsey Vonn, the gold medalist alpine skier, often seen, eyes closed in a silent, mental, pre-race run down the course last olympics? Read this and find out. The book is a little long considering the relatively simple, straightforward ideas in it. The prologue from the revising author is entirely skippable, and forgettable, but the main ideas here are not.
