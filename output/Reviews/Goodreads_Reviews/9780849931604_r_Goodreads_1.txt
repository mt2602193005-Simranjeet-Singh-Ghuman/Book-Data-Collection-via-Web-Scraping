This was a lovely read. It was full of stories about how people discovered God, and what they did to make themselves ‘born again.’ Each one of the stories was beautiful. It also included definitions and talked about how important it is to have God in your life. However, it didn’t really have tips about how to be Born Again until the very end, so it’s a process to get there, first you read the stories and some bible verses that go along with it and some biblical stories and the end it’s the same way plus the tips on how to make yourself born again. It’s a beautiful read, it just takes a long time to get to the whole point of the book.
