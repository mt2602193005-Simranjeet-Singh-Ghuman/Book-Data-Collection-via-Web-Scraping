R.L. Stine is at his best in Halloween Party. The book is about nine seemingly random students from Shadyside High School that were invited to attend a Halloween party at the home of the new, beautiful transfer student, Justine Cameron. Justine Cameron's home is on Fear Street and that means nothing is what it seems. One of the students invited, Nikki, is a deaf girl with a large curiosity. Nikki makes it her mission to figure out why Justine invited the select nine that she did, as well as determine why she spent so much on her lavish party for such a small group. Justine prepares them for Halloween by playing practical jokes with a promise of a huge surprise at the end of the party. The party is a lot of fun until odd things begin to happen, like finding a body in another room. Could it be Justine's pranks again, or something much more serious? I remember reading this book in the sixth grade and literally staying up all night to finish it, in the safety of my bed. R.L. Stine is a main force in why I enjoy horror today.
