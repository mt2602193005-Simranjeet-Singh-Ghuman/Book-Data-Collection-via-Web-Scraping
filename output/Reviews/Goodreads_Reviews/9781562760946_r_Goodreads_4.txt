Very useful for refreshing my memory on how a computer is put together, how the parts communicate with each other and over the Internet, etc. Especially since I hadn't bothered to really learn any specifics since about 1992. Only problem is that even though the copyright in my edition says 2013 (and the ISBN for that edition isn't found in Goodreads...its a BN exclusive edition with a free iPad edition on an included DVD) the 9th edition was first published in 2007 so some of the specifics are a bit out of date. The iPad version has some very nice diagram animation. But this loses a bit for some sloppy editing - there are occasional repeated paragraphs (almost like filler) and some diagrams have some wacky colors that make them hard to read. A nice book that covers everything from mother boards to the internet to digital cameras to color printing.
