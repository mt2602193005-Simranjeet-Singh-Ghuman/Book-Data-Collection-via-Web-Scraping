Selby's grandmother recently died after revealing a shocking secret. Because of which, Selby decided to face the one person who destroyed her grandmother's life in the past. Through a tracing agency, Selby miraculously found his address in Halifax. When she finally met Danny Forest, however, it was clear he was far too young to have met her grandmother, but his grandfather did and she was determined to wait for him to come back from his trip. Would Danny,the junior, allow her to meet his grandfather when he finds out her true motives for sticking to him? The writer described the heroine as impulsive, but I had the impression all the events were impulsively silly!!. :( The language is simple and there is no real depth in emotions or events. The hero kicked the heroine - physically mind you - out of his house a couple of times, called her "dumb" and shouted "Shut up!" on her face till the last chapter!!! What kind of hero is that!! When we know that the heroine had lessons in physical self-defense, yet didn't reply to these verbal attacks, then we know there is something definitely wrong with her as well!
