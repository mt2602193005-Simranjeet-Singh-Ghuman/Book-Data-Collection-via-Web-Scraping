I love the character of Olivia King Dale; of all of the people to imitate, I feel like she would be the one. This is one of my favorite episodes, because it highlights the qualities that are desirable in a man. Sure, everyone dreams of a rich, debonaire type, but pitch him against a compassionate, attentive type and he will pale! I love that Jasper and Olivia finally find true love in each other!
