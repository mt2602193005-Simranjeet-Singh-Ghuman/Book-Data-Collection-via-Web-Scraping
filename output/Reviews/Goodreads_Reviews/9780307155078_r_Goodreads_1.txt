I chose this book because it is a classic! It is full of fairy tales that I learned in my childhood and I really enjoy it because it takes me back and reminds me of the old days. This book is full of 14 different fairy tales and is great for parents to read to young children. There is a lot of text and not as many pictures, but the pictures that are in the book are really detailed and colorful so it truly appeals to the eye. The text is a little more difficult for a young reader so I do suggest having an adult read to younger children. The themes vary in all of the stories but a lot of them have to do with life lessons and happy endings. I recommend this book because it is full of so many different stories which are really fun for younger children to read, and it provides good life lessons for the children to learn as well!
