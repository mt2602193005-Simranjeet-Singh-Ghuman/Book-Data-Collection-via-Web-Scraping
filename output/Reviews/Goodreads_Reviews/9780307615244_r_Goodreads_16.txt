Warning: This reissue of the first book in the Trixie Belden, Girl Detective series, first published in 1948, will not be for everyone. That said, it’s not only for Boomers like me looking for nostalgia while re-reading the literary staples of our childhood. But the fact that the book’s being re-released shows that there remains an appetite for a spunky thirteen-year-old who — unlike the perfectly coiffed, icy Nancy Drew — who acts like a real teen. Trixie’s impulsive, strong-willed, a bit sneaky and frequently wrong. She’s also compassionate and open to self-reflection. In other words, just like teenagers throughout the ages. What a breath of fresh air she must have seen in those post-war years! That’s not to say that the book’s not a wee bit dated. It actually contains this line: “Gosh,” Honey breathed. “Golly! Golly! Golly!” Cringey, yes? But plenty of tweens would still enjoy the adventures of a nosy girl with good intentions and some great friends. And it ends with a cliffhanger that made me want to read the sequel, The Red Trailer Mystery . What’s not to like?
