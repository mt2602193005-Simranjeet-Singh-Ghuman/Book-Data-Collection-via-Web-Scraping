4.5 stars! This was so much fun, a western historical romance from Johanna Lindsey with a heroine who meddles in other people’s relationships (and got herself in a bit of a pickle) and the gunslinger hero who is flirty, protective, and very much gives touch her and die energy (literally). Cassie meddled in a relationship between rival families in town and now they both want her out of town, one of the families even threatens to burn down her father’s ranch if she doesn’t leave. Enter Angel, a gun for hire who shows up to protect her but she tries to play it off like they’re actually just engaged. Then their bluff gets called and they find themselves walking down the altar! This one was so fun, we have western adventures, Marabelle the panther (which is also on the stepback), Angel’s past of being abducted as a child and trying to find out who his family is, banter, flirting, lots of secondary side romances, a stampede, traveling to Wyoming, bad guys, and more! I for sure want to go read the rest of the series now, especially the previous book since Angel’s friend Colt makes appearances in this one too. Johanna Lindsey’s historical romances are always so much fun and hold up today!
