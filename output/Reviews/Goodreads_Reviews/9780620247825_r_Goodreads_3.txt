Interesting to read an Everest adventure from a woman's perspective and to see yet another perspective on the 1996 tragedy. Generally a very inspirational book. Cathy admits her own fears and limitations but also talks of her romance with Ian Woodall. I guess if she hadn't started the romance with Ian, she probably would not have become the first woman to summit Everest from both North and South. Mostly she speaks positively of Ian despite the end of their marriage. Cathy also shows great respect and admiration for their sherpas and fully admits she would not have managed either summit without them. Other western climbers are not so honest. Unfortunately, the copy of the book I have is a bit hard to read due to printing errors putting black splotches over some print, blanking bits of print and even doubling up pages (eg pg11/112 is there twice). No idea how that got through or if reprints have the same issues.
