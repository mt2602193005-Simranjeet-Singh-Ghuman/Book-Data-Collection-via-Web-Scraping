Koontz has the knack of writing clear and succinct, a bestseller's skill at maintaining less is less, not more is more. Economical prose aside, holy shit, this is one of the rape-iest novels in the English language. While also bogged down with pages and pages of subliminal and mind-control descriptions, this conspiracy-thriller plays-out so many scenes of forced entry and sexual violation. And the sad thing is this novel can't transcend its own bland sadism. I am the key. I am the lock. No mind control here. I nearly forgot the book once I closed the paperback. I enjoyed Koontz's 'The Bad Place', and 'Phantoms' was hackneyed fun - those are worthy in horror's old-school canon.
