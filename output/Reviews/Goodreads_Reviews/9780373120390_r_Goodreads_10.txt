Had the potential to be a lot better. But right from the start, there was a jarring note in the interactions between the leads. The intro scene was good, eye catching. Sparks flying between a cynical alpha male hero and a successful business woman heroine who happens to a widow. Though the friction between them was initially enjoyable, his continued barbs and her prickly reaction became tiresome over the pages. As always their mouths and minds speak antagonistic languages, whereas their bodies are singing the same melody. I don't know why authors over use this concept. Just because these are romance books, doesn't mean the leads have to act like sex slaves. What ever happened to mind over matter !@?#? The guy turns into a stalker and she turns into a shrew ! Anyway, the lady has a kid from a disastrous first marriage to some heartless tycoon with one leg in the grave. Going by her track record, hero assumes she must be a mercenary gold digger. She in turn keeps comparing all men to her slime ball ex husband. This pattern repeats pretty much throughout the tale. The hero has 2 brothers, I guess they are meant to be leads in other stories as part of a series. These fellows push and prod, tease and flirt. It helps to break the impasse between the leads. And of course after sufficient pages are filled, they declare undying love. Incidentally, there isn't any mentionable love scene in the story ! Ok read, nothing remarkable. 3 stars. Show more
