Yes-I'm a middle aged dork who grew up reading R.L. Stine and still loves his books. This one is a super fun locked mystery style story that takes place during a Halloween Party. Super spooky! It gets even spookier when the bodies start dropping like flies. This is a fast paced read with a killer (pun intended) twist ending that we've all come to know and love from Stine. IMO, this is one of the better Fear Street books and definitely work a read (or reread) this Halloween for the nostalgia factor alone.
