This book is about a littlele girl who goes to park and finds a hat and stuffed dog that didn't belong to her, when she left the park for the day she returned the act the lost and found but kept the dog.. She got home and everyone was kind of making her feel guilty about taking home the dog.. SO the next day, first thing in the morning, she returned the dog. After she returned the dog she went and played on the playground and there was little girl who had lost her stuffed dog.. Jamaica being so nice, took the girl right to the lost and found where she had put it a little while before.. It was really good book. The book teaches some valuable lessons to readers. The book has many textual elements involved and all are shown through many parts throughout the book. I personally really enjoyed this book and the lessons it has to teach readers. I'd recommend this book for readers around the age of 6-8.
