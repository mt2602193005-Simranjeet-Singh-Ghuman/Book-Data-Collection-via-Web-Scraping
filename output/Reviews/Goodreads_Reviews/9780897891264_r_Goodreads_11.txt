Literacy: Reading the Word and Reading the World is another gem full of lightning rods from Paulo Freire. Readers are treated with an exploration of Freire's conception of literacy -- an exploration that is not in the least bit prescriptive and instead thought provoking. Freire's quick dismantlement of the different approaches to literacy is something every teacher should weigh and consider, but so few like to read Freire because he doesn't map it out and do the thinking for you. Freire's emancipatory literacy is the best form of student empowerment that I've seen yet. Here's a favorite quote, "a person is literate to the extent that he or she is able to use language for social and political reconstruction."
