The unlikely hero. I remember the first time I read Pride and Prejudice. I imagine every woman does. It's like the first time you have sex. Except good. At any rate, I had somehow made it to my early 20s without getting the plot spoiled, so when Lizzie got that letter from Mr. Darcy, I was floored to find myself in love with him. And I'm not comparing my feelings for Mr. Darcy (and yes , I still have feelings) to Kitty's man. But around the halfway mark, much like in P&P, my feelings started to undergo a shift that I did not originally see coming. I do not want to spoil this one for anyone, so I'll just give the skinny gist of the story and leave it at that. Kitty is an orphan who was adopted by her father's rich bachelor BFF when she was young. Now, the old guy is a skinflint, but he's loaded. AND he's leaving everything to Kitty, as long as she marries one of his nephews. One is a stodgy old vicar, one has an exceptionally low IQ, one is an independently wealthy dandy who doesn't want to get married, and one is a handsome rake (Jack) who didn't even bother to show up when his uncle called him for the announcement. Now, Kitty is feisty and really doesn't care about her guardian's money, so she turns down the offers that some of her faux-cousins give her. Shocking! But she does hatch a plan to go to London and see the sights using the sheer force of her personality against the dandy - who is her friend if nothing else. Since she knows he doesn't want to marry her, she concocts a fake engagement to allow her to visit his family in London, and perhaps even show Jack what he's missing out on. Kitty hits London like a sock full of nickels upside the head, and the hijinks ensue. And that's all you need to know. Now, quit asking me questions and go read the book. Highly recommended for fans of Regency Romance.
