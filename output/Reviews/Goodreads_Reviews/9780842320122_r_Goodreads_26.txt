While I shelved this as "historical fiction", I really felt like I was reading history. The author did her research. A lot of it. And it shines through in this novel. I found myself hoping I could grab some time to read 'more'. You will recognize some names as those as based on facts; a few are created. Each is brought alive in this novel. The author addresses the lives they lived, why no ships arrived for a long time, where the colonists went and even what could have happened. I highly recommend this book. It's a great read. But the 'historical' aspects of it make it fascinating to the history buff as well as a genealogical researcher. I'm thankful this was a free Amazon Kindle download. And as I write this review, it still is.
