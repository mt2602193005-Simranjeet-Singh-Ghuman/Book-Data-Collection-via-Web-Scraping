2.5 Stars. This wasn't really much of a mystery. The book spent much more time on Jenny's job as a mall Promotions Director than on the murder. I did find that somewhat interesting, but overall it made for a slow read. Not very compelling, I found I could keep putting the book down to do other things. Also, the characters weren't very deep, I couldn't keep any of the other mall managers straight in my head because they were so flat. I found the main character Jenny to be a bit quirky and her relationship with Helen was unclear to me. I might have given this series one more try, but there doesn't seem to be a follow-up book.
