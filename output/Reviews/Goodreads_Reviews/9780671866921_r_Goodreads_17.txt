So this was first published in 1991, but oh wow, it could've been written in response to the events of today. Robots taking over jobs! And the comments about unemployeds living off taxpayer money? Owwwwww, that was hard to read. So that was interesting and I liked this for the most part, though the characters didn't really stand out to me. The ending weakened it. I liked getting a glimpse of their future, but, come on, "We met up with a group of 10 other people and within a year each of us had married one of the other 10!" REALLY? It worked out that perfectly??? Especially since we're talking TEENAGERS. Anyway, glad I finally pulled this off my shelf.
