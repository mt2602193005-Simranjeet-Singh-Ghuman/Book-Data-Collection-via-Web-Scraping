A favourite that I read and re-read as a kid; my 10-year-old self would have given it 5+ stars. I stumbled across a copy again several years ago and held on to it. Lynn suspects that her sister Judith is becoming a witch, under the tutelage (and spell) of Mrs Tuggle, the spooky old woman who lives in the house at the top of their hill. Lynn enlists her best friend Mouse to spy on Judith and read up on the habits of witches. The narrative in this first book could have gone either way: Judith could be becoming a witch and Mrs Tuggle could be starting a coven, or Judith is merely becoming a teenager and Mrs Tuggle is spooky but innocent. Subsequent books conclude that Lynn is right about the witchcraft, but I always liked this first book the best because the author leaves the questions unresolved. The book is dated and hard to find these days, so I would struggle to recommend it to anyone. But I read it every few years at Halloween.
