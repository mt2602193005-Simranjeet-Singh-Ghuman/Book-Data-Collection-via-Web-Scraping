Ty is still in the hospital, and Amy misses him. It is really difficult without him. In Heartland, a new horse arrives. It is a mustang. Amy tries to train him, but it is pretty difficult. Amy starts feeling discouraged in herself. She doesn't know what to do. What will happen? Will Ty get better? Will Amy train the new mustang? Read this awesome book to find out! I really like this book. You have got to read it!
