The Power of Ideas was a well written textbook with a lot of philosophical examples, public figures and unfamiliar vocabulary. The book makes you feel more studious if anything; while reading through the book for my philosophy course there was many highlighted boxes with famous philosophers and their ideas which helped me understand what I was reading a little better. The book guides you through philosophy in a chronological order and points out the most significant points. With a very accessible and easy index section the book is excellent to use for studying or researching. Overall, I did like the book because it helped me understand many famous philosophers, it helped me understand theories formed around the world, and also helped me write a good research paper. I recommend this book to anyone interested in someday pursuing a career in philosophy, or someone who needs to do a little research on philosophy in the past and present, because this textbook has a lot of information to offer.
