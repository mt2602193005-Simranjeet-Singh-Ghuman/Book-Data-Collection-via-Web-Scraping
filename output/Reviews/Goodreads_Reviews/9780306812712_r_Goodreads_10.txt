Most interesting part of this collection: Derogatis' revealing account of his time at "Rolling Stone," where he was told not to give poor reviews to new records by Hootie & the Blowfish and Dave Matthews Band, because they were the biggest bands on earth at the time. Honestly, as big of a fan I am as "Rolling Stone," their rating system in recent years has always struck me as odd -- like, they'll attach *** to what is clearly a negative review. Could America's greatest counterculture mag bend beneath corporate pressure? Uh, yeah, probably...
