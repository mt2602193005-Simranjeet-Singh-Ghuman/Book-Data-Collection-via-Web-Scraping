If you, like I, have a spot in your heart for Lucca, you will really enjoy this book. Detective Arbati is in Lucca to accept a prize for his latest poetry book. While there, he joins an old friend from police academy days in solving a murder mystery. The book isn’t perfect. The English people are a bit stereotyped. The descriptions of driving in Italy are fun but also kind of superfluous to the story. On the plus side, the humor in the book is delightful. Descriptions of Lucca’s architecture, food, people, history and surrounding areas are so clear that you feel like you know the town. Show more
