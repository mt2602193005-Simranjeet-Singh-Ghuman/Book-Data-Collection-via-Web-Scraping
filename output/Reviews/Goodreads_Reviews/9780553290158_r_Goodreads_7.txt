This is a long book, over 560 pages, describing some of the wealth and hazards required to get underway with a transcontinental railroad. The beginning is in Omaha where a half-Cherokee young man gets taken on as a scout for a journey across the great open plains. A gentleman wants to survey possible routes. His daughter will be coming along so he is bringing staff and cooks and intends to dine properly every night. Well, by the end of the tale the Indians are fighting back at their lands being taken and the scout has fallen for the daughter he at first considered a nuisance. If you enjoy a well-detailed romance epic this will reward you with a lively read. I downloaded an e-ARC through Fresh Fiction. This is an unbiased review.
