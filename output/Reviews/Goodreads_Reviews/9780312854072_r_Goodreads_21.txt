This book starts out with a Gilligan’s Island premise — a diverse group of tourists visit a remote planet to see the rare sight of a supernova. It unfolds into something like four separate stories with those characters, and I feel like each would have been a characteristically stellar (wink) Tiptree short story, but the combination isn’t so cohesive. There’s an older woman psychically connected to her comatose sister, a group of teenage sex workers whose options in life are limited because of their work, a follow-up to the Stars Tears story and a revenge story for a destroyed planet. If that sounds like a lot and kind of random, it is!
