I picked this up at a sale because my father-in-law likes Abraham Lincoln. I wanted to read the book before I gave it to him. I learned a lot more about Abraham Lincoln and how much he loves to tell a little stories and parables. He seemed to have a very developed sense of humor and pretty solid mindset. Of course there's some things that I do not like about Abraham Lincoln namely that he killed a bunch of Dakota Indians for no reason. Of course that was not mentioned in this book. I will not remember much of what I read as far as the stories go but it was a good collection. It was a little bit strange has some of the stories were organized but I think the author did the best they could to make them easier to find. I appreciate the timeline at the end that showed what Lincoln was going through and what was happening in the larger world of the time because it's a good context to have. I was glad to read Snippets of his speeches as well. It's also very sad to compare the way he speaks to how president 4547 speaks and the state of ruin that that president has put upon us. It's embarrassing especially when you look at how dignified and intelligent and well spoken Lincoln was.
