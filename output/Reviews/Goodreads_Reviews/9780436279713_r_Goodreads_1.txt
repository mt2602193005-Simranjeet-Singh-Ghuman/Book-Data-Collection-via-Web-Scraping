Published in 1989, CARIBBEAN is a 920 page work of historical fiction whose story is represented in sixteen chapters. Each chapter selects a separate island to tell a slice of its history via a singular story. A great deal of despair, bloodshed and barbarity describes the force of man to achieve the hard quests for fame and fortune. The inhumane treatment of those who are different is continuously in the forefront. I admire Michener’s work, but found this book to be difficult to read at times. Historical detail is abundant and it caused my eyes to wander, but I was steadfast that I would complete the book.
