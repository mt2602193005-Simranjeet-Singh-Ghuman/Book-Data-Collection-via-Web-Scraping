Funny and absurd and full of truth. Stories about people and their routines being distorted by life. Something I love about Beattie is the way her stories work to set up this false sense of logical procession before turning in ways I never expect. Mostly, I come away from her stories feeling seen and understood. Little glimpses into the unpredictability of all of our lives and individual searches for happiness and meaning. Couple of just ok ones in here, but pretty incredible average for a debut collection. Fave stories: Vermont The Parking Lot Imagined Scenes
