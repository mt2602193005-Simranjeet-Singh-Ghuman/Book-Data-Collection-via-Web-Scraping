An eBay origin story grounded in solid facts, but ultimately lacking depth, insight, and relevance. Focusing on eBay’s early years, the book presents a clear historical account from both the company’s and the merchants’ perspectives. While the factual foundation is strong, it stops short of offering deeper analysis or sharper insights that would help readers truly understand the platform’s strategic significance. The material also feels dated, with no discussion of later, critical developments such as the PayPal and Skype acquisitions, which limits its value for contemporary readers.
