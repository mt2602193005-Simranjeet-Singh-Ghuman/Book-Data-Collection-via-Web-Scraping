There are a handful of extinction level threats from the last century that are publicly known, the Cuban Missile Crisis being the most famous. In this short book, RFK gives us a glimpse into his perspective during the sensitive meetings that took place with President Kennedy and a few other department heads and senior military officials. It's a bit surreal reading through the discussions that were occurring along with the public transcripts between President Kennedy and Nikita Khrushchev. President Kennedy was walking such a tightrope, having to grapple between the conflicting advice he was getting. RFK also writes his thoughts on the philosophy of government, including how its role has evolved in both theory and practice since the founding of the United States. Even though this section was kept short, this really shows me that his tragic assassination robbed our country of a brilliant mind in an era of such chaos. Overall, this memoir is worth the read for any history buff out there.
