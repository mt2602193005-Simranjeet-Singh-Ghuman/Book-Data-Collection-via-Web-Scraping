A few days ago my 85-year-old neighbor found out that I'd grown up loving Herb Caen and read his column until he died. She lent me her first edition copy of this book, and I went through at record speed, afraid of damaging it. Made me miss Herb all over again! A charming glimpse of San Francisco, socialites, movie stars, sports stars, and 'local color', as described by an sui generis journalist!
