After reading the hypnotic, addictive dream-like Flowers in the attic series this was my first Andrew Neiderman venture. The formula is there but just lacks something. Ruby Landry has a dead mother who died at birth, lives with her grandma, her grandfather is a violent, evil, filthy drunk who lives in the swamp. Her first love at school she finds out is her half brother. She nearly slept with him anyway. Then the big bombshell drops. Ruby has a twin sister she never knew about. Her real father is one of the most affluent families in New Orleans. Ruby's grandfather tries to marry her off to a disgusting piece of shit. Ruby tries to escape but is chained to the bed. Lucky her grandfather is a dumb shit and didn't do an adequate enough job. The moment Ruby sees her twin Giselle for the first time, she is not happy but the father is delighted. The family make a story that Ruby was kidnapped and now has returned. Gotta keep up appearances. Ruby tries hard to fit into the luxurious world of the Dumas family. Giselle is a complete bitch constantly getting her twin in trouble. So frustrating. Ruby with the help of a servant go to a voodoo queen and put a curse of Giselle. Now Giselle is a paraplegic ouch. Daphne her stepmother is such a prick. To the point she tries to get Ruby committed to a mental asylum. When this started happening I was shocked. Nice play Neiderman. She escapes and forgives her stepmother and Giselle starts to be nice. Still am secretly addictive to this soap opera trash. Ruby is a sweet but needs to stand up for herself more.
