I read this book years ago and just re-read it this morning, I dunno why. I was avoiding the laundry, maybe? It wasn't much of an escape, however, since the main character is mentioned to be doing her laundry no less than 470 scintillating, page-turning times. This book has a very important lesson to impart and that is: you'd better keep your rich, Australian boyfriend around in case you some day become a financially-destitute and helpless widow. With the inclusion of the always-convenient neighbor character, who is thoroughly utilized but never feels used, this book would be much more accurately classified fantasy. I mean even more than most romance novels, okay? This was a very silly book purchase - I must get this book out of the house before my daughters are old enough to want to read it.
