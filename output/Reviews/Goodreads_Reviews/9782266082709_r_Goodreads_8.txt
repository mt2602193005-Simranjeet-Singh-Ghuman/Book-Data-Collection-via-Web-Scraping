The brilliant idea of Stendhal, in this novel inspired by an act, is to use love relationships as a paroxysmal place where personal motivations and the constraints linked to belonging to a class will express themselves. Of course, the evolution of feelings brings to our wake most of our human passions (vanity, an instinct for domination, ambition, fear, self-forgetfulness). Moreover, it allows the author to reflect on a particular social context (in this case, the restoration).
