Book title: Montana born and bred Author: Alexis Harrington Rating:10/10 Comment: I got bored in the first chapters but I was motivated to keep reading even though I was bored in the first chapter. However, after the fourth chapter the book was so amazing that I couldn't put the book down. I really like how Zach turned out in the end, but I wish I had known more about Ethan Pembroke's background. I need to figure out why he was so cold-blooded and as for Pricilla, it makes sense that she was mentally unstable.
