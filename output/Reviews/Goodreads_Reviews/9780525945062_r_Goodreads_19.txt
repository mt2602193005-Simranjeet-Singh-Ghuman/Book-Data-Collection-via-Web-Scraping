Although not literary fiction, it was an enjoyable read when I read it back in the 90s. This was when Guyanese people didn't have a voice in the book world. For a long time it was only Mittleholzer. And then speedy reference to VS Naipaul and Sam Selvon et al. It was wonderful to read a female Guyanese author. I think that is what is lacking, readers are so used to reading established histories...American, European, Indian...that when a South American/Caribbean voice comes along no one knows the history or seem keen to exploring it. Yes,it could have been so much more, but brava to her!
