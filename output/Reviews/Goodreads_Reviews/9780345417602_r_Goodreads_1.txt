This book is basically 450 questions (ex: Have you ever been on the radio?, Have you ever gotten a hole-in-one?, Have you ever experienced a recurring dream?) It's fun if you make it into a game with a group of three or four people that you already know. However, I bought it because I thought it would have interesting conversation starters and/or conversation starting advice to get to know people in college. It's pretty useless for that, the questions are a little too random for people you've never met.
