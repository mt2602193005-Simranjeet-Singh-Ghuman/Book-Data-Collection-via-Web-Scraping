Old school sci-fi/fantasy story about a quest to kickstart civilization after an apocalyptic fall. It's a decent short novel, told in Simak's somewhat talky style. It's reminiscent of A Canticle for Leibowitz, with humanity's rejection of science and technology and with a few remaining outposts of learning, awaiting the renaissance. There's also a touch of Lord of the Rings, as a band of heros quests for salvation from darkness. A quick, untaxing read.
