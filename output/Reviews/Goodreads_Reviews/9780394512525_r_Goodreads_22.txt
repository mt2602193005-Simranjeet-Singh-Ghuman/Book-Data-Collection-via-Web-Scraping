My father wanted me to read this book as he had read it as a child and remembers it as being his first taste of sci-fi. It’s not a bad book by any means, but unfortunately it wasn’t really my taste and found it a slog to get through. I found the language a bit tedious to read and it took a while to hook me, if it ever did. If it hadn’t been a short book to keep me occupied on my breaks at work then I doubt I would’ve finished it.
