Lots of yummy sounding and fun Star Wars themed recipes in this cookbook. The book was broken down into the following categories: Breakfasts, Beverages, Snacks and Sides, Main Courses and last but not least, Desserts, and included everything from Mos Eisley Morsels and Jawa Jive Milkshakes to Obi-Wan Kebabs, and the recipe on the front cover, Wookiee Cookies! Definitely plan to try out a few of the recipes from this cookbook!!
