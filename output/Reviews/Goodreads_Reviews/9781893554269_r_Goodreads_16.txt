This book was written by two Classics academics who basically are predicting the doom of Classics education, at least in this generation, and it is all the fault of academia and Classicists themselves. Some really interesting parts, but also a lot of redundancy. You kind of have to get through those parts to find the good stuff. I expected to learn more about The Iliad, which I teach, but I got no great insights on that score. The book was pretty good, only if you have an interest in education or the Classics. Pretty damning on the college model.
