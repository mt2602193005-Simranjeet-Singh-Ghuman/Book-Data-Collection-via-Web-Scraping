4.5🌟 A highly entertaining, vintage "soap opera" by Elizabeth von Arnim! After some frustration and disappointment reading both The Caravaners and The Benefactress over the past month or two, I was very happy to dive into this light read (that has a hint of The Enchanted April.) I struggled with the first chapter of this book. EVA introduces around 15 characters at the beginning of the book and I really had to push through to understand who was who (and even what was going on!) It's strange because, after this chapter, you only really need to know a few characters. I can only guess that she used these characters to describe the other guests at Daisy's (the main character) dinner party. I won't spoil the plot but, once it gets going, it's really hard to stop reading. I love the tension (which I don't usually enjoy), the mostly unlikable characters (it makes it easier because almost no one is entirely lovable) and the witty conversations. It truly reminded me of a daytime soap opera, but in the best way. Besides the feeling that I wanted to shake some of these characters into sanity, I enjoyed pretty much the entire book (except for that first chapter and possibly some of the last chapter). I plan on reading this Elizabeth von Arnim novel again soon because I think it's one of those books that gets better the more you read it. So many thanks to Caro (@carosbookcase) for buddy reading this book with me! I love chatting all things Elizabeth von Arnim with her. There's always so much to say and discuss! The perfect summer read, but also a good choice for winter when you want a taste of extra warm days, cool drinks and drama! Highly recommended!
