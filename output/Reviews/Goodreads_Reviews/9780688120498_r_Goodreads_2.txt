Crazy is as crazy does. Alice plays with her kittens at home when is suddenly intrigued by the reflection of the wall-hung mirror beside her. After carefully examining it she discovers that she can step through it into a new world. This is the story of her fantastic adventures, and her second journey into Wonderland. Well, this was even crazier than the first one! A book that can be read as a standalone, since most of the characters and events from the first book are barely referenced or mentioned. In this crazy sequel we get to meet more eccentric characters; like the Red and White Queen, the twins Tweedledum and Tweedledee, the fragile Humpty Dumpty and the Red and White Knights, among others. Wonderland still an insane place and possibly the all-time biggest expression of the nonsense literature. Sadly this time couldn’t enjoy it as much as the first, I definitely need more sense in my readings. The poems, rhymes and puzzles certainly didn’t help either, wanted to drop it from the first chapter on. But, as always, my stubbornness won and just stuck with it till the end. Yippee.
