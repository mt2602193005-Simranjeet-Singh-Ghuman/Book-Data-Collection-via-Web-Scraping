Pretend You Don't See Her was an enjoyable suspense book. Was it the best mystery/suspense novel I've read? No. But it did keep my interest and I wanted to finish it. Lacey was a good main character, but she did some very foolish things...which is probably the point, since most people would most likely behave the same way in her shoes. The ending was more sudden than I expected, but it did wrap up nicely. Not my favorite of this author's books, but it was definitely a good read. 3.5 stars
