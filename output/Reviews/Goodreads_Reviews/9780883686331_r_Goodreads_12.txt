This little paperback is a collection of sermons delivered by the late Pentecostal preacher, Smith Wigglesworth. They were transcribed by those present and then collected into this volume. Not only do you get to hear what this man of faith shared with people all over the world, but you can read stories of people who were healed, delivered and saved after being touched by his ministry. Each message is fairly short and delivered in simple language. Wigglesworth humbly, but unashamedly confronts sin and challenges people to take God at His word. He shares examples of people he ministered to and how they responded to the presence and power of God. Wigglesworth was a simple tradesman who loved and served Jesus. But when he was filled with the Holy Spirit, he became a powerful preacher of the gospel who traveled the world over to bring people to Christ. If you are curious about the Pentecostal movement from the last century, this is a good book to introduce you to one of the pioneers of that period. If you read with an open mind and heart, you're sure to find yourself inspired to believe God for more than what you previously imagined--both for yourself and others!
