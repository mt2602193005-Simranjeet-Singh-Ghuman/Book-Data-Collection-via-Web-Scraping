This story is about two little kids, Charlie and Katie who help out their friend Dr. Green with putting away certain items around the museum. Dr. Green had asked the children to help her put items back, but they had to do it all on their own with only the help of clues Dr. Green had left them on little pieces of paper. This story would be great for students who are about to go on a field trip to a museum because it gives a preview to what is at museums. Also, this story was easy because there were many decodable words that students could be learning. The pictures were also a great aid to help understand more about what was going on in the story.
