Amusing, frustrating, and above all, an odd tale of a bucolic country existence being turned upside down for an event-packed 24 hours--and, perhaps the presaging of a midlife crisis (vis a vis Earl's changing attitude). Berger's prose is excellent and he certainly spins an interesting story. Naturally, my impression of the characters was influenced by the largely forgetten film adaptation with John Belushi (Keese) and Dan Aykroyd (Harry).
