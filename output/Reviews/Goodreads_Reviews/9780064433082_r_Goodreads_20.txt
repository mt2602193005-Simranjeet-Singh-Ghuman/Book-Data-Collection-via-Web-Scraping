This is a Reading Rainbow book told from a little boy point of view. Instead of his Mama telling him he tells his Mama about his adoption process. Of how he held onto his pictures of his new mama and Daddy and house and dog. His new room and bed etc. How he was scared to ride on a plane; how he was scared to met his new parents. And all the new things around him. But how he eventually did find comfort and happiness in the smiles and faces he had grown used to seeing.
