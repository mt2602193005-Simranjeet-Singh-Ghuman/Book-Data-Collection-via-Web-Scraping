My 2016 review I don't have much more to add. Adam will never be a favourite Heyer hero for me - too class conscious and often, too ungrateful. But he grows on me as does the maturing of his and Jenny's relationship. But the character I really loathe is Adam's late father. Such is GH's gift with characterisation that the late Viscount never has to appear on screen for us to get the picture of a thoroughly selfish man who doesn't care about the destruction of other people's lives and dreams.
