Every time I read this, I enjoy it more. Ira Levin did a wonderful job with his sensitive portrayal of a female MC in Rosemary Woodhouse. This is a rarity among horror writers of previous decades, and many male authors today could learn a thing or two from Mr. Levin's book about how to write the inner dialogue of a woman. And of course, listening to the audiobook as narrated by Mia Farrow is a treat.
