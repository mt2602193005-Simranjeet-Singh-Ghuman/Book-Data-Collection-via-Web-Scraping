Incredibly helpful in dissecting what exactly Roman Catholics and Protestants do not have in common— and boy, are the differences both significant and consequential. I would’ve preferred a bit more content on addressing things like Rome’s claims to apostolic succession and whether or not there was true unity/ uniformity amongst the Church Fathers on issues like the Eucharist, but those are just some nit-picky things. Overall, really enjoyed this book. Praise God for His love and finished work on the cross, once and for all.
