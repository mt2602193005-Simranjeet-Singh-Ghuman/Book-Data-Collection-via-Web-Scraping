I absolutely LOVE Diane Ackerman's writing and have read several of her books. However, I just could NOT get into this one. Maybe its me... Maybe its the book? Whichever it is, I couldn't finish it. I kept picking it up and reading a few paragraphs, then skipping pages and trying again. Anyway, I've donated my copy to the local library. I hope someone sees it and loves it. Its just not for me. I'll stick with A Natural History of the Senses and Cultivating Delight.
