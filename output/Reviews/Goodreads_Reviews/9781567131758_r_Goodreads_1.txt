I really liked the ideas in this book. The challenge will be to implement them consistently. Here are some important rules of parenting that Latham suggests to keep in mind: 1. Seize opportunities to have frequent positive interactions with your child. 2. Clearly establish and communicate your expectations. 3. Clearly establish the consequences for compliance and noncompliance. 4. Ignore behaviors which do not threaten the basic quality of life, limb, and property (which include most unacceptable behaviors). 5. Attend to inappropriate behavior in an unemotional, precise, and directive way. 6. Do not question a noncompliant child about his behavior, or ask him to explain his inappropriate behavior. 7. Use the inappropriate behavior of one child as a cue to you to attend to the appropriate behavior of other children. 8. Smile and laugh, talk and touch-- a lot. 9. Assess behavior analytically and treat it clinically.
