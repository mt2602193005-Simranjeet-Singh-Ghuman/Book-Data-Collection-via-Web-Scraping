Western historical romances are just not my thing. I keep on trying them, and I keep on finding them so boring. I really felt like not a whole lot was happening in this book and ended up DNFing around 60%. The hero was fine. The heroine was fine. They just kind of hung around the ranch. There was stampede. The heroine was meddling with feuding families, so the town hates her. I wasn't excited to continue, so I put it down!
