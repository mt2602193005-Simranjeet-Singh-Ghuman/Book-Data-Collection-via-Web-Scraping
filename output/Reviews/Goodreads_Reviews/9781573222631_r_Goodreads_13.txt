This was a quick read. I liked the main character. His life was coming apart and his problems were weighing heavily on him. I could empathize with most of them and I liked him as a main character. I liked the other characters as well. I found myself wanting more description of them. Some were a little vague. My main problem was the format. It was a narrative one and I'm not a fan of that style. This could have ended up as 2 stars, but I think the fact that the MC was well liked helped. It also had a sweet ending.
