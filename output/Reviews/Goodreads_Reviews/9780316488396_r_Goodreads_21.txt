I loved this book when I was a teenager. One sunmer my friends and I must have watched the craft to many times cause we were hypnotized by this movie. We found this book and would sit all day picking out the right spells and thinking of just how we would go about getting all the objects to make the spell work. As the summer ended I think we never really completed a spell. Just mostly sitting with lit candles, meditating, and trying to connect with the dead. Much fun and good memories but hey, does witch craft really work? The book is delightfully cute It's small easy to carry around in your bag of items to do the spells. The chapters were very charming. Such as love, health, wealth, happiness, and even pets. Quick read, but you have to read it over and over just to make sure your doing the spells just right. Best part the woven binding has stitched in ribbon bookmarks to make the book more loveable. Helping you mark your favorite spells for easy referance. I do love this book. Maybe one day I will flip back through it and see if maybe I can make one of these spells work for good.
