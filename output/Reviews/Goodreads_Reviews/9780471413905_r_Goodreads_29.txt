Cordain, the author of this book makes a compelling case that a higher protein diet is the optimal way to maintain good health and the optimum weight. This he argues is based upon our paleohuman history which for 2.5 million years designed us to be omnivores. Our primary activity was to be hunter gatherers subsisting on hunted game and gathered fruits and vegetables.It was not until 10,000 years ago (a blink of an eye as he calls it) that agriculture and domestication entered the picture and we began to raise cattle and grow wheat (grass) that our more serious diseases started to appear. Cordain argues that we were not designed to eat "grass" and our natural diet includes game. He disputes the charge that too much protein leads to constipation as he argues it is difficult to eat too much because the protein quickly satiates our hunger. Unlike Volek and Phinnon ("The Art and Science of of Low Carbohydrate") he argues against including dairy and overly fatty meats (bacon and suasages...they argue to include it in your diet) in the diet somewhat partially agreeing indirectly with Campbell's (the China Report) exclusion of dairy but not with his assertion that meat is the cause of cancer. In fact Cordain disputes this finding arguing the exact opposite. Again Cordain makes the case that the ultra processed foods are the main cause of our obesity and modern disease and eating the Paleo diet of lean meat and fish and low glycemic vegetables and fruits with a low glycemic load go a long way to restoring our health and achieving optimum weight. This is a very readable book that I finished in a day and that I highly recommend. By following and somewhat merging the advice in this book and that of Volek and Phinnon I suggest you can reach optimal weight fairly easily and permanently keep it off while improving your health and restoring your blood pressure to an optimal level.
