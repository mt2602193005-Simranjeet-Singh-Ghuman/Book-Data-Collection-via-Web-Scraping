Another of my three 'fat' cookbooks. I have two other Silver Palate books--the recipes are great. This book is beautifully designed, and perhaps because the authors are caterers, it has some unusual sections. There is section at the front called 'Amusements' that contains really interesting recipes for finger food. Another section is called Microwave Miracles, although the authors also have some reservations about microwave ovens. As you would expect for a book on basics, there is a page or two of information on handling and preparation of the standard ingredients, and helpful tables for ingredient types like citrus fruit, beans, and salad greens, where there are a lot of choices.
