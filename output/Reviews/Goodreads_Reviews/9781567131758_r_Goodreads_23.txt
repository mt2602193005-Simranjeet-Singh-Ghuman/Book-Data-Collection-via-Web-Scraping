I found this through listening to Ralphie from @simplyonpurpose. We bought her audio course at the beginning of quarantine and it's really changed how I (am trying to) parent. Glenn Latham is someone who she quotes often so I thought I'd go to the source. I really loved this 'parenting manual'. It feels like a textbook with the way it's set up with lots of 'assignments' or things to try. The basic overarching theme is that kids respond significantly better to positive consequences, environment and reinforcement. Instead of having a coercive home where kids are punished for misbehaving, having a positive way of helping them WANT to choose to behave. Really highly recommend.
