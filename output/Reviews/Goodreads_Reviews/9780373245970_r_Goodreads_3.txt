Isn't It Rich? by Sherryl Woods was a cute book to listen to. Set in northern Virginia, it brought back memories of when my husband was stationed at Quantico, VA and we would hang out up in the area. Melanie Hart is a PR executive who meets Richard Carlton, a very successful business exec, who is considering running for public office. Since this is chick-lit/romance, you can figure out the rest of the story. What I really enjoyed is that the focus was on the relationship of two people. Also, the narrator did a nice job keeping all the characters separate. I look forward to listening to the next book in this series. Go Cards! L1C4!!
