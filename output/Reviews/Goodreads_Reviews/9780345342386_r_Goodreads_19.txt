4.1/5: really solid stories with clever/imaginative premises. niven has some pretty interesting ideas about society and technology as usual and backs it up with some strong narrative. there were some moments when the writing was a bit less sharp and i couldn’t help but feel a bit lost, but it wouldn’t take long to recover and feel mostly up to speed. the protagonist is pretty hyper-masculine, which felt a little excessive, although not really in any toxic ways to be fair.
