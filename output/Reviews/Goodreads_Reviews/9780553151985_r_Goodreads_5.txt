A charming little story about Blanche, the Blue-Nosed Witch and her little black cat, Brockett. The illustrations are pure Halloween fun and they would look fantastic in black or orange frames hung up around the house during Halloween. The story is simple and interesting, though the part with the grumpy old man threatening them with a sling shot was not my favorite. Overall, the black and white illustrations themselves make the story appealing and I enjoyed reading it. This vintage book is a great choice for the spooky days in late October, especially right before trick-or-treating.
