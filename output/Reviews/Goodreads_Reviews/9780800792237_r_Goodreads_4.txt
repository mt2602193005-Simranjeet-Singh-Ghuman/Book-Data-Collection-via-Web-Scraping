I rated this book five stars when I first finished it a year ago. But since I’ve grown with more, Holy Spirit-lead teaching, I’m not so sure many of her “teachings” in this book are biblical. Some may even be dangerous (e.g. praying against territorial spirits). We don’t read anyone doing that, teaching that, or anything close to that in the Bible. Just because they are mentioned (like in Daniel), doesn’t mean we are meant to engage with them - it actually illustrates the opposite since it was not Daniel, or any human, who fights back with the entity. But, this is just one of many unbiblical teachings in this book. Some of her experiences are inspiring, and some points of her teaching are insightful (like her analysis with Joseph in the first part of the book), but I cannot endorse the book any longer given such flagrant and dangerous teaching in later chapters. I actually threw my copy of the book away :(.
