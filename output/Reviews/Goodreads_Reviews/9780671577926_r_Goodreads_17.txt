3.5/5 The second installment in the War God series continues the adventures of Bahzell Bahnakson as he travels the land and adds more people to the Order of Tomanak. What I liked the most about this book is that it added two new characters, a badass woman warrior and a snarky apprentice who grows up throughout the novel. I think my favorite thing about this series so far is the characters as it feels as if each one is fully fleshed out and complex. I can't wait to see what new characters get added to the ensemble in the next book.
