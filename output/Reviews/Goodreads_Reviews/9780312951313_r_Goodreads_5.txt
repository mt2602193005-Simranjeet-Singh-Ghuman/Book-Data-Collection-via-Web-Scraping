This has been a weeklong nibble and such a protracted affair may have colored my reactions. The Trial For Murder and The Haunted House are exemplary tales, different but creepy. The Signal-Man is perhaps even better. I did not reread A Christmas Carol in this edition but will undoubtedly return to such repeatedly. Dickens provides a range of perspectives and tones and more than a few devices which puts him above most practitioners of the ghost/horror story. While there’s a sense of cosmic justice being a motivation, there’s little detail afforded to social conditions. I find that rather interesting.
