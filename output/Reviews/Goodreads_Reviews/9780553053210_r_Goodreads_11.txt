The casual misogyny of the author made this book difficult to get through. It’s expressed in obvious and direct ways as well as more subtle ways like who is included and left out of the story. Written in 1988, the writing style feels incredibly dated. So much unnecessary flourish. So much showiness and “notice me, the author!” in his writing. It’s still worth reading for the pictures and some of the information about ABT. But the overall feel the whole time is like listening to someone’s wealthy, problematic uncle trying to impress you with his vocabulary and stories about famous people he met that one time.
