Well, now that I have a messenger bag instead of a purse, I'm afraid the phenomenon of "purse books" are behind me (little books I snatched off the free rack at the library, usually crappy romances). And after reading this one, I can't say I'm sorry to see this era in my life end. I picked it up because I have a soft spot for stories about high school / childhood sweethearts who meet up again later in life and "refall" in love. But this was one of those books that romanticized victimization, low self-esteem, martyrdom, and abuse. And that just don't impress me much. There wasn't even any good sex to redeem it.
