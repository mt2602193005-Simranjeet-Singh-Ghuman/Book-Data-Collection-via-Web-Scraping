3.75⭐️ I feel like my review changed from 4 stars to 3 and then to back 4 while finishing the book. One thing to keep in mind is that this book was written 25 years ago, so I feel that parenting tactics, parenting “culture” (eg. spanking) and our understanding/ knowledge of child development has increased and changed. I feel like things are not quite as “black and white” as the book may portray, in terms of root causes of boundary issues and kids’ behaviour. That being said, there are SO many good points in this book in regards to teaching and demonstrating healthy boundaries. Main points include being consistent with the boundaries and consequences you put in place, “let your yes be yes and your no be no”, and emphasizing that kids can experience “hurts” and disappointments, as this helps with the overall big picture of boundaries and healthy development. Lots of great notes taken from the book. It would be great to see an updated version from the authors in the future!
