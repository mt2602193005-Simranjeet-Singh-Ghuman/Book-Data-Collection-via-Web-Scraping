I've read about a dozen Asey Mayo stories and this one (The Annulet of Gilt) has some unique and off-beat aspects. It's got kind of a messy mix of equal parts mystery and action-adventure storyline. There are several car chases. a few rounds of gun-play, stranglings, foreign intrigue, and a sidebar with a wandering elephant. At the core, there's the usual cast of wealthy visitors and colorful Cape Cod locals to serve as suspects and interested bystanders. One nifty part of the foreign intrigue plot line is that we learn more about Asey's espionage career during WW1 and other times. It's kind of over-cooked, in the too-much-going-on in the plot, but having written that, I really enjoyed the story and recommend it. I'm reading the series more-or-less in chronological order, and there are a couple of new characters in this story that would not surprise me at all for them to reappear in later Asey Mayo stories. Either that or the author was test-driving those characters to gauge reader interest for other series.
