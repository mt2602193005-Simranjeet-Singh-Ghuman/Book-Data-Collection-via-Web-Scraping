3-3.5 stars An animated, spontaneous, and generally light-hearted view of family life from the perspective of 10-year-old Kenny up until the family's eye-opening road trip to Birmingham, Alabama where there is ultimately a shift in focus to a more somber event in U.S. history. Although the plot summary notes the 1963 time period as one of the darkest times, race is not directly touched upon until after the halfway point where it is only addressed intermittently until the aforementioned event at the end of the story and in the epilogue. I imagine that was the author's intention so the book would be initially engaging and not overly heavy-handed in order to draw in its intended audience of children/young adults.
