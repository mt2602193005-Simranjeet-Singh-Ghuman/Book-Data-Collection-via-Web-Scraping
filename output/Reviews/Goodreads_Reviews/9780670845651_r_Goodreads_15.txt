The second half of this book is worth getting through the first half, which can make you wonder when the story is going to get more interesting. These are slow to get there, but I do enjoy where they go by the end. This one a little more shocking than the previous ones. I started reading this series after watching the 2-season TV show, and I'm struggling a lot with the difference in the characters. I don't like the book characters as much (I wonder if that's usually the case?). I don't care for the portrayal of Sid AT ALL, including the audiobook narration of his character. I don't like the status of the relationship between Karl and Cassandra after YEARS have passed. But I feel committed to stick with this through the last book, #9.
