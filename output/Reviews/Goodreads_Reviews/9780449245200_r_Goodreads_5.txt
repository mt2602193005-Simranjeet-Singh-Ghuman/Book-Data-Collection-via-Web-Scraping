This was excellent, and it was incredibly edgy for a book published in 1982....and incredibly edgy by today's standards. Rusty is a super interesting character, one that would definitely be more about wish fulfillment for teen readers than realistic. But she's very open and honest about sex and sexuality, which was refreshing and also made me realize how rare it is to see that in YA today. The middle sagged a bit for me, but the ups and downs of family relationships, of romance, and of the way fame can impact all of those things was really well-done and kept me going.
