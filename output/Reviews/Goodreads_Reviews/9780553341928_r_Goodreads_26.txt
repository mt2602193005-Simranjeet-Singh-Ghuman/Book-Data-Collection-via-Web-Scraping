About a man who fishes and philosophizes, and finds a girlfriend and God. Was very much into all the descriptions of nature and metaphors of spirituality to water. I wasn’t into the love story, although a major point of the book is you can’t understand being in love unless you’re in love. A good counter argument though is not being in love hasn’t stopped me from liking any other romance book, so maybe this love story really is just lackluster. I wish being in a love wasn’t a necessary step for the main character to find God, I think it would have been much more powerful for him to understand God on his own. I also wanted to see Gus struggle through trials after his spiritual revelation to see how his beliefs changed in light of tragedy. I feel like The Brothers K accomplished exactly what I felt was missing from this book, and delivered the same themes but much stronger. Overall though it was an interesting read despite my complete lack of interest in fishing. I was also very much surprised by how funny it was! This is basically a comedic, philosophical fishing story which I absolutely wouldn’t have ever read if it was by any other author. I truly hope David James Duncan puts out another novel!
