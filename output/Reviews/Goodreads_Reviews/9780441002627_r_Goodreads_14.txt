I've been a fan of Patricia Anthony for years (probably about 20). I came across this one in my stacks and had to re-read it while waiting for new novels to come into the library. It's still a great sci-fi novel. A little hard to follow the main character's line of thinking at times, and I had to re-read the last few pages to fully understand what happened, but a very satisfactory novel.
