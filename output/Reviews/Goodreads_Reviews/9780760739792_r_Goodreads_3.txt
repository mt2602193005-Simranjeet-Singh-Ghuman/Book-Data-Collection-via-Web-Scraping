A fairly wide variety of weird stuff from across the Garden State. It used to be my job to go over there every few weeks and visit our clients, so I know the state very well. I rate it very highly - there are a lot of wonderful things about it. I used to fly over on a Saturday, leaving all of Sunday to go and explore. From the legendary Jersey Devil, to ghosts, to lights in church towers, and badly developed photos, it's all in there. Very comprehensive. Shame I only found it late in that period of my life.
