"If no demand is put on you, then you are in a sense excluded." "From what?" "Life itself. To be demanded of gives us dignity." A book about how we dignify one another by depending and by being depended upon. Perhaps the strongest theme of the Austin series is that no man is an island, and in this story, it is drawn out through searches for healing, belonging, and beauty, set among real churches and imaginary fraught communities. It has haunted me since high school; I hope it always does!
