The Peach Man is another InuYasha story I love. InuYasha is weaken by his Human state, and has to work fast after the Peach Man shrunk and ate him. I love how horrifying this story is, because the Peach Man is no demon, but a man with too much knowledge and a greedy personality. Sango, the demon exterminator, is introduced as well as caught in another one of Naraku's many tangled webs. I love Sango, and her village!
