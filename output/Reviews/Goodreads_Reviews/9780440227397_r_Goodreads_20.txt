A powerful story told from a young boy’s perspective on slavery. After being kidnapped and forced to work aboard a slave ship, young Jessie witnesses the harsh realities of the illegal slave trade between Africa and the Americas. Forced to play his flute to keep the enslaved people moving and their muscles from weakening, he experiences firsthand the greed and brutality of the slavers. This was a difficult but important read—I wouldn’t recommend it for children under twelve. The ending was fantastic!
