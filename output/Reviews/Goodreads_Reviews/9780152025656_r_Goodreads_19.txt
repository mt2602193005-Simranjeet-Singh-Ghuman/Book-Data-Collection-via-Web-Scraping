Such a fun and sweet family story! This is a perfect middle grade book for summer reading. Set on Fire Island, we follow the Pye family as they head off with their father who is an ornithologist and is tasked with studying the birds on the island. Upon arrival, they are quickly gifted a cat - the Pinky of the title - and the adventures continue from there. If you want a nostalgic summertime read (especially if you are a cat lover), this is it.
