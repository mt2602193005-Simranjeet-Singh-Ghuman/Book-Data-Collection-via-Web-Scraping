Tomie dePaola's Book of Poems contains so many different themes and concepts. There are poems on animals, holidays, etc. I even saw a multicultural poem. It was written in Spanish and translated in English. That is a great way to expose children to diversity and so forth. I skimmed around and saw a lot of illustrations each corresponding to a particular poem. The particular poem I read, The Witch, contained a lot of rhyming and alliteration. It introduced these literary concepts without taking away the essence of the poem.
