4.2/5 This is a really tough book to read because of the true events that occurred. I found it to be traumatizing and haunting, something I think about whenever I consider just how much power a nurse has over their patient, especially children. I read some other reviews that the legal perspective was boring, but I actually found it very interesting. I would also remind you to prepare to be made angry by this book. The nerve of someone to commit these atrocities is mind-boggling. Overall, very well-written but really quite disturbing.
