This book explains things from the perspective of someone that walked through dyslexia and figured out how to help it. My daughter needs help with her dyslexia, and this book has given the best explanation I have found so far of what might be going on in her mind as she tries to learn to read. A few details do not resonate but so much of it does. I looked up people in my area that teach with the Davis method, and I will look into it.
