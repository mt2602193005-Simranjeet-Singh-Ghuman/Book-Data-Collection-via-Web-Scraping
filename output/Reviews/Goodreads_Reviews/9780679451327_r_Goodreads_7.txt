Nora Gallagher writes a lovely book that rambles through a liturgical year. Highlights of it surround caring for friends that are dying, working in the church soup kitchen which is populated with unusual characters, and seeing a new pastor into office, which seemed like it would be controversial because of his sexuality, but wasn't, because the church loves him. My personal favorite part was the inclusion of this prayer: May you walk with God In the sharp pain of growing In the midst of confusion In the bright light of knowing May you live in God In God's constant compassion In God's infinite wisdom In God's passion for peace May you walk with God And live in God And remain with God This night, and forever. Amen.
