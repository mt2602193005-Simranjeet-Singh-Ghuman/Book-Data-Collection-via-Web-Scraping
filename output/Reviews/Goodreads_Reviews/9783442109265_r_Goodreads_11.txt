It is a book that is set out to change the way we think. It is supposed to make us more creative in our thinking and actions. It is a good attempt but all the chapters can be summarized in one line: You are good at everything, all you need is to first learn, copy the masters and practice. This is the synopsis of every chapter more or less. The only good thing that can be inculcated in our daily lives is to make mind maps. It does help to sort out thoughts, creates connections and create new ideas. Other than that the book has a lot of exercises that are too much to actually attempt. It is unrealistic to expect one to join a singing group, drawing group, dance group, poetry group, etc. at the same time. I would not recommend this book for sure.
