This was a VERY good story & a great way to end the series. I really liked the finishing touches this novella put on this series about Rome, Sarah & their children, & Max, Claire & their 3 children, plus Derek's mom Marcie, now happily married. Finally about Derek & how well his life turned out & now he to has found his happily ever after. The passion was described a bit on the lighter side, but still a great read. I like that the author didn't repeat the story line as some authors do. I've enjoyed this series & I would recommend it for a good read.
