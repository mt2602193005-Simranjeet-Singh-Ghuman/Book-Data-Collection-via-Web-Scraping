Complicated feelings about this one. By today’s standards it’s grossly misogynistic, but that may be the cultural standards of the time, when a woman could be “ruined” that the author is responding to and not advocating for. I’m not sure I want to write off entire periods of literature just on principle. And for all that Janey was a victim of her time, she had a surprising amount of agency, pride and skills. She manages to turn the tables on those who sought to teach her a lesson, while still learning a lesson and showing a bit of growth. There’s some beautiful description of the Arizona desert, and I was intrigued by what the heck Janey thought she was doing, so it wasn’t all bad. The Western isn’t really a genre I have explored before, so I’m not really sure what to judge it against. I’m not sure that based on this I’ll be venturing back into the genre any time soon.
