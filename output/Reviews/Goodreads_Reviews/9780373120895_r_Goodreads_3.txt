Not my favourite Sophie Weston, but not terrible. Heroine is a bond trader who dropped out of school at 16. She has an active social life, and can hold her own in conflicts with a variety of people, which is in keeping with her job. She's treated with suspicion by the hero (naturally - this is a Presents, after all), but eventually proves her qualification to be an HP heroine, because she is martyring herself by giving up all her hard-earned money to support her mother and sister, who needs expensive treatments . Pros: Angsty, embarrassments of being "trashy" in an upper-crust world are acknowledged Cons: Hero's about-face was not totally convincing, his career as a scientist-explorer seemed like wallpaper - there was nothing about him as a character that really seemed connected to that, heroine's late-in-the-story freak out seems more designed to stretch out the conflict rather than something organic to the character
