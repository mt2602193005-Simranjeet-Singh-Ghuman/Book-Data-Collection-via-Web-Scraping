30 years later, this is still a classic. It is the only travel guide that tells you which warlords take American Express. Pelton went to all the places your mother warned you about, then wrote a guidebook so you could go too. It reads like Lonely Planet had a midlife crisis: here's where to eat in Mogadishu, here's how not to get kidnapped in Colombia, here's which checkpoints prefer cash. The man is either the bravest journalist alive or completely insane, and the book never quite settles the question. It's funny and genuinely informative, assuming your idea of a vacation involves flak jackets....or you're a wannabe soldier of fortune.
