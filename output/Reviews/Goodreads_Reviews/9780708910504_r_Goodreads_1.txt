Ever since the publishers of Lucy Walker ebooks have started bringing out her later books as ebooks I have been a waiting customer. I loved a lot of her earlier ebooks when the female protagonist was not 17 years old and the male protagonist was not 30-35 and they marry. All the stories of older heroines were good ones. Pure love stories set mostly in outback Australia, a background I know little of. Her later books are not. This one tells of the whole Mongomery family leaving Australia to study or tour England and Ireland. Theodora is now married to Sam who is working on his PhD. Gerry has fallen in love with Reggie Arbothnot, a sailor officer on the ship bringing them to England but her love story is not told by Gerry, but by Theodora in the first person which doesn't lend much excitement because she doesn't really know much and is busy telling stories of her visits with the Vicar while acting as a teacher in a school for children. A really dull book. At least for me. I did not review the earlier ebooks (Shining River and Six for Heaven) because I just didn't like them. This is so far the best of them but worth only 2 stars.
