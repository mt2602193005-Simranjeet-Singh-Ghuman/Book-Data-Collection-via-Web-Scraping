“To hear, one must be silent.” I haven’t read much fantasy recently but have enjoyed many of Ursula K. Le Guin’s works, so I rolled the dice, and she did not disappoint with this tale of Ged, a true-born wizard, and his harrowing journey. The build was slow, but when dragons made their entrance around the halfway point, I couldn’t turn the pages fast enough —my hands were a blur. A Wizard of Earthsea is a magical romp across enchanted lands and blackened seas to find one wizard’s truth - and maybe his redemption...
