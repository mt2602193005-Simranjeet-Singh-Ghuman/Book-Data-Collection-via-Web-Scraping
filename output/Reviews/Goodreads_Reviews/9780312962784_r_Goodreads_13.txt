Not bad, though it didn't keep my attention to the extent that the 1st book in the series, The Line , did. The Gate didn't seem to come into focus quite so well, though the climactic ending is reasonably exciting. Overall, the writing is serviceable, with little bumps in the road where gets a subjunctive wrong or commits another small grammatical peccadillo. This second volume also has a heavier smattering of f-bombing--which for me is not a point in its favor.
