I enjoyed the narrative voice and it was nice to read a good book with a canonically nonbinary protagonist that was written in the 1970s. I felt their gender exploration was not made fun of and was actually treated somewhat sensitively. However, the book unfortunately does not manage to avoid the trope of trans feminine person as murderer and psychologically unbalanced, which was a shame and the reason I ultimately can't recommend this book.
