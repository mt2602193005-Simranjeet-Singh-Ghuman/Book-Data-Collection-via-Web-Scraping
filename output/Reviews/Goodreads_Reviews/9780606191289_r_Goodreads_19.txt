Wow, I almost kept a journal while I was reading this book just to keep track of my thoughts about it. I generally do not like to read "Oprah" books but I really found this book full of great characters that you wanted to know more about. It took me about 50-60 pages to really get into it and once I was I didn't want to put it down. I felt so awful for the majority of the characters and the issues they were dealing with (don't want to give anything away till Katie reads it!) I loved Valuable and her sadness towards her family. You just wanted to reach out and help the girl. I think it was very good at describing what was happening in the book, how people were feeling etc... When I finished it last night I wanted to call Brittany to talk about it! Eeeek! I was not expecting that ending and would like to go into more detail later about it. I didn't think it was fair to some of the characters.........
