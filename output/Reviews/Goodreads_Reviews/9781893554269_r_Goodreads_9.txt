Yes, Homeric is indeed dead, and as the authors pointed out in 1998 we are left in the ash heap of a society that has abandoned classical learning and wisdom (and I would add Christianity). The authors are right, the tradition won’t be wholly reclaimed, but there is hope for a life boat that can preserve these ideas and ideals at least in part. If one is interested in classical (Christian) renewal in education, there are better books out there than this one.
