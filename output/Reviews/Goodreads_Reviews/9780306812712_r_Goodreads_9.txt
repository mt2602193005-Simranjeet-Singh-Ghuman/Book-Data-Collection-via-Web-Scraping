Milk It! is a solid overview of the 1990's rock music scene, touching on a bit of everything. DeRogatis devotes sections to the major heavyweights (Nirvana, Pearl Jam, Smashing Pumpkins, etc.) while also delving into Britpop, RiotGrrl, the concert scene (Woodstock 94, Lollapalooza), and a host of other topics. He obviously misses a few things, but overall, you'll end up with a fair snapshot of what it was like to be there. Someday, someone will make sense of all the craziness that happened in that decade, and do the decade justice. Until that time comes, this is a pretty good place to start.
