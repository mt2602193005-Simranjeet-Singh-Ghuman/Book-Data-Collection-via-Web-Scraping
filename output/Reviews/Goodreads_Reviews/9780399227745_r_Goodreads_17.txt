Personal Reaction- I like this book a lot because the story is very cute and also presents something new to students. Some of the things in the book may be new to students. For example, some might not know what a firefly is. The story also keeps children guessing because it asks about different lights that could be seen outside at night and distinguishes them from a firefly. I loved this book as a kid and I think others will too! Purposes- I would read this aloud to kindergarten students for several purposes: -exposure to new insects and possibly some other vocabulary -enrichment/enjoyment due to the story and illustrations Curriculum: read aloud -possibly used for insect unit -could possibly be used as a more advanced beginning to read book Overall, I think that this book is very beneficial to students in many ways and I think that it is one that many young students should read.
