Ms Kantra scores again with a winner in this fourth and last book in the MacNeill series. The two main characters are well drawn and draw the reader into the conflicts each has in his and her lives. Characters from the first three novels in the series reappear so the reader is updated on the situations in their lives. For this reason, I strongly suggest reading the series in order. The story line in this novel is an extension of a situation in the earlier novels with the intro of a new character, "Mad Dog" Palmer. Ms Kantra is a fine writer and I will be looking for her other Carolina novels. Show more
