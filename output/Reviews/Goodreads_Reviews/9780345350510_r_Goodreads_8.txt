A rather early Niven. A great set up, micro world in N-space, top of a mega mountain is the only habitable place for crew and colonists to live (about the same size as the state of California.) There is the element of a dictatorship government. The class conflict and rebellion,I liked the whole organ bank angle used by the upper class (crew) as power to enforce control over the lower class (colonist.) The book reads like good old fashion pulp. There is the problem of the weird eye ability the protagonist has, giving him the ability of becoming not quite invisible but rather forgettable as way of outsmarting his antagonists as well (as keeping him a virgin.) This element was a poor attempt at trying to put in a "new-wave" element into the book? Hardly. Over all, it was a fine read, but Niven gets better real quick. "Protector" even though written soon after, was a master piece.
