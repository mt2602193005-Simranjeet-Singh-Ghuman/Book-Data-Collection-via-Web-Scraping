Corking book and led to a lively discussion at book club. We often view these ancient accounts through our modern lenses, but perhaps we have lost their meaning by doing so. Can we explain away the miracles of which Bede tells us? Are we able to understand the way they lived? As modern science has proved some of the accuracy of his claims, should we take all of his claims as true? As someone raised in England, it's interesting to think of how little I knew of my own history, and I thoroughly recommend.
