By the end of this book I was like ??? From the start I was enjoying this. I read Zane a lot growing up (knowing damn well I shouldn't have been lol) The plot went from predictable to a cop out, because there was so much build up only for molestation to be the cause of her personalities and actions...Which could've been better handled.. It just felt rushed. The father was also molested. And now her father has another daughter.. who he's alone with frequently........ It was all just like a car accident on the side of the road I couldn't look away. The start had potential.. the sex scenes were fun and raunchy which is fine but damn by the end Judes sex extravaganzas were just about as random an strange as it gets. Between the pizza boy, the mattress manager who she damn near forced into doing it and Ummm I'm Pretty sure my ass checked out after she went to a confederate racist club acting a damn fool and finding a white boy to still sleep with it was just... a no for me lord lol. The love interest was very insta lovey from the jump and just too damn sappy for me. It all was coming to a close of what exactly was happening as far as the story goes only for it to take a huge left turn and then be rushed to wrap up it was just .. under whelming and disappointing. I didn't have high expectations but damn lol the more I think about it the more I want to give it 2-2.5 stars but I'll leave it at 3 for the mild enjoyment of it I guess.
