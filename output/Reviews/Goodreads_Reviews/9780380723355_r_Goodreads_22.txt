This book was somewhat entertaining, but the idea of the beautiful, tall, blonde female detective who is at once vulnerable and totally tough when it comes to life and love is so ridiculous yet so cliché that I really couldn't take the book seriously at all. The hurried, romantic ending left me cold. I really couldn't relate to any of these characters. I found the solutions to the murders convoluted and hard to follow. There were just too many people involved to be believable. I don't know. I wish the author the best of luck. She seems to have many enthusiastic followers.
