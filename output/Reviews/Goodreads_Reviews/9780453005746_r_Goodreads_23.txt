Another 70's horror. Like many I've read this one is just fine, with a few elements that really stand out. I like the way this plays with the haunted house concept, going in directions you really don't expect. The cult stuff didn't come together the way I would have liked, though. It is funny to me how much the Back to the Land folk horror of the 70's permeates the entire horror landscape now without always understanding where it comes from.
