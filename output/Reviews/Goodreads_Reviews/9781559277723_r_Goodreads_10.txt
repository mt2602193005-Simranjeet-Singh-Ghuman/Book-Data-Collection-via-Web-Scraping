I guess there’s a reason I don’t read Christmas novels. I greatly enjoy Evanovich’s Stephanie Plum series, but this novella —which takes place between books eight and nine—about a hawt Christmas angel (or whatever he is) and a bail-jumping toy maker was far too silly and self-consciously whimsical for me to get very far. Stephanie, I’ll be back with Tasty Kakes when the holidays are over.
