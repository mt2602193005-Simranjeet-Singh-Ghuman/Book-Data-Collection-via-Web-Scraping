I read this for class, so I didn’t really have any preconceptions going into it, but I was pleasantly surprised. Obviously, this is a very heavy read. However, I feel like it was handled with tact and Glancy clearly did a lot of research for it. I do feel like the story was a bit repetitive at times, but frankly, walking from North Carolina to Oklahoma is probably a bit repetitive. I do appreciate that the story focused more on the characters and their experiences rather than the collective atrocities they faced. I feel like a lot of Native American literature, especially those written about, but no by Indigenous people, focus more on the “whole” rather than the individual. This felt a lot more human in that way.
