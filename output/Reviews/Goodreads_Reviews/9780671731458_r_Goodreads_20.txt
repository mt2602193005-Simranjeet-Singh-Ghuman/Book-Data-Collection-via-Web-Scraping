This was, I think, in the vanguard of the 1970's tsunami of self reflection and self help books. With it you could interpret your mood through the order of your selections from a set of eight single-color cards. Not that anyone in my circle felt the need to interpret mood through that book and cards but we were entertained by our evaluation of ourselves and one another, sometimes debating what was written there. This is a much simplified version of Luscher's full test but I never learned if there was any more substance to the full test than there was to this condensed version. Since I've read no mention of it for decades I'll guess that there wasn't. Worth perusing if you're of a mind to grok where our minds were in the 70s.
