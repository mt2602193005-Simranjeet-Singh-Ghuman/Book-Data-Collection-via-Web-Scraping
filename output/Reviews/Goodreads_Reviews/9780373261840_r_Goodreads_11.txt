So British that the author has included a list of expressions in the back for reference. Written in 1993 I believe this series was updated in 20111. This is book one of 18. I expected more humor form all of the hype about this series. Newly promoted to Det. Inspector, Irish Catholic Joseph Rafferty's new partner is Dafyd Llewellyn is a Welshman and son of a Protestant preacher. Their first case - solving the murder of a young woman found on the grounds of a high-end, posh psychiatric hospital. A steady investigation and a conclusion that came out of left field, though not unexpected. My TBR pile has books that interest me more than continuing with this series at least for now.
