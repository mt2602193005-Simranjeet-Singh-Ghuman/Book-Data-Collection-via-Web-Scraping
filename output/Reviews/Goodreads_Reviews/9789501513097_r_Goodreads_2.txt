My favourite Johanna Lindsey book, hands down! I’ve lost count of how many times I re-read this over the past 20-odd years. Nothing else of hers I read has quite measured up to this book. The humour is fantastic, and the chemistry between Angel and Cassie is sizzling! I think the best part of the book is the dialogue. They just crack me up. The secondary characters are great too especially Cassie’s parents. Believe it or not, some married people actually use their children as an intermediary instead of speaking to each other, and this goes on for years (I had an ex-colleague whose parents had this sort of marriage; I was super shocked when I heard about it, I thought this doesn’t happen IRL). I think this book is a great introduction to JL’s body of work.
