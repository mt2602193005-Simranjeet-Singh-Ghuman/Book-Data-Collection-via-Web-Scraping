Trollope's Can you Forgive Her? was a enjoyable story that explores the tensions between women and men and the world of tradition and one's place in society, especially where women wish to have [some] independence whilst decisions are made around their betrothals and marriages. There are interweaving characters and subjects such as politics, widowhood, status, intra-family influences and money - especially the importance of and access to money held by the women characters. This is a long book at 830 pages, excluding notes and the introduction, but it does reward the reader with a fine story that holds a mirror up to the contemporary society that Trollope observed and wrote of. It is the first of the six "Palliser" novels and I look forward to the second. My copy was the paperback Penguin Classics as shown for this review.
