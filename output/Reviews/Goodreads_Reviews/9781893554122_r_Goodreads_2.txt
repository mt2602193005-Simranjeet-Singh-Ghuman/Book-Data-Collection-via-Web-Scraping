Some theorists have argued that the traditional method of looking at history is archaic and should be discarded. Instead, these scholars argue, we should look at history through different lens. Those lens might be structuralist, deconstructist, Marxist, or critical. Windschuttle pulls no punches against scholars, many of them which are postmodern, who use these lens on history. He argues that these revisionist scholars obscure parts of history that matter so they can craft their own narratives. To them, ideology is more important than good history, Windschuttle contends. Overall, I liked this book. Chapter on structuralism was my favorite. Love Windschuttle’s ability to explain postmodern ideas in layman’s terms.
