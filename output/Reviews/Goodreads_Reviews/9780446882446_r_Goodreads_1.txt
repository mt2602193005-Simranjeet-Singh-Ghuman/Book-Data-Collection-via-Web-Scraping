Published in 1966, this collection of comic stories is so brilliant, it's hard not to envy Woody Allen's writing talent.;) The ideas are pure gold (Count Dracula mistakes a solar eclipse for his usual night out; Nazism through the eyes of Hitler's barber; the parody of the scholarly review of the "long awaited volume" of Metterling's laundry lists; a salesman's Death climbs in through his window but turns out to be a quintessential schlepper; a parody in the style of film noir where a femme fatale tasks a hard-boiled private detective with locating... god), and the implementation is perfect. Read in December 2022.
