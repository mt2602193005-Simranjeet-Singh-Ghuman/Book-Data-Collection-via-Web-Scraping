I just finished reading this book tonight. I've been putting it off until I realized he was publishing a new novel in 2008. I didn't want to fall behind. I don't know what to make of the book. It revolves around an Italian Ameican protagonist who basically is having to come to terms with the fact that he needs to more firmly ensconse himself in the lives of his family and friends. Spoilers below with cast listing Cast of characters: Jerry Battle: protagonist, distant, mid-life crisis, enjoys flying airplanes, works for a Travel Company Rita: former nanny, Puerto Rican American, now ex-wife, raised Jerry's kids Daisy Han: Jerry's first wife, Korean American, once manic-depressive, drowns in family pool Theresa Battle: Jerry's daughter, college professor, pregnant, suffering from Non-Hodgskin's Lymphoma Paul Pyun: Theresa's husband and fictitious foil of author, Chang-rae Lee Jack Battle: Jerry's son, whitewashed, married to Eunice with two kids who watch Britney Spears, mismanaging the "family" business
