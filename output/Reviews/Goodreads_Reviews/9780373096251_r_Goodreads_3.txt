4,5/5 I enjoyed this series finale, though not as much as the first three books. Trace and Gillian were an interesting and sweet couple, especially when they had to pretend to be lovers during their mission. The idea behind the story was solid, and Trace was a well-written character, though not as impressive as I had hoped based on the expectations set by the previous books. Gillian, on the other hand, came across as somewhat superficial at first, but she grew on me as the story progressed. The romance was subtle, shown more through gestures and quiet moments. I really liked the way Nora Roberts built each character's story: the first book focuses on Abby, a widow living a secluded life on a farm; the second follows Maddy, a Broadway ballet dancer; the third centers on Chantel, a talented actress; and the final one features their older brother, Trace, a spy.
