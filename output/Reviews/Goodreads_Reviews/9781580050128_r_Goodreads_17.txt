I liked the book until the ending. The book was very interesting and I found myself wanting to read more and more. I loved how gay it was and I enjoyed learning a bit about Jewish culture and lore. But the ending was not my cup of tea. Rainbow falls in love too quickly and it annoyed me. The whole book she is obsessed with Riva and then as soon as Anya admits her feelings she is in love with her. Even after Anya held her at gunpoint. She also completely forgot about Kokos at the drop of a hat even though they seemed to be becoming friends. I liked Kokos character though and I guess her decided to go into movies wasn't a horrible ending for her even though it was a bit strange to me as she seemed to like possessing people.
