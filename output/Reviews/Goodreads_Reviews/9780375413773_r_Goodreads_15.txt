This set of essays about the Founding Fathers and the documents they created was interesting, but it would have been nice if the author had tied them all together into a single narrative. I thought the most interesting piece was on the Federalist Papers (Chapter 4). It's amazing how many of the dangers warned by the anti-Federalists came true - high taxation, the federal government dominating the states, etc. Didn't happen overnight, and I'm glad they didn't win the ratification debate, but their views are food for thought on how we should move forward from here as a country.
