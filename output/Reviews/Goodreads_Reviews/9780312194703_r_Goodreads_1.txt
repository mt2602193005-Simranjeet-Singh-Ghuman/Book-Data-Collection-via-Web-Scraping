This would be a very good book for adults who have never danced before and wish to try adult ballet classes. Discusses the health benefits of ballet, how a class is structured, the differences between a nonprofessional adult class and professional classes, attire to wear, goes over the lingo and some basic steps, suggestions for modifications for bad knees or other joints & has some exercises one could do at home to target specific muscle groups. There are a few small areas that I found dated based on my experience as an amateur adult dancer.
