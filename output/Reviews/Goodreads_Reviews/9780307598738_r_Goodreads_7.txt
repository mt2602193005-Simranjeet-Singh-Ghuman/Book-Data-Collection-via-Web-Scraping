I used this book in the classroom as a read aloud with an art activity that followed and the kids loved it. Reading this book helped the students understand what going to the dentist was like and what can happen at the dentist. Not only do I think it is good for students, because dental hygiene is a Kindergarten standard, but this could also be used to read to your own children if they are nervous about going to the dentist. There is also verbage that is introduced to the kids, such as cavity and braces, which I think is great when learning about something such as this.
