"For the mystery of human being does not solely rest in the desire to live, but in the problem—for what should one live at all?" "Some of them—more rebellious and ferocious than the rest—will destroy themselves; others—rebellious but weak—will destroy each other; while the remainder, weak, helpless and miserable, will crawl back to our feet." "Were there really anything like life beyond the grave, surely it would never fall to the lot of such as they!"
