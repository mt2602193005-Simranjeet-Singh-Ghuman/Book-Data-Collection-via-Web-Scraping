I received this book as an ARC through the publisher and NetGalley, in exchange for an honest review. Shortly after receiving the ARC, I realized that I already had this book on my Kindle. This book is just fantastic, Rosanne Bittner has been one of my favorite authors since the early 90s and this book actually originally came out around 1991 or 92 by another publisher, But as I was saying, I absolutely love this book; it is an engrossing saga like most of her books are. It encompasses about 12 years and sees the characters through a wagon train trip out west to see if the dream of a transcontinental railroad is possible, the Civil War, the actual construction of the Union Pacific Railroad and the meeting of the Union Pacific and Central Pacific, as well as many adventures within the years. I definitely recommend this book, as well as any of her other books.
