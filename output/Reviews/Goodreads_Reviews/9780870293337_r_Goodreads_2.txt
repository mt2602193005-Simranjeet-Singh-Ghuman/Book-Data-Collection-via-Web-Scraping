I had some time to kill before an intake appointment so I decided to read a short book while I waited. Boy, this book while it has good intentions, it often does not know what it wants to say to kids about divorce. For example, it says divorce is hurtful, your life will change, but both of your parents love you despite them saying mean things about each other to you, making sharing time between each parent difficult, etc... Honestly, I think the intentions are good with this book, but as an adult reading it (which is not the intended audience) it seems like it misses the mark in really understanding how to support children of divorce and the parents who are divorcing. In many ways, it attempts to normalize divorce (which is good), but then somehow makes it seems like there won't be hurt feelings, trauma, differences, and that your life will forever change.
