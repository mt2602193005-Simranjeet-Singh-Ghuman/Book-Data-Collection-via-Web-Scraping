The Wit and Wisdom of Abraham Lincoln is a brief, yet varied collection of thoughts from Lincoln. Here are two favorites: "As I would not be a slave, so I would not be a master. This expresses my idea of democracy. Whatever differs from this, to the extent of the difference, is not democracy." (Note, August 1858) "That the Constitution gives the President a negative on legislation, all know; but that this negative should be so combined with platforms and other appliances as to enable him and, in fact, almost compel him to take the whole of legislation is what we object to [. . .] and is what constitutes the broad distinction between you and us." (Speech in the US House of Representatives, July 27, 1848)
