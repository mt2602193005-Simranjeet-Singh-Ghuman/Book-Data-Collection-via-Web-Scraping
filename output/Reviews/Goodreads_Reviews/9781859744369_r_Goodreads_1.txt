I recently attended a program which introduced me to the idea of making sushi at home. This was one of the recommended books that the presenter suggested as an additional resource, so I checked it out. I like how the book is laid out with gorgeous pictures which inspire you to keep learning all the required new vocabulary terms. The author assumes that the reader knows nothing about sushi, which is very helpful. I would have liked to have seen a few more technical details on the actual creation of the sushi. While I appreciate the photos and recipes, a few graphs or blueprints would aid me in my first sushi endeavor. I look forward to my first sushi making tonight, and I feel motivated and confident after reading Sushi Made Easy.
