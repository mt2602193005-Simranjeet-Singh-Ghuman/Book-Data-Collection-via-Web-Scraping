I've intended to read Hans Christian Andersen 's famous fairy tales and I just had the chance to do it recently. I started with The Ugly Duckling . This is basically how The Ugly Duckling looked like after hatching.. I think it's rather cute, dontcha think? I also think that all creatures look cute when they're little. Most of you must be familiar with the story. It reminds me of switched at birth situations when the child can't fit in with the new family merely because that child doesn't look like the others within that family. The poor duckling is constantly teased and bullied because everyone thinks he's ugly. What makes it worse is that all the bullying started by his supposedly own folks. How terrible! After a journey of agony and misfortunes he finally gets to know who he really is.. Guess what? He grows into a very handsome swan. What I disliked in the story is that the tormented fellow kinda gives up when everyone is against him. WTH, dude? Man up for God's sake! Screw them all, to hell what they think, but DON'T GIVE UP! Moral of the story: The world is such a messed up shallow place. Accept who you are and move on.
