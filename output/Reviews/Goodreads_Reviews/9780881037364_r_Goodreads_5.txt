Such an odd story in many ways but a deeply moving one in the end. When I finished the last page, it became clear to me that this ended up being a journey of the soul for Dave. He moves from loneliness to belonging, from anger to love, from defiance to a vulnerability that can accept help and care. I love how every character in this big found family helps Dave with this: Emily, the Austins, Canon Tallis, Mr Theo, the Dean, and the two doctors. Dave also faces the biggest temptation, makes the biggest mistake, and has the most profound redemption arc. He’s not always the sole focus of the story but he’s its heart. I so love reading about all the characters though. L’Engle has created such a lovely world. Thank you Libby for the inspiration to finally read this!
