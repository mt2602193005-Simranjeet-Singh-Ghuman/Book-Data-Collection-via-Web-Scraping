This was a very interesting book for children. To start off, the pictures on every page were awesome and vibrantly filled with color. The art work, I think, was the saving grace of this book. Some of the words they used for the insects in the book, were to me, a little advanced for a child's understanding. That would have to be the only criticism that I have for the book. Other than that, this books goes off a very good imagination for a child, and they are able to enjoy everything they see and read in this book. I would definitely read this one to kids and share it, and sure they may not know what everything means, but that doesn't mean they would enjoy the book at all. I would recommend this one to any kid.
