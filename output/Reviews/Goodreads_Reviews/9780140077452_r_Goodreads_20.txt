Meh. This was not a terrible book, but not a great one either. It plodded along in a somewhat formulaic way to the end. I found that the main character, Patience McKenna, was not all that likeable, though there was little about her to dislike either; she was just somewhat bland. I know that this is the first book of a new series, and I am usually more forgiving of first (and second) books, as the author develops his/her voice and characters. However, in this case I am not sure if I would bother trying another novel starring this character.
