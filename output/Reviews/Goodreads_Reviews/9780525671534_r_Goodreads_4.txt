This book really feels like perspective of older elementary/middle school preteens. It’s written for most reading levels. It’s cute how much Darci likes Travis (though this might change in the end.) I like how they handle the lovey “boy stuff” for this age bracket the author is not trying to mature the characters too quickly. Matt McGrath isn’t as bad as he seems he’s kinda sweet. Read for a good story. It’s also set around Valentine’s Day in February so it is a good winter read. 5 stars!!
