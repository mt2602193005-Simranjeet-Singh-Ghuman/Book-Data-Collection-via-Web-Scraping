This novel is old-school, but in a bad way, not a good way. A "Lemme tell you 'bout this story..." kind of book. Nearly 300 pages of that. The author posits an Earth near-Future from his reference point of 1992 that so misses its mark you have to tell yourself this is an "alternate-timeline Earth" in order to suspend your disbelief. Life is just too short to get trapped reading such books.
