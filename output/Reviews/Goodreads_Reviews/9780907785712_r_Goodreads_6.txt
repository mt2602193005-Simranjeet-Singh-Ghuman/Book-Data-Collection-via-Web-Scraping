"My doctrine is this, that if we see cruelty or wrong that we have the power to stop, and do nothing, we make ourselves sharers in the guilt.". BLACK BEAUTY, with the help of some of her friends and acquaintances, narrates her own life story in this absolute gem of a children's classic. I've always loved the beauty and grace of horses, but now have an even greater respect and appreciation for them...... and their needs. A few heartbreaking stories are told here, but the positive, encouraging message and education for children and adults alike is priceless. EVERYONE should read BLACK BEAUTY!
