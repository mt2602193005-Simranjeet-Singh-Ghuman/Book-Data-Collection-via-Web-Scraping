Review first published on my blog, Nine Pages . Adding lift the flap elements to this story is an improvement on the first Monster at the End of This Book , but otherwise this book falls rather flat in comparison to the first. I would rather have The Monster at the End of This Book done in this format. In this, Grover and Elmo argue over whether or not to turn the page and come closer and closer to the promised monster at the end, and of course the monsters are just themselves as Grover was the monster in the first book.
