I love Mercer Mayer’s art style so much, something very comforting in it. Art aside, I really liked the story. A story and wonderland-esque theme of acceptance of who you are and patience. I think students who are frustrated with their height could benefit from this story, a warning on being careful with what you wish for and also being very clear when you speak with your elders. Though majority of the text is beginner reader, there are some words that students will struggle with due to unfamiliar pronunciation; such as ‘Axel’, ‘Thistle’, and ‘Howl’. Over all I’d recommend this story for any students who are self conscious and impatient about their height, or believe doing something over and over for a means to an end will get their goal accomplished.
