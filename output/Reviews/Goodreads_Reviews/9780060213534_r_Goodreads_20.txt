On their balloon farm in Mohawk, New York, Carl Myers makes balloons for his wife Carlotta who is the most famous female aeronaut in America. In 1882, Carlotta races Bernard the Brave, the greatest gentleman aeronaut in America. While they waited for the race to begin, Carlotta lets her daughter, Ariel, wait in the balloon’s basket. Ariel gets tired, crawls into the odds and end box, and falls asleep. Carlotta doesn’t discover her until after the race begins. The young girl’s weight provides extra ballast that causes her mother many difficulties during the race.
