i work in an office where there is a lending library so sometimes i just grab a book for the commute home not knowing anything about it. and yes, i liked the cover on this one. it was a quick read and interesting in that it was written by 'a god' who is watching things unfold for a women who 'has lost her soul' but is getting it back after a near death experience. it was not deep intellectual reading, but kept my interest and frankly that is the reading phase i seem to be in right now...
