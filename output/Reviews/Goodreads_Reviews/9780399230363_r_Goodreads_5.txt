Mother Possum taught Joe how to save himself from their enemies by playing dead. It was a crucial trick for a possum. When they were practicing, Joe wasn't able to hold his laugh, his scream, his giggle, and his wiggle. But, a dead possum not laugh, scream, giggle, or even wiggle! While Joe's friends were entertained to watch Joe practicing, Mother Possum was worried, wondering how Joe would handle a real dangerous situation. Meanwhile, a bear lurked as they practiced outdoor. Sabio and Mom really enjoy reading this book and the 2 other books by Keizo Kasza that sabioandmom has. The story is funny and warm, also not superficial.
