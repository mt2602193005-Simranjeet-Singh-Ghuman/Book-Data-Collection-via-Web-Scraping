A Good Read Detective Ray Flowers is on sick leave after being attacked while at work. He has to consider his future, should he stay in the police force or make a new start? He decides to live in the cottage his aunt had left him on her death, while he considers his future, but strange things start to happen to him. I enjoyed the book but did get a little fed up with the constant going between Rays life and that of Kitty, who lived centuries ago. I am not a great believer in ghosts.
