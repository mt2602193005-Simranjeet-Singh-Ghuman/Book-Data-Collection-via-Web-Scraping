It took me a little bit to get into the book, your thrown into a weekend party, so I had to do some back and forth to sort the characters. The premise of the novel is not new, but I enjoyed how it was told. I liked most how the thoughts would run through the different characters so you got a perspective view from all sides. I wasn’t expecting the main event, and thought the way it played out rather interesting, and also not what I expected. I enjoyed the book as the writing style is unique and von Armin has a wonderful command of descriptions where as a reader you can almost feel the soft breeze and smell the soft scent of flowers in the air.
