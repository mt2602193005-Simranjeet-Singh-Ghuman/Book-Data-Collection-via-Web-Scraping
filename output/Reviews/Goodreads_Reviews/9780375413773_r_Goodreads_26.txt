This short book by an excellent historian and writer gives us more than several character sketches of key Founders of the American government. Its focus is on the Constitution and delves into the conditions of the time to carefully explain why they wrote what they wrote in setting up our government. In the chapters on the Federalist Papers, especially, you really see the world from the authors' perspectives - including the Anti-Federalists. I highly recommend it, especially these days when we seem to confront one constitutional crisis after another.
