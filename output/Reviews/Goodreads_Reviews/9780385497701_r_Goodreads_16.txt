Could very well be THE biography of 'London', Peter Ackroyd's 2000 publication is a monumental eight hundred page delight. The scope and coverage is breathtaking, from the last ice age to the domain of wild animals, to the Roman and Saxon foundations to it's present day sprawl. The capital city with all the trials,tribulations,fog and flame from Aeneas to Ziegler. Ackroyd has produced a masterpiece. It is clearly a life times work, and not just a historical one. The reader is taken by the hand and led through the streets, alleyways, squares, fairs, churches, palaces, ale houses and hovels. Through time travel and through the eyes of a myriad of characters, writers, artists, princes and paupers, we can imagine the sights, sounds, smells and spirits through the centuries. A huge feast of a book that is easily digested in concise and so very well written portions.
