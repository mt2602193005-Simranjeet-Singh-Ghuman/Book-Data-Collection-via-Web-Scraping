D is for Deadbeat 31st May-1st June 2013 This is the second time I have read this book. I did enjoy it. I think the author is starting to inject a little more personality into her work, deviating from the slightly more rigid structure of the earlier books in the series. There was enough mystery in it to keep me reading, even though I'd already read it. E is for Evidence 1st-3rd June 2013 I had no idea whodunit, or why. So I just had to keep reading to find out and who would have guessed! Never in my wildest dreams did I guess why!!! Crazy! But in an enjoyable way! F is for Fugitive 3rd-6th June 2013 Really enjoyed this one. The author cleverly snuck in some very funny stuff. There were so many suspects and seemingly random clues. I really couldn't guess whodunit or what was really going on until about the page before it was confirmed. Nice.
