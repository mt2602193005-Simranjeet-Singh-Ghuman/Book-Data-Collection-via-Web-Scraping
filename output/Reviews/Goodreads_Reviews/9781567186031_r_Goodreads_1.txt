Back in 1999 I was running a side gig as a busking fiddler and storyteller. I picked up A Bard’s Book of Pagan Songs to add new material to my repertoire. It did not disappoint. I should mention that this is a Llewelyn book, with all that implies. It contains a fair amount of New Age Paganism-Light that one expects from that publisher. If you are into that sort of thing you’ll likely enjoy this book even more than I did. Me, I looked past that material for what I considered the meat of this collection. The gold in this book is its Welsh mythology — stories and songs reshaped and retold from The Mabinogion, a medieval collection of Welsh myths. Hugin took tales from the Four Branches of The Mabinogion and created original songs, both words and music, from them. Each song magically captures the eerie beauty of these myths. Before each song he gives a thumbnail outline of the story that inspired it. Each song is presented with a simple melody line of notes, and with guitar chords preceding it. The book includes a CD of these songs so you can listen to them performed even if you are not musically inclined. A Bard’s Book of Pagan Songs is an excellent introduction into the gorgeous mythology of The Mabinogion. It’s also a fine resource for storytellers. If you can find it, it will be a great addition to your collection.
