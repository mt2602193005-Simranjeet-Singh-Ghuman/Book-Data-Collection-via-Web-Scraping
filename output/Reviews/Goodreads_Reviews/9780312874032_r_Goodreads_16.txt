This was the first Terry Bisson book that I read. It wasn't until later that I realized this is the same person who wrote "They're Made of Meat", which is one of my favorite science fiction short stories. The only problem I had with this book was that it just didn't ring true. The world didn't seem like one that would stem from the world we live in. A quick check of the copyright date gave me the answer as to why... it was written pre-2001, and published right around the 9/11 attacks. For a pre-9/11 story, it's excellent, but it was also really interesting to see just how much the world has changed, and how that affects my perception of futuristic sci-fi.
