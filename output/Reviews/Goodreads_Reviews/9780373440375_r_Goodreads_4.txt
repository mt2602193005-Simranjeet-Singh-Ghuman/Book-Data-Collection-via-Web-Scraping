Another great romantic comedy by SB This is the story of Alan, who has been jilted at the altar by his fiancé Jo, and Pam, Jo's best friend. Alan decides to go on his honeymoon alone. Drunk, he asks Pam to go with him. They are too opposite persons, Alan is uptight & conservative, Pam is a flamboyant bombshell. The vacation turns to a nightmare, and the two have to fight the attraction sizzling between them. A series of hilarious situations, between the exuberant Pam & the uptight Alan, will guarantee a few laugh out loud moments.
