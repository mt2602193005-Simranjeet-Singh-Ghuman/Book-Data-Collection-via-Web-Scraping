4 Stars ~ When Monique's mother died, she gave up her dreams and settled back in the family home to take care of her brother and sisters and her stepfather. She's now 27 and her Aunt Amabel is afraid she'll continue to sacrifice her young life and miss out on her own adventures and romance. So Amabel bequests Monique a very nice sum of money, enough to travel and live comfortably for a year. The only catch is that Monique must leave home completely, allow her siblings to take over their lives, or the money isn't hers. So Monique quits her job, severs ties with the man she'd been dating, and told her family they're on their own, and she sets off to find her roots. Not 50 miles away is a small community where her grandfather had come from, and there living are his two sisters, Monique's great-aunts. They don't know who is she, as her grandfather had left home in disgrace and had changed his name. She's come to the estate to help the elderly women make their home a historical site and open it up to tourists. And watching her moves and motives is Edourad, the man who purchased the bulk of the estate's farmland and shares the aunt's home for their security. This is another lovely romance by Ms. Summers. Monique has a generous heart and she cares deeply for her great-aunts. Making their homestead a tourist attraction gives them the income they need to continue to live there. Edourad is a man with a soft heart for damsel's in distress. He doesn't hesitate to jump in when he thinks Monique needs a champion. I really enjoyed reading Edourad and Monique's story.
