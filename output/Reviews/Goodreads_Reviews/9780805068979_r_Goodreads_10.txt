Travel, island life in Greece in the Sixties/Seventies, family, Mediterranean culture This was a good holiday read; not too demanding, and entertaining enough. However I would have liked more autobiography from the author. For example, she suddenly discloses that she has another child, a baby, whom she is taking on holiday to Corfu, to her parents’ home. A little bit more of her own backstory would have made the book more relatable. If you have watched the entertaining series on Netflix, The Durrells, and indeed if you have read Gerald Durrell’s ‘My Family and Other Animals’, you will find this book in much the same vein, albeit set three decades later. What is notable is the author’s father’s steadfast wish to have no road access to his remote property. Of course the road did come later, along with the tourist hotels and their blaring disco music at night! The book left me feeling I would love to go on holiday to Greece, again, and that’s a good sign for a travel novel!
