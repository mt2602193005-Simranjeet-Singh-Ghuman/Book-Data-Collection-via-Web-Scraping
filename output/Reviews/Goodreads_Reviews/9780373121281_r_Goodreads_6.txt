The Playboy's Proposition was a good HP. It did have a pretty clueless heroine prone to judgments about the hero, but well the hero Tyler had managed to hide his feeling so well that Michelle couldn't know the truth. Michelle's boyfriend of ten years dumps her by sending a wedding invite, Tyler their rich friend from university consoles her and tells her home truths about him that she has always been blind to. They go the wedding together and start an affair. It was fairly evident to me throughout the book, that the hero suffered from unrequited love in the book. The book was decent.
