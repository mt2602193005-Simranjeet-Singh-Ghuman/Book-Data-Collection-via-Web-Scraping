Football Dreams was a pretty good book. I only moderately liked the text, but I really did like the ending of the book. I can't spoil anything, but I'll say that watching Dan Keith's development of emotions and realizations as the book moves on is really interesting and entertaining. I also really liked some of the other characters in the book, like Nick Kaiser, in particular. What I didn't like about the book was that the plot could move on very slow at times, and could be uninteresting in between the parts about the actual football seasons. Also, the character of Sara Warren is never really easy to understand. It's obvious that her and Dan have a connection, but she kind of trails off at the end of the story without explanation. I also found some parts of the text without dialogue really boring. The author's storytelling might be a little flawed, or not to my liking. I would still call it a good read, but it's hard to trudge on at some points because the author's storytelling can ramble without intrigue at times. I would recommend this novel about a high schooler trying to win his dad's attention with football to any other high school boy, and especially one who also enjoys/plays football.
