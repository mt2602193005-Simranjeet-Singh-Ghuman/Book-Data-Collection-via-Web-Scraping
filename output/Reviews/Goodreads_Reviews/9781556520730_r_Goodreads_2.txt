This book was given me by an Egyptian nephrologist from the Loyola University Medical Center who was married to one of my wife's two sign language interpreters there. A liberal, poltically and religiously, Mo was justly concerned about the overweening influence of conservative Israeli interests on our domestic politics and foreign policies. This book, by a former Illinois Republican congressman, explores and criticizes this influence while adducing diverse voices in favor of a more balanced policy as regards the Middle East.
