I forgot that I read this shortly before I went to Savannah/Tybee Island, and realized while I was walking around a museum that one of the scenes took place in the house I was standing in!* So that was weird. The book was okay. A twin (actually a lookalike cousin) takes her "twin's" place in her loveless marriage, the inevitable ensues. A quick read, I had some issues with it (the other "twin's" husband genially slapping her around to get her "under control," presented with apparently no irony whatsoever), but it was an okay read. Midnight in the Garden of Good and Evil was a better preparation for a Savannah visit. *It's now the Ships of the Sea Maritime Museum , and the scene was the one in which Juliet hosted the Scarbrough party, with President Monroe attending.
