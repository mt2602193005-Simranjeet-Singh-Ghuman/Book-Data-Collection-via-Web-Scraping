It's been nearly a month since I read this one and very little of it has stuck with me, possibly because I kept waiting for things to happen--the exciting part comes almost in the last five pages of the book. I do remember, from my childhood reading, being extremely frustrated with Jim's stupidity when Trixie tries to convey certain very urgent information secretly, and I was frustrated again this time. I did love the descriptions of all the antiques the Bob-Whites collect, either for sale or display, though the two Japanese gentlemen come across as a little stereotyped to a modern reader. This is the first book in the series written by "Kathryn Kenny" (actually a Nancy-Drew style syndicate of writers) and I wondered if it would come across as very different from the Julie Campbell books. It didn't.
