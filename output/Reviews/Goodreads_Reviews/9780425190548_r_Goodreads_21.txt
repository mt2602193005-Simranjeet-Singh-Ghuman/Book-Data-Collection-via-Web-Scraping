Raw and direct look at life in Perma on the reservation. I liked that Charlie had a first person perspective, I think he was the main character, or at least the narrator, even though it focuses on Louise’s life and Baptiste. I was pretty surprised at the happy (maybe) ending and had expected it to be harsher. The whole book was a little sadder than I generally like to read but gripping.
