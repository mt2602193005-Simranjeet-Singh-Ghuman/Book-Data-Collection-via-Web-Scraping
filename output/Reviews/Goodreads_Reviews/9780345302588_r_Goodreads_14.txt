A bizarre book, to say the least. An assassin is hired for a job, plunging the galaxy into chaos and causing him to go on the run. It starts well, with the title device being an interesting piece of technology. However, the story has the protagonist becoming a rebel bent on changing the status quo of the galaxy. There wasn't really much action, the tension was blase, the supporting characters very one dimensional, and the pair that pop up often were too far fetched. I'm glad I read one of Williamson's books, but I won't be returning to this one.
