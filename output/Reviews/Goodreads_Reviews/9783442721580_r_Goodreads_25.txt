Weird... I skimmed over my JCO "to read" list and wondered why I hadn't not marked this collection as read. I will never forget reading the last story, "Martyrdom", during my lunch at work. Warning: do not read this wrenching story while eating. The vivid, lurid descriptions made me nauseous... needless to say I didn't want to finish my meal. Trust JCO to make me almost cry over a rat's fate. The post-apocalyptic tale "Thanksgiving" is also one not to read while eating. Come to think of it, with some of these stories JCO has hit upon a great diet aid: make the reader sick to the stomach. There's also lighter albeit still malevolent fare like "The White Cat". Never underestimate the power of the feline majesty. A solid, disturbing collection that truly touches on the grotesque side of nature. These stories may be horrible in places, but they aren't "horrifying", if that makes any sense. They will frighten you because they make the reader doubt the humanity of the human species, not due to a spooky factor.
