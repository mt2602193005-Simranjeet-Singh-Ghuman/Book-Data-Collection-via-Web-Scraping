I admit to knowing almost nothing about the Mexican bandit Pancho Villa, and so when seeing it, I was intrigued. The book, however, gave me little about the bandit and revolved around a relationship between an old soldier and a young recruit. The relationship formed as their cavalry unit pursued Villa from the border towns between the U.S. and Mexico. Taking place in 1916, before the U.S. entered WWI, familiar names. LAST REVEILLE is an exciting, well-researched account of ‘Black Jack’ Pershing’s 1916 expedition after Pancho Villa. It’s a cinematic, end-of-the-west epic, but also a gripping, moving character study told by a true master. Morrell is an excellent writer and storyteller; you can definitely get the feel of the barren wasteland and the horrific conditions the soldiers endured. It did light a fire under me to search for other stories about Villa.
