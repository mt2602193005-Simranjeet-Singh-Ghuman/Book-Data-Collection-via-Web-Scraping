I just couldn't get into this book, not for lack of trying, though. I have put this book down more times than I care to remember. I managed to follow through and read to the not so exciting end. It should be a winner - the perfect setting, the cool no nonsense male protagonist and the serial killer "Mr Gruesome”. But what a drag it was! I just have to mention the overuse of similes, it's on every other page, and it kills any good intention to finish the story. But I did, and I'll give it a generous two-star prize. I probably won't be coming back though.
