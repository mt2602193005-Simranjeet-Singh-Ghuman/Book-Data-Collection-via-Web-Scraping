People at different stages of life intersecting by proximity or chance, reflecting life’s unanswerable questions onto and off each other. More or less exactly what I wanted from a full-length Ann Beattie novel based on my impressions of her shorts. There are a couple of sections describing love that are some of the most beautiful things I’ve read all year. Something about her writing really resonates with me, even if I can’t quite put it into words. I think it’s probably just a matter of wondering about the same questions.
