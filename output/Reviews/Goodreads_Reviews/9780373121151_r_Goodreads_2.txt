This was a quick easy read. Nothing remarkable about the characters and not much to the plot, other than a cursory introduction of "another woman" at the very end. On the other hand, it was a nice change of pace to have a couple who were reasonably likeable (the heroine actually changes a flat tire by herself!) and a hero who didn't spend the entire book making the heroine miserable. Good enough for a slow afternoon when one isn't in the mood for a big drama.
