As one born and raised in Alabama I can relate to much of this hilarious story of one crazy Bama Belle. This twist and turns in Mr. Childress book will leave you wanting more. Lucille starts in a little town of Industry, AL, yes I know where it is and have relatives there to this day, and winds up in LA, not Lower Alabama but LA as in Los Angeles, California. Follow her trip through crazy ideas and hilarious doings with Peejoe, her poor nephew. I promise you'll laugh yourself silly and never look at Tupperware the same again. God bless from the Deep South ; )
