I think every group of people likes laughing at themselves sometimes. And Mormons are no different! In this book you will meet extremes of many people in your average Mormon community. But through it all there is love and understanding (or at least attempts at understand). That's what a love about books like this. We don't need to be perfect in order to be loved! **Talking Points - who do you identify most with in this book? If you were going to write a book called Around the Ward in 80 days about your own ward (or church), what characters and characteristics would you include? Have you have people in the past that were hard to be friends with, how did you deal with that? Did friendship ever form? Do you have people now that are hard to be friends with? Why? Can one person develop a friendship, or does it take two?
