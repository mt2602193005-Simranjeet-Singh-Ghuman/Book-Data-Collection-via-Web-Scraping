Very revealing insight into the inner workings of Mars and Hershey's (along with the candy industry as a whole), two of the world's largest candy makers. In a highly secretive industry, taken to the extreme by the Mars family, very little was known about the company--until now. Gives a nice juxtaposition between Milton Hershey, who's company's objective is to fund an orphanage, and the Mars family--who continues to sink every dollar they make back into the business (and run with no debt)--although the family could easily be worth billions.
