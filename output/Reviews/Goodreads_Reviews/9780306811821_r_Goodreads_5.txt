'The Dark Stuff' is a collection of Nick Kent's publishings from the 1970's. Some really good stuff in there, and by 'good' I mean that his journalism is top notch, not that the scenarios that he is referring to are good; some of these rock stars he writes about are monstrous. Not as good as his autobiography, however, I still felt like I'd muscled up my understanding of musical history upon finishing. Solid 4/5.
