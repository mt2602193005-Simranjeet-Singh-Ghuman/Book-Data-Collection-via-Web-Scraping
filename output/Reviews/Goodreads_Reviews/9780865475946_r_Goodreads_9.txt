The best reads for me are the ones I find a relationship too. In this case, I live in Nevada. I've visited Mountain City several times and even bought something in the store. Also, I've camped along the Bruneau River-the most beautiful wilderness area I've ever experienced. And, in one of my capstone classes at UNR I took Basque History, one of the my best classes-hard, but lots of substance! This poignant memoir telling the story of a Basque family living in a remote mining town is both funny and sad in its telling of a disappearing culture both for old Nevada mining towns, and in the Basque heritage. The sense of humor is great. What is the capital of Wyoming? W. But my best take away is this story tells us how children show grow up. Learning about their heritage through chores and interactions with a strong family. Today, we sure need more of this lifestyle.
