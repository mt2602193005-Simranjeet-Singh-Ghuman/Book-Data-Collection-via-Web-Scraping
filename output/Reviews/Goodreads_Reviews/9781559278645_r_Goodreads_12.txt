Jamie has her life all planned out. In a couple months she will marry safe, dependable Phillip. But all in not right in her small home town of Beamont. Nothing seems to change but the taxes that keep going up. Jamie owns the town paper, or at least most of it. When her silent partner, Max, comes to town, with his special car, things start to change. Her friend, Frankie, running for mayor, promises to find out just where the tax dollars are going, and things start to heat up with several murder attempts. I really liked this book but while a fun read was not a funny as JE's usual Stephanie Plum books. I liked the two main characters and I thought the addition of Muffin was a good move on the author's part but maybe a bit of a cop out too. I would read on with these characters and give the book a 3.5 if I could.
