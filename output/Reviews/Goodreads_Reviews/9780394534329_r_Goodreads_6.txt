The Middle Heart is a sweeping history of the family of the House of Li, spanning their lives from 1932 to 1989. It is set in the times of the greatest upheavals in Chinese history, from Imperial China, through the world wars, the Japanese invasion, and the communist revolution that put Mao into power. Throughout the decades, the plot follows the lives of Steel Hope, Mountain Pine, and Summer Wishes, who in their youthful companionship in 1932, form a lifelong bond, and take an oath that will impact their lives in the most profound and unexpected ways. Bette Bao Lord, also author of the splendid "Spring Moon," unfolds and reveals the China that most Westerners know nothing about.
