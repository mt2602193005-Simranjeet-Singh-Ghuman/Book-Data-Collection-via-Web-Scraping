3.5 stars. Nothing particularly memorable here (and one wonders why so many of the woodland creatures are so unfriendly to poor Mousekin!) but the illustrations are beautiful and very nature-ly oriented--no characterized mice here, Mousekin looks just as you'd find him in the woodland, and the representations of the birds and other creatures, as well as the flowers, are equally realistic. Lovely, and a nice change from the more "cutesy" renditions of mice with big ears or funny smiles ("If You Give a Mouse a Cookie" comes to mind!) that are so prevalent in children's illustrations today (though I do love them, too, of course!)
