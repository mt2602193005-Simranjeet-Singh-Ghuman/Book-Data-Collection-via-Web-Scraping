4.5 I honestly thought this was a solid thriller. It’s one of those that keeps you guessing what is real which has you focusing even more deeply when reading. What really sets this story above others is the descriptions. The author did a fantastic job with the imagery and vivid descriptions. You truly feel like you are part of the nightmare, I could easily visualize everything that was going on which only helped my immersion in the story. Overall, I would recommend this one!
