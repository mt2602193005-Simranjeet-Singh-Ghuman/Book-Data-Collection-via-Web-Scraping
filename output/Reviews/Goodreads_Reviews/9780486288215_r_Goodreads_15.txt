#9 Peter Rabbit children's series Ooooh, this charmed me from the cover -- cutie pie Jemima Puddle-Duck with her baby blue bonnet and pink shawl. I spent an extra hour after reading, ooh'ing and ahhh-ing over the wonderful artwork. I could easily keep this on my bedside table and immerse myself in the art & story. Absolutely loved the innocent Jemima Puddle-Duck happily walking along with the nattily dressed fox. Somehow as a child I missed reading the Peter Rabbit books. In 2017, I plan to read the other 22 books in this precious series. Inside cover says Beatrix Potter's farm had a real Jemima duck who wasn't good at hatching eggs, and that Potter's favorite sheepdog, Kep, was used in the story as the duck's wise friend who rescues her from the deceptively charming sandy-whiskered gentleman (the fox).
