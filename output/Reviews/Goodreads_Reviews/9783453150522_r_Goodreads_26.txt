I read this book a bit less than a year after finishing Into Thin Air (Jon Krakauer). It was a little hard to read about these tragic events again but I think it's important to read this book as well as Into Thin Air. You get a very different perspective - Anatoli was a guide, Krakauer was a client with nothing close to the experience Anatoli had (in high altitude mountaineering). In Into thin Air Anatoli is painted as the "bad guy" but in fact he was a hero who saved three lives while seriously risking his own. Krakauer's relentless attempts at discrediting Anatoli's character really baffles me. As you read this book you get to know Anatoli's character more and you come to understand him a lot better. What an amazing guy.
