Ahhh! The Pine Barrens of southern New Jersey, I know them well and have done pretty much everything there is do in them. I have hunted, fished, crabbed, skinny dipped in the cold water creeks, and have gone on ghost hunts.. As a little kid, the only thing I knew about the Pines was they were a patch of woods we traveled through on our way to Wildwood. As an adult I realized they were so much more, they're a couple hundred thousand square miles of deep woods, salt marshes, deep cedar water creeks, and scattered ghost towns, with no people, no lights, and very limited cell service. They've been home to many things over the years as well. Native Americans and Dutch settlers, British Loyalists during the war for Independence, millers, and trappers. It was also home to undesirables, pirates, thieves, bootleggers, and moonshiners... It is also home to a legend. The Jersey Devil, AKA the Leed's Devil, AKA my favorite cryptid/monster. This book puts a different spin on the Jersey Devil legend and really hits home while describing the bad parts of the pines
