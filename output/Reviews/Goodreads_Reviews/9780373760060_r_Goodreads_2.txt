This isn't a review, but a "Why I bought this book" Moment. *smile* I found a copy of this book on the shelf of my local library. The cover snagged my attention so hard that I not only read it, I sent Harlequin/Silhouette a money order for the price of the book plus what I hope was enough for shipping and handling and. A month later, the book was on my doorstep and I was on my way to purchasing each and every book in the series. So, it was the eye-candy on the cover. Fortunately, I loved the book and the series. While the book isn't IR, it does feature "Multicultural" themes. Meaning....the Tallchief family are the descendents of a Scots bride and a Native American Chief and as such, they are very proud of their heritage and used their heritage as a way of keeping them together as youth.
