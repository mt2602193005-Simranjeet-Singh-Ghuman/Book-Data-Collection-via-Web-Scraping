The ending of this book was so bad, I decided it must be brilliant. I actually went back and reread the ending the next day, as it seriously made me think. Is it out inability to change that leads to disaster...or is it our salvation? The only characters that might actually be capable of change are the stalker magician and Bobby, the drifter. For everyone else the story seems to end more or less in the same place it began. It would all be harmless enough if it weren't for the kids, who definitely need someone to put them first - for a change.
