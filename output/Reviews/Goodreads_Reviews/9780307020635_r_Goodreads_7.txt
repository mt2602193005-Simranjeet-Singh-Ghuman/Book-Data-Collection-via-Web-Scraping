This has been always one of my favorite Christmas Golden Books to read since I loved animals so hearing the Nativity from the animals is a wonderful way to go. And it is always interesting to see what you pick up on when you are re-reading a book for the first time after having not read it in a long time and now I can see it as a counting book so apparently my younger self didn't really pay attention to details that much. The story is a beautiful one that relates the Nativity in a brief yet memorable rhyming way as the animals contribute to the story or remember others of their species who partook in the special events. What made this special to me as I re-read it was to see the kittens telling a part since there is a Christmas legend that explains the tabby's M on its forehead was put there by the Virgin Mary as a memorial to the cat who purred Baby Jesus to sleep. And I love the illustrations for they are quiet and subdued in a way that is fitting to the story. Most of the Golden Books have the same type of illustrations and this quietly solemn artwork is the crème of this book. And may it bless your Christmas reading as you share it with your loved young ones....
