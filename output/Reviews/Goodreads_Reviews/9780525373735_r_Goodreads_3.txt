The kids and I are doing a summer of reading books aloud and then watching the movies to compare. I started with this one to have SOMETHING for the 3 year old. Although my 8 year old boy was quite annoyed to begin--after the first story/first day he was the first to come for the next ones. The stories are quite charming and fun to read aloud. Even the 12 year old boy joined us for the last day and laughed aloud several times. Very enjoyable. I was surprised it was such a hit.
