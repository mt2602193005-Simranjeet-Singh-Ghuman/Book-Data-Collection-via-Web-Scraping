This is a great and powerful memoir about soldiers during Vietnam. Russell was part of an operation that involved putting U.S. Army forces and forward operating bases along the Cambodian and Laotian borders in an attempt to disrupt the North Vietnamese coming down the Ho Chi Minh trail. His accounts and realism during his time are pretty intenseat times. I would recommend this book along with 'Comanche Six: Company Commander Vietnam' by James L. Estep for anyone who likes first-person wartime memoirs. Thanks!
