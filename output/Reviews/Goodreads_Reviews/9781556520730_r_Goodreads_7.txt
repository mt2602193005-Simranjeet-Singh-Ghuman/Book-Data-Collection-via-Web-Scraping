Worth reading if you’re interested in domestic and foreign politics surrounding Israel. As a longtime congressman and foreign affairs committee member, the author is a candid critic of the Israel lobby in America and what he calls its stranglehold on American politics. The book is chock full of examples of the appalling behavior of the lobby and Israel itself. He is one of the first congressional victims of the lobby , with his mild criticism of Israel leading to smears, intimidation, and of course an extraordinarily well funded challenger that ends his career: Written in the 1980s, Findley is sadly prescient about where this state affairs will lead America, Israel, and the Palestinians. He didn’t live to see Palestinian hopes of self determination realized, but should be remembered as a courageous and principled voice against Israeli atrocities.
