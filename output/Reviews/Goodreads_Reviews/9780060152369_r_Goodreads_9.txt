Read 2004-2006. This book has stuck with me for nearly 20 years. I’ve thought of it often, and have been unable to remember the title until very recently but have stumbled across it thanks to some strategic googling. This is the sweeping family saga you need to read. It reminds me of the movie The Story of Us which I love. It’s about an unhappy marriage but also everything that made those spouses what they are This story covers centuries and is full of heartache and break, just like real life. It’s so immersive and reminds me that every choice has a consequence.
