It’s hard to find any of the scenarios presented as thoroughly researched and accurate as the author declares. While he saw fit to lay out his “conclusions” for the final chapter, no bibliography accompanies the work to verify the veracity of the accounts presented. From my own research, these anecdotes are misrepresented including, even, many of the mythological ones, pulled from obscure rumor, or created out of whole cloth. Still, the writing isn’t too bad and it’s a coherent piece. The only positive to come out of my reading this book was the rabbit hole of Etruscan history I subsequently found myself in.
