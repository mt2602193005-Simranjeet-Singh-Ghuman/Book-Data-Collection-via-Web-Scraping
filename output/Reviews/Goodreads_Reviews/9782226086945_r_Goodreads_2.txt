3.5 I should probably not read two books by Amélie Nothomb back to back! I still love her writing style, her originality, the fact that it is SF, some punch lines throughout the book, the reflections she brings to the table - here, the past, the future, responsability, ecology, politics, the way she judged both beauty and intelligence. But I couldn't get properly into the book. I thought it was a bit repetitive at some point. I just couldn't absolutely love it.
