This is beautiful story to read a bedtime. Luckily it was one of my daughter's favorites for several months so I got to read it over and over again. With each reading I discovered new amazing things. The best thing about this book is that it is not only a lovely story with a comforting rhythm, but it is also a search and find book. On the inside cover are several things that the fairies took from Isabel's room. On the last page is a collection of objects the fairies left in place of what they took. One object from each place can be found in each of the places the fairies visit in the book. We also liked calling the fairies on the covers by name and discussing their personalities. If you are a fairy lover, you should definitely check this book out!
