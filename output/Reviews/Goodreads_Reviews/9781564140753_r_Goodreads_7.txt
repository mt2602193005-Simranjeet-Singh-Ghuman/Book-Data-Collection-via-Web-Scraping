Doesn't answer the question of "How do I study?" It tells when and where to study. The thing students generally struggle with is.. once you're in a well lit room on a chair at your desk with your books in front of you... what do you do? So this book is useless if that's what you're trying to find out. If you are an advantaged student looking for some general tips or if you're a parent with good income then this book may be of use to you. For the great majority of students this book is useless.
