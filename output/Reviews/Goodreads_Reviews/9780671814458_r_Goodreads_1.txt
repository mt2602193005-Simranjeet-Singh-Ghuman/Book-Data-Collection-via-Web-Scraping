The first six issues of The World’s Greatest Comic Magazine in teeny-tiny mass market paperback size. A terrible format for these glorious gems from the minds and hands and Jack Kirby and Stan Lee . Mister Fantastic, the Invisible Girl, the Human Torch and the idol of millions, the ever-loving blue-eyed Thing confront the menaces of the Mole Man and his monsters from the earth’s interior, the sneaky shape-shifting alien Skrulls from another galaxy, the mysterious marvel of the Miracle Man, the return of Namor, the Sub-Mariner and even the deadly Doctor Doom makes his first appearance in the pulse-pounding pages. These are the stories that not only launched Marvel’s legendary first family , and the Marvel Universe, but also ignited a pop culture revolution in the early 1960s. You can also find these stories collected in better formats: Marvel Masterworks: The Fantastic Four, Vol. 1 , The Fantastic Four Omnibus, Vol. 1 , Essential Fantastic Four, Vol. 1 , and Fantastic Four Epic Collection, Vol. 1: The World's Greatest Comic Magazine .
