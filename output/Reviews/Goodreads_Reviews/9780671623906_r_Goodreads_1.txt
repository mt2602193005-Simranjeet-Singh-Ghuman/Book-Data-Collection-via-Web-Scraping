Twenty-minute menus by Marian Burros Tips on how to cook quick meals that are healthy as they are using real fresh items, not prepackaged. More vegetables and grains and some with meats. How to cook recipes for 3 persons where you can take leftovers for lunch the next day. Substitutions that are healthier than some items called for. Some items are special and you will need to get them, read through recipe first before making it. Shopping lists included, no nutritional information but the items used are fresh and very low in fat.
