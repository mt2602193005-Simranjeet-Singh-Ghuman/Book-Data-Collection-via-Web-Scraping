A Cute Madcap Romance Cristabel Swann is a music teacher at a lowly finishing school for girls in Bath when she receives a letter telling her to come to London regarding the Will of her now deceased uncle, Lord Harwell who has lost all his property to a Captain Chase. The shenanigans that follow Cristabel from the time she quits her job (she is sent by Captain Chase) to live at a newly discovered property (Rooms for Rent) which looks strangely like a bordello) to the end of this book (she realizes that Lord Winstoke is man of her dreams and she is sure he loves her as much as she loves him, but why doesn't he offer for her) are hilarious. Barbara Metzger is a funny writer. Recommended.
