I discovered that the many stories of horses that I enjoyed growing up (King of the Wind, Misty, etc) were the interruptions of the author while trying to write this book. But she kept finding out so many interesting things as she did research into the history of horse breeds. So many things are now changed- for example, AQHA is the largest registry in North America by far... Which is such a massive change from being a fledgling stud book in the 1950s. My favourite essays were the Suffolk Punch, the Appaloosa and the Shetland. ❤️
