2 to 2 1/2 stars I did not care for this book. The heroine was spineless and unsympathetic. The author tried to redeem her character in the last few pages of the book, but, frankly, by then it was too little too late. I really liked the hero and felt he was charming, honorable, and swoonworthy. I will say, though, I had a hard time believing the hero continued to harbor strong feelings toward the heroine during their two year separation, or that he would have considered taking her back. I also felt this story fell short in the romance department. The hero and heroine spent only half the book together, then spent the remainder of the story having feeling of animosity toward each other. Not to mention, the ending felt rushed and anticlimactic. I did not believe in this couple's happily-ever-after. Overall, November of the Heart turned out to be a disappointing read.
