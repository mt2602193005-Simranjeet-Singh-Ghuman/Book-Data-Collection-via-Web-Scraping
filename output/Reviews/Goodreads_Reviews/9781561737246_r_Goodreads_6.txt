My Mom got into the cozy habit of reading this immortal poem aloud to us kids - whether when we were Toddlers - or at the time of being the Jaded Grownups we all too soon afterward became, every year On Christmas Eve, whether we liked it or not. And now my bro STILL reads it to his sons by long distance phonecall each year, again, whether it elicits Cheers or Jeers from them! Which reminds me to call him early on this night before Christmas, BEFORE the line is tied up till the Wee Hours! But did you know Clement Moore recited this little gem for Fifteen Years to HIS kids until one of them sent it to the local paper, thus gratefully cementing his Apotheosis up until the era of all of us 21st century sophisticates? So goes the sanitized authorized version, mainly to quiet over-questioning kids - who may have watched the Hallmark movie over the infamous authorship debate, in recent years. But it’s the Quiet Ones you've gotta watch... Well, whoever wrote it, the poem is largely responsible for creating our modern Santa Claus, in kiddies' eyes! And apparently it's the most famous American poem in history. So, on yet another Night Before Christmas - from our little house here in Canada - Long Distance: To all and sundry, You can hear me exclaim, ere I drop out of sight: Merry Christmas to ALL - and to ALL a Good Night!!!
