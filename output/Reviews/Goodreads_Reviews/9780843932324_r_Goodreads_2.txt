Gosh, this wasn't an easy read. You get deep insight into a rural, rather backward society, formed by the "pineys". The characters are interesting and well crafted (Athena, Doris, Steve, Matty). Murder is taking place in this god forsaken hick area. Who did it? The Jersey Devil, as you want to believe in the course of reading or some inbred inhabitant of the area? What about Matty, the retarded son of Athena. Her husband Wally grew up in the pines and never wanted to leave them. What about Ernie? A bit long winded murder mystery with a shot of supernatural lore (Jersey Devil). At some parts very tedious, at other parts very compelling. Sometimes I had the impression of reading a chamber play. If you are looking for a very dark, sometimes a bit confusing novel about something strange going on in the woods you could risk a look here. It's not a quick read, oh no. You can get lost here like in the pines. But the author is highly professional and gifted. His prose can be magic at some parts. The way he describes the characters make you feel with them. At the end I was glad getting out of the woods alive, having finished the book...
