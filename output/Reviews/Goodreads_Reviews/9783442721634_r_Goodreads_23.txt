A French mystery The protagonist, Elise Andrioli, is a quadrapalegic who is also blind, mute and confined to a wheelchair. People start sharing their secrets with her since she is a safe holding vessel. By chance, she hears a disturbing tale from a neighborhood girl about who may be behind some recent child murders in the area. Now she has to try to solve the mystery. Elise shows that despite profound difficulties and severe disabilities, one can be smart and successful. Elise is also the narrator so the story is being told as she tries to understand the information she is hearing and processing it into finding the murderer while being unable to interact in any meaningful way. This is definitely an interesting point of view. There is a significant plot twist about 80% into the story and its continuing twisting turns things upside down.
