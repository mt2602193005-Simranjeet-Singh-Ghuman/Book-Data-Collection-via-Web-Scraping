This book took me by surprise! I love a family with deep, dark secrets, so the Landry family surely kept me on my toes. Ruby is a teenager from the Bayou and if I had to guess off reading, this book has to be set in the 50s or 60s based on the old school beliefs and customs. I wouldn't necessarily classify this as a thriller bc it feels more like suspense-fiction. I loved the Louisiana setting and some of the details are reminiscent of Where The Crawdads Sing. If you liked that book, you might like this one too! This is the first installment of a five book series; however I decided I don't love it enough to continue.
