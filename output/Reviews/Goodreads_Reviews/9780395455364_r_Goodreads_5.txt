Tacky is an odd bird. Yeah, I just don't dig it. Perhaps that's why none of the kindergarteners this year and last seemed to dig it either. Can you sense ambivalence? I tried it last year when I was trying to build my kindergarten readaloud list (so many places recommend it) and it bombed a bit. Same thing happened this year. Yes, I put energy into my reading. Tacky's an interesting characters, one with lots of promise. However, I didn't find the book funny. The plots a little odd. I don't particularly like the other penguin's names (6 year olds don't get the joke) nor did I like reading them in one long breath. That said, many many people love this book. And I'm good with that. I'm just not going to read it during story time anymore.
