Lacey Farrell, a successful real estate professional in Manhattan, becomes an accidental witness to a murder. The dying victim claims the killer was after her deceased daughter's journal, which Lacey hands over to the police but not before making a copy for herself. Placed in witness protection in Minneapolis, Lacey assumes a new identity. While there, she meets Tom Lynch and initiates a cautious relationship. However, when the killer tracks her down, Lacey returns to New York with determination to unveil the culprit behind the murders before becoming the next target. I've read a few Mary Higgins Clark books before and they all seem to follow a very similar format. This one kept me entertained but there were a lot of characters that I had trouble keeping track of and remembering how they fit into the story.
