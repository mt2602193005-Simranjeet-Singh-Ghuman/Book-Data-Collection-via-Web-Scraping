An absolute mess of a book from the clunky pacing to the out of the blue pay off to the libertarian premise. Universal basal income is evil and the only thing worth doing is pulling yourself up by your bootstraps. I checked out around when The Game pulled two people out of gainful employment for the luls and no one complained or did anything at all. How come the parents who could pull strings suddenly lost their strings? How come so many plot threads showed up and blew away? How come we had to have a JK Rowling epilogue? Not my favorite that’s for sure.
