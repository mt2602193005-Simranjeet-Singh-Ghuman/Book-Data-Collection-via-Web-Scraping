2 1/2 stars. A fairly typical historical mystery set in late Victorian England. This is the only one I've read in the series, and I think it is fairly atypical from most of the series. Based on description of the series, a lot of it dynamics come from the partnership between working class Detective Sergeant Bragg and his aristocratic Constable Morton working in London. In this one, Bragg leaves the capital city and his partner to recuperate from a work-related injury in the country where a death leaves Bragg suspecting murder and the local police resentful of his presences and very much against his opinions. There is nothing wrong with it, just nothing that stood out either.
