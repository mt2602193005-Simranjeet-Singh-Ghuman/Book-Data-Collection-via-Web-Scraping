This is a sad book to read, because the subject, Ludwig II of Bavaria, indeed had serious mental health problems. In addition, his upbringing was appalling, reinforcing every action that made him feel like a omnipotent god-designated ruler. These were poor characteristics to have in the latter part of the 1800s in the turbulent Germany principalities. Ludwig came to the throne at 18, never had any impulse control, spent huge sums of money on support for Wagner. As he aged, he became less willing to perform any of the elements of his role, hid in his increasingly elaborate new palaces, and tried to rule by fiat in a country that actually had a democratic form of government. Sad story of a chaotic life.
