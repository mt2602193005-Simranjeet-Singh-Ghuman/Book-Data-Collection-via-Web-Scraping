Short stories set in the West Virginia mountains, of coal mines, coal miners, moonshine, and cockfights. The coal dust is a part of everything, the air that you breathe, lodged in the creases of your face, the very cells of your skin. Catch the waft of wet ashes and vomit that hang in the air, hear the unending drone and buzzing of the blowflies that never seem to go away. Grim and bleak, without hope. These folks are stuck in rut from which they are unable to extricate themselves. Bitter, with a dull acceptance of there being no way out. It is my understanding that these are the only stories from this author that exist. All written before he committed suicide at the age of 26. What a loss. Thank you to Robin, who brought this book to my attention. My library did not have it, but was able to acquire a copy from a college in St. Louis through the Mobius program.
