It's been a long time since I read a book in one day. It took me two sittings to finish this book and I absolutely loved it! I started reading it at the beach, took a break to get back to the camping, shower and eat, and I just finished it. A light, feel good story about love, trust and courage. Warm and well described characters, vividly displayed. A recommendable, enjoyable read! Will definitely read other books by Sherryl Woods!
