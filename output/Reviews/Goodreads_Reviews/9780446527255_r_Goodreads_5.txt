I enjoyed the overall mystery/investigative plot that went along with the romance. Normally tossing a lot of side character names around is overwhelming, but the author does it in such a way that you can follow. I love the personal growth of the main characters; even those side characters get their shine which is a nice touch. My only qualm is the words they use for the spicy scenes, but I’ve noticed these words are pretty common amongst older romance novels. Not worth taking a star away! A good, quick read all in all.
