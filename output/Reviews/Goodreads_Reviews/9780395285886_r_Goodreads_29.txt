I always enjoy Morgan Llewelyn, her books may be fiction but they are well researched in the history, myth and folklore they present that you feel like you are learning something while still enjoying a fiction book. This one in particular, you can tell she keeps to the old religion of Ireland despite depicting what post Christian Ireland is like. I will continue to read and enjoy her books quite a bit. The only downside is I think she is overly harsh to paint the Norse as villains, you can definitely see her favoring of the Irish.
