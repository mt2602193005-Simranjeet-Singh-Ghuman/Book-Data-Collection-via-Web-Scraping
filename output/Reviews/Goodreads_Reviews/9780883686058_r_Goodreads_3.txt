I have mixed emotions about this book. I got it from a very dear friend who is one of the morst spiritul christians I know. She told me how much this book encouraged her. Perhaps this made me expect too much. I'm always sceptical when I book tries to tell me "new revelations and new truths". Who woudn't like to discuss God's plan with angels like Gabriel and Michael? Who wouldn't want to visit God's throne room? Roland Buck claimed that experienced just that. I'm sure he himself believed every word. But I can't help having doubts. These angels don't behave as the angles in the bible. I can't fathom God giving him a paper with 120 prophecies (with concrete dates and names!), but the next day of the paper there are left only ashes. The book sure contains truth about God's love and Jesus' sacrifice, about grace and forgiveness, obviously pastor Roland needed the angels to understand these thruths. The bible says that we are sent to go and make disciples and the Holy Spirit will work in the hearts to convince and translate. It's not the angel's role to bring people to Jesus.
