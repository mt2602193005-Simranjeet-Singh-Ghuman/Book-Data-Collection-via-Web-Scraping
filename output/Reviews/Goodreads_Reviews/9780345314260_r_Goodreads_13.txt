I invariably enjoy the Perry Mason books for good light entertainment. Once again Mason's client is lying to him from the start for reasons that are not immediately clear. The ingredients are blackmail, a car crash, a company involved in smuggling artefacts and of course a murder (the blackmailer). The fake is, I suppose, the female detective brought in to play the part of the hapless, soon-to-be, main suspect. The final post courtroom stage of the denouement begins with his client telling Mason - "I don't know how on Earth you worked it out". Readers will no doubt agree.
