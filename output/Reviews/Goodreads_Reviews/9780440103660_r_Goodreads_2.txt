I recently reread this story of struggle and survival of a young woman and her mother during the horrendous days in the Soviet Union under Stalin. There is a real love story intertwined throughout this tale; not only the love of a Russian movie actress with an American naval officer during the closing days of the war, but also of the love of the daughter in search of her American father. This story is very special to me because the daughter is in fact my cousin... a cousin I never knew existed until she wrote her story after coming to America. From the moment we first met, Vikka has been very dear to my heart. How is it that she, a Russian movie actress in her own right, could be my cousin? Her father, the American naval officer who fell in love with her mother, was my uncle. Now for the rest of the story.... We love you, Vikka Dear.
