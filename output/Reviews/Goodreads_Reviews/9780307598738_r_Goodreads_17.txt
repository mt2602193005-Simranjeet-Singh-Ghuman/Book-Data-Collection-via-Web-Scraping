Little Critter is going to the dentist! He's a little scared of going, like other children. He gets to experience what it is like in the dentist chair, waiting room, and speaking with other children in the waiting room. During the process of getting his teeth cleaned, the dentist noticed he had one cavity, but everyone seemed to make Little Critter comfortable. Little Critter thought it was cool that a grown up asked him to spit in the sink. Then he got a free treat after his appointment. Little Critter didn't mind going to the dentist anymore, he knows that he will just be tired after. This book is such an adorable story to maybe help those children who are scared to go to the dentist. The illustrations are also just as adorable.
