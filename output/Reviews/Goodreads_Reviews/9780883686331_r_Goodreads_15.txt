Smith Wigglesworth tells the stories of daily life as he walked/lived his life in faith at every turn. He truly puts to action Matthew 10:8, Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse those who have leprosy, drive out demons. Freely you have received; freely give. His walk is overflowing with these very things - healing, raising from the dead, casting out demons as a daily part of life. It is a diary more than anything, shared to encourage believers and bring glory to God. Inspiring!
