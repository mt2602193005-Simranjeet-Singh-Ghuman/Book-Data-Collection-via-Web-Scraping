The Morland family experiences ups and downs against the background of English history during the period from 1885 to 1898. It is best to start this series from the beginning to understand just who everyone is and how they are related, so the rest of this is hidden. This story focuses a great deal on Henrietta. Now that she has been widowed, she's free to marry Jerome Compton, only he is divorced, so the church does not recognize the marriage. They move to London, along with Henrietta's daughter Lizzie, where Jerome becomes a stockbroker. There Henrietta meets her cousin Venetia, who has had to fight to achieve her ambition to become a doctor. Back in York, Henrietta's brother Teddy is able to buy back Morland Place from the bank after his brother's death, but he stays in his comfortable house in the city. But surprising changes are in store for him as well. And eventually family member return to Morland Place. Fascinating glance into this era of history. Recommended.
