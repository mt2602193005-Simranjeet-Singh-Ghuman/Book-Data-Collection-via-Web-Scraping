"Full Moon" is a very beautiful photo essay, this book is a collection of images which were taken by the astronauts on the Apollo missions of the late 1960's and the early 1970's. "Full Moon" is designed for laypersons, you don't need any advanced understanding of planetary science to understand or to appreciate this book. The last manned mission to the moon was in 1972.
