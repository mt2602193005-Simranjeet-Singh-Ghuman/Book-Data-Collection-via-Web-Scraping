This book is great! Right from the start, you know something is going to go terribly, horribly wrong, and you will not be disappointed. The main character, Jim, suddenly realizes that four hours have passed, and he has no recollection of that time period! And to top it all off, he seems to have misplaced his hat during that time! So starts Jim's quest for his hat. Jim believes that if he finds his hat, maybe he'll remember how he lost it, and in remembering how he lost it, remember what has happened in the last four hours. This Suspense/Horror novel takes you on a thrilling ride of intrigue and terror, all heightened by the knowledge that the worst has already happened, and may be happening again! Jim's lack of memories strengthens the belief that something has gone wrong, something he isn't supposed to remember, and never lets you down once it picks you up!
