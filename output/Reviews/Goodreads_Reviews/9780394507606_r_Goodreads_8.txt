This book is to thank for my interest in trees and for my goal of becoming an arborist. When I was in seventh grade I picked it up for around ten bucks at 2nd and Charles and suddenly realized how fascinating trees were. I identified the trees on my property first; then I took it to school, to parks, and even started going on hikes just to look for trees I was curious about. Since then I've identified every single species of tree that's native to my region; flipping through the guide, there's a penned X mark on nearly every entry. I aim to become an arborist and forester after graduation and that's pretty much thanks to this book. Five years later, I still carry it around with me on every trip and vacation; it has stickers and signatures up and down the empty pages claiming it as my own. I recommend it to EVERYONE. I even homemade a field guide for my girlfriend using this one's photos and information. It's fantastic
