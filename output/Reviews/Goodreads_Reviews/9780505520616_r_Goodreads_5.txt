This book was bad in a different way. At no point was I groaning about the whining and lack of actual -story-, at no point did I want to stop reading. But it was supposed to be a horror story - woman moves into an old house that used to be owned by an "eccentric" old man whose wife had died shortly after their marriage, and he waited around for her to "come back". Weird things happen, and she determines it's haunted by the old man. She seems to be slowly going crazy - it's revealed that she had a schizophrenic aunt, and had some kind of mental breakdown when she was in college. But the ending... is not what I expected at all. As for being horror, I was never scared at all through the entire book. More like... confused? I guess just the fact that there was a ghost in the book got it the "horror" claim, but he wasn't a scary ghost. Meh.
