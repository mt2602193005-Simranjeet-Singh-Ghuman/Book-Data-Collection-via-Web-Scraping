An incredibly moving memoir that details the intricate relationships of a multi-generational Jewish family in Egypt. I learned so much about recent Egyptian history and was given deeper insight into the continuing global struggles of the Jewish people. The role that British colonialism played in the safety of Jewish communities was entirely new to me and extremely eye-opening. I highly recommend this book to those who appreciate memoirs and Jewish history and to those looking to better understand Jewish culture and experiences. The audiobook performed by Edoardo Ballerini is easy to follow and expressive. He does a wonderful job of bringing the memoir to life by finding the right energy and voice to put with each character. I'm thankful to Netgalley for the chance to review it.
