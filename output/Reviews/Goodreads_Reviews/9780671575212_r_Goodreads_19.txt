2020 This one is one of her better mysteries, in my opinion. I used to think really highly of her work, but lately I've realized that it is very much the same format in all of her novels. There are multiple people who know more than they are letting on. And always multiple people who are out for their own advantages. Plus, the girl always meets the guy and it's basically kismet. But I like this one because the heroine of the novel gets placed into witness protection, which has always been a fascinating subject for me. I also think that she was dumb as h*** for telling her mom where she was staying. Honestly, at that point if it had been a horror movie, I would have been rooting for the murderer to kill her.
