While some of the stories were interesting, I found myself just not really liking the author. She's very judgmental about owners having to put an animal down when they can't afford expensive treatment, yet when she adopts a dog from the clinic that turns out to have behavior issues that she didn't really seem to do a lot to fix before giving up, she decides to put it down. Thankfully, the staff couldn't bring themselves to do it and the dog ended up with a good home with another client. She discusses how the clients in her area are more "committed" to their pets, but I'm guessing it's more an issue of money. I agree that when we take on a pet, it's our responsibility to do all we can for them but the author's judgmental attitude really grated on me. I have another book on my shelf about an emergency vet clinic and I'm hoping it's better than this one.
