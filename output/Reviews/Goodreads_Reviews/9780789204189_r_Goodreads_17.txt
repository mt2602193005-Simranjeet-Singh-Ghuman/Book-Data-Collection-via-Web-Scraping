Nick picked this out while I was pregnant and guess which one of us read it. One of the better parenting books I've read, honestly. I like that it went in depth about what the baby might be learning each month, as well as what you and your partner might be feeling. The tone was of a gently reassuring adult, not lecturing like On Becoming Babywise or teen-magazine casual like What to Expect When You're Expecting . I aim to be a gently reassuring adult, so this worked well for me.
