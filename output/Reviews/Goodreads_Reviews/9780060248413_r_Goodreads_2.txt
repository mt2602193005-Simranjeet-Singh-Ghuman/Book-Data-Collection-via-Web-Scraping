The books describes a family going to a zoo and their adventures while they are there. The two children are typical children who go to the zoo and want to do and have everything. It describes the point of view of one of the kids and their thoughts and feelings about being at the zoo. They want something but have it and its all they think about the whole trip and when they can have it, they are much happier and positive about the time they are spending there. The illustrations are very life- like and support the text very well. While the little girl describes her want of a balloon, a balloon shows up in every picture she talks about it. This shows how even though she is amused by what she is doing the thought of not having the balloon is still very much on her mind. The illustrations are colored very lightly and could represent the light hearted tone the author is trying to establish. overall the illustrations are guided by the text and hardly show any more meaning behind it then what is being said.
