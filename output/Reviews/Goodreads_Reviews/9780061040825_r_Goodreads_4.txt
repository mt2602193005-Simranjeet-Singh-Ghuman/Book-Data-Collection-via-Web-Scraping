Could have been a good book Disappointed with this attempt at a romantic tale....It was so boring at times throughout I almost didn't finish reading the entire book. It is a great plot but incredibly boring as written. I forced myself to plow through the first several chapters. It was dry and at times pointless. There was a lot of good material to work with here. Possibly a good editor may have made the difference. I wouldn't recommend this book...It will put you to sleep.
