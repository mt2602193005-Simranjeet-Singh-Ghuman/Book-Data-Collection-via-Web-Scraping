I picked this up for a research project in high school and found it intriguing, well-written, and wonderfully in-depth. Women warriors are vastly underrepresented in historical study but Blanton and her co-author have made an excellent contribution to the canon. They use a wide variety of primary sources as their foundation, but what really makes the book is the way the authors write. Rather than just dry, basic facts like so many other history texts, the authors instead tell a story. Even if you aren't a big history or Civil War buff, I think you can find some intrigue in this book. Overall, I loved this book and reread it frequently.
