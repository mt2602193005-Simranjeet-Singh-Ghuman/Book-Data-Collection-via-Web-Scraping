I have never read the novelisation as a massive fan of the film and thought it just wouldn't be the same. I was pleasantly surprised that the book adds narrative that the film doesn't require so is not just a complete carbon copy.....although removing For England James was a bit of a disappointment. All in all I enjoyed the way the book has been written and it has lead me to now watch the film as well. I will definitely give the other novelisations a go now too to see what the others have to offer!
