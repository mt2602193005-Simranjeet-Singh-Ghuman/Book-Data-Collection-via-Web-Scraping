i appreciated this book for two reasons. firstly, because diana athill lived a daring life according to her own standards despite what society expected and demanded from her. secondly, because i am obviously a huge fan of the publishing world, so it was exciting to read about real experiences of another person who has spent her whole life working in it. and yes, i discovered a lot of new writers i had never heard of before. hopefully i will get to read books by some of them.
