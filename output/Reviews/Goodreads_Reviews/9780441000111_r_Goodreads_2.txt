In a nutshell: basic and dull. I would like to think that this is simply a product of it's time, but I'm not convinced it was that great even back then. Sure, the ideas of aliens and robots would have been more fantastical then, with less scientific depth required. But this falls into a trap I find many short story SciFi writers fall into. Each story is basically an extrapolation of a single concept that can ultimately be summed up in a sentence or two. The writing is shallow with only the most basic of depth necessary to propel this singular concept leaving the stories unfulfilling and annoyingly simplistic. I have already read an early Robots compilation, many of which are included in this collection. In practice, I left this collection half way through as it simply wasn't enjoyable and my time could be spent reading much more fulfilling material.
