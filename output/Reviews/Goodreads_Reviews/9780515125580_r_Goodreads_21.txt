My impressions as I went through this: implausible but slow to get going, much in common with the later Dan Brown Langdon novels but the treasure hunt was less entertaining and the plot more credible, decent finish but with a credibility level about equal to early James Bond novels, and finally the conspiracy plot in some ways is likely closer to reality than a lot of people realize. In terms of rating it swings from 2 to 4 and I think 3 is a fair rating. The story without the manuscript could be shortened by about a third without losing the 4 stuff but excizing the 2 stuff..
