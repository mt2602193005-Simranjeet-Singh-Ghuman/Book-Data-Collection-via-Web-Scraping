This book is by far a complete waste of time. The premise of the book is to identify differences and often failings of many facets of Canada. The book could aspire to 'Why Mexicans don't drink Molson, by Andrea Campell' but this particular book quickly reveals itself to be a poorly written opinion piece of the worst degree. The overall tone is that of a rambling lunatic who can't seem to organize his thoughts in any objective or constructive manner. Throughout the book, the author uses 'the radical right' as the culprit for any and all problems in Canada. Only on three occasions in the entire book does he mention 'left wing' .vs. the 200+ references to the 'radical right' He lays blame throughout the book and on the back of the dust jacket, (which I had failed to notice or read) is a list of his nominated enemies of Canada, including; David Suzuki, Collin Powell, Dan Akroyd, John A MacDonald, Martin Sheen and a huge number of others he claims are complicit in the betrayal of Canada. If the author made any attempts of satire, I didn't notice any. Had this book been more thoughtful it could have been a much better read with far more credibility afforded to the author. Though I am not familiar with the author prior to this book, it is revealed that he was a prominent publisher so I suspect he may have published this disingenuous & sometimes blatantly dishonest book himself. On a more humorous note; if you read this book as a drinking game; Drink every time he blames the right or the radical right for anything. *please do not read this in one sitting and definitely do not drive, for your safety and the safety of others.
