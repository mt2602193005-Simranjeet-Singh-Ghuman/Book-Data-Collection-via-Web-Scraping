The second book in the series frustrated me slightly when I first read it (way back when through the mists of time) because it introduces a new protagonist. Not only that but the protagonists from book one don’t appear at all and their existence is only hinted at. My ten year old self did not like this at all! Perhaps because I was expecting it this time, my forty four year old self didn’t mind the protagonist switch at all. I enjoyed revisiting this book immensely and can safely say I enjoyed it more this time than when I first read it as a wee nipper. Great fantasy based in British folklore, the likes of which I cannot get enough of! I am, as ever, a complete sucker for a magical quest! On to book three!
