Not only was this poor woman, Alice McArthur, murdered, but an extremely boring book about the case was written and published. I had a hard time keeping track of all the people, the conspiracies, the accusations. The murder victim became beside the point as the police department fought the sheriff's office and one hare-brained conspiracy after another was proposed. In the end I have no idea why Mrs. McArthur was murdered and where the idea originated from. Hard to believe it was Mr. McArthur, but also hard to believe it was not. If Alice had married someone else though I think she would still be alive, or at least not murdered, and she would not have been the subject of the most boring true crime book ever written.
