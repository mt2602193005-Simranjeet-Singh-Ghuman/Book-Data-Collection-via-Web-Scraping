The Lord of the nutcracker Men is a story set back in the First World War. It's about a young boy called Johnny who's being effected by the war (his dads out in the front, his mother sent him away ect.) I really liked how the character Murdoch had escaped the war and I thought that was a really cool twist to the story. The book helped me understand that Johnny's thought on war was just a game as he played with his shoulders while the real men were fighting at the same time. I thought the last couple pages were a little rushed I thought the ending was fine but it would have been better if it was explained slower. But overall the book was pretty enjoyable to read.
