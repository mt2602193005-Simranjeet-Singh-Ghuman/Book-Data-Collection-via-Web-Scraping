I reread this book while in Italy and walking the ancient Roman sites. So much better the second time around 5 stars. I really appreciated all pre- republic history. Really amazing to be reading about Remus and Romulus as you are exploring the ruins of their hut ! Or learning about Caesar while walking the spot where he was assassinated! 4.5 Stars As a devoted fan of ancient Roman history and a great admirer of Tom Holland’s elegant prose, I thoroughly enjoyed Rubicon. Though I mistakenly read the trilogy out of order—having already devoured Pax and Dynasty—it didn’t detract from the pleasure of this earlier volume. In fact, it offered me a unique vantage point to appreciate Holland’s development as a narrative historian. Rubicon charts Rome’s dramatic transformation from monarchy through republic to the reign of Caesar's adopted heir. The depth of research is evident throughout, yet the storytelling never loses momentum. Holland skillfully balances scholarly detail with vivid character portraits and compelling scenes, bringing the ancient world alive without sacrificing nuance. Having now completed all three books, I can say that Rubicon stands strong not only as a foundational piece of the trilogy but as an exceptional work in its own right. It's a gripping, enlightening journey through one of history’s most fascinating periods. I do have one bone to pick with Rubicon- Let's give Cleopatra her due! She wooed two of the most powerful men in the world and changed the course of history!!! More then just a femme fatale, I feel she gets short shrift in the book. I will have to check out the Goldsworthy book next!
