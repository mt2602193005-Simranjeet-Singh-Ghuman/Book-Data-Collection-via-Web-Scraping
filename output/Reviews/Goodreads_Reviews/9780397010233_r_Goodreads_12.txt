I have heard this is a Susan Branch recommendation so I had to read it. True to its title, this is a literary book about the seasons of New England. Divided by seasons, starting with winter, Gladys Taber journals tid-bits about her 1690s home in CT, intersperses seasonal recipes (this book was written in the 70s so imagine very simple foods), the joys of nature and family life. Reading this book was like sitting across from the author, with a slice of cake and hot coffee and talking about the simple pleasures of life. This is a different kind of book for sure, it’s not for everyone as it’s a sub genre of cozy, domestic, simple living, non-fiction.
