Very good This review is for the entire trilogy. At first, I wasn't very impressed. It seemed a bit boring at times, and the writing seemed forced. But, as I came to know the characters and to care about them, it became more intriguing and I couldn't stop reading. The descriptions got better, more intense, more visible, and the story came alive in my mind's eye. Perhaps the writer got better at his craft, or more inspired, but gets better and better. Funny, exciting, life-affirming, absorbing and uplifting, I highly recommend the Silverwing Trilogy.
