This may be one of my favorite novels of all time. The story line of The End of Days is fascinating, and the writing is beautiful. It took me longer than usual to read the 400 pages because I often had to pause to think. The reference to the Dutch Uncle really "rang-true" with me as I was forced to struggle with the truth of signs and preparation of The End of Days and the importance of Clowns. I'm not sure why this shows the novel at 240 pages as my copy has 402 pages.
