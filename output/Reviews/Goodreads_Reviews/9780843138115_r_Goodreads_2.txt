This book was a trip down Memory Lane for me. I was reminded of the songs my grandmother sang to me. We sat in the back seat of our car on daytrips and with my head in her lap she would sing my favorites -- "Six Little Ducks" and "Over in the Meadow." Fast forward to my children -- "This Old Man", "Baa Baa Black Sheep" and "Pat-A-Cake" were favorites. On to my teaching days with "Sing a Song of Sixpence" and "Hey Diddle Diddle." I played "Pat-A-Cake and "This Little Piggy" with my grandchildren. The favorite lullabies were written by Brahms and Mozart. These are just a few of the nursery rhymes and lullabies in this wonderful book.
