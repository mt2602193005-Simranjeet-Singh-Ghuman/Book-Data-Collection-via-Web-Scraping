A lovely book, for it is not just a cookbook, but filled with poems (a lot of James Whitcomb Riley, whose poems I really like!) and family anecdotes from years past-so nostalgic and a slower way of life, cooking and baking for those you love, but preserving food to carry them through hard times and cold weather as well. I had purchased this book for my mother as her birthday gift October 1993 and inscribed it as "so appropriate as it celebrates motherhood and cooking, for you are the best at both"; after her death the three Jane Watson Hopping books I'd bought for her came to my bookshelf. I love the margin scribblings and X's she marked the book with!
