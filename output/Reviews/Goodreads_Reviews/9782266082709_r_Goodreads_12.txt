Blah blah blah, blah blah blah, blah blah… This was not a Hugo, neither was it a Dickens or a Tolstoy. Therefore with all due respect, this book should go and have a seat next to Vanity Fair on the ‘lengthy, garrulous and dreary books’ shelf. It could have been an interesting 300 page book, if it wasn't an almost 600 page one about an ambitious young man who would do anything to climb the social ladder. Add some mind-numbing melodrama and tedious reflections on morality and existential nonsense and there you have it: your insomnia is no more.
