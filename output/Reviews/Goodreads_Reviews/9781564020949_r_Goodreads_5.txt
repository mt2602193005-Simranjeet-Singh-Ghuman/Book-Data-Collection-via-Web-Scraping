Marmalade Ned, Delicate Fan, Pitter and Pat, Captain Bligh, and Shadow love to meet at the end of Tiffany Street to dance. One night a man in a van comes and takes all of them away. All of them except Shadow, that is. While the other cats are locked in the van, Shadow hides under the man front seat. The van drives through the night. In the morning the man stops and puts the cats in an empty turkey shed. Shadow stays hidden all day. At night she climbs to the top of the shed and sings about the cats of Tiffany Street. The man, asleep under the shed, tosses and turns all night. In the morning he lets the cats loose. They all run away, all except Shadow. Shadow stays because she realizes that the man isn't really mean, just lonely. Four months later, the other cats finally get back to Tiffany Street. Now when they meet on Tiffany Street to dance they think of the man and wonder what happened to Shadow. ©2024 Kathy Maxwell at https://bookskidslike.com
