Gillian Fitzpatrick's brother and niece have been kidnapped by a terrorist outfit, who want to get their hands on a serum her brother created that can inoculate people against a worldwide nuclear meltdown. She is recommended to hire mercenary Trace O'Hurley. He's ready to retire, but takes on the job because of the money. Of course, he finds Gillian hot, so that might have something to do with it! He then organises meetings with various people while Gillian demands that he tell her everything and bring her along. This was all a bit ridiculous and Gillian should have driven me crazy with her too-stupid-to-live behaviour, but I enjoyed it. Although it looks as if it was originally published for the Silhouette Special Edition line, this feels more like a Harlequin Intrigue, because of the suspense element. It's mostly Trace brokering a bunch of ammunition deals to get closer to the terrorist leader and Gillian insisting on being involved (in between throwing herself at him), but it's an easy read, and Trace and Gillian have interesting backstories. Most importantly, their conversations matter . They don't just chat about food or what happened in the other books (Trace's triplet sisters all had books before this one.) Indeed, there's a conversation that addresses the ethics of a nuclear fallout vaccination - how would they decide who gets one? Those little moments of depth (well, more than a typical category romance book) give this some extra oomph. I recently read one of Nora Roberts' latest books, and it was awful, but I certainly might check out other earlier stuff by her if this is anything to go by.
