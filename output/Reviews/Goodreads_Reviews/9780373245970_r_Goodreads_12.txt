More 3.5 stars. Three men orphaned as children, their aunt steps in to basically raise them. All are now adults and she feels the need to see them “settled” aka married, before she can focus on herself again. We start with the oldest, who took over the family business at a young age and is basically your typical workaholic. Dated some society women - because of course he did - but his aunt throws a woman at him to help him in business and a potential political run. Polar opposites. We also get a glimpse at the other 2 brothers. One is a former NFL standout, and one is an artist on a farm he bought, basically a recluse after a love gone wrong (we don’t get full details on that).
