I'm rounding up. Things I disliked: - VERY repetetive and just way too long overall. - The scenarios were too contrived. The dialogue was hard to read because it just sounded so fake. - The 90s era slang came through HARD. That's not really a flaw since this was published in 1990... but it was distracting and probably contributed to the dialogue sounding fake to me. (Although I did love the recommendation to give your child a small "edible" as a reward for potty training 😂) - Classifying lazniess and pornography as 'Potentially life-threatening' while animal cruelty was listed as 'unreasonably annoying' in a behavior chart (on the same level as swearing or "bizarre dress"). This was the point at which I decided to just start reading the summaries at the end of each chapter. Overall, I think this book was just too prescriptive. Basically just look for the good in your kids and compliment them! Ignore the bad/annoying behaviors unless they're hurting someone or damaging property. (Although animal cruelty is apparently fine...)
