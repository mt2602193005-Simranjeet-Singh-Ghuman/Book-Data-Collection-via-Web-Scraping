I have fond memories of reading Keith Laumer's tales of Jame Retief of the CDT (Corps Diplomatique Terrestrienne). In retrospect, my appreciation was more a product of the age, an appreciation for the amusing political satire and diplomatic gaffs in a thinly disguised struggle between East and West over who could gain influence in third world nations. In these stories, Retief is a cross between Sean Connery in the Bond films and James Coburn in the Our Man Flint movies. This set of tales is one of the last in the series. While glad to have added it to my collection, I'm sorry to find the humor does not survive well over time, and at this distance is repetitive and off-putting to say the least.
