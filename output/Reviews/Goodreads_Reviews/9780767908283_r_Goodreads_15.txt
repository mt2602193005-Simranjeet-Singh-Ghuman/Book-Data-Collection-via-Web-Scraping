While I'm not entirely sure it meant to be, this was a laugh a minute. Relying entirely on facts, including diaries and letters, it details the absolute disaster that was the famous Bourke and Wills expedition. While there may have been many great men and women in our nation's history, we can't ignore the arrogance, incompetence, and on occasion malice of our 'heros'. This book is a necessary salve for any nationalistic fever pitch.
