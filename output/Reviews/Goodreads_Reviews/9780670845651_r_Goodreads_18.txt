ya know, it was a good story of a bunch of messed up people. Written that way on purpose I'm sure. Our hero, and his heroine, show up here and there and kinda tie something things together while others are left hanging. A commentary about love, about a guy with problems but no real way out of his situation, our hero couple and the difficulty of falling in love, and some other stuff here and there. If you were looking for a police procedural, this ain't it. Since I'm into whodunits, this didn't make it to the top of my list. But interesting characters 'telling' a story.
