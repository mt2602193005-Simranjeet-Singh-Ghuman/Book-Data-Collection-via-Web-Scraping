I have trouble remembering if this was another book from this series I read when I was younger. It seems vaguely familiar but also not. The facts generally seem to line up, though (other than the talking penny Benny, haha), so this is a nice story about the value of saving money. I do have to wonder about Ben shortchanging himself by asking James for only half the money he spent on Ben's food, then only spending half of that on food and saving the rest. Mentally, I imagine James spending massive amounts of money on food, when the reality was probably that Ben found other ways to eat if he was still hungry after spending only a fourth of what James was spending. On the whole, a good book advertising exactly what it says on the tin. Recommended for open-minded younger readers... assuming they can find a reason to believe in delayed gratification (though having a " walk-away fund " is always valuable).
