Trevanian came through! I was ready to quit reading as a whole and devolve into a caveman after the disappointment of my last book, but this has brought me back. I guess I’ll keep reading. This was way different from Shibumi and from anything else I’ve read before. Not a whole lot of plot but it was really fun to read. It reminded me a lot of a highschool romance I had, and led to some unexpected reminiscing. I’m glad I took a break from hitman books with this one, although i suspect my next book will be a return to form.
