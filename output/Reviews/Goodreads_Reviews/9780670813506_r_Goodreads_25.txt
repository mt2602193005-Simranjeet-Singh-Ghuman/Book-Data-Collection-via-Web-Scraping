One of the wildest books I’ve ever read. Totally synchronic mindfuck to the point where the book culminates in the director of the CIA having my name—felt like reading a script to my own dreams. A strange mix of journal, dream, vision, cut-up hallucination, and thoughts on paintings, friends and lovers. I read this a couple years ago and found it both inspiring as a creative (painter, writer, photographer) and also intimidating—a glimpse into Burroughs’ inner world where these things just come to him and he records, deceiving in it’s simplicity and terrifying in the way he strips away at his experience revealing a raw genius. I just found another used copy where I found my first a couple years ago and decided to snag it for my brother, hoping that he too will find some inspiration and education in his tortured writing. There may be some dark passages I’ve blocked out that may be a little much for a recommendation. This book inspired me to start a short story that I’ve slowly been mulling over. Maybe I’ll actually write it. Word / image as virus indeed.
