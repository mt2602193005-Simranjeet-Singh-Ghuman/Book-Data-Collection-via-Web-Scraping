I enjoyed this second in the series as much as I did the first. In this one her old friend returns to town after the sudden death of her father at sea. Even more curious is that he left his boats to the person she suspects caused his death. Everyone in town is still speculating who the father of her two year old is, but Del still isn't telling. When she is attacked, the search is on to find the father, but Merry gets a whole lot more that that. Her other case is the fire that destroys the pier and several of the boats and yachts. Why are the selectmen in such a hurry and in private consultations to replace Town Pier? There is not a lot of time for personal interactions in this but Merry does a lot of reflecting just the same. I borrowed a copy from the public library and assigned this author as our December read.
