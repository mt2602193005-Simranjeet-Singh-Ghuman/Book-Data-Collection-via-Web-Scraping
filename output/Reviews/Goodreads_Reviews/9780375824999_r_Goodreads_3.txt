We really enjoyed these short stories. Perfect for a 7th/8th grades middle school (upper MG, lower YA) music teacher looking for reading to supplement the English teachers' objectives. Stories are from many different POVs, such as: a story about an Abenaki boy by Joseph Bruchac; a Polish Jewish guitar player; a gay singer / songwriter; a Muslim piano player in Ramallah; a violin audition to raise money for a youth orchestra tour; two teens with synesthesia who find each other through a shared piano; and more. A really good collection for the musicians in your family, as well! Looking for more book suggestions for your 7th/8th grade classroom and students?
