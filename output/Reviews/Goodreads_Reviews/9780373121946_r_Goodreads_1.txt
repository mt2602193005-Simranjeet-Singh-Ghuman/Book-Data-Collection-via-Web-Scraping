So, I clearly read this book in English but this is the only version I can find for it so here we go. I’m sorry...what was all the angst about the “affair” that Danie had with Jonas’s ex-brother-in-law? We find out in the epilogue that Danie is apparently a 28-year-old virgin. Can you say you had an affair with a married man if you never slept with him or loved him nor him you? Isn’t that basically...a friendship? Spending time doing things like going to movies or dinner with someone you don’t have sex with or love romantically. That’s a friendship. But this is a terrible book, not just because it was full of bs angst for an affair that never seemed to have actually happened, but also the lack of *anybody* speaking to one another fully and honestly. Oh, and the heroine interchangeably calling her father “Dad” or “Rome” —a nickname from his first name, Jerome that I’ve never experienced before—was weird. She’s supposed to be so close with her family but they all keep secrets from one another about pretty big things and she addresses her dad half the time by his first name instead of as her dad. Right. Super close family. Lastly...the main couple knows one another for about two days time...and really only about 12 hours (being generous) in one another’s company...and they are declaring they are in love with one another. They don’t know the most mundane of things about one another, like if they are morning people, or how the other takes their coffee or if they even like coffee, or if they want kids, or how they handle money, or how they would handle travel and long distances...but they love each other after fighting twice and kissing once. Right.
