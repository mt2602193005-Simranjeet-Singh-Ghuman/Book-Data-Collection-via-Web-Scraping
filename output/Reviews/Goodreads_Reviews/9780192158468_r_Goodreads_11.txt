This is a very hard book to evaluate. Jan Morris is very famous as a travel writer and her reputation stands high. But I don’t think this is a travel book. Nor is it a history book. It’s almost a eulogy to wales and the welsh - it’s all of these things. It’s beautifully written but if you are not deeply interested in Wales it is not for you. I think it is for people of Welch heritage who want to really understand where they came from. If you are looking for a.n amusing travel book on wales or a short history this is not it. And dare I say, it’s a little self indulgent.
