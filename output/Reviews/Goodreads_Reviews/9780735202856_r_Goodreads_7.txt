One of the best books I've ever read. If there is a "secret" to success and happiness this is the closest thing. Psycho Cybernetics is a book written by a plastic surgeon turned author. The author noticed changes in patients life and personality or the lack of changes after the surgery. Patient would become a completely different person after a successful surgery but also surprisingly some patients stayed the same. The discovery author has made is that it is our 'Self Images' of ourselves that defines our life and happiness. It is what enables us to make extraordinary achievements but on the other hand could even kill us. We are what we think we are. If we can deliberately change our thoughts of ourselves and visualize the success/happiness, our subconscious will run in the background to make it happen. Dont get me wrong, this isnt a cliche positive psychology of glass half full. Where you think good thoughts once and life will be sunshines and rainbows. But it is deliberate and hardwork of re wiring our brains to channel our thoughts to accomplish great things. Must read, and must re-read for me.
