If you told me that literary legend Updike wrote a dystopian novel, I wouldn't have believed you because surely I would have heard of it by now. Especially if it was a 'quiet' depiction of an unhappy future, which I gravitate to much more than the war-infused or zombie versions of the genre. However, after reading it I can understand exactly why it remains in the shadows of Updike's career and the entire genre. There just isn't anything good or noteworthy about it at all. Large parts of it felt like a writer trying to produce words without anything to say. Cormac McCarthy's The Road is still the clear winner in the literary version of the apocalypse and Paul Auster's In the Country of Last Things is still the clear winner in literary dystopia. Updike's novel isn't even worth an honorable mention.
