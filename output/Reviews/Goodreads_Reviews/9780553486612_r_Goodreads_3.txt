Good grief this book bordered on being downright awful. I read it to Julia (our sweet foster child) and she loved it but truthfully Julia has been so starved for attention that I could probably read the phone book to her and she'd love that, too. There are only 8 chapters in the book and it is a mystery but you don't even get to find out what the mystery is until the 6th chapter. And by then, I just didn't care. I gave it two stars because of the love Julia had for it while she cuddled up with me when I read it to her. I get to pick the next chapter book to be read aloud. :)
