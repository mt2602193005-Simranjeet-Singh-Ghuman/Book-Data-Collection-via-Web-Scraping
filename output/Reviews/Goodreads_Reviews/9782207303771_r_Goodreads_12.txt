What the heck did I just read? Wow. This is just bizarre. I started the book not quite knowing what to expect (I don't even know where I got it), and I still don't know what I got. It's got humour, sometimes in an absurd Douglas Adams-way, but it's not that funny. It's got ghosts, but it's not spooky. It's got gore, and terror, but it's not terrifying. What shines through the humour, the supernatural entities and spilled guts is a very interesting pragmatism and matter-of-fact way that Disch deals with the material. In that he manages to create something quite unique, without shining in any specific criterion. It's nothing really special, but I'll admit I enjoyed it quite a bit. An unexpected little gem :)
