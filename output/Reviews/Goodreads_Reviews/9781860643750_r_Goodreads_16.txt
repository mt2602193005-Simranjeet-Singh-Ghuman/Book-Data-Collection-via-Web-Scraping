This book is terrible. At least the first pages. I give it another 30 mins. I quit. Unreadably bad writing. Like an undergraduate history paper ... only longer. Hopelessly Euro-centric in its perspective, and executed with a stupefying dullness of style. Though there are moments of levity, at the author's expense, I cannot endure any more. Rather than leave on a low note, let me share my favorite sentence of the few pages I was able to endure: "In addition to the characteristics already mentioned - tolerance, unhygienic habits and frequent drunkenness - the mongols possessed other personal qualities." This sums up the author's general perspective and approach ... and made me laugh.
