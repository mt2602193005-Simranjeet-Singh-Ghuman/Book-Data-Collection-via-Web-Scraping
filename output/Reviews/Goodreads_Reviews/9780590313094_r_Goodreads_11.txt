"The Arabian horse is the only breed with the courage to face a lion." I read a lot of these books when I was young, got read of all of my horse books when I decided I was "too old" for them, and now, as I'm collecting them all again, I'm realizing I never read some of them. This is one of those. What I like about these books, besides the horses, is that, if you've read The Black Stallion, you can really read any of the other books about the Black, in whatever order, because they are all self-contained stories. This one is full of intrigue and drama and, of course - horses. One very special horse, to be exact. It's a quick read, and another spell-binding edition of the Black Stallion series.
