Review of All the Presidents’ Children by Doug Wead: The first of two books on presidential families by Mr. Wead, this book examines the children of U.S. Presidents . Despite occasional errors, Wead insightfully looks at the difficulties and triumphs that sons and daughters of our Commanders-in-Chief have had to endure. We meet successful children like John Quincy Adams, Robert Lincoln, Alice Roosevelt Longworth and TR Jr., the three Taft children, and George W. and Jeb Bush. We meet troubled children, such as G.W. and John Adams II, Payne Todd, and sadly many others. This is an excellent read and a much needed addition to the literature on the U.S. presidency. Rating: 4.5/5
