"The light danced between the water mists, the shadows became dark green curtains. There was a pattern in the movements there. Out of the silence there came to me the faint and myriad sounds of millions of little things of the earth. The silence was no more and the earth and the ferns and the cool, damp undergrowth was full of sound. I understood at once that I must listen to see the fairies. They are part of the sound as they are part of movement of light and shade. They are the rhythms of life, its echoes from profound and unsuspected depths. They are reverberations of that affinity between the human and the everlasting. They are the aspiring sweetnesses of music not known but only suspected in the undiscovered and imponderable depths of the soul." Show more
