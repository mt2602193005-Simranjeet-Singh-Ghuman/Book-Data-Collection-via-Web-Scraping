I have a policy that I try to give every book 50 pages to convince me to keep reading; I was ready to throw this book across the room before I was done with the prologue, but I persevered. I was not rewarded. There is a rape on page 8. The woman who was raped becomes pregnant, gives birth to a set of twin boys, and dies in childbirth on page 9 without ever even having been named. It is heavily implied that the reason she dies is that the second boy born is evil. The rest of the 53 pages I read hold little to recommend them. A very large cast of characters is introduced, but the only one that seems remotely interesting is the elderly "witch woman" who most people think is crazy. There is a forced marriage, explicit spousal abuse, multiple instances of a man threatening a woman, and several prophecies that the characters spend several chapters arguing about while threatening each other and posturing.
