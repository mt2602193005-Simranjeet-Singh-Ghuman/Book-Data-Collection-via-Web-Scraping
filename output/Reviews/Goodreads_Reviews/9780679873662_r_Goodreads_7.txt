This story is about a girl named Thistle Howl who only wants to grow. She is the smallest member of her family. Every morning she drinks a glass of purple pickle juice and says a chant to wish for growth. Suddenly, Thistle's Auntie Bell appears who does a series of spells to help her grow. At first Auntie Bell's hearing gets in the way and a spell is granted to have snow and row. Finally, Auntie Bell gets it right and brings Thistle back to her house to drink a potion to help her grow. Thistle become enormous and asks to be made small. Then Auntie Bell places another spell on her and makes her as small as a bug. Thistle then realizes she just likes the size she was all along. She continues to drink purple pickle juice just because she likes it.
