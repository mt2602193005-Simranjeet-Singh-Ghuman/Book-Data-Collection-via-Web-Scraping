This silly ghost story may entertain children, but I was not impressed. Then again, I am not the target demographic, of course. As stated in the front of the book, "this easy reader contains only 50 different words, repeated often to help the young reader develop word recognition and interest in reading." With large fonts and only one short sentence on each page, it's a fast read. Perhaps it's more engaging and effective with young children than with middle aged librarians-in-training. The illustrations are very cartoonish, with plain white backgrounds, pastel colors, and few additional decorative details to convey the concept of a haunted house.
