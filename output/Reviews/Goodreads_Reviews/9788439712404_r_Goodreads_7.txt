I started reading into Manuel Vazquez Montalban's Pepe Carvalho novels as the great Andrea Camilleri is a fan of Vazquez Montalban's and named his own Commissario Montalbano after Vazquez Montalban, and since the Pepe Carvalho novels are Vazquez Montalban's best known novels. So far I've read three Pepe Carvalho novels but haven't really gotten into them. Havin finished this standalone book by Vazquez Montalban I have to say that I might continue reading other standalone novels by him. Unline the Pepe Carvalho books, this book, its whole atmosphere and storytelling kept me interested all the way.
