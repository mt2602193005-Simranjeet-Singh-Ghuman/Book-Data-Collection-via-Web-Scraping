I never got around to this book when I was reading 70s/80s paperbacks as a kid. I wonder if reading it at a younger age would've had a difference on how I felt about it. I did enjoy the boarding school setting. I know the author probably intended for readers to view Alice as a girl with a lot of moxie, but she was just a hateful brat. Snobby, arrogant, mean, judgemental...I LOATHED HER. She's why it took me forever to read a book I should've finished in a day. I mean, I'm impressed that a book from this era handled a teen girl's bisexual awakening, but even the girl Alice was crushing on was a sniveling rat. The ending sucked, too. Because there was no real ending. It was like the author ran out of crummy things for Alice to say or think about the other girls in the school and just quit.
