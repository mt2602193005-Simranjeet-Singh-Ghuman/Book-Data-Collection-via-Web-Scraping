Unpleasant romance set in a picturesque English village, where two sisters vie for the affection of the sardonic new squire. The heroine is the eldest of the titular "doctor's daughters." She has kept house for her father, her ungrateful younger sisters, and her hypochondriac aunt, ever since her mom died. When she meets the man who inherited the village's abandoned manor, she judgmentally categorizes him as an uncouth and vulgar Canadian backwoodsman. Then, she rejects him as a rake who makes passes at anything in a skirt. The hero plays a cat and mouse game with her, blowing hot and cold, making moves on her, then dating her younger, glam puss sister, remaining sardonic and cryptic until the conclusion of the story, as was the trend with many of these vintage romances. Unfortunately for this modern reader, this made me not warm up to him, or her, so their inevitable happily ever after left me unmoved.
