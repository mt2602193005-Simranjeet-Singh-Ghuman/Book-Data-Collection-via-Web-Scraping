I'm sure this book has some good things to say, but it's very, very, very "churchy" for lack of a better word. I'm aspirational when it comes to hospitality, but this book, while probably very well intentioned and has been held up as the gold standard for books on hospitality, is just too much. It over-reaches on things like helping others and is quite dated with its terminology for the present day church. We need to meet people where they are, not preach at them.
