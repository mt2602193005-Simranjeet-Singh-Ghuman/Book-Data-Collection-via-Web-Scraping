This selection of essays by Manguel didn't quite have the interest or finesse of The Library At Night. There were many chapters invariably dealing with South American politics and political writers/authors, which were interesting to a point but a little repetitive in nature, and the Borges chapter I felt was slightly too long. All in all there were some flashes of inspiration but this is one Manguel that I wouldn't really return to in the future or recommend to a first time visitor to him. Just ok.
