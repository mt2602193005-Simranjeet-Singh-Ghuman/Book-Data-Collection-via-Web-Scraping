This was a rough read. The writing was beautiful and the story was heartbreaking. It follows a young Native American woman, Louise White Elk, on a reservation in Montana as she’s basically just trying to survive in a world where everything is stacked against her. A big part of the story is her relationships with all the men who try and “court” her, and lets just say I’m definitely adding this to my “men are trash” book list. The book occasionally switches to the perspective of one of these men, Charlie Kicking Woman, and it’s crazy how different their perspectives of the relationship are. I will admit I sometimes found it hard to follow the story or the context, and honestly I’m not even sure what time period it is. However, I still thoroughly enjoyed this novel. Earned an easy 4 stars from me.
