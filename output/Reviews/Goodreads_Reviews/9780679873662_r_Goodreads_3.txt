This was one of my favorite books when I was a kid. It means something different now that I'm older, having reread it over and over again for inspiration, whenever I'm stuck. Looking back at it, when I was little, this book helped me put things in perspective. I always wanted to be in a place before it was my time to be in that place, both time-wise and psychologically. As children, we always wish to grow up faster than we are, and when we become adults, we wish we were kids again. This book is about becoming at rest with who you are at the now. I'm happy I had a book like this when I was a kid!
