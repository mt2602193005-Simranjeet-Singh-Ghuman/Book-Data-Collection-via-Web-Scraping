Georgette Heyer, the lady whose impeccable writing skill was/is/still second to none. I’m torn about rating this one: on one hand the main couple was terrific and I loved them to bits but on the other hand, this book has THE most annoying character I’ve ever met in Regency novels. And I’m talking as someone who have endured Lydia Bennet in Pride & Prejudice many times over! Lydia doesn’t hold a candle to Miss Tiffany “I’m-too-beautiful-for-this-world” Wield! I wouldn’t have minded it (her) so much except that she was featured quite prominently throughout the story and with 3xx pages, it got to the point where I had to skim her parts just to keep my will to finish. Because like I said, I wanted to finish, Ancilla and Waldo deserved at least that from me! Oh they were wonderful both as a couple and also as individuals. rating: ★★★½
