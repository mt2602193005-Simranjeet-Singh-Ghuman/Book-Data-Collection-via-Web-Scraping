This is a well-written, entertaining, science fiction novel. It has wonderful characters, wit, mystery, vividly described settings, NYC history, twists and turns, and a satisfying conclusion. I listened to the abridged audio version of this novel, and the narrator, Campbell Scott, does an outstanding job depicting the characters and their personalities, and has a captivating voice. I look forward to reading the full length version of this novel, and its sequel, with great anticipation.
