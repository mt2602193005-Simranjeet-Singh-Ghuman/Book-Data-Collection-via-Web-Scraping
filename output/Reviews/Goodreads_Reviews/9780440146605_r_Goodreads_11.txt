ARC for review (re-issued e-book edition). Very sweet coming of age story of Woody and his interactions with his neighbor, Lady. Some great characters, but a bit too long and drawn-out for the ultimate resolutions (one of which I expected, one I didn't). Worth reading for those who are big fans of Tryon, but don't be fooled by the cover or by exposure to other Tryon books - there is a bit of mystery involved, but this is not a horror or mystery novel, just a nice, small-town story.
