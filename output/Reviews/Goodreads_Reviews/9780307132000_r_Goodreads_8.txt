Illustrator: Mercer Mayer Publisher ‏ : ‎ Golden Books; First Edition (October 22, 1999) ISBN-10 ‏ : ‎ 0307132005 This book is great to integrate since it shows a solid message that "Bullies" should be confronted and not allowed to continue bullying without consequences. At some point in a child's life, they are going to experience a bully and this is a good way to introduce how to reach when you or someone else is getting bullied. In the book it promotes using violence but I think this would add to the discussion on what not to do and how that might make everyone feel in the situation.
