A wonderful novel which is also a pretty horrible example of terrible marketing. The front cover and back cover blurb bills it as a supernatural novel of occult nature - is the child the son of Satan or not? However, the only religious elements of this novel is an overly abusive religious zealot of a father torturing a boy he BELIEVES to be the son of the devil, causing him to develop multiple personalities as coping mechanisms. Granted, one of the personalities IS malevolent and evil, but more because he's a sociopath than supernaturally evil. That aside, it's a terrific novel. My first by Silva, which mean I'll be seeking more of his work out. Show more
