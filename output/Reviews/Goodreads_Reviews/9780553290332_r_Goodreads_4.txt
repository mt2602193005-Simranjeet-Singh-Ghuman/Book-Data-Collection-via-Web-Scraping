A really great read about Denver CO history. The Kirkland family drama took me on a wonderful visual journey especially since I live in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains an hour north of Denver. The descriptions of Denver and surrounding areas was spot on and the family tale kept me hungry for what was going to happen next. Finally a book that actually ties the complete book at the end. I get so tired of reading a book looking forward to seeing how everything ends only to read a rushed seemingly "left hanging" ending. It frustrates me to no end. This ending was a beautiful culmination of beginning to end. I actually felt I had read a complete novel.
