First book I have ever consciously stopped and did not finish. Horrible writing! The book is mostly about Harry Truman. I recently completed Margaret Truman’s book about Harry Truman and McCullough’s biography Truman. Hoped to get insight into his wife but Margaret admittedly preferred her father (good for her!) and it shows. I made it to page 106/410. Her book about HT included anecdotes and personal stories. This book had none of that, that I saw. The stories about Bess came exclusively from her correspondence. Even so, many times, HT’s side of correspondence was quoted with only a passing synopsis of Bess’ side.
