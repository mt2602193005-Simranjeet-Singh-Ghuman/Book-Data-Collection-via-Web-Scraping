This is an older book that my mom had among her books in her closet. I have vowed to read them or at least as many as I can before I checkout of this life (which is hopefully not soon). I loved this saga of a small rural hospital in Montana, and the doctors who were employed there. It was fascinating and I loved the story of the older doctor (seeing himself as godlike and infallible) and the young doctor who could see himself having the same kind of sort attitude. It was a great story of the good and bad that all humankind find within themselves. The story becomes which will win out. Not sure if this is a book that is still readily available, but I thought it was a really great story.
