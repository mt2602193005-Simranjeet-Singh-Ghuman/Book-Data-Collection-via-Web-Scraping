There was a big marketing push by the record companies in the late 1980s through the mid-1990s to push "world music", which is to say, I suppose, "Third World" music, to the USA and other big markets. That's simplistic on so many levels it defies easy explanation, and I won't do it (things like vintage blues, Cajun music, and Irish fiddlers all seemed to fall under this same broad rubric, not not mention all the synthed-up, disco-fied "worldbeat" blends). Anyway, this guide was one of the first attempts to bring some order to this for the music-buying consumer. Its main advantage is brevity. Its main drawback is brevity. The sampling here is simply inadequate, even though the explanatory cultural/historical text does a valiant job at giving context. This guide was pretty much blown out of the water in 1994 when the "Rough Guide to World Music" came out (see that http://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12... ).
