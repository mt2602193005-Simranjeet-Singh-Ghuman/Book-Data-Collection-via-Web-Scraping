Grade: Pre-k and up Summary: This vivid book is a classic story and song that all school aged children know, and if not they will soon. Its colorful illustration brings the book to life and captivates the readers. The song that goes along with this book is full of motions and repetition. The song is easy to remember and fun to sing along to. Most children take the bus to school each day so this is a very relate-able and appropriate song for an elementary classroom. Review: This song is fun and engaging and almost all young children love to sing along and act out the motions. I think it creates a fun and playful environment for the children to just enjoy and be merry. In Class Use: This song book would be great for a transition song to get the kids up and moving, or utilized as a fun way to spend some free time. This also encourages reading when the song is followed along with the book.
