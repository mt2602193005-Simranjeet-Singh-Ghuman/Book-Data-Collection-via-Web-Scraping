I first read some of these books years ago and have just re-found them on Kindle. This one is set at a writers’ retreat and offers a perceptive and amusing analysis of these events, wrapped into a murder-kidnapping mystery. I will go on to read more of these, having also enjoyed other series by Judith Cutler, though I hope Sophie will get her act together a bit in future- she is written as bright and perceptive but failed to spot the obvious with regard to her relationship with Hugh, which was less than convincing. In general these are well written books by someone who obviously cares about written English, so it’s a bit sad that whoever prepared this particular text for Kindle has introduced an irritating number of typos. I’ve reported several using that feature on Kindle as I’m sure Cutler would be anxious to amend them, but it is disruptive to the reading process. Overall, an entertaining and fast paced story, which I enjoyed and which deserves more readers (and more meticulous publishers).
