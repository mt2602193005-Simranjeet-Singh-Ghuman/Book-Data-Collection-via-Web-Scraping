I believe there are angels, and that they appear to individuals, I can assume. No problem with that. However, in this book I read things that seem really implausible to me. It is doubtful that the main angels (Gabriel and Michael) visit this regularly an individual while this has never been the case in Bible stories. But even more, I lost all belief when I read teachings on grace, atonement and especially eschatology, where I should 100% believe it because an angel would have said this.... The message given is in my opinion not in line with what the Bible teaches (the eschatology and doctrine on hell for sure) that is looks like the writer's imagination has gone wild... I do not recommend this book.
