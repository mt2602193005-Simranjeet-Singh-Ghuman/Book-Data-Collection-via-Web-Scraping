Kramer's debut is along the same strains as Tanizaki's Naomi (which the author must have read). This one's set in Japan too, but here the main character is an ex-pat, a gaijin, in Tokyo. Meadowlark, a clumsy, unconfident English lawyer, becomes obcessed with a much younger, savvy and streetwise Japanese girl. She uses him, agitating his natural predisposition towards melancholy and madness. A disturbing read, and a great subject for a gaijin-in-Japan book, but it feels immature and, in the end, dissapoints.
