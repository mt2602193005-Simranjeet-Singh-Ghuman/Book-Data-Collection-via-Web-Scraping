Absolutely classic Norah Lofts, Gad's Hall has it all: history, politics, family drama, shifting protagonists and all the little details of socio-economic realities for each of them. I love that she puts one of them to work writing, pretty much, Norah Lofts novels! The hardcover I have includes The Haunting of Gad's Hall as well-- not my favorite Lofts, as I feel she's painted herself into a corner a bit by the end, but still worth a read as a follow up to the original Gad's.
