I dare you to try to read this and NOT sing it! My daughter seems to like it more because my husband and I sing it together, pointing at the pictures (which are pretty cute, by the way) as we go. Although the style for the illustrations is nothing to write home about, if you look closely at the different characters, there's usually something amusing going on. For example, there's a bratty little girl on the bus that likes to stick her tongue out at everyone. When the bus goes up and down, a woman has difficulty holding on to her basket of eggs and the old ladies open their eyes wide in surprise. I'd like to own this book some day. :) It seems like it'll be a go to for a while (fortunately/unfortunately).
