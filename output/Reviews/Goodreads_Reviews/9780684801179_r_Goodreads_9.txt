A delightful read from start to finish... and despite the book being more than 50 years old, there were times the sentiments of our hero, Si Morley, were timeless. Its dual settings of 1970 NYC and 1882 NYC was the hook that kept me reading... the details were fascinating and endless, and yet stayed easy to read without getting boring. The New York of today and yesteryear were both populated with unforgettable characters, charming scenes and historical facts and figures sure to please any lover of NYC history. A total sci-fi treat with a dash of mystery thrown in for good measure. Maybe my fave book read this year! (Reviewed 10/9/16)
