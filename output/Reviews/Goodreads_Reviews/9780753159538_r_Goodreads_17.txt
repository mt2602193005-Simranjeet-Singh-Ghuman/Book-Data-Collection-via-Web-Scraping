One of my favorite parts about this book was the use of dreams to heighten the fear, and the tactics Koontz used to bring those elements into the characters’ daily lives. The aspect of foreshadowing and premonition was also used through the dreams. Receiving phone calls from a dead family member was creepy as well. God, the mystery in this book was fascinating. The fact that this teenage girl with amnesia appeared to be remembering multiple different lives whenever she was sent back in time in a hypnosis state, added a whole new level to the strange and creepiness of this story.
