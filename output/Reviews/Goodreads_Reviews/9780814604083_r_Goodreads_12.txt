This is an interesting and thought-provoking short book in which Merton encourages the reader of the Bible to approach it and engage it on its own terms so that the reader, religious or not, may truly learn from it. As a religious reader of the Bible, I find it particularly helpful in the way Merton points out the need to put aside my own preconceptions and let the Bible speak to me with its true message. The same it needed for the non-religious reader as well.
