How can you not love a book with a character named Mad Dog? Mad Dog and Annie's love story is a story about moving on from the past and getting the second chance to fall in love. Annie Ross, whose story really begins in The Comeback of Conn MacNeill, is going through a tough divorce from the town football hero. Maddox "Mad Dog" Palmer has returned home to lick his wounds from a tough work experience. When Mad Dog finds out that Annie is no longer married, he sees it as his chance to get the girl that got away. Mad Dog and Annie is the fourth book in the Sweet Home Carolina series, but you could definitely read it if you hadn't read the other ones. I really enjoyed the first two books in the series, but the third wasn't my favorite. I was really excited to read Annie's story and find out what happened to her.
