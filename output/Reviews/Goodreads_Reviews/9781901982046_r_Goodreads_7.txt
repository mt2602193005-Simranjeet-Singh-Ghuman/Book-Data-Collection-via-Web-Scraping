I wasn’t really a ‘fan’ of the whole JFK-conspiracy-theory-stuff when I came across this book, but that didn’t matter, as it’s a great read. As a thriller, it works really well – believable characters, well thought- through and gripping plot (an over-used phrase, but I genuinely couldn’t put it down), and a locale that one wouldn’t associate with a conspiracy thriller (Hertfordshire, UK), but is totally real. I am not sure if this provides ‘closure’ on the JFK story, but go along for the ride and don’t be too analytical, as it’s a great story from an under-rated and under-known author. © Koplowitz 2005
