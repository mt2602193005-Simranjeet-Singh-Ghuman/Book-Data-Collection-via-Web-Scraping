Genghis Khan: Conqueror of the World by Leo de Hartog. 2/5 rating. Book #101 of 2019. Read August 22, 2019. It was kinda interesting to learn about a world leader who had a huge impact on Asia and Europe, but this was purely factual, no narrative at all apart from the fact that it went chronologically. This made it a slog to get through and not too enjoyable.
