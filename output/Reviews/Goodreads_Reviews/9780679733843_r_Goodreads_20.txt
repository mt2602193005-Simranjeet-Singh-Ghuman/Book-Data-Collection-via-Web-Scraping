What I take away above all is that revolt, however legitimate, cannot lead to or be reduced to blind vengeance and the mere expression of resentment, lest it contradict itself by fostering the advent of a totalitarian order that is the very negation of the freedom it claims to uphold. By discarding religion, which attempted to provide a long-term solution to make this world, founded on iniquity, acceptable in a hypothetical future, humanity prides itself on becoming God-Man. To this end, it elevates the imperatives of History (with a capital H) to an absolute principle, subjecting humanity to its rational logic, which is, however, only a part of reality and refers, once again, to an Elsewhere embodied in a fantasized Future. It thus transforms ideology into a new religion, or vice versa. But reality is fluid, hypothetical, relative, and can never be contained within an absolutely rational logic. “Measure, in the face of this disorder, teaches us that all morality must have an element of realism: pure virtue is murderous; and that all realism must have an element of morality: cynicism is murderous. This is why humanitarian rhetoric is no more justified than cynical provocation […].”
