This is another alphabet book that would be good for younger elementary school kids. It would help them learn their ABC’s and associate words that start with the letters of the alphabet. This book is about Miss. Spider and her other insect friends. Each letter has a sentence and within the sentence each word starts with the letter that is being talked about. Activity: I would use this book to start talking about insects to students. Once my students have a grasp on the alphabet then I will start to read this book each day. We will discuss the letter and the insect and then I will get the students to write me a story about the insect we learned about that day.
