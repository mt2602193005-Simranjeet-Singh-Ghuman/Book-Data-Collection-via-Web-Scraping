My first by Dean Koontz. I have mixed feelings about this one. On one hand it's eerie and on the other characters are too good to be true. They seem to always be understanding and seem to be a success no matter their upbringing or situations, a little too goody good for my taste. Most of the novel is shrouded in mystery. Keeps you trying to understand what is exactly going on and why. This had some good plot ideas. I enjoyed it on the whole. The ending was too abrupt, would have liked a little more closure. I liked the eeriness and mystery of the whole thing.
