I am somewhat sentimental about this book as I have had it since I was a kid. It is a good story, but really wordy and a tad boring for a younger kid, although Marcos seemed to enjoy it and wanted to read it several times. The black-and-white pictures are simple but detailed enough to be intriguing and are different than what you would see in most children's books. Iglook is also sentimental and cannot fathom killing a baby seal so he convinces his father to let him keep it. His father thinks Iglook will never be able to feed his seal, but Iglook soon discovers that Silak is more than capable of feeding himself and the rest of the village. Show more
