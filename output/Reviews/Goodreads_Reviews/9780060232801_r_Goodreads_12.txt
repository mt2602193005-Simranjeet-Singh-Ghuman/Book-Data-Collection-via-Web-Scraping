Who doesn’t like a good book about hot dogs and guys who sell them? Stan retires from his job at the bakery and embarks on his life’s dream—to be a hot dog man. He buys a hot dog truck and goes out to make the world a better place by selling hot dogs. It’s clear that Stan is not in it for the money—he gives away free hot dogs to community helpers and creates impromptu sales to help out a family in need while preserving their dignity. The only instance of Stan’s foolishness is when he goes out to sell hot dogs in a snowstorm, but even then he “happens” to be in the right place at the right time to give aid and comfort to school children stranded in the storm until the diligent snowplowers come to save the day. In the end, Stan is just a regular guy doing what he loves and meeting the needs of others. We could all stand to be a little more like Stan! This entire review has been hidden because of spoilers.
