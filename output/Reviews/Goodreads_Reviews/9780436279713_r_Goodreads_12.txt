While aware of Michener for many years, I’d not attempted any of his works as historical fiction is a lesser interest for me. However, 10 days in the “Spanish Lake” were the perfect setting for this one. His blend of deep research and weaving together historical events across several islands made for both interesting and satisfying reading. Spanning over 600 years, he made both famous historical characters and those of his own invention deeply engaging, overcoming the limitations of character development within a single chapter. While a more consistent through line would have been an improvement for me, the themes of competition among nations, the hugely negative impacts of slavery, and racial / national identity brought together a story with both an enjoyable conclusion and real-world lessons
