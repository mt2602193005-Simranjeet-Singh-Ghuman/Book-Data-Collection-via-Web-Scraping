A really interesting behind the scenes look at the 1991 Chicago Bulls season. I really enjoyed reading about the turmoil between all the players and how they were able to (spoiler alert) win the NBA Finals despite the fact that most of the players did not get along/like each other. Also very interesting to read about Jordan and his general attitude to basketball/people and to realize how different he is. I gained a new appreciation for Phil Jackson and how his job was a lot more difficult than he probably gets credit for. One of my favorite books I’ve read in awhile!
