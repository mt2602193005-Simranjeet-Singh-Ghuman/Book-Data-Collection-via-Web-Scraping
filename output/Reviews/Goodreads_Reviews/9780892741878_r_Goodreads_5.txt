I have mixed feelings with this book. God can heal us instantly and you should pray for that. Yet, we do suffer trials and sometimes we have lifelong afflictions that are NOT going to be healed on this side of heaven. He did not heal Paul's affliction. We are strong in our weakness, through Jesus Christ. What are we learning in the midst of the pain? Most of the time that is where we are closest to God, and that my friend is worth the sorrow....that turns to Joy! This book, in my opinion only points to belief in complete healing with many, many bible verses pulled out of their context. I wanted a book that gave me inspiration to thrive in my constant chronic pain, this book ONLY focuses on the actual healing. Does this make sense? :/
