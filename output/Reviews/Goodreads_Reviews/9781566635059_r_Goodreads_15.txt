This may be a better self-help manual than Jordan Peterson’s books by showing you what not to do, what not to emulate, and how not to live your life. Dalrymple is a masterful writer, laying bare the degeneracy of modern Britain (really, the modern West) through personal examples and wit. The sheer depravity of some people made me cringe. -1 star for not taking into account genetics. Yes, modern academics destroying all pro-social bonds and making all good and bad into “tolerance of all” has led to the decadence of modern culture displayed in this book. But there is more to the equation. See Sarraf et al. 2020 for a most illuminating perspective ( https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/5... ). Closing our eyes to the fact that we are biological beings with genes will do us no good, and just lead us to more folly.
