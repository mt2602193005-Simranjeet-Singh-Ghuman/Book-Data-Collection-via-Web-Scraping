I am in awe. This might be the best book I've read this year. It might be one of the best books I've ever read. If you like epics, this is for you. Romance, intrigue, artistic expression, mystery, combat scenes, sex, violence, passion, compassion, bitter revenge, redemption. It's all here. This book was what makes fantasy great. It is what makes historical fiction great. A perfect blend of the two, with very human elements there to give life to the characters. The reader is constantly on the edge of his/her seat, and the payoff is in spades. I loved Tigana , my first experience with Kay, when I read it last year. This tops it. I don't know what else to say without spoiling anything. I will say there was only one thing I didn't like about this book: that the experience had to end.......
