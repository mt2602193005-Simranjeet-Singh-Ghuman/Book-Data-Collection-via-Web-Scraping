I have such nostalgia for this series. Imagine my surprise & delight when I found out there are *40* books in the series. I only read the first 3 or 4 the first time around. Under normal circumstances, I'd give this book 1 star because all of the characters are judgemental mean girls (yes, even the boys). But this is like a comfy soap opera that middle-school me LOVED. There's the Insecure Virginal New Girl who finds herself in love with the Sophisticaed Bad Boy...who has a girl friend hidden away at board school! The Beauty From the Wrong Side of the Tracks who wants nothing more to escape to glamourous NYC...but she finds herself hopelessly drawn to the Hometown Hunk (who's a garbage man, no less)! The Straight Up Unapologetic Bitch who's planning revenge on everyone! The Aloof Mystery Girl with a ~tragic past and a domineering mother! It's got all the tropes!
