Set in 1893 Dorset where London Police Sergeant Joe Bragg returns to recuperate with family after a knife wound and encounters two murders in a small town where the local police are very antagonistic towards the city plod. The harsh realities of farming life as well as the slower pace lend much to the narrative as does early-40s widower Bragg and realistic characters like attractive spinster Fanny and devious cripple Ernie. The solution relies on speculative leaps like tracing baggage from a decade ago, but it reads smoothly
