[3 stars = I liked it and may read again] Bahzell, the hradini barbarian has to find his way in service to Tomanek the war god. The plot is interesting, but it is not terribly suspenseful. At each step of the way, the next step is relatively clear. There is no long development for the villains, so one becomes familiar with the protagonist and his allies, but villains and enemies are introduced on an as needed basis, kind of like the wizard of Oz. There is a lot of discussion about the whys, but the book states them baldly instead of allowing the reader to develop a theory of why based on varied sources of evidence. As a result, it reads more like a journal or history of what happened than an engaging work of fiction. Despite this, the world, its people, and its history are interesting, so I'm likely to continue the series.
