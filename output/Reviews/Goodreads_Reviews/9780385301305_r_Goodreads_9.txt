A worthy Gold Dagger Winner in 1990. Dalziel plays God in the York Mysteries as he tries to convince his colleagues that Swain a builder is a murderer. Chung the director of the Plays convinces him to play God and Swain gets cast as Lucifer. In addition, a woman is sending letters indicating she will commit suicide. Pascoe is tasked to investigate the letters. As the story progresses the body count rises. Swain is able to convincingly twist the circumstances and leave Dalziel frustrated. This story has so many twists especially with the identity of the letter writer. SPOILERS AHEAD Swain’s wife a wealthy American heiress turns out not to be the dead woman who shot herself. Instead she is a drug addict blackmailing Winterton. Swain killed him as he was likely to blab and buried him, and Swain’s wife in the police car park his firm was redoing. He also killed his partner Arnie Stringer who thought he had accidentally killed his son in law where in reality Swain did and then killed Arnie to stop him confessing. Thanks to bats urine Pascoe provides the evidence to convict Swain. Three women who Pascoe thought were the suicide letter writer were not. Instead it’s Chung the pageant organizer.
