I feel like I owe this book a review because I'm not going to rate it well. However, it's not because it's a bad book. It's more that it's a book that I couldn't get into. This book has detailed accounts of various historical battles, and what took place within them that was a strange factor which entirely changed the outcome of the battle. I have a hard time with history in general. I couldn't get into this book, because I didn't know most of the battles, or the people who where being referred to in them. I felt the author could have done a better job with describing who was who - sometimes I was unsure which side a person was fighting for/against. Anyways - if you are interested in history, and battles, and want a little bit more information and some interesting sidenotes in history, I would definitly recommend this book.
