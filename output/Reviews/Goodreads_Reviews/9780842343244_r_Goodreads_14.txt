Book #2 in the Left Behind Series This is the second book in the series and the saga continues with the four main characters who are left behind after the pre-trib rapture takes place. The four are Raymond Steele, an airline pilot, Chloe Steele, Raymond's college-age daughter, Cameron "Buck" Williams, a young hot-shot reporter, and Bruce Barnes, a pastor who didn't take faith seriously until after the rapture. Together these four form the self-described Tribulation Force as they prepare for the seven years of the Antichrist's rule.
