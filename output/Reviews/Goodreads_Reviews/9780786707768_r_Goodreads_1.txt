One of those one-hit-wonder authors who delivered brilliance and then disappeared. This has all the anger and atmosphere of No Country For Old Men, mixed with the bittersweet philosophy of James Lee Burke’s Robicheaux series. A wildly violent roadtrip which introduces the best Texas Ranger I’ve yet met, and a trio of ne’er-do-wells, trembling on the edge of redemption. Seek out an old copy: it’s worth the effort. 9/10 There are more reviews of (many!) other crime-thrillers over at http://www.murdermayhemandmore.net
