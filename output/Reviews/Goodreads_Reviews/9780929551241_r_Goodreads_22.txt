This was an interesting read. My husband and daughter may be dyslexic and we're learning more about it right now as our daughter is getting older. I know that there are many different types/symptoms of dyslexia and I felt this book mostly focused on one type (probably that of the author), which is fine. I will probably find other books to read as well. The things I found most useful/interesting were the descriptions of the way dyslexics think and the part I found least useful was his method for helping these types of dyslexics (the second half of the book).
