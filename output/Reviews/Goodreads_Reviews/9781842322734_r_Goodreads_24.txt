If you’ve never read his books, you need to give them a try. Many were written in the 1940’s. The characters are always interesting and some are very quirky. This one was about an airplane crash (in the 1940’s) and some aircraft researchers’ dilemma in proving that it was a structural problem rather than pilot error. Throw in some animosity between England and Russia to mix it up. I had to look up the word planchette to understand that it was basically using an ouija board.
