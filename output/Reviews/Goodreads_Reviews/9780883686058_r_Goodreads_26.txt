I'm presently writing the second book in a fictional series entitled, Angelic Army Conquests, and read Angels on Assignment for research purposes. I found Roland's accounts of his Angelic encounters the most interesting parts of the book, especially when he found the high ranking angelic waring angels had come to visit his home one night and were directing warfare from his living room. Arch Angel Michael suddenly showing up was a great surprise. He's an angel that is featured in my books. Gabriel is as well. All the angels Roland encountered directed him to the truths found in the Bible, and gave him a better understanding of the scriptures. They also told him of future events, and gave him specific names of people he would meet to confirm the genuineness of the visitations. They focused his attention on Jesus, the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world, and worked in conjunction with the Holy Spirit. Overall the book encouraged me that God is on the job. His angels are watching over us, and we do not need to be afraid. It was a great resource for research, and confirmed much of what I already wrote in my fictional books.
