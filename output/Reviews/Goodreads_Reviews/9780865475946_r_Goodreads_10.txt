I just finished this fine book. Laying on the couch. 10:41pm. So good I had to stand up, walk to the bedroom to get this computer, and type out some kind of review. Nevada is as much a character as the writer's family, as much as the Owyhee, as much as Mountain City. Cold, strange, desolate, hints of the world in between the Shoshone and the settlers, between cellars and air conditioning, between a general store and a Target. The writing is just superb, stunning. If this book was a thousand pages long, I would have just kept on reading. About mice, coyotes, snow drifts, Mel's jokes, and the general store that kept the story grounded. Thank-you, dear Gregory Martin, for captivating this here reader for many nights on the couch before bed.
