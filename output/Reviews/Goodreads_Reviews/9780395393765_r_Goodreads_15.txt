The book "Jamaica's Find" by Juanita Havill is a Children's picture book. The book was about a Jamaica finding some items left at the park. She turned in a hat, but held on to something else she liked. She took it home and her mother explained that some child might be looking for it. I gave this book 4 stars because the message of the book was good. (If you find something you should always try to find the owner if possible because you don't know what it means to them). I would read this book to my students so they get the right message. It will teach them responsibility and the right thing that should be done. I think this book will be appealing to children because of the puppy on the cover.
