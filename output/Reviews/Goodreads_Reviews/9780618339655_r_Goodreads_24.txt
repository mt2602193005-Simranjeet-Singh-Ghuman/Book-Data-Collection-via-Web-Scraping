Beautiful descriptions (and i usually feel like i'm slogging through a novel if it has this much descriptiveness about objects and places) and a great array of voices tell the story of a murderer and his crime and the place where it happened and the people it happened to. Compare this Irish novel of murder with Flann O'Brien 's The Third Policeman and John Banville 's The Book of Evidence . Highly recommended for anybody who loves good writing; anybody who wonders not only what madness might be like but what it might be like to have a madman among your people. This book deserves a much better overview, but i don't feel up to the task. Just read it!
