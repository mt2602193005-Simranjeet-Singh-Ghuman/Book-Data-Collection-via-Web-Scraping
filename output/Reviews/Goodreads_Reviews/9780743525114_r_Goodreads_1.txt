Solid 4-Stars. Larry McMurtry's Sin Killer (The Barrybender Narratives, #1) is packed with wonderfully exasperating, sharply funny characters whose flaws are half the entertainment. The hilarity begins right out the gate as the Barrybender clan begins bumbling their way through the American frontier with the aristocratic cluelessness of people who've never had to understand the world they're in. All throughout, I kept laughing at their grand declarations, obliviousness to danger, and the way practically every character around them reacts with weary disbelief. It felt like I was observing a traveling comedy of manners crash into the wilderness. The humor works because McMurtry never forces it-I just kept stumbling into moments where personality, pride and sheer nonsense collided in the best possible way. Narrator Alfred Molina does an extraordinary job in providing both male and female characters with their own separate and unique voice. It's hard to believe he was the solo performer. Thank you for the flawless listen.
