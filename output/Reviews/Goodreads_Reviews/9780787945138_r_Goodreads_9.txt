It's odd how people assume you are a Rand fan when you read her work and other books about her. Rand was guilty of this herself. I find her and Objectivism interesting but flawed, in its one dimensional and limiting emphasis on individualism and selfishness. Reading her work brings your values into focus. I've heard various politicians, pundits and educators employ Objectivist principles to justify their positions, without acknowledging the source. Some, like the writers for the TV show, "Mad Men"and politician Paul Ryan have admitted Objectivism, Rand and Branden are their inspiration. I'm not certain, though, to what extent Rand and Branden are still the primary source, or whether it makes any difference, but, to our detriment, the beliefs of Objectivism still influence business practices and the Republican party in particular.
