Mostly classic ham and beans "mysteries" everyone is familiar with (Bermuda triangle, Benjamin Bathursts) but with an appealing ly skeptical approach and a few deep cuts sprinkled throughout. Some of these cases are difficult to find info on anywhere else. The citation style in this book is nonexistent so some cases I wasn't able to research further at all. Probably some of them are just made up. Apart from the lack of citations I also would have appreciated a bit more context or I depth discussion especially in cases from historical texts. My favourite story was the one about the Bristol merchant who dropped his knife into the ocean while at sea off the coast of Ireland, only for it to fall thru the open window of his wife back home. Spooky right? When you look it up apparently back in that time it was written they believed that the sea and the sky linked up in a moebius strip around the earth.
