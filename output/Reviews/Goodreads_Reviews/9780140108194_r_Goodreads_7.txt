This is a very free form book where I feel you absorb it rather than read it? Does that sound pretentious?? Throughout there’s a lot of use of poetry, litany or psalm inspired writing, and prose. You also don’t really ever know what’s going on, but rather strung along on a crazy ride of the present and past of the 7 women who live in the hotel. The rollercoaster of events doesn’t take away from the impact-fulness of the writing. The major theme is the words and phrases that women have said to them and say to each other by their friends, mothers, fathers, strangers, uncles, the media etc etc etc. I’m interested to dive deeper into this genre of writing, however I will say I had to put this down for 2 weeks bc of travel, and getting back into the last 50 pages was not easy because of the writing style. Recommend reading.
