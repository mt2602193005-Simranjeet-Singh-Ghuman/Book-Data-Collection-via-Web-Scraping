This book came up in recommendations from a couple of my favorite authors. I had never read anything by this author so I thought I'd give it a try. It took a little while for this book to suck me in, but by the end I liked it very much. The world-building caught my attention and while the plot was a little predictable, it was predictable in the direction I usually enjoy, so it didn't matter. I'm happy now that I have another book by this author to try.
