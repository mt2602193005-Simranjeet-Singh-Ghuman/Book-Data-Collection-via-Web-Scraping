Malina is an anagram of ‘animal’… The novel is an extremely complex and grievous, but somehow poetic, tragedy on the theme of insanity… Time and space… Heroine of the story has problems with time… And she has problems with space… …my blood pressure begins to rise and at the same time the tension begins to fall, the cramps which attack me in unknown places abate, and although I keep walking faster and faster, I finally attain a happy, almost urgent tranquility. I don’t get sick the way I do with Time, although Time and Place suddenly converge. Mind and reason… Heroine has problems with her head… She thinks she abides in the special locus… The tremulous anxiety, the high tension hovering over this city and presumably everywhere has almost completely abated here, and schizothymia, the world’s schizoid soul, its crazy, gaping split, is healing itself imperceptibly. Her identity is split… Her reality is in fragments… She thinks she is a writer… And she is writing her life… And she is writing the universe… And her world is in her head… A day will come when all women will have redgolden eyes, redgolden hair, and the poetry of their sex, their lineage, shall be recreated… The mind distorts reality and if reality gets distorted too much one goes crazy.
