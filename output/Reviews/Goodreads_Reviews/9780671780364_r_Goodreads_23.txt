I found this to be a quick and entertaining read since I am interested in Nixon as a character. Not a significant book in the grand scheme of things imo, but it was interesting to see the roots of our polarized, low-quality political discourse in the Nixon campaign strategy. Crazy focus on tokenizing POC while also using racist dogwhistles in the South. Some of the people working for Nixon had a great sense of humour in describing the impossible project of making him palatable - "it's like trying to grow grass on the moon." This is one of many insults I will remember fondly.
