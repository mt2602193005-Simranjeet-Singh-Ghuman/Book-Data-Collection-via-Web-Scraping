"Unfinished Business" is the story of Vanessa and Brady. A sweet, slow-burn, second chance romance in which our successful heroine returns to her hometown twelve years later to confront her past, which includes an absent mother, an ignored best friend, a close acquaintance and a deceiving lover- but is forced to re-evaluate her existence when the truth reveals itself. Comfortable read with likable characters, an adorable dog, angst and revelation drama, followed by lots of introspection, self-discovery and HEA. Enjoyed it. Safe 4/5
