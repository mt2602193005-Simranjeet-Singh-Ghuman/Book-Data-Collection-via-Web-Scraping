This book has made me feel empowered to speak and interact with a children in a Christlike way. It has inspired me to fill my home with love and happiness. The parenting style feels "right" to me. It's not sarcastic or coercive; it's a way to parent with love and respect. I do have one disclaimer: in the beginning of the book there are a few places where someone who does not believe in a "traditional family structure" may feel offended. I felt the language was harsh and the same things could have been said with more love. Other than that, I would recommend it to any Christian.
