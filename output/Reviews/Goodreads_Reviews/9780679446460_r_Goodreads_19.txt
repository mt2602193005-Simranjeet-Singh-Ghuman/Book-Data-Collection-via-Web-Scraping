Take three parts Ghost World ; dash in a quarter helping of Welcome to the Dollhouse ; shake very liberally; and serve over iced tropes of teenage ennui with an existential wedge for garnish. Had Williams let a judicious editor prune just 20 percent…well, what could have been. What IS is much fun drawn over too many pages. Nothing less than enjoyable, anything but original. Hey, shit, high class pulp gets Pulitzer nom; no one need heed me. (Just try to read this fucking thing without imagining Brendan Sexton III as Rick. Go.)
