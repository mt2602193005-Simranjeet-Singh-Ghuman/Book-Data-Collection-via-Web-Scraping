I first came across this book many years ago (1999-2000) while working at a library. It became one of my favorite books to check out due to the CD in the back. Sadly, after a couple of months, someone returned the book without the CD, and I have never heard the music since. However, I still to this day sometimes sing one or two of the songs (My favorite is the song about the island of the mighty. "Then Matholwch misused the Cauldron, fed into it parts of the dead, and from it came demon warriors, that all of the mortals did dread...")
