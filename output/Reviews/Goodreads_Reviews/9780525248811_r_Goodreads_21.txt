Nemesis is a novel about an incident at a distinguished music conservatory where a young student was sexually assaulted by one of the school's composers-in-residence. The aftermath of the incident results in a bloody trail of bondage and death. You know, it's an Oates novel. The thing that bothered me all the way through Nemesis was trying to figure out if I was reading a transgressive noir or an average paperback suspense potboiler; it had that kind of tacky sheen. In addition, the protagonist, Maggie Blackburn was such a dull character I couldn't identify with her at all. The creepiest thing about the book was Oates using the pen name of Rosamond Smith. Her late husband's name is Raymond Smith.
