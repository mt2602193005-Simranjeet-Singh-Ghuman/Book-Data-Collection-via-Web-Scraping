one of my favorite for this year!! this book addresses a number of emotional issues, the book is a wonder of writing, a demonstration that no matter how brief the storytelling it can get the idea across. I did find it surprising when the protagonist is revealed to be a ten year old girl and child because i assumed she was supposed to be a teen! This is a good book for teaching kids and also teenagers the value of responsibility, caring for others, and selflessness. # VERDICT: ( 7.7/10)
