Sci fi mystery for kids which I enjoyed. There are villains, naturally, but it's full of goodness and hope. Puck makes friends with a being very different from herself, which helps heal some of the hurt she has from a school where she was bullied by other girls and misunderstood by staff. She has deep compassion for him and his people. She also discovers gifts she never dreamed she had. I would say it's good for 10 and up. We are told a few times that Puck uses bad words, but not what they are. I find this amusing - also you don't have to worry about your kids copying them. Presumably we wouldn't recognize them as bad words since this is set in the distant future. Puck's expression for something amazing is "Absolute Zero!" I would enjoy another book about Puck, but I don't think there is one.
