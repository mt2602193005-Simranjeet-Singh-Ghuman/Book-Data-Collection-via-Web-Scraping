My late aunt gifted this book to me when I was a very young child. I still love and adore it to this day; it made the cut as I trimmed my bookshelf. All of the Tacky books are wonderful, but there's something about this entry that makes it my favorite. It's probably because it was my first introduction to Tacky the Penguin, but I also simply adore the illustration and text that read, "Tacky looked, well... tacky." God bless you, Tacky. If my patronus from Harry Potter was a children's book character, it would be Tacky the Penguin.
