Volume two of this series is just as bawdy and perhaps more violent than the first. However, there’s also a fascinating love story developing between Jim Snow and his new wife, Tasmin. Why has someone of her station become attached to this independent, seemingly untamable man? Likewise, what is it that draws him to her? It’s fun watching them try to fit in and adapt to each other’s worlds. Tasmin’s quick acceptance of his Indian wife was a surprise. The introduction of numerous other characters continues to expand upon the idea of a shrinking West. Favorite quotes: “You will just have to get used to the inconveniences, Papa. You’ve strayed into a democracy – a great mistake from your point of view, I’m sure. The citizens around here are rather determined to do as they please.” – Tasmin Snow (nee Berrybender) “Which was better: freedom with its risks, or the settled life with its comforts?”
