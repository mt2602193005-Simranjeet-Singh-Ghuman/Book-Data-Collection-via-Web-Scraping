The Murder on the Links by Agatha Christie is Hercule Poirot #2 ! Hercule Poirot receives a distressing letter from South American millionaire Monsieur Paul Renauld beckoning the Belgium detective to come to his aide at his home in France. Sensing the urgency of the request, Poirot and Hastings leave immediately. Upon their arrival, Poirot and Hastings discover that Paul Renauld has been murdered and his body dumped into an open grave at a nearby golf course. French detective Monsieur Giraud is assigned to investigate the case and views Poirot as a rival. A wager is made between the two detectives on who will solve the case first. There's nothing wrong with friendly competition when endeavoring to solve a murder, right?... As always there are plenty of suspects: M. Renauld's wife, Eloise, owns the dagger used as the murder weapon. His son, Jack, can't be located after the murder. And, his mistress visited the Renauld residence on the night of the murder. The characters are aplenty here, so who knows, there may be more... As in The Mysterious Affair at Styles, Hercule Poirot #1 , Hastings walks us through The Murder on the Links , as our narrator. His observations and speculations are often a tangled mess within Poirot's clear view. Their interactions are always interesting and appear to be challenging lessons for poor dear brow-beaten Hastings. The 7H 27M audiobook is expertly narrated by Richard Armitage whose narration and voicing are an experience. Honestly, Agatha Christie's Mysteries has the best narrators. Really! The Murder on the Links has multiple twists and turns, an extended cast of characters, and even a surprising romance. Agatha Christie is a master storyteller, writing genius, and magician at pulling it all together at the end. This is her third novel, published in the UK and US in 1923, and I highly recommend it! 4⭐
