Being a big tea drinker, I’ve already read “The Book of Tea,” first published in 1906 by author Kanuzo Okakura and narrated for Audible by Nicholas Tekoski, but decided to listen to a narrated version. I started drinking hot tea when I was in my teens, and actually raised my son to drink it since he was about six years old. I love tea so much so a few years ago, I purchased a tea plant and actually make my own black tea. Okakura was a brilliant writer, and this book is almost poetic. The author was extremely insightful, discussing tea in combination with philosophy, architecture, design, history, culture, and even flowers.
