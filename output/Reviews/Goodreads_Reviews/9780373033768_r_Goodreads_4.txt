It's funny how you sometimes get a wish right out of the blue! After reading one too many books where the h does a disappearing act because of some dumb reason, like she assumes the H was cheating (she has no proof, just thinks he was), or he doesn't really love her, had some ulterior motive for marrying her, etc. (again, without any proof, just assumption) or - the ultimate in TSTL - she actually listens to the vindictive OW, I said to myself that just once, I'd like the h to walk out for a good reason, motivated by her love for the H, not her own insecurities. Well, I got it! The h in this book actually has a good, valid reason for leaving the H, and while it does include some self-preservation, mostly it's because she loves the H and believes he'll think he's better off without her in his life. (I won't explain why and ruin the story, I'll just say that in her place, I might have done the same thing.) I'll also admit that I have a thing for amnesia stories, so even before reading it, this book had a good head start. Also, I liked both the H and h, they have their moments of misunderstandings and arguments, but nothing over-the-top, and there's no OM/OW. (There is another man in the story, but not in the usual way; he's part of the mystery in the plot.) I'm proud to say that I solved the mystery about 3/4 through the book. Just like when I was reading all those Agatha Christie books, it gave me a feeling of pride to know I figured it out, before the truth was revealed! And it's a pretty good mystery, too! I really recommend this one!
