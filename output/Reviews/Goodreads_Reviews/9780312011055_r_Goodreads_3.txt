Selection of ~20 years of Russ stories, both genre and not. Some are quite slight - essentially just drabbles - but there's more than enough here to showcase her range and her fierce intelligence. She does laugh-out-loud funny ('The Cliches from Outer Space' had me cackling), melancholy ('The Little Dirty Girl', 'How Dorothy Kept Away the Spring'), twists-in-the-tale ('Nor Custom Stale'), and heartbreaking rage ('Daddy's Little Girl'). More sadness-in-suburbia than I'd expected based on my previous Russ reading, but always razor-sharp in both wit and feminism. Bonus: 'The Experimenter' felt like an unacknowledged Alyx story.
