No, I wasn't expecting much and I only read it because my neighbor let me borrow it but damn was this terrible. Yes, it was for a whole $1 and it definitely read like it. It's only 162 pages long so there's not much information given and everything moved so quickly, almost as it the whole book was an afterthought or an idea, which brings me to my last issue. Why are we continuing to publish books like this? It's not informative, so what's the point? Men are creeps, we get it.
