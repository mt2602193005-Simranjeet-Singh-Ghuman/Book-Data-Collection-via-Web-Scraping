Nate Welby is a single father who wants to give his children everything they deserve. The one thing he cannot provide, however, is a mother. Enter Leandra Flanagan … single, talented, pretty and fun. She is in charge of the annual Christmas production and when the committed tells her to hire Nate to construct the set, she falls madly in love with his children. Will he have the same effect on her – and vice versa? I don’t read a lot of traditional “romance” novels, but every now and then, I pick up one that strikes me as interesting. In this case, I’m glad I took the plunge. This is a truly enjoyable read and a great story for a cold winter day.
