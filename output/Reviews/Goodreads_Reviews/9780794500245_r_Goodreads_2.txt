Dinosaurs is a non-fiction book with everything to do about dinosaurs, from the way they are structured, eat, and how they became extinct. Each page has several paragraphs of text along with numerous captions. There are lots of pictures to look at. This book talks about the role of a paleontologist and the most recent discoveries. Most of what we know about dinosaurs is due to the information collected within the past forty years. This book describes characteristics of specific animals like the stegosaurs and the dromaeosaurs. I like this book a lot and I enjoyed learning facts about these extinct creatures. However, I do not like pages 24-25 that state some dinosaurs are "slow and stupid". I do not think this is appropriate language. I like the combination of drawn illustrations and photographs. "Terrors of the seas" were my two favorite pages because they explained water dinosaurs. I would not read students the part of the book that mentions dinosaurs being stupid but I do like some of the other facts. I would show students the pictures in this book especially. There is a website connected to the book and I think I would let students browse it.
