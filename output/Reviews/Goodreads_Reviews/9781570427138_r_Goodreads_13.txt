I can't stand the character, Alex Cross, however I really enjoyed the story line and the villain of Pop Goes the Weasel . Alex Cross is facing a new killer, one that is totally random in his killings. He seems to blend into the crowds and no one notices him. The reader knows right away that the villain is British diplomat, Geoffrey Shafer, however the whole point is whether or not Alex Cross can figure out who the Weasel is. I don't like Alex Cross. He doesn't seem realistic at all - perfect father, boyfriend, investigator, psychologist and he can't do any wrong. I don't like reading the parts with his family and with his girlfriend, Christine. It just makes my eyes roll. I liked the villain, Geoffrey Shafer, he was so smug and evil. I also enjoyed how this book was more than the hunting down of the killer you also got Geoffrey's trial
