I'm kind of obsessed with The Arctic and the indigenous people who live there. I wrote a graduate seminar paper about children's literature set in The Arctic and this is one of the books that I came across in my research. I've never forgotten this beautiful and haunting story. It's riveting and deliciously horrifying in that Grimm's Fairytale sort of way. Amazing illustrations. Just a stunning book altogether. If I could give it ten stars, I would.
