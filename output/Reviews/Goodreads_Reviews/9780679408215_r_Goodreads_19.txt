A truly gripping story that held me from start to finish. Nicky Wells is a media war correspondent , but her life is shattered when she loses the love of her life , Charles Devereux . Nicky finds peace and is content with life work and her friendship with photographer Clee Donovan. After a beautiful romance in Provence, Nicky wonders if she might finally be ready to love again. I quite enjoyed reading this book , but it was way too long , l think it could of been half the length but overall an enjoyable romance and an interesting storyline 😊
