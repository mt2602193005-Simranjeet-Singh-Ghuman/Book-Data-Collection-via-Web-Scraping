I thought I'd enjoy this book because "multi-generational storytelling" and "saga" and characters without redeeming qualities are normally right up my alley. Unfortunately, the multi-generational story was just a summary of a bunch of miserable marriages between horrible people who don't communicate much. The characters went beyond the usual unlikeable but ultimately relateable mold and straight into completely uninteresting but terrible people. I gave this two stars for the last 5% of the book where there is a sudden reversal of all the attributes the rest of the book set up and then an abrupt ending. tl;dr: Read Shining Through instead.
