I just added this to my long list of Vietnam books I've read. an easy 5 stars. the book is based on the authors experiences as a child with his dad who was a veteran and continues through his personal experiences in Vietnam and after. i don't want to give anything away but even a thorough reader on the subject will learn a few new things about the war and its toll from this book. i read this in practically 1 sitting. a very well written book. wish this author had more.
