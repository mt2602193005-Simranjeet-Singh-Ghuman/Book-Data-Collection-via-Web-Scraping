It was a fairly captivating biography. Dr. Seuss was an intriguing person and I enjoyed learning more about how he became such a celebrated author. One thing I found fascinating is that they believed that he had a perfect sense of color similar to a person having perfect pitch. He put in countless hours of work and was never really into writing for the money. I loved the challenges he received to write books with very limited word counts. He definitely redesigned the idea of beginning books for early readers. I was saddened to hear about his first wife’s death.
