Learn about the fascinating species of dinosaurs with this read! You can learn about fossils, breeds of dinosaurs, their diets, deadly dinosaurs, and cute baby dinosaurs! Rachel Firth will have you obsessed with dinosaurs after reading interesting facts along with vivid pictures to help you understand the life of dinosaurs! I personally enjoyed this book because I find dinosaurs very fascinating. They are a very mysterious species and I enjoyed learning more about them as I read this book. I would use this book in my class to help teach my kids about dinosaurs. I think they would enjoy the pictures contained in this book and it would help them learn many fun facts about dinosaurs!
