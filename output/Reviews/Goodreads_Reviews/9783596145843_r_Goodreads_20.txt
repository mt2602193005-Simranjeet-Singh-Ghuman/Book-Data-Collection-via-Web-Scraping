The first hundred pages of this book was the best I read in 2020. I was immersed, I wanted to read it when woke up, before sleep, while I was a sleep _ I legit had a dream about reading it. This post apocalyptic novel was ominous, atmospheric, seedy and isolated. I was calculating _prematurely I might add_ where to cram it in my "best of 2020". Then, the rest of the book happen. So, I ended up not liking it as much as I wanted to. The whole story shifted from an atmospheric post apocalyptic story to the survival of the stupidest. A bummer.
