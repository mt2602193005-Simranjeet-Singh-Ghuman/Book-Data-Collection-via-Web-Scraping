I occasionally listen to Jim DeRogatis and his colleague Greg Kot (Chicago Tribune) on their radio show "Sound Opinions." I've usually been more in agreement with Greg Kot, but perhaps that's because I also read him in the newspaper. Anyway, I picked up Milk It! because of the screaming graphic cover, and I'm glad I did. Jim DeRogatis is passionate about music, he's honest and funny. He is unafraid to disagree with other critics, fans, and musicians. It helps that he has such a Kurt Cobain jones. I have a whole list of albums to either go back to and listen again, or in some cases for the first time.
