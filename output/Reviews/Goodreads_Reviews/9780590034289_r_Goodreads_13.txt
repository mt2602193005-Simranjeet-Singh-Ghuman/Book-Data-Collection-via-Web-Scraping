"Never say 'no' to adventures." This was a complete and utter delight. I've been a fan of the movie/musical since I was a small child, so when I saw that David Tennant had narrated this audiobook, I picked it up. What's inside is a story that's far different than the film, but just as whimsical and just as fun. There is no Truly Scrumptious, but the kids have their own mother. There are Toot Sweets, but no rollicking dance number. There is no magical land and no need to disguise as living toys, but there is a famous sweet shop and stopping mobsters from robbing it. So really, which is better? I might say the movie, actually. Simply because it's so wacky and so wonderful and it has Dick van Dyke in it. But the two are so vastly different that there's really not much of a comparison aside from Chitty herself. And gosh, Chitty's cool in the book. But even though it wasn't the story I know and love so well, I had a wonderful time listening to this little adventure. The family was sweet, the intrigue was enough to keep me interested, the kids were SUCH icons, and now I just really want to watch the movie again. I had a lot of fun, and for that, I give it 4 stars.
