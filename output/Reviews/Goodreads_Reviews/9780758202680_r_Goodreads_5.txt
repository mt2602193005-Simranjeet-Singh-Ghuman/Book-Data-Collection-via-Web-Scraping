By passed Bertrice Small, Susan Johnson, Thea Devine and headed straight for Robin Schone’s, A Lady’s Pleasure. This micro review is based on that book alone. Why did I pass the others? Because the reviews made me that’s why and I have a keen aversion to domination and cruelty in supposed ‘romance’. Highly improbable scenario but Schone had a way of drawing you into the intense desperate yearning created by these two lost souls while tearing at the heart strings. It was good until near the end. ...sure you wanna go here? Okay...don't say you weren't warned. Spoiler ahead: To my way of thinking if nature had intended both orifices for fucking one of them would be redundant but really what the hell did I expect, vanilla in an erotic novel? Sheesh. If that doesn’t bother you then don’t listen to a word I’ve said, it really was quite good up to that point. Guess I'll just head back across the divide and read hot steamy vanilla sex with chocolate syrup and be content in my narrow world.
