The book is great as a general reference, but it is pretty outdated. We biked the loop, Midway to Penticton and back to Midway via Mckinney Rd at Oliver, this month. It was absolutely gorgeous. Don't count on a resort for showers at Hydraulic Lake - apparently it has been closed for a number of years. Milkshakes were great at Chute Lake, but no running water. The Myra section was incredible. We plan to ride another section next summer!
