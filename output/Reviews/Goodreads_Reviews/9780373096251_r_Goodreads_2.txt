I really liked this story. The ending was a little bit rushed but over all, there was good pacing and an interesting plot line. I also REALLY enjoyed taking a look at Trace O'Hurley. None of the other books really had very much about him because none of his family knew much about him. This was a great conclusion to the series, pulling everyone together. I recommend reading Waiting for Nick from the Stanislaski series after reading this series. Nora Roberts is brilliant and connects to this series with that one, really delving in and deepening the connections in her world. Anyway, there is sort of an epilogue of this series in that book, looking at the family as a whole a few years down the road. Back to this book, I really liked what we learned about Flynn and would like to see more of him at some point, even though I kind of doubt that it will ever happen. Definitely at least 5 stars, if not more.
