This book was different that I expected it to be after reading the description on the cover. I expected that there would only be two main characters, the twins Evvie and Kate, and that the majority of the book would take place during the war. Instead there were four main characters, including the twins, Maggie an aspiring dress designer and Josie, best friend to Evvie and daughter of a poor, large and dysfunctional family. The book follows these four girls through their lives, their successes and joys, and their relationships. This is far from a romance novel as the majority of the romances in this book are doomed to fail in one way or another. The characters were well written, I wanted to slap some sense into Kate for her romantic choices, and Josie too who let herself be swept away but foolish decisions at times. The war doesn't begin until close to the end of the book. But despite this the book is fairly accurate in capturing the horror, slaughter of the front lines and the fear and sadness that hung over everyone waiting at home. It was a very good read, tough to put down. I was drawn into the lives of these characters and I still wonder what became of them all after the last page was read.
