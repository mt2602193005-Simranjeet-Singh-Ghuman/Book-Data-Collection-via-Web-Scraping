Engaging and effective in achieving Kayano's goal of making readers begin to better understand the Ainu, their history, and their future. As many other reviews point out, what was at the time of publication the future is now the past, and unfortunately that hasn't been much better than the past that was the past for Kayano. That being said, it's hard to read this memoir and say that the act of writing it or that any of the things he describes doing in it were in vain given that things might be even more bleak for the Ainu if he hadn't, so I wouldn't say it's all doom and gloom! 3.75/5
