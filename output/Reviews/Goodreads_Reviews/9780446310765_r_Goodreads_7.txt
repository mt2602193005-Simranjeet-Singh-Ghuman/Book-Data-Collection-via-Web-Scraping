A family forward This book has a slow start. Once you get to know the members and see their failings, you can also predict the salvation of the family and all of its members. There are surprises and predictability, but you can see the love. Unfortunately, it's takes each of them time to realize that the love is for each other and not the railroad they run and save and almost lose several times. It is set in the time of the robber barons, and the family is a part of that genre. In time, the family will retain the wealth, but will the road survive? And who will bring it to the next chapter?
