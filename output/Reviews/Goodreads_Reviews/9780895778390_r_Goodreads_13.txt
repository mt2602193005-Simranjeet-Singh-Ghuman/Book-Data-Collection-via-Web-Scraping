4.5 stars Essential reading I would recommend to anyone getting their first dog. Sets the scene well with giving an overview of genetics, breeds and how dogs sense the world differently to us. Then goes through how to raise a puppy well using positive reinforcement and reward based training. Lots on training, grooming, house breaking, socialisation, leaving puppies alone, preventing aggression. The socialisation plan at the back is great! The list of recommended reads are also very handy and I will make use of these. The number of sticky notes I have in this book is a lot which is a sign it’s a pretty good book and that I’ll be coming back to it. Some new takeaways: 1) jackpot in rewarding - reduce number of treats/ play when rewarding once puppy knows the skill. Being unpredictable with amount of reward at this point keeps them more ‘hooked’ akin to gambling 2) prevention is always better than cure - set things up well from the start to prevent behavioural issues later 3) the most important thing you can do for a dog is socialise them well - above literally all else. The window for that is so short too - up to about 16 weeks 4) in games, make sure to teach the dog to stop. A good way is to offer higher value treats in exchange when training 5) importance of training in different places to avoid association with environment 6) standard commands and signals table Had to deduct half a point for some bits being outdated (ie getting puppies at 6 weeks old). However, all in all, an accessible read great for beginners
