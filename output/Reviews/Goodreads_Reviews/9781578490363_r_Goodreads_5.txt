3.5⭐️ I far prefer this to Rosaria Butterfield’s “The Gospel Comes with a House Key”, which makes you feel like they did not even have a door on their home. Practical tips, solid questions, plenty of examples. She does not try to do it all herself but relies on others as well, recognizing that as part of the gift. She had to stop hospitality for a season due to physical weakness and ministry demands. Details and encouragement are practical and grounded.
