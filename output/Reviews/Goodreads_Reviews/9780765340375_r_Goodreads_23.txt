The Forbidden Lady by Kerrelyn Sparks Quincy Stanton is a Captain of a ship but dire times for the Colonists call him into the world of spying. Acting as a Loyalist he uses his time at their parties to find information on what the British are up to. Quincy knows the dangers he faces and the consequences if he is caught what he didn’t expect was trying to play his part when a beautiful woman enters into the picture. Virginia Munro and her sister Caroline were spending time in Boston getting to know their Aunt Mary. She had been married to a cruel man who was a strong Loyalist. Now that he was dead Mary Dover was doing what she could for the other side. And what could be better for spying on the British then attending parties at the Loyalists homes and her young nieces hobnobbing with the British soldiers? Virginia however was mostly interested in spying on the confusing Quincy Stanton, something just didn’t add up and she was going to find out what. This book originally came out as For Love or Country, Kerrelyn’s first book before her Love At Stakes series. A book where the only nibbling of necks were with kisses not fangs. The Forbidden lady has a unique blend of history, suspense, action and romance. Not knowing who could be trusted or who the enemy was during these pre-war times.
