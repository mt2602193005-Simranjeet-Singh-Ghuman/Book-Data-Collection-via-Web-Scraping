Hooray. It’s over! If I hadn’t had to attempt this for book club, I would have given up ages ago. That was hard to admit because normally I can something I can connect to in a book but this was impossible. I felt like I was wading through mud. I understood Chaucer’s Canterbury Tales, which is what Peter Ackroyd tried to modernise better than this. That’s saying something as I only studied it for two weeks at university. The changes in language, from Latin to English is what I struggled with the most. I kept losing my place because I was looking at what words or phrases meant. It was very frustrating. There was also a lot of sexual innuendo everywhere. I am used to a bit of sex but there’s usually stuff going on around it I can engage. In short, what was the point of this? I hope the next choice is better.
