Cute book, 3 short little stories in one about the adventures between Arlo the gopher and Vic the bear. Great story about Vic moving in next door to Arlo and they get off to a rough start. Arlo's sprinkler keeps getting Vic's stuff wet as he is trying to move in. Arlo feels bad and offers to help, they make up. The next one Arlo accidentally locks himself out if the house in the middle of a story night, he stays with Vic and they read a story, but the ending is missing and Arlo just can't stand it until Vic makes up and ending. The last one is Blueberry Frost, Vic is having freezer issues and asks Arlo to keep his ice cream safe for him. Arlo tries it and ends up eating it all, and then can't find a new one for Vic. He tries to fake it with vanilla and raisins and has to admit what he done, then he offers his special ice cream to Vic and he loves it.
