Any children’s bookshelf needs a good anthology of nursery rhymes, for singing and reading. Faith Jaques illustrations are in a very traditional style and lovely, and the rhymes are a nice balance of popular and arcane. A lot of longer rhymes like A Was An Apple Pie or The House That Jack Built wouldn’t normally make it into a smaller collection and that’s a strength too, stretching the attention span and involvement of the very youngest listener/co-reader.
