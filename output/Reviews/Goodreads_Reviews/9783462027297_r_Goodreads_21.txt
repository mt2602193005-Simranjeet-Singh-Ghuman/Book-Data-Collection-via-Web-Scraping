This is my 6th Remarque novel, beginning with his ground breaking WWI classic, All Quiet on the Western Front (1928), which was also his debut. Three Comrades (1936) was his third novel, and like most of his books that followed his debut, it is set in Germany in the years between the two world wars. Altogether he wrote eleven novels and since the six that I have read have been outstanding, I look forward to reading the remaining five.
