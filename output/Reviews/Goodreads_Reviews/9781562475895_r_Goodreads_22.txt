#2 in the Josefina main series in my opinion. I surprisingly liked this one a lot more than the first few books in Josefina's series! I would rate it maybe a 4.25. I think a lot more happened in this book. We get to meet a new character, we get a new setting, we get to see Josefina's excitement at the stalls as she searches for what she'll trade her blanket for, we get actual stakes when it looks like Patrick O'Toole has run off, and Josefina has to save the day before her father makes a big mistake. Also, Josefina is such a sweet girl. I loved her seeing how happy the violin made her dad and aunt and deciding to pool her sisters' blankets to trade for the violin rather than trading her blanket for the farmhouse set she loved. Josefina is so generous and caring and lovely. I love how her generosity was rewarded in the end and that Patrick gave her and her sisters the gifts they wanted. Josefina is so anxious and mature and responsible and just wants to please the people around her. It makes me sad for her, but also I love her a lot. She's a kid I would genuinely be happy to know in real life (unlike, say, Felicity, who would exhaust me, or Molly, who I would want to put in a very long timeout).
