This is a haunting, true and bewildering story of a young girl gone missing from a small town. The town must deliver justice even though the two young suspects are poor and uneducated men. I find it heartbreaking and I am sure it happens multi-times over in every corner of the world. It stayed with me and how sad for everyone...this author did a truly remarkable job in bringing all the town folks to life. He detailed every moment and especially near the end at 'count down' time. I highly recommend it.
