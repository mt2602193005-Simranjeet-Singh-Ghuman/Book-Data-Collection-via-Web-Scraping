Loved this so much! I loved this story! There are romances written by this author that I somehow have managed to miss over the years and this is one of the. Brilliantly written, playing on the theme of desert abduction buy with a wily, intelligent heroine and an honourable, principled hero who is trying to do the right thing by his absent half brother. The dialogue is crisp and witty - and the scene where the amusement of our heroine's captors is rapidly reversed as she neatly hot wires a land rover to make good her escape is just one example of how good a story this is. Five stars!
