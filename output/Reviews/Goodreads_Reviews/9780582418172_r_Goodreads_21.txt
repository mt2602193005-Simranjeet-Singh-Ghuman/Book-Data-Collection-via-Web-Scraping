Reading this book reminded me of how much I love reading books that are either narrated by a children or feature children in them as the main characters. This was a great coming-of-age story. It made me smile, laugh and, feel sorry for the kids. It's a great story of friendship and childhood. This is my third Stephen King novella and something I've noticed is that all his books seem to have references from the other books. For example, in this book the fictional prison of Shawshank is mentioned. That was a reference to Rita Hayworth and the Shawshank Redemption. Really cool! All in all, it was a quick and fun read. Would recommend. Going to check out the movie to this!
