Tiptree continues to stand out to me as one of the most talented science fiction writers. She was able to mingle her sense of wonder with loneliness and alienation, of beauty with acts of cruelty, her appreciation of what is worth living for and how the future might embrace it and how evil and darkness will still turn up, the worm at the core of the apple. In this novel, her last, all these themes are present. It has a full cast of characters to hint at the universe on the whole and lots of moving parts with several small- and large-scale tragedies woven within. It culminates on a night of pure horror and dread when it was intended to be a celebration of a unique event: the final collapse of a dying star. I really, truly enjoyed this. It doesn't pull any punches and there are disturbing acts described in full. Tiptrees future of the galaxy has some intriguing social mores in regard to sex. It's maybe a bit overstuffed with details and an overly long denouement, but it's bold and interesting. Have a little patience here and be rewarded tenfold.
