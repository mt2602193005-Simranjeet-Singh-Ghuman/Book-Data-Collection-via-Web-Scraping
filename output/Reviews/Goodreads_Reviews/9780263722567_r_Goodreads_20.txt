Dangerous Masquerade is a pretty pleasant romance novel. It is clearly outdated, as I'm pretty sure that at this day and age the plot devised between Laurie and LaRaine would work - at the time the novel was originally written there were no smart phones and no social media, so it was easier to keep the ruse. But if you can ignore the above, I still enjoyed very much the way Laurie and Rian fall in love. So if you're looking for a quick, no strings attached romance novel and don't mind the fact that it's a bit outdated, you may want to give this one a try.
