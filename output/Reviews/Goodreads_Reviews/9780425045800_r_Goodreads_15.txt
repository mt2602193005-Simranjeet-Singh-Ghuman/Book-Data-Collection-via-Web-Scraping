Another John Varley book finished. This one is a collection of the 8 worlds series. Earth has been invaded by Aliens that come from a world like Jupiter. They view cetaceans as sentient and other lifeforms including humans as vermin. The destroy every single human made thing on earth resulting in wide spread death. Humanity survives on the other planets mostly, even though they are not very hospitable. In these stories we meet and re-meet characters such Fox who runs away from the far side of the moon to the nearside which has been abandoned for a century. There he meets a very old man who is doing something children of Fox's generation have no experience with, the man is dying. In other stories we meet a talking black hole and a talking bomb. We read about an almost unsolvable murder in a religious colony who all have had surgical alternations so they look like Barbie dolls. As in others of the 8 world stories, people change their biological sex like we change our hair color. The idea of fatherhood as someone involved in a child's life is unheard of. One of the disturbing things is kids experience very early puberty and age of consent is right about then. I don't remember being as shocked by that in the 70's as I am now in 2026.
