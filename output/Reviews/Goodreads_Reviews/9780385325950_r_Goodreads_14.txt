It was only after completing this book that I decided to give it 5 stars with perhaps what would be a silly reason to many. Early on in the story there were several signs that I felt God was giving me through this book to let me know He is with me. I started this book while my mother was in a government funded nursing home corrupt evil facility, after having been in the hospital and what they did to her there. But it goes back in further to her ignorant doctor putting her on 20 meds. I have no doubt that my moms death was caused by the medical system and food industry that are all about power and money, intentional harm and damage. But that is not for this review, but a book God is leading me to write one day. The book started with signs from God about my mothers situation and ended with mentioning, "The Brady Bunch." There is a lot of history with me in regards to that show, another sign God meant for me to read this book. My original thought to not give it 5 stars was based only on the story being kind of flat, not much movement, no real story arc. However, I was pulled gently in to wanting to know what was going to happen. Equally or perhaps more so, the authors writing, Heather Quarles, is a gifted writer and deserves the claim on the cover as a winner of the Delacorte Press Prize. If the book had been poorly written there is no way I could have continued reading it. I am happy to report that I am glad that I didn't give up on, A Door Near Here. It is such a crazy mix of gently secular with a quiet undertone of Spiritual faith. The message is so subtle I imagine there is no way it would offend a non believer, yet has the potential to plant seeds. Other then no dramatic story arc, I recommend this read for the lessons in life it provides and the wonderful writing.
