Lewis makes many excellent points in this book, the most notable being that absolute truth is not merely a social construct that can be dismissed on a whim. To deny absolute truth is to deny the very framework of existence. At the same time, though, I was very confused while I was reading and had to backtrack and reread several times to grasp Lewis’s point. I found the writing and presentation of arguments to be fairly convoluted. This definitely merits a reread in the future.
