This book is a classic and can be read again and again to encourage our faith. Roland Buck relates his supernatural encounters with angels bearing messages of God's love for us today to encourage and remind us that God has not left us to flounder on our own but is with us every day watching over us , involved in every detail of our life and using HIS messengers angels to intervene constantly, though we know not when it happens. Such significant messages that were so encouraging to me: If there is one believer praying in a family, they are highly favored by God and so are those being prayed for. God continually sends HIS angels to minister salvation to them and if they reject what is presented to them ,He starts the cycle again. He never gives up on the people you love, because He loves them even more then we do. Also God has forgiven every sin we have ever committed , and you can review your past sins here as I did while reading this, and realize not only are they forgiven but completely forgotten as they never existed. I can took this to heart and began to realize that every sin that has overcome me in my past and every wrong choice I've made, is forgotten by God, as if it never happened, and will never be brought up again by HIM. I knew this but now after reading this and hearing this message relayed in a supernatural way, understand it more in my spirit. Then angels spoke of the priorities being the Word of God, Fellowship and communion with God,The Holy Spirit working through us, which gives us more power and authority to live a blameless and Holy life for Him , the atonement and blood of Christ and His return. One of the best books I have read in a long time that brought such encouragement to my soul. God is still using supernatural encounters to get our attention as He did in His Word, Old and New testament. His Word never changes and neither will HE.
