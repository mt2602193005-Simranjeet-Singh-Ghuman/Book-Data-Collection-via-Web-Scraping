Also known as Rape Mountain . In my review of Rosanne Bittner's other work, Tennessee Bride , I noted how the book's heroine "has the unholy power to compel absolutely every male on the face of the planet to want to rape her." Sweet Mountain Magic has a protagonist cut from the same cloth. Venado/Mary has had a shit life before she even hits her twenties. She's been raped multiple times by multiple men, has been pregnant multiple times, and attacked by multiple grizzly bears (who probably wanted to rape her and get her pregnant the way this story was going.) Like the hero of Tennessee Bride , Sage MacKenzie spends most of the book trying - and not always succeeding - to keep all these men off of Mary. (Rosanne discovers the word 'travois' and goes hog wild with it.)
