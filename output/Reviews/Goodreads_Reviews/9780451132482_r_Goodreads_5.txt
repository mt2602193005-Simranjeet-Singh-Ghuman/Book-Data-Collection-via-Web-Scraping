A collection of short stories by Georgette Heyer. I found the titular story miserably boring. It is the sole reason I haven't gotten to this volume before. Once I passed it, the stories weren't so bad. I enjoyed some, particularly Hazard. A widow makes an appearance in another one, which represented a welcome break from the ever-present 19 year old heroines fluttering about. Elopements, Gretna Green, and Heyer heroes pepper the stories and eventually blur them together. If you like Heyer, this is a pleasant stroll. It contains a few gems. Unfortunately, it also feels rather paint by numbers. The same personalities emerge - not just from her other books - but from the other stories within the volume. Almost all female characters fall into her "younger female" category and I just don't like them as much. I also felt a little concerned for how many of them met the Heyer hero and then were talking engagement! Not much development. I think I'd still buy this one. There is creativity in it. Just not as much 'new' as I'd hoped.
