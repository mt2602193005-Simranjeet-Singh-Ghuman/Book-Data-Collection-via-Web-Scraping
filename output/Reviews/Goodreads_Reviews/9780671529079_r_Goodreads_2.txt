Hammel does an excellent job of simply letting the veterans speak, while weaving a cohesive narrative between that tells the reader the greater overall picture without bogging it down. Simply put he lets the veterans words and works shine. I appreciated that the book wasn't just about success, it shared stories of failure, capture, and death as well. Also appreciated was the fact that the 9th and 15th Air Forces were not shorted content wise in favor of the more well known 'Mighty 8th'.
