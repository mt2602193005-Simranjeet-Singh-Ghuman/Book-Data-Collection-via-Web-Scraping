This was a funny but endearing book about a 13-year-old girl, Ratchet, from Florida, whose inattentive mother drops her off with two old aunts (90-year-old twins!) in Maine (in the middle of nowhere!) for the summer! There's quite a story that twists and turns through the book -- as Harper, another young girl, also gets dropped off at the aunts' house -- by accident! Overall it is sweet, cute, and funny, and at times you'll also want to cry. But it is all very entertaining (especially the aunts!) and I highly recommend it :)
