The last installment in the O'Hurleys series has a completely different feel than the rest of the books. Trace O'Hurley is a tough secret agent, so the story doesn't really fit with the romantic tone set in the previous books. This book is not a romance novel, but a mystery/suspense one. Nonetheless, it's very tense, and there is a romance as well. Nora Roberts manages to pull it off, but I can't help but wonder if this book needed more fleshing out to fit better in the rest of the lovely series. I can see Trace's character distant from his family while working plenty of other jobs. What bothered me even more is that it's hard to believe that Frank O'Hurley was such a wonderful, thoughtful, and considerate father to his triplet daughters, but such a stick in the mud when it came to Trace. Their conflict felt forced as a result.
