I think it’s just the beginning of reading books for the sake of my daughter, and I’m OK with that! The Boxcar Children books are cute and harmless and each have little mysteries so I know why she loves them (and why I did at her age). But I think my favorite part is a quote from the last page about Gertrude Chandler Warner: “She liked to stress the Aldens independence and resourcefulness and their solid New England devotion to using up and making do.” I said to my husband, “oh is that what we New Englanders are devoted to?” (Being from CT) We use up and make do! Love it.
