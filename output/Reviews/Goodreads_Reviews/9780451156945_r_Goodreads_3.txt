2.5 stars, rounded up to three because that’s how many times the MMC ripped the FMC’s clothes off her…or was it four? Five? One loses count. One thing Catherine Coulter doesn’t lack is imagination. Each of her books is creative, unique, and fantastical. That last quality tends to be the problem. Surely, SURELY, not even a horny old faux uncle would send a 17-year-old into a brothel as a pretend-prostitute or allow, no, ENCOURAGE her to watch the clients having sex, or put her on the block in a virgin auction. And yet, he did. It was distasteful. No wonder she decided she would never marry. And that sets the background for the romance, a concept almost giggle-worthy. And speaking of the romance, it started as a coerced sexual encounter. I use the worse “coerced” instead of non-consensual because Alex maintained that Giana used the coercion as an excuse to have sex with him because she WANTED to have sex with him. I kid you not. Worse: he might have been right. There comes a point in any Catherine Coulter book where the reader either stops reading or decides to just go with it. I went with it and there was some fun to be had.
