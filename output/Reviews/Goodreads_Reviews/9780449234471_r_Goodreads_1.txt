Let me tell you a little about my thoughts with this book. I loved it. The library hard copy from 1970. It still has the card holder where you would stamp the date for returning the book. It has the old paper with a few spots on it from someone reading while they ate their lunch over the last 50 years the book has been around. Now to the story. The plot is clever as Mrs Pollifax goes from one danger to the next where someone is just in the right spot to help her out. The kindness of the characteries and the twists and turns of the plot just held me engrossed till the end. Since this was written in 1970 there were no cell phones or internet. Pan Am was still flying. WWII is still mentioned as the characters from it are still spies. You may not feel as I did that there is comfort in reading an old book.......But again I loved it.
