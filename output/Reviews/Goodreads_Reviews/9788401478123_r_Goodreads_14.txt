This book was supposedly published in 1970 but reads like it is written by someone who did not graduate high school. I read about 10% of this book, I am just stunned at the poor quality of the writing, & the content. The author does not use quotation marks and jumps from a conversation to another conversation with totally different people and you frequently have to figure out what is going on. The nonsense written is just beyond belief and 1927 people rated it 5 stars. How anyone can keep reading this story is beyond my comprehension never mind giving it such a high rating. From this point on I will always be very suspicious of Goodread ratings.
