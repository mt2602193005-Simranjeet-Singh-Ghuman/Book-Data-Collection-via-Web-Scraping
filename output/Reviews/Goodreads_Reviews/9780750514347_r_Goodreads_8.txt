I first read this book as a teenager, and it has lead to a decade of re-reading the story. I haven't read any other books by Lyn Andrews, but this one... it has a special place in my heart. The story centres on two teenage girls, twins, who are both similar and dissimilar to one-another in equal amounts. It touches on love, and growth, and heartbreak - a story that shows us the girls growing up under a shadow of European war and then draws them onto the battlefields. It's truly an amazing read, and one I think I'll enjoy re-living in perpetuity.
