The story was ok. However, I never felt for any of the characters. What Helen did was unacceptable to me. Worse, in my eyes, she had the audacity to be haughty when she finally spilt the secret to Dane. Although Dane had my sympathy for most of the book, I didn't particularly like him as a character either. He came off as too cavalier for my tastes. Josh seemed ok, but he was barely in the book.
