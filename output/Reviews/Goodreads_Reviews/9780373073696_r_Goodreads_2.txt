3.5 stars Our hero is star detective who has been burned by a rich woman once, and our heroine is an heiress but doesn't live like one. When the book starts both of them are on the same flight and notice each other. Their next meeting isn't as great and is when the hero is assigned to her dad's homicide. The heroine grew up materially rich and emotionally poor and has found her place in life, earning a living as a therapist for children who are deaf. The hero's preconceived notions about her are shattered at every end, but however his superiors are hell-bent on arresting the heroine for her father's murder. This one worked for me despite me wanting to smack the hero for his bias a couple of times. The hero finds himself pushed to the extreme for his job despite his belief that the heroine is innocent and does his best to protect her, pulling off a tight balancing act. I found this a satisfying read. Show more
