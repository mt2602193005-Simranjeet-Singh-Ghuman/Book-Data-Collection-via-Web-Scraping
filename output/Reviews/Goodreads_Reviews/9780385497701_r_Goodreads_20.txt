I have read one other book on London's history. Peter Ackroyd's massive research shows in this huge book. I really learned a lot, which is why I love historical novels. I can honestly say this is the only book you will ever have to read to pretty much understand the history of London. With that said, this is Non-fiction book and parts can be a bore. I even skimmed a few chapters myself. Overall, it's a 4 star read. You can learn so much from this book. One chapter literally goes over the old Cockney accents. Like, go fetch me da Piper ( not paper). These little tidbits show the extensive research Ackroyd did on this book and he should be commended.
