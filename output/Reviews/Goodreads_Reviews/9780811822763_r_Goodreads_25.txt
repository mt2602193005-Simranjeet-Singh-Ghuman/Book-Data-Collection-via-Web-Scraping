I loved this! I learned so much about the folklore of Irish Fairies...mostly that Irish Fairies are considered to be very mischievous and sometimes, downright evil. This book dedicates a few pages to each, and talks about the history and geographical origin of each fairy. The illustrations are gorgeous in this edition. I'm sure I'll read it again sometime. I bought this book when I visited Ireland in 2006. Started it and never finished it (needed this for a Reading Challenge.)
