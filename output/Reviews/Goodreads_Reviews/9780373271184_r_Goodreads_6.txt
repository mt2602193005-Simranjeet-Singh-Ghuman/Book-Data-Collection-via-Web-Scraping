I liked this one - for some reason, the MacNeill series is far and away better than the Eden series. Things I particularly liked here: Annie's phone call to the cops after Rob threatened her, because it demonstrated her taking back her own life in a concrete way. She rejected Rob's version of the truth and asked for help, because she was finally starting to believe that she could get it if she wanted. I liked that she had to work for this realization, that she didn't magically become better when the hero arrived. Things that I didn't like: Maddox came across as a giant walking nerve, as though he was moments away from going caveman on other people. I find that kind of unnerving in someone given a gun and authority. Yes, the author tried to make him seem conflicted and hurting, but... as soon as Annie was added to the mix, he might as well have just spoken in RAWR*chest thump*. So while I wasn't wild on the hero, I thought her treatment of the heroine was quite good.
