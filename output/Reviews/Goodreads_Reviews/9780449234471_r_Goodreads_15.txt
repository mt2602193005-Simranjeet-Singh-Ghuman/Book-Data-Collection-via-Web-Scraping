Double agents, gypsies, and, of course, Communist intrigue...what more could you want? Mrs. Pollifax continues to delight as the grandma-turned-spy who saves the day against all odds. I really found this book delightful and funny. I love how the book circumvents my expectations...an elderly gentleman gets introduced and I immediately think, 'ah, she gets a romance.' But that is not exactly how it turns out!
