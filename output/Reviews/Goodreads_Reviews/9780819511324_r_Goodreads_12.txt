A brilliant, poignant collection of verse that resonates with the poet's visceral pain and longing - whether for his Kashmir, parents, all that he could not regain - or, his beloved Akhtari Bai and Faiz sahib. It sits by my bedstead, to be consumed, relished and agonized over, each night. As a shaayar has stated in his ghazal: "us ne naasuur kar liyaa hogaa zaKHm ko shaa.erii banaate hu.e" [In essence, the suffering itself appears to have become the muse for his verses]
