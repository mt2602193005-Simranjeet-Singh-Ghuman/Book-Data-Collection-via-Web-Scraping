Oh, yeah - where was this book when I was a boy?! I own a half a dozen or more speed math/mental math/math shortcuts book, but this is my favorite. A really cool layout, and great explanations. For example, breaking into factors before multiplying: 16 x 35 = 16 x (5 x 7) = (16 x 5) x 7 = 80 x 7 = 560 or 16 x 35 = (2 x 8) x 35 = (2 x 35) x 8 = 70 x 8 = 560 Anyway, it's a small book - 130 p., with index, printed and bound in landscape mode, with good tips on how to simplify numbers in your head (or on paper, if you prefer).
