This was incredibly boring. The hero, the heroine, and even the execution of the story were dull. I never felt engrossed or emotionally connected to any of the characters—they all came across as flat and bland. The hero rescues the heroine from hanging by marrying her, yet instead of gratitude, she responds with constant attitude. There’s absolutely no chemistry or intensity between them, which makes their interactions frustrating rather than compelling. Everyone seems to take everyone else for granted, and nothing feels earned or meaningful. I give up on this one.
