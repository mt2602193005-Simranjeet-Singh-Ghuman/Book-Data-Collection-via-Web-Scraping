Not sure why, but I didn't bond with this book. I've read books with far more far-fetched plotlines, and the quality of the writing's good, but I still ended up skimming. I was also surprised that the heroine had unprotected sex with the hero, even if only for one night. The whole plot was about her and her illegitimate child from when she was targeted and seduced previously. She's full of shame for breaching the moral teachings/standards of that time and place, yet goes and risks another pregnancy out of wedlock.
