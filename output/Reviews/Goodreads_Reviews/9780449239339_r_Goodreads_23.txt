Generally thrillers aren't my thing. I bought this book at a second-hand book sale because I couldn't resist the red faux-leather binding (my copy is part of The Companion Book Club and published in 1975). It's slow to start, picks up the chase in the second act, but then fizzles in the third. Another one of those books where there's so much buildup to nothing. It also would have helped to include a map of the region as I'm not familiar enough with former Czechoslovakia, Austria and Switzerland to follow along in my head. It wasn't badly written overall, just not my genre.
