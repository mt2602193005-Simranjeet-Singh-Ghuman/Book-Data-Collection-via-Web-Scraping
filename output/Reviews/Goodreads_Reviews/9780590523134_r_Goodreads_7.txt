Really good and emotional book about how Kristy and her two older brothers deal with their estranged father Patrick suddenly calling them out of the blue to invite them to his wedding. Their dad's a jerk (like did he legit forget David Michael), and I like the clear-eyed way Kristy observes his fiancee Zoey, who seems an awesome woman overall, entering into the relationship with both eyes wide open. So even though Kristy and her brothers aren't completely sure what Zoey sees in their father, they respect her enough to trust she's making the right decision for herself. And I like how Kristy notices that Zoey kinda has Patrick's number and gets away with calling him out on stuff (whereas he gets pissed off when Charlie tries calling him out), and also how Kristy reflects that maybe Zoey being so serious also makes her kinda attracted to Patrick's child(ish)-like approach to life. LOL I'm clearly not a fan of Patrick and I think Zoey could do better (you're 40, Zoey, my friend, you know better!), but I like that the book gives a nuanced portrayal of this story.
