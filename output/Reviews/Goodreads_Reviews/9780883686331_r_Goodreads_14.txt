This is the first book by Wigglesworth I’ve read…I’m still not sure what to think…it’s packed with anecdotes and Biblical reasoning regarding healing, deliverance, the other gifts of the Spirit and more. I see myself as ‘part Pentecostal’ having grown up attending Elim and other Pentecostal churches but in that regard I’m probably more ‘centrist’ (If Elim was on one end and refusing to believe in Gifts of the spirit is on the other) It’s an interesting read. I’ve rated it 4, merely because I think all such books should have a conclusion to ‘pull everything together’. Recommend as it will encourage and give food for thought.
