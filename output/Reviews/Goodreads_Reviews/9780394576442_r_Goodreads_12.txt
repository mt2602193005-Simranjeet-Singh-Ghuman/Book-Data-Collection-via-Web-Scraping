I went into this blind, only knowing it’s about “family, trauma, race and the destiny of a nation corrupted by slavery”. The topic itself was about what I thought was a mentally ill father (and daughter), despite being a brilliant surgeon. Long story short, it’s about incest and a bit unhinged at times which makes it shocking. It was cleverly written in multiple POVs, in a non-linear narrative that is intentional, almost like a poem in a collection of memories/musings.
