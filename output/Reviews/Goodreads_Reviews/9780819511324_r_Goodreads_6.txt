Kashmir shrinks into my mailbox, my home a neat six by four inches I always loved neatness. Now I hold the half-inch Himalayas in my hand. This is home. And this the closet I'll ever be home. When I return, the colors won't be so brilliant, The Jehlum's waters so clean, so ultramarine. My love, so overexposed. And my memory will be a little out of focus, in it a giant, negative, black and white, still undeveloped.
