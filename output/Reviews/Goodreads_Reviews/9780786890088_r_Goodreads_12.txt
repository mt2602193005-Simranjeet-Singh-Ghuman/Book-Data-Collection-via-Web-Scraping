Great writing and a very good story. The author clearly did a lot of research on several complex topics to write this novel. I really like the Boldt character, I like the Matthews character much less. Unfortunately, this book features Matthews more than Boldt who all but disappears 90% of the way through the book and throughout the climax. Still, a solid read and whicle not as good as “undercurrents” an interesting and enjoyable book! Great narration as well on the audiobook !
