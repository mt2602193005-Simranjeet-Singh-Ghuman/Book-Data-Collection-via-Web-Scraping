The book has a very heavy language (not the kind of too much terminology, but of too much words and over-complex sentences). It's a good description of the "psychopathic personality disorder", called this by the author, otherwise (as he explains) it falls somewhere under "antisocial personality disorder". It's split in four parts - intro, an overview of the patients he studied (about 20 cases), a comparison with other disorders and diseases and a description of the elements of the disorder, and a discussion on some of the unanswered questions (treatment, legal status, etc). This is the basis for a lot of the following research (like the one of Robert B. Hare) on the topic of psychopaths, their behavior and their danger to society.
