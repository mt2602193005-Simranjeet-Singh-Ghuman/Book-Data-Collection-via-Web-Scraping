Mediocre Hangers-On Culling Notes: Nowhere near the worst of the Ghostwriter’s oeuvre, but I don’t care enough to bother with the rest of this series…so into the Goodwill bag they go. I’ve had the Ruby books for 15 years—if I truly felt moved to read them, I’d have done so already. My initial impression stands: Ruby lacks the emotional punch of Cathy or Heaven. She's an insipid Mary Sue without a backbone, & that gets old really fast. 🙈 Yeah, she’s a teenager, so it's not surprising that she'd be intimidated by a sophisticated stepmom, but the way she lets Gisselle (& everyone else) walk over her, taunt her, & treat her like shit? Shoe-scraper central. Cathy & Heaven were also victims of circumstance & adulthood, but they never allowed that crap from someone on an equal footing. Compared to the original VCA’s depiction of victimhood, Ruby has the presence of an ambulatory cardboard cutout. That said, it still earned a soapy VCA 3-star rating due to Ruby’s improper feelings for her half-brother Paul, which amused me greatly. Oh noes! Who can resist such SHOCKING DRAMA!! 😱😂
