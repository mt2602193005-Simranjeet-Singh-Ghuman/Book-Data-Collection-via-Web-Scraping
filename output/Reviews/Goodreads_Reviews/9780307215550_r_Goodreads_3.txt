The Mystery of the Missing Millionaire by Kathryn Kenny is the thirty-fourth book in the Trixie Belden series. I didn't like how little of Diana, Dan, and Brian there was in this book. Trixie getting jealous about Jim was cute, though. The mystery boring and cliche. Con-artists get old. I was happy to see Mr. Lytell getting his own plotline! Not an amazing book in the series, but I didn't hate it. I can't believe this is the last Goldencraft book!! 3 stars out of 5.
