I couldn't read this fast enough. It has been my second read from Christopher Paul Curtis and I ate up the story like a kid eating a sucker behind the couch his mom told him not to eat in the first place. It was funny and serious with racial issues thrown out to the reader. Told in the narrative of Kenneth Watson, it follows his family and life in school focusing on his bully brother Byron (By). Each chapter is hilariously recounted as only a kid in third grade can remember the events or understanding of the events. The family finally travels from Flint, Michigan to Birmingham, Alabama where in 1963 they learn a scary lesson about hatefulness. I grew fond of Kenny and his family esp his parents. Would recommend this to YA readers to learn something on history; things history books don't teach. Will be reading more of Curtis. 2 thumbs and toes up. Lol
