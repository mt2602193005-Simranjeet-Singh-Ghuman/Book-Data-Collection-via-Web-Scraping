Forget (almost) all about book one, this is a fresh start (seemingly)! Will is about to turn eleven on Midwinter's Eve and strange things are happening to him. He finds out he is an Old One - and one with quite some special powers at that - and has a quest to fulfill so the forces of Darkness won't win. He is hindered by heavy winter weather and frightened people but also aided by others like him. I loooove the magical quest inspired by British folklore, the symbolism, the entire atmosphere created by the season, weather and animals. So utterly cozy and yet gripping and riveting from the beginning. And the writing itself! Not least the rhymes and riddles and rituals. It really is like huddling under a favorite blanket. Equally, the characters were all very lively and varied and highly memorable. No wonder so many people love this book (indeed the entire series) so much! Now, I have a confession to make: I saw the movie adaptation . I remembered pretty quickly. Of course the book is better but I had no idea just how much better (I mean, the movie had Ian McShane)! So yes, I am definitely enchanted and can't wait to discover the rest. Especially since it will be interesting to find out if all 5 books are only loosely connected through the magic or if the story will continue with these characters this time.
