This is John Le Carré’s spy thriller about Israel and the Middle East. It has a great deal to do with the Palestinian people. A British woman is an informant for the Israelis and falls into a relationship with a Palestinian man. She is in something of a relationship with her Israeli handler as well. Confused and conflicted, there is no good way out for her. The author does not avoid taking sides and that is clear by the final page. Tension and tragedy. An important story. Relevant for 2024.
