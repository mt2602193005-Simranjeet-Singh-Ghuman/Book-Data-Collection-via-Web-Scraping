I was soooo excited when I discovered this book because I have been searching forever to try and find a good story from the pov of a snake, as a massive snake lover with a python of my own!!! 🖤🖤🖤 and I was so happy to have found this book and immediately started reading it FIRST the book is a lot of fun. A little hard to understand at parts, but its creative and clearly the author thought long and hard about how to write from the pov of a reptile. The main protagonist is a Burmese python, a super cool and lesser known snake species so that was fun to read about!! I love basically anything with snakes 😭 and this story had TWO!! as well as many other reptiles and creatures :)) My only issue really, as other reviews have pointed out, were the facts in the story that were just wrong. For example, it says the Jacobson's organ is on the bottom jaw, but its actually located on the top jaw of a snake!! As well as snakes can't close their eyes like they sometimes do in the story since they don't have eyelids BUT if you love snakes or animals in general this is a must read and I 100% recommend!!
