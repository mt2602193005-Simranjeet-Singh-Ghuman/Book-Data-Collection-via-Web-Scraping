My thrill and excitement for this novel started to dissipate a little more than halfway through. Possibly at my own fault, not sure. There were moments in which I wasn't sure if the author desired to tell a great story or to anecdote a great textbook. The story would be moving along quite nicely and then we'd pause, take a step back away from the setting & characters, and discuss real-life biology or geography or science history. Hard to get a good cadence with these odd interruptions. I found myself skipping thru them; perhaps they'd benefit another reader who cannot accept fiction as fiction. The conclusion was very claustrophobic. It puts me in the mind of a Douglas Preston novel which makes me ask if authors Douglas & Richard share more than just a last name. Take it or leave it. I've owned this book for maybe 5yrs and am just getting to it.
