What an amazing memoir! Dr. Rosita is a badass who spent a seven-year apprenticeship with a Mayan curandera in the jungles of Belize. She spent sweaty days cutting down medicinal plants with Don Panti and then watched him cure alcoholism, diabetes, and more. She sent lots of samples to the NIH for study. When Panti died at 100, the New York Times ran his obit. Fascinating. How much wisdom are we losing in the loss of the rainforests?
