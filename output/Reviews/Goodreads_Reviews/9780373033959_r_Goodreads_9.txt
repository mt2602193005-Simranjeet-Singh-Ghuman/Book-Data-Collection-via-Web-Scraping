This was my least favorite of all the books in this series-- mainly because I really, really didn't like Karen. I didn't understand her, although I tried very hard to relate to her (and Macomber did a fantastic job explaining her "why," even though I just didn't buy it). Matt I liked, and I found immense pleasure in his quest to learn how to woo his ex-wife, but I often found myself hoping he'd just find someone else. Of course, being deeply involved with these characters at this point in the series, I wasn't about to stop reading, and still found plenty to enjoy outside of the main storyline. If you've read the other books in the series, don't skip this one, but if you haven't read the others, do that first!
