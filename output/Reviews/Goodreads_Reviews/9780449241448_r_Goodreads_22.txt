I doubt if I would give this book 5 stars if I read it, but as an audio book, it is 5 stars thanks to George Guidall as the narrator. It takes some skill to hold my attention reading about a board meeting that hews to Roberts Rules of Order. Guildall succeeds in spades. In this mystery, the board meetings for the Barnards Crossing Synagogue are an important plot point. My husband and I figured out who murdered a wealthy raging antisemite with many enemies well before it was revealed, but we still both enjoyed the story to the end.
