3.5 stars Mallory, Claudia, and Stacey find an old trunk in Stacey’s attic. Stacey willingly gives it to Mallory. Once the old trunk is finally pulled open, Mallory discovers the diary of a girl nearly 100 years ago. In it, Mallory learns all the girl wanted was to prove her father’s innocence. He was accused of stealing a painting by his father-in-law, and disinherited. Can the Babysitter’s Club find the truth after all these years? This book was good, but I’m afraid a little forgettable. The whole painting mystery felt lackluster (although the “twist” at the very end was fun). The girls held a seance, while on brand for middle schoolers, felt random to the story. It lasted all of two seconds after quite a bit of planning. As an English teacher I loved the focus on different reading techniques to get kids interested in books (in the middle of the mystery, Mallory is tutoring one of her babysitting clients). I enjoyed it; there just wasn’t anything especially memorable.
