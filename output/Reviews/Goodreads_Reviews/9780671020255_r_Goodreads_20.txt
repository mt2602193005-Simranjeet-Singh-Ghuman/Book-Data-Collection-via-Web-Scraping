I really hated most of this book. But, that being said, once the plot twist hit I can't say I wasn't entertained. HOWEVER. I still really hated reading this book and I gotta say, though interesting, the plot twist did not make reading this book worth it at all. Was I supposed to be under the influence of some sort of drug while reading it? THEN would it actually make some sort of sense? The premise was just SO. DUMB. It also seemed like this book was comprised of multiple half-baked idea fetuses, all thrown into a Ninja blender. For those who have read this book...y'all CANNOT tell me that the Illumni "beings" weren't giving CULT. Like, Heaven's Gate parallel? Hello?? What I thought would be a light and spooky ghost story (assumed from looking at the cover) turned into a world with too many sci-fi elements to be understood by ANY RATIONAL PERSON. Lastly, it was just soooo. Slooooowwww. That much context was not needed and the characters were all so fucking boring that I just couldn't even force myself to care about their stories. It was a mundane and hard-to-understand read, put simply. I basically read this book so you don't have to...you're welcome. 😌
