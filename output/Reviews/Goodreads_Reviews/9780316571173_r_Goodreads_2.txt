This is a very enjoyable book about a Navajo girl and her fears that her grandma will die. I appreciate that there was strong plot and character work, which is often lacking from books about Indigenous people written during this period. It is interesting that the main character lives a very traditional lifestyle in a hogan but takes a yellow bus to school. There may or may not have been Navajos living this way in the '70s, but few if any live that way now.
