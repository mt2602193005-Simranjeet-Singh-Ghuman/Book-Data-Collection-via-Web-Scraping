Alice goes into another world whose inhabitants are either chess pieces or characters from nursery ryhmes. She meets Humpty Dumpty,Tweedledum and Tweedledee,among others.They are all either full of criticism or advice for Alice. Maybe my expectations were higher,but the sequel is not half as good as the original.There are a few good lines but the magic that made Alice in Wonderland such a classic,is missing here.
