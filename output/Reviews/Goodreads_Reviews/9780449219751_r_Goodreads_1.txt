I enjoy reflections on life and humor. It is evident Robert Byrne does too. His quotes create smiles, tears, a pondering of others lives, and diverse thoughts. The Contents page divides the quote topics into three sections, Part Three being for "One thing and another." At the back of the book, you will locate listings of sources, references, notes, Index of Authors, Index of Subject, and keywords. His books remain on my library shelves for rereads to refresh my smile and reopen my mind to unknown others when a busy life tries to get in the way of a little joy.
