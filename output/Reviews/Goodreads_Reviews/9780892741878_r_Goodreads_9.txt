This book covers/summarieses almost all the key bible teachings of the leading faith-cure (healing in the atonement) movement writers and teachers of the late 19th and early 20th centuries on healing. Much is borrowed from FF Bosworth's classic book on biblical healing 'Christ the Healer'. This book literally transformed my prayer life around in regard to praying for the sick, as well as enabling me to receive healing for myself. It is a real faith builder, and everyone should read it at least once. I have re-read multiple times to reinforce the messages.
