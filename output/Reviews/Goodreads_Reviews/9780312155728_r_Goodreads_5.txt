It’s not a compulsive read, and sometimes a little hard to follow, but ultimately I loved the weird combo of earthy wisdom, magical realism, free-wheeling and often flamboyant prose, and a large and bizarre cast of characters. Plot? Ish. Character development? Erm, sorta. Cool imagery and weird allegories and people you care about and just a lot of reveling in life and its lushness? Yeah that’s it.
