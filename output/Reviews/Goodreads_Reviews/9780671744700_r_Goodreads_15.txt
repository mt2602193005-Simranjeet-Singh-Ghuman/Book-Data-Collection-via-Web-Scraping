This book had story lines from the different lives of the characters, the villain, the victim and the hero. A story of a cop, Jake who was married to androphobic Sally, the woman he had saved from her boyfriend whom had sexually assaulted her. A story of his brother's family who had been murdered save for the young survivor, Jake's adopted nephew, Winston. A story of the killer, his past and present, who took the lives of Roger Harrow and his family. A story of a young boy who had witnessed his family being murdered. A story of Sally Harrow, with a heavy fear of men, she befriends a man who just happened to be the killer. Jake and Winston team up to find the killer.
