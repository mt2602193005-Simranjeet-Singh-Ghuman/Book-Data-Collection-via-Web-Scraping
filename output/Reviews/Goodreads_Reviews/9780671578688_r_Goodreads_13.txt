Mildly entertaining but mediocre retelling of the story of the minotaur. Takes some liberties as far as making the gods into simply long-lived mages, and the minotaur having some intelligence, but it feels more convenient than creative. On her own, the character of Ariadne is strong willed and intelligent, but with Dionysus she's simpering and stupid. The labyrinth and Theseus are thrown in at the end in a very hurried fashion and the ending is abrupt and forced. As one of my favorite myths, I was hoping for something deeper, but this is all surface. Show more
