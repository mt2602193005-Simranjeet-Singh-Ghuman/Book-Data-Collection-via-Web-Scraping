I really did make several attempts at reading these stories, but unfortunately couldn't get past the dialogue of Alice. I understand that this was the intention, and I'm sure most would disagree with me, but it was very 'rapid-fire,' scattered and downright anxiety-producing at times. I never thought I could have that experience while reading, prior to this experience. While the book was intriguing, I just couldn't stick with it, which was a shame, as I really wanted to finish the story.
