Goodreads has mixed up the listings for Sweet Mountain Magic and Shameless both by Roseanne Bittner. As the photo and other reviews in this listing are for Sweet Mountain Magic I’m leaving my review here. The description in this listing is for Shameless .. which is confusing. Sweet Mountain Magic by Rosanne Bittner just didn’t do it for me. I’ve read over 40 of her books and this one is near the bottom of the list. The story was interesting but it dragged for more than half of the book.. I skim read whole paragraphs. It was very repetitive… especially with Mary and Sage’s inner thoughts. It only really got better in the last quarter after Mary recovered her memory and Sage took her home. The ending was really rushed … would have liked to have read more than a few minutes of their reunion. I found the hero … Sage, a mountain man of the West to be too wimpy and the “woe is me” type. I disliked his immediate lusting after the heroine when he first found her. Mary had been abandoned, all alone in the wilderness, suffering from total amnesia which was the result of many rapes and abuse from Comanches. Sage just couldn’t wait … had to have her. It came across as creepy to me. Overall, not my cup of tea. ⭐️⭐️
