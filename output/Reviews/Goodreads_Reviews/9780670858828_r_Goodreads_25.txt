Research for a novel I'm working on set in a similar time period in South Africa. Reading this was no easy feat as the writing style was lengthy and hard to read at times. But I gained some insight into the political atmosphere and climate during the late 80s, early 90s. I thought Vera was an insightful and fascinating character, although not exactly the kind of character you want to emulate, in terms of her personal life.
