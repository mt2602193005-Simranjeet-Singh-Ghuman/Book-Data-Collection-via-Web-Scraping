Claidi is kidnapped. On the day of her wedding, no less. She is swept across the sky in a balloon and then sent far, far away to THERE. A jungle island with giant rabbit-tigers, a handful of lifelike dolls, and one young man who absolutely seems to despise her. She’s not certain why. If the stupid house would stop switching all its rooms around and just stay STILL, she might be able to figure it out. Wolf Star is the second installment of Tanith Lee’s fantasy series about the heroine. I liked Venn—the young man on the island. And I enjoyed learning all about his sad, magical, mysterious past.
