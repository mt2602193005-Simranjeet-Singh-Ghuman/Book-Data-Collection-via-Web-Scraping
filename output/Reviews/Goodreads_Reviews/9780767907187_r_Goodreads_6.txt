A little dry for me. I actually ended up skimming through most of it because you could get the main point of each chapter in each chapters' final paragraph. I can see why this is a popular book, as Shiller discusses a lot of history about the stock market. However, I do not feel as though I've really learned any new aspects about the markets, besides some small details of history discussed. If you're reading this book then you probably already know that there's more to the stock market than luck, or the latest headline. If you're expecting Shiller to solve the stock market problem for you, move on.
