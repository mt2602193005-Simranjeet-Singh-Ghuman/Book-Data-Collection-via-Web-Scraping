This book is really such an amazing book! It's about a girl name Wonder Blake whose whole world came crashing down when her sister ( lucky) died when she got hit by a drunk driver. Wonder is now a lonely girl working at the local Dairy Queen. Ever since her sister had died her life isn't as great anymore due to family problems. When a big time record producer comes into the Dairy Queen and hears her singing to the radio her life is about to change. He offers her a huge record deal then she really cannot refuse. At first her mom says no but with the encouragement from Wonder her mom finally says yes! Wonder turns into a pop stars and gets to start to hang back out with Lucky's two best friends. This is such a great inspirational book, with the message behind it of never giving up.
