*** 2025 reread - Complicated yet told in Hemingway's simple, journalistic prose. Hemingway lived in Spain during the war and covered the atrocities for international press. Three years after he returned to North America, he completed this novel and this was first published in 1940. Poignant. Sad. Disturbing. with a denouement that leaves the reader feeling empty and spent. It's Hemingway, if you close the book feeling good about things, you probably did not read it correctly. American Robert Jordan has joined a group of Spanish Republican guerillas to struggle against the fascist forces of Franco. Like all great books, this has everything: love, violence, family, ideology, humanity, struggle, conflict and a great affection for the Spanish people. Hemingway spends little time with generals and politicians, this is told behind the lines and with a peasant's sensibility. Brilliant.
