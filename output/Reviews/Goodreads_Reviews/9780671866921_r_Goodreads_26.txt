Old-school YA of a kind I miss! This reminds me of William Sleator crossed with a bit of Suzanne Collins and stirred up with a huge middle finger toward Lord of the Flies . The pacing is bonkers insane -- so very very rushed, like Hughes was given a 200-page limit, which she might well have been. But I admire the efficiency with which she dispenses with her plot and ideas. I just wish the characters had been stronger (by which I mean: existent).
