A serial killer has been murdering women...but is he murdering all the women who have been murdered? It's not Dalgleish's case, but he's caught up in it anyay... Not my favorite of her books. It feels like a lot of extra busywork and complexity has been added in order to muck up the reader's ability to solve the case. A lot of social stuff about nuclear power plants that's more legerdemain than anything else. Aaaaand most of this doesn't focus on Dalgleish, and his "replacement" isn't nearly as engaging a character. Still lots better than a poke in the eye with a sharp book.
