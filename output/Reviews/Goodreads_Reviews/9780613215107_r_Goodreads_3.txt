A year has passed since little Danny’s death, a year since his mother began the grieving process and painful process of acceptance. But one night, Tina Evan’s swore she saw her Danny in a stranger’s car. Then she dreamed Danny was alive but when she wakes up, she finds a message waiting for her in Danny’s bedroom. Two words scrawled on his chalkboard: NOT DEAD For Tina Evans, this is a mystery she can’t escape. An obsession that would lead her from the bright lights of Vegas to the cold shadows of the High Sierra Mountains. A terrible secret seen only be…the eyes of darkness 👀 Another revisit from my bookshelf at home, and I’m glad I picked this one out. I remember enjoying this when I first read it a few years ago and that’s still the case after this reread. This was Koontz’s way of dipping his toes into other genres, mixing suspense/thriller, supernatural elements, romance and amongst others too. I’ll say he does a good job of it. The main story of Tina figuring out if these anomalies are trying to tell her that her son is alive was very engaging the whole way through. I think Tina Evans has become one of my favorite fictional characters. There were a couple of characters and subplots that felt pointless and unnecessary, it didn’t deter me from the story as a whole. Can’t wait to dive into more of Mr. Koontz’s work! Happy reading everyone! 🫶
