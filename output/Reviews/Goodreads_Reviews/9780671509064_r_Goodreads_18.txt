Just realized that I never actually wrote a review for this book. Dailey's indepth look into the lives of American women serving as military pilots during WWII remains high on my list of historical fiction. SW, SB tells a great story about some the unsung heroes of the war, and details the hostility, danger, and challenges they faced along the way. Almost 20 years after reading, I can still remember devouring this novel and loving the women, many of whom were presented as brave and complex people complete with flaws and weaknesses. Highly recommend for folks who like historical fiction, women-centered stories, and/or pioneers who broke barriers.
