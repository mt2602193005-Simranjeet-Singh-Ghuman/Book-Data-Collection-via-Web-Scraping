A woman goes missing in rural southern Sweden and Martin Beck is sent down to find out what has happened to her. An old adversary and convicted sex offender just happens to be the woman's next door neighbour and was the last person seen talking to her. Surely he has been up to his old tricks. As ever Beck grinds his way to a result, clue by painstaking clue. I liked the way that he warmed to the pace of life in the Skane region. In parallel a shoot out between a couple of car thieves and the police leave two dead (a 1-1 score draw), hence the title of the book, though not all is what it seems. A clunky coincidence links the two issues and I found that this jarred with what was otherwise a good read, hence four stars instead of five. There are also odd passages of lefty liberal anti police and capitalist society invective, almost as if the authors remembered to pause every now and then to add the message alongside the compelling plot. As a card carrying liberal I found this quaint.
