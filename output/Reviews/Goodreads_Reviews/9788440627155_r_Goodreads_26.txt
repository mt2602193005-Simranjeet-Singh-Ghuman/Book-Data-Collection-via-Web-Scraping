Historical fiction especially something ancient like this is fascinating to me and I like historical fiction in general, it gets my attention. I've read Clan of the Cave Bear and was hoping to find another series that would grab my attention as well. I'd heard good things about these authors and their long list of books in the series so I thought I'd give it a try. It was confusing from the start. A wolf is a Wolf even the wind is not just wind it's Wind Woman. Initially I thought Wind Woman was a character but it means the wind. Sometimes it was hard to tell the difference between a character and a thing, or animal. And all the names, too confusing to remember them all. And an alien way of thinking for me, dreams and spirits and such. This book just never clicked with me, I found it frustrating and not at all engaging since it was so confusing. I recommend it if you want to try something new, like the authors, want to check out something about the Clovis people who lived so long ago. I only say this because you might be one of the people that like the book/authors/topic. Good luck.
