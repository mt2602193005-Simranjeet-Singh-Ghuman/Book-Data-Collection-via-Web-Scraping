I found the writing in this book to be very beautiful in several passages, however the plot did not drive me to keep reading. I rarely had that exciting elevation in my heartbeat that makes me want to turn a page. I also had a hard time investing very deeply in the struggles of each character because each one only narrates for a chapter at a time. In a book that was only 250 pages, with five narrators, there just were not enough opportunities to establish a strong rapport with each character. All of that being said, there were some excellent images in many sections, and the writing was crisp and uncluttered. Overall, 5/10
