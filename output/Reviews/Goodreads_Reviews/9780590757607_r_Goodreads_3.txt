4.3 🌟🌟🌟💫 What a fun read, with beautiful illustrations!!! Upon first glance I thought I knew what to expect with this one. Boy, was I pleasantly surprised. It is truly an imaginative take on a pet show and the different animals that can be recognized and appreciated. The best thing is that this book is bound to get the imagination of a young or new reader working overtime. Pick it up. You won’t be disappointed.
