This first book in a mystery series by Sheri Tepper actually had me laughing at time. The plot got so convoluted and the parade of characters was non-stop. Yes, I know in a series you need to introduce everyone but after a while it got comical. The lead character, a straight interior designer/antiques specialist, his gay employee, the old lady neighbor who watches everyone from her window, the missing wife of the lead character, the son of the lead character, the neighbors who use the lead character to watch their dogs, various clients who want antiques, and that's just some of the people. And many of the characters turn out to be either murderers or thieves. Crazy and fun and foolish and an easy read. I'm reading the second in the series so I guess I liked it enough.
