So I wanted to read this book to find out why there was a mutiny on the Bounty. Some of the book was very interesting, but much of it was a slog of too much detail. Why did we need to know the weather report for each day of the court martial trial? Why did we need to know the minutiae of the background of each judge and many other people who were really bit players in the grand scheme of things? All in all, I came away still not knowing why there was a mutiny on the Bounty, but that was not the author's fault. Nobody really knows why. There seems to have been so many lies and misdirections from almost all those involved that the truth was lost in the fog of it all. I'm glad I've read it, but I'm very glad I've finally finished it.
