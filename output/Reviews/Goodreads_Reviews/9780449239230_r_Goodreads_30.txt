I’m missing Agatha Raisin and trying to find some books that have that same feel. The first Mrs. Pollifax book was written in 1966 (the same year that my husband was born). There are some things in the book that haven’t aged well (think casual racism). I’m always unsure how much,”times were different back then,” racism and sexism is okay to overlook in older books. This book was entertaining. I’m rating it a 3, which means I am not sure if I would recommend it. The plot? Oh! A 60ish year old American woman is wondering if life at her age is worth living. After almost walking off the roof of her apartment building, she decides that she has to strike out and seek novelty and adventure. Mrs. Pollifax takes herself to Langley, walks in to CIA Headquarters, and offers herself up as a spy.
