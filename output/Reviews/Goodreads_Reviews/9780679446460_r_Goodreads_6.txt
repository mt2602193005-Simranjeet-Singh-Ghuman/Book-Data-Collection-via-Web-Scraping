I know I said I wouldn’t rate books this year but this one deserves her flowers 💐 Absurd, morbid, dark, soooooo funny and amazingly electric. So many chapters had me in stitches, laughing so hard when I really felt like I shouldn’t. Joy Williams has filled the Moshfegh sized hole in my heart. She is clearly a supremely underrated and overlooked writer. I can't wait to move on to her short stories!! Really was a one of a kind book for me and a new favorite.
