This is the third book of the Soulmates Dissipates series, in which we were introduced to Fancy Taylor. Fancy's desperation to marry a rich man and love of material possessions had her making foolish choices. It was difficult to feel sorry for her, but she had the love of Desmond and Savoy to craddle her as they truly felt sorry for her. Year after year she used her body to gain entrance into the lives and wallets of rich men, only to be let down and hurt. Good read that kept me turning the pages. It got a little weird halfway but the author brought it back together. Cannot wait to read the 4th book of this series. Will Fancy finally love herself and forgive her mother? Will Darius be good for her? Will she make a clean break from Byron? Will SaVoy and Ty have a happy marriage? Will Dez still be in love with Fancy or will he finally wake up and move on?
