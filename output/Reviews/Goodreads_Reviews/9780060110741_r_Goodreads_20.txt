This was really fun to read. You always hear conspiracy theories about Bigfoot, or Loch Ness, and for the people that believe them, the evidence is irrefutable. This book is that evidence for Dragons and it is wonderfully presented and thought out. Not to mention, I learned so much about the 'history' and 'lore' of dragons. If you're trying to write a story with dragons in it, here's were you start looking for tropes. It was excellent.
