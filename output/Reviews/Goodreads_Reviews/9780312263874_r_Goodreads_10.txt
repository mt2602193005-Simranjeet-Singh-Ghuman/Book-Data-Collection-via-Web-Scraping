I must say I enjoy reading this book . I am not much of a science person but I love Star Wars . Jeanne Cavelos writes the book more for Star Wars fans the scientists, and that is a good thing . She uses examples from the film, she makes science fun to read . The book is broken down into 5 parts . 1) Planetary Life : She writes about all the planets in Star Wars , how life might of started on them , or even if life could start on them . I like her take on each of the planets . I did feel she may have spent a little to much time on Tatooine . Buit it still was interesting to read . 2)Aliens : She talks about the different aliens , what type e would meet in space . There a vary interesting section on The Hutt . 3) Droids :why R2D2 and C3PO act the way they do , how far are we away from robots like R2D2 and C3PO . and how far away we are from bionic limbs like Darth Vader. 4) Spaceships and weapons : She writes how a ship can travel near or faster then light . How there could be Antigravitational vehicles may work . She covers LASERS and Light Sabers .( sadly she does not say how one might build a Light Sabers) 5) The Force : She goes into how Jedi might use the force and the ways that it could be done . All in I liked this book It a good read , the part about the Force got a bit to Science for me in places . I would like to see her write an update for the book , she only covers four of the films . Pules we learned so much since the last film has come out on March 18th, 2005. I am looking forward to reading her book The Science of the X-Files. Read on and may the Force be with you
