Fantastic Fox Mr.Fox is like any other fox except he has class. He is fancy but he does need to get out to feed his family. He has 4 hungry children so what does he need?? Food of course !! So our fantastic fox goes and takes a chicken or two. These greedy farmers can spare some but instead they hunt the fox and now he is trapped underground forever. Give this read a try and find out how it all ends. I read this to my son over the course of 3 nights and it was a lovely story.
