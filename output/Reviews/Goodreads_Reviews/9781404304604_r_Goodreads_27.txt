I gave this book 4 stars... out of the memory of loving this book as a child... Rereading it... Ugh! What I saw in it back then is not what I see in it now. The protagonist is a self centered, pretentious... pedophile! At least that's what I get from it now. Maybe I just wasn't in the mood but I never got past halfway through... I just couldn't take it any more. I'm changing the stars to one, and wish I hadn't tampered with the memory of a book I once enjoyed.
