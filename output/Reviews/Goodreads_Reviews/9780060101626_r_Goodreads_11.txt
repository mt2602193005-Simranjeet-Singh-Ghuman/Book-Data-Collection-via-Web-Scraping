By the North Door by Meg Elizabeth Atkins is a strange and creepy novel about a young woman who goes missing. Originally published in 1975, it is both a little old-fashioned and very modern for its time. Inspector Henry Beaumont has a family connection to the missing woman and makes discovering what happened to her a personal mission outside of his role with the police. Strange characters and modern witchcraft. Not sure what I really think about By the North Door, but I wanted to know what happened! NetGalley/Endeavor Press Mystery/Paranormal. 1975. 2016.
