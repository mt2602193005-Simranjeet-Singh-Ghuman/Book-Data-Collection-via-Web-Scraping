I wish there were half star ratings so I could give it a 3.5-- it deserves more than a 3. On the whole, this novel felt like listening to a close friend speak frankly, with humor and acceptance, about their life, albeit a friend who was capable of incredible insight and turns of phrases that made you stop in wonder. There is such poignancy revealed in the in-between moments of everyday life, which he manages to reveal with such a light touch; nothing is heavy-handed. There were, perhaps, a couple of slower-moving parts for me, but on the whole, I felt it to be a quietly affecting, enjoyable read. Thanks to my beautiful A for lending it to me.
