Will Self's debut short story collection weaves through wildly different themes and tones with confident ease. Whether it was an ode to a lost parent or a wild goose chase across Soho's toilets to decrypt gay hidden messages, I was equally gripped throughout (mostly). Consistency is rare among short story collections and this manages it, save for a rocky hiccup in the form of "Mono-Cellular". Despite that the other stories are awash with a labyrinthine, rich prose and brilliant wit. It's easy to see why he was such a phenomenon.
