This work is the first A. Maalouf I've read, and certainly not the last. Everything is clear and well-explained. Everything is covered: what is the feeling of belonging? Why do we have it, and why don't others? Why do some fall into fanaticism? Globalization: Is the standardization of cultures a good thing? Not necessarily, not wholly. We have several identities: the one we come from, the one we acquired over time, that of our parents, our religious identity, gender, and sexual orientation, and no one prevails over another; we are all that by being one. There should be no prioritization. Obviously, will you tell me? Not that much, considering the world around us. It's so fluid and accessible to read that it should be offered to all high school students to help build the world of tomorrow.
