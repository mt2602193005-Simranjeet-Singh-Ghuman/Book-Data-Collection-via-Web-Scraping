I enjoyed this book and did learn a lot about the 90s impeachment of Clinton. One has to assume everything is true in the book - and that IS an assumption. But that aside, I did like how it focused on the members of Congress and their motivations. It is too easy to characterize the respective parties' motives, so I liked the focus on the individual members, the House manager, the Senators, the attorneys and staffers, and those in the White House. For anyone interested in US history, government, or politics, I really recommend this book. It does show that congressional members were thoughtful about how they handled this, on both sides of the aisles.
