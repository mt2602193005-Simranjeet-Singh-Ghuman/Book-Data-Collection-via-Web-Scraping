Another fun witch adventure, but nothing to ring the bells of the soul of the reader, no moral dilemma, no coming-of-age, no overcoming-adversity.... Just a very nice story if you're into these. There are better, though. Start with those of Ruth Chew , or The Witch Family , or others from my shelves that I've rated higher. Or if you want something a little newer for slightly older children, try Eva Ibbotson , for example Which Witch? .
