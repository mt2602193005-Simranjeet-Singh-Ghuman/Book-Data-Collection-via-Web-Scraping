The first quarter of this book was great, it was about a pompous history professor going to the house of a deranged history professor who claims to have a son who is monster. He meets the son, who is not a monster, but just has some sort of "glandular problem", but he's writing a book. This is all just a frame story for Freddy's book, which the professor reads. Except it's not really a frame story, because it never returns to the professor's viewpoint. Freddy's book is all there is for the rest of the pages, and I was not into it at all... it was about a Swedish knight trying to kill the devil or something, but like old Arthurian tales, it gives very little reason to care about the characters or what's going on. Abandoned for now, and probably forever.
