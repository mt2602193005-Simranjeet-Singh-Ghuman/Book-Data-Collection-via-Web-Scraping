Really nice setup with a lot of potential, but it's one of the weaker entries in the series. The solution to the mystery is exactly the same every single time, annihilating the incentive for multiple trips through the book. You can solve the case simply by surviving to the end, even if you've uncovered none of the clues. There are a couple of bottlenecks, too, where all the paths just reconvene despite some of them no longer making any sense. The murder-mystery idea is a good one, but none of the choices really matter apart from avoiding premature "The End" choices.
