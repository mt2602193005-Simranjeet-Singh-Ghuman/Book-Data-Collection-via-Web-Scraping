This book focuses in 10 herbs, fairly thoroughly, including potential 'magical' uses as charms. That was interesting! Its pretty thorough in its explanations of ways to make a bath or drawer sachet, growing, and so on, and has extensive information on uses of each herb, from potpourris to beverages and flavored butter. I found quite a few recipes I'd like to try and am inspired to start growing some different herbs this year. I liked this book a lot.
