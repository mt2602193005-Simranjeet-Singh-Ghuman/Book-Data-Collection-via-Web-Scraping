Billy Graham is quite possibly the most respected man in the Christian world, at least in my opinion. I have many of his books, and I am trying to build an entire collection. In his book, How to be Born Again, Mr. Graham gives practical advice for new Christians to aid in their growth. But there is also much information that can be gleaned from a seasoned Christian as well. Listen to this quote from page 145, Chapter 11 (The New Birth) "But as Christians we need to do something about social injustice, slums and ghettos. We cannot sit back with the attitude that the problems are too overwhelming or insoluble. We need to get involved in helping to make this world a better placeto live for the unfortunate who standard of living is so low as to defy imagination, and for those who live under terrible political oppression." (end quote) This quote reminded me a little of David Wilkerson's The Cross and the Switchblade. (If you haven't read that book, please do.) Becoming a Christian is more than having a name on a church roster, attending church on Sunday or the potluck on Wednesday night. It is getting your hands dirty to help others. But before any of that can happen, you must know Christ, your salvation and everything that entails. How to Be Born Again shows accepting Christ and then walking the way he did while he was on this Earth and what we need to do until he comes back. It is a natural progression that only comes with complete devotion and surrender to the Lord. Have you been born again?
