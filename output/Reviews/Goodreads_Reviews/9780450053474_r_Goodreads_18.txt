I haven't read this book for a long, long time, but I was reminded of it when recently reading a book debunking some Great Pyramid myths and suggesting a plausible explanation for how it was constructed. I went through a period of intense fascination with the Bermuda Triangle when I was a teen, and this well-researched book came along at the tail end of it. It pretty much rips holes, case-by-case, in every "In Search Of..." and made-for-TV special that suggested some supernatural (or extraterrestrial) forces were at work. When I say "case-by-case", Larry Kusche actually started each chapter with a brief, or occasionally not-so-brief - for the more sensational ones - recap of the individual legends: Flight 19 (the five USAF avengers), the USS Cyclops , the SS Marine Sulphur Queen , the Carroll A. Deering , the Mary Celeste ...and so on, and so on. Then he proceeds to fully debunk where possible, or at least come up with much more rational explanations for each 'mystery' than the conspiracy theorists ever bothered to attempt. In many cases, it's obvious the legends were exaggerated to heighten mystery, or that contradictory evidence was flat-out ignored.
