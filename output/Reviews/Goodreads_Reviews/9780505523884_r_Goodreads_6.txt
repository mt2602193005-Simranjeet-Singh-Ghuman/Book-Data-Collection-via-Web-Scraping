Read this western several times. Entertaining story, great H. Last 5-10% seemed a little long, story is over 300 pages. Liked epilogue. Half the books I've read by this author I really liked, half I didn't care for. The ones I've liked are: Sullivan, Cash (books 2 & 6 of the Rock Creek series) and of the fairy tale romances I liked Someone's Been Sleeping in My Bed and One Day, My Prince.
