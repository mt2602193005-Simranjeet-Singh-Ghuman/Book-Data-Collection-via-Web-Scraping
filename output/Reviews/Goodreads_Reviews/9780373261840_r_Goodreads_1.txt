Very average but that is often the case with first books in a planned series. Author tried to fit waaay too much about Rafferty's past/personal life in & the exchanges between Rafferty & his underling Llewellyn often felt forced - & again too much background information was given. While I am being critical, throughout the book there was too much telling & not enough showing. By the end of the book (around the 77% mark) I was starting to get bored & skimmed a bit - so I had to reread a bit to work out the reasons for the crime. I wouldn't rule out reading another book by Ms Evans - now that the characters of Rafferty & Llewellyn have been sketched out the series may improve. But I'm not in any rush.
