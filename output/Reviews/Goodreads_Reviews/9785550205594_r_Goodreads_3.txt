This story had potential but my gosh how poorly it was written. The story follows one family's life through different generations. It was like a bad day time soap opera in a book form. The way story progressed was lame and the characters acted factitiously most of the time. Female characters were especially fake and cringeworthy. I even skimmed through some parts because of boredom. Overall, I am proud of myself for battling through this way too long and tedious piece of writing. Would not recommend!
