I found this book intensely disappointing. I'm usually very impressed with Silverberg's work, but here he seemed to be channelling the same vein of zeitgeist that gave us Portnoy's Complaint and other laments of the middle-aged white/Jewish guy whose dick doesn't rise as quickly in his forties as it did in his teens. The premise is excellent: a guy who's been a telepath all his life, mostly secretly, finds that his powers are fading and has to cope with the loss of his superpower and the prospect of interacting with people without the inside knowledge he's always depended on. If he were at all likeable, or even sympathetic, or if the story focused anywhere except inside his own depressed, dissatisfied head, this would be a much better book.
