The Blue-Nosed Witch was written by Margaret Embry, with pictures by Carl Rose, and originally published in 1956. This was a very short and fun children's book about a young witch named Blanche who is compulsively late but has a special feature about herself... Her nose can light up (like Rudolph) to a blue-colored glow. One Halloween night, Blanche sets her alarm clock ahead so she won't be late for the big Halloween flight with the other witches of Scurry 13. While flying over the town she notices some children dressed up and going trick-or-treating. The children take to her when they see her nose light up and they all go on some simple Halloween night adventures, which include a mean old man who owns a slight shot and an adult party that Blanche crashes while trying to back it to Dead Man's Bluff for the midnight flight of the witches of Scurry 13! This is a very simple, but fun and creative book. The illustrations make up for a good portion of the fun. Thank you, Carl Rose. I think this would make a wonderful read for adults and younger children to get them into the Halloween spirit. My rating - 3/5
