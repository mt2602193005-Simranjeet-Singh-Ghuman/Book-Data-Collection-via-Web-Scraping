The first six issues of the Fantastic Four, reprinted in mass-market paperback format (ie: small) in the Seventies. I had this one as a kid, and found it at a flea market recently. It's pretty good for what it is. There are enough comics for grown-ups out there these days that this stuff looks kind of childish, though it has more going on than most things from its era. The good guys don't all get along, and they have problems in their real lives. And, given the plethora of Marvel comics movies going around these days, it's nice to get a chance to pick up the stories from the beginning. If you're interested, this can be found much, much cheaper than, say, the Marvel Masterworks volumes; a couple of bucks. They're lower-quality, and the shrunken size makes them hard to get all of the art detail, but it's an economical way to start.
