Oh dear! This was sooooo boring. Only made it through chapter 4, skimmed the next three, then gave up. I enjoyed the prior P D James novel I read, as I loved the TV adaptations, so thought I'd like to try another. Nope. Too much extraneous detail. Long drawn out plot line which meandered aimlessly through descriptions and connections (which detracted from the main story line), and left me wondering if the story with Inspector Dalgliesh would ever resume. I'm actually not a great lover of novels with so few details they are vacuous and boring for that reason. But this book was totally overloaded with written description of character and place. I'll stick with the TV adaptations - at least the two hours watching will save me the many days it would take to wade through the book.
