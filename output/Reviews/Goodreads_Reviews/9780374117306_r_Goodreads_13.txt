So much military writing focuses on the US military. Here's a story about the craziness of fighting on the Pakistan/Indian front - with everything from high altitude sky-diving to corrupt platoons running drugs. There's a lot to like, and it's written well. Some of the plotting gets muddled, to be fair, and the twists and turns stop seeming clever about 75 pages from the end. Still worth reading, especially if you like any sort of military writing.
