A few take homes from this one that have me thinking in terms of my current parenting challenges. Use “natural consequences” as much as possible. In some cases, this means ignoring certain behaviors. Help children earn privileges with their positive behavior. Find ways to discipline that are instructive, rather than merely punitive, and help the child see that connection. Make statements that encourage moving toward positive behavior, rather than simply away from negative behavior. Be empathetic, calm, consistent and direct in your responses. Work as hard at enjoying your children as you do worrying about them. And finally, today is not forever—maintain hope.
