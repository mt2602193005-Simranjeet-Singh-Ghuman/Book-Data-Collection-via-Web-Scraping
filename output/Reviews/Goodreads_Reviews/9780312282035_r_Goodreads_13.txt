A true story told by the victim herself. The author, a successful Hollywood screenwriter, is conned by her psychologist over a period of about a year. She hands over $160,000 all because she trusts and loves the wrong man. In the end, she learns a valuable lesson about the dangers of trusting when your instinct tells you not to and begins to piece her world back together. Somewhat verbose and very confusing. She repeats all the garbage that he tells her without provisions for its error and even in the end, the reader wonders whether she has enough closure to truly move on. That being said, it is an amazing story and worth a (skeptical) read.
