The premise sounded right up my alley. The dual timelines, witchcraft accusations, an old cottage with secrets, and a mix of mystery and supernatural elements…but I struggled to stay invested for most of the book. Kitty’s storyline in the 1600s was definitely the stronger part for me, and I did feel for her as an apothecary’s daughter being treated with suspicion and fear for simply knowing how to heal people. But the present day storyline with Ray never fully pulled me in emotionally, and I found myself disconnecting from the story a lot. The pacing also felt really slow at times, and while the atmosphere was supposed to feel eerie and haunting, it just didn’t leave much of an impact on me personally. I kept waiting for something to really grab me, and it never quite happened. It wasn’t terrible by any means, and I can see why people who enjoy slower gothic mysteries might really like it, but for me it was just an okay read overall.
