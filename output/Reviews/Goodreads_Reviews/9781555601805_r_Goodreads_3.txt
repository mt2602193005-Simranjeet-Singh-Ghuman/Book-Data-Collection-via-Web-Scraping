After Rolemaster, the game that defined my weekends as a teenager. To this day I love the premise, the world, the dark, gritty blend of fantasy and near-future sci-fi that works so much better than it seems it should. System wise, I still feel that the 2nd edition magic system is one of the best ever. Its loose, free-form, and alows the user to amplify power on a near unlimited level, at a high cost of risk and possible death. And of course the net-running rules are quick and intuitive as well. I was nearly always an Elven Decker. Combat was nothing amazing but it worked and was quick, so it lent itself to story-based games where a random ambush didn't bog down the entire evening. All you needed to play were D6 (imagine!), which lent to convenience, if at a loss of nerd-cool points; borowing mom's yatzee dice is simple, but sort of lame.
