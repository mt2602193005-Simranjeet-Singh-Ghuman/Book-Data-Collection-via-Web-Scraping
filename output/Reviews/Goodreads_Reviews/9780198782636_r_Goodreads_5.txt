This book is highly dense; it covers everything and then some. It is also, I would say, a successful attempt to explain its topics from the more relevant perspectives. The editors' definition of relevance in this case made sure that we got a measured diversity. I find this reference similar to this one , but I found the former to be more digestible. Again, I take into account that I used this book for coursework rather than purely for reading. There were significant updates in the edition, notably mentions of the Trump administration. I managed a PDF copy of the 2020 edition, which was technically perfect for my purposes. Compared to another dense main course reference this semester , I didn't notice any spelling- or grammar-related discrepancies in this one, if there were any at all.
