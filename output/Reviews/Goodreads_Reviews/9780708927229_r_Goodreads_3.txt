This is a lovely Essie Summers romance set in beautiful Akaroa and the Banks peninsula. We'll be visiting New Zealand in April and Summers alone is responsible for most of our itinerary as I would love to visit some of the beautiful scenery described in her books. Monique Belfield is given an opportunity to restore an historical homestead near Akaroa which was the childhood home of her grandfather. As he had left the house under a cloud, she doesn't declare her family interest immediately, but set about fulfilling the dream of her great-aunts to open the house to the public. Edouard Fergusson appears in her life and provides the necessary romantic interest with its inevitable misunderstandings. As it is one of her later books, a number of characters from previous books are mentioned and its is quite closely connected to The Bay of the Nightingales which is set in the same area. She remains a lovely author to add some innocent romance to life. I have just noticed that some wonderful publisher is finally converting her romances to ebooks and this is one of the books that is available.
