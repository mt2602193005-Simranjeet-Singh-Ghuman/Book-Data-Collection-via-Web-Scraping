I expected a film script when I bought this book. In a way, that's what I got. In another, it was as unconventional as the movie. The script is definitely there though, even if it is written up a bit more in book-form than I think is usual. And there are pictures. Loads of pictures. My favourite part of the book was actually the end, where we got a little more background information about the characters. It was very enlightening and I liked it a lot. But anyway a good script for a good movie. It was a good read.
