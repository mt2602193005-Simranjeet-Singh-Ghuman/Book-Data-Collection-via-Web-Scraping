I remember reading these in my elementary school during our free time during classes. Yes my elementary school, Catholic school I have to specify, had these books on their bookshelves. Mind blowing lol 😂 anyways these books scared the crap out of me, especially those disturbing illustrations they had like HOLY CRAP, and right there with my Goosebumps books back at home too. Ah good times 😊 Now that I’m an adult and have read and watched my fair share of horror media, these books are pretty tame in comparison sadly. That’s why I give this one a solid 3 stars. It was decent listen but it didn’t “wow” me. Though there were a couple of good stories that I enjoyed in the compilation, a couple of them being: * The Toe * The Red Spot * The Hitchhiker Also the narrators were a bright spot in the audiobook too. Three narrators for the three books. Patton Oswalt did the first one, Melissa McBride the second one, and Alex Brightside the third one. They each put their own unique flare to the stories they narrated and made the listen much more enjoyable. My streak of good narrators for the audiobooks I listen to continues 🎉 Give this one of try if you ever need something new to read. Plenty of chilling stories in here heheh 😈 Happy reading! 🫶
