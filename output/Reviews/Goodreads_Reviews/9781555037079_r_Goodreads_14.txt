Kitty de Ruyter had an idyllic childhood on the island of Java until World War II broke out. When the Japanese invaded her island, she and her entire family were sent to concentration camps. The story of her survival is truly mesmerizing. Her faith in God sustained her through horrific trials. She casually mentions how her mother gathered some of the camp children around her and told them stories of Jesus to distract them from the sight of other prisoners being decapitated. After the camps were liberated, her biracial identity (half Dutch, half Indonesian) made it difficult for her to find a landing place until she eventually emigrated to America. She talks about her conversion to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and how it brought her life into focus. A truly wonderful read!
