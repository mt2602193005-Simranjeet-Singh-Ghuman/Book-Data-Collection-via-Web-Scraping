The worst part of this book was that it unlocked a new fear in me. I'm already worried about the germs that I know exist. Now you're telling me that I also have to fear a whole slew of things being cooked up in a lab? Ugh. The second worst part was this guys writing style. Maybe that's how people wrote in 1998. Maybe it's cuz he's a man. Maybe is Maybelline. I don't know. It was like one second I'm reading a fiction novel with a plot and dialogue and all that jazz. The next second I'm reading some sort of scientific textbook on what a neuron is or how a bioreactor works. That was simply not needed. The book needs more vibes and less science.
