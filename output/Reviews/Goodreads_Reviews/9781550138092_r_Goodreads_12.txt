I grew up hearing about the battle that took place in Northern Ireland. I never really understood it or was able to grasp what it was like to live in in that area. Patrick Taylor has written some marvelous stories describing the life of both sides.. the innocents that were effected as well as those that were behind those attrocities.. Great reading-great followup to "Pray for Us Sinners" and "Now and in the Hour of Our Death". but dont' forget to read his IRISH COUNTRY DOCTOR SERIES as well.. light hearted look at the life of Dr. O'Reilly in his small irish village.
