Dr. Panti was an incredible healer as well as teacher. What an honor it must have been for the author, Rosita Arvigo, to have been his apprentice. I can only hope that someday I will have a mentor as special as Elijio Panti. In the meantime, however, if we continue to destroy our rain forests, there will be no medicine left to administer. As wonderful as this story is, it also serves as a cautionary tale to all of humanity. I highly recommend this book!
