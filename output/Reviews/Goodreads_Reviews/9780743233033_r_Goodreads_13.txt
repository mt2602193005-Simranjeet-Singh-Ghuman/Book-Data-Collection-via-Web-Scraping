These Berrybender novels are like an HBO series, following a mad band of English aristocrats, their diverse European servants, and a collection of feral trappers and trackers as they wander the wild American west, dropping like flies along the way. The story is shocking and darkly comic, filled with soap opera family drama and inconceivably bleak circumstances at every bend. At the center of the story stand the newly married couple Jim Snow and Tasmin Berrybender. Snow is known as The Sin Killer, an orphaned raised by natives and then an oppressive preacher, taught a strange sort of Christian morality that has turned him into a quiet warrior of universal renown. The only person more impressive is his loud, educated, tough talking new wife, a woman of abounding beauty who cows every man she meets by brute force. Picking up within weeks of where The Sin Killer left off, The Wander Hill feels like an equal as a next episode in the lives of this meandering bunch of lordly, wilderness misfits.
