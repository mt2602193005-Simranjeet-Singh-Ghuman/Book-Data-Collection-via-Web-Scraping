This is a detailed book about the star’s life and career up to about 1970s but it gets less detail from 1960.It’s probably of more interest to the movie buff keen on the early years (1930s- 1950s) with lots of stories about the rep theatre before films and his early British movies.It’s not full of humorous behind the scenes revelations but it does carry the reader along as he covers all the productions he was involved in.Nothing too personal is revealed but it’s interesting enough.I was surprised at his academic record- first class honours in architecture from Cambridge- but he never really explains his decision to become an actor, nor his divorce.He clearly didn’t want to give much away.Still interesting to an older film fan like me but I wonder who remembers him now.The book is peppered with the names of lots of actors and actresses whom I’ve never heard of.Fame soon fades.
