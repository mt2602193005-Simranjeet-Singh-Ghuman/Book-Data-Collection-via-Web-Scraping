A fascinating look at the real side of the modern space program. Goes past the glossy headlines into what life as an astronaut really requires. The stories from Linenger's time on MIR are particularly captivating. One can't help but marvel that accidents weren't more common. The writing is good, not exceptional, but the author's voice comes through. An enjoyable read for anyone with an interest in the space program, particularly if you've only read about Apollo or the early days of NASA.
