This is a compilation book as Bova initially tells a story about a Mach 3 fighter plane called the Arrow where three of four prototypes inexplicably crash in the Arctic. This sounds too much like Canada's Avro Arrow interceptor which had the capability of Mach 2. The cause of the crashes is determined to be laser beams fired from an "enemy" bomber. Bova goes on to explain the history of light research and the development of the laser. A logical but weird combination of book parts. Strange, but still a good read.
