Ugh! I could barely get through this book! I used to read Norma Klein as a teenager in the 80's and I was excited to see one of her books on kindle. What a terrible purchase! Not only was the story boring but confusing. The main character has more than one nickname so I didn't always know who was who. Also, there were so many typos. I had more fun trying to decipher some of the words than actually reading this book. This is not one of Ms. Klein's better books. Torture!
