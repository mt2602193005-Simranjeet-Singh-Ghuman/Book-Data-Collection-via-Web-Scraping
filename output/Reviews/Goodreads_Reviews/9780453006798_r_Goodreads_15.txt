Not my favorite Tanenbaum, I couldn't keep the characters straight. I guess if I read subsequent books, I'll bookmark each character the first time he appears so I can refer to his introduction. As tedious as that is, it beats not knowing who this character is and what is his business there. This is an alert not a spoiler: there is grisley, ghastly, graphic violence and torture described herein. A fraction of these would have carried the story. I wish I'd seen this warning before reading this book.
