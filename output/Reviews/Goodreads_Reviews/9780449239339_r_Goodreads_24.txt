So, when I found a stack of books by MacInnes @ the library sale, I should have bought all of them instead of just one to try- I had never heard of this author, but the books sounded exciting. Written in the 70's, this suspenseful novel is set in Iron Curtain Era Europe. a fun read, & I will definitely have my eye out for more books by this author- sadly, there are none in my home library.
