5+ stars (7/10 hearts). TALK. ABOUT. A. MYSTERY. Oh my stars!! I was soo confused and soo ready for answers when I was just about 20% through, and the further we got the more mysterious things became… right to the very end! I was so hooked! I loved how this book started off right where book 1 left off. It was so much fun seeing Honey, Trixie, & Miss Trask travel in that cute little trailer-house and scour the countryside for Jim. I loved how vintage the book was! I loved wonderful Mrs. Smith and her excellent husband so much. Jim was as amazing as ever, of course, and Mr. Lynch, although he barely shows up, was just swell. As was Joeanne's family. AND THE STATE TROOPER AT THE END. My heart, he was simply wonderful. <33 The plot, as mentioned before, is intense and perfectly intriguing. There seemed to be three distinct mysteries—where is Jim? What’s up with the Robin ? Where is Joeanne?—and I was delighted with how they converged. I loved how everything was revealed and explained and how normal it was—not like dear old Nancy Drew who pushes even MY suspension of belief. I also appreciated how Trixie didn't do anything she wasn't supposed to do, haha. The climax is epic, the ending is SO perfect and sets us up for a great new book, and really... man, I just loved this book. Basically everything a mystery should be. <3 Content: Mild euphemisms. A Favourite Humorous Quote: “Trixie stared at the map for a whole minute before she realized that she was holding it upside down. Even when she had righted it, she was as baffled as ever. ‘I simply can't follow maps,’ she said ruefully. ‘Maybe we'd better go back to the ranch and start all over again.’”
