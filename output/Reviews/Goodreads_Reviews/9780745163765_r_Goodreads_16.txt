In a post-apocalyptic world, everything different from the norm (animals, fruits, humans, etc.) is eliminated in the name of respect for God and his laws. Many children struggle to hide their secret power—they can communicate through their thoughts. Friendly and interesting. You don't need to be a science fiction fan to enjoy it; the characters are engaging, and the story is captivating. There's a touch of New Age in the final speech (the dream of a future world where we will all be connected) and a few philosophical digressions, but nothing too insistent; overall, it's a light and pleasant read.
