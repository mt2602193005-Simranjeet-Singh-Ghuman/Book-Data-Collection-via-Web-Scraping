Has a lot of great tools to use while in your wait, helpful Scripture to ponder and apply. The book breaks down into 7 sections: The Highly Overrated Virtue of Patience, Gathering Your Tools, Knowing Who You Are, Rediscovering the Guidance That’s Hidden by Doubt, Rediscovering the Courage That’s Hidden by Fear, Rediscovering the Peace That’s Hidden by Worry, When the Waiting is Over
