This was a strange story and I don't think I ever really "got" the narrator's character. I had a hard time discerning the tone of the events as they unfolded -- were they tragic or laughable? It was entertaining to learn about aspects of how Western males living and working in Japan prey on young Japanese women and also how many of those young women have an obsession with designer clothes. I wouldn't recommend this book but I'm not sorry I read it.
