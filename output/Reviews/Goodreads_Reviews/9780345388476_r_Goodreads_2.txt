A pretty good Jack L. Chalker novel that did not involve too many crazy alien species or oddball transformations from one species to another. As an added bonus the raunch factor present in so many of his works seemed toned down a little bit. "The Matrix" like overtones but written well before the debut of "The Matrix" film. Also some very interesting insights on the NSA and other government spy agencies well before the truth was leaked by Edward Snowden. Heavy on the Alice in Wonderland references, but a good story all the same.
