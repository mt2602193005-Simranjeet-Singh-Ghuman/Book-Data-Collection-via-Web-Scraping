Once again we have a very silly Spongebob book that gets my 5 yr old interested in reading by himself. It's got a lot of works and difficulty-wise, it's probably at least a level 2 reader. The story is typical for Spongebob but endearing for his fans. A great read for a kid who likes Spongebob who you're trying to encourage to read more. My son gets so excited lately when he sees a Spongebob or Curious George book!
