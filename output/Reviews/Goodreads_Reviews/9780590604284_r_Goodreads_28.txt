I always loved this one when I was a kid! This was before the mystery books in the series really took off, and it was just super creepy and fun. I've always loved ghost stories and scary movies, so this book was right up my alley! Plus, you know, it was the 90s. Chain letters were a legitimate thing. I especially enjoyed the ending, when they really stuck it to Cokie Mason and Grace Blume. That cemetery scene was so great as a kid, really justified and fun. I still like it today, I have no shame about that.
