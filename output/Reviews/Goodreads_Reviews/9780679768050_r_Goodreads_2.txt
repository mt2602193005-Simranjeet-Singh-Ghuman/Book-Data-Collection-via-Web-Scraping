This book read more to me like a memoir instead of a novel but being that the protagonist is a single mom of two boys who lives in NYC and drives a snack cake delivery truck and the author is a single mom of two boys who lives in NYC and used to drive a snack cake delivery truck I am going on the assumption that it is at least semi-autobiographical. :) Anna is imperfect, makes a lot of mistakes, and works hard to try and survive in New York with her two young boys after her husband leaves her. It highlights the difficulties women had and still have entering the "man's world" of blue collar workers and the extra struggles women face in the work place. Interesting read.
