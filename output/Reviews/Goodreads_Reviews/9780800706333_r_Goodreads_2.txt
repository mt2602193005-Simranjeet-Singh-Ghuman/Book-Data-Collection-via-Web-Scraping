Bob Mumford by the grace of God has layed out this book well. I learnt that temptation comes my way to get me ready for the provision God has in store for me. The very delicate balance between the devil, God and the person depends on how the person responds to the temptation. I learnt that when God allows temptation to come my way, He removes my awareness of His presence so I am left to make a choice between obeying His command and focusing my eyes on His promise or giving in to the devils cunning tricks. The devil as always is a great liar just like the bible says and he tricks the human being by making available easy solutions or an easy way out, always to make the person disobey God's initial command. I learnt that in the midst of a storm ( life struggle), when a person panics, God is merciful to help him/her, but this will earn the person another lap around the wilderness. I am still depending on God to make me the person He created me to be and I still go through many temptations in life and this book by Bob has enlightened me with some knowledge about the reasons why temptations are allowed by God to bring to the surface what is in my hearts.
