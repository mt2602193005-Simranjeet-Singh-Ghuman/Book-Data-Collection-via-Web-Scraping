This book is one of the most powerful and transformational books I have ever read. I came across this book at at time in my life - where my world felt was filled with stress, anxiety, low self-esteem and more. One of the key concepts that was truly healing was the idea of healing emotional scars with forgiveness. And there are plenty more ideas worth reading or listening to this book for. What most people don't know - is that the original version of this book helped inspire and influence the creation of NLP (neuro linguistic programing) , and NLP later became some of the foundations for Tony Robbin's work.
