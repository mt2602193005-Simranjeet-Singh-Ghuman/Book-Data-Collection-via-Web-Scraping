Zane is very well known for her sexy novels. But underneath them all are deep, moving stories with very well-rounded characters. If you are easily offended, then books by Zane are not for you. But, if you don't mind a good story with really good sex thrown in, then these are books for you. "Addicted" and "Nervous" (the sequel to "Addicted") were awesome. I highly recommend them both. Awesome narration by Nicole Small. I will seek out other audiobooks with her as narrator.
