This is wholesome Halloween story for the beginning reader from 1956. The plot employs the basically incompetent little witch who eventually finds a place for herself within the group in which she had a difficulty fitting in trope. Blanche is a child witch who is always late to her synchronized flying group, and if it weren't for her magical nose that she can make glow blue and flash on and off, Scurry No. 13 would have expelled her a long time ago. Determined not to be late to the next meeting on Halloween, both she and an older group member set her alarm clock ridiculously ahead, so she wakes up just after dark. For once things go smoothly, and she leaves the house on time, which puts her out during trick-or-treat hours. She encounters a group children who are very impressed with her nose and mistake her for a fellow human. Blanche trick-or-treats with them, thoroughly enjoying the candy. When old man Skinner plays a trick on the children by giving them bad apples from the previous year, Blanche plays a trick on him in return. She even does a bit of "Halloweening" on her own, knocking on the door of Martini party. True to form, Blanche is late for her meeting but only slightly so, and because she brought a trick-or-treat back full of doughnuts and candy, she is quickly forgiven.
