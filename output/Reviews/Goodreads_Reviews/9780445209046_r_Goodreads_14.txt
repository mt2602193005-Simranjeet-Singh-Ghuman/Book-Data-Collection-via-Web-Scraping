If I could rate this a 4.5, I would, as there were a few pages I felt unnecessary. However, this was an extremely fun read. felt like a cross between the movie Vacation, Hitchhikers guide to the galaxy, and a third that I dont think exists. Nothing too big on themes or charachter archs or lessons to be learned. But a fun, imaginative, horror filled road trip that kept me laughing and smiling throughout. For that reason I will round up and give it the 5 stars. Looking forward to reading more of Alan Dean Fosters work.
