A very hard-to-follow story with so many untied loose ends. A note: this story mentions "an ugly black dwarf" and "12 sailors with black-faces", rare for a Grimm tale. I seriously doubt this refers to a person of African descent; since most of Grimm's tales are from medieval Europe - a feudal society devoid of slaves. Instead this might mean olive skin, akin to what Ireland today calls "the black Irish." Still, a badly written story.
