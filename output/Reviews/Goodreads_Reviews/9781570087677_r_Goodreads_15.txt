Daily reader. Each day has a small scripture plus a quote from the Prophet. Some of the quotes I found had nothing to do with the scripture, IMO, which is really just a reflection of the editor. I like a little more in-depth study, but overall, a decent book if you just want to skim the surface. I wouldn't buy it again in hindsight. I got much more from "Teachings of Gordon B. Hinckley" in 2 volumes.
