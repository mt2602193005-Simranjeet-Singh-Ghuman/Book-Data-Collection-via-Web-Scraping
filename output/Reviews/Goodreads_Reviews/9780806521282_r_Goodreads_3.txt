Oh dear, sweet gods! Am I ever glad I got this on a remainder table and didn't pay full price! For all that he has a lot of wonderful correspondence for someone to work with, the grandiose claims full of historical inaccuracies overshadow all else. If you decide you want to read this a) borrow it from someone or the library; don't buy it. And b) be prepared to wade through a LOT of crap.
