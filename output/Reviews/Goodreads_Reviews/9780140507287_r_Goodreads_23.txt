At first, things seem ordinary enough. Wendell is going to spend the weekend at Sophie's house. They're both preschoolers. Of course, all the characters in this story are mice. Which might alert parents, at least, to the fictional nature of the adventure being presented. Wendell turns out to be bossy. Inconsiderate, too. But Sophie shows some spunk. By the end of this story, both of them learn a little bit more about how to play together. FIVE STARS to writer and illustrator Kevin Henkes.
