Wow this book was fantastic! It read more like a textbook, inspiring and super informational but definitely not a light before bed kind of read. Major takeaways: the pro-choice battle is not over and will continue like a “see-saw”. Check out political candidate’s stances on abortion prior to voting! I’m very appreciative of Sarah Weddington’s life commitment towards reproductive justice, and I am now inspired to volunteer towards a supportive organization.
