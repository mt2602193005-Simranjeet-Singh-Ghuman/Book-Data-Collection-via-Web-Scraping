Shadow of Death (Father Koesler, #5) by William X. Kienzle. This was not a favorite of mine. The author explained in minute detail the workings in the Catholic church from the Pope down. Possibly if it had not been the focus of the first third to half of the book it would have been more enjoyable for me. Fortunately, the main characters surrounding Father Koesler appeared and my interest was perked. I stayed with it since I truly enjoyed books in this series. Only 3 stars for me.
