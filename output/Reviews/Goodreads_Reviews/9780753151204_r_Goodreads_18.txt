I kind of loved this…it’s startling sad once you figure out who all these characters are and the tragic (or at least disappointed) lives they lead—but it’s also very funny in an understated, tragicomic way. It’s necessary to read between the lines here…Bainbridge never comes right out and makes her point—the story can be quite elusive at times. Still, she captures a time and a world with such economy and wit, it’s a marvel. And poor, brilliant Stella, with all her faults and naivety, is a character to fall in love with.
