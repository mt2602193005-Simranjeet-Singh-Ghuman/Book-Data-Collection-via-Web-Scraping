I've never once been able to read this book without crying. It is just so sweet and so sad. 😭😭😭 The little boy loves his stuffed rabbit with a love so pure, and that love is returned by the rabbit. After the rabbit helps the boy overcome scarlet fever, the rabbit is forced to make the ultimate sacrifice. But finally he is rewarded for his love and sacrifice for the boy. This is one of the greatest children's books of all time. Just be sure to have some tissues nearby as you read it to your child.
