This is instalment #2 of The Great Trixie Belden Re-Read of 2025! A fairly strong sequel that I honestly didn’t remember much of. There were quite a few different mysteries threaded throughout the narrative and they all had satisfying endings. I like that Trixie still hasn’t picked up on the fact that she’s actually quite good at solving mysteries. This wasn’t as good as the first one but I’m excited for #3 because the gang is really going to start forming soon.
