I have liked the novels of Clifford D. Simak ever since I read Way Station and City . A Choice of Gods is in many ways similar to the former book, being set in a rural area with a threat of invasion -- not by aliens -- but by the descendants of the technologically-leaning Earthlings who left Earth thousands of years ago. Remaining on Earth are a few humans who have never left for other star systems, including a few American Indians and a highly developed society of robots. The Indians avoid the robots, but some of the humans interact extensively with them. This is one of those books which grow on you as you read it. RE-READ NOTES: Five years later, I think the same.
