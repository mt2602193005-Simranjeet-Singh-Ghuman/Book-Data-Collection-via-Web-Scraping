This superb novel is set on Merseyside in 1920. Back Cover Blurb: When her husband dies suddenly, Louise Gilmore and her daughters, Edna and Celia are left with nothing but debts. Forced to move from their fine Liverpool house with servants to a run-down cottage in Hoylake, the three women must learn to make their way in an entirely new world.
