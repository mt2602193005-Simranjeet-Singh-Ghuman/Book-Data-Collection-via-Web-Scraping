Monique has spent the last few years caring for her step-father and her half-siblings after the death of her mother. The bequest from a friend to take the money and go do something for herself was very timely. She has a chance to go help two elderly ladies create a museum from the white elephant of a family home. It is also the family home of her grandfather who left home under a cloud as a young man, changing his name and making a new life in Australia. Before she has a chance to go, she meets Edouard, a charming young man subject to fits of knight errantry. He saves her pride when they witness Monique's boyfriend with another woman. Little does she know that he will appear again in her new life, as the less than impressed nephew of her new friends. I've said it before and I'll say it again, misunderstandings make a regular appearance in these stories and with a dearth of red headed heroes and heroines ready to leap to conclusions it's not surprising things get fouled up. Of course time proves all things and we have a lovely happy ending.
