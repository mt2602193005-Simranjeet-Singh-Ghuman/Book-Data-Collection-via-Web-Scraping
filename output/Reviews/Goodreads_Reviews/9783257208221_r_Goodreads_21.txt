I don't have so much to say. I'd been meaning to read this for a while, what with it being one of the greatest screenplays of all time in the genre I also write. I enjoyed it but wasn't swept away or touched until the end when it came quickly and all at once to break my heart. So here it is, that pithy sentiment: it was beautiful.
