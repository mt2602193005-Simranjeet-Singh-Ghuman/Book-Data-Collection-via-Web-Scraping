Early Essays. The Good Doctor has not quite found the special touch. Good enough, of course. ---- On rereading I found this much better. It is tougher as later books which is probably due to the subject. In one of his auto-biographical books Asimov says that his essays on bio-chemistry might be his weakest because he knows too much about it. The book is probably outdated but it you want too know how life developed on planet Earth and are not afraid of some more technical discussions this might be something for you. Very nice the essay The Sound of Panting where he describes the agonies of writing a text-book and of being a scientist and keeping in touch with the literature . Also included his famous articles on the Marvellous Properties of Thiotimoline , the stuff that dissolves before it makes contact with water. How does the Thiotimoline "know" in advance whether the water will be ultimately added or not. A deep question. There must be some kind of entanglement . And then there is the short story about the goose that is laying golden eggs. Ask yourself how many writers could write a (decent) story given the premise? -- This was another book by Asimov I read again in Hardcover. And it was especially nice because the book I bought was not a former library book. I am adding a star.
