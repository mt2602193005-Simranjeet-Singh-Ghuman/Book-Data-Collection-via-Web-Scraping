It's been many years since I was on a sailboat. My current plan is to join a local sailing club and get out on the water. With the pandemic currently easing its grip, and summer officially here, I thought it would be a good idea to read up! So, I checked this book out (along with a couple of others) from our local library. Man, was I rusty! Fortunately, this book is easy-to-read and features several "test yourself" sections. Reading Sailing Fundamentals, I now am more confident--and better prepared for future sails.
