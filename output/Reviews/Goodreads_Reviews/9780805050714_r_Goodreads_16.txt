A re-read. I bought this aeons ago when I was much younger, came across it on a shelf and thought to reread. I didn't finish it . There are occasions when you come across a writer so gallus that they believe they are better than they are and big with it. Such is Erickson. Stick to writing film treatments, Steve. This is so badly written. It appears that the author thinks he's being SMART or cool writing obtuse narrative whilst trying to sound like some kind of cross between Chandler and Beckett. It doesn't work. Second hand shelf.
