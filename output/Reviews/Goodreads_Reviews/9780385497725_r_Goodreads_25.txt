I think it was brilliant, with some caveats that are reflected in other reviews. First, Ackroyd's writing style. I love it, but that doesn't mean it is easy. He moves swiftly and changes direction at a whiplashing pace. Second, his assertions must be understood as his truths constructed from this interpretation of facts. Third, I considered myself relatively well versed in medieval history and literature. Ackroyd uses authors and individuals as short hand for entire ideas and assumptions in a way that assumes the reader is incredibly familiar with his material. I was exposed as being woefully ignorant and will need to do considerable background reading before I read this book again (which I will). Albion is the kind of rewarding read that provides the satisfaction of swimming across the English channel in stormy seas when you set out thinking this was a marathon swim, but in an Olympic pool....
