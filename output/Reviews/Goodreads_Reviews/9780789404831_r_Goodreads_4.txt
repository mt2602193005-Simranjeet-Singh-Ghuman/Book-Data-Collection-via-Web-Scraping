This book show a lot of different kinds of wild animals and interesting facts about them. I loved how this book showed many different animals that children do not see on a regular bases. It was a great visual for the kids to learn about the animals and also see a picture of them. The facts that were in the book were very interesting and I even learned some things in the book. This book is great for animal lovers and kids who have been to the zoo before. I would definitely keep this book in my classroom and would have the kids read it and look at the pictures on their own as well and I would read it to them. I would use this book when learning about animals and for an activity, I would ask the kids to tell me about any of the animals in book that they might have seen before. I would also have them choose their favorite in the book to draw for me to hang on the wall.
