A sweet novel that seemed more old fashioned than its publication date of 1985. A spinster who has spent her life teaching in a Minnesotan Catholic school, and one who is firmly opposed to any and all reforms, faces retirement. And changes in her life when she welcomes her latest "hardcrabble girl," as she calls them, to live with her while waiting to have her baby. Then Agatha, or Miss McGee as she's more generally known, strikes up a penpal relationship with a gentleman in Ireland, and then decides to go visit him... 3.5 of 5 stars, and nearly 4, for a wholesome read with depth of characters and added nostalgia
