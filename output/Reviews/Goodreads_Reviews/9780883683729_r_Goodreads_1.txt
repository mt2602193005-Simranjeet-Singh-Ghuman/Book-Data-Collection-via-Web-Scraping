Until the last chapter, I was tempted to give this five stars. But the last chapter is worth about five extra stars in its own right. Personally I think the work of sanctification has to be progressing for a significant while in anyone's life, before being ready for the teaching here. The last chapter concerns asking for the rendering of a judgment in the courts of heaven - and that's not something I'd encourage anyone to do without serious self-examination first. Used with discernment, however, this is an invaluable book regarding some of the trickier aspects of spiritual warfare.
