Why in the world would a slow person—a turtle person—need to read a book like this? Well I don’t know exactly. For one thing it was on my shelf. For another I had never read it. It is rather dated. And yet the philosophy is mostly sound. It’s full of suggestions for taking hold of your life and LIVING it rather than just getting through it. Mostly I’d already learned (usually the hard way) to let go of being a master multitasker. The emphasis in the book is on the being rather than the doing, which is completely counter cultural. We are usually so into proving our worth by how much we do and accomplish, we never stop to realize we will never be able to do enough ... but we don’t have to; in fact we can’t. It’s not about that. Although we can’t stop working either. My only criticism of the book—and this is huge one—is there is no mention of God. It is all about us. (sigh) Man is the measure of him-herself. No wonder we have to go around proving ourselves. So this book goes into the recycling but I’m still glad I read it. There were a couple good quotes.
