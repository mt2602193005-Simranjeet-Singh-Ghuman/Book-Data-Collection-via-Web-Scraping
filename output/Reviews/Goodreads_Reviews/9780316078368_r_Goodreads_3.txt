Alice is a cat that lives in an apartment that is on the third floor/story. While looking out of the window she wishes that she could be outside. One day her wish come true. She slips out of the kitchen window. Alice meets up with another cat that she ends up following to the park. There she sees a lot of new things & adventures. Later she sees her new friend from earlier on her way back home. She can't get in because the window is closed. Her owned later sees Alice & lets her back in.
