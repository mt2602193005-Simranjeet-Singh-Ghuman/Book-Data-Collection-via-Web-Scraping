I often joke about having a tea date with Jesus. When I saw the title of this book, I knew I had to buy it. This book did not disappoint me. Most days when I read this devotional, they appear to be written just for me and what I am facing that day. Other days, they provide deeper insight into the particular Bible lesson or verse. If you know of a Christian who is an avid tea drinker, this book along with a box of specialty teas will make the perfect gift for any occasion.
