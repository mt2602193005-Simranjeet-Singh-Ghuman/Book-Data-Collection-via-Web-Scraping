It's definitely an odd read. It feels dated considering it was written in the 80s. Most of the characters are bizarre in one of those "this is a science fiction book so every character has to have an odd quirk based on their planet of origin" types of ways. The overall plot would seem decent in an outline but hits some absurdities that can only be explained with some wild coincidences. That said, what was turning into one of the most average reading experiences of my life was heightened a bit by some truly great moments tucked away. Makes me want to give Tiptree another go as I think she would potentially be better in short stories.
