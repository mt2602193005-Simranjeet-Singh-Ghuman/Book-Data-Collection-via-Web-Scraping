Mr. Fox is great at what he does: providing for his family by stealing from three greedy farmers who hate the sneaky animal with a passion. One day, the farmers had had enough of their goods being stolen by Mr. Fox, so they came together and thought of ways to capture him and put an end to his antics for good. But little do they know how cunning the fantastic Mr. Fox really is… What a fun story! I am sad to say that I did not grow up reading Roald Dahl’s books, but lucky for me, Libby exists, so I can see what I missed out on. ☺ And apparently, I missed out on a lot because I would have loved this book as a kid. Oh well. Better late than never. 😉 I love stories that anthropomorphize (try saying that word three times 😂) animals, so it’s safe to say that I really enjoyed this book. I will definitely be reading more of Dahl’s books in the near future! 💜
