Twenty-five detective magazine articles from 1980 through 1993; writers include Gary C. King , Walt Hecox , John Dunning , and Don Lasseter . These 1990s anthologies (usually edited by Rose G. Mandelsberg ) are far superior to the new compilations that pop up on Kindle Unlimited. This one features crimes that occurred while people were on vacation, hiking, etc., though some of the links to "paradise" are rather tenuous.
