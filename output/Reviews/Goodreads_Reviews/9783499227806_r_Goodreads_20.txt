Working my way through Manguel's books, as I have loved what I've read so far. But this book didn't do it for me. The ideas flow too fast and at times it feels like he's simply name-(or book-)dropping. The theme of Alice in Wonderland is thin and disappears very quickly. I only finished this because it is Manguel. Not even In Love with Borges could grab me! My highlights included: A Reader into the Looking-Glass Imagination to Power!
