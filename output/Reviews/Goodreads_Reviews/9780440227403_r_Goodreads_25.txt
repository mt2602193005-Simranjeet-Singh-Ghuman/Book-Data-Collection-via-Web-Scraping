Second Review: I read this aloud to my boys (7 and 5) as we studied The Middle Ages. Both boys enjoyed it, although I personally felt there were tedious descriptions. The characters speak in Old English so can be a little difficult at times for younger kids. Great for knowledge of how people lived/ate/dressed back then, but made the book move slowly. I didn't expect to tear up at the end, but I did when everything comes full circle. I appreciated the character development and the message. First Review: This book moved slowly, and I felt it lacked a solid plot. However, I appreciated the theme and message of this book. I can understand why a younger reader would enjoy this book.
