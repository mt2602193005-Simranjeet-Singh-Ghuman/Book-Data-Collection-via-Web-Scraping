I think this stands out among Ms. Wright’s books because of the character development. Tomboy Christina and her reclusive uncle are stuck together in an old spooky haunted house. The ghost story is straightforward, but the characters give the book extra depth. I got a Betty Ren Wright book in elementary school through the Scholastic book orders. (My mom let me get as many books as I wanted.) As an adult, I discovered she wrote more than one book! (That never occurred to kid-me.) I found some at the thrift store, and this was the only book the library has that I hadn’t already found. I enjoy spooky tales involving ghosts and haunted houses.
