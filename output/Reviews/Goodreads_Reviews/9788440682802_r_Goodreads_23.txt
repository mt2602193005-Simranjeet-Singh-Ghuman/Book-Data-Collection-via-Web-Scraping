Benford, Gregory. In the Ocean of Night. 1977. Galactic Center No. 1. Aspect, 2004. In the Ocean of Night is the novel that put Gregory Benford on everyone’s science fiction pay attention list. Like many novels of the period, it started out as a few short stories that were cobbled together and expanded into a substantial, if not always structurally obvious, novel. Nigel, a NASA astronaut imported from England, is on a mission to rendezvous with a dangerous incoming asteroid and blow it to relatively harmless smithereens. But then he discovers there is an alien spacecraft inside with a dead crewmember onboard. He starts ignoring Houston and it is game on. Complications ensue when the alien craft steals a page from Arthur Clarke and emits a signal aimed out of the system. Longtime readers of Benford will not be surprised at where things go next. The novel is surely a creature of its time, but it is a classic of hard sci-fi space opera. 4 stars.
