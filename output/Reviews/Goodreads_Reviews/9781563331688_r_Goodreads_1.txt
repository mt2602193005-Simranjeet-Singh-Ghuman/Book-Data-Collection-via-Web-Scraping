Via a computer bulletin board, a young man finds himself drawn into a series of sexual rites that transform him into the willing slave of a mysterious stranger. Gradually, all vestiges of his former life are thrown off and he learns to live for his Master's touch, his Master's flesh... A fable of ultimate surrender. Very similar to Mr. Benson (John Preston) in a more modern way. But still all the sex is bareback. And there is a lot of SM scenes. This is a good D/s and BDSM fantasy. ---------- I re-read it in December 2018 and I still loved it. Even if there are similarities with Preston's Mr Benson book, Kyle Stone is a much better author. This book (Rituals) is full of realistic descriptions of feelings and emotions. It made it a great read.
