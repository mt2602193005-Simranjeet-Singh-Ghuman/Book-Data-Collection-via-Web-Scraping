For most of this book, I would have rated it as 3*; but then I got to the end, and it was delightfully wicked. It is the usual tale of a medical person killing patients. The reason for the killing seems obvious until close to the end. The romance in the book seemed a bit awkward at times (at least in the descriptions - and I found one error in my book where he is taking off a skirt rather than a shirt) but it all works out in the end, or does it?
