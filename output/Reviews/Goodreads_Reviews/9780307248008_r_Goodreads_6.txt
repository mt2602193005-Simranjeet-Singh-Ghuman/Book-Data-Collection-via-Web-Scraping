We have all heard the stories around the curses put on the fier family. Fear Street sagas each share a story about the family. At the beginning Emma Fier Reade had the dark powers and had enclosed then in a journal hiding from the wrong hands In the present The journal has been found and is being used by a group of girls. The chanting they do calls out to the evil where things start to go deadly wrong. This is my favourite series that I highly recommend
