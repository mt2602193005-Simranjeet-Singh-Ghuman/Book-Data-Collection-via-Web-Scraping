Loved a good love story I have not read one novel by Ms. Harrington that I haven’t adored. This story was wonderful. Love the way Ms. Harrington weaves a beautiful story line where two people meant to fall in love, are being challenged in their love by characters trying to tear their world apart. There’s characters you love and will never forget and some you love to hate. Only a mama can know the real pain of having a child you love ripped from your hands and there’s nothing you can do about it. Zach, a hero, will always be a winner in my book! Of course, there’s always a few surprises thrown into her novels - a bit of mystery. But in the end, the good guy, always gets the girl! Love a good love story!
