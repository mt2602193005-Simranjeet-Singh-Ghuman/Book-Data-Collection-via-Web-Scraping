While the structure is hard to follow at times, two messages were clear to me: address your regrets before they become callused distaste for yourself, and chase your dreams before they become calcified remnants of a life you wished you had lived. Knowing many of the referenced places from a childhood visiting southern Oregon, personal drives, and other books, I felt a yearning in Kittredge’s words. I wanted to go and find these places. I wanted to go scrub memories clean of regret. I am inspired to aim for a more authentic life and to pursue the things I long for before they become regrets or should-haves or I-wish-I-hads. I’m still obsessed with the West and the era of cowboys, so this was a great itch scratcher.
