This is the 6th book in the Forgotten North Americans series, and just like everything else written by the amazingly talented Kathleen O'NEILL GEAR, it is meticulously researched and compellingly written. Even though this book is the sixth in a series, it can be read as a standalone and since each book is a story in and of itself, they can be read in any order. At 816 pages, readers definitely get their money's worth. Kathleen O'Neill Gear has the uncanny ability to make readers feel as if they are not only reading the story, but are actually experiencing it alongside the characters in her books. An adventure tale as well as a work of historic fiction, this book provides readers with hours of entertainment. It is a great read.
