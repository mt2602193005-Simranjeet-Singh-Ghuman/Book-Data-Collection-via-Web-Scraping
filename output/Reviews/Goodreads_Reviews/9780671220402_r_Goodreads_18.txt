I received “How to Live with a Calculating Cat” by Eric Gurney as a gift from my aunt Julia, who found it in Brussels. It was a very enjoyable read. I especially loved the humorous drawings throughout the book. They perfectly captured the quirky personalities of cats. I found myself recognising my own cat’s behaviour on every page. It took me about 30 minutes to read, making it a relaxing break. Cat lover & looking for a light-hearted read? Read this one!
