And the worst part is that Loki is planning on getting Gaia for himself and finally leaving Ella behind. Well, fuck him. He wants a war . . . he'll get one. And she will start with killing Gaia once and for all. Will she succeed? Wow. This one left me speechless. So much twists, and heart-pounding moments. I wanted to know what was going to happen next, and that for me, is a good sign. By the way, Just when Ella is finally understood and less evil, she gets killed!! It always happen in every book!!
