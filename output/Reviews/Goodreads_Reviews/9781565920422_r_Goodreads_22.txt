Perl is the first computer programming language I learned well -- the first language I was able to use for doing useful things. It was this book that helped me to learn it. I had tried other Perl books, but they just confused me. Schwarz understands Perl better than anyone and writes about it so well; he explains it in ways that keep it simple and comprehensible to newcomers. Over the years I've thrown almost all of my computer books into recycling bins -- using e-book versions instead -- but this is one of five books that I still want in printed format -- to treasure, as silly as that may seem, and to always have at hand.
