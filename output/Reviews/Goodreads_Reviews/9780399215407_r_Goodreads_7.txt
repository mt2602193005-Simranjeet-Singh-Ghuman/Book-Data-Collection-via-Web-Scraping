Identify the poet (or collection of poets) There are so many poets in this book from Emily Dickenson to Robert Frost to an 11 year old boy named Dylan. • Explain why this is a particularly good resource for quality children's poetry This is a good resource for kids because there is such a large variety of poetry in this book. There is something for everyone. Some of them are long and some are very short. They are all generally happy and nice. So most students should be able to find something that they like in this book. • Identify specific concepts that could be integrated into the classroom. Rhyme is a big element in a lot of the poems in this book. The students could search for it and use it as a model for their own poems. • Provide any other suggestions that would be useful regarding literary content, reading level, and other ways in which the poems might be integrated. There is an 11 year old boy who wrote a poem in this book. I think that this could be used as inspiration for students to see that someone as young as them, who is not famous, is published in a book. It would inspire them to work hard and that they could do things like be an author of an actual published book. Also the art work is really fun and inspiring. It could be a fun way to introduce the idea of illustrating to go along with their poems.
