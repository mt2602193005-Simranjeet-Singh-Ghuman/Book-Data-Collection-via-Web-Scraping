I thought it was very informative. It teaches you the basics of Wicca without leaving out anything you need to know. At the same time, it's not overbearing; it gives you the freedom to discover things on your own. I was pleasantly surprised; magick is a very delicate subject to write about and Dugan explained it well. All in all, a good book. I've been Wiccan for three years, and I've read this book countless times because of the extensive information it provides. I've read hundreds of Wiccan books, and this is by far one of my favorites thus far. I hope whoever else is interested in Wicca picks the book up and finds it as helpful as I have.
