2.5 Technically an interesting premise with interesting concepts reminiscent of Simulacra and some P.K.D works. What is real? What is reality? How do the characters influence it? While some aspects, such as changing of the 'character' are rather interesting, I feel they could have been explored better as it does not seem that even gender swapping leaves much of an impact on the personalities. But the biggest issue with the book is that it is just kinda boring. Things are happening, lot of people are talking but I just could not care about any of it at all.
