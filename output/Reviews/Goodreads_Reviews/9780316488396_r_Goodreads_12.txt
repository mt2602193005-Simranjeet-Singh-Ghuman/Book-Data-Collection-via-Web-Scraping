This charming little book was a treasured part of my bookshelf for years. I lost my original copy in a house fire and I have been trying to find a new copy off and on ever since. I recently found another copy of the original red hardcover online and I am eagerly awaiting its arrival. I always loved the attention to detail (the little ribbon bookmark is a wonderful "old world" touch) and the spells are easy to follow and great for all magickal skill levels. It feels like a family heirloom and should be treated as such.
