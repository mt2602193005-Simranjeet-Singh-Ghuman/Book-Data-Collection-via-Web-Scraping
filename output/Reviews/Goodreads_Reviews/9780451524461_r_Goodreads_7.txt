Hard to ever criticize a great like Herman Melville. The story itself, to me was a little weird, but I think that the way he goes intensely into characterizing people, the nuanced emotion, the little details that give you a very deep perspective of what is going on. It's crazy. I mean it reminds me of my mother when she goes into a restaurant (her place of extreme expertise) and notices stuff I would not even begin to pick up on. It's amazing. This and all the stories in the book are classically what made this guy a great writer. You will def get Billy Bud, all his depth as a character and then the nuance of those that had to do what they had to do.
