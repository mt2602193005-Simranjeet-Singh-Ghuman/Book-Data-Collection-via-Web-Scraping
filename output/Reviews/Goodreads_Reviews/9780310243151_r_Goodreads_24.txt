This is the best parenting book I have read so far. 2 key points that stood out is that the pain of staying the same must outweigh the pain of change. Don’t rescue your kids, allow them to experience the consequences of their actions. Another interesting point is that parents need to let the consequence be the deterrent, not the mood of the parent - I.e. yelling, being passive aggressive towards your kids when annoyed with what they did.
