If you haven't read any of LaVyrle Spencer's historicals yet then all I can say is you're missing out🤷‍♀️This book, another unforgettable and unique story that will have you unable to stop reading. Hero is a boatbuilder of Norwegian descent (super hot) and a young, feisty, independent passionate heroine from the upper classes. They are drawn to each other against the norms of the society in which they live. As usual, the historical detail in this book is nothing like you would see in modern historical romance, going above and beyond...truly amazing. Rich with interesting secondary characters. An angsty, emotional story of forbidden love immersed with the love of being on the open water and the natural world. An all-around beauty.
