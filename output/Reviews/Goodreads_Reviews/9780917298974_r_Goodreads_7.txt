Ellen Baumler is not the author, she's the editor. The author, Mary Ronan, is also the subject of this book, so it should probably be considered an aubiography, of sorts. It could also be a memoir, or just a diary. Anyhow, this book is a celebration of what it means to be a woman. But it's also a love story. I live in Ronan, Montana, which was named after Mary Ronan's husband, Major Peter Ronan, circa 1910. The fascinating love affair between these two people is an inspiration. I recommend the book just based on that alone. More though, I'm so proud to be living here, just because of these two heroes, whose contribution to the pioneer development of Montana was enormous.
