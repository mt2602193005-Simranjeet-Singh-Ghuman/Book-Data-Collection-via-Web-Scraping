I was thoroughly invested in this family and their drama. Each character was well developed and maybe it’s just because I live in Thailand, but I could see the story play out so clearly in my head. I had many moments where the protagonist is critical of Thai people and I simultaneously felt like “don’t talk about my family like that!” and “so true though”. The book gives such an authentic, nuanced view into Bangkok that I really appreciated. I really hope to read the original Thai language version one day, I couldn’t find it as a ebook!
