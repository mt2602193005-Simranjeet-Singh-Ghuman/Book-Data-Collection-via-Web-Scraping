This memoir is a fascinating journey of a young American’s quest to understand the “Evil Empire” of Communism. As a young journalist starting out she seeks adventures. Throughout her travels she learns that life is filled with grays, and ideology is never as black and white as seems from afar. Grier’s spent four years visiting nations in the Communist bloc between 1996-2000. She visited twelve countries that all experimented with communism by force or by choice. This book focuses on her friendships & experiences in Moscow, Beijing and Havana. When you travel it widens your lens and broadens your scope. It was fun traveling along on her adventure. Having recently explored many of the same places the cultural landscape has changed from three decades ago yet many ideologies remain. As we currently grapple with a divided nation understanding political ideologies is more important than ever. Understanding political isms helps us understand how societies work: liberalism, conservatism, and socialism. These belief systems guide how people view the role of government, economics, and individual rights. Traveling through Communist States she learns that revolution is messy and complicated. What does Democracy really mean? Things don’t always fit into neat tidy boxes. Most people want the same things happiness, peace and love. How do we achieve this in a world filled with contrasting ideologies? Come along on her adventure as you take your own. Sometimes you have to travel far to look inside your own heart.
