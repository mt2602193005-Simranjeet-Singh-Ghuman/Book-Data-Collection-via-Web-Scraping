Arthur needs Toastmasters! I'm learning I am not a big Arthur fan. The stories are contrived. D.W. seems to rescue the story each time. Stories that deal with school seem to have an idyllic notion that hearkens back to an earlier time. While this is fiction, the idea that an entire class would just get onto an airplane and travel to the White House stuns me. That would never happen. Ever! The problem of the story is that Arthur is nervous about reciting his story to POTUS. But that wasn't what the contest was; that was added by the teacher. Why should a student have to recite a writing he wrote? Seems silly and not realistic.
