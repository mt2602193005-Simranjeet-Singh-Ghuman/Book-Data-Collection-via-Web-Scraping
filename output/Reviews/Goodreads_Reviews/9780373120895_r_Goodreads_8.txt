I enjoyed this, as with most SW HPs but the romance seemed to have sprung up out of nowhere. Lots of physical attraction and awareness, annoyance, snoopy suspicion (H) and outraged privacy (h). I likely will up to 4 stars on reread. Sister’s story is excellent. Initially H is unbelievably insulting and snobby, convinced h is a con artist and many of the men he talks to about her disparage h, claim she slept her way to top. A seconds worth of thought would smash that notion but it takes H a lot longer to regrow his brain. Reread and upped to 4 stars.
