The BSC is always here to save the day, or at least my day. God, it felt so good to go back to this world right now. Mary-Anne receives a chain letter, doesn't forward it and ends up having incredibly bad luck for a few weeks until the Club finds a way to break the spell. Absolutely charming, I do love Mary-Anne a lot, and she's a bit different from the others what with having a steady boyfriend. I really like Logan. This book is set during Halloween so that was extra fun, and it's got a climax in a cemetery as well as a lovely scene where the girls frantically look up a good luck charm at the library. I enjoyed this a lot, this series never disappoints.
