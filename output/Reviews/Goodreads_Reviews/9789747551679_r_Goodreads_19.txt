Such a beautiful read. I recognized so many elements of the Thailand I know and love today, through a faded old timey lense. There is so much history and strength written in the tales of the normal people living and surviving in a big city, and this story had the added benefit of coloring it's POV with Chinese and Thai-born Chinese reflections. The intimacy of the family, the raw struggle of their lives together, and how it plays out against the progressing world, was wonderfully told through the heartbreaking letters of a lost son to his mother.
