I got this book by mistake when I ordered another one, but rather than return it, I took it as a cosmic sign to read this pulpy tale of high seas adventure and romance. I imagine this is what I would buy in an airport in the 60's to read on the plane if I were taking a long flight somewhere. It's written ably enough and if you're into the pirate romance genre, I suppose this would be a good read for you, but it was just a bit too boilerplate for my tastes.
