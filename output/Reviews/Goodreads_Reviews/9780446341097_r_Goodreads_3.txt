Before the Dawn Candace Camp ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The world is on the verge of destruction and Alyssa Lambert an American has found much passion in the arms of a stranger. But Philippe Michaude holds a deep dark secret that would soon tear them apart. When Germany moves in and the City of Lights plunges into darkness, Alyssa has no choice, but to flee France heartbroken. Now however she has returned. Now in Nazi-occupied Paris, she joins the French Resistance, transmitting coded messages to the Allies. Disguised, she must live and work in the darkest shadows of the city. But there is no disguise good enough to keep her hidden from the eyes of Philieppe Michaude. Can her love save him from the darkness or will he totally betray her? Candace Camp is brilliant. I don't usually read this type of book and it's not normally my thing, but I have to admit to being pleasantly surprised. I went on an emotional journey in this book that left me with jumbled emotions which in my opinion makes a great book. Ms. Camp you did a wonderful job with this book and it was really enjoyable switching genres for a short time.
