A promising set up with some engaging characters, most especially Sidney the rat, unfortunately let down by a supremely silly plot. Judith Cutler can write which makes this book even more exasperating. I don’t mind the willing suspension of disbelief, otherwise I wouldn’t be reading this sort of detective story, but there needs to be some logic to how characters act and the results of their actions in the context of the world into which they are written. Here the police are apparently rank incompetents while the heroine-protagonist does their job, gets deservedly into sticky situations and then somehow while being described as a perfectly ordinary human with normal physical capacities pulls off a caper worthy of Modesty Blaise.
