The h's character is so beautifully written, so poignant and brave. Her dialogues and her stance wonderfully depicted. This book by far is Catherine Spencer's best work. This virgin is definitely not the typical milksop that you can expect in the Harlequin ladies. H is a widower and has the typical male failings of the Harlequin heroes but, is still relatable and is redeemed by being a truly good single parent.
