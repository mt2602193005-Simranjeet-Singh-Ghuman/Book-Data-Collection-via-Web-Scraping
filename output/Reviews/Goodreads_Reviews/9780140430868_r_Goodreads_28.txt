As a huge fan of both Charles Dickens and British history, I thought Can You Forgive Her? would perfectly suit my interests. Unfortunately, that was not the case. I found Trollope to be inferior to Dickens in almost every way--plot, humor, and, most obviously, character development and scene descriptions. But, perhaps what I liked least about Trollope's writing was his tendency to explain rather than show. In other words, he would, for example, tell the reader that a character exhibited a certain quality rather than craft the story to let the reader see this for him- or herself. After experimenting with this novel. I think Trollope and I must part ways, never to meet again.
