I grabbed this book at a used bookstore and it’s wonderful. I will enjoy keeping it as a reference, but also I learned so much about the benefits of meditation, different techniques, ideas for the physical space in which you meditate, etc that I actually became excited to visit this vacation in my mind. Meditation doesn’t require any supplies or tools. It’s a skill anyone can do and anyone can improve upon. Having a book around to remind me of this will be imperative, because it’s so easy to skip meditation in favor of something with more immediate and visual results. My copy is from 2003 (second edition) and I’d love to see an updated version that discusses phones and screen time and how they’re stressing us all out! A very well-done book!
