Re-read. A nice simple collection of historical romances. I don't read these for intellectual stimulation, I read these because they are fun, easy to read on the bus, and I'm left with a happy feeling afterwards. This book fitted all the criteria, so it was just what I needed. No glaring historical errors, and on re-reading, the one story which seemed a little weak to me first time around, worked this time as I noticed things that I missed before.
