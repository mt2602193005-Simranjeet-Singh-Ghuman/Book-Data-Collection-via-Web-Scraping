Look, this is a book that was clearly written for me. It's effectively Sliders meets Quantum Leap meets What If... meets The Twilight Zone. Dimension hopping from Sliders. Righting wrongs from Quantum Leap. Fun concept twists from What If... Twist endings from The Twilight Zone. I am going to love reading the rest of this series. Should this maybe be a 4-star rating? Maybe. But This book got me excited about a Marvel comic, again. And for that, I give it 5 stars.
