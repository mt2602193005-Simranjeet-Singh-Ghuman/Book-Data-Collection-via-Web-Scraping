First of all I am a MASSIVE James Herbert fan. At first this book was really slow moving and I started it over many times before finally reading it through. I have a original print form 1986 which still had spelling mistakes in. Anywho, as i said it was slow to start off but at it gets into it and the two main characters Mike and Midge begin having suspicions about the Magic (capital M for a reason) it really turns into your typical James Herbert page turner hard to put down book. I felt that it ended too suddenly as there was a BIG showdown then 5 pages later the book ended, it didn't really do justice to what the book as a whole was putting across in being VERY descriptive about even the littlest things. 8.5/10, RH
