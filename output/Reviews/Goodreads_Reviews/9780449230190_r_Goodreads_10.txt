There are some iconic books that you just need to read in your life and this is one of them. Familiar with the tv series back in the day I already knew I would be in for a great read, but in fact Gerald Seymour has given me an outstanding read. Detailed storytelling without taking sides this is an excellent portrayal of the troubles in Northern Ireland. The tension, the hatred and the everyday occurrence of murder are exceptionally portrayed by the author and gives real insight of life at that time. I was expecting this book to be dated but it has more than stood the passage of time and is an excellent read
