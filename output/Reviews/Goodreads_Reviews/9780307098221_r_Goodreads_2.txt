Were any single cookbook to be designated ‘The All-American Cookbook’ this would be the one. It has graced generations of kitchens. My mom used it. My mother-in-law owns it. I received it as a wedding gift. It endures because it is sturdy, well organized, and full of useful, uncomplicated recipes from salads to deserts. I’ve collected many cookbooks over the years, some that I now use more often than this one. Tastes change, and skills expand. I became a vegan, which can demand more specialized recipes. But for certain old favorites, I still go to this cookbook just like my mom did. My scalloped potatoes and toll house cookies still come directly from Betty Crocker’s Cookbook (with slight alterations to accommodate my vegan diet). Sometimes you just can’t improve on the old standards.
