Many years ago I read lots of this author's books. In fact I have a whole shelf of them sitting waiting in case I ever fancy a reread one day. However I found I had never read The Men and the Girls so I decided to give it a try. I am glad I did! Trollope's greatest talent has always been her character development and there are a few real delights in this book. The main character, James, is an absolute charmer and his almost step daughter, Joss, is fascinating. I rather liked the twins too. Oh and Beatrice of course. Kate, on the other hand, is the kind of person I dislike, but I still would not wish what happens to her on anyone. I enjoyed the lovely setting of Oxford, England, a truly beautiful city and I found the story intriguing. Relationships broke up, some people fell in love with the wrong people, others made life changes sometimes positive , sometimes negative. Nothing was over romanticised or perfect just like real life. The ending is sudden, sharp and very real, yet not disappointing. This book has reminded me how much I enjoy Joanna Trollope 's writing. Perhaps I should do some of those rereads soon.
