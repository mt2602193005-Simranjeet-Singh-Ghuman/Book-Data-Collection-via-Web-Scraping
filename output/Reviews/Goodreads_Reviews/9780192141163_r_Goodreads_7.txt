You usually find some duds among the winners in any collection, but Pritchett's rate of not-so-good short stories is much lesser than that of the sci-fi short story collections I've read in the past. (And although I don't have a review of them here, I remember reading sci-fi short stories from the 60's through the 90's since they were in my house when I was a child) Some of these are reprinted in other short story collections, but I think that is since they are truly good stories. I liked The Birthmark, for example. And the one a little later on which discusses getting up at four. So the forty-one stories here beckon for you to come and frolic in their ways of thought!
