I was adding a book to my top book list and got to some where I think I like it more than those, and then I went down further and saw Tombs of Atuan, and was like, well I definitely think I like this clearly more. so up she goes A very different story then The Wizard of Earthsea, and one I enjoyed more. This one is definitely a much darker premise. A girl abducted at the age of 5 and forced into a role of power that is also a prison. It also felt more character driven, and in general I felt we were much deeper in Tenar/Arha's head. Ged is no longer our main character, but he plays a role, and I really enjoyed this. This was very much a story about the conflict within the human heart. I can't quite say it is among my favorites, but I really enjoyed it. 8.3/10
