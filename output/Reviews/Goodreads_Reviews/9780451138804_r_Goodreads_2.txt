This book is very heterogeneous, and for those who do not know Truman Capote, it provides an overview of the author's writing. This book combines abridged novels, the short story "Coffin Made to Measure," and portraits and conversations the author had with famous characters (such as Marilyn Monroe) who, under Capote's pen, suddenly come to life before our eyes. In the short stories gathered here, the author takes the reader to the four corners of the United States and confronts them with characters who have absolutely nothing in common. That was a delicious read that only confirmed my view of the author. It is the second book I read before devouring all the others, one after the other, until I exhausted the stock. To discover!
