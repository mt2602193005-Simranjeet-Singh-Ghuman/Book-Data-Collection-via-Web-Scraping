The gushing (non-Goodreads) reviews/blurbs for this book are very misleading. This book needs a massive content warning. Why? Because this book is 98% about incest and assault. Like, SO much incest. And it’s really graphic, in a way I find entirely unnecessary. And yet one blurb said it had a “tone of universality”. The book jacket said it was “erotic.” What!? The publisher’s choice of blurbs/descriptions on the book felt made to confuse. I enjoyed the unconventional, stream of consciousness style of writing. I loved the very specific DC location of the book, down to exact routes I’ve walked through the city! I liked getting to know the characters and the bit of dystopian future. But my gosh, this book is SO dark and graphic and traumatizing. It also felt very disjointed, like it was one story (about one family and their incest) and then another story (about racism/a dystopian future/prophetic vision that also related to incest). They didn’t feel “tangled together” like the book jacket said, it was like two different novellas. I think all of the blurbs that said this book was about slavery were only written about the last 20% of this book, maybe? Or perhaps they were written in 1991 when this book came out and people had very different reactions to SA. The author’s afterword was also so pretentious. Extremely stylized and out of touch.
