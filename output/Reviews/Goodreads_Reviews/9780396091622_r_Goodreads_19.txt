I figured out some of what was going on with the murder and all the foibles of the accused. This is unusual for me with an Agatha Christie novel. I think it may have been because this was a Poirot and Col. Hastings affair. Poirot tries to nudge Hastings towards an understanding of what's what and this lets the reader into the workings of his "little gray cells" too. Anyhoo, this was a very intricate and befuddling murder, with lots of red herrings to throw the police, even a detective from the Surete, off the trail, but not the little Belgian. Oh no! He has got his thinking cap on and he sees what others don't.
