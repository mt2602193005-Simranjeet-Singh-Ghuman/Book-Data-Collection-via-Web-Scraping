Through Moon and Stars and Night Skies tells the story of how a little boy was adopted. Ann Turner tells the story from the point of view of a little boy speaking to his mother. This adoption story gives a different perspective of adoption, and shows the international side of the topic. The child is old enough to remember the entire process, and talks about receiving pictures from his new mom, flying on the plane, and being afraid when first arriving to his new house. As a teacher by using this book; I will be able to shed light on the international adoption process. The children will find the illustrations engaging, and gain more knowledge about the topic. This is a great family book, and Turner does a great job making it interesting for children.I am not sure if she is apart of the adoption culture, but she seems to be educated in the topic.
