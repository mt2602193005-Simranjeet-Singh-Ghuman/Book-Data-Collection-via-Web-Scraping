Sarah dreamed of adventure and a handsome prince who would love her, but instead she got seven underage orphaned sisters and a wounded lawman who spent almost the entire book leaving them. Some prince. I really liked the first 75% of the book. The main characters were appealing, the seven sisters were great kids, a secondary love story was thrown in for good measure, the romance grew believably. Unfortunately, with only a quarter of the book to go, the MMC proved himself to be the dumbest lawman west of the Mississippi—there was absolutely no reason NOT to tell Sarah what was going on and a hundred reasons to tell her—and it was sheer dumb luck he didn’t get half a dozen people killed, including himself. Then Sarah jumped to conclusions and Sarah and Joe ugly-fought and Sarah’s mother had a complete personality change and my enjoyment disintegrated like cake in the rain. Sad. But the epilogue was lovely.
