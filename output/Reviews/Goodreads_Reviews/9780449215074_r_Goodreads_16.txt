This was such a good gothic romance/mystery. Favel was more sensible than most of the Gothic heroines and the atmosphere of the house and the town Pendorric was in was described really good. I could not solve the mystery until the 90% mark which is impressive considering the book had cliches. But the cliches were inserted into the book professionally. I would advise this book to mystery lovers because the Favel and Roe's romance is not the main story in the book.
