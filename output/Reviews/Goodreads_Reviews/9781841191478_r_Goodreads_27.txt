As the children age, the narration becomes more well-rounded and I genuinely like that because Nefret & Ramses are my favorites. This one had some torrid romance, the letters were awesome and somehow Amelia admitted that she didn't know something that she was refusing to see. Lots of loose ends were tied up, potentially this may have been meant to wrap up the series of Amelia and switch over to the kids as adults, but that decision must have been scrapped since 8 more books were written with Amelia at the helm. All in all, possibly the best in the entire series. 4.5 Stars
