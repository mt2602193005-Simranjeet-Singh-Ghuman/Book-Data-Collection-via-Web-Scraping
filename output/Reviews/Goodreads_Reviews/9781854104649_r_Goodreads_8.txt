3.5 rounded down. I definitely know a lot more about Bavarian history after reading this. Most of it was good, but it was repetitive in parts, probably because of the format of the chapters. It wasn't strictly chronological--it grouped events together that were mostly chronological but also by subject. So some things were repeated. I think it could have been edited a bit to make it shorter. This seemed like a biography about Richard Wagner also since he played heavily in Ludwig II's life. He was a real jerk. I came close to ditching it near the end when King was describing Ludwig's construction projects. I skipped over a lot of those chapters. It picked up quite a bit after that when discussing the sad end of Ludwig's life. Overall, I'm glad I read it, but it was quite dense in parts. One other thing--this book needed some maps showing where the wars and territories were!
