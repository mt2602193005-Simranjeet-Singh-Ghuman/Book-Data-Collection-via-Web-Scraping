The Friends Forever spin-off starts off with quite a bang with this book! Kristy's deadbeat dad Patrick is getting married, and invites Kristy, Sam and Charlie to the wedding. Kristy's relationship (or lack thereof) with Patrick has always been heartbreakingly fascinating to me, probably stemming from my childhood love of the BSC movie where that part of the plot is the most interesting. This book is surprisingly deep for a BSC book, and I loved that Sam and Charlie's characters were fleshed out so well here. (In particular, we realize that Sam's constant joking in serious situations probably comes from Patrick). To this day, I still want to know why Patrick left out David Michael from the invites. It's clear that Patrick has no relationship to look back on with DM as he was just a baby when Patrick split, but he doesn't even acknowledge that DM exists. Kristy and company say things about him, and Patrick doesn't even respond. The whole thing is just really bizarre.
