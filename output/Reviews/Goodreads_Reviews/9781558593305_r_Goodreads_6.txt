This is the second edition of Dr. Ruth's work in which she writes of various art works and a way to look at these works detailing their erotic subjects. In speaking of these works, she places the art in their time and exposes some of the symbolism in the art. Having not read the first edition, I can't tell what has changed, but I can guess that the new works addressed in this volume are non-western art. It was interesting. Was it very enlightening? No. It is filled commentary that is to be expected of Dr. Ruth. Sometimes engaging, sometimes fun. Has Dr. Ruth lost her edge when talking about sex? No, but some commentary seems old school. My thanks to Edelweiss/Above the Treeline for providing this free ebook in exchange for a unbiased review.
