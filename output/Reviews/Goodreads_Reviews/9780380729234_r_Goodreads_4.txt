This book made me want to quit my job, practice pool all day every day for six months, and then go on the road, take performance enhancing amphetamines, and gamble until I’m either rich or busted. Would not recommend to someone who wasn’t obsessed with pool, as the plot requires a pretty nuanced understanding of 9ball, 1 pocket, snooker, and straight pool. Very helpful for expanding my billiards gambling understanding and my trash talk repertoire, but a little light on action. Interesting mostly as a realistic look into a culture that was well past its glory days even in the 90s when this was written. Also, it should have been a hundred pages shorter.
