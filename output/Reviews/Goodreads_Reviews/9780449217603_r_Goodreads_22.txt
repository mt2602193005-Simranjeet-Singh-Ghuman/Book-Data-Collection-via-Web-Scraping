Christabel is a rector's daughter; a former schoolteacher; and now the deed-holder of what turns out to be a brothel on Kensington Street. Chase / Winstroke is a new viscount; former Navy Captain; and now strangely into the new prostitute on Kensington Street...OH WAIT. There were nonsense sentences, smidgens of You've Got Mail , and a Danny DeVito cameo--all in all, an entertaining romp.
