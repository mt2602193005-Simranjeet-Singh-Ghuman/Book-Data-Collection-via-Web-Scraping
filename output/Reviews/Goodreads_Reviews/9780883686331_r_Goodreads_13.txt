The Testing of Your Faith I got this book because we as the church really need to seek to grow in our faith on a daily basis. All living things need nourishment and if we are not feeding on God's word as a part of that daily nourishment then we have become stunted in our growth. I chose this author because he truly was a man of God and lived out his faith. Sometimes this walk seems more complicated than we would like but the more we trust wholeheartedly in Gods word the easier life becomes. Trials will always be there but we need to keep our eyes on Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith. These testimonies and Smith Wigglesworths have helped me tremendously. I am no spring chicken, age 73, and I have a ways to go but faith is stronger today than yesterday. Thank God for these teachings.
