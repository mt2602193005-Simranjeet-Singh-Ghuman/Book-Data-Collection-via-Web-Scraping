Grim, gruesome, and painfully sad, this devastating procedural is the fourth in the Factory series. Our unnamed officer pursues the truth with empathy and rage, this time chasing a serial killer. Our detective finds the diary of the killer's latest victim, and her words stir his heart. This isn't an easy book to recommend, with its bleak subject matter and grisly details, but those with strong stomachs and big hearts would do well to experience it.
