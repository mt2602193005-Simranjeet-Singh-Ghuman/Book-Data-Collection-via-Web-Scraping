Trigger warning - "hero" rapes heroine. I don't like leaving bad reviews because authors put so much time and hard work into their books. But I couldn't get behind this one. Author included a lot of interesting historical information and plenty of detail (pro or con depending on the reader). On one hand, the author did demonstrate how difficult it could be for anyone to survive and thrive during the time, especially a woman. I often felt utterly helpless for the heroine, who is thrust into worse and worse circumstances by the men around her. So at times I thought the book had potential to really bring a dialogue about important but difficult topics...but then it felt totally thrown away when the heroine falls for a guy who rapes her and forces/tricks his way into staying with her. I feel a big rant coming on, so I'll leave it at that.
