This was my first time reading The Light Princess, and I had to press through the absolutely ridiculous full-cast audiobook narration edition (which I frankly hated, but it was what I got on Libby). I’m glad I pressed through, though, because the story itself is wonderful—a mix of whimsy and fairytale madness and gravity (pun intended). I really loved it, in the end. I will have to sit and read this with my eyes someday to see how it strikes me without overacting in my ears…
