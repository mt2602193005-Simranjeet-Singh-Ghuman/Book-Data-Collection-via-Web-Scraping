I've never read a novelization of a screenplay before, and if this is representative of the style I think it goes to show how different it can be to successfully write for the screen and for the printed page. This follows the plot of the movie very closely, and while the film (the first 90s bond film and the first of four starring Pierce Brosnan) is considered to be a highlight of the series, in novel form it feels quite bare bones. This novel would have benefitted from an expansion on the screenplay that fleshes out the characters and plot, but I understand that that wasn't John Gardner's mandate - it was merely to reformat a screenplay into something that could be sold as merchandise to support the film. I don't think I'll be going out of my way to read more bond novels produced in this way, but I'd be willing to try one Gardner has written based on his own ideas, and since I'm nearly finished with the works written by series creator Ian Fleming I suppose I'll have to.
