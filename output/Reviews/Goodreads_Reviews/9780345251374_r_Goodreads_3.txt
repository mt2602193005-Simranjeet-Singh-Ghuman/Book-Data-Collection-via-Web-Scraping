It's so cool to realize that Uhura's earpiece has become the Bluetooth and Kirk's Communicator has become the Smartphone. I am not a Trekkie...or Trekker...or whatever we (they?) are called. No, I am simply a fan of an old American TV show that captured my heart when I first saw it as a child Down Under. This is a collection of fan art, episode summaries, and a lexicon of all that matters, with a the ability to cross reference, which proved very valuable to a youngster in the days before online databases. Just glancing through it again brings back memories of shouting at the TV because something bad was about to happen to those guys in the red uniforms. Even the cover is a delight, as you can move the wheel to get the show title, which then provides the page number and, most importantly, the Star Date. The Star Date! How righteous was that.
