I found this book on my shelf and am so glad to have rediscovered it and Claire Massud. These novellas were masterfully written and are united in constantly shifting perceptions. I was never quite sure what to believe, especially in the second novella. The first person narrator is of indeterminate gender and they spend the entire book trying to suss out the relationship between the mother-daughter neighbors in the downstairs apartment. Interestingly, that is never resolved on a couple of different levels. While I felt impatient for the twist that was foreshadowed to be revealed, I was aware the entire time of how unreliable the narrator was.
