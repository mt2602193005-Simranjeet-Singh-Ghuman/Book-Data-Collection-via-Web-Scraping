I think I like child-rearing guides from 20 years ago better than what's coming out now. This book is British, which makes me like it even more. There's just too much nonsense with American parenting today for me. The authors include a lot of first-hand testimony from parents of twins, twins themselves, and the same of triplets and quadruplets. I like to read about the experiences of higher order multiples as a therapeutic sort of Schadenfreude. I only skimmed through all the pregnancy and newborn chapters. From reading this book I learned you can keep tiny tots busy for a little bit by putting a piece of Scotch tape on their fingers and letting them get it off. How awesome is that? Also I learned that I am ready to go back to work after spending 3 years at home with my daughters.
