The creation of Elmo was an act of brilliance. Children love his playful, curious, and engaging character. He talks directly to small children in so many ways. First of all directly, but also in just who he is as another child navigating his way of learning through the world. This book is engaging and fun for the littles and talks about all the things that children love and relate to. It is so comforting to enjoy a story with the characters from Sesame Street, especially from Elmo's perspective.
