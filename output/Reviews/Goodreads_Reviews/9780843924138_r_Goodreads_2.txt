Solid, well-written Leisure horror with perfect pacing, if a lack of surprises and wow factor. After the mysterious death of their fire-and-brimstone parents, young'n's Kiel and Justin are sent to live with their illustrator aunt and alcoholic uncle in the town of Winter Creek Mills. But, soon, the town is plagued by a series of ghastly murders... could one of the children be to blame? Might they really be the "spawn of Satan," as their father claimed? I'll give you one guess! [i]Child of Darkness[/i] reads like a less bucolic [i]The Other[/i], with some attempts at Stephen King-esque small town world-building. Silva is a surprisingly adept writer for someone so rarely read amongst genre fans (upon further research, looks like he was more prolific and successful in the short story realm), keeping you interested even with the generally PG-13 proceedings. Everything happens more-or-less as you'd expect, but his taut prose and character work are well above average for MMP slop, and it's not padded to oblivion either! A quick, enjoyable read, even if it's one you won't remember for the rest of your life. [i]Autumn of Horror 2025: #4[/i]
