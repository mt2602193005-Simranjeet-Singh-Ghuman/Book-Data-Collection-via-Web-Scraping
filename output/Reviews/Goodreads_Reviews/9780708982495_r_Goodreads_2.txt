An episodic tale of a young, abused, indentured servant's rise to a wealthy landowner in 18th century England. Girls grandfather dies and wills his property on the other side of England to his servant granddaughter. She walks clear across the country alone, except for a big gray dog she picks up. Her many adventures and loves comprise the book, and it is a good one. I would give this 4 1/2 stars if I could. Highly recommended.
