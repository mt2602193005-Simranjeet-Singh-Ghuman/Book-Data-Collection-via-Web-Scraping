This thing was pretty much a train wreck. There were so many things going on, different timelines and spirits and people (real and imagined) and dream sequences and turtles…huh…wait…turtles?? Yep. Turtles. Hey, don’t ask me, I already told you it was a train wreck. The really weird part was that I actually liked parts of it and parts were written well despite the lack of focus and coherent storyline. Unfortunately, I can only give this one a wee over the dreaded one star. Too bad, I really wanted to like it a whole lot more...but it really was a train wreck.
