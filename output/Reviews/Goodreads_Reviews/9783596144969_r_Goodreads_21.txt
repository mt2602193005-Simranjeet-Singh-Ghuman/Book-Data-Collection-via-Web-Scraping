This started off really great and then I began to like it less and less. I didn't care for love interest Nicholas at all and Phil's best friend Kat turned out to be rather unsympathetic as well. The second half of the book contained So Much Drama and how cliché was the test a lesbian character put Phil through to determine whether he was a 'homo'*? I had expected better from a gay author. So, 4-4.5 stars at first and 2.5 stars by the end, rounded up to 3 because the writing was very good. *original German: Tunte, Dutch translation: mietje. I don't know which derogatory term was used in the English translation.
