How fun to spend a few hours with my first favorite detective and his charming, if not slightly befuddled, sidekick. How I managed to miss this one is beyond me, but I really enjoyed reading this early Poirot. Certainly Ms Christie fine tuned her characters as the series continued, but I was not surprised that Hercule’s mustache was an early sartorial priority for our “egg-shaped’ friend. Slightly melodramatic, Christie threw a lot of tropes in here including some slightly complicated, double-crossing lovers. Plus some added over the top fun included a younger Hastings in love. They just don’t make them like this anymore… now to see if PBS produced this one with David Suchet as the Belgian. 😎 (Reviewed 3/5/22)
