I normally avoid weighty novels these days but this came to me with the highest recommendation. The setting is the early West with a romantic theme that is sometimes at the fore and others it recedes into the background (not that its completely forgotten of course) as Luke and Lettie struggle against all odds to forge a future out of the wilderness with sheer grit and determination and in an environment that is at once completely hostile and unforgiving. It's all in here, marauding indians, desperados, kidnap, revenge, tragedy, passion, jealousy, betrayal... I loathe cheating and have trashed many a good novel with this theme. My heart dropped and its extremely unlikely that supremely proud frontiersman Luke would have been as forgiving (or even close or as quickly) had Lettie done the dirty on him. The punishment should fit the crime, he should have had to work harder, a whole lot harder for Lettie's forgiveness and she should have made him after all the tragedy that brought this about did not only happen to him. If you like epic pioneer sagas (590 pages long) with two inherently strong characters who love each other but temporarily lose sight of that love under the constant weight of tragedy that plagues them on the wild frontier you just might like this book. 1994/590 pages/Bantam Bks
