Type: Nonfiction Awards: None Summary: This book goes through animals that are wild and gives facts of each one. This book also provides questions which can give me prompts. Critique: This book is good for those who want to know more about animals. Prompts: Some of the prompts provided are What do pandas munch? Does a tiger need stripes? What do trunks do? So this helps ask students if they know about these animals and the text will have the answers. Elements: I can use this book to introduce to young children how to do basic research.
