While this book of stories is slated for young adults, it's actually for older ones too. Legends, based upon historical fact, are simply tales told from word of mouth until someone finally writes it down. By then, as with any child's game of telephone, it changes from person to person; and what is told at the end is so far off from the truth. This book enhances the stories with photographs of factual people and places, so if you have visited a place, for example: Mesa Verde National Park; you can see the Cliff Palace in it's actual glory. Being a writer of the Four Corners area of the USA Southwest, this book is definitely having a place in my research library.
