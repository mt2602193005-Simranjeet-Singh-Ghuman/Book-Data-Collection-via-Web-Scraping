I thoroughly enjoyed this book (have read it more than once) and it held my interest right to the end. It takes place both in the past and the present. There is mystery, suspense and the interplay of both good and evil. It is about the reincarnation of people from the past appearing in the present and the theme running through it begins with the love of a young priest for Celia de Bohun and hid love of his God. No spoilers but do give it a try if you like this type of historical novel.
