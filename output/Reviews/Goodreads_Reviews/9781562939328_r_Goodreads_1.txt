A children's first book about horses technically with the reader being introduced to horses, what they look like as adults and as foals. It also explains how adult horses mostly live today, a bit of their history with humans and of course what some horses are used for today. The writing is very simple to read and understand while it would make for a great learning-to-read book for children, especially those interested in either horses and/or animals. What really makes this book special though is the brightly colored illustrations of the horses with gorgeous details and some indirect nods to famous horses from literature. Again a great starter book for readers on horses without being too verbose yet as a result it won't capture the minds of children who really do want to know more about these powerful animals than just a brief scan.
