3.5/5 rounded up Despite a few issues I did really enjoy this. I enjoyed it more for the story than the characters though. It's definitely showing it's age, because we have teenage boys with the emotional range of toddlers. EVERYTHING (no joke, basic interactions with literally anyone) causes them frustration and humilation and immediately leads to anger and bad behaviour. For one boy it involves lashing out violently and gaining enjoyment from the power of doing that. It's all very awkward. I did love how we have this typical fantasy, chosen ones, a journey and learning of magic to face a great evil. Then we get slapped with some scifi elements out of nowhere and it was honestly really fun. This made the world so very interesting and I'm eager to learn more about what happened in the past. I do hope the character work improves though.
