Wherever you were, Faiz, that language spoke to you; and when you heard it, you were alone — in Tunis, Beirut, London, or Moscow. Those poets’ laments concealed, as yours revealed, the sorrows of a broken time. You knew Ghalib was right: blood must not merely follow routine, must not just flow as the veins’ uninterrupted river. Sometimes it must flood the eyes, surprise them by being clear as water.
