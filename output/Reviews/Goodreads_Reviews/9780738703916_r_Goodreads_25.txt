Blue Is For Nightmares is the first book in this young adult paranormal fantasy series. I was immediately captivated by the author’s writing style and the storyline of this book. I especially loved the witchy and magical aspects of the story. This book had such a unique plot and I loved all the characters in this one. I flew through reading Blue Is For Nightmares and I can’t wait to read the other books in this series to see where the story goes.
