I've had this Little Golden Book for way longer than I've had a child to read it to. I encountered it at least ten years ago when I was looking for Christmas books for my house. I loved the illustrations, so I bought it. Now I do have a child to read/sing it to. And I still love the illustrations. And so does my five-year-old son. And my son really loves it when we add the words "with Molly Kitten" to the end of the song, but that is another story.
