I liked it! Light workplace thriller that keeps you reading because the characters are interesting and compelling. The plot isn't a light subject at all, the story unfolds from a psychologist's perceptive and sometimes empathy can be a luxury and a burden. He is suspected of the brutal murder of his girlfriend, and then there are other brutal murders. The novel takes you into a world from the perspective of those who grieve these victims when there is no justice. When someone you love is the victim of a crime that goes unsolved, and everyone looks at you with doubt. What would you do?
