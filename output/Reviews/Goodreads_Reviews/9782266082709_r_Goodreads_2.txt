In the recent years, I've read quite a few French classics and loved almost all of them. Unfortunately, I cannot say the same thing about this novel. Ok, I've read the subject before and it wasn't anything new. Young man tries to enter the high society by all means necessary. However, this wasn't the problem. Yes, it was long and boring but the writing was what bugged me the most. Too wordy and the love stories were quite unbelievable. It was supposed to be a satire but I did not feel the humour, the effort only annoyed me. It was ok, i even liked some parts but overall, not my favourite French Classic.
