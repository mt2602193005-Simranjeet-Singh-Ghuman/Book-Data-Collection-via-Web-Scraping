An enjoyable and informative book. Although I was a big fan of many of the Seattle based bands back in the day and to some extent still now, I wasn't really aware of the undercurrents and social/commercial wranglings that were going on at the time and just loved the music. This book certainly filled in a lot of the gaps and went some way to explaining many of the events that happened around the commercialisation of what was originally a pretty spontaneous grass roots musical movement. I've got to say though that I am glad the fashion for men in a pairing of shorts and long johns soon faded out!
