Warning to potential readers - don't assume this is your run of the mill old-skool bodice ripper. Yes, the heroine is kidnapped and sold into slavery, but for reasons she's able to keep her virtue, so there's not all that rapey stuff one finds in most of the older harem/slave books from back in the day. Hooray, some of you might say, but I'm going to have to burst that bubble, because unfortunately nothing else happens except our merry band of captives and captors do nothing for 95% of the book except travel across the desert, set up camp each night in a different place, cook, drink coffee, sleep, break up came the next morning and start all over again. Over and over and over and over and over and over again. Then towards the end there's a hint of maybe some romance, but by that time the reader is so numb from boredom one doesn't care anymore, plus there really isn't any real chemistry between the H and h. The sex scenes are pretty tame too, so don't pick this one up expecting sexed up wallpaper. I do give the author credit and the extra star for taking the time and effort to research 19C Arabia, but she just wasn't able to bring the era and settings to life for me. YMMV.
