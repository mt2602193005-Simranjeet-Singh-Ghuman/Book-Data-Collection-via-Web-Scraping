So much fun! The kids and I listened to this on audio because David Tennant does the narration and it was excellent. When reading along with the text, one of us noticed that our print edition changed a number of words. For example, pocketbook was changed to wallet. Yes, an American is more likely to say wallet than pocketbook but this book was not written by an American and I hate it when publishers change books when they are published in the states. If I had known changes were made, slight as they might be, I would have ordered an edition from the UK.
