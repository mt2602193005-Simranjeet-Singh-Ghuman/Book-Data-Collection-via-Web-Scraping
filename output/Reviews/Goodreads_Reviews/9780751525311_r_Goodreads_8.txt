Really good opening which intrigued me when I picked it up in the library. I knew it was part of a series but hadn't realised it was No. 24. I've not read any of the others. Meticulously historically researched but could perhaps do with an acknowlegements or bibliography. A scene from Lizzie's schooldays about the buttonhole is neatly lifted from A London Girl of the 1880s by Molly Hughes. If I can tell exactly what book you used in your research it would be polite to acknowledge your sources. The plot meanders and is almost too crammed full of historical detail to work as a novel. I like a bit more structure. I might try another in the series but the author isn't the next Georgette Heyer.
