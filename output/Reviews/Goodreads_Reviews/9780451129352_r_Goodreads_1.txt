This novel centres on Bili Morguhn and the events just after those related in "The savage mountains" the earthquake has hit and everyone was running for their lives. Bili is now dying and is journeying in his mind to over 70 years back to that time and how after he and his band joined Prince Byruhn and his crew of half man, half giants. Aid is given in the aftermath of the quake but is it free or does Byruhn want something off Bili in return...?
