For Ages: 3-7 Summary: Illustrations that take a bear through a house in the forest as he eats food, takes a nap, and then gets chased out. Review: A book that tells a story without using any words. The reversal version of Goldie locks and the three bears. Classroom Use: To show the children how a story can be told without using text, and to show an original story portrayed in a different way showing them stories have different angles. I could have the children draw out a story and have the other students come up with a story to see if it matches.
