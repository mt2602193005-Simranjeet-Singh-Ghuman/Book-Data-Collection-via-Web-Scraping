The author of this poem is Nikki Giovvani. The illustrator is Tomie Depaola. This poem has an A,B, C,B pattern in the first stanza. In the second stanza it has the same A,B,C,B pattern. I would definitely try and get a couple of books that have poems for my classroom. Like this one it has a lot of poems in it. All the poems are short, which means you could read them in a read aloud to the students and get more read in a certain period of time. It has illustrations in it, so I could ask the students to analyze the pictures in this book. Then when they are done analyzing the pictures I would have them pick their favorite verse of the poem and explain why.
