Deep play is about the kinds of activities that connect us to the eternal flow of life, bringing complete presence, aliveness and often an experience of ecstasy or transcendence. In this book, Diane Ackerman explores this concept with a playfulness and zest that is highly contagious. Of course she not only studied deep play as a writer but especially as a brave participant in some of the many ways the benefits of deep play can be found. Whether you are an extreme athlete, a lover of the natural world, an artist, or a seeker of deep spiritual experience, this book will resonate not only in your heart but most probably in your very soul.
