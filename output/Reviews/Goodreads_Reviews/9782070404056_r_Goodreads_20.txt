This attempted to answer the question, "Why does God allow good people to suffer?" It follows the tragic life of a poor, lonely, and simple girl who dreams of love and happiness but cannot attain it. This is paralleled by the story of a man who cheats his way through life, uses people for his own selfish gain, and ultimately succeeds in achieving his goals. I wanted to quit reading, because it was painful to watch both characters' lives. Yet, Endo's writing style gave me a sense that this story was going to be worth my while to see through to the end; he had something important to say. I'm glad I stuck it out. The conclusion is truly profound.
