An easy 5 shiny stars rating from me! 💫💫💫💫💫 Oh my goodness! Such a beautiful treasure of a story! I absolutely loved every second of it 💛! This story takes place in Georgia and Sam, the main character, is a sweetheart! He's 81 years old and his lovely wife just passed away. After 57 years of marriage, he's alone. Neelie, who has been working with him and his wife, is a funny person! She makes sure that his children go to take care of Sam. Soon, Sam sees a white dog on his front porch liking up grease. Sam thinks he must be a stray and takes him in. The funny thing is that no one else sees white dog. Eventually, two of his daughters see him dancing with white dog! This story is true!! It's a story of love, family, devotion a pure heart felt delight! I very highly recommend this emotionally moving book to everyone!!! Enjoy and Happy Reading 🤍🤍
