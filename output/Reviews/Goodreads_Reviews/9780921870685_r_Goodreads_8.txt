This is a very strange portal fantasy that wraps up a bit too quickly at the end as if the author ran out of ideas which is a shame. It is one of those, I really don't quite understand what is going on but I like the ride and the mystery of it and it has great momentum so it is enjoyable. These types of stories are quite important. Very great on the imaginary level, making the portal world of the egg feel quite real. The creatures of the world stand out a lot for their strangeness. Each encounter is strange so that it carries that kind of spirit that Alice in Wonderland has. Except in this case, the creatures are usually malevolent. The world of the egg is threatened by a dwarf God Gho. He was not born a dwarf but forsook his mother and thus became one. Just interesting on a purely imaginary level. I recommend this for those looking for an interesting escape. Show more
