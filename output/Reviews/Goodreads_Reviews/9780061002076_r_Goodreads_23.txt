1970's Macho spy thriller. Plenty of twists and turns, exciting if sometimes the motivations for some twists are vague or unlikely. The last outing for the nameless spy that first appeared in the classic Ipcress Files - this is not up to that standard but a good one. In previous books much of the fun is derived from the wise cracking nameless Spy - this time the wordplay is doubled with the addition of a partner Major Mann. A worthwhile flash back to the 1970's.
