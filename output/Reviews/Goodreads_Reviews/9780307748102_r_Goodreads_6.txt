This is a story about a little girl that goes to visit her sick grandmother. She does not follow her mother's directions to stay on the path and when she leaves the path, she runs into a wolf that asks where she's going. She tells him and then the wolf beats her to the grandmother's house, eats the grandmother and tricks the girl. He then eats the girl but both are saved when the hunter comes and finds the wolf. I have always been torn with this story. On one hand, I think it is a good story for kids and on the other hand, i wonder if there is a way the story could be told without them needing to be eaten. (Traditional Lit)
