Author T. L. Tedrow picks up where Laura Ingalls Wilder left off by novelizing her further (hypothetical?) adventures in Missouri with Manly. As a big fan of Wilder's, I eagerly plowed through this book only to be sorely disappointed by Tedrow's pedantic and juvenile writing ability. Although the concept is great, the characters and dialogue aren't at all believable. Writing: D- Plot: C Level: Easy
