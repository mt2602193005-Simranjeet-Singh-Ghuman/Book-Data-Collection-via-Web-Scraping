Where are the numbers? This book is great for advice; however, it doesn't teach you a system of how to keep score of studying. It could have been more quantitative, with spreadsheets, baselines, and formulas. Like ball players, many students get excited when they see their stats. Once again, this book has great advice. If you are scared of numbers, this book is excellent. It was a fun read. I would recommend it to young students. The reason that I gave my rating is because education has become so competitive that parents and students demand to see improvement. I thought that this book would tell how to keep score.
