I really enjoyed this book. It was a Fahrenheit 451 take on art, instead of books. In order to make more room for new art and artists, old art is taken and destroyed. The pick-up artists is the man who is in charge of rounding up all the old art - ranging from paintings to music. He become enthralled with a record he picks up and wants to listen to it, so he sets out to find a record player. Even though it has been over a year since I read this book, I still think about the story and the characters in it.
