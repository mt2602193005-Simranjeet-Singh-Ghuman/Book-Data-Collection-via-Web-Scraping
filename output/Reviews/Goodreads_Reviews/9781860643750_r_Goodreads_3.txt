If you feel like the world is messed up, read books about the Mongol crusades! So violent!! In many ways, we've never had it so good! I picked this up in a second hand book shop and I learned a lot but there was a lot that I couldn't follow. The names of tribes, places that no longer have those names and leaders often got lost to me but I was able to follow the general trajectory of the raids. Basically, the Mongos went north, east, south and west and they could not be stopped. When they raided a town, they killed indescriminately and when the raids were over they divided up the people into three groups: the artisans were sent back to Mongol territory to bring their crafts and trades to the Mongols, the women were raped and enslaved, the rest were used as cannon fodder for the next battle. Repeat. Human lives had very little value. When the Mongols decided to slaughter an entire town, each soldier was given 300 people at a time to behead. When Genghis Khan died, his heir ordered 40 beautiful women chosen from high-ranking families to be slaughtered on his grave to serve him in the afterlife. It's hard to imagine living during this time of constant death. Be glad you don't!
