H.G. Wells is the kind of dude who will give me nightmares on a daily basis. Seriously, I had no idea that a sci-fi could make me sob like a maniac. At the time when this was written, the British Empire was the apex predator of the globe. The author did an amazing job here forcing his Victorian readers to experience what it is like to be on the receiving end of a technologically superior, colonizing force. It is a humbling, terrifying reversal of roles. H.G. Wells invented a nightmare that hasn't ended. The War of the Worlds is an alien invasion story not because of the technology it predicts, but because of the primal fear it taps into. Martians aren't little green men. They are cephalopod-like entities encased in towering, three-legged fighting machines. The imagery of these metallic giants wading through the English countryside, howling "Ulla! Ulla!", is still haunting over a century later. They don't just kill. They harvest. ᯓ ✈︎ Pre-Read Need to read this after watching the fantabulous movie!
