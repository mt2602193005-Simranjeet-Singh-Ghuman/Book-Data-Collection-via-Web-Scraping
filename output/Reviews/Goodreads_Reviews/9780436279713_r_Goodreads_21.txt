Excellent insight into the islands. Only took me 7 years to finish it! Actually, I read half over Christmas 2012, and finished it this Christmas, both coincided with trips to Little Cayman, so I was able to experience the Caribbean firsthand. There are obviously more islands that Michener was able to cover in any depth, but the Caribbean experience seems to be remarkably similar all over the islands - most of the native influence is gone, and the rest is European and African, colored by the slave trade and the sugar trade. Remarkably horrific time in human history.
