Cheers to Amélie for keeping a book interesting with two characters and a beeline dialogue! I picked up this book at a friend's house, dived in and emerged only once I was done. It basically read itself. Nothomb is a very famous writer in France and Belgium, and because of that I avoided her for many years. All I knew of her was that she had a peculiar sartorial style and showed up on TV sets more often than a writer ought to be. I'm glad my misconceptions were wrong. This book was clever, witty, engaging and called for some good healthy brain racking. I hadn't read enough (if any) Sci-Fi to know if each and every futuristic idea in the book is her own invention but she sure knows how to make said ideas them interesting to read.
