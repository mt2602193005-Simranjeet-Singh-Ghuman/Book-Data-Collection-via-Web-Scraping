4.5 ★s I read this historical account of the Burke and Wills Expedition some years ago. I've re-read it this week for my face-to-face book club. My feelings about it have not changed - it is still an excellent book which tackles the human aspects of the ill-fated journey as much as the geography. Most members of the book club greatly enjoyed The Dig Tree and we had a long and enthusiastic discussion which mused on Robert O'Hara Burke's personality and lack of skills, the resilience of William Wills, and the politicians and social elites who meddled in the affair.
