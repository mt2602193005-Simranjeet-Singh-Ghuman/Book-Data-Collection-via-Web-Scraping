This is primarily a book for people who are just looking to have fun or purchase a novelty for a friend. It isn't really meant to be taken too seriously. It is small, light, and compact therefore ideal for travel and very easy to conceal in a purse or large pocket. If you are a serious practitioner I would let this one get away . If you are a newbie , and/or just curious , this books redeeming quality is that it is fantastic as a practice manual . Most everything in the book is bogus anyhow, so you can't do any damage and it will get you used to the steps needed to perform simple spells and incantations.
