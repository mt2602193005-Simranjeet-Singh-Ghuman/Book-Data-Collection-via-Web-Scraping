‘80s YA novel about a Jewish girl who thinks her nose is too big and wants a nose job. So that part is very poignant, but there is a lot of funny stuff about dating, white rats, being accused of shoplifting, and a school entomology project, a la Marjorie Sharmat or Jean Van Leeuwen. There’s a good bit of fat shaming in this book, but by then end the main character Henny is feeling better about herself and her body.
