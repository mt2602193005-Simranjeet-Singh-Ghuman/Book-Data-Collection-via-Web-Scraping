Invitation to the Game was a fascinating, thought-provoking and fun book to read. My only problem was that there was pretty poor characterization through the story, though that is kind of part of the dystopian genre I think. Finding your identity. The author takes you through three different main settings (and many other different atmospheres in those settings), and this was written in a seamless way that really engrossed me. You do have to get used to the writing style, and the quotes are not defined as to who said them (which was kind of annoying). 4.25 Stars
