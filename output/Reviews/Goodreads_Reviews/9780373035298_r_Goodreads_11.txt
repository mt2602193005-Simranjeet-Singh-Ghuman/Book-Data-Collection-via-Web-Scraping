This is a light read in an enjoyable style, but there's too much wrong with the content for me to recommend it. The characters aren't great. The lead male doesn't demonstrate the intelligence he's supposed to have and is always trying to boss people about. The lead female is immature and manipulative. This edition was marketed as "young adult", but although it lacks raunchy scenes, it promotes some dangerous ideas for that age group; things like if a man bosses you around it shows he cares about you, and that it's acceptable to go after a man already in a relationship.
