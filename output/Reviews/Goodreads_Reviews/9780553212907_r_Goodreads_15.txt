This is one of my favorites. It is a hymn to marriage with much poetry, song, and general fun. I also LOVE Kenneth Branagh's film version-one of my favorite adaptations of all time. Coming on the heels of Much Ado, you can see that Shakespeare is writing in a time of his life when word play, wit and romance figure greatly. Of course, the next play is Hamlet-not quite so airy. UPDATE on AUDIO: Don't listen to the audio if you do not know the play. Because of the girl playing boy parts it is hard to follow the plot if you don't know if the girl is, for instance, Rosalind or Ganymede at any moment.
