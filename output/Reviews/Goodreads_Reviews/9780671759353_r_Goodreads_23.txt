Ruby was one of the first Andrews books that I read and I thought it was a time to give an old favorite a re-read. Out of all the family saga's, The Landry family has got to be one of the best of them. I had forgotten so much of this book. I couldn't remember what happened to Ruby after she met her father and evil twin sister. So that part was like reading it all for the first time. I think now I'm no longer a naive teenage girl, I have maybe a little less sympathy for Ruby. She does tend to get herself into some stupid situations she should have just walked away from. My opinions about Gisselle have not changed. She is still a nasty bitch. However my opinions have Beau have, I used to like him but actually he's not really very nice. He rushed Ruby into sex and he's already banged her sister which is just wrong! He sounds like the kinda guy who would brag about sleeping with twins. I think Ruby should have turned him down. I'm really excited to read the next one, if I remember rightly, Gisselle has some horrible things in store for Ruby.
