I listened to this audiobook. This is the 5th book in the Karl Alberg series. Karl is the ranking staff sergeant in small towns along the British Columbia Sunshine Coast. The books always feature unusual characters. This book is no exception. Emma O'Brae is married to Charlie who is older than she. She seems to strive to be the "perfect" wife. This is her goal in life. She is obsessed with performing wifely duties perfectly. When Charlie leaves for work in Vancouver one morning he simply vanishes. He is an adult. This is not a police matter. Karl is on compassionate leave dealing with the death of his father. His number two in the police detachment asks Karl to look into Charlie's disappearance as a favor. So that's what Karl does in an unofficial search. We are treated to what I think is Emma's unstable mental thoughts about herself and her marriage. It looks like Charlie completely planned his escape from his marriage. Karl does eventually find him. Meanwhile, a second story involves a mentally and emotionally unstable man in Vancouver who is insulted by a college student. She is unaware of the slight but Eddie obsesses over this insult and the subsequent insults he imagines her to have committed against him. She lives with 3 other girls in a house and Eddie begins to stalk his imagined nemesis. Eventually this leads to violence. Then the two stories dovetail at the end of the book as Emma decides to confront Charlie. I find these books okay but kind of weird.
