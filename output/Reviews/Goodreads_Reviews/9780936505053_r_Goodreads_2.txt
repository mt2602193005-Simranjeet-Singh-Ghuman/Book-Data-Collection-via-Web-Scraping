This is one of the better books on an individual old time radio program. It screams "self-published before the Internet", and is a fairly delightful read for any Fibber McGee and Molly fan. It goes deep into the personal history of Jim and Marian Jordan as well as most of their other radio appearances. The book could have used a final edit/proofreading, but the sheer wealth of information and the obvious love of the subject will make any reader completely ignore the occasional misstep. Definitely reccomended for the OTR fan!
