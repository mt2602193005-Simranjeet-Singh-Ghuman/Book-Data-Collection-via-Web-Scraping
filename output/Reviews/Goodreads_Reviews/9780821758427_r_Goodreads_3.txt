Engaging, but I couldn't quite figure out what the word "love" meant in this context. In a nutshell: Rae finds out her husband of five years, a travelling salesman (yes), has another wife -- or had another wife. He's dead now. His brother-in-law, Dallas, comes after her looking for the money he claimed her bigomist husband stole. On the suspense front, this wasn't bad. A few twists and turns, some of them predictable, but not overly so. The drama was high, and the pace was fast from the start. It never really let up. That would be the engaging part. As for the but...Rae and Dallas are enemies. He comes into her life, threatens her, pushes her around, and there's just this magical spark of attraction between them. At some point, they start to call it love. Now, I know everyone has their own theories about what love is and what it feels like, but in my world, it's not something with a force of its own, completely separate from reality. It's also more than lust. In this case, I couldn't for the life of me figure out what either of these two wanted with the other, or why they would trust one another, let alone love one another. The author has a great style and voice, and I would read more books by her.
