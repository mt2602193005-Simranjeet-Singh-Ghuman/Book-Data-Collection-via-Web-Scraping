Sometimes you come across a book that surprises you with its simply told but elegantly constructed story. Something that makes you look at your life and relationships with fresh new eyes and reminds you that its the tiny pleasures of our daily lives that defines the boundaries of love and joy. At heart it is a common tale of war and lovers caught within its web, but go deeper and it becomes a tale of innocence lost and youth revisited, of what it felt to be young and so sure of everything, but also of growing old(er) and coming to terms with the reality that not all of your dreams will come true. For a book that has been translated you still get a feeling of rhythm and pacing which just makes you wonder how much better it would be if you could read it in the original Italian. I picked Prince of the Clouds because of its ties to military history but stuck with it because of Terzo and the loves and minor triumphs of his life.
