I bought this book on a whim at a library sale. Overall, the book is not bad for a concise history of the "World Conqueror" and the empire he built. My problem with the book was that the author switched formats in that in the beginning he had chapters covering specific topics such as the organization of the Mongol Army but then later on changed to a time line. A good book for those with a simple curiosity and not wanting to get into a lengthy, weighty more in depth level reading.
