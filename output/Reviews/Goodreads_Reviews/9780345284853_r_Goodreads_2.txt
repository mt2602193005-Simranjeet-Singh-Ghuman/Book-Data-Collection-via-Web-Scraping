This collection of ten stories is the last one to be published in a mass market edition during Tiptree's lifetime. Four of them were originally published under one of her other pseudonyms, Raccoona Sheldon, and two of the longest are original to the book. There is no introduction in this one. While she still examines the topics of feminism and equality, the later stories in particular take a dark and sharp focus on mental health and the whole book has a bleaker tone overall. My favorite was The Screwfly Solution, which was published in Analog as by Raccoona Sheldon and won the Nebula award for the best novelette of 1978. With Delicate Mad Hands, an original novella, is pretty much a knock-out read, too.
