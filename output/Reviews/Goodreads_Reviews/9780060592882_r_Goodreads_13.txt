Individually, I like most of the stories. I wasn't blown away by any of them, but with the exception of "Me & Wolfie, 1979," they held my attention well enough. As a whole, I just didn't feel much drive to keep going. I'd finish one, experience some relief to be through a story, put the book down, and essentially forget it. I did like "The Instructor". I also really liked all of the stories in Part Three, which was definitely the strongest stretch for me. They're dark and unsettling, having to do with things like murder, child neglect, and statutory rape. My favorite in the book is probably "The Mutants". I don't think I will revisit this collection, but there's always the chance I fall hard for Joyce Carol Oates and give it another try. For the moment, I'm glad to be done.
