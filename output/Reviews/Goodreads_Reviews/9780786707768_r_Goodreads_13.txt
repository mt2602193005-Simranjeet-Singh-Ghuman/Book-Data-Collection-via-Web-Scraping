Gently mix some vintage Elmore Leonard plotting, a touch of Carl Hiaasen's quirky characterization and a lyric Texas setting and you have Christopher Cook's highly readable book. Ray Bob and Eddie rob a convenience store after shooting the clerk in a dispute over a penny and then begin a bloody rampage across Texas, pursued by an aging Ranger and his dog, Lefty. It's a high-speed read with some human twists that make it more than enjoyable -- and quite worthwhile.
