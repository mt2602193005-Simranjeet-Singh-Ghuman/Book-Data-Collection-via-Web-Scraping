Re-listening - this time with Simon. I love Dorothy Gilman's observations of human nature. "I have a flexible mind. I believe it's one of the advantages of growing old," [Mrs. Pollifax] explained, "I find youth quite rigid at times." This description of Mrs. Pollifax's reaction to surprising news resonated with me: "The smile spread through her warm wine until it merged in a laugh of purist delight." I was amused by the use of the word 'decorated' when describing the airport, which is looming ahead of them - "a gloriously clear space a mile or two away decorated with runways and a control tower."
