Hell, 5 stars. I thoroughly enjoyed myself, even when Koontz occasionally veered off into the corny. But it gave what I needed: a full-throttle, thriller/horror novel—a fan favorite amongst Koontz fans for a reason. Not to mention the unusually compelling villain. One could argue the climax is rushed, and I would agree. But the journey was truly more important than the destination. This book has been on my tbr since, like, 2012 and I’ve finally read it. Rad.
