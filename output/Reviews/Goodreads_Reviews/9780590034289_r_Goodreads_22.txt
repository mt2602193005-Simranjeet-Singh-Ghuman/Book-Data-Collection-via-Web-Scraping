If you expect the story to resemble the movie, don’t! Yes, there is a flying car, but that’s where the similarities more or less end. Instead of a dreamy adventure in a magical land, we have the kids trying to save a robbery from happening. I don’t mind the book’s story, but it is a little too simplistic. I read it with my 12-year-old nephew, and I believe he was a little too old for it. Still, it’s a wonderful read and highly enjoyable.
