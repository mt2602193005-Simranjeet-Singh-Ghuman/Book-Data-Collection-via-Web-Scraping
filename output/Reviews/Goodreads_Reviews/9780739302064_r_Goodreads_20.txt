The Coffee Trader, by David Liss, is a book of intrigue and an absorbing historical novel. I became so wrapped up in the historical aspect that I felt as if I had gone back in time and place. My senses were infused with Liss’ extremely detailed prose. With his strong word-imagery, Liss transports the reader to Seventeenth Century Amsterdam. It is the aftermath of the Spanish Inquisition, and the Dutch city is streaming with Jews who fled Spain. In fact, many others, from all over Europe have come to Amsterdam to try to make some money. There is a fine line running through the pages…regarding Judaism, morals, mores/ethics, and what is acceptable within business dealings and everyday personal life. Liss defines these situations with clarity. The story line is intriguing. The historical value is quite worthwhile, in itself. Even though some sections seem a bit slow, I was enthralled with The Coffee Trader, and recommend it to everyone.
