What a wonderful collection of short stories, whick took me over a year to read because I kept putting it down for other books. Some of my favorites: The Exact Science of Matrimony by O. Henry: a story about grifters with a funny twist at the end. Charles by Shirley Jackson: who hasn't had that troublesome friend in kindergarten? If Not Higher by Isaac Loeb Perez: reads like an old Lithuanian folktale. The Sniper by Liam O'Flaherty: another twist at the end, this one sad. Three Letters......and a Footnote by Horacio Quiroga: A reminder not to miss the opportunity.
