I'm not a fan of this series. There is no chase between the characters. Just sleeping together shockingly fast (although not as bad and unrealistic as the first book) and then they spend the marriage fighting over absolutely nothing and keeping secrets that make no sense. This book was particularly random in that at first you think there is going to be some big drama with Patrick traveling and being an ambassador, but it never even happens. It all just falls flat. Was there really a reason at all to make Patrick a duke? I felt like the story suddenly wrapped up in the last few pages of the book and she jammed every conversation they hadn't had over months into a few paragraphs.
