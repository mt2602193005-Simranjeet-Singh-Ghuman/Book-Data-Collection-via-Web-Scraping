I have so many fond memories of this book and cassette tape. I would listen to it constantly as a child. Sing every song and could even recite the story between songs. While on vacation my brother decided to borrow my boombox and didn’t realize this cassette tape was in the player and lost it at a party. I cried when I got home and found it lost. My mom made him buy me another tape which sadly I do not still own. Here’s to finding another cassette tape soon.
