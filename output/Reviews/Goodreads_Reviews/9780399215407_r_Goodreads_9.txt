Tomie de Paola's Bool of Poems by Tomie dePaila This is a collection of poems by several very famous authors with illustration done by Tomie dePaola. The illustrations showcase dePaola's talent and flair for creating a backdrop that compliments several different poems written by some of history's most famous authors, such as: ---Emily Dickinson ---William Blake ----Byrd Baylor ----Lewis Carroll ---Jack Prelucksy My favorite poem is Cat Kisses by Bobbi Katz Sandpaper kisses on a cheek or a chin--- that is the way for a day to begin Sandpaper kisses-- a cuddle, a purr. I have an alarm clock that is covered in fur. This is a book the whole family could enjoy, but is geared toward 4-6 years old.
