Don't let the rather basic synopsis fool you--I rated this five stars purely because it is quite literally the WEIRDEST holiday-themed book I have ever seen. It is Alice in Wonderland meets a fever dream meets pink. Seriously, this book is so surreal I had to read it like three times as a kid because I was convinced I'd missed something and everything couldn't really be as off as it seemed. But I absolutely loved it for that too. What a wild ride.
