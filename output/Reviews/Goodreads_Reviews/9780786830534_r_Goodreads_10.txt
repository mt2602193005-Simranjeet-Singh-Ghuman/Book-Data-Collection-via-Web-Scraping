Winnie the Pooh has set up a feast for his friends and himself to partake in. As they are getting ready to eat they decide to tell each other what they are thankful for. Everyone is thankful to have friends, but they come up with some other interesting things to be thankful for too, such as Owl being thankful for landing on his feet instead of his face, and Rabbit that seeds have the good sense to sprout in his Garden. I think it’s a cute story for children who like Winnie the Pooh to be introduced to the concept of Thanksgiving and what sorts of things people (and animals) can be thankful for. Show more
