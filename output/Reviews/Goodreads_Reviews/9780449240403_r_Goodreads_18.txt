Beginning of Gad's has the makings of an unspectacular "couple-moves-into-house-with-spooky-secrets" airport paperback-- but that plotline completely disappears halfway as we step back 100 years and meet a gutsy widow and her tedious family. Thanks to Goodreads, I now know that there is a Gad's Hall #2 and maybe that's where everything starts to make sense. But I don't care.
