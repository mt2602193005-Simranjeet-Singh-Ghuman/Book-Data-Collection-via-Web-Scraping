Story of a teacher, and preacher's daughter from Wisconsin, Evelyn Gibbons, who travels to a South Dakota reservation to teach the Native American children. She has had dreams of a warrior, who turns out to be Black Hawk. He also had dreams of her before her arrival. She encounters a lot of prejudice against the Indians and also a couple of really slimy bad guys. There is rape in the story, although most of it is 'off the page', and the perpetrator is not the hero. The love story between Evelyn and Black Hawk is the main story, but it doesn't feel that romantic since there is so much going on with the many other characters. There is a lot of stuff that transpires before our hero and heroine get their happy ending. The H/h get their HE at the very end and there is no epilogue. Ms. Bittner is a great writer, but her stories are not light and fluffy romances. 4/5
