This story is about a sweet young child whose family is in search of freedom. The book depicts the family as they escape from slavery. His father leaves first and then his mother and he go after. they make it, but it's not without constant danger and hardship. I do wish that it had given more details, but as this book series is shorter and I think meant for a younger audience, I get why there wasn't. I feel for teachers this might be a good book to use as an educational tool for younger audiences.
