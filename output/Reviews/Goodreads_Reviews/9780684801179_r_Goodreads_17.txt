This is on my top ten list of all time. I think I first read it shortly after it was published in 1970. It became a favorite then and I always meant to reread it, and just recently got the chance. To my surprise, I remembered nothing about the book except for how much I loved it, so essentially I got to read it again for the very first time. Time travel and science fiction is generally not my thing, but I just went with the flow here and accepted everything I was told about the "how". It seemed possible, still does, but as far as I know, books and movies are the only way to visit the past. Here, Si Morley is invited to participate in a government project to travel back to 1882, for research purposes only, of course. He is told not to interfere in any way with people or events, but of course that's impossible. He does get involved, goes back and forth between the past and his present to report on what he finds. What we are treated to is excitement and suspense in both time periods. Throw in some love, a few villains, a little love, a little mystery that finally gets solved, and you end up with an unputdownable novel that you fall in love with yourself. Back on my shelf it goes, for maybe a third read somewhere in my future.
