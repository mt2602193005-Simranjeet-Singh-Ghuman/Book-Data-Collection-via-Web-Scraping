Very easy to read. I read the whole thing in a couple of hours. I like how much it makes me think of the ways I can judge others and the things I can work on. It helped me see a couple things I hadn't thought of before (like how important service is to keep us from judging, why we sometimes don't want to do certain things, and how thinking of other's circumstances can really make you change how you feel about them). It made me think and see where I need to change. Thats a good book, in my opinion. I will say that I got confused a time or two. Occasionally the book is written in first person only to switch to third person. The only reason this is confusing is because sometimes I thought I was reading from Max's point of view only to read a few pages later something about Max in third person. I think the co-author thing is what confused me. I tend to read things in a certain way depending on the gender of the author and since this was both, I was just confused sometimes. But overall, it is good, basic book.
