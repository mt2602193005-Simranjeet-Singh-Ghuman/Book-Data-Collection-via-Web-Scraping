This was a sweet story, but nothing really interesting or exciting happened. Tia Dolores is teaching the girls to weave blankets and she decides she also wants to teach them to read write since they don't already do. The girls (well, mainly Francisca and a little bit Josefina) are worried that if they learn to read and write it will take away their memories of their mother. She never knew how to read and write so if they learn to read and write, they will be less like her. Or something like that. Honestly, I can't imagine being in a world where I couldn't read or write. I would just feel loss. Of course Josefina changes her mind when Tia Dolores shows her a book of poems that her mother loved.
