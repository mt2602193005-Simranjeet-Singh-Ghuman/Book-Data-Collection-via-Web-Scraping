C.S. Lewis had a mind unlike any other author or theologian I've ever come across. In his book Surprised by Joy , he talks about the different philosophers he read. The amount, and scope, was dumbfounding. Later in his life, he was given a position as a professor of philosophy in the university where he was employed. Once an atheist, Lewis literally philosophized his way out of atheistic thinking by confronting the impossibilities he found in atheism. This book is short, but potent and mindbending. Still, expect to take your time getting through it. There's so much to chew on. I'd rate this book a G.
