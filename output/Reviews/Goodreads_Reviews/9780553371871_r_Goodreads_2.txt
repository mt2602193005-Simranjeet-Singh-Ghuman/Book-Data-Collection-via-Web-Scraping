Although this is a level three easy reader version of the Old Befana Italian Christmas legend, I can imagine this one being read aloud to a group as the language is somewhat poetic and the illustrations are better than most of those found in easy readers. It does have all of the elements of the folk story, Befana is visited by the Kings, refuses to join them, and each Christmas searches for the baby king that she did not find on that first Christmas night. A well done easy reader.
