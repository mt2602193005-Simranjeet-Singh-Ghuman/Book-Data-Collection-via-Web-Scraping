The only other Berenstain Bear book that I just really wanted to rate and review is this one, because I believe it is one of the most perfect books to read to children of any age during Christmas time or the holidays. Mama Bear begins to look around the treehouse and realizes that their family just owns such an enormous amount of material things...things that they don't even need and things that they don't ever use. The family first thought that they could just have a yard sale and make money off of their old items, but Mama Bear had a different idea in mind. The Bear family cleaned out their house and then the story follows them as they go from a nursing home, to a hospital, and then to a charity store dropping off and distributing their goods to those who were less fortunate than them to have so many material things. I definitely want to read this to my future students, and ask them for responses about the story and why it holds such a high importance, and how they could apply the actions of the Bear family to their own life.
