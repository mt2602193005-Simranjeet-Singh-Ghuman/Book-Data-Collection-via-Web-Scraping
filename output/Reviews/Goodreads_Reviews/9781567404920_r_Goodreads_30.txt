She Said Yes is the story of Cassie Bernall, a 17 year old girl who was killed in the Columbine High School shooting. The story is told by her mother and includes the last few years of Cassie's life and how she had gone through a complete change in attitude. She was in a really bad place and had a horrible relationship with her family. Her parents find letters from a friend detailing how they should kill them and that's when they realize how bad things had gotten. The story goes through what her parents and some new friends do to help her work through depression and overcome the negative influences she had let into her life. I enjoyed reading this true story about a girl who goes through so much and overcomes a lot. Even though you know from the beginning that she dies, I was still rooting for her to make the changes necessary for her to be happier. I felt that the mother telling the story, Misty, was pleading with other parents to be more involved in their children's lives from the beginning so that when they begin to come into contact with negative things and influences they will be comfortable coming to them for advice and guidance and acceptance. I also got the impression that she wanted readers to appreciate all the time we have with our children and family.
