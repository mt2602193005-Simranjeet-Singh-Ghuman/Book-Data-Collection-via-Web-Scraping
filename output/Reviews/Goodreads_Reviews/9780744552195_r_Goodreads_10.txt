I have just realised that I often find young, grumpy characters adorable. Sophie is one of them. Sophie knows her own mind and is very determined, but often does not get her way because she is very young (4 at the beginning of the first book, almost 6 by the third book). Sometimes she behaves badly (e.g. when she got angry and pushed two sneaky kids over), but the way the story unfolds makes it easy to justify such behaviour. These are beautiful slice-of-life stories about school, family, friends and pets - I am looking forward to reading the final three books in this series.
