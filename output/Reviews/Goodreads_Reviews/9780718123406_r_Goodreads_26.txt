An old one I’ve read before but good to see that it’s aged well! An Italian kidnapper targets the horse racing community, as a UK firm with particular skills work with local police to get the various hostages out. The plot races along (see what I did there 😂), strong characters and it just reminded me why I used to love his books, in spite of not being at all interested in horses. Going to try a few more Dick Francis rereads I think.
