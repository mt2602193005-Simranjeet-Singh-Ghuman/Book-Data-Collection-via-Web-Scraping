i don't think i've given any of the bsc books five stars but this one deserves it. there are not enough words for how much i love claudia. of all the girls, i actually believe she's 13 when i read her books. she thinks she might be adopted and of course she's not because that would be a HUGE lore drop this deep into the series. as much as i love the rest of the club, it's time to declare claudia kishi one of the great characters of middle grade media and my absolute favorite bsc member. she's smart, she's loving, she's funny, and she's an excellent friend. i'm going to read another claudia story asap! edited to add: turns out i've given five starts to 4 other bsc books - one mary anne focused and the rest are claudia books :)
