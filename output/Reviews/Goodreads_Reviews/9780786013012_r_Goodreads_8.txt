DEALING WITH THE CRAZED BROTHER Another great adventure involving Smoke Jensen and the people who become friends, some don't, but they never forget him. There's humor but it's dark and you will laugh. Dagger his horse 🐎also gets involved in backing Smoke's plays. You will love reading this fast moving intriguing synopsis of the lives these frontier settlement, and small ranchers, farmers are constantly fighting to survive..you can almost say it's the survival of the fittest.
