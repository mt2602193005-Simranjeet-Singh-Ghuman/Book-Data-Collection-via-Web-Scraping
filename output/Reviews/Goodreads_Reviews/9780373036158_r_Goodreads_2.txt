Wow, this was terrible. The heroine (22) is asked by the slut hero (36) to move in to look after his niece whilst his brother and SIL are in hospital for two months. The very first night he goes out on a date, then spends the rest of the WHOLE book dating 3 women, including having them for intimate dinners whilst she is in the house. He laughs at her for her virgin status, is rude and smug, and even when he is jealous of her cousin, it comes off badly. Why she loves him I do not know. They have no chemistry and he’ll regret the day he married her, because he’s clearly just interested in quantity and not quality. I never like nanny tropes, but this was next level demeaning for the heroine.
