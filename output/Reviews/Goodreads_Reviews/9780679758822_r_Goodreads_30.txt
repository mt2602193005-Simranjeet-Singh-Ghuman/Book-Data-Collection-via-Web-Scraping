Mayle enjoys writing capers, not murderous or violent stories, usually, but not totally legal all the same. Generally they are excellent, particularly Hotel Pastis: A Novel of Provence but this one doesn't hold water. The premise of a miracle truffle growing formula is interesting but the between the vengeful billionaire, the swindling ex-girlfriend, and the go with the flow protagonist it isn't a good read. You don't like or sympathize with any of the characters except maybe Georgette, who is basically a footnote to the story. And by the end you don't care who's gotten away with what...
