dePaola, T. (1988). Tomie dePaola’s book of poems. New York: G P Putnam’s Sons Summary: A compilation of famous and new poems from writers as well-known as Lewis Carroll, Robert Louis Stevenson, Langston Hughes, Robert Frost, Jack Prelutsky, etc… Reviews/Awards: Publishers Weekly- 1988 School Library Journal- 1989 Curriculum Connection: 1st graders (I have done this in the past) can put on a poetry play. This goes along with their Listening and Speaking goals. In groups, students memorize and act out a poem to present to their study buddies, parents, etc… Personal Connection: I was given this book when I was in college in the late 90’s, once I realized I was going to be a teacher. My favorite poems are: Cat Kisses by Bobbi Katz, The Storm by Dorothy Aldis, Rain of Leaves by Aileen Fisher, The Secret Place by Tomie dePaola, Secret Door by Myra Cohn Livingston, My Brother by Dorothy Aldis, Some Things Don’t Make Any Sense at All by Judith Viorst, Growing Old by Rose Henderson, I Speak, I Say, I Talk by Arnold L. Shapiro, Eagle Flight by Alonzo Lopez, Autumn Leaves by Aileen Fisher, and Bed in Summer by Robert Louis Stevenson. I love the classic Tomie dePaola illustrations that can lend comprehension to some of the slightly-more abstract stories. Grades: 2-5
