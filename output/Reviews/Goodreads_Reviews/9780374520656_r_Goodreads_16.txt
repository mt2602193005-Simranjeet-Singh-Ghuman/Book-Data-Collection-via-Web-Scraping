McPhee is probably my favorite writer and I think one of the greatest writers ever, he reminds me a lot of this guy Jon Bois who makes long form youtube videos. They’re both able to take a subject that may not be the most interesting an get you completely wrapped up in it and learning, often with a nice narrative piece to go along with it. This book in particular was a little tough at times because I know very little about geology and didn’t take the time to slow down and figure things out but it was still a great read
