I like stories in which the protagonist becomes a hero. In this book we follow Micky who saves an old man on a parking lot and as reward gets 10 shots of a drug that makes him superintelligent. So far so good and I was actually intrigued what would happen. The old man he saved is a superintelligent man due to his own drugs, but he is on the run for a black government organisation and a industrial tycoon. So when Micky starts to take the drugs and develop his understanding of science and the world, he becomes involved in a big man hunt. I think the balance of the book is off. The beginning of the story and the awakening of Micky was nice but near the end it started to rush too much. I think the origin of the drugs (extraction from human brains) really required more details, and an opportunity to move the story in a vampire like story was missed. The character development in the book was more that reasonable and also the sidekicks were give sufficient attention. An entertaining read of 3 stars. The book is very related to the movie Limitless, but I liked the latter better. Show more
