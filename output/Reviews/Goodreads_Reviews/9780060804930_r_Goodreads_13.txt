*I received a copy of this title from NetGalley. This is the first Nigel Strangeways book I've read, and I didn't enjoy it as much as I thought I would. I found the foreshadowing to be heavy-handed and the ending was glaringly obvious from early on. I don't necessarily mind knowing "whodunit" before the end of a book as long as the scenery and writing are engaging, but this fell short. The characters read like cardboard cutouts rather than real individuals with dimension and it is doubly true for all the female characters - who seem to be written only as objects of either pity or contempt.
