Eh. I loved Final Appeal so I expected to love this one as well. Yes, it had a different main character, but this one stars in a series! She must be great, right? Well, no. In all honesty, Rita Morrone is okay as a main character but I don't love her. Same with the book -- it was okay, I finished it, but I didn't love it. While I'm not necessarily going to turn away another book in the series, it's unlikely that I'll be reaching for one any time soon
