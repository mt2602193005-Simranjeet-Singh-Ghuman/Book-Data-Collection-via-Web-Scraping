This book was like a wild fever dream. It was all over the place when it comes to plot and characters. Zulu was tstl to a degree, but I admire her hustle, and she didn't spend too much time angsting. If she was angsting she did it while trying to fix the problem that arose. The cover is misleading tho. I had to keep reminding myself that this was not set in more modern times because the dress reminded me of a summer dress possibly from the 80's. Why four stars then? I have a soft spot in my heart for this one. It didn't drag and it was entertaining.
