Absolutely amazing! The only place I've seen more breathtaking images of space, the moon, and earth is at the Rose Center for Earth and Space , inside Hayden Planetarium in NYC – which is a permanent exhibition of the best of this book's prints. Light's work is incredibly stunning on that large scale (this hardcover's dimensions are smaller than your standard coffee-table book size) and I recommend spending time there if you can. That said, this is a great little book to have around, as a reminder of both astonishing accomplishments and meek human perspective.
