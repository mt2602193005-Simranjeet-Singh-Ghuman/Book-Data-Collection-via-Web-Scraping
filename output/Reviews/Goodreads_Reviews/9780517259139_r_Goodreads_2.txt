Part 1 of this book is the History of Romance-- A nice overview of the early French Chivalry Romances, Metrical, Prose and later Romantic Histories as well as those of the Spanish Peninsula, so well loved by Don Quixote de la Mancha. Part 2 is a selection of the tales themselves, somewhat decluttered English accounts of Tales of Arthur, Charles the Great, and many others. The 1913 1st Edition features 8 plates in color and 36 in monochrome. Part of the ''Myth And Legend In Literature and Art'' series 1st published by The Gresham Publishing Company Ltd.
