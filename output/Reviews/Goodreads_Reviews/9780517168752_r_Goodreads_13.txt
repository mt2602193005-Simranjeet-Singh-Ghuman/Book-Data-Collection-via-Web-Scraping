The chief pleasure of a Trollope novel for me is her believable dialogue and her effortless construction of characters. If it wasn't for these two outstanding talents I don't think I could put in the effort to get through a whole novel about people I can't bring myself to like or care much about. One or two of the cast interest me (including James, "head" of the household of rather random individuals in Richmond Villa and Joss, who would be his step-daughter if her mother had ever agreed to marry him) but mostly - though beautifully drawn - I can't empathise with them. And I love the ending.
