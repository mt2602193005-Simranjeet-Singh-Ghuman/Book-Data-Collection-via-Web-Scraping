3.5 Super cute, wholesome fun. Don't judge a book by its cover and all that. I have always preferred Trixie Belden to Nancy Drew and I'm really enjoying revisiting a childhood favourite. Funnily enough my first trixie Belden was something I randomly found in an opp shop in Sussex inlet when I was a kid and it seems fitting that I found this on the community library wall in Dapto Mall. My edition was some one's 13th birthday present in 1976, I love it when people write messages to the receiver on the flyleaf.
