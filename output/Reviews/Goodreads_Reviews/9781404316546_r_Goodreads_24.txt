If you have taken the path of hunting in your life this is a must read. An amazing story of a man named John Henry Patterson and his almost in possible obstacles with building the Uganda Railroad construction, and hunting down two of the most vicious man eating lions on the East African plains. It's an absolute if you are fan of the movie "The Ghost and The Darkness." The book proceeds after the hunt for the lions are killed and tells a tale of even more adventures with some big trophy animals and near death experiences.
