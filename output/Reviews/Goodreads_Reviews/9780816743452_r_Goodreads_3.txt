Found this little OOP gem on one of my gleaning forays, & when I saw it immediately thought of my friend Ari & his mom & dad. They're a big reading household, & mom works in the biological sciences, & I just knew it would be a good fit. I enjoyed this read-aloud little book about nocturnal animals and life in the night, & recommend it to anyone with small children. As a society, we need to be raising generations of humans that love and respect the natural world, and who understand science rather than relegating it to 'experts' and those who 'choose' to 'believe' in it. Although I don't have children of my own, I think this is one way to encourage youngsters to develop an affinity about animal night denizens of all kinds. Show more
