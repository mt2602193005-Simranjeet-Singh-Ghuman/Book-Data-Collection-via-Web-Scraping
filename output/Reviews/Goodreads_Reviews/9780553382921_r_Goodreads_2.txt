The only reason for 3 stars is because the book was written before consolidated war against women's contraceptive rights began to sweep through the country. The loss of access to contraceptives would have profound impact on our way of life today: The entrance of women into the workforce, the ability to decide when and how many children a family could have, and the burgeoning care of health care in the country. I wonder if the companies who feel they shouldn't have to pay for birth control have considered how much employee health costs if they had to pay for more and more pregnancies and births. Not only women, but also men should fight back against this attack women and against the American way of life.
