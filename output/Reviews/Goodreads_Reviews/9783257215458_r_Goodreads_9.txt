God knows why I wasted my time on this one after the vague but distinctly negative memories of reading Happy Birthday Turk many moons ago. It starts well enough with Turk looking into a Green Party-style act of sabotage tied in with the murder of an evil pharmaceutical executive, but it quickly devolves into a series of pointless beatings, absurdly quick recoveries from same, and endless acts of idiocy and irrationality. Oh, and not one bit noir. Why does this sell 20 copies, much less a million?
