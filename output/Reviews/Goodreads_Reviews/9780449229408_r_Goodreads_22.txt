3.5 I feel cheated by the abrupt ending. It doesn't even end with the two main characters. That being said, it surprised me how much I enjoyed this book. There is no real plot one could point out. The Nonesuch 's strength are its characters. Some of them are adorably lovely (I loved Julian), some hilariously childish and greedy (Laurie), gossipy and mean (some of the village matrons), goodhearted (Mrs. Underhill, Miss Trent's employer) and some will drive you crazy (Tiffany). I don't even want to give her more attention than she deserves (most characters gave her more than enough attention in the book). As for the love story, it kind of feels as if Heyer forgot to explain or at least show why would Sir Waldo fall for Ancilla Trent. I liked her, but I got to read her thoughts. He didn't have that. Still, even with the flaws I had lots of fun with these characters.
