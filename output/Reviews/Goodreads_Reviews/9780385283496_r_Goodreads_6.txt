“Money was never a problem, passports were never required. There were always new places to dance.” I know Tim O'Brien's go-to Vietnam war novel is The Things They Carried (which is really good); however for me, I think it will always be Going After Cacciato . The mythical quest of going after fellow soldier, Cacciato, who is reportedly leaving the war and walking to Paris, has a dream-like quality to it. This imaginative journey takes Paul Berlin and his platoon to nearly unimaginable places, but it never lets them escape the horrors of war. Beautifully written. It was great to meet the author of this wonderful novel a few years ago after he gave the keynote of a conference I was attending. I've read Going After Cacciato at least four times, and it always resonates with me. “You have taken many risks. You have been brave beyond your wildest expectations. And now it is time for a final act of courage. I urge you: March proudly into your own dream.”
