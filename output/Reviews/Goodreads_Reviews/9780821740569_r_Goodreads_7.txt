Shameless (1993) is a wild adventure … humorous at times with a generous helping of emotional angst …. an engaging read of horse thieves, an honest by the book army officer and camels! Bittner sure has written a creative story! Nina and Clay are two people from different worlds who share a common past of loneliness tho for most of the book they don’t trust each other. Early on they’re attracted to each other and Clay does everything he can to rescue Nina when she gets into harrowing situations. According to Bittner’s note at the end of the book the U.S. government in 1855 did import camels as an experiment to use in the arid West. 🐪 Overall, a page turner and a splendid love story. ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
