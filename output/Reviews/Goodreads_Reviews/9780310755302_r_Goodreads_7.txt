Walter Wangerin Jr.’s Reliving the Passion has been a companion for my Lenten journey since a preaching professor first placed it in my hands in 1998, and I find myself returning to it every couple of years because it never seems to grow old. Wangerin has a gift for bringing the humanity of Christ and the emotion of Holy Week into focus without ever drifting into sentimentality. Each reading feels different because I am different. Few books have shaped my imagination for preaching and devotion as consistently as this one, and it remains one of the few works I revisit not out of obligation, but out of hunger.
