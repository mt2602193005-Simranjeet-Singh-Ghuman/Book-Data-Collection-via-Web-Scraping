As I Have Loved You by Kitty de Ruyter is a autobiography. Kitty lived during WWII. The book is about all the trials her and her family faced. What here family faced is that they were moved to a concentration The plot is very interesting and well written. All the plot is is her life, but it is very well written and constructed. The setting changes a lot during the book. Each time it changes though she stops and describes it and makes it feel believable and like you have been there before and know the people that she meet there. Some themes you can take from this book are love, kindness, spirituality, and listening to your heart. Her mother shows the most in every one of these traits and you feel like you have met the mother before. She is such an example to all. I would recommend this book for all ages, genders, and ethnicity. It Is a good, fast read that teaches you about WWII and her life.
