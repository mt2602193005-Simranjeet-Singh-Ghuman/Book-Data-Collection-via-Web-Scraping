I absolutely loved this book, and whoever translated it needs praise as well. The themes are so wonderful. Some will see history, but that is there to demonstrate some truths about love and honour and uncertainty, and the importance of passion beyond methodological order. Overall, it is about living in a life of uncertainty, and knowing your own heart. Use your talent, be who you are. Absolutely beautifully written and evocative of time and place as well. Masterful.
