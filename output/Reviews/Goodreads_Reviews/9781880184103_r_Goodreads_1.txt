I finished it. I finally finished it. Kids, before there was the Internet, and long before Wikipedia, we used books—physical, page-turning books—to get information. Compact Classics, based out of Salt Lake City, did the corporeal equivalent to digital Wikipedia, and the subtitle is quite perfect: “More Single-Sitting Summaries of All-Time Great Books”. I acquired this as a hand-me-down in around 2005-6, and I’ve finally finished it. Six hundred pages of detailed and engaging 2-page summaries of hundreds of books you’ll probably never get around to reading, let alone knowing about, from the works of ancient Chinese philosophers to stuff published in 1992. I can’t think of a better book for the bathroom. Digital-detox yourself while hunched over and grab a book like this from your local thrift store.
