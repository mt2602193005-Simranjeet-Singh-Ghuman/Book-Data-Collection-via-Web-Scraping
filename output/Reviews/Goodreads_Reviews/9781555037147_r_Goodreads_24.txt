am reading these books out of obligation (it's a long story). but lets just say, once you start, you can't stop. these are seriously the best and most addicting LDS romance books you'll ever find. everyone i know in this town loves (and refers often to) them, i'm pretty ashamed i'm even giving them the time of day. but if you want a clean, fun, easy to read, love story (and you're going through withdrawals without twilight) i'd recommend you read this series of books. take them to the beach, get a tan and dream about your BYU co-eds.
