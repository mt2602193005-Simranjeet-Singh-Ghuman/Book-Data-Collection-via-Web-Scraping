Intense family drama...... Really liked this very long novel about the gold rush in Colorado, the Kirkland family drama really had me way too tense. I don't ever remember having 3 nasty characters in one of this author's books that were so evil! The only reason I didn't give this a five-star, it did not bring me too tears, I was sad, upset, angry, but didn't need the tissues. The one series that had me sobbing, way too much in all 4 books was the Outlaw Series, a well deserved box of tissues.
