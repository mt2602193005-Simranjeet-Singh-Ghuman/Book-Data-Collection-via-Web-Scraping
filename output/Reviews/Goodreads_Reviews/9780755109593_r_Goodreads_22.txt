Historical fiction, set in the United States, four years after the battle of Waterloo The exciting story of Juliet, who has recently been left destitute by the loss of her father. She takes her cousin's place as wife to Hyde Purchis, when Josephine tries to rescue Napoleon from the island of St Helena. I't an excellent story, well-written with lots of excitement, and a most satisfying conclusion. I enjoy it thoroughly each time I reread it. Recommended to anyone who enjoys historical fiction.
