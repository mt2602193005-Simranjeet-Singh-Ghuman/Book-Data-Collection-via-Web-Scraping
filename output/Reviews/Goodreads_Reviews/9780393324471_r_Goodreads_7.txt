I've just started a new journey with Beth Kephart in her memoir Still Love in Strange Places. I fill my senses with her poetic images and am just as curious as Beth about Don Alberto, her husband's grandfather in El Salvador. In Beth's writing, places become characters. I finished it! The culture of an unfamiliar place made real, laid bare, for the reader, the characters living and breathing. A turbulent land, El Salvador. My heart ached for its people, for Don Alberto when he died. A great man; a gentle, caring man. The good news is that he transferred that love to his daughter, the mother of Kephart’s husband, and then to Kephart’s husband. Like Kephart in Still Love in Strange Places, I had trouble learning a second language as an adult in college at the University of Pennsylvania. Readers can connect on many levels with Kephart’s struggle to fit in to the puzzle of her husband’s family, to the language barrier when she visits his country, to making her immediate family a unified whole. A well-written journey. You won’t be disappointed.
