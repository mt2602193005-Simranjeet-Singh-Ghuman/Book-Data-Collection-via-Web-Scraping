In Name Only is about Beth Carol Barnes, the daughter of a wealthy business magnate, who runs away to Hollywood from the small town in which she grew up, when she becomes pregnant from an affair with a boy from the wrong side of the tracks. In Hollywood she falls into the bad hands of the man she eventually marries, who forces her to act in a pornographic movie, prostitute herself, etc. but she still retains her innocence (or stupidity), somehow. Her child, Raleigh Barnes, becomes one of the greatest child stars who has ever lived in Hollywood, until tragedy strikes. But all is not as it seems. This book exposes a lot of the decay in Hollywood, kind of like Olivia Goldsmiths's Flavor of the Month The ending of this book, is glorious. I have read it so many times and my copy is now a tattered mess.
