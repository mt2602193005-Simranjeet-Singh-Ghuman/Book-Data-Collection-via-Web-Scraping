I borrowed this book from Nich, who is the early American History expert in our home. I, have still never had formal education of Early American history. However, I did enjoy Bailyn's 5 essays on various topics surrounding the American Revolution. At first I thought my favorite essay would be the second one, on Jefferson's ambiguities and how he struggled with his idealism and the realistic problems he faced throughout his life, but then I read the essay on the Federalist Papers and now I'm torn. The discussions of the importance of the papers over time versus how they were written was fascinating. I would recommend this very smart, a little difficult, but very insightful book to anyone who has interest in our government.
