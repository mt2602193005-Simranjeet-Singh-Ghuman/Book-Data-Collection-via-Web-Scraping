Choo Choo is tired of her humdrum life as a commuter train and decides to run away, pursued by her engineer and the rest of her staff. It's a decent read - and the art is nice - but it isn't as deft or emotionally compelling as her other works. 4DS/2DD were OK with it but haven't been pursuing me with demands for a re-read the way they did with Burton's more popular trio (Mike Mulligan and His Steam Shovel/The Little House/Katie and the Big Snow). I'm sure someone train-obsessed would appreciate "Choo Choo" more, and bitterly rue that one of my small cousins recently (and abruptly) switched his fetish from "All Things Train" to "Ninja Turtles 24x7". .
