Buddy read with my emerging reader, to capitalize on her recent interest in this classic character. As a read aloud, she was happy with the dog. As a parent who’s been doing all the reading teaching, it suffers from my usual annoyances with early reader books, which is that they throw in all sorts of words that are too hard to decode for a kid who is still trying to figure out CVC words. (I realize this one is a level 2 reader, but the point stands.) It also lacked the simple charm of the original series, both in tone and in illustrations.
