The rhyming didn't suit the story. I think it would have been better had the story not rhymed, especially since the rhyming wasn't great. But I guess it would make it more engaging and fun for kids. I also didn't think it was a good idea at all to have animals telling the story. They were actually talking and telling the whole story. What a dumb idea. Why not have the people telling the story, because, you know, people can actually talk. It made no sense to have the animals speaking once all the people have left. It was cute though that the donkeys were remembering one who gave a ride. I also thought it was cute how the story was incorporated, that the calves are munching on hay that was like the baby Jesus lay on. The page with the big star shining over Bethlehem was pretty. He sky was such a pretty shade of blue and there were circles of light around the star and rays of light spreading out.
