The Hiding Place is Corrie ten Boom’s story of her work to help Jews in World War II during the Nazi occupation of Holland. Her story demonstrates so clearly not only the darkness of the world, but the light and beauty of the Gospel that outshines the blackest night. God preserved Corrie’s life, time and time again, for her to be able to tell this incredible story of His faithfulness. This book is difficult to read at times because of the heavy themes, but it is a true treasure and a must read.
