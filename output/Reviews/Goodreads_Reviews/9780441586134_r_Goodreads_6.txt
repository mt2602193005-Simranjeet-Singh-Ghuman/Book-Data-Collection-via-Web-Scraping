I read this whole collection before realizing the one story I wanted to read isn't actually in here. Oh well. I actually really enjoyed this collection; despite being "classic" (by which I mean older than most of my grandparents), it's quite readable, immensely entertaining, and surprisingly thoughtful, both in philosophical and scientific areas. Of course, you'll have to deal with a lot bit of pretty-female-as-plot-device syndrome, but bearing that in mind, this is still a worthwhile collection for any fan of pulp SF.
