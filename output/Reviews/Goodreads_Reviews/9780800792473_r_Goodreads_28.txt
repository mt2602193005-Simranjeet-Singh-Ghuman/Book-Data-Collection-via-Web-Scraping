"There's more work to be done..." Corrie ten Boom's story is incredible, but her faith makes me even more awestruck. She endured so much, including the loss of her father and sister while imprisoned in a concentration camp. Yet, through it all, she held on to the only sure foundation she had left: Jesus Christ. Betsie, her sister, loved everyone: including the people who would whip her. She bore no hatred for anyone. What a wonderful person! I also bear a great admiration for their father, who was faithful until the end. A great man indeed. Though Corrie struggled to trust and hope, she did not struggle in vain. Her story still lives on today, in the hearts of her readers, who were impacted by her story. Betsie's dying words, "But there's still more work to be done.." hit hard for me. There IS more work to be done - the spreading of the gospel and the love of Christ. Even in her death, her words live on.
