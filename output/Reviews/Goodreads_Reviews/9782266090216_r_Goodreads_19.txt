Probably something I would have enjoyed when I was younger, more misanthropic and when my taste was less refined, but now having read it, I just find Lautréamont to be an immature, overly conceited, misogynistic misanthrope whose (at times) nice imagery and surrealistic driftings do nothing to save the text as a whole. I couldn't wait for it to be over. This edition also includes Lautréamont's Poésies , which are a collection of humanistic aphorisms in contrast to Maldoror . I found this section to be much better, although as far as aphorisms go, they weren't that great.
