Exotic Zoology is a difficult book to review, as your enjoyment of this book is dependent on the reader's interest in tales of flora and fauna from antiquity, myths and legends. If you like to read historical documentation and stories about these topics, this is a book that you will enjoy. Approximately 20% of the book is made up of scientific interpretation, measurements and conflicting information that may become boring for the average reader. There are so many topics covered, that if you find your interest flagging, skim the sections that you're not interested in, and you will be rewarded with gems of information in subsequent chapters. As an example, I am personally interested in carnivorous plants and found the chapter on The Island of the Man-Eating Tree to be fascinating, as it covered a topic that I had never heard about.
