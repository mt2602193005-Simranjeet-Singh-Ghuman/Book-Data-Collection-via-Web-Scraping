Sarah Kincaide thinks she's found a safe refuge for herself and her infant son Danny in Lame Horse, Montana -- until Zach Garrett arrives to force them back to the powerful family that deceived Sarah and laid claim to Danny. Traveling across Montana with the handsome but seemingly ruthless Zach, Sarah is heartbroken about losing her baby... but she would be horrified if she realized the fate that awaits her son, in the hands of a woman who has already mothered two other babies to their deaths. Meanwhile, Zach is growing ever more unhappy with the part he has to play in Sarah's tragedy -- but how else can he reclaim the land he loves? An absorbing, sweet, slightly adventurous story with an interesting twist -- Munchausen Syndrome by Proxy in the old West.
