Summary: This story was a sweet tale about how a little dog named Spot was about to have his first day of school. When he first arrives, he is nervous, but as the day goes on, he becomes more comfortable with his surroundings and the new animals he becomes friends with. He is able to find new adventures and new activities that he really enjoys. By the end of the day, he does not even want to leave. Analysis: I thought this book was a sweet story with cute pictures and flaps that showed fun things that Spot was doing at school. The entire series of Spot doing things is a sweet series with fun introductions to things children might not be comfortable with. Classroom Ideas: I would probably use this in a kindergarten or pre-k class to help children be more comfortable with the changes in environment.
