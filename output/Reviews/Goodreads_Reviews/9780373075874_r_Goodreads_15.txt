I really enjoyed this book. Barbara Samuel did a fantastic job of creating her characters, and the world in which she placed them. The characters were believable, the plot built to a fantastic climax, and it kept you enthralled from start to finish. The main characters were both a little broken, and neither one was looking for or wanted to find themselves in a relationship. I enjoyed watching them find themselves as they worked their way through Mattie's situation. I definately recommend Breaking the Rules.
