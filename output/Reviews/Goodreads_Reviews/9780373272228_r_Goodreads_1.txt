The Detective and the DA is about two divorced couple who gave up on each other when Kelly mis-carried and instead of sharing their grief with each other and took refuge in work and decided to call it quits. It's been four years when Ash is attached to Kelly's case, a high profile one. Kelly is an assistant district attorney and Ash is a cop. Kelly is shocked to hear that she has to work with Ash and even Ash is not a happy camper but they can't do anything about it, creating a fuss would only reflect badly. Both of them can't help but feel that old pull of attraction but the case keeps them easy because it seems that the the rich society husband got away with murder. When there is political pressure over their line of questioning, the DA and police chief decide that it would be better if Ash and Kelly pretended to reconcile and attend social functions together. Kelly and Ash function well as a team and there is a moment when they both share grief for the child they lost but Kelly isn't sure that she is willing to trust Ash again. It is about both of them making their way to each other, both of them just shut off instead of dealing with things. I really enjoyed these two coming together.
