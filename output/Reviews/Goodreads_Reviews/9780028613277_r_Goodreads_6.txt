THe Coyote Cafe tamales are still my absolute favorite tamale recipe--probably because of the butter :-) But they are also lighter, fluffier, and I could eat them week in and week out if it weren't for the calories and the carbs :-) We have made a couple hundred to take on canoe trips and they are just amazing when eaten after a long day in the sun on the river, heated up over a steaming pot on a fire...I like the corn and jalapneo and cheese ones, all the pork options, and the chicken and chili are not bad either. I haven't had any of the beef ones but I suspect that would be good too, but I don't make them often enough and so tend to stick with pork. Yum....the only tamale cookbook you will ever need...
