Jamaica is a young girl who finds a stuffed animal at the park. She adores the stuffed bear, but her family advises her to return the stuffed bear to the lost and found. Her returning the bear makes the owner relieved that he has his stuffed bear back. This book teaches a good lesson about never keeping items that do not belong to us. Even though Jamaica found the bear, her parents taught her the right thing to do was to return it where she found the bear. I would use this book to teach story elements. This book has a distinct setting. Also the characters and problem and solution; this book would be a great one to do a mini lesson on to practice identifying these literary elements.
