I love Liz Fielding’s books - she has a beautiful writing style and I usually love her characters and plots. But I ended this one not at all sure what I felt about it. Truthfully, I was confused by a mix of very different feelings. There was so much of this that I liked but so much left unsaid and unknown. I never properly understood why Rose had married her older husband and what their relationship had actually been like. It was so understated as to be invisible. I didn’t understand why Hassan kidnapped Rose. As a plot device, it seemed very contrived to me. I loved Hassan and I loved the way LF revealed his feelings and thoughts to him. Watching him fall deeper and deeper in love with Rose was delicious. I found the journey Rose was on rather convoluted and difficult to follow. There was so much to like but the ending was so tame and so fast. I turned the page expecting an epilogue but no - it was finished. I think the problem for me was that the rather dated feel of the book (understandable since it was published in 2000) and the closed door bedroom scenes. I didn’t feel the chemistry between them and even if there are no sex scenes, I really like some sexual tension. I found the dialogue to be particularly dated and several of Rose’s speeches jarred with me. But on the other hand, some of the dialogue between Rose and Hassan was beautiful. A pleasant and enjoyable read but not up to LF’s usual 5 star ratings.
