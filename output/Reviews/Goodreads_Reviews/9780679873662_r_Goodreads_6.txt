I actually read this book as a child. I was maybe five when my grandma brought this book home and I loved it to death. It still sits hidden on my bookshelf, one of my coveted childhood books. Now that my baby sister is learning to read (yes, there is a very big age difference, lol) I had a reason to take it off my shelf and share it with the world. We also found more books in the series at our local library and I am so pleased with them! But this book in particular will always hold a special place in my heart. It was a good learning to read book, as well as an adorable story of Thistle Howl that will surely stay with you forever. Purple Pickle Juice is a ten out of ten in my book any day! If you have a little monster in your life that is learning to read, I can guarantee they will love these stories!
