𝐌𝐢𝐧𝐢 𝐫𝐞𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰. ┈ ⋞ 〈 Julotta 〉 ⋟ ┈ ★★★★☆ ╰┈➤ Quick little Christmas book (the best kind 🥰) I’ve already read 4 Christmas novels this season and this one is definitely able to compete with the big novels in my opinion. I love, love, loved the inclusion of the Swedish recipes ahh. Lil backstory into why I loved it so much hehe: I grew up with a Swedish-American family for about a year or two when I was younger and boy it was such a great time. Not only do they have an amazing taste for food, but they have such a beautiful way of celebrating Christmas. It is still my favorite thing ever to go to my friend’s house and walk through the decorations of the Gävle and the beautiful candles decorated with plentiful stars and wreathes. The very definition of a cozy Christmas imo <33 If you want these vibes I heavily recommend you take a lil look into this book :) I loved the CLEAN romance, with the inclusion of a whole story in so few of pages. It was a great lil dip into a historical Swedish Christmas, with the customs of dating etc in the process as well. Trope: -semi love triangle -childhood friends to lovers Content: Nonnee :)
