This story is about a family of bears that had a whole lot of stuff. Harboring all these things made mama bear feel compelled to give it to those in need. At first, everyone is reluctant to let go of their things but by the end, they are happy they did. It shows the journey of the bears going to those in need and giving their items to them, and then feeling happy that they did. I think this book is wonderful! It teaches all about having a giving heart which I don't think is talked about nearly enough. The bears go to places like children's hospitals and nursing homes and it gives children a good idea of where they can go to serve. I would use this to teach service. I would explain how some have very little, and some have a lot. So much so, that they have things they do not need sitting around and never being touched. I would encourage them to help those in need. And, even if they cannot give things, they can give time. The bears in the book spend time with those in the nursing home.
