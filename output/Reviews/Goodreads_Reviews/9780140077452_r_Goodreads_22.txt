I guess this is the real name of one of my favorite authors - Jane Haddam. This is apparently a young effort, when she first started and not much like the Gregor Demarkian mysteries I have come to love; except for the elements of humor. This was more light and frivolous than the Demarkian series seems to be, yet enjoyable. I liked the book, but has a vague sense of disappointment. Just a bit too silly at times to respect, even though it was a good read.
