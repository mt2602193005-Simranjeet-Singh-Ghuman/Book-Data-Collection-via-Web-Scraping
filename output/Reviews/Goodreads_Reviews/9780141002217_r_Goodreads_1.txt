0.5 star for the title 0.5 star for the content 1 star for the spine to write a book about Einstein! The only good thing about this book was its compelling title. I picked it up because of that, even after bad reviews, and boy was it disappointing. Long story short, I did not like this book. I realized this midway, so I started speed-reading it. Every once in a while, the author’s voice becomes so loud (and narrow), the focal point gets lost. I reckon maybe the reason behind this, was the subject person of the book - Einstein. It must be very hard to write about him. I mean writing a book about Einstein’s ‘work’ is understandably hard, but here author tried writing both about his ‘work’ and ’personal’ life simultaneously. A misfit’s failed relationships with everyone and other drama surrounding it, just like it happened with Steve Jobs’ personal life, explained some of his theories briefly in a simplified version, and add a few contradictory opinions on a conspiracy , add some more so-called ‘facts’ that were never proven, and you have this book.
