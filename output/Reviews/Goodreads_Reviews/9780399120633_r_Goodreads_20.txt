This was a quick, "fun", horror thriller. I mean, it was the trashy and cheesy kind of fun. This was my first Koontz novel (and the year of me reading messed up books apparently). While it met my (honestly low) expectations, I don't think this was that great or would recommend this particular novel from him. It does suffer from that 70s/80s "this was written by a man" syndrome. There is distasteful sexual content and animal abuse. Oh and of course murder.
