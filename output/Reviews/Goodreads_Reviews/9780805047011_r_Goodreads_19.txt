Three separate essays, one on each on blue, yellow and red. They were highly enjoyable and readable and you left each essay with an overall impression of how each color is represented in nature and in society despite sometimes contradictory uses. In the end though it felt like an endless stream of trivia about color. I didn't feel like he really paused long enough on any given aspect of a color or its use to dig in. Luckily it was a highly entertaining stream of trivia.
