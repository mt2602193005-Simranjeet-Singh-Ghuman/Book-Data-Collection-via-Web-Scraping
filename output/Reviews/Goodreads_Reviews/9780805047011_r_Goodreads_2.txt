If you find yourself a little put-off by all our bluster about Theroux The Curmudgeon, The Cantankerous, The Opinionated, The Smarter-Than-Thou, (never to apologize for a large vocabulary, thank you) please find yourself a copy of his Primary Colors or perhaps his Secondary Colors . In these volumes you might indulge yourself in that rare bird, the Therouvian Essay, erudite and Burtonesque. The style is restful and the voice is endearing. Lovely.
