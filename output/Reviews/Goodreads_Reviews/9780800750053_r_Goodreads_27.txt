This book made me truly understand the meaning of heart-rending. I was gripped from beginning to end. This book made me feel more than I have from any book in at least 5 years. I cannot recommend this book with more sincerity. This is historical fiction focused to a fine point, making the main character's story come alive while also providing insight on what it was like to be a Christian in 19th-century Japan. Ayako Miura is a startlingly talented writer. I will make a point to read more of her books and I will certainly watch the Shiokari Pass move as well as check out the location since I live here in Hokkaido. Please don't pass on Shiokari Pass!
