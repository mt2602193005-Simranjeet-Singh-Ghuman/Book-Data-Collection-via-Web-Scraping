Mrs. Maier, the librarian at my elementary school, probably got tired of me checking this book out, week after week after week. I practically memorized it. Good illustrations, with tons of different animals, it has no real story, just a parade of various fuzzies, flippered, feathered friends. and people, in a zoo, at the end, if we don't protect them. (yes, this probably led to my years as a vegetarian. i got better.)
