Not only is this book actually great (it's layered, dealing with a lot of complicated emotions - plus it's nice to see more of Charlie and Sam) but I actually enjoyed that the story didn't get interrupted by silly baby-sitting chapters. I missed the other girls, they're pretty much only mentioned in passing, but keeping the flow of the story and the emotion made it seem that much more impactful. I also liked that this book didn't try to make Patrick into some kind of a saint now. He still sucks. He didn't pick up his kids from the airport, he sulked because his ugly tux choice got vetoed, he had a temper; and I liked that Kristy liked his new wife more than him. It was an interesting story, and it's too bad that it's a book that a lot of people probably missed out on.
