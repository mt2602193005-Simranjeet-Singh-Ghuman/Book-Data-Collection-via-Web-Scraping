Music of a Life by Andreï Makine This is a wonderful portrait of a Russian musician who flees his life to preserve his life when the "artistic elite" are being imprisoned by Stalin. It is also a portrait of the battered Russians Makine labels "Homo soviéticas"—people who are so numbed by the unfair whims of those in power as well as their broken infrastructure that they simply bear it. It is a cautionary tale for our times. A note: The library Kindle copy I read needs a good proofing. Possessive apostrophes as well as other punctuation are often missing.
