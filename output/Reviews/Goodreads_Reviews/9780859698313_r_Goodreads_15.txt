This was always a tricky read for me -- a little too close to home in some areas and a little too complex in others. It's a relief to read things I barely acknowledge to myself, to prove I'm not totally alone. It's tricky because it doesn't get better and that relief doesn't fix the inherent pain or damage or endless secondary diagnoses I accumulate. This is a fantastic book to read, it's full of information - perfect for the family, the person or anyone who fancies learning. But if like me, fixating on it (not just learning because that's different) makes it a little harder to bear and makes the grief of what was, what's lost, what could have been and what will eventually happen worse. Read carefully.
