This is a fun and bouncy story about a little girl with a blister by Amy MacDonald and illustrated by Marjorie Priceman . It's a pretty silly tale and we enjoyed listening to Judith Black narrate the story on CD while we followed along with the story. The ending is sweet and as everyone knows a kiss from Mom makes everything better! This kit from our local library came with an audio CD and a paperback book with ISBN 039565744X / ISBN13 9780395657447.
