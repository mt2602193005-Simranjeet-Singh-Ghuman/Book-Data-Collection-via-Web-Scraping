Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon Finally make it to Gala, and have to ensure a peaceful transition to democracy as the monarchy dissolves, and find the secret first born heir to the throne. To accomplish they split up Qui-Gon goes to find the heir Eland among the Hill People, and Obi-Wan has to hold down the fort at the Palace and figure out what’s wrong with the queen. Another fun adventure while it’s neat to see our two leads split up. I do like it best when they're together as they have a good dynamic. Some fun light political intrigue in this one, with a mention of Offworld to keep Xanatos in our minds. The musings about legacy are nice if a little under explored but Gala is a decently developed setting for how long the book is. It’s probably a good sign that my main problem with these books is that I wish they were longer. Not much to say about our leads Obi-Wan continues to be pretty competent but both need the other to get over the finish line here. Qui-Gon's doubts about what legacy he lives as a rootless wanderer are interesting but not really dwelled on, Obi-Wan's thoughts about character and family ties are even more perfunctory. Bit noticeable that Obi-Wan is still reluctant to confide in Qui-Gon seeing him as a bit stern bit of a communication issue, In a more positive angle Qui-Gon is more and more sure Obi-Wan is going to be a great Jedi. Another solid B but a little lower as the formula begins to settle in thankfully we have a good arc coming to break things up and give us a more overarching narrative.
