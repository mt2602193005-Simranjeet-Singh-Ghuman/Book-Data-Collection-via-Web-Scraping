Eh. It was an interesting idea, I guess, but the execution bored me entirely. When the Green sisters find out that their mother had an affair and their father easily forgave her, 17 year old Joanna and 15 year old Amy move in with their older sister, Meg, who's attending Yale. This is Joanna's story about that year, with comments from her sisters every now and again. It twisted Little Women enough to be interesting, but....blah. I didn't really like any of them much, and the ending was so abrupt as to be obnoxious.
