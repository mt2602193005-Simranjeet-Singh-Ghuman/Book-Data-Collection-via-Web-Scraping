In this story the main character who is a little critter has to go to the dentist. The whole book goes over the whole dentist appointment and how the little critter felt about his first time going to the dentist. The little critter feels reluctant to go to the dentist at the beginning of the story because he feels like he does not need to go to the dentist. Once he makes it to the dentist he observes his surroundings and enjoys little moments of his experience, such as another young girl's braces, and how the room looked like a spaceship. He also enjoyed being able to spit in the sink and he ended the unexpectedly enjoyable day with a sucker! This book was an easy read with detailed illustrations that sketched. There were only a few sentences on each page that were brought together with pictures that went along with the words. I liked the simple story line of this book and how it ended with a mood change from the main character. I enjoyed how his attitude started negatively and ended positively. I would use this book in the classroom focusing on the mood change and exaggerating the excitement in the critters voice when he finds multiple aspects of the dentist that he thinks are cool.
