I've decided recently to try this book again. I have no issue with it, or its contents. I love Gordon Hinckley. It's a great little book with great little thoughts. My rating is more an indictment of the style. I have never been one that loves the 365 day inspirational message type book. It feels like it should be in calendar form, and I'd flip through it as eagerly as a Far Side or Most Amazing Facial Hair calendar. I just wish this book was in narrative form, and a tad more substantive. We'll see how it goes the second time around, and please don't doubt the validity of the message (an excellent one) because of the averageness of my rating.
