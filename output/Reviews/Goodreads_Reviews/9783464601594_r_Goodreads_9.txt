The Last Man Alice really captures the spirit of what being a student at this school would have been like with Neill, the imaginative and legendary caregiver. Neill was known to be able to make up stories on the spot and his memory must have been remarkable to be able to write this all down so well. Neill wrote that out of the 20 books he wrote in his lifetime, this was his favorite one. There may not be a connection, but the plot begins very much like the anime, Dr. Stone.
