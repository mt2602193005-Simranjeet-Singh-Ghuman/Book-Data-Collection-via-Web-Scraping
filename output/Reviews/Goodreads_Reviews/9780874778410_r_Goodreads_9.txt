I stumbled onto this book about twenty years ago. The title jumped out at me and after thumbing through it for maybe a minute, I bought it. My first read-through was downright fun. But I knew the benefits were in actually trying some of the "razors". Twenty years later I can now say without hesitation that it had a profound effect on me. I wore out two copies and have a third one on the shelf. My wife has her own copy. I've also given a dozen copies to various people in my life.
