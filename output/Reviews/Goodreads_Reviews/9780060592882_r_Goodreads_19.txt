I’m not really a huge fan of short stories but I thought I’d try these out because I’ve heard good things about this collection and I like this author. After reading this... I still am not a huge fan of short stories lol. These stories were mostly character studies instead of being plot driven and many seemed to end abruptly/ambiguously which just isn’t really my cup of tea. That being said I can appreciate that these are very well done short stories even if I personally didn’t enjoy them that much.
