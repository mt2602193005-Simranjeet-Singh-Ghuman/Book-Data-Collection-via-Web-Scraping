P.I. Rex Carver is hired to find the heir of a minor fortune and ends up chasing a fortune in diamonds with climax on a boat on a French canal close to Champagne. You may call Victor Canning James Bond + intelligence and humour. You will never call his thrillers (of whom there are a lot!) funny, but it is intelligent reading, yet human and interesting. You might nearly guess, that he was a good friend of Eric Ambler! The book is very wellwritten (in 1966) and the age shows in the atmosphere -the new hard ruthless evil of today's novels is happily absent! I like it and have read several of Canning's novel, which are all recommended.
