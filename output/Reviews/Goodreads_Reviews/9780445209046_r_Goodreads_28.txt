Horror isn't my favorite genre but I found this pretty enjoyable. The plot moves along briskly and the characters are engaging. For a nearly forty-year-old book it has not aged too badly, although I would have preferred to see the (human) female characters play more active roles in the story. (I'm in no position to comment whether the portrayal of Native American characters is culturally appropriate.) Violence is not too graphic, profanity is mild, and there are no sexual situations included, so this would be appropriate for teens and even mature tweens.
