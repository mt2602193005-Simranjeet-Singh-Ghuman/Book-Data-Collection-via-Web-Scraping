If there was any book needed to reignite one's anglophile spirit, this is the one. It is undeniable that this island is a tabernacle of a truly ancient and mystical tradition that differs from other countries with an early Christian history. Bede is a great read - mapping the political relationships of each region, the role of their assigned Bishop and ruler, and how these all play a part in how nations grow, are graced with a period of peace, or are subject to Divine punishment. There are many parallels and archetypes used with the journey of Britain into a Catholic nation and that of the Israelites into the Promised Land. Most likely intentional, it paints Britain as serving an eschatological purpose (yes, it did spark some British Israelism interest). 10/10. Would read again.
