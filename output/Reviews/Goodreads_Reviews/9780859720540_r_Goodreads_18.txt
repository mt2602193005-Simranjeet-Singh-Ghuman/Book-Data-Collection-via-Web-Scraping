This Victorian novel is well worth reading, it is full of pathos and trips along at a good pace, but I found it most interesting for the following reasons: Use of dialect – lots of occasionally impenetrable Lancashire dialect which gives it richness. Elizabeth Gaskell eat your heart out! View of Manchester – it was written in the 1860s but is set decades earlier at the turn of the nineteenth century and follows the development of the shock city of the industrial age. Peterloo massacre – insights into this notorious part of British history.
