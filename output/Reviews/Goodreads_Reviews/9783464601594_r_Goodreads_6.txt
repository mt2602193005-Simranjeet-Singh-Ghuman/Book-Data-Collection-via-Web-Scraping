I read this book when I was probably a bit too young. This feels weird to say, as someone who got my grubby little hands all over my dad's very adult scifi books and survived without any noticeable eotional scarring, but there's a certain combination of "very young protagonists" and "not pulling punches" that would be a delightful scary read for a precocious pre-teen but ended up being realy terrifying for elementary school me. This is all to say, I have FEELINGS about this book! It was absolutely formative and it's a pleasure to revisit even now. I love it.
