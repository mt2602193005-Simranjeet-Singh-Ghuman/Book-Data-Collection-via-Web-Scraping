This book pissed me off so bad. What an interesting premise and what a terrible cast of infuriating caricatures - sorry, I mean characters - stuck in the most nonsensical (derogatory), plotless stakes possible. The rock is a fortress but it's also a dragon but it's actually a worm with a dragon inside of it that will release OTHER worms and they're unstoppable apex tank-predators but also there's still a literal flying, fire-breathing dragon that was the psychic bodyguard tulpa of an ancient (alien?) race who enslaved humans inside of the wormfortress to farm their psychic energy to feed the dragon to keep them safe from germs like WHAT????????? "Oh, the twists, the spoilers! Go into it knowing nothing!" they all said. They were wrong. They babytrapped me, except the baby was bewildering nonsense. I could be generous and say the stereotypes and caricatures were a product of the time in which this book was written, but it still wouldn't excuse the fact that it was a terrible book. To be frank, I SHOULD have DNFd (which is equivalent to one star, more or less) but I was so mad that I decided to poison my psyche and see it through. Don't be me. Just let it go.
