DeRogatis's biography of Lester Bangs is terrific so I was happy to discover this anthology. Like the Bangs bio, "Milk It" is an opinionated, funny blurring of 'fan' and 'critic.' DeRogatis is crazy about rock and roll and takes its artists seriously but not too seriously. He calls out bullshit and doesn't hesitate to criticize musicians who are derivative, lazy, crass, or willing to strip rock of its ethos of rebellion. Thought the pieces in "Milk It" span 10+ years and originally appeared in fairly disparate publications, a cohesive narrative about the pop culture and bigger social context of the 90s emerges. It's also funny to look back on pieces from the early 90s and see how often DeRogatis got it right. I mean, I disagree with his takes on the 1993 Lollapalooza line-up, post-Automatic R.E.M. and Tone Loc, but he's usually spot-on here. Also, DeRogatis is probably more or less correct about nostalgia being an enemy of rock, but as someone who started this particular decade in eleventh grade and ended it starting to work on my dissertation, I had lots of flashbacks, mostly good ones, reading "Milk It." Fantastic read.
