I very rarely ever give SpongeBob books more than a 3 star, but this one I felt deserved more than the others. It wasn't 'hyper' nor degrading to one another etc. SpongeBob wanted his friend Patrick to go to the circus with him. But when Patrick gets sick he wants SB to stay with him but SB doesn't and goes onto the circus. But while he's there he tries to enjoy all the neat things he's never seen but then starts feeling guilty about his friend not being there with him. So he leaves the circus and goes to Patricks house and puts on his very own circus for him! Remembering all the things he saw he recreated so his best friend could enjoy it too. A very touching storybook of friendship and love for your friend.
