This book has a lot of recipes from full meals, to sauces and fillings, to cakes, to drinks. There are also guides such as syrup guides that help you gauge the temperature for your creations. My one and only complaint about this book is that some of the descriptions are really lacking and I imagine I’m going to have to look at outside references to learn how to do the more technical parts of some of these recipes. Other than that I was super pleased with the content and I’m excited to start trying these recipes out.
