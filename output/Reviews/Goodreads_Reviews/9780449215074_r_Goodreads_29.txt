From the heyday of gothic romance novels and I devoured them all back in the 60s. Know I read this before but no recall at all. Definitely not as riveting and romantic as these books felt back in my youth. The male lead did not come across as particularly worthy of the mad love of the heroine. He was handsome but not terribly nice or warm. The pace was a bit leisurely but there was a good picture of the beautiful Cornwall coast setting. I pretty much figured out who was trying to kill our Bride if not all the particulars. Tastes do change over the years.
