I’ve purchased this book a few months ago from a local book sale. I haven’t read Harlequin books for a long long time and when I read its book summary, it strengthen my will to purchase it. But dang! I wish I didn’t. Expectations really hurt. But luckily, I was able to finish the story without totally ditching it. It was so boring that I can’t help but skip some lines. Though honestly, I haven’t read a story before with the same plot, but I just hate how the author weaved its story. And what’s more excruciating is that the author doesn’t use very reader friendly words. While reading it, I keep on consulting my friend Meriam D. for the meaning, and that sucks. This is actually my first time reading a book from this author and I don’t think I’ll hang around and read other books from her. I guess adult romance isn’t for me. I sounded like I really hated the story that much eh? So why 2 stars? Well, I also need to give credits to those times (I think that was twice) this book made me feel “kilig” and smile. So that’s it. That’s behind those intriguing 2 stars rating. About its characters. Well, honestly, I don’t find Jeger (main male character) that appealing. It’s really hard to picture out how he looks like. And Fennia (main female character)! I really hated her character. She keeps on stammering which is really annoying. And the part where the two of them confessed on each other’s feelings? My oh my! Can’t the author think of other romantic scene for that?! Oh well, I just realized that this book isn’t worth my money.
