I just finished Sleep While I Sing (Karl Alberg #2), and I’m not sure if the novelty wore off or if I’ve simply adjusted to the 1980s world: the typewriters, the cigarette smoke, and the lack of modern forensics. This one felt darker and not quite as spellbinding as the first, and I would’ve liked a bit more romance, which was very sparse. I guessed the murderer almost from their first appearance, but I still enjoyed the story. The quiet coastal atmosphere and gentle writing style kept me turning the pages, and I’ve already started book 3 in the series, I’m excited to see where it goes next.
