This book has been sitting on my tbr shelf on Goodreads for a while and I finally managed to get around to reading it. Actually, I listened to it on audible and the narration was good (but I sped it up to 1.1 since she reads a little slow) The book certainly accomplished its task. I found myself praying as I listened, and more than once, I had to put it aside so I can focus more on a time of prayer. That said, I also had moments where I was simply thinking, "That is extra-biblical theology" or "sometimes these people are just weird". Although I was listening to the 2009 revised and updated version, there were plenty of moments when some of the craziness of the early 90's theology of the original was peeking through. I do have to say that CJ is not as extreme as some of the others in the Word of Faith heyday were. She does give a good amount of moderation against some of the extremes. In all, the book is good, but if someone is looking for a book on prayer, I would recommend Dutch Sheets Intercessory Prayer or Richard Foster's Prayer rather than this work.
