I'm making very slow progress reading my backlog of John Gardner Bonds* ... I have about 10 on my shelf, but this will be read next (out of order) as I have acquired a second copy. *so many other spin-off books and homages! (26/05/26) *moderate spoilers* According to the listographers this is the 15th (of 16) of the John Gardner books, and unusual as it’s been adapted from the screenplay* This is a strange beast, set in 1986 (post-Cold War), written in 1995, but immersed in the Bondian language, prejudices, and sensibilities(!) of the golden era of the original books, i.e. late 1950’s/early 1960’s. Of course there are trigger warnings for anyone born in the last 40 years (including a rather close to the knuckle sex scene), but it’s Bond, so yeah. It was fun to read and imagine Pierce Brosnan et al and running through the film in my head … the one with Xenia Onatopp, Judi Dench’s debut as ‘M’, the tank and the train (St.Petersburg), the big lake satellite dish (Cuba), Alan Cumming overacting as a computer geek with an exploding pen, and the existential fear of destruction from space (GoldenEye) etc. etc. It all seems so quaint and retro now, I love the 1950/60/80/90’s nostalgia! *Or, book 33 in the extended series. Gardener’s 9th book ‘Licence to Kill’ is the same. There is, of course, a much larger Bond universe that I merely scratch the surface of!
