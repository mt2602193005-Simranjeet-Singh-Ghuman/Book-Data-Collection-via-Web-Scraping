I generally find that in books of short stories there's some I like, some I don't & some I just don't get! So "Piranha to Scurfy" is a bit of an oddity for me in that there were some I really liked, some I liked & NONE that I didn't like! Now don't go into this expecting a set of Rendell crime/psychological tales, these are..mmmm.... I'd liken them to being sort of "Tales of the Unexpected"ish but much better! Rendell is adept at building a sense of unease in so few pages with some tales having a touch of the gothic & supernatural thrown in & all with a little twist - some of which you may suspect, others you may not. Overall a book of short stories where I enjoyed - & understood!! - them all (which must be a first for me)
