I will read just about anything if it's set in ancient Egypt, so this book was certain to be read. I find that the story works less as a mystery (though it is there), and more as a portrait of ancient Egyptian life and also a political thriller, in the court of Hatsu, the Pharaoh Queen. This is very readable, but I was never entirely immersed in the mystery. But I would still recommend it to anyone who loves Ancient Egypt.
