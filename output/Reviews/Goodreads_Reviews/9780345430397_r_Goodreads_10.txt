After the big bad demon gets yeeted back to wherever demons go when they lose, and everyone is sad, a plague sweeps the land because apparently defeating one apocalyptic evil wasn’t enough. Why have one downer when you can have two? This book doesn’t have a single chapter that doesn’t feel like it was scored with minor key violins. Salvatore writes grief so thoroughly you start wondering if he lost a bet with his editor. The fight scenes are still crisp and violent, but they’re now interspersed with 3 page internal monologues about the futility of existence. The central thesis of this book Suffering builds character Repeated At length With swords I suspect Salvatore doesn’t actually like his characters very much
