Star Bridge, by Jack Williamson and James E. Gunn (1955), is a solid, entertaining and well written vintage space opera. Of course the book shows its age, but like old wine, not like an old gadget. Highly recommended. This book is a great classic of science fiction, with galactic empires, super science, future societies, intrigue and mystery, and cosmic vistas full of stars.
