This book was okay, kind of funny, but not what I expected. Summary: This book is about a little boy named Elbert, who was at a party and was hearing a lot of bad words. Being as young as he was he repeated everything he heard. So, when something fell on his foot, he expressed his feelings the only way that he knew how and that was by saying bad words. His mother took him to the bathroom and explained to him that he shouldn’t say those kinds of words. Elbert was sad, so he walked through the woods where he found a wizard and the wizard told him words that he could use in place of the bad words. So he went back to the party, and some how the same thing happened again and something fell on Elbert’s foot. Everyone was expecting him to say more bad words, but he expressed his pain in a much better way. How to use this book with students: You can use this book with Kindergarteners through 3rd graders to talk about bad words and why you shouldn’t say them. It could also be used to teach manners, because it is not polite to say bad words.
