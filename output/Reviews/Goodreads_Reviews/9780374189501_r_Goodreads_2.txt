This book was a weird one because it started out like, what is she talking about? Then towards the middle, Oh I actually like the characters and I kinda know who is who. Then in the end I actually teared up for a second. Living in Hawaii is making me read more local stories and this one gives an inside glimpse of what it is like to live in a local Chinese Hawaiian family. I understand pidgin so it wasn't hard for me to read it all, but a glossary would help, especially for the Chinese words. The literary style is particular and unusual. You don't understand who's talking, some stories are nostalgic, some make sense, some forgettable. It's written more as a memoir for your family, not to forget, rather than intended for the general public. If you don't live in Hawaii, or know any pidgin, don't bother. If you are local, you might like this book.
