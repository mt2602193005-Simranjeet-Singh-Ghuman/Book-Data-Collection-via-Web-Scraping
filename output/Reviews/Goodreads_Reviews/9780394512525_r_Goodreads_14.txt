I remember getting this out of the public library. I'm not sure about the year. What I do remember at the tender age of 8 - 11 whenever I actually read this book, It enthralled me. Lionel painted such vivid pictures of this subterranean world, with its futuristic amusements and inhabitants. To be honest today it isn't so clear in my mind, but I do remember the haunting ending which is quite remorseful. Which even today raises questions whether it better to remember fully, half remember or forget wonderful events which you can never repeat. I'll freely admit I don't know. I wish I could find it again and see if it is as good as I remember I really do, but I can't.
