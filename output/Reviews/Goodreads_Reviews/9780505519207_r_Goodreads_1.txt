A great love story with a happy ending. I really enjoyed this book. The author has an interest in archeology and based the book around items found from 11,000 years ago. The story that She came up with fit very nicely together. It was not the typcial romance book, so it was good to not know the plot the ahead of time. Terah was captured by Mamut. Terah grew to love Mamut, but didn't tell him that She loved him. She chose to dwell on being an captive instead of admitting her feelings for her. I was little put off by the characters eating warm, fresh, raw livers from the animals they killed. But I suppose 11,000 that is very possible something our hunter and gathering types of ancestors did. Overall a very enjoyable read. This entire review has been hidden because of spoilers.
