I LOVED this. My very first romance I ever read without trying hide it was also by Jane Feather, so this was truly a nostalgia read for me. Overall the FMC is strong and bold and smart, and the MMC tries to control her at first but ultimately realizes he loves this little hellian just as she is. Questionable repeated use of a word for Romani people that is a slur now (this was published in 1995, and not that it makes it BETTER but it is used as a term of endearment, not an insult). Multiple references to skin color of the FMC (tanned versus the pasty white from lack of sun exposure).
