I know. I’m not 8 years old anymore. But the other night this 50+ year old had some major insomnia and thought maybe if I read something I’d already read (so I didn’t get new adrenaline) that it would help. Well. Not only did I suffer from major insomnia maybe getting 2 hours of sleep but I finished the damn book. No regrets. But it did make me realize that the writing was so incredibly juvenile…perfect for an 8 year old. But I also found lots of typos and grammatical errors. The storyline was sweet and predictable and nostalgic. Then I got to the end of the book. Read page 334. Then the next page was 325 (same as the earlier 325). Then the final page of 336. There really is something to be said about re-reading childhood favorites. But also as we get older and wiser we learn that we want more meat in the stories we read. I will continue to go through my cherished Trixie Belden series but know I need to lower my expectations. My book club picks for October are American Predator (TCF book club) and The Other Wes Moore (other book club). Have been looking forward to both so excited!!! Also at the top of my list are Songs in Ursa Major, House in the Cerulean Sea, and The Bell Jar. Love that I just hit 50 books for the year!!!
