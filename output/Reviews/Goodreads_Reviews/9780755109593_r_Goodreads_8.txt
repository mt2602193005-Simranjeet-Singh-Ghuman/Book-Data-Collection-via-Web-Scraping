3.5 stars. This is a solid book. Well written with no major flaws. Well other than the ludicrous notion that one cousin could replace another and not have anyone notice simply because they look identical. But if that set-up is too ridiculous for anyone then they should not be reading this book. I quite liked the husband and how he dealt with all the circumstances he found himself in. Juliet was likable and I enjoyed her troubles as she tried to pull off the charade. Josephine is less well drawn but fortunately she was not in it too much. An all round pleasant read but not probably something I will remember in a few months hence keeping it at 3 stars for GR. Show more
