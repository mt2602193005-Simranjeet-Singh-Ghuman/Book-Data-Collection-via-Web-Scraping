Super summary: Technical skills are important. However, they are not sufficient, and you may need to engage in politics. Be nice to people. Have sufficient sleep. Be honest. Be prepared. Have proper time management. Be pro-active in asking for deadlines to be adjusted together with change in requirements, and in making the coding process better (hiring testers etc...). Other options besides being a programmer include going solo, being a tester, vertical industry, sales... These are the main messages of the book. Reading the book will give you an appreciation of what goes on in the career of a programmer.
