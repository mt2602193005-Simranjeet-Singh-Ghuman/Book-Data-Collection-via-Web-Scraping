Could barely make it through- I stopped about 75% of the way because I felt like I had already wasted enough time. It was formatted to include author and reader notes that only complicate the story. This book was extremely dull and had no real, major plot points. The characters are privileged, seem to have no realistic concerns about finance, and the parents never question their living situation. The girls are not relatable or funny, and lack a the true spirit of an independent women living in the city. I am a junior in college and picked this up thinking it would speak to me. Spoiler: it did not. Read the real Little Women book.
