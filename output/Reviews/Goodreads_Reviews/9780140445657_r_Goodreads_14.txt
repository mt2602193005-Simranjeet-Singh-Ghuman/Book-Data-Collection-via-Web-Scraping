A very interesting and well written, (mostly) concise history of the first few hundred years of Christianity in England. I thought it was significant that London hardly got a look in (probably under the East Saxons?), and the bishoprics of York, Rochester and especially Canterbury carried more weight. I would like to read more about Medieval England to understand all of this better. Bede’s mention of miracles and cures were enjoyable to read. His fixation on the date of Easter got me thinking about what medieval Holy Week liturgies consisted of, especially for those parts of England/Ireland that didn’t initially place Easter on a Sunday.
