This is what I call a halfway book..................parts were very interesting and other parts were not, at least for me . The title tells us what it covers and why these offspring of US Presidents ended in "triumph or tragedy". This history has gotten good reviews and rather than addressing each person mentioned, I will list my reasons why I gave it only three stars. Pros: *there are many children of which I was unfamiliar and their lives were often quite surprising. *it covers almost every President from John Adams through George Bush. *it pulls no punches about the alcoholism/mental illness of quite a few of the subjects *well written Cons: *the end of each chapter contains a "psychological" reason why some of these subjects were failures and some were successes. This is my biggest complaint as it could apply to any offspring and not just to Presidential children. In addition, it would be almost impossible to psychoanalyze children of early Presidents. *the author jumps around in time rather than taking the book in chronological order. *there is quite a bit of information on the children of fairly recent times which most readers already know; i.e. John Kennedy Jr., Margaret Truman. If you can buy the psychology sections then this is an excellent read. Even if you don't, it has some interesting disclosures.
