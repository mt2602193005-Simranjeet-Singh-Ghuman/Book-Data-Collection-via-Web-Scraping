"What I want from you is the fifty years and more I saw between the Gannons today. The love and understanding, the memories of a lifetime. I want that from you.” REMEMBER WHEN was a fun detour from the usual In Death series with a two part story set in the past & present. We have one author penning both stories-Part one: Hot Rocks by Nora Roberts & Part two: Big Jack by J.D. Robb which you would think would have both voices sounding the same but honestly they were very different. Part one set the stage with the backstory of how the diamonds were stolen and getting to meet our leads Laine Tavish and Max Gannon. This was way too insta-love for my tastes so the romance wasn't sold for me. I did enjoy the whodunit and the side characters, especially Laine's father, Jack. Part two is set in the present with Eve investigating the murder of Lainie & Max's granddaughter's friend and trying to solve the mystery of what happened to those missing diamonds all those years ago. Of course this section was my favorite because 1. I love anything with Eve and Roake and 2. Peabody trying to find her new Detective 'style'. Poor thing was having a tough time😂 The ending was solid but honestly I'm excited to get back to just the In Death series that I've come to known and love.
