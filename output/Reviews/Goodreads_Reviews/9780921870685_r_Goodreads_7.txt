Read in the original. A dreamy SF/F novel, more focused on imagery than plotting or characterization. In an early (problematic) part, we're given some backstory on a sort of glass egg taken from an island off the coast of Africa. For most of the novel, we're given a tour of the fantasy city ... in the egg ... as the narrator encounters strange beings there with dubious motives. There may be some influence from Lovecraft's Dreamlands stories, but there's a lot that's wider in its sources: Atlantis vs. Mu, euhemerism in general, and that kind of thing. Show more
