This was amazing. I cried a little when I first read it. So tragic! I found my old battered copy from my teenage years after I moved in with my partner. I had to sticky-tape the cover back on. He read it too, but he won't admit to crying at the end. I know he's lying. Christopher Pike never took the easy way out with his books. He always made the characters hurt. And the reader hurt. So much hurt! It's got romance, horror, humour and freakin' time-travel! Even the title has heart-breaking relevance. Don't miss it!
