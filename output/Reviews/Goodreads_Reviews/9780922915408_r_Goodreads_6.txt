I can’t even classify this as a book. It’s more like the Danny Rolling hour. Frankly, I’m surprised it was ever published because the writing is awful and you never know if it’s the author or Rolling telling the story. Interspersed throughout the book are awful poems and second rate drawings. It is evident that Rollings had a high opinion of himself, which most serial killers do, but thinking that he had anything of value to offer was delusional. Don’t let me get started on the author, a term I use begrudgingly because she is not a writer. All told, don’t waste your time. If you want to delve into the mind of a serial killer read, The Gates of Janus by Ian Brady. At least that book is legible and easily understood.
