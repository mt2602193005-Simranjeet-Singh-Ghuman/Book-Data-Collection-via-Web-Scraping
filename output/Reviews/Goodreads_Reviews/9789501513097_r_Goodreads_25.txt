I am a sucker for western romances with a bit of tortured hero on the side; and although this story had some of my favorite tropes going on I did not enjoy it as much as I would have liked to. I felt there was no chemistry between Angel and Cassie, and no emotional depth to all the events going on. Given his past I would have expected more of an internal conflict from Angel, and more depth to the feeling that these two supposedly developed for each other. No character development and the emotional side fell flat...so a two star rating from me.
