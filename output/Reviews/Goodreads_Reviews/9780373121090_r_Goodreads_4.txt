4 stars is a bit generous i know. 3.5 stars is more like it. But coming from Anne Mather, this book is a good unexpected change. The leads were sincere, morally sound people. The presence of 2 bratty, incorrigible teenage girls added spice to the tale. Though they were too young to play the OW, they were irritable pests for the leads. Good fun to read. I loved the dignified way the heroine carried herself. Good read, kudos Anne Mather !
