I picked up the novel - written at the end of the Weimar Republic - thinking that it was about cabaret in 1920s Berlin, but it turned out to be erotica. Each chapter sets up a new fantasy for the bachelor protagonist Manfred, and a few offer fascinating windows into sex tourism in the city at the time (for example, there is a great scene where a party held at 'the steam baths' turns into a lesbian orgy). But at the halfway point the fantasies start to become downright appalling... Though revealing as a historical artifact, I think it is out-of-print for good reason.
