I'm not a huge fan of historical fiction or the old west, but the characters and dialogue in this entertaining time-travel romance hooked me and kept me reading until 2 a.m.! Kristen is a cop with a penchant for not only getting herself INTO trouble, but also getting herself OUT of it...which I loved. She's practical, tough-minded, and fiesty...and her training and ferocity serve her well when she's catapulted back in time and sucked up in the antics of a band of three outlaws. The characters come alive the moment they open their mouths, and the banter and subtext add dimension, humor, and tension to the interesting plot. PG-13 for some language (although no F words), sexual situations, the mention of prostitution/brothels, and an alarming near-rape scene, but none of it is gratuitous, and all of it deepens the plot and makes the characters impressively real. It IS a romance novel set in the old west, after all--so all that should be expected! The level of emotional intimacy portrayed for "soulmates" was a little light, IMO; however, I am a very intense, expressive romantic, so my idea of "soulmate" might be a lot more intense than what was portrayed, even though there WAS a lot of great sexual tension (and release). All in all, great plot twists and it kept me reading to the end in one sitting. My nails are all chewed off and I don't regret the late night. :D
