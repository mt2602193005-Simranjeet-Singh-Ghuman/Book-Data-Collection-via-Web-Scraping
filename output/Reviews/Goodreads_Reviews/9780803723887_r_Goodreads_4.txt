Genre: contemporary realistic Grades: k-4 Lottie is a little girl who went to bed dreaming of princess’s and dresses like most little girls do. When she woke up to get ready at for the next day of school Lottie decided she wanted to wear her princess dress. Her mother may not approve, but you’ll have to read to find out what happens between Lottie and her mother and what things might happen if she wears her princess dress to school.
