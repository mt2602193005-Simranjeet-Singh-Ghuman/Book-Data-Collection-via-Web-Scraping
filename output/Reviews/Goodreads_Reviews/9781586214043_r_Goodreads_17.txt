Sometimes you just want a “comfort read”. One that you know will grab your interest right away and keep it. James Patterson’s Alex Cross series is always a good go to for me. Tender moments with Nana mama & the kids to tense moments when the killer(s) are gaining ground and putting Alex’s life at risk. You’ll get a little more of Sampson’s story in this one too and that’s a good thing. I love that guy!
