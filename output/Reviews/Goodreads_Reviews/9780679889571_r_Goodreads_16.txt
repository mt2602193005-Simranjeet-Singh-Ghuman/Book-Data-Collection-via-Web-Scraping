Wonderful book to model compassion and sharing made doubly good by the fact that the Bear family gives not only donations of materials but also of their time and energy to improve the lives of other bears around them. As a pagan parent, I appreciate how accessible this book is for non-Christian children. While the book does mention Christmas (when the Bears stop to sing carols outside a thrift store), it models compassion, charity, and community spirit outside of any religious systems. Great message, gently given, about helping others around you and valuing time spent together above material possessions.
