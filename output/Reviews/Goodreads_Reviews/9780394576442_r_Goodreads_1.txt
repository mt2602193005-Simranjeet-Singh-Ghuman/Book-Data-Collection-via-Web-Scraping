A tough read this - both in terms of content and style. I am not sure if all of it works, or if my jet lag might have blurred my reading at times, but as a whole it is powerful, brutal and beautiful stuff. Sad and angry. Structurally complex and full of some exceptionally good writing. Certainly belongs on any list of essential African-American 20thc lit. Be warned though - there is rape and incest and neither of these are shied away from in terms of detail. But that is this world and that is truth and that is the long awful legacy of slavery. Highly recommended.
