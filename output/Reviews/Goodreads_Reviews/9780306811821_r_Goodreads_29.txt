A fascinating collection of articles by rock journalist Nick Kent, who spent much time interviewing and hanging out with the baddest, the saddest and ugliest characters in the rock scene. There are more misfits and brooding cats here than you can shake a drumstick at. The netherworld of rock 'n' roll is portrayed in all its glorious decadence. Petty ego-trips, insane drug intake, infantile bickering, and sad musings; all this is what made, makes, and will continue to make rock music the magnificent beast it is. While some of the articles really don't fit into the overall theme of this collection, the ones that share the common thread of maladjustment and misery are something to behold. The article on Roy Orbison was especially poignant. Here's a man who had more than his share of suffering in life, but carried himself with such grace and calm, that it makes everyone else in the book seem like a bunch of little brats. But then, we need our brats too. Rock 'n' roll is a restless animal that should never be tamed.
