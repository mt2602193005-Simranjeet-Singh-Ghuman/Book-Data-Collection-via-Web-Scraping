The author drew me in with an immediate feeling that something bad was about to happen. But it took way too long to reach the point. By the time I was getting close to the mystery the protagonist had become so annoying that anything could have happened to him and it wouldn't have bothered me. Why does someone want to read about a character that generally deserves anything negative that happens to him? And more, why would you want to write about it? The pace is too slow, the plot is not particularly interesting and the other characters are caricatures.
