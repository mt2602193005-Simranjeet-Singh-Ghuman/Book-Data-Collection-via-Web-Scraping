I never read all of these, as I was getting a little too old for the series by the time they started. I did read one or two, but then life happened and here I am, reading most of them for the first time as a 33 year old woman! Yeah! ADULTING! Anyway, I do like what I have read of these. They're a bit more mature than the regular series, but not quite as mature as the California Diaries. This one dealt with Kristy's father, Patrick, remarrying and wanting to actually see his children. I really especially enjoyed Charlie in this one, and the story overall wasn't bad.
