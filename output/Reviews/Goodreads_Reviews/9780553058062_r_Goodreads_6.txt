2025: this was the first teenager book i ever read and i had such daydreams of finding a genie of my own and wishing for the life of my dreams. rereading this as a grown-up made me look back on young chelsea with such love. i think she’d be pretty impressed with the life i made all on my own with no magic at all. maybe it’s not the single greatest book of all time but maybe it means everything anyway.
