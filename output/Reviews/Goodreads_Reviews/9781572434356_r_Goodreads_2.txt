This book about Dale Earnhardt is a limited edition and a tribute and farewell to the Intimidater. I never watched Nascar until the year 2000 when I moved in with my parents and they loved stock car racing. They got me hooked on racing also. I only saw Dale Sr. race in 2000 and 2001 when he crashed and lost his life at Daytona. In 2005 when the Sahara In Las Vegas was still open they had a huge racing Museum and the whole history of racing was shown. They had great displays and a lot of the exhibits was dedicated to him. He had 76 victories to his credit. Other racers before won more races but they were at the end of their days racing and he was up and coming. He is credited with taking racing out of the South and bringing it to all of America. He had an aggresive style of driving and a winner take all attitude. He is considered to be by many to be the greatest race car driver who has ever lived. I can tell from all the years I have watched Nascar now few drivers have shown his ability with a race car. He will always be a legend in his sport. I have gone to several races at the Fontana CA speedway and it is excellent in person. His son Dale Jr continued the family racing legacy. He raced until last year when he had concusssion problems and he wanted to get married and have a family. The book also has a section about Daytona being the Granddaddy of Nascar tracks.
