Andrew Douglas' job is countering kidnappers to secure the release of the victims. First incident is in Italy when a female jockey is taken. Second is in England where the young son of a Derby winner is taken. The third is in Washington DC where the senior steward of the Jockey Club is taken. It seems there are similarities and the work of one mastermind but can Andrew stay ahead of them and secure everyone's release? It's been a long time since I've read a Francis mystery. I didn't know when I started if it was to be three cases (three countries) or somehow one. Full of typical Dick Francis action, this was enjoyable.
