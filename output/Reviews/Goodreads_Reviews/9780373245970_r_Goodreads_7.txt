#1 in the Perfect Destinies romance series I'd rate this 3.5 stars if I could, but I was somewhat disappointed so I'll bring it back to 3 stars for Goodreads. The characters were good, but there was something that didn't quite mesh for me. Richard is a workaholic who is considered a great catch but stodgy. Melanie is a fiery and impulsive PR person who is trying to land his account. They are pushed together by Richard's aunt, but there's immediate chemistry between them. I think it was the aunt that bothered me--she was too smug and pushy, assuming she knew best and pushing them together relentlessly. Heck, they're grown-ups, let them figure it out. But it was all a big rush since Destiny seemed to feel there was no time to waste. The romance was nice, if rushed, and all the romantic elements were there. The main characters were intelligent and professional and did seem to be a good opposites-attract type of couple. I'm interested in the secondary characters of Richard's younger brothers, so I'll probably continue with this series.
