I have to admit. I didn't know what to expect. I was hesitant yet eager to read His Desert Rose; the cover and the little excerpt inside intrigued me. The writing style definitely surprised me. I didn't like it, and Mrs. Fielding wrote it like the whole thing was some... interview or newspaper article. I read the first 16 pages before skipping a few. I wanted to see if I could find where the excerpt came from. I didn't, but... Well, let's just say I am curious what happens towards the end. But I couldn't handle reading it anymore. I don't like that writing style. Period. I am a kid at heart with, I assume, a reading level of 10. I am sticking with that, even with the books I write.
