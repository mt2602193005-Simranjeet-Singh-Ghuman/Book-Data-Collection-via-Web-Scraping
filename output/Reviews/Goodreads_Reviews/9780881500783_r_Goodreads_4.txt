A little disappointing to me. I liked the espionage angle and didn't understand the elephant and the "Carney." Love the "Camera Club" and hope to see more of them in the future as well as the new doctor. It was like the murders were a secondary plot and never reached a satisfying conclusion to me. Still, I plan on working my way through all of the series. Love the silly banter like "waiting on my potty." I think much of it will go past today's audience. You have to appreciate a madcap Marx Brothers film to get the jokes or it makes little sense.
