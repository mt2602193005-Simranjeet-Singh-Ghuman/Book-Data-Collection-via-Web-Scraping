I don't remember where I heard about this book, but this is not at all the story I was expecting when I read the synopsis. I was thinking it would read more like Silence , that is, rather hard to read in the way certain classics are, but also in the way certain stories about persecuted Christians are. Instead, I got very simple prose that was very easy to read. And also, I just really enjoyed reading the story. It felt honest and simple, and at the same time it had a depth that you don't really see in a lot of books now days. From what I've read (I did a little research on the true story after reading the book ;P), I'm pretty sure a lot of it was fictionalized, so I'm not sure how much of it is actually accurate. But a lot of it just felt so real. The way the main character was written was so relatable. Sometimes painfully so. (The sheer amount of overthinking... AHH!) I also really enjoyed learning more about the Japanese culture, and what Christianity was like there. It's always refreshing to read about true believers who will follow their convictions no matter what. In conclusion, I kind of loved this book. And then it ripped my heart out, and I think I actually felt physical pain from the heartbreak, and made me wonder if I would ever recover. Which is why I took a star off. XP And how on earth did I not see that coming??
