When you’re writing a rather bland memoir - i.e., there’s nothing dramatic about it - then you better have some gorgeous writing or interesting/funny stories. But there was just nothing. At one point, the author pauses to actually write a paragraph about how his siblings have turned out as adults. Weird. It was as if he was writing with his immediate family in mind. The book might have had the makings of a wonderful literary memoir but the author just couldn’t pull it off. I had attempted to read this book some years back and picked it up again in case my failure to finish the first time had been due to my mood, but nope, it’s just an uninteresting memoir with mediocre writing.
