Solid nostalgia read for 80's children. Who Killed Harlowe Thrombey? was my absolute favorite Choose Your Own Adventure book as a child, and I read all of them in existence in the 80's. I can't count how many times I checked this book out of my elementary school library and my public library, and as an adult, I have always wished I would have asked my parents to buy me a copy of the book when it was readily accessible. I have literally been searching for this book for YEARS and have only found used copies for sale for outlandish amounts of money until recently when the book was rereleased this year (2026). I was so excited to find this book on Amazon for a reasonable price. Even though I read this book a million times as a child, it just never gets old. Every time I start reading it, I always have hope that I can make choices to stop Mr. Thrombey from getting murdered, but it never happens. He gets killed in all the endings, hence the name. The murder mystery unfolds quickly in each story path, and some would probably criticize the book for that because there isn't a ton of buildup, clues, or character building. However, for a fast-paced Choose Your Own Adventure book, it does a pretty good job packing a fun mystery into each story path with different murderers depending on the choices you make.
