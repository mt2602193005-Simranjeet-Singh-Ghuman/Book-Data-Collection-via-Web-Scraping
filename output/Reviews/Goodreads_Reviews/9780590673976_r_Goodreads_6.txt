Mallory and the Mystery Diary is a Baby-Sitters Club novel I don’t ever remember reading or even knowing existed, which is odd for one so early in the series. With that being said, this book was a brand new read for me, which was refreshing. In Book #29 of the BSC, Mallory and Stacey have become neighbors. While helping Stacey clean out the attic of their new-old house, the girls discover a locked antique trunk. Stacey doesn’t want it, so Mallory begs Stacey to let her have it. Of course, Stacey quickly obliges. A good portion of the book is spent on Mallory and her pack of siblings devising ways to get the trunk open. Once they finally do, they discover the diary of a young girl who lived in Stacey’s house 100 years ago, as well as a mystery about the prominent families of Stoneybrook. Naturally, if there is a mystery to solve, the BSC are on the case! The subplot of this book concerns Buddy Barrett needing a reading tutor. Mallory takes on the job because his mom is “too busy” working her part-time job and because she has no “quality time.” Ahh, the start of the lazy, selfish parenting we see so prevalent today. As a former teacher, I had a lot of issues with this tutoring set-up and Buddy’s inconsistent vocabulary and abilities, in addition to Mallory’s tutoring approaches. I did think it was neat that she ends up giving Buddy reading material that he is actually interested in, but as soon as he reads a book he likes, he goes from stumbling over every word to be a star reader, which sends mixed messages to kids in my opinion. In all, this book was a bit of a bore, but it gave us some insight into Mallory, her personality, and hobbies. This story is right up her alley!
