4.5 stars (for the genre of classic children's lit.) Just like in the first story, Lewis Carroll's writing style and whimsical queries are perfectly suited to a children's novel. This is another collection of Alice's episodic adventures, and I don't think it's any worse or better than the adventures of the first book. I do prefer this one though, especially because it has the inclusion of my beloved jabberwocky poem. Also, most of the iconic quotes that people associate with Alice in Wonderland are from this book, not the first.
