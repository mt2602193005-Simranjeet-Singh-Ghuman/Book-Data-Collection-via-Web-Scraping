Read in 2017 This was my first Road Dahl book. And I'm not sorry that I still love it🤫😅 If you haven't read this, then I'm so sorry to inform you that you didn't really enjoy your childhood. A golden ticket to pure imagination—where poor Charlie Bucket wins a tour of Willy Wonka’s sugar-fueled fever dream of a factory and the other kids get served their just desserts (sometimes literally). It’s a cautionary tale wrapped in candy wrappers: gluttony, greed, pride and bratty behavior all meet their match in Oompa Loompa verse. Dahl’s wit is sharper than a Everlasting Gobstopper. And Wonka? He’s half genius and half madman XD
