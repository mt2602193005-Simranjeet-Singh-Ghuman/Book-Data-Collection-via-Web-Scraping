"This was more agreeable than the present style of the research papers or books which have so much symbolism and formulae on every page. I am turned off when I see only formulas and symbols, and little text. It is too laborious for me to look at such pages not knowing what to concentrate on. I wonder how many other mathematicians really read them in detail and enjoy them." This is quite remarkable coming from one of the top mathematicians of his time. His own approach to mathematics was definitely more verbal and less symbol-based.
