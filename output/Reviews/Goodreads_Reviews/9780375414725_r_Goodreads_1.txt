‘There’s an old joke about Herrzegovinan women. Why do the husbands always die before their wives? Because they want to.’ (Well I laughed…my wife didn’t). Madeira holiday book#5 one of the Library finds here. Obviously discerning readers. As this is probably the most engaging, disturbing and historically interesting read of the holiday so far. Yes of course I have read book 2 in a series of…wait for it…2. But that is ever my way. The story is based around an ex Bosnian detective- Vlado Petric - who had to escape from Sarajevo (grrrr book 1). He now lives with his wife and daughter in Berlin as a labourer. However, his past comes back to haunt him when the International War Crimes Tribunal want him for a sunbeam. (Oops sorry had watched a documentary on Nirvana in the UK on the flight over.) They want him to lure a WW2 murderer out back home to arrest. At times this was like reading a le Carre in style and plot. That means it was ‘bloody brilliant’ to use the best descriptions from elite book reviewers of which I am not. It moves from Germany to Bosnia and the climax is in Italy. Vlado is betrayed by the CIA and other spooks. Finds out about his father and is almost killed twice. But the plot and writing style are gripping. I note that the Author has written tons of other books. I am off to the library here shortly as it is a changeover day and most will have returned books. Ps typo on page 142 - ‘placed’ for ‘place.’
