🍷 A definite glass of wine book. Offbeat, thought-provoking, and entirely satisfying reading. Lennox Kemp finds another quirky mystery to try to solve, and does so but also so much more…these M. R. D. Meek mysteries are as good as they get. Fifteen were written between 1983 and 2004, and while you’ll never find all of them, if you search and come across any, you’ve found treasure. I still have three more of the somewhat available volumes left to read, and I’ll take them slowly and savor each one.
