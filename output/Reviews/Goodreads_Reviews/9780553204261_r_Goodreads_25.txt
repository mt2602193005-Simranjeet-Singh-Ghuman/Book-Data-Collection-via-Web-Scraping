Bradbury is remarkably talented at combining mundane moments with wildly absurd fantasy. He specializes in this niche and he executes it well. This book collates a series of short science fiction stories, each mere pages in length, throwing the reader into new settings with new characters every chapter, which personally made it more diverse and interesting to read than Bradbury’s Martian Chronicles. Not every story was a hit for me but honorable mentions go to “The Wonderful Ice Cream Suit” and “Dark They Were, and Golden-Eyed.”
