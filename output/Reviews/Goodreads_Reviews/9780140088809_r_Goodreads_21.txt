A woman's body, sliced at the throat, is found by a dog in a clearing a few miles from Sechelt. She had been posed lying at the bottom of a tree, but with no identification on her. Alberg and his police members search for the culprit, and the owner of the dog is the first suspect. Alberg hires a local art teacher to paint the woman's face so they get a picture to circulate to find her name. They discover she's an LA actress who had come unannounced to Sechelt to meet with her friend who's been visiting for three months. This was a short but very engrossing book which kept me anxious to read more. And I definitely will. Written in 1986.
