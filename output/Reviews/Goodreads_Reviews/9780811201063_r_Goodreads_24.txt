People don't write like this anymore. Henry Miller's biting reaction to 1930s USA, after a long time in Paris, could be written about today. We've built a world of convenience and up until recently, tremendous prosperity. But it isn't a happy world we've built, the economic and political model are linked to consumption and the prosperity to overwork and a soulless modern scientism. Henry Miller's travels across the US is a search for art and beauty as outlets for souls that are desiccated and barren. There is a lot to think about in these pages, these are times of change in the US but none of it seems to be change for the better. But maybe we can commandeer the frustration. A quick word too about home. Of course Miller loved Europe. He loved Paris and Dublin the same way folks from Paris and Dublin love LA or New York. When you are not from somewhere, when you have no stake in the success or failure of that place, and when you don't speak the language or understand enough of the story of that place to be able to make common cause, you are able to see it from the vantage point of a disinterested observer. And because you cannot either succeed or fail in that place, because you are not from there and will not be judged by the locals, it is safe. I have felt this in Europe too, it is the safety of tourism, the safety of a museum continent that looks only to the past. It is naive. But I get it.
