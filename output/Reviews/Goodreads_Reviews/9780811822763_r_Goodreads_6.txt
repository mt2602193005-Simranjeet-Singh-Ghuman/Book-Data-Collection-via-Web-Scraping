This book is… misleading. I appreciate it for what it was probably meant to be: a quick and easy introduction to Irish mythological entities. However, the entire book seems fixated on the ways in which each creature is “evil” or in some way out to get you. Don’t get me wrong—the fae are not good. They do require a great deal of caution and respect. But I’m pretty sure they aren’t all trying to kill you all the time. I’d love to find a more nuanced view that explores both the dangers and the beauties of Ireland’s mythology.
