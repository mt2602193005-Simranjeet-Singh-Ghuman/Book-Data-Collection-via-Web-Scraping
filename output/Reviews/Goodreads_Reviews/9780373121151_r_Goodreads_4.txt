What spoilt it for me, is the fact that the woman has a daughter from another man. I don’t like it when either the man or the woman (or both) already have children. I like the women in the Harlequin romances to be virginal. I know that is not realistic in this day and age, but I don’t read Harlequin because I want to read realistic things. I want the dream. And I also didn’t feel any chemistry between the man and woman.
