Quick-look at the Book Detroit's very own Archbishop is about to become a Cardinal. In Rome. Koesler and his posse along with the two competing journalists all head there to witness the occasion. And then Cardinals start dropping off like flies! Thoughts on this Series This is a nice enough cozy mystery series. The central character is Father Koesler who by no actions of his own--mostly--keeps tripping over murdered people and getting roped into police investigations. While the characterization and motivation behind each suspect are well-described, there can be often too many POVs or too many details that detract from the action. That said, the author repeatedly hits you over the head with the same details about priesthood and religion. If you can bear with that, and are okay with the protagonist arriving at the euphoria! moment while in the bathtub or car or...you get what I mean...read on. My advice? Space em out.
