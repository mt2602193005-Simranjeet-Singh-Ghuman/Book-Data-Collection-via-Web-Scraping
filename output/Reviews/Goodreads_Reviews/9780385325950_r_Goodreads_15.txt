A door near here is a realistic fiction book not for the faint of the mind. I would say that this book would make you mad/angry/upset all in the time span of a chapter or two. I would say over all the book sounds a little off to me. i found myself feeling bad for her situation but also she comes across naggy and is almost opposed to doing anything to fix her situation. Why sit here and do all of this just fix the problems at hand and keep moving on with life. I've finished the book but this was one that i had to keep coming back to just because of the constant annoying fact that she can fix the problem but doesn't. The book is okay over all but i will not be re-reading it. I will recommend the book if there's nothing else to read.
