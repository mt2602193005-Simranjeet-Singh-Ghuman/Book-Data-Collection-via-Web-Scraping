I really liked this book! So far, there hasn't been a book by this author that I haven't liked, and I intend to read them all! I liked both the H and h in this story. While I don't agree with the H keeping some important details of his past from the h, I understand why he did it. As for the h, even when she h made a wrong decision (one that so many h's make), it was for the right reason, as she thought she was doing what was best for the H, under the circumstances. Both the OM and OW are the villains in this story, and I'm glad they both get what they deserve. There's also a secondary story about an interracial couple which would have been a good separate book. I recommend this one!
