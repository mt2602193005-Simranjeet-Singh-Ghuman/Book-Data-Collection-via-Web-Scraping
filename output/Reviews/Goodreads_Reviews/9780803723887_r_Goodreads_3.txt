Appropriate grade level(s):kindergarten, first and second grade. Summary: This book is about a little girl in kindergarten named Lottie. In her dream one night she was a princess and wanted to wear her princess dress to school. Her mother was not happy with her that morning, but they both ended up wearing special dresses for a perfectly special day. Review: The descriptions of how Lottie’s mother talks can be modeled for children while being read, bringing life to the story. Her mother’s tantrum reminds children that adults get frustrated and just like children need to apologize for their behavior too.
