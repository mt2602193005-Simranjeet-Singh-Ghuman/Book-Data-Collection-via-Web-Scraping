This book felt so authentically English, that I was surprised to find that the author is American and never lived in Britain. It's a simple tale and much more could have been done with it, but its value lies more in the vocabulary and ambiance, than the plot and characters. Worthy having on the shelf, but not a favorite. Also, some of the covers of certain editions look rather ridiculous, more farcical than anything, so don't let those deceive you into thinking this is anything other than a genuine English story about battles and knights. Newbery Medal winner in 1950.
