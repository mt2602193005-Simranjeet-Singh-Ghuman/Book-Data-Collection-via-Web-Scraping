For Anyone that is Married or Single This was a very hard book to read. It gives you insights to what the different types of affairs are, how it has affected some, how one can recover. So many good stories in this book that anyone can relate to. I recommend this book to everyone that wants to know what can happen to one's relationship. Keep an open mind.
