What a bizarre read. As a boy I was fascinated by this book; a strange, disturbing cover that would be on my Dad's bookshelf; a book he always told be I could read when I was older due to 'adult themes'. He certainly wasn't wrong. The Throwback was a hilarious and vivid satire on everything from sexual repression to economic mismanagement. Sharpe would occasionally fire off a salvo of explicit description to be followed by a light, matter-of-fact account of a place, person, or thought. His literary style picks the reader up and carries them through this scathing, hilarious satire, and it was brilliant. As a resident of Northumberland I found the book particularly funny. Despite being written in the 1970s, a context to which many references are owed in the book, I found the read to be startlingly well-observed.
