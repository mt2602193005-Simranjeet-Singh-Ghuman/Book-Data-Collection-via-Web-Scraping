I got up to the "How to Use Your Library" and I gave up. There is nothing earthshattering about this book. There is nothing you will learn that will truly help you than what teachers have already advised in class. The tips are extremely obvious and unhelpful. For example, the author rambles on about using the Dewey decimal system to find books in a library. While I agree this can be informative, is it really nescessary? Anyways, for me, I found the book extremely boring to read. I would not recommend this book to anyone.
