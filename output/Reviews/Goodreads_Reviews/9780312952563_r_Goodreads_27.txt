Bernard Taylor's signature writing style is on display. On a technical level, it is very well written. There is something about his writing that I enjoy, even when the plot would not normally grab my attention. Mother's Boys, however, has been my least favorite experience with Taylor's writing. It's a very disturbing and bleak story. If you have ever read his work before that is nothing new. However the conclusion felt unsatisfying. It didn't justify slogging through all the tragedy. This wicked tale could potentially work better as a short story. Sometimes a bitter experience made short makes it more palatable.
