Crumley's third and final "Milo" tale. Or more like a, booze and coked up road trip from hell. James Crumley had inspired the likes of, Connelly, Lehane and Pelecanos, with his hard-boiled crime stories. Reading one of his Milo or Sughrue cases....gives the reader feeling like they have the hangover. I like this comment on one of his Sughrue novels, "The Right Madness" - "More madness from the king of hard-boiled crime: 'Crumley writes like an angel on speed ... That pretty much sums up any of his novels.
