Wonder is the first emotion. Wonder at the world. We wonder at the faces around us. We wonder at the sounds they make. As we grow, we wonder at our increasing size. We wonder the next day when we are made to feel small. Tears make us wonder. How many more will come? Will they ever stop? Will I go down with them and not come up? Words are a wonder, wonderful as bees. When they sting we are hurt. When they give honey we call it love. Wonder is everywhere that there is life. The message of this book is simple. Look up.
