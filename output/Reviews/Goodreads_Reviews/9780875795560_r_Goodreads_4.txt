I've read this book several times. It's two separate true stories, one about a childless couple who longs for a child and decides to adopt. The second story is about an unwed mother who decides to place her baby for adoption. It's just a great story, not only for those who have dealt with adoption or infertility (which I have experience with both!!) but it's great for everyone because it can be eye-opening if you haven't dealt with either of those scenarios. Great book.
