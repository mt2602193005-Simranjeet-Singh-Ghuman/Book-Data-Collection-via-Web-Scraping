This one fascinated me. It starts with very disconnected stories: Emma's "perfect husband" disappears, and a stock boy gets bitter with a young woman who scorns him. Karl is on leave because of his father's death, so he decides to search for the missing husband as a way to keep himself busy. The missing husband case was weird, and there was a lot of tension in that story, while the wife played around with the husband's old gun. I thought it was building up to a very different conclusion... and YET... the way it worked out brought together the two stories in a shocking way.
