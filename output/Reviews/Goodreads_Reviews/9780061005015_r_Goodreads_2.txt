I am embarrassed to say that I thoroughly enjoyed this book. It was a straightforward battle of the sexes romance reminiscent of 1930s Hollywood when sex in film was taboo. The plot was a series of absurd scenarios, mainly mildly comic in which the heroine Janey is kidnapped by Phil, the archaeologist hero, at the bequest of her Dad to scare her into marrying him. Needless to say the plot goes wrong and Janey gets some measure of revenge before, of course their love for each other results in a happy ending. Does the book have any merit? Probably not but if you happen to like this kind of story, well - you'll enjoy this. I certainly did!
