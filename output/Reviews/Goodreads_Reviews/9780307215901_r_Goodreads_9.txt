I would like to thank myself for reading and loving this series as a child (and also my mother for purchasing all those oval paperbacks), because otherwise I would not be enjoying this trip down memory lane, forty-odd years after first picking up Trixie Belden and the Mystery of the Queen's Necklace . This one wasn't exactly a trip down memory lane though - there are four or five in the series that I'd never owned and apparently never bothered to get from the library as a child. This was one of those but it still felt as familiar as the house I grew up in. Also, if we were to track Trixie's age based on the season in which each book takes place, I think she'd be roughly 75 by the end of the series :-)
