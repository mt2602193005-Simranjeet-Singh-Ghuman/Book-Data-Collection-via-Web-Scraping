This is not the most practical cookbook I own, and I think most people will be hard-pressed to try all of the recipes. It's also not simple or designed for the casual home cook - some of the ingredients may be hard to get and some of the techniques will be time-consuming and/or difficult. All of that said, the recipes I have tried have been universally excellent and I believe there are several recipes that all on their own justify owning the book. I don't look at it as a staple French cookbook (others, including Julia Child, are better for that), but it's a great book for cooks who want to stretch a bit and try some high-end recipes.
