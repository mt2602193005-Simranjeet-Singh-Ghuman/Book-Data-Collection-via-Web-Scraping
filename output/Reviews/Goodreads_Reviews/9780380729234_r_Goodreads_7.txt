Playing Off the Rail: A Pool Hustler's Journey by David McCumber is about the writer's travels and gambling trips. He financially sponsors a good pool player. They go on road/rail trips to play and earn money. It takes them a lot of time and effort to set up games that seem spontaneous to hustle their marks.
