"A gripping courtroom thriller, seen through the eyes of the accused." Not what I would call a thriller. However, I found the story had me from the start. There are many stories about someone who has amnesia. Most of them do not work. This one does. You will believe she really doesn't remember anything that happened that morning, and why she can't remember. AND why she shouldn't want to remember. "The eyes of the accused." Yes, Clarrie had me inside her head as she weighed the consequences of her actions. Her actions? No, she couldn't have done it; unless she did.
