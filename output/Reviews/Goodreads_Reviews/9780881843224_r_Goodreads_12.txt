I haven't read this genre for a long time. It is an insightful look at Weimar-era Berlin decadence, written and originally published between the two World Wars. The writing is light but with clever allegories and swipes, with just enough quick description of the city to add jaded colour. The chapters can be read as stand alone, but form a continuous story that flows smoothly yet with plenty of twists to maintain curiosity. The eroticism is continuous and lovely, plenty of silk stockings and sweet and non-gratuitous S&M that could offend no-one. An easy read, almost certainly guaranteed to keep a smile on your face.
