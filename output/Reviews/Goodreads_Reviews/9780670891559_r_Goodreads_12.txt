I feel like a lot of work went into this book and I did enjoy it, but as chef books go, it's not my favorite, which is likely due to the book really being three separate stories, though Ruhlman does tie them together in the end. The many details and terms can also get tedious, but overall, this is a three-part book about a test, a successful restaurant, and a successful chef, culminating in a philosophical discussion about what makes a chef truly great and what defines success. I do love the perspective, the details, the descriptions of the foods.
