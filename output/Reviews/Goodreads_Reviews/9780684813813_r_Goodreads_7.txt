This is the last book in the Karl Ahlberg series, which is set in Sechelt and Gibsons on the Sunshine Coast of B.C. Once I started this series, it was hard to stop. The main characters are RCMP Karl Ahlberg, and Cassandra, the town librarian, who come to life and make us care about their insecurities, disappointments, and dreams. These mysteries also delve into the mind and soul of murderers, often people with uncontrollable obsessions. The Sunshine Coast is a beautiful, yet sometimes bleak backdrop against which terror and bliss, despair and hope, and murder and justice play out.
