I already have book 2 in my shopping cart. Countdown is an end of the world story in the vein of Left Behind, but without the Christian focus. The world has essentially ended. Most of the population has died, melted into puddles of black slime and their clothes. All that is left are teenagers, and most of them will wish they had died early on. January is the introduction so most of this book is character set up and establishing the world the series will inhabit. Their are hints as to some of the larger themes at play, but really this is all about getting to know our heroes and villains. I feel like this book established everyone well, and set a good idea for what is to come. The story is surprisingly dark, and there is plenty of death to go around. Tension is also high, and the small page count keeps the pace moving at a rapid pace. The story is serialized, so understand that this book ends on a cliffhanger. An effective one, I might add. Looking forward to seeing what February has in store for this group of kids. Highly recommended.
