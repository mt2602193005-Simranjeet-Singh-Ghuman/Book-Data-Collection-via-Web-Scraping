This book was interesting as we didn’t get any babysitting really apart from the art lessons. I liked how it dealt with grief and how different people react differently. I thought it would be good for kids to read that. Also the family starting to “move on” regarding Mimi’s stuff. If I ever meet Ann M Martin, though, I’d love to ask where the name Mimi comes from. Im married to a Japanese man and live in Japan. Mimi means ear in Japanese, though it could be a shortened form of Michiko, Emiko, etc But Japanese is so big on using terms for family that I can’t imagine Mimi wouldn’t be “bachan.” Also I’m curious how there wouldn’t be more Japanese language in the family, especially when Mimi was sick. And considering Mimi moved to the US at 32 and had children and grandchildren there, I doubt she hadn’t had pizza before going to that restaurant with Kristy and Maryanne.
