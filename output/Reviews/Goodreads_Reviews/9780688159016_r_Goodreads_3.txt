I am a big James Purdy fan and this book, in his usual abstruse style did not disappoint. It is full of mysterious characters with mysterious purposes and never really answered the question of what happened to Gertrude but it is heck of a literary ride through underground Chicago. I wonder if this Gertrude was based on Gertrude Abercrombie, the painter, but the book never fully really answered the questions although some characteristics of the fictional Gertrude were similar to the real one. There is a hallucinatory scene where Gertrude does reappear to her mother, Carrie but the book never revisits it. Carrie herself, is a bit a cypher but I am sure that was the author's intent.
