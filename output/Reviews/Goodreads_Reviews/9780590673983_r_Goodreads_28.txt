(LL) This book does a decent job tackling: merging families and sibling fights. The main issue with the book that I had to take a star off for was the fact the Arnold twins shared a room when there were TWO empty rooms in their house?? Come on, there is no way a parent would have their kids share a room and have two rooms be empty. I know the mother was obsessed with matching, but two matching rooms would have made more sense in that context, so either way it is so silly. A four bedroom house but only two bedrooms are occupied? I call bullshit, sorry.
