About half way through the book the story took a dive into the ridiculous (ordinary Mom saves two children out on a precarious ledge in a quarry by climbing through dark holes with dead snakes to get to a narrow bridge). What? It also tosses in social/political statements about pro-abortion versus anti-abortion. It's a mess with a disappointing ending.
