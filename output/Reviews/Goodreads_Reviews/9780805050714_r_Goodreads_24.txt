Reagan era Faulkner. The poet of a dying world, writing with fervor, contempt, and deep rooted understanding. And maybe most importantly, love A beautiful triptych of a book written in typical SE fashion: characters appear and die and reappear as apparitions or shades, American dreams and nightmares personified into visages of avarice and seduction and endless self searching. Native American connection rended to LA hyper capitalism, the diaspora and mirth of America 1 and America 2. “He was born, after all, in a country that forgotten the dream of which it was born: rare was that, once having forgotten it, he had come to remember it again” 4.1/5 I prefer Arc D’x and Tours of the Black Clock, which probably lowers my score for this one out of relativity.
