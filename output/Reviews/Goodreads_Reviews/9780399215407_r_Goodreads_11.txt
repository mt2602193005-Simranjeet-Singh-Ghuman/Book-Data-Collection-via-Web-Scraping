Format/Length: Picture book/Anthology of Poems; 128 pages Review: This is a great collection of poems. There are 86 poems of various topics in different forms. The anthology includes poems that are considered classics, as well as poems that are playful and fun. I love the variety this anthology provides its reader. This anthology is beautifully illustrated by Tomie DePaola, which I feel the beauty lies in the simplicity of the illustrations. I feel that this anthology would be a great addition to the curriculum in grades P-3 to introduce poetry to younger children through read-alouds of various poems within this collection.
