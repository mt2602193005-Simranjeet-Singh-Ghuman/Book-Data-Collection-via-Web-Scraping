Another classic doll horror story from, in my opinion, the Queen of the genre. Ruby's books are the definition of "cozy horror" to me and I mean that as a compliment. Her stories have such a timeless feel to them despite being set when she wrote them (with flashbacks to the early 1900s and even 1800s depending on the book). I will say, that with this story, you will find yourself wanting to scream at the stupid decisions made by some of the characters. But characters making dumb choices is a trademark of the horror genre lol.
