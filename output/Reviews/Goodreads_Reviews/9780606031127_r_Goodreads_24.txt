Dune Messiah is a decent but not outstanding continuation of Frank Herbert’s famous Dune. The story follows Paul Atreides as Emperor, struggling with political intrigue, conspiracies, and the heavy weight of being seen as a messiah. While the book expands the universe and explores deep themes like religion, power, and destiny, it often feels slower and less exciting than the first novel. The characters are interesting, but the plot can be confusing and at times underwhelming. Overall, it’s a thoughtful sequel, but not as memorable or gripping as Dune.
