What a strange little book. The concept is really great; it's sort of an encyclopedia of things that can go wrong with your cooking and how to fix them. In practice, though, I can only see myself using maybe 1/8 of the tips in this book. If you cook with whole foods on a regular basis, most of these tips are useless: I am not about to start keeping canned hollandaise sauce and vanilla pudding mix on hand so that I can smother ruined dinners in them. Most of the problems with vegetables addressed in this book arise from using frozen veggies rather than fresh--another problem I don't have to worry about. Surprisingly, there is very little information on fixing ruined bake goods, which I feel the book could have used. However, there are some good tips for rescuing ruined cookware, getting stains out, etc. And the authors have a great sense of humor, which makes it a pleasant enough read. This could perhaps make a nice gift for a newlywed couple who are just learning to cook and have no problem with prepared ingredients. Overall, though, this is not a book I will be adding to my collection.
