I read this book many years ago. Probably well over 15 and it stuck with me. A few years ago I was at work and I typed in to a search engine, San fransico, Emma,star and ranch and I found my book. It was one of my favorite sweeping "epic" stories I ever read. It got me from the first page and held me to the end. I have read this book probably 10 times in my lifetime,I was twenty the first time and when I reread it last year I was afraid I would have outgrown it but I hadnt. Its just a good read. Very satisfying . Im not great at writing reviews but if you like books that span many years and drama,action,heroes and villians. This is the book for you.
