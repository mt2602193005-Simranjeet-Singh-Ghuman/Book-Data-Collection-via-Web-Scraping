I have yet to read a Susan Mallery book that I didn't at least like and what better time to read a book than at Christmastime. The only problem is it wasn't near the holiday when I read it. That didn't make a difference but I generally like to save my holiday books for the holiday it was set in. Molly and Dylan are a cute couple. Who doesn't love it when a couple young, impressionable 14 year old kids get a crush. One thing that I believe is true for women, is that we never forget a promise! After stumbling across something one day when she is packing, Molly remembers such a promise that Dylan made to her years earlier. For me that's where the fun began.
