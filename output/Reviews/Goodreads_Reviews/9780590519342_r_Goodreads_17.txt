The Mark of the Crown by Jude Watson . This is the fourth book in the Star Wars - Jedi Apprentice middle grade series. This story continues the deepening relationship between Jedi Master Qui-Gon and Padawan Obi-Wan. The two of them are sent to a planet which is holding elections for the first time; they are sent to observe. But not everyone on the planet is interested in democracy... Another solid story. I gave it 4 stars.
