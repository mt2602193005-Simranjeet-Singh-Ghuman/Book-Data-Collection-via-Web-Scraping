Unlike Hilari Bell's other novels, this one took me a while to get into. I thought the subject matter was excellent, but Ahvren's problems were just not as exciting as I anticipated. Ahvren's detective work is prolonged and just plain slow. At times, I really wanted to skip to the end and just read the conclusion. (Ok, I might have done that).
