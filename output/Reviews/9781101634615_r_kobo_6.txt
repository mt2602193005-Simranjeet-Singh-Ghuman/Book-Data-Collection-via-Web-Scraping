I can totally understand why this book is getting a lot of hype, but it is not a book I will be recommending to anyone I know. The way it jumps from character to character overlooks the most compelling stories to focus on the heads of the household. There is perspective missing. And there are frequent instances of really obvious cliff hangers in the middle chapters. It felt like a lot of cheap "suspense" tricks were being used to keep me reading. I mean, they worked, but it made me feel real cheap.
