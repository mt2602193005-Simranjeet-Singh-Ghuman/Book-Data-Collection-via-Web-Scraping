Reviewer: reluctant reviewer I bought this book based on the two 5-star reviews available then. I wish I hadn't. This wasn't a bad book by any means but, as much as I appreciated the subject (traumatized war hero) and as competently as it was written, it also seemed both confused and flat at various times. The emotions of both H&h swung back and forth between black and light, hot and cold, too often for me; the lack of communication between them became uncomfortable even beyond the angst of the story; the dramatic conclusion seemed to be too much of a convenient story device; and the epilogue just seemed silly considering all that had gone on before; etc. Even without the 5-star reviews to balance, I don't think I would have given this book more than three stars. Read less
