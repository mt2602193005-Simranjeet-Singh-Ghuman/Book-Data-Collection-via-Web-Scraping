Reviewer: Dylan128 This is the second book that I have read in this series and it was as good as the first that one I read. This book is a story of the redemption and the strength of the human spirit. The author created a great story that makes you feel for the characters struggles and triumphs. The author vividly describes the way life could be in the gold mining towns of the west. This is a great story that I would recommend very highly. Read less
