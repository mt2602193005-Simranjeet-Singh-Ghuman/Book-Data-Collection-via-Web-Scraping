Reviewer: Thomas Lawrence "The Lord ofthe Rings and Philosophy: One Book to Rule Them All" by Gregory Bassham is a surprising survey of philosophical concepts. Metaphysics, Epistimology and especially Ethics; based on and using characterizations, scenarios, issues and examples from the Lord of the Rings. It allows a refesher of some of the philosophic concepts previously learned or for new students of Philosophy an enjoyable juxtoposition of those concepts in a great piece of literature. Read less
