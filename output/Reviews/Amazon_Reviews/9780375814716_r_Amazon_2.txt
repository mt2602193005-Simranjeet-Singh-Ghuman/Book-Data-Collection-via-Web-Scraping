Reviewer: Sam The Tortall series are a childhood favourite of mine and I remember borrowing the Protector of the Small quartet from the library a long time ago! Brilliant finale to the quartet and with interesting author notes at the end relating the situation in the books with a real world scenario. The world and character building is so immersive - I immediately had to pick up my copy of Trickster's Choice after as I couldn't bear to leave Tortall yet! Read less
