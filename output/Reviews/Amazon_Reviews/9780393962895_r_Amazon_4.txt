Reviewer: Mark Twain A huge tome. Very thin (but remarkably resilient) paper, and a *lot* of pages. A colossal collection of literature from Anglo-Saxon, through Middle English, the Renaissance, the Enlightenment, the Romantics, Victorians, and modern writers. Too many to list (and I have written many reviews tonight. Maybe I'll expand on this one later). The quintessential and comprehensive sampling of every school of thought, every era, in poetry, drama, and narration both long and short, comprising most English writers of note. Vital reading for students of English, and of Literature. Read less
