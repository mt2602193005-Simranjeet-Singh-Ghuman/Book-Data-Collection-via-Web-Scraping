Reviewer: iYvonne What an absolutely beautiful book! You know, books like these are for anyone and everyone. Not just a 'Black' person book. It is enlightening. It is a credit to all of us when any individual achieves: male or female, Black or White. Take the story of Clara McBride Hale. She raised 40 children and all of them went to college and graduated. She then decided to retire, but lo and behold, the needy babies kept coming, so she raised another 22. In total, she has taken care of over 600 addicted babies!!!! Who does that????? She is an ordinary lady, rather frail looking, no money lady; you would pass her on the street without a glance. But, my oh my! What she did! So humble she is. Now these are the kind of stories our children and adults need to hear. She does not consider herself a hero; just a person who love children. A must for every library ( public or private).
