Reviewer: Gwen This was a very mysterious book. I can't say I don't blame Elizabeth for making the decision to take her nephew Joseph to meet his father's family, because obviously she could not provide for him and her sister would have wanted her to.But, once I was introduced to the craziness that was the family I would have immediately devised an escape plan though. Everyone was a suspect on my list,so it was hard to narrow it down. Read less
