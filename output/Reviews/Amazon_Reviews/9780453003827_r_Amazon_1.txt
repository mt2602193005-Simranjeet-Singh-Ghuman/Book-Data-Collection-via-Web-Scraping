Reviewer: william newmoon What can I say about Erica Jong, her writing this book, and my awe of her accomplishment? 'Her', is the key. So far I have read three of her books: Fear Of Flying (1973), How To Save Your Own Life (1977). Witches (1981); with FANNY she's outdid 'herself'! It's a period piece, early 1700's. It's a 'her' book, female, written for females, but readable by enlightened males. There's a lot of action, the story moves quickly, you 'feel' for the characters, it has poetry and philosophy, movements of tragedy and comedy; yes, best of all, humor - it's a good book. I recommend this book to anyone who can appreciate the ups and downs of the female of our species. Well done Erica, all hail FANNY! Read less
