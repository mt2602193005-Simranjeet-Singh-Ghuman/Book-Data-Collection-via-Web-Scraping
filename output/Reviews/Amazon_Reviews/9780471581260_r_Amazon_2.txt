Reviewer: SkepticalGuy He talks a big talk in the begining of the book as of he were about to impart so secret knowledge to the world.In truth the book is short an doesn't reveal anything that wouldn't be encountered in a cognitive psychology class the graduate level. My biggest problem with the book was the very last page where he decides to lambaste evolutionary thought with great fervor. Why? His book is on cognitive biases. Whe would you choose to end a book the that with a hughe dig at all who look at human behavior as the result of millions of years of evolution? I am sorry I wasted my money. The only thing you really take away from the book is that humans aren't very good at intuitive probabilities. But, anyone who has taken a graduate statistics class already knows this. Very poor. Read less
