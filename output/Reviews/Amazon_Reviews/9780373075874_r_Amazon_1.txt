Reviewer: Amazon Customer VKP This is the first time I have read a book and then promptly reread it. It was that good, I didn't want it to end. The characters were extremely vivid and quite plausible. Yet the author writes in such a way that you can use your own imagination to fill in areas of the story. I was totally wrapped up in the plot, characters and scenery. Steamy in places, but definitely not porn. If I can't quit thinking about a book when I am at work, driving, shopping....it's a good book and yes I couldn't quit thinking about Breaking the Rules. Did I also say... I HATE ROMANCE NOVELS....sigh...for this one I'll make an exception! Read less
