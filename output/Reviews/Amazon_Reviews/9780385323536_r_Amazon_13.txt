Reviewer: manchester maz I love this story. It is great for a relaxing holiday read and to be honest it's not what I would usually choose but someone bought this for me years ago and now I have bought it as a Christmas present for someone else. It starts off a bit slow but it soon picks up and you can't put it down. I like the way it has a story within a story. It is basically about a couple who are splitting up after being married for years and then it goes on to tell the story of another couple in another time (hence the ghost) and of course goes on to a happy ending. I would recommend this book to anyone who wants a good story and easy reading - I'm sure you will like it. Read less
