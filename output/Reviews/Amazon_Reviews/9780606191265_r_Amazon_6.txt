Reviewer: Deepak The notebook, I had watched the movie before I read this book. I actually wanted to feel the story even more when I watched it so ordered the book from Amazon and I've to say that Amazon is doing a great job as far as the delivery is concerned. Coming to the book, it is not exactly as same as the movie but is equally beautiful, I must say. The author engages you completely into the story and makes you feel every bit of it. The story is about a man Noah who once sitting old in time reads a notebook to his lady(Allie) who has lost her memory due to a mental disorder and ageing. He takes her back into the time and reminds her of each and every moment they spent together. The complications in their relationship at one time seemed to be endless but they didn't give away their love for minor challenges in life. "Nicholas Sparks" the author has the ability to drown the reader into the emotions of his characters and he does this beautifully. The story is very simple yet it intoxicates the feeling of enritchment in the reader. I would recommend this book to every reader who likes to be fascinated. Read less
