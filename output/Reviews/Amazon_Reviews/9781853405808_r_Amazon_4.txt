Reviewer: Alison Howell Having read and enjoyed Fanny's previous novels, I had high expectations of her latest novel, With a Friend Like You, and I was not disappointed. In this book, she touches on many contemporary themes, amd I was quickly swept into the lives of all the brilliantly drawn characters. As a retired teacher, I could sympathise with Megan in particular, but I felt that all the other characters were very realistically drawn. Fanny has a knack of observing even the smallest detail, and she has no trouble delving into the world of the self-centred teenager. Her story has an interesting plot, and it is eminently readable - probably the best contemporary novel I have ever read. What a refreshing change to read about normal families, leading normal lives! Fanny writes in a very intelligent way, using apt language for each character, in a seemingly effortless way. Her use of vocabulary is clever without being pretentious. In short, I was sorry the book had to come to an end. Looking forward to her next one already! Read less
