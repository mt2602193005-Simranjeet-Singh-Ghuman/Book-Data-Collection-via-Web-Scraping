Reviewer: talmine Natural curiosity drove me to download this book onto my Kindle cloud reader. Apparently there was an earlier edition in 1997 so there seems to be a demand for this kind of work. The contents amount to a series of documented case studies (I don't know how many: 50 say?) selected no doubt for their variety. The title does say 'fantasies' but these are invariably accompanied by actual experiences. Was the book interesting? Yes .... but not enough to reread any of it. To some extent the book reminded me of the Kinsey report(s) which were published in the 50's Read less
