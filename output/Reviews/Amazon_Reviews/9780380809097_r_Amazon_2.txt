Reviewer: Animal Lover This emotional superb book is very difficult for me to summarize - there is so very much happening. It is the story of two wounded souls - one because of love and the other betrayal - and it encompasses the prior 3 books explaining the 'whys'. Lucien, the Duke of Blackheath, grew up at the age of 10, losing both his beloved parents within days. He vowed he would protect and insure the happiness of his siblings, 3 brothers and his infant sister. He did so by setting into motion situations he controlled without their knowledge. They thought him arrogant and playing God, still loved him, but never understanding. He meets Eva, an American cousin of his sister-in-law married to his brother Andrew. She is a widow, the Comtesee de la Mouriere, who is trying to attain the help of France in the American Independence War. She is beautiful, intelligent and has a contempt for men. It is a journey of knowing and understanding oneself, trusting and believing. It is a tragic story and one of deep love and commitment, overcoming obstacles and adversities and finally seeing the truth of your heart and soul. Lucien and Eva find what they were looking for but believed did not exist for them. In the end, they both realize how blessed they are for finding life's greatest gift. It is a very moving, beautiful story to stir your heart. The very best of this marvelous series! Read less
