Reviewer: TDMac I LOVE the horror genre and short stories are even better. This book, of what I've read so far, has engaging stories that at times are VERY short. I wouldn't say it's my favorite of the genre, but for the price of one penny, it was certainly work a look. I read it on vacations and during down times. That's where having short stories is best. You can finish one or ten in a sitting and still have more waiting on you for next time. If you like odd and/or horror stories, this is one to check out. Read less
