Reviewer: Romantic at Heart Great story. You have 3 different plots going on. Ondine is falsely accused of treason and is on the run. Warwick finds Ondine at the gallows. She will die for pouching. Warwick marries her in hopes that by bringing home a wife he can draw out his dead wife's killer. Ondine agrees to the marriage hoping to escape so she can clear her father's and her name. When she can't escape Warwick she agrees to help him and then she can go her own way to finish what she needs to do. You also have another plot going on as two people who want different things try to accomplish their goals against Ondine and Warwick. Love the characters. Recommend reading this book, especially if you like reading her other books like Lie Down in Roses, Knight of Fire, Golden Surrender series, or any of her other romance books under Heather Graham like Bride of Wind or either of her civil war series. Read less
