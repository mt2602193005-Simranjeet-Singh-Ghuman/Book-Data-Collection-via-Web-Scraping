Reviewer: Phineas T. I bought this copy for a fourth grade student I'm currently tutoring. I'm retired now, but when I taught, I read this book to my third and fifth grade students. I would use voices and expression to differentiate the wonderful characters Mr. Senn created. This book sparks the imagination in ways no other book, with which I'm familiar, has done. My current student is interested in science and has a very active mind. But, his reading skills, and especially his motivation, are lacking. He prefers nonfiction, which is okay, but I really feel he could benefit from a strong dose of fiction as well. There is much to be learned about life and human interactions in fictional works. I'm hopeful I can get the boy to appreciate fiction by using this book. I definitely feel "The Double Disappearance..." is a highly underrated and under-respected piece of imaginative fiction. Read less
