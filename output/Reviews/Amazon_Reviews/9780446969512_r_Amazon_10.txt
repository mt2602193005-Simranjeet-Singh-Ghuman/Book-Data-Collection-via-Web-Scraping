Reviewer: J Bannion Elizabeth Roffe finds herself heading a colossal pharmaceutical empire, following her father's death in a climbing accident. She is young, attractive and immensely rich, the whole world should be at her feet. But things are never as they seem, Elizabeth is in mortal danger. A traitor from inside the firm has been systematically sabotaging the company at every turn, his sole intention to force the company to go public. The mysterious adversary will do anything to achieve his ends, even murder. Soon Elizabeth discovers that her father may have been murdered and that she could well be next on the list. But who can she trust, who can she turn to? An amazing rollercoaster of romance, mystery and intrigue where thee reader is immersed into a well designed, classic mystery storyline. Highly addictive. Read it now. They don't write them like this anymore. Read less
