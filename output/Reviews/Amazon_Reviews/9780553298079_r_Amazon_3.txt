Reviewer: D.L.R Jake is a wanted man. Five thousand alive and three dead. He is accused of murder, robbery, and rape. He had a difficult childhood his mother and brother were killed by his father. He kills his father when he sees him attacking a young girl that Jake thinks he loves. There starts his hard life. Miranda lost her husband in the civil war. Her brother runs away, her mother died years back and raiders killed her father. She decides she is going to go find her brother but makes a trip into town to visit her friends and buy some supplies to get ready for her trip. While she is shopping Jake comes in the store and she noticed him. The store owner tells him about the widow who has just lost her father and in walks a bounty hunter cracks Jake in the head and Jake shoots him in self defence. Miranda pulls her gun out during the conflict and ends up shooting Jake, he runs from the store and flees. When she returns home she finds Jake laying in her bed unconscious and bleeding to death. She pulls the bullet and stitches him up, cleans him and some how asks him to take her to her brother after a few weeks of healing. He of course refuses and after leaving he gets to thinking about the woman who shot him and decides to go help her and not a minute to soon because when he finds her she has been left for dead in a trading post. Honestly I’m so impressed with this book. I enjoyed reading it. I would love to read more because it’s so well written and an honest book. Such emotional pain Jake has gone through in his life you find yourself hoping that he gets through everything that happens. You won’t be disappointed with this book. Read less
