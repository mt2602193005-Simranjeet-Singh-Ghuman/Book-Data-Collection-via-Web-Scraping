Reviewer: Neha Jhangta Hyperion is one of those rare science fiction novels that feels both epic and deeply human. I loved the structure. Different characters sharing their stories as they travel toward the mysterious Shrike and each tale adds another layer to the world. The stories that really stayed with me were the first one about Father Hoyt and the one about Sol Weintraub and his daughter Rachel. Both are emotional, haunting, and give the book real heart. Dan Simmons balances huge ideas with quiet, personal moments in a way that’s hard to forget. A powerful, beautifully written sci-fi novel that completely deserves its reputation. Read less
