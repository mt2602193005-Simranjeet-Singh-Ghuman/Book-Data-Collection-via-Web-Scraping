Reviewer: Darrell Henry I am a great Fan of L. M. Montgomery. I do not read the Anne stories. I listen to them. One of my best friends is a professional dramatist. She reads and records stories. She has recorded all of the Anne series. She interprets all the characters in the stories. She makes it as real as life itself. Every character comes to life. As strong as the story is, I can see L. M. Montgomery all though the story. She is the mother of all her characters. She has the insight to develop each character so they are totally believable, and they are very colorful. She develops new characters in every book, but brings back some of the great characters in the previous books. There are so many wonderful moments in her books, I will listen to them again and again. Read less
