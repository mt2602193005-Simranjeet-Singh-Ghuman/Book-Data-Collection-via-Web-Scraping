Reviewer: Gadget Woman This is an okay book for beginners who want a simple overview of Generative AI tools like ChatGPT and Midjourney. It’s very visual, easy to flip through, and works well as a coffee-table conversation starter. Pros: - Beginner-friendly with clear visuals and simple explanations - Good overview of AI tools for content creation and productivity - Nice design—works well as a desk or coffee-table reference Cons: - More expensive than similar AI starter books - Content is basic for anyone already familiar with AI tools Overall, it’s a solid reference if you’re new to generative AI and want something simple, visual, and easy to skim.
