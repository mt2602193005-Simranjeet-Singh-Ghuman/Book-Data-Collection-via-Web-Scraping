Reviewer: Glenn Laycock I remembered this cover on the original book we got as kids .. so 40-some years ago. The drawing and so on were VERY different than the Disney movie which is maybe why the movie was sort of a disappointment for me .. I remember finding the first 1/3 of the movie terrific and then it being a yawn in comparison to the book. It is a large and impressive book .. wonderful for mother and son (or daughter) to share at bed time or on the couch. I'm not a novel reader .. but books like this I can SEE the fonts and I still like drawings and so on. I would recommend it .. the value is terrific, and it could almost be a hand-me-down book. Read less
