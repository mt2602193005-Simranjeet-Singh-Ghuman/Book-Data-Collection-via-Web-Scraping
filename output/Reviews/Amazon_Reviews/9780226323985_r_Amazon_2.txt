Reviewer: Ramsey Tupper - Raven's Foot This book has given me so much information to further research and quantify as regards the history of Babylon, the origins of the 'big three' religions and the Egyptian take on trinity worship. The stories presented are fragmented to say the least, and the interpretations provided give enough to go on to draw your own conclusions about the overlaps in the stories with our modern day explanations. Referencing the deluge in ancient accounts as the common thread with our ancient recorded history shows that our religious history may not be so exclusive at all. Most of the stories are constructed from the Babylonian and Assyrian tablets that have survived, and I would recommend this book to anyone with a vague interest in hocus pocus mumbo jumbo modern day hoodwinkery religions. It is also printed in a typeface like on an old typewriter and really feels like you are perusing an old document. Well presented, well written and and good points raised. A great book. Read less
