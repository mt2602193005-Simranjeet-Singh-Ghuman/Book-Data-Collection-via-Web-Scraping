Reviewer: Jean-Claude Well written as one would expect from such a classical author. A true to life story and a compelling easy read. Even though sheltered from needs in terms of parents' love and money, one bad decision can change all of that overnight and shatter one's future. The story highlights what some women have to endure whilst looking for happiness, including the abuse of a mother's unconditional love for her child. Happy ending. Quote " La vie, ca n'est jamais si bon ni si mauvais qu'on croit". Great value at no cost from Amazon. Read less
