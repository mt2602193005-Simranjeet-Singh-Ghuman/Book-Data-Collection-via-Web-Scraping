Reviewer: Richard and Margaret Treadgold The theme of a man thrown back in time and his dilemma in choosing whether or not to significantly change history is fairly common, and always fascinating. But here it is kept interesting by good plotting and characterisation of the main protagonists. The parallel story of the present-day investigation of his disappearance is well-written with its own interesting characters. And the device by which the investigator - and by inference the world at large - are oblivious to the changes wrought by the "intruder" in the past is well presented as we the readers are almost inadvertently told of them. Read less
