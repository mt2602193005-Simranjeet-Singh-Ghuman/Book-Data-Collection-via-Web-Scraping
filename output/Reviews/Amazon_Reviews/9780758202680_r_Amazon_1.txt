Reviewer: L. Murphy Found this to be a bit, well, samey. The stories were good, but didn't really grab my attention, to the extent that I can't remember them - one was about a secret, magical society ruled by women - which was actually quite steamy, now that I think about it. The other one that I remember was the last one, by Robin Schone, which was the best of the lot for me - made my heart ache - was very sad, but also very sexy. I was going to give this 3 stars, but for the last story. I guess it was a good introduction - hadn't read any Robin Schone before, but am going to have a look out for her name when I'm next browsing. Read less
