Reviewer: Harrikins I bought a used copy of this on the recommendation of a speaker at a Master Gardener program. He spoke about lots of "pest control" issues, including weeds, etc. He raved about this book, saying it was the best pest control book he had ever seen. When I got it, it was entirely about animal pests (including insects, spiders, etc.) with nothing about plant "pests". While the advice about dealing with mice, aphids, etc. seems sound, I was hoping for a more comprehensive tome. Read less
