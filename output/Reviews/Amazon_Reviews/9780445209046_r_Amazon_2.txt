Reviewer: K. Norris I read this originally years and years ago. I loved every moment of it. It was funny, weird, terrifying... I dunno... every character is fascinating. I will never forget Burnfingers Begay.... Very well done. It's a good book and a satisfying read. Even after the fifth time. Side note: I've worked in the industry, and I thought this could make a good movie. I wrote a bit of a treatment and talked to Alan Dean Foster and asked if he would mind if I adapted it into a screenplay. He was like, sure, and gave me his lawyer's email. The lawyer never responded, so I guess the book is what we get. Ah well... Read less
