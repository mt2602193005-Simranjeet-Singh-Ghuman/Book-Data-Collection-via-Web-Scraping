Reviewer: Aletheuon I bought this for a friend who keeps giving an abusive husband yet another chance, so the real test will be whether she find it helpful. However, I've dipped into it, and I'm hopeful. Abused women quite often do not know what constitutes normal behaviour and passively allow themselves to be controlled and lied to. This book has a chapter on how to tell whether your partner is potentially good, thoroughly bad or utterly hopeless (a sociopath). Another chapter describes common traits and tricks of abusive men and another the ways they justify their behaviour. The book also discusses the payoffs of their behaviour and how the abused partner should respond. It seems packed full of truth and just plain sound sense. There are also reasons that women let themselves continue to be abused and if these are stronger than the good sense in this book, I suppose my friend will continue to choose her abusive relationship. I hope and pray that she will not. Read less
