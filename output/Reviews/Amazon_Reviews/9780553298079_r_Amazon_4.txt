Reviewer: Kathy I’d like everyone to know that Rosanne Bittner has captured my heart and mind. Her writings are authentic, historically, personally, emotionally, sensually, and a depiction of real hard-working, loving human beings. I am totally amazed at the way she draws the reader in and allows us to feel like we’re right there in the middle of the battles, struggles and loving emotions that real people feel. She doesn’t cut any corners with what goes on between two people that truly love each other. It’s not a nasty, dirty romance at all. The way she uses the sensuality in these readings is wonderful. Especially if you’re a woman reader and probably all so for a man, you feel like you’re right there being loved, cherished, and sexually desired, protected and deeply loved. I stand amazed. I thought savage destiny series was wonderful and maybe the best she’d ever written but out my heart is right up there with it. I can’t wait to continue this series. If you are a historical western reader and like a little romance in your readings, this author has got it all. I speak these words as truth, as I know it, and I will continue to search and read all of her books. Thank you, Roseanne for the great work you do. Kathy Read less
