Reviewer: ASB I enjoyed this book and learned something new about this time period. This was the first book by this author that I’ve read, and I wasn’t disappointed. This was a clean read with no foul language, graphic sex or religious swear words unless you can count the word ‘Dhu’. I don’t know what that means but the people in the story worshipped the sky god and Mother Earth so that could be related🤔. The intimate scenes were fade to black and very tastefully done, and I loved the characters and felt they were well developed. I did wonder what happened to the two evil men who were cast out of the tribe. They kind of faded away after they carried out their revenge. It seemed there should have been more angst associated with that than what there was. Either way, this was a great read and I look forward to more from this author. Read less
