Reviewer: MJ The plot just doesn't hang together. Two men kidnap and hold hostage the heroine. At the end their reason is revealed. They want her to like them. Unbelievable. There are other stupid plot devices. For example one of the men is a successful artist, but takes pains to hide this. Instead he allows the heroine to believe he's a dangerous criminal. As all men do when they want a long-term relationship with a woman I'm sure! Then the most significant failing of a novel in this genre. The sex scenes just are not hot. Not even lukewarm to be honest. There's a sameness about nearly all of them. The context scarcely ever changes. To summarise one or both of the men wish to have sex with the heroine. She doesn't want to. They ignore her refusal. She orgasms anyway. I've read many "black lace" books. This is the worst of them. Read less
