Reviewer: SYED SAIF ALI RIZVI The book was really the one to be cherished for a long long time . This was my first venture into Stephen Alter's books and I really believe that this book was one of the best books on this topic ( mussoorie and Woodstock ) Frankly speaking I don't like reading autobiographies , but this one was different from those boring , long autobiographies that leave you in a state of tumult. This book not only describes the culture of the eminent Woodstock but also the lives of the students . I had decided to read this book only because I have been to Landour and I had really liked the atmosphere , the surroundings and the'far from the madding crowd ' feel . This book is not only a traveller's guide but also your best past time on cool rainy day. Read less
