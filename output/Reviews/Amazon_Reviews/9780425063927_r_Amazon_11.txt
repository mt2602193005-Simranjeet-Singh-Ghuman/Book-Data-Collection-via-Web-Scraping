Reviewer: Simon Gareth Ingram You want to get a feel for the ‘real’ James Bond? - forget the movies, they are an assortment of semi accurate to downright inaccurate renderings of these books and of the man. Efficiently written, Ian was clearly a professional who approached his writing as I expect would Mr. Bond himself. Perhaps not surprising. These are stories of their era, the attitudes and comments reflect their time. Do not look for a story with todays style and treatment of politics, gender and machismo. Put yourself into the post-war era and you will be rewarded with a fine story that in many ways is better than I had expected in the portrayal of the people and their behaviours. The books miss the dramatic visual effects of explosive action but more than compensate with the richness of character development. I was particularly impressed with “Quantum of Solace, which is not a story about Bond or spies. Read less
