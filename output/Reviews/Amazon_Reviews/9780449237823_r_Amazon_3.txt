Reviewer: Lomaharshana “The Caves of Steel” is the first volume of the four-volume Robots series of novels by the great American sci-fi writer Isaac Asimov. It was first published in 1954. This edition that’s available from Amazon India is from 2018. It was published by HarperCollins Publishers in England. I really don’t know why some reviewers did not like the quality of the paperback. I think it is great. Certainly much better than most other paperbacks. The cover artwork is so evocative. And the text is typeset in the elegant Janson Text typeface. The binding is not going to last for fifty years, but then what do you expect from a paperback. The story is set in New York City of the future, a couple thousand years in the future. New York City is an entirely enclosed, underground, hive of millions of humans—a cave of steel. Strangely though, just next to the City is another enclosed settlement. This one is called Spacetown. It is occupied by Spacers, who are human beings from the planet Aurora, descendants of humans who left Earth centuries ago to colonise other planets. Spacers are hated by native Earthmen, and envied too, for their wealth and health. Well one day a man is found murdered in Spacetown. A smart detective from New York City, Elijah Baley, is summoned to solve the crime in collaboration with a Spacer, Daneel Olivaw. … It is a beginning of a story that will last fourteen novels and 20,000 years! How can one not want to read these books! How can one not want to buy these books! Read less
