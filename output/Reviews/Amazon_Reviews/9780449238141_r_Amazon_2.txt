Reviewer: april very good...menacing, convoluted, intricate...great setting, fairly standard characters...silly ingenue, stalwart hero, evil villains, dramatic scenes, violence, betrayals and general skulduggery...what makes it unusual is the setting, and the historical background...well written, well paced...one of ms norton's few "gothic" novels, and worth reading, for that reason alone, even by her fantasy and science fiction fans... Read less
