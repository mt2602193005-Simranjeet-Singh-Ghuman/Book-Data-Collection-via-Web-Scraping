Reviewer: April Andre norton wrote mostly fantasies, but also a few romances, and quite a lot of science fiction…sometimes the genres mingle a bit…this text, fairly early in her career, is pure gothic romance, with a very silly heroine, a delightful hero, an interesting setting and a very good plot…the action plods along a bit dully at first, but the pace picks up, and the denouement is rapid and exciting…a very good entry, though i am glad she did more fantasies, instead of continuing in this particular vein…she was a mistress of the craft of writing, and a delightful person as well…this text is fun, charming and well done…interesting and amusing… Read less
