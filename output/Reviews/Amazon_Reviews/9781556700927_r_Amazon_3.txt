Reviewer: Raga Makeda Cannon A totally inspiring account of great women. I absolutely love it. I can never tire of hearing stories about the strength and determination of these beautiful black women. Many are known activists like folk-singer Odetta & Coretta Scott King. But there are MANY women in this book with just short, one page accounts, and boy are these stories amazing. From reading the account of Marva Collins-- an educator who founded her own school, I immediately purchased a DVD on her life. (The Marva Collins story). I got it from "Oldies" and was absolutely enchanted by the oozing love she gave to so many children and how greatly she empowered them. If you can afford it, I recommend your getting as many DVDs, biographies and autobiographies of these women as can be found, because we must keep these stories alive and in the public eye. Whether they are black or white women, or male individuals, I find stories about the human condition and human triumph to be very worthwhile and inspiring. The photos are equally inspiring. Thank you Amazon for keeping the ball rolling. Robin Carter Cannon Read less
