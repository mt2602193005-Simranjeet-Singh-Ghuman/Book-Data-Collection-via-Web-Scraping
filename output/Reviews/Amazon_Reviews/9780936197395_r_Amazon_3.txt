Reviewer: Comdet I've read several books on back pain and this has been the best by far. Brownstein takes a truly holistic approach to dealing with back issues. He stresses the need for basic rehabilitation exercises (mainly stretches) but also stresses the need for "mind" issues, such as relaxation techniques. It's a wonderful blend of approaches, and it really struck a chord with me. Best of all, it does work! I've made very good progress in dealing with sciatica pain, and have a far more positive outlook than before. The writing is clear and direct. The book begins with the basics of spinal structure, muscle groups and how everything works together. Nothing groundbreaking here, but nicely structured. Enough detail to get the key points across without burying the reader with too much detail and anatomy lessons. The section on stretching is excellent and alone worth the cost of the book. Again, nothing novel or outlandish - just basic stretching routines (several of which I was familiar with from my physical therapist, but quite a few new ones as well). The book brings value to the process by explaining each stretch clearly (both what to do and not to do) and proving illustrations. The combination of text and illustrations made it much easier for me to follow than other books that stressed one format over another. There's much more to the book, including relaxation exercises, tips on a proper diet, maintaining a healthy back, and a wonderful (but brief) chapter on what to do in a back emergency. If you have back pain, this is absolutely worth reading and trying. Read less
