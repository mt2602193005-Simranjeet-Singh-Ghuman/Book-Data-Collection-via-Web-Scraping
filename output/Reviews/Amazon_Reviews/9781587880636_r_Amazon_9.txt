Reviewer: Aussie.Abroad I thought I had all the Dick Francis books being a long time fan, then I saw this and it did not ring any bells so bought it. It was a trip down memory lane, Dick at his best (sadly no more as he has recently passed away) He wrote this one in 1999 and published in 2000 - a story of a glassblower in England - has the typical Dick Francis association with Horse Racing - but only two scenes at racecourses - pure Francis in that he researches his topics with a thoroughness that other authors should copy. Great side characters and for this first time a female detective gets a major role. Love it. For all of you who have never read Dick Francis you are missing out. You do not have to be into horses just love the thrill of the chase! Read less
