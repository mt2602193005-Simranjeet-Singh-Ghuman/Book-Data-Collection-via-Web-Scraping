Reviewer: Janeway The Characters in this story were wonderful and I had a difficult time putting the book down. After just coming off a visit to the UK, I liked the historical time period of the book, just before America's Independence from Great Britain. I like the fact that many of Kerrelyn Spark's female Characters in her books are strong and independent. Her stories are intriguing and very exciting. The spy element in this book kept me at the edge of my seat. The Forbidden Lady was quite a delightful and fun read and I was disappointed when the book ended. I have read all of Kerrelyn's books and I am counting the days to her newest release at the end of December. Read less
