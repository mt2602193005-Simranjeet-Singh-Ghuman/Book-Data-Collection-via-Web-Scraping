Reviewer: Lin Bee I enjoyed these books about the discovery of Virginia by Mrs. Hunt. The blood and gore were a bit much for me so I only got through the first two in the series. I really liked her fleshed out characters as they felt real and alive to me. The research for this series no doubt took much time and it amazes me how she is able to do so well and write so many great books. I recommend this series about the discovery of America but warn those who might be squeamish about bloody killings and torture. I just could not go on and read the remaining three. I was happy that the two main characters in the first two books finally got together and went on to make a happy life for themselves at the place where their lives began in Roanoke, VA. A great read and am happy I read them. Read less
