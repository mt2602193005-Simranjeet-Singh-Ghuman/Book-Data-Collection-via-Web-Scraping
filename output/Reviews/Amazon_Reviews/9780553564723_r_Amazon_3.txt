Reviewer: Carolyn A story that takes you on many highs and lows in just living life—especially family life. Loved Luke and Lettie. Raising children is hard isn’t it. Trials and trials but much perseverance. I was annoyed a bit with Tyler,the oldest son. Glad he matured finally. I don’t think the Ramona situation was love at all—just a young man driven by the excitement of available sex. I wished we could have heard more about Nathan’s Indian wife, Leena. I would like to have seen her personality and her adjustment. Read less
