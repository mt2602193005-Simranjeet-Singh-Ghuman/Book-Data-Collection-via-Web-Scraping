Reviewer: Roland Johansson Short story (128 pages). After a spaceship accident leaves the passengers (crew are dead) on an unknown but hospitable planet the 5 women & 3 men debate how to survive - with few supplies and no tools. They decide to colonize and populate their new home, except one woman have other ideas and rebel. It is seen as a kind of betrayal as no co-operating expected from her. Story told from her point of view, thoughts and deeds. Soon one found out she hates men, well every human (she's wicked IMO). I genuinely think much of it is the authors view. *Warning* Spoilers. She kills everybody, one by one, including the innocent 12 year young girl/child. That's her "nice" choice. After the first 3:rd of the novel I hoped it would go from bad to...instead it went worse. I almost never unfinish a book but in this case in a way I did as I skipped quickly through the last 3:rd part. It was still painful. Author was a lesbian feminist and much of her agressive views radiated trough everything she wrote in this book. 1st and last one I bought by her. Per se I have nothing against an authors views if it doesn't destroy the pleasure of reading a book. Alice Sheldon aka James Triptree, Jr. also lesbian feminist, wrote some of the best and amazing SF stories I ever read. Sheldon's "Her Smoke Rose Up Forever" is a masterpiece I bought from amazon.se. Read less
