Reviewer: C. Martin I bought this book for a friend as she had just broken up with a loser. This book helped me years ago before I met my wonderful husband. I think the book helped me to recognize a great guy when I saw him. I know if helped me see the red flags losers raise. The book also explained some of the past games some of my dates had played. I would recommend this book to any female who is tired of dating losers or who wants to avoid all of the games. Read less
