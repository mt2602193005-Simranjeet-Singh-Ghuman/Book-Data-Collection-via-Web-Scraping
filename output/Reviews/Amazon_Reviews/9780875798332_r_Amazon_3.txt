Reviewer: A. Shettles I loved the characters of Alex and Briggie. Alex has been estranged from her parents for many years and after being widowed for a few years she decides to visit her parents to find out why they sent her away in her teens. That visit changes her life forever and brings to light a family secret that others are willing to murder for. Briggie teams up with others of their friends to protect Alex and solve the mystery to save Alex. Exciting as they use genealogical information to solve the mystery. Read less
