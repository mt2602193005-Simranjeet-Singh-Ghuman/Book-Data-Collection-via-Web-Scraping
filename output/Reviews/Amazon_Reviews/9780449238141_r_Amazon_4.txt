Reviewer: Barbara Minton I grew up reading Andre Norton's science fiction and historical novels and loved them. Now that I'm much older I've been rereading my favorites and finding more books I didn't know about. I spent 18 years in Florida and was drawn to this book because it is set in the Keys and based on events that actually happened on Indian Key during the Seminole Wars. As always, I am amazed by Andre Norton research. As a transplanted Floridian and sometimes homesick, I would have liked more detail as to the vegetation and geology of the keys, but that was never Norton's strong suit. It took awhile to get through the slow start but had a great finish.. And thanks to my referring to Wikipedia as I read, I learned a lot about the history of Indian Key, wrecking in the Keys, and the Calusa Indians. Read less
