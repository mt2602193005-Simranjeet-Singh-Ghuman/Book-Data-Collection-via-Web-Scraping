Reviewer: Nathik Generally I tend to stay away from Horror genre. What really intrigued me to read 'Red Dragon' was a random article on the real life inspiration for Hannibal Lector. Dr. Alfredo Balli Trevino from Mexico who methodically murdered his friend and possibly murdered many hitchhikers is the real life inspiration for the Hannibal Lector. Now coming back to "Red Dragon". This a fast paced thriller for a novel written 36 years back and found it surprising that it didn't sound dated. 'Red Dragon' basically set the tone for future fiction on offender profiling, serial killers and FBI investigation on killers on all medium. Recommend if you are looking for a fast paced thriller.
