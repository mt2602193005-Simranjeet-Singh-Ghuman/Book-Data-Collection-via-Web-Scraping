Reviewer: Michael H. Haussler This is a wonderful book for a reader who may pine after the fresh days of yore, when perhaps nearly all kids were sweet, obedient, and a wee bit neurotic in their efforts to please the grown-ups. The biggest problem was well, kids just kidding around. Thus, you will learn all about Darci and her friends and how they think about; well, boys, and their own reputations (who has my diary!?), in the pre social media age! Tossed with a dash of romance and a generous helping of day-to-day drama, this book will take you back to a cheerful place and time where innocence is the backdrop to the happy ending. Read less
