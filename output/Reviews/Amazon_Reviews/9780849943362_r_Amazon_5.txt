Reviewer: Shana Stoll I think it would be of great benefit for every parent, teacher and person who works with children (from elementary up through college) to read this book. Frank Peretti's story of bullying is so powerful and hard to read, but in the end we can make a difference in the lives of kids if we are more aware of what they are going through. No one likes bullying, and it has become such a problem recently. We have got to do something to help combat it before it gets any worse. Read less
