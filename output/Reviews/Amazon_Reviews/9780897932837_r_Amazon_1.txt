Reviewer: Jeff Nailen Great book that addresses a silent and seemingly growing problem in our culture. I especially liked Chapter 3 regarding the abuser's tendency to project or externalize blame onto the victim. This adds insult to injury for the victim or survivor of abuse and of course is a cynical cop-out by the abuser. Blaming the victim is one of the cruelest forms of emotional abuse used to subjugate, humiliate, and utterly control victims through brainwashing. Psychological enslavement of their victim is their goal. My girlfriend and I know from first-hand experience of what Pamela speaks. But I liked Chapter 5 the most regarding understanding how the abuser thinks and what abusers believe because this gets to underlying causes. As Pamela says, "People act out what they think." There are several overlapping causes of domestic violence but the proximate cause is the belief system and resulting behavior of the abuser him/herself. Getting inside their twisted mind to understand their beliefs and motivations is essential in understanding and solving this problem individually and socially. Well done. The references to the O.J. Simpson case also bring it home while reminding us that it occurs in many contexts, not just among the poor. Jeff Nailen & Angeles Lopez AngelWingsSantaFe.com Read less
