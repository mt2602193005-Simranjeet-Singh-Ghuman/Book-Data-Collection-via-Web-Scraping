Reviewer: Resan I usually like CR's stories, but recently read one that was just blah. This one was a lovely read, just the right amount of low grade angst, lots of love. The story moved really smoothly. Of course 9year old Drew is the best part ... with the most entertaining situations revolving around him. To my mind h&H would not be able to achieve a life balance, given their work overload - which makes for hardly there parents. While this issue is highlighted a few times, it just hangs there. Read less
