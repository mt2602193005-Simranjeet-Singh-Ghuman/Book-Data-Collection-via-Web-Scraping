Reviewer: E17 LOVED Fear of Flying so had to read book number 2. It was enjoyable and again, read like a good meal - I went through pages withough really realising. I like the unexpected twist in the marriage plot line and again the internal dialogue of the main character is wonderfully honest. I think the sex is a bit more full on with this book and sometimes I just felt like it was a little unbelievable and overwhelmed the narrative. The main takeaway for me was that there was less philosophical lines I could stop and think.. "hhm" and underline. I think the character went from a funny, self deprecating car crash to a privileged sex-focussed celeb - didn't feel as connected to her. Read less
