Reviewer: J. Hopkins The writing is good, the tone very turn-of-the (21st) century. The protagonist spends most of his time just as lost as the reader, which is pretty lost at times. The technological underpinnings could belong to mid-to-late 21st century, which is where the story seems to be placed. The style isn't my favorite, but it's a well-written narrative which rambled only as much as it should have, given a 1st-person viewpoint. It was worth finishing, which is more than I can say for a few books I've started recently. Read less
