Reviewer: A. L. Addams A great book from Victoria holt, though not her best. There is an element of padding in here, just a little. It goes from genteel Britain to the new world at a time when Australia was just beginning to be inhabited. Rough living and wives brought over from the homeland to share the loneliness of the outbacker. Twists and turns as usual in her books, but even in those days (late 19th century) women could make choices but were encouraged not to, so a little frustrating. Not finished it yet, but there are some characters in there that make your blood boil and some that make you want to slap them. Our main girl is very switched on though and I think she will work out the plot and find her brother (one of the ones you want to slap for his idiocy) but all in all I recommend it. 4 stars for the padding only. The rest of it is a really good read. Read less
