Reviewer: Ashley Ferguson "Christina's Ghost" by Betty Ren Wright is a captivating children's book that effortlessly blends mystery, friendship, and the supernatural into a heartwarming story. From the very first page, readers are drawn into the intriguing world of Christina and her ghostly companion. Wright's masterful storytelling keeps readers on the edge of their seats as they follow Christina's journey to unravel the mysteries surrounding her new home and the spectral presence within it. What sets "Christina's Ghost" apart is its ability to tackle complex themes such as loss, acceptance, and the power of friendship with sensitivity and grace. Through Christina's interactions with her ghostly friend, young readers are encouraged to explore the deeper meaning of empathy, compassion, and understanding. The book's richly drawn characters and atmospheric setting create a vivid backdrop for an unforgettable adventure that will stay with readers long after they've turned the final page. As a parent and an avid reader, I wholeheartedly recommend "Christina's Ghost" to anyone looking for a captivating story that will enchant and inspire young imaginations. Whether read aloud as a family or enjoyed independently by young readers, this book is sure to spark lively discussions and foster a love of reading. With its timeless themes and engaging narrative, "Christina's Ghost" is a must-have addition to any child's library. Read less
