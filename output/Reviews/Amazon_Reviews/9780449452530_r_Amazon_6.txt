Reviewer: Nanette Feuling I was required to provide security answers recently. I chose, “Favorite Author: Victoria Holt.” No … that was high school—come on! Only, this book has a quality script and is a fantastic read! High school or no, Victoria Holt is a good storyteller … a great plot here, highly recommend if you like victorian romance murder-mysteries to challenge Daphne Du Maurier. One of the best I’ve read in a long time—probably since I stopped choosing her books to placate my ego with my boring credible authority. Read less
