Reviewer: Tzarina Tea At first, I was not going to read this book because I thought it would be a rehash of Michael Crichton 's, "Pery," but with Mulder and Scilly thrown in. However, thankfully, it's not. It has it's own take on nanotechnology effecting the world; which made this a completely enjoyable read. All in all, I would say that this so much better than, "Prey," and I would recommend that one should give it a try if one has the time. Read less
