Reviewer: Pat V My Pastor's Wife recommended this book that was recommended to her many years ago. When I heard the title "You're Late Again, Lord," I knew I had to buy this. I appreciate the raw honesty of the writer. Within the first few pages I read, I quickly started highlighting points that spoke directly to me. I was seeking answers from God, but only got silence or so I thought. This little honest book has peeled layers of me like a skillful surgeon's knife and I feel the healing beginning in my heart. I am going to tell others about this book and also buy it for someone else. I know it's addressed to women but I know some men who could benefit from the lessons if they would humble themselves. From one writer to another, thank you Sister Karon for sharing your life lessons. Read less
