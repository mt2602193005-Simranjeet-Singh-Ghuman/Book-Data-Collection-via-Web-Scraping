Reviewer: G. Frome I am just about through with the 3rd and last book. I like everything Margaret Weis and Tracy Hickman have written. Towards the end of the 2nd book I was thinking it might get too magic heavy. But it didn't. I like the personalities and the plot more than the magic (although magic is important in their books). All of their books are page turners for me and this trilogy is no exception. Read less
