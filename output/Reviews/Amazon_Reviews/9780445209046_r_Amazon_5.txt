Reviewer: Jerry K This book is more fantasy/horror than sci-fi. I found myself irritated with the repeating chase scenes - monsters chasing the group of protagonists - and yet as someone noted the book is full of philosophical nuggets and the writing is mostly pretty fruity stuff. I couldn't quite bring myself to put it away without finishing. One thing I liked was the author's honesty about suburban American life and the way he cut to the quick of each character's real desires, even if they weren't so noble. There is a whiff of extremely frank authorial confession in these pages which every now and then has a way of getting in amongst you and challenging your complacency and rationalizations. The book is also faintly allegorical and the repeated admonition that Evil thrives where Chaos reigns has some stark applicability to our present political difficulties. Not my favorite Foster but worth a read. Read less
