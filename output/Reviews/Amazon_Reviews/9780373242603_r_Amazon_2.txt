Reviewer: paula legate This really was a sweet story. Fate brought Lisa, and Jared together. They both wanted a baby. Jared had paid a lady to be a surrogate mother for him. He paid a lump sum up front, and he was going to pay the other half once the baby was born. The lady took the money and ran. Jared and Lisa were both using San Diego fertility clinic. By mistake Lisa was impregnated with Jared's sperm. He wanted his baby, and attempted to pay Lisa off, to have the baby. Lisa thought this might be here only chance to have a baby because of some health issues. The couple talked, fought, wanted to scream at one another, and kissed. Lisa was a strong heroine, and I giggled at her a few times throughout the story. Jared had some past issues to deal with. With the love in Lisa's Heart, he was able to know real love for the first time. This was not a book with everything perfect for the couple. They had a lot to deal with, but in the end love won out! I loved Ashley in this story too. She was Lisa's best friend, and she was very protective of Lisa! Everyone needs a best friend like Ashley! Sample from chapter 9 Jared ignored the mild bickering. Something much more important was being revealed to him. Lisa loved him. He'd seen the emotion reflected in the depth of her golden eyes, the caring in the tenderness of her expression. For a moment he felt whole, complete for the first time in his life. Joy flooded his being. Read less
