Reviewer: Jannette Good to read at any time, but if you want a Christmas theme 'Someone to Hold' is nice because the first few chapters take place during Christmas time. Christy has been in a rut since her husband left her. When Cameron comes into her life she tells him she only needs a friend. He gives her that friendship and talks her through her feelings until she can let go of her pain. Reading between the lines we can see that Cameron is holding back until she is ready for something more romantic. I enjoyed reading about each stage of their developing relationship, and their conversations about life. It is a feel-good, gentle story. SPOILER ALERT: Two thirds into the book there is a dramatic shift in tone when they are caught up in the events of 9/11. I like how the author shows the drama, and the way strangers pull together and help each other. However, if you want to stick to the romantic part, you can stop reading when they return from their honeymoon. Read less
