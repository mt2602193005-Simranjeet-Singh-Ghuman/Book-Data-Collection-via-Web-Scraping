Reviewer: FryLady I reread this book as part of the NYT Summer Reading Challenge to "reread a book you loved as a kid." Well...I was 13 or so when I read Scruples and I was enthralled from the beginning. As a chubby, shy teen, I loved the part where Billy goes to Paris and unwillingly is put on the "French Diet" and comes back home thin and beautiful to conquer the world. I ended up becoming a writer, so I came back to this book expecting to be disappointed. No! THIS BOOK IS JUST AS GOOD AS I REMEMBERED. It's the literary equivalent of eating a giant bag of chips on a sunny day at the beach while someone gives you a foot massage....feels so good and you don't want to stop! The storyline is fast-paced and propulsive, it just goes and goes and goes. And now that I'm in my 60s, it's also a fun nostalgia trip, a "time capsule" of all the beautiful people and ultra-chic places I read about when I was young. As my vision isn't what it used to be, I was happy to find Scruples on Kindle. That said, the typos seemed to increase in volume as the book went on. There are plenty of talented proofreaders looking for work, Kindle! I can't recommend this enough as a fun read. But be warned: DO NOT read it in the doctor's waiting room with the font size at 18 because you never know when an explicit sex scene may pop up. Read less
