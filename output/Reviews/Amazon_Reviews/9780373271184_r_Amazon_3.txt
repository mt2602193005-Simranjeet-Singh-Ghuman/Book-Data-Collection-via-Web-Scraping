Reviewer: Hedgehog If you are a lover of "authentic" romances this, plus the preceding three books, is worth every penny you spend on acquiring it. This has not been an emotionally easy series for me. My usual preference is for light undemanding fare but I continued reading book one out of pure stubbornness since I had downloaded it for free and I don't throw anything out unless it is really bad. This series is about life, folks. About subjects that make us cringe, about hurt human beings trying to make their way back to normalcy. We weep with them, we become co-sufferers, we cheer them on and we are elated when they finally get their deserved HEA. Thanks to a brilliant author who does extremely well when it comes to drawing us into the very plausible world of her creation! Read less
