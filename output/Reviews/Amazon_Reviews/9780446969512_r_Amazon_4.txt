Reviewer: Priya Yet another captivative tale from Sheldon which narrates the life of Elizabeth Roffe transforming from an introvert girl to a confident & bold enough to run the Roffe & Sons company. The storyline is interesting and keeps you engrossed, but the story has too many characters to remember. Climax of the story is quite disappointing as it ends abrupt and leaves unanswered for many questions.. Read less
