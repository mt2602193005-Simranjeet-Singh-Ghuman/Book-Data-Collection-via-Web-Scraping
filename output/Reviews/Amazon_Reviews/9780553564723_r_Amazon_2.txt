Reviewer: Dales10701 This is one of my favorites. To the reviewer who said this book does not have enough romance in it. Maybe you were not reading the same book that I did, because I found that this story was full of romance. The book takes you through the lives and Luke and Lettie, and yes they do end up together quickly as far as pages that you read, but they spend the rest of the book engrosed in romance. They go through years and years of romance. Not only is there romance between Luke and Lettie, but also in Tyler and Katie. The story of Nathan was compelling and I would have liked to have read what he was doing while he was captured by the Sioux Indians, and how his relationship developed with Half Nose, but you would need a whole other book to do that. Rosanne Bittner's description of the land and historical events that are in her book is fascinating to read and I enjoy every minute of it. Thanks Rosanne for another AMAZING ROMANCE NOVEL!
