Reviewer: Kindle Customer This saga starts from humble beginnings and tells the story of the evolution of a city, whilst also mirroring the evolution of the characters who built it. A massive book, brilliantly written with huge characters who are complex and whose motivations are complicated. David Kirkland was the man who, from start to finish, held my huge sympathy. His demise is so sensitively written that it had me crying buckets. Very much recommended read. Read less
