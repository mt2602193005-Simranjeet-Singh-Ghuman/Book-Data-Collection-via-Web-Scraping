Reviewer: 異人 The main character emigrates from China to Thailand, leaving behind only a brief farewell note to his mother, and then writes her 96 letters from his new home, which form the 96 chapters of this book, without receiving any replies from her. They describe his business success in the Chinese community in Bangkok, his marriage to a beautiful Chinese girl there who, to his disappointment, gives him three daughters and only one son, his determination to train his son to follow in his footsteps, a series of family catastrophes, and the stalwart efforts of his sister-in-law and his youngest daughter to look after him. A great book in an unusual and very readable format. Read less
