Reviewer: Shradha I bought the Penguins Classics edition and boy was i disappointed! Although the quality of page was really perfect, what didn’t work for me was the font size and the contents of the book other than the story. First of all, there are 80-100 pages in the front and 10-20 pages in the back which has ‘notes’ on Alice’s adventure in Wonderland. Second, the font is so small, so- so small that please do buy a magnifying glass together with the book! Again, i do not understand why are there notes going on for about 50 pages ( like Ross- front and back!) about a such a simple story? If you’re buying this book for academic purposes please go ahead. If not, please buy some other edition of the book. Even tho i got it just for Rs130, I just couldn’t stand it and had to return. Read less
