Reviewer: GSC I mean that. This is such a well written story, so very well told. There is a richness of detail that makes you feel as if you are there, in that time period. Living that life. I don't know how to explain it any better than that. LaVyrle Spencer brings her stories to life in a way so few writers can. I don't understand why her books aren't as popular today as they were when she wrote them. No matter how many times I read them they always pull me in. And I always feel a sense of regret reading the last few pages. Knowing it's over. There are some typos in this kindle edition that should be fixed. These are older books. I suspect whoever put them on here simply didn't know how. But even they hardly detract from this story. If you like historical fiction that is incredibly well researched and even more well written this author is for you. Read less
