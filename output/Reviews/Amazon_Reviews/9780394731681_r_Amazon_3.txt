Reviewer: Cameron Personally I loved this book. It pokes a bit of fun at our mild insecurities to point out how irrational they actually are. From some of the other comments it seems some take this the wrong way. It’s definitely a self help book so if your not suffering anxiety or similar I don’t know why you’d buy it otherwise. Otherwise, it’s a relatively short read which is good for me, and the copy I have seems to be one of the originals which puts it nearly 40-50 years old which is really nice. Read less
