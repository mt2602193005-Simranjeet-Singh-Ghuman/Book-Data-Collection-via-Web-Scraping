Reviewer: DebbieB-Canada This was a heartwarming book about spousal abuse and learning to love and trust again. The book was fantastic from start to finish pand I was on the edge of my seat until the abusive and manipulative ex- husband got his dues. You won't be disappointed - read this book The rest of the series was also fantastic. Every book was terrific with very different life stories but each one heart warming and thought provoking. The most powerful was Patrick MacNeill with unconditional love and dedication to his son. A beautiful read! Read less
