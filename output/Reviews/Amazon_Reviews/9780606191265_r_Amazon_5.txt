Reviewer: Snehith Abin Nicholas Sparks is one romantic fiction author who has the capability to grip his readers till the last page.He captures reality, love and all the emotions in a beautiful manner. The way the characters are described, their unconditional love for each other, the manner how they fell in love, the negativity that trys to separate them, getting back together despite all odds — all these things evoke emotions in the reader. How much they yearn for each other, the care, bringing out the best in the partner, all this is enchanting. The reader is able to connect to the situations, the emotions and hence, feel the love. After all, everyone of us must have fallen for someone in our lives!! Read less
