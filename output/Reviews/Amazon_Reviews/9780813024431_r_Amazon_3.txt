Reviewer: WriterTales Very interesting insight into Marjorie Kinnan Rawlings and her relationship with her mother. Writing this must have been a type of therapy for Rawlings. She did enter it in a contest, so it could have been published in her lifetime. It’s a raw look at a troubled mother daughter relationship, written some years after her mother’s early death. You see the early marks of a great writer to be and learn about her early years. Probably more of a “fandom” piece than one for an average reader. Read less
