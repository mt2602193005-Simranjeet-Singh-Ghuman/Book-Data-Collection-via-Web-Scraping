Reviewer: Rajiv Chopra “The Hot Zone” by Richard Preston starts by describing the infection and death of a French expatriate in Africa. He named this person, “Charles Monet”. The description of his infection and death by Ebola was absolutely frightening. This description is probably one of the terrifying things I have read in a long time, and I felt physical pain when I read it. He moved from Africa to the US and the discovery of a strain of Ebola. When a strain of Ebola was accidentally discovered in a shipment of monkeys, Richard Preston went into overdrive. From this point on, the book read like a thriller. Richard ended the book describing his trip to Africa. He did become a little philosophical in the last two pages. The book ended with a dire warning. We humans have slowly pushed the natural habitat back, and have covered it with concrete. Nature may be staging an immune response to the destruction that we are causing. These are thoughts that we have to think about if we want to live in harmony with nature. Read less
