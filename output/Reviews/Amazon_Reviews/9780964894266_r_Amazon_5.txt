Reviewer: Amit verma I found this book to be an amazing guide for professionals who want to handle challenges with intelligence and grace. It takes inspiration from Birbal’s timeless stories and applies them to modern workplace situations. I really enjoyed how this book brought Bangalore’s buzzing startup scene to life. The way it shows the energy, struggles, and creativity of startups made me feel like I was right there. Sid’s story is so relatable and full of lessons that I could connect with, even if I’m not part of the startup world. It’s inspiring to see his ups and downs and how he grows through them. This book left me with new perspectives and motivation. Each lesson feels practical and easy to relate to, offering valuable ideas for dealing with real-life corporate scenarios. I especially appreciated how the book bridges traditional wisdom with today’s fast-paced work culture, making it a must-read for anyone looking to improve leadership skills. Read less
