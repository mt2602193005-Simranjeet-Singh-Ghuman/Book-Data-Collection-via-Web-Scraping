Reviewer: Ada Ardor This book is incredibly readable, and Jong has a natural affinity for writing. I was fascinated with Isadora Wing in Fear of Flying, but in this book, she has devolved into a whiny martyr, who is all about blame, blame, blame. She is so focused on pointing the finger, she cannot perceive her own faults. Moreover, although I like confident characters, this one was extremely conceited - repetitiously repeating praise for herself - and worst of all, not even having the politeness to feign some humility. I don't understand Isadora, and I didn't really empathize with her. I don't get how she could just jump in the shower with a stranger and let him wash her. The ease in which she had an orgy was just baffling - to say the least. I will concede a very interesting read - I was entertained the whole way through - well, with one exception: loathed the love poems at the end. High marks, though for the superb writing. Read less
