Reviewer: Lorene I have taught "Simply Trusting" several times and am now teaching it to my "Senior Singles" class. It is awesome how women who have been Christians for many years can still learn so much from this study on faith. Things you don't think about and challenges you must learn, believe and trust, from the Word of God. This book has so much "food for thought". It brings much thought provoking challenge to your walk with the Lord. We must step out in faith and stand firm on His Word if we want to grow in our Christian walk. There are 3 other books in this series but I must warn you, they are all getting hard to find. The other 3 are: "Boldly Asking", Deepening your walk with God through prayer; "Joyfully Following", Deepening your walk with God through joy; and "Quietly Resting", Deepening your walk with God through rest. (Spiritual rest [trust] in the Lord). This series is easy to teach from but very thought provoking and challenging. Read less
