Reviewer: lawrence3991 I have worked in the pest control career field for almost 40 years and this is a great book any homeowner or pest management professional. It gives good information on identification and the control methods mentioned are on point also. The book itself was released in the early 90s and there have Ben a lot of changes so yes the book does need some updating. But, the general information on identification and control methods still make this book a really good buy. I am not sure how others will view this book but personally I think it’s a really good buy and at the time I purchased it, the price was really good! Read less
