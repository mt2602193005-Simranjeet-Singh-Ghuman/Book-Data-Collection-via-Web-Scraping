Reviewer: Bunny Mattie isn't after much from life, she's got no big job plans, she just wants to be a wife and mom and have a family of her own. Despite being engaged, things go down the plug hole fast when she realises her soon to be husband doesn't run a legal or ethical business, seeing him murder people makes her realise she's next, so she runs. As her ex gets closer Zeke, a drifting bad boy, helps her to escape. While on the run she gradually starts to get under his skin. A real page turner that got all nice and soppy towards the end.
