Reviewer: Carolina Consumer I've been a reader of Victoria Holt's books for years....having read many of them more than once. She is a first-rate, classic, gothic romance author, and her books never cease to entertain and intrigue me. This one is no exception. It has its rather spooky elements, the obligatory romance woven throughout, and enough mysterious happenings to keep the reader guessing. I thoroughly enjoyed it, once again! Read less
