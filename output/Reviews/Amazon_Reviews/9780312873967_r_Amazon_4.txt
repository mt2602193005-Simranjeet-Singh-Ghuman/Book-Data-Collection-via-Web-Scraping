Reviewer: Clay It's a little rough in some places but in the end I only wish that this was the first in a series of books about the same main character. Greg Costikyan provides an entirely fresh perspective on the 'aliens visit earth' scenario, one that focuses on the economic impact in a humorous way. As a veteran of a couple of tech start-ups I especially liked how he takes the challenge of finding a place in an international market and successfully imagines the challenge of entering an 'intergalactic' market. A friend recommended this book while we were talking about 3-D printing and how it might (someday) upset the way that products are made and sold (only vaguely related to subject matter of the book, in hindsight). Because the book is out of print I had to buy the it 'used' from a third-party seller but that went smoothly, as usual. Read less
