Reviewer: Grandma Dee Our son absolutely loved the Serendipity books as a child, so we passed down his small collection (12 books) to our granddaughter. I thought it would be fun to add a few books to the series for her, and was shocked to learn that there are now over 70 books in the series! So I picked out another dozen or so for this birthday. And I think "Daddy" will have just as much fun reading them to her! These books have sweet stories, with the most beautiful illustrations by Robin James (just amazing). I believe any child would enjoy them. Read less
