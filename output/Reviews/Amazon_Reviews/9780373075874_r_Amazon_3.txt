Reviewer: B.J. ...and then I didn't want the book to end. The synopsis has been told, and very well, by other reviewers. So, just my feelings about Breaking the Rules: This book held on to my heart, rooting for good to triumph over evil, for good people to get the reward they deserved. It was one soul reaching for another, but afraid to grab the gold ring, afraid they would not be able to live up to their potential. With Mattie running in fear for her life and Zeke withholding his feelings from the world, it seemed that each had nothing to offer the other. This book kept me engrossed and entertained, was well developed and fast moving. Yes, I'd read it or buy it again. Read less
