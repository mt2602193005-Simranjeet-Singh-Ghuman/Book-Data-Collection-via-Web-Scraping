Reviewer: Amazon Customer Well of Darkness – Sovereign Stone Trilogy 1 This is the first Weiss-Hickman I have read, and although I appear to have come to it later in life than most, I was not disappointed: human characters that I can relate to, some magic, but which does not dominate the action, and an interesting analysis of human feeling. For a long time I was wondering if there was any rich fantasy out there, that did not indulge itself with top-heavy characters or with notions of magic that simply took away all element of ingenuity in the resolution of conflict... And I think Weiss-Hickman have constructed a great tale, that keeps a good pace but that does not rush nor lag, has enough characters but not too many, and enough connections between the protagonists but not too many such that the text would become convoluted and unreadable.. The way in which the two main characters (what I call the the two main ones) fall in with each other and then never quite manage to fall out gave the novel the extra depth of interest that so often lacks in other fantasy. I enjoyed the story very much, and felt the Darkness in the atmosphere: There is definitely something ominous and garish about the events. I was compelled by the story and the writing, simple but not simplistic. It goes without saying that I shall read the next novels in due course. Read less
