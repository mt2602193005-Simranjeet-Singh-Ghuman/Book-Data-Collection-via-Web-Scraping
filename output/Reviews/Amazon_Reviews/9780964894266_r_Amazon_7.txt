Reviewer: Placeholder Corporate Birbal is an amazing read by author Vivek Vaidya. This is a story of Indian B (IIM Bangalore) school graduate who pursue entrepreneurial journey through his innovative and thoughtful mindset. Sid's journey takes us in and out of Bangalore - the famous start up capital of India. The challenges faced by the young inexperienced Sid with spunky spirit to create something unique is very well depicted through Sid's venture "Creativity Unilimited". His meeting with investor, choice of employees, business sense to create unique business propositions and various paths that he takes feel like the reader is part of this journey himself. Start ups has its own challenges as scale goes up and Creativity Unilimited is not an exception to it. As the greed of investor takes the organization to different path that loses the core foundation of the business. This detour of Creativity Unlimited is very thoroughly depicted in book. The challenges faced by Creativity Unlimited, its employees and emotional tubulence of Sid takes us through its upheaval. At various junctures, challenges faced by Sid have solutions in the form of Birbal stories which many have enjoyed in childhood. Thesw stories comes to rescue of Sid all the time. The situations and places in this book takes us through byelanes of Bangalore. These stories appears just like it was happening next door to some of our friend. Very engaging tale of Enterpreneurship, lessons learned through Birbal stories and transformation after a hiatus, this is very engaging must read!!!! Read less
