Reviewer: Finally Free Who would have known Frank Peretti, a well known and loved Christian fiction author, had a problem with bullying? I read the book because I was interest in reading a non-fiction by Frank Peretti. I love his fiction books, but this book helped me in my personal life. Having been in a terrible marriage, and I use that word lightly, for over 20 years I needed resources to understand what I was going through. No More Bullies came at the exact time I needed it. But what I appreciated about Mr. Peretti's book was his examination into possibly bullying others himself. That is a very humble thing to think about. It made me examine myself and make sure I am not doing what has been done to me. This is a great book to get to know Frank Peretti better, understand how to cope with those who bully, and make sure you are not doing the same. Read less
