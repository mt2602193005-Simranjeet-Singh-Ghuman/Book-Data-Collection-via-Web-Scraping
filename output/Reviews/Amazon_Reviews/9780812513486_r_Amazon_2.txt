Reviewer: D. E. Evans I read the Borgo Press trade paperback edition. This is a tight bound print with quality pulp/paper, not like a mass-market paperback. George Barr's illustrations are beautiful, especially the cover. This is Anthony's third novel (fourth if you count his bachelor's thesis, the first being Chthon). If you like Arabian Nights, this was excellent, but is not Anthony's standard fare fantasy, and had some ups and downs keeping my interest. However, it had a satisfying ending, as I've come to expect from Anthony. Read less
