Reviewer: Helcris In times of "recession" it is a book that makes you re-consider your attitude towards life! It makes you think that maybe there is a light at the end of the tunnel, because you simply decide within you that "the light is there", in other words, you should pack yourself with inner strength throughout difficult times in life and "Never Give Up"! I consider it an excellent reading. It will make you feel a different person after reading it. There are some sad moments, it makes you cry in some parts, but that's exactly what will show you that sad episodes occur in the lives of absolutely everyone, but our response to such moments is what makes the whole difference. Such moments can lead you to either success or failure, but this book makes it clear that it is your choice, it is totally up to you to make it happen! I loved this book. Thank you Ron Heagy for sharing your life! Read less
