Reviewer: Brendan A great read for those who are intermediate beginners in the Chinese language. The audio that it comes with is quite good. The book is NOT an English translation. The book is in simplified Hanzi with pinyin on top of them. The pinyin can also be blocked out with a special card that comes with the book, which I liked. One problem is that I've heard some people not receiving this special card. Also, the book has some incorrect pinyin here and there, but not so much that it will affect understanding of the book. Also, some of their definitions of some of the words do not work with the given context. Other than that, the book is good and helped me learn a lot of new vocabulary words. I recommend it to those who somewhat have an okay background with Hanzi and wants to improve his/her reading skills. Read less
