Reviewer: Charlotte K Thompson I have searched for this book for years after reading it in high school (I'm 80 years old now) thanks to Amazon it is in my hands once again and I will relish the beautiful jungle once more. I never cared for a lot of description. After reading the Green Mansions that attitude disappeared forever. The story was good too, but the walk I took through the jungle and all that life and deep green almost changed my life. I haven't lived in a large city again. Read less
