Reviewer: Heather S Another thoroughly enjoyable story. I was so caught up in the drama and - just as with all the other works I have read from Fanny Blake - there were plot twists that genuinely surprised and threw me. No spoilers here but the final twist/revelation in the story really did make me have an 'wow, I wasn't expecting that' moment. Absolute gold. A really enjoyable read. I saw someone describe this as chicklit but I don't think it is that. Chicklit makes me think of light romance with younger protagonists. This is an older woman's story about family life, dilemmas and friendship. Another hit. Read less
