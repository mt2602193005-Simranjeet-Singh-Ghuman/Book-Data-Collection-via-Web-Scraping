Reviewer: J. A. Sergent The Golden Torc pretty much picks right up on the heels of The Many-Colored Land. Julian May is an excellent writer. Her prose flows well and keeps me going through the story at a good pace. And she’s not afraid to do bad things to her characters. Felice, my favorite from the first book, changes, grows, suffers, changes more, which leads to a jaw-dropping moment - for me anyway. My favorite part of this volume was seeing the influence the exotic races had on mythology and (to an extent) geography. I’ll defintely be continuing the series. Even though it’s sf, I’d recommend the series to fans of science-fantasy and high fantasy. Read less
