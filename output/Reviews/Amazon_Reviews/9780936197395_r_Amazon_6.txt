Reviewer: kelvin253 Having suffered from lower back pain for many years, I have read a number of books on the subject, and often been disappointed. This is the first one I've read that is based on the personal experience of the author and this gives it real credibility. Brownstein describes how he tried all the usual approaches to resolving back pain and then discovered what really made a difference to him. The key points are that chronic back pain is usually caused, and certainly exacerbated, by muscle problems, and that any planned treatment should focus on understanding the back anatomy, and stretching, and exercising your back and abdominal muscles. The exercises that are included in the book are helpfully divided into sections depending on whether you are suffering from acute pain at the moment or a more general susceptibility to back pain. I have found them really helpful, easy to do and to repeat on a daily basis. After only 3 weeks of exercising (less than 5 minutes a day) I can feel a significant difference and am no longer in constant discomfort. I would highly recommend this book. Read less
