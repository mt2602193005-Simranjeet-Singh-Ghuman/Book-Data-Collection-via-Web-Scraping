Reviewer: E.P. McLean Norton is best known for her science fiction and fantasy books. What some do not know is that in the last half of her career she experimented with other genres. This book originally published in 1979 was one of three gothic romances she wrote and in my mind the best. It concerns a young woman in the 1830s rescued by wreakers in Key West, the the mysterious history of her family and the island she finds herself taken to from her sinking ship. Not Norton's best work, but a nice tale with history, villians and a touch of the paranormal. A good evening read. Read less
