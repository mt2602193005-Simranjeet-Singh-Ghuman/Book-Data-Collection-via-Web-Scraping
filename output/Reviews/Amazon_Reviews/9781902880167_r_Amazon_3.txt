Reviewer: gg in stuart This is a zombie novella that tells of a family on the run. They live in the country and have to flee their home, and they try to find their daughter in the closest town. The perspective is told by the young boy, and the story goes back and forth from the present to the past, where the relationship between the boy and his older sister takes form. They eventually get to the town and it ends in the usual horror fashion. In other words there was no unexpected ending. The one thing of note is the zombies are everything, like beetles, ants, etcetera, just as Keene's world of zombies, and they also move fast like Keene's. Other than this interesting similarity, this is only an average novella by Lebbon. White, The Unfortunate and Meat are examples of better Lebbon writing. But this is still a fun and easy read. Even though it's not his best work, its still Lebbon which means it's worth the read. Read less
