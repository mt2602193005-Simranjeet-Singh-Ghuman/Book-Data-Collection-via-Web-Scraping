Reviewer: Palak Shori Hannibal Lecter, being captivated into the clutches of reality, inspires Francis Dolarhyde to realise his 'becoming.' This book (2nd in chronological order) of Hannibal Lecter series, takes you on a journey of self-loathing criticization, and what evokes as a result of endless turmoil endured during early years of age, due to disabilities. However, "The Great Red Dragon" didn't exactly appeal my tastes, due to its nightmarish sexual energy emitting from the epochs. A good book, nevertheless. Giving this paperback, 3.5/5 stars.
