Reviewer: Marilyn J Corey Great instructional book if you are looking for something on becoming a prayer warrior. I was not sure if God is guiding me toward such as this or not. It sounds like a big job to me...more than I feel I could handle. But this book makes it more doable than I ever thought. It guides you through and helps you figure out if this is God's will for you or not. Breaks it into different kinds of warrior (did not know that) and different levels of response required (did not know this either). I would highly recommend this book if you are curious about what a prayer warrior is and what they do...even if you do not feel led to be one. Just good information. Read less
