Reviewer: Anon This is a fabulous book, inventive and original with well developed characters, well written prose, and something of a genre blur between sci-fi, fantasy and historically inspired fiction. The second in the series and a clear quickening of the pace. I first read these books as a teenager and now coming back to them find them still engrossing if in a different way: nuances that were not apparent in the first reading and a more "adult" appreciation perhaps that I'm bringing to them and the joy is that the books support both. I was worried by other reviewers prior to purchasing that the quality of rendering into the digital format would greatly detract from the reading experience, but whether it has subsequently been resolved or they are simply more sensitive to it I found that, while there were errors - a couple of instances of numerals used instead of 'L's for instance - it was not too intrusive. That's not to say that it wouldn't be preferable to have the publisher remediate the issues further but that it shouldn't stop you buying a book that lamentably is now otherwise out of print. Get it buy it enjoy it! Read less
