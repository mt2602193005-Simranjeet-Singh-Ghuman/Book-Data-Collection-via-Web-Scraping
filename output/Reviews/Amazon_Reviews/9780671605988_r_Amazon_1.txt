Reviewer: Hamid Sheikh Very good book. I have just started reading this book and am on page 30 currently. I like this book, because it is straight forward. If you are completely new to the topic of sailing, like I am, you might find some of the basic terminology that is casually used in the first few chapters confusing. The important thing is to re-read chapters before moving on and also if you don't understand a term used in the book, make sure to look it up so that you solidify your understanding of the author is trying to tell you. This is such a good book that you can actually try to get a basic boat and set sail on a small body of water after reading this book with understanding. Read less
