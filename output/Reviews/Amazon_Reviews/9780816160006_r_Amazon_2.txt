Reviewer: Tipsico A thoughtful novel of how an educated and somewhat callow young academic changes the ancien regime from a society that has so many historical, crippling roadblocks it couldn't survive. Johnathon matures during the story as he also introduces ideas developed over the several later centuries that manage to ease the stresses and save many historical catastrophes from occurring. I especially liked how his earlier introductions of innovations ripple in to the future, although I would have liked the monarchy to have evolved into something more egalitarian. I would like to see the series continue. Read less
