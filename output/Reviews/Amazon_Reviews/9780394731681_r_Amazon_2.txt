Reviewer: CGB I bought this book many years ago at a book store. I was reading it in the store and just had to buy it! The humor is droll and plays on human quirks. We all may be guilty of doing a few of them. It really allows one to laugh at the quirks of oneself or someone you may know. It brings up many things that I would personally have never thought of in a super funny way. It's meant to be ironic not mean spirited. I related to a couple of things in it. I found it so fun to have a laugh at my own expense. ahah! It also made me think how silly some things really are. Good stuff, very cleaver. Good therapy for the pessimistic you may know in your life. Good gift for someone like this... I am more optimistic by nature. I think this could perhaps really help the 'self made miserable makers' If they are not too miserable to read this.... It's a super fast read. I personally found it to be a riot to read. Fun book - all that is required is a healthy droll/ironic sense of humor. Somewhere over the years I loaned it out an it didn't return. I repurchased again and found it just as funny as the first time I read it. Read less
