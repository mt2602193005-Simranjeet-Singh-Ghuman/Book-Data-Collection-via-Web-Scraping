Reviewer: Laura I am reading novels by Zola for over a year now, so I am very familiar with his style. This book is very interesting because it tells a story in an unusual way and because Zola is able to so beautiful describe personalities and activities reminding us of people and situations in our real life. As always with the realists, there are many descriptions and while most of the times they are very beautiful, connecting us to a different world, very colourful and vivid, sometimes they can be boring, particularly for vegetarians who read long descriptions of different types of sausages and otherpork products. This is why I have not given the book a full five stars. Read less
