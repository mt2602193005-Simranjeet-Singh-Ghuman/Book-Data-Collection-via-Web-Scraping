Reviewer: K. Jain The stories chosen are good.. illustrations .. colors/ pictures are good. The length of the story is just right for 5-6yr old children.. its neither endless n long.... nor too short. However in many places very "shudh hindi" tough words are used (like chestha & prayatna) where simpler words (like koshish) could have been used. These books are for little children who have started learning... so simple hindi... no urdu words, not tough hindi words ... ideally desired. Rest they good books... priced well too. Read less
