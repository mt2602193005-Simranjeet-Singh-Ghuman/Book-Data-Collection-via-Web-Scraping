Reviewer: Jennine As the follow on from Linda Howard's book "Sarah's Child" I found it was a lovely read. In the first book Derek was partially hidden and one was left with a need to know more about him and this is Derek's romance. Well-written, magical in a way it was a book I could not put down. I would recommend reading it after you have read the first book as there is an assumption of knowledge held within that book but yes I enjoyed it. Read less
