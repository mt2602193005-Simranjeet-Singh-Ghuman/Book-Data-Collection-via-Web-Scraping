Reviewer: sandrawheeler I had a little trouble getting started on this one, but stuck with it and the book and my efforts paid off. I really enjoy early American Historical Romances. This one just finally kicked right in and fed my imagination. I felt the fear when the girls and Aunt Mary decided to spy for the Union, and laughed at the messes they ended up in. Fell in love with the Tory and the Patriot as that love triangle began. Stressed out when the spying was discovered and then just heaved a sigh of relief when then turned out the way they did. Good read, Great job Kerrelyn Sparks. Read less
