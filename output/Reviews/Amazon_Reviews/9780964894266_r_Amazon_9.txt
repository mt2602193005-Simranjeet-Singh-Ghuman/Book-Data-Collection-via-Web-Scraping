Reviewer: Sai Vaidya Vivek Vaidya's "The Corporate Birbal" is more than just a novel; it's a captivating exploration of the complexities of modern business. With his signature blend of wit and wisdom, Vaidya masterfully weaves together ancient philosophies with contemporary challenges. The story follows Sid, a young and ambitious management graduate, as he navigates the treacherous waters of the corporate world. Vaidya's insightful portrayal of corporate politics and the human element creates an entertaining and thought-provoking narrative. Read less
