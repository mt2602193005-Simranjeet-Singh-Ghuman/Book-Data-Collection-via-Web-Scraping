Reviewer: Indrajit Brilliant Heyer at her best, writing about a heroine filled with a sense of the ridiculous who finds herself alarmed but also amused at falling in love with an impostor. She is well aware that this imposture is most inappropriate, but the enigmatic impostor's charm and discernible depth of character attracts her in irresistible fashion. Meanwhile, that worthy young man, totally unaware that he has been detected, grapples with his unsuitable love for his twin's fiance-to-be, his great anxiety for that disappeared twin, and his despair over his charming but hopelessly extravagant mother. The denouement is entertaining, and fast-paced, and both the romantic lovers get their happiness, sadly to be delayed by a few months to unravel the deception and set things right, but inevitably to end in a fairy-tale ending.
