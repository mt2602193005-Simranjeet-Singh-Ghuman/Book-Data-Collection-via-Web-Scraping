Reviewer: Richard Murphy I’ve found it somewhat difficult to find good texts that a native English speaker like me, who enjoys conversing in Chinese Mandarin, but wants to enhance their Chinese reading skills, can use to support this goal. The abridged version of the first volume of Ba Jin’s famous trilogy is presented in just the right format to achieve this. And it’s a pleasure to listen to the wonderful pronunciation captured on the MP3 – a very nice bonus! Excellent value for money. Read less
