Reviewer: ♫NewHorizons♫ I finished this one up this evening, Devall's third book on Deep Ecology. Not as good as his first book with George Sessions, Deep Ecology: Living as if Nature Mattered (1985), nor his second book Simple in Means, Rich in Ends: Practicing Deep Ecology (1988). This third book of his, published in 1993, covers similar ground as his second, and doesn't seem as radical. I think he wrote it to try to convert wealthy middle class white people. Meh. 3.4 stars out of 5. Read less
