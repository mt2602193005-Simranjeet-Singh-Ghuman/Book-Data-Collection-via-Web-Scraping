Reviewer: Lime A very solid adventure story, with some dated language that marks it as a product of its time. Sandeagozu shifts between many different perspectives, but none of them are as interesting as that of the main character, Sherahi the Burmese python. The book features some unique terms such as fnasting (how snakes taste-scent the air with their tongues) and inwitting (projecting one's mind/consciousness across space), and overall it does a good job at showing the world through the eyes of its different animal species. Sandeagozu doesn't have the most in-depth characters or themes to it, with only glimpses of these greater moments showing up at times such as Manu's "stars run blind" speech, but it does succeed at making an enjoyable animal adventure with a satisfying (and even moving) ending. Alongside the much more recent Serpentia series, this is one of the only snake-focused fantasy series I've found, and so if you're interested in that niche, then this one is definitely worth a read.
