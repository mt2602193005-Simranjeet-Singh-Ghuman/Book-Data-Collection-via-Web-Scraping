Reviewer: Rekha 2.5 stars. I loved the first book - the Wild one and I would definitely recommend it. After reading it, I know I directly read 3rd book -Defiant one. I was amazed at the ratings. It was too diabolical and convoluted story. Now I was curious about Lucien, the wicked or Manipulate one. So my curiousity got better of me and I downloaded this book. I agree with one of the reviewer that though the story is narrated well, the plot is silly. It is like TV soap which doesn't make much sense We have Eva who wants American independence and is upset that when she stole Andrew's invention, Lucien got better of her and made her laughing stock so she climbs the window and steals into his room. They have confrontation and she manages to best him. Lucien tries to interfere in his sister's love life only to send her love to the death door. After his sister hates him, he approaches Eva to help find the information about the man. Eva is a widow who hates men. This is repeated again and again throughout the story.They have sex and he leaves. She comes to England to have her revenge. His brother and sisters in law invite her though she had mistreated their husbands in the previous story. They feel that Lucien deserves comeuppance, they both deserve each other so they plot their marriage. At this time, I was speechless. Seriously, the plot stopped making sense. There are no witty banters. The plot is too convoluted. I am no longer interested in reading the next book if it has similar silly plot. Overall can be avoided Read less
