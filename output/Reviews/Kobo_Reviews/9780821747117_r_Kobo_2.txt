Although a decent follow up to Broken Pieces, I think that this book lacked a little something. Heart maybe? I don't really know. I loved how the boys all finally gave up a little bit of control and finally started confiding in and relying on each other, even when they weren't sure that they could. They truly became a trio in this book, even if the two alpha males (Tristan and Mateo) continued to do dumb things under the guise of 'protecting Josiah'.
