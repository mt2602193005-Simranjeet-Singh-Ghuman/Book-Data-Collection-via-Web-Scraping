This story had me from the first page. I have been a Nora Robert fan for a long time so I appreciate her way of setting the scene quickly with character descriptions. She is also skilled at describing scents, sounds, and settings through the senses of the characters. Nora has written quite a few books about love and magic and this story is set in California and not in Ireland (except for the end) but it was no less magical. The story line sizzled with erotic tension, but also the struggle for, and the many facets of romantic and platonic love. My only negative critique is that it ended too quickly.
