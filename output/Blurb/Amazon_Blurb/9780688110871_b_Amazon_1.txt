About the Book: Lewis Carroll was the pen name of Charles Lutwidge Dodgson (1832–1898), an English writer, mathematician, and logician. Known for his imaginative storytelling and love of wordplay, Carroll created some of the most enduring characters in children's literature. Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland and its sequel Through the Looking-Glass have secured his place as a master of literary nonsense and fantasy. About the Author: Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland is a whimsical tale that follows young Alice as she falls down a rabbit hole into a fantastical world full of curious creatures and nonsensical adventures. From tea with the Mad Hatter to a peculiar croquet game with the Queen of Hearts, Alice's journey is filled with imagination, wordplay, and wonder. This timeless classic continues to enchant readers of all ages with its playful tone and surreal storytelling.
