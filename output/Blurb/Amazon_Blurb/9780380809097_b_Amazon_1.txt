A Georgian romance where the master manipulator finally meets the one woman he cannot outmaneuver. He's schemed his way through three brothers' love stories. Now it's his turn to be undone. Lucien de Montforte, Duke of Blackheath, has spent years pulling strings, engineering matches, and bending the world to his will. The wicked and wily duke answers to no one—and likes it that way. Then he arrives home to find a dangerous, vengeful woman waiting in his bed. With a pistol in hand. She has every reason to want him dead. He has every reason to be intrigued. But thanks to his scheming family's successful attempts to turn the tables, Lucien finds himself entangled in a web of his own making—and married to the one adversary he never saw coming. The master manipulator has finally met his match. And she's not afraid to pull the trigger. Wickedly entertaining and utterly romantic— The Wicked One is a Georgian romance for readers who love scheming dukes, fierce heroines, and the delicious moment when the puppet master becomes the puppet. Perfect for readers who love: – Scheming dukes undone by fierce heroines – Enemies in the bedroom turned lovers – The satisfying fall of a master manipulator – Meddling families who finally get their revenge
