Start this big conversation with openness and honesty. This is a kids’ book about divorce. When divorce happens, it happens to everyone in the family, and kids are left with a lot of questions. This is a tough conversation, and some of the answers can hurt, even if they’re necessary. This is why it’s so important to have the conversation in the right way. This book was made to help kids aged 5-9 understand divorce – and what it means for them. It can help kids begin to understand what to expect when parents go their separate ways, and is written by a parent who has been there before.
