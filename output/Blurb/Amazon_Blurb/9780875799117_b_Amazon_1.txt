Who can consider the tumultuous and catastrophic events prophesied to occur in the last days without being at least a little bit frightened? In this book, nine respected Latter-day Saint scholars take a calm and rational look at the signs of the times, pointing out that those who are prepared need not fear. The authors explore such topics as: The safety there is in belonging to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints The admonitions and counsel given us by latter-day prophets How to avoid deception The ways in which the Book of Mormon can help us face the tests of the last days How to detect false Christs The blessing it is to be of the house of Israel What it will be like to live during the Millennium Why we needn't be fearful
