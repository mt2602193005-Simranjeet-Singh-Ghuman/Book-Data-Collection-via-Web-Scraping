We share our planet with a teeming variety of plants and animals. The kinkajous, horned beetles, seladangs (giant Asian ox) and capybaras (the world’s largest rodent) of the title are just a sampling from the almost 200 entries found herein. The authors, Jeanne Hanson and Deane Morrison, introduce us to the strangest and most remarkable forms of life found on earth, deepening our awareness and appreciation of the incredible richness in which the world around us abounds.
