A rural saga in the Thomas Hardy tradition. An indomitable fight for land. An old cottage, a few acres of moorland and some sheep on the farthest side of England: to fiery destitute Rachel Jedder they are beyond riches, a home to risk her life crossing the unknown wilds of the country to reach. However, even the harshness of her pauper childhood cannot prepare her for the struggle ahead -the lovers and enemies, tragedies and triumphs, wealth followed by misfortune, the gambles risking everything hard won. Yet Rachel succeeds, with fervour and humour and spirit and brings to lusty fruition her greatest love - Jedder's Land, the inalienable soil of her roots and the ground on which she will build a thriving dynasty...
