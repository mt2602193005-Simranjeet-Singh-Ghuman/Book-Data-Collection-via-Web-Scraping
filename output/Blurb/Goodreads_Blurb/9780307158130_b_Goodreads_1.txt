This book has been designed to show children the amazing variety of mammal and bird life. Through his accurate and fun illustrations and thoroughly readable text, the author conveys much information and answers many of the questions that children have a way of asking: which camels have two humps and which have one? How can you tell the difference between African and Asian elephants? Which animal can run the fastest? The book is intended for children from 6 to 12 years old, but will appeal to anyone who is interested in animals. The book was originally published under the title Joe Kaufman's Wings, Paws, Hoofs And Flippers: A Book About Animals.
