The lawman was captured by another man's wife! Two high-profile guests die under suspicious circumstances at the Zimmermans' resort in Apache Springs, Arizona. Deputy U.S. Marshal Jed Mallory comes home to investigate, only to find that his old flame Gabriela - now Mrs. Brian Zimmerman - is one of the suspects. Jed knows Gabriela's marriage is in trouble, but he also knows Gabriela is far too loyal to leave. Is her husband a legitimate murder suspect, or is Jed just hoping to prove him guilty in order to win Gabriela back? Suddenly the lines between right and wrong become blurred, but Jed, a lawman to the end, determines to uncover the truth.
