Now placed at No. 1 in the 'Permanent Collection' of the Erotica Readers Association, and their 'Book of the Month' when first published, A Journey Round A Darker Sun is the story of a beautiful young girl called Claire. She meets a man, and falls in love. Throughout the year the story tells, she tries to change herself to be the girl he wants. Paul is devastating, wealthy, deadly. Paul is everything she never imagined, never dreamed of. He gives her sex, but not his love. He gives her presents, not his time. He also gives her discipline, as and when he thinks she needs it. Claire is impulsive, flirtatious and full of life; she is used to men falling at her feet, and finds Paul's rules bewildering. Claire has many friends, many admirers. Of all the young men who follow her in awe, her favourite is Tristan. In every waking moment, he is thinking of her. Trustingly she shares her secrets with him. Foolishly he breaks that trust, and finds Claire uses dreams to break him. And so there is a planetary system ~ Claire round Paul, and round Claire, Tristan.
