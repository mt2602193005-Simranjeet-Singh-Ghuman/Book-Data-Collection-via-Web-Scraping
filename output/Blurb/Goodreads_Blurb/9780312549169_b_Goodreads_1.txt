In this yummy book Barbara Albright and Leslie Weiner present more than 75 mouth-watering recipes guaranteed to satisfy even the most discerning muffin-lover's taste. Included -Sweet blueberry, chocolate chip, papaya cashew, marzipan raspberry muffins -Savory beer cheese, Irish soda bread, pepperoni pizza muffins - lemon glaze, nutted-cheese spread, tahini peanut-butter spread Plus many, many more. There's even a special section on creating your own muffins! Everyone knows there's nothing like hot, homemade muffins, so get cooking...and bon appetit!
