COULD THEY REWRITE HISTORY? Sarah Crewes was at it again, creating chaos with her archival diggings. First she discovered that Ramsey Falls was celebrating its tricentennial one year too early. Now she was about to unearth an even more startling historical tidbit. The true ownership of Riveredge, the Creweses' ancestral property, was in question. And worse, the legitimate heir seemed to be none other than the disquieting Australian architect, Tyler Lassiter. Sarah wasn't sure why Tyler suddenly appeared in town, but she suspected he was out to right some old wrongs--and some new ones.
