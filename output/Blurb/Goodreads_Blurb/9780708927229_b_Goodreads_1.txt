An enchanting romance set in an unforgettable corner of New Zealand. To Monique Belfield, Aunt Amabel's legacy meant freedom - to fulfil her dream of proving her descendance from the Beauchamps ofPort Beauchamp. Her return to the corner of New Zealand that was forever France meant the return of Edouard Fergusson into her life- the Saint George who rescued her from a pointless romance. Soon,Aunt Amabel's words "to bring you joy" took on a deeper meaning.
