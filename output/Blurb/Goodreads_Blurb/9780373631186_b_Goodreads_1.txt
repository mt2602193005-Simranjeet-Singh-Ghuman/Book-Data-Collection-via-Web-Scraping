All Dr. Adam Fletcher had to do was sign a paper saying he was willing for nurse Sarah Tremaine to adopt his nephew Sam. Sarah had assumed that because Fiona's family hadn't seemed to care about her before her death, they wouldn't want Sam, either. But Adam hadn't known about his sister Fiona's baby - at nine months old, Sam did look amazingly like him - and Adam wasn't at all sure he wanted to give up any rights to the child. Within a very short time, he wasn't sure he wanted to give up Sarah, either....
