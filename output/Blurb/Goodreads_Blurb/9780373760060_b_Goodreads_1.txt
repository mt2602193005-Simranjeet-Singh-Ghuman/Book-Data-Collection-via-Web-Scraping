The woman who brings the cradle to a Tallchief man will fill it with his babies... Once Duncan Tallchief had desperately wanted to find his family's missing heirloom baby cradle--until he'd sworn off getting hitched and having babies. But then, the sexiest single mother in Amen Flats, Wyoming, decided to change the hardened cowboy's mind. So she brought the cradle to Tallchief Mountain... The Tallchiefs: One family finds the kind of love that legends -- and little ones -- are made of.
