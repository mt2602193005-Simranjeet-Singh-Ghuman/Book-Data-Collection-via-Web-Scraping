Micah McKendrick was Accustomed to Solitude A pilot in Vietnam, Micah had returned from the conflict shattered by the violence he had witnessed - and perpetrated. Withdrawing to Michigan's remote Upper Peninsula, he devoted himself to his solitary study of endangered birds. Simon and Rachel feared their brother's psychic wounds would never heal - until Carrie Granger came to work at the research compound. Her love and faith in him aroused emotions Micah thought he'd never feel again. But Carrie was pregnant with another man's child, and Micah was still afraid of his capacity for violence. Once before, he had destroyed someone he loved. Dared he risk loving again?
