More than just an entertainer, a bard is a spiritual teacher who cements bonds between those for whom he or she performs. A Bard's Book of Pagan Songs, hand-lettered by Hugin the Bard, is a unique treasure that will enhance any feast, festival, or other magical gathering. It includes nearly 60 completely original songs in condensed lead sheets that include the key signature, chords and melody line of the first verse, plus the chorus of each song, lyrics, and chord charts. Each song is prefaced by a story or anecdote interwoven with Wiccan lore to add context to the lyrics.
