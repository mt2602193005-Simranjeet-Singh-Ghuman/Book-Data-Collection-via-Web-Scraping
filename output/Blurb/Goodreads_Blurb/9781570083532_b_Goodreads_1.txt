The Ten Commandments were given to Moses on Mount Sinai some 3500 years ago, but the principles they encompass reach back to Adam's day. Fundamental to all gospel dispensations, these commandments are reinforced by Restoration doctrine. In user-friendly style, eleven gospel scholars here address the subject of the Ten Commandments in our day. One author sets the stage with an instructive introduction; ten others each discuss one commandment in detail, generally indicating the impact the commandments had on society and customs in the Old Testament days, then moving to modern times. Much information and good counsel, perhaps even warning, emerges
