Grace Holbrook had thought she couldn't have children. But now she was wondrously, amazingly pregnant after just one night with Ford McCabe. She was delighted by the news, but not so delighted when Ford ordered her to marry him. Ford had always wanted Grace. Even back when he was the town rebel and should have known better. Now a millionaire businessman, he wasn't going to wait any longer to make Grace his wife. Even if he had to pretend for a while it was just for the sake of the baby....
