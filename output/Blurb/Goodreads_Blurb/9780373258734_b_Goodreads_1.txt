SWEET TALKER #3 -- BEAU STUART Prodigal son and bad boy extraordinaire, Beau Stuart is finally coming home. He knows the town isn't anxious to welcome back the rebel they drove out years ago. But one person is very happy to see him. And she's about to make him an offer he can't refuse.... Lily Matthews is tired of being a "good" girl. It's time she learned what it is to be a real woman--with a real man. So when she hears that sexy-as-sin scoundrel Beau Stuart is coming home, she knows he's the one to help her. After all, what hot-blooded male could possibly turn down some good old-fashioned Southern comforts...? Sweet Talkin' Guys: All talk -- and all action!
