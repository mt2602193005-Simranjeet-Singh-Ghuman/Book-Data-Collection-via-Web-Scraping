This LDS pocket book is useful for anyone who is giving a lesson or talk and needs a quick reference to a certain gospel topic. The book includes hundreds of topics listed in alphabetical order and is easily accessible. It provides quick-easy access to scriptural references & quotations from apostles & prophets. It also has a summary of World Religions. The book includes a detailed Plan of Salvation Chart, Chronology of the LDS Church, Archeological Evidences of the Book of Mormon, and much more. Examples of topics include Apostasy, Evolution, Baptism, Chastity, Resurrection, Fasting, Performing Ordinances, Marriage, Millennium, Parables, Godhead, Jehovah is Christ, Thoughts, Leadership, Anger, Listening, Conversion, Catholics, Seventh Day Adventist, Judaism, & much more.
