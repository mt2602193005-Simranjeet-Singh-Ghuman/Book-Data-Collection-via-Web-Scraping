An outback marriage? Lydia had been thrilled by her temporary assignment on an Australian cattle station-- until she came face-to-face with Joe Jordan on her first day! Tough, sexy Joe: the man Lydia's impulsive sister had planned to seduce.... Only, Joe made it clear that it was Lydia, not her sister, who intrigued him. And he wanted to do more than work with her -- he wanted to marry her! Joe's passion overwhelmed and excited Lydia, but did he just want a convenient wife?
