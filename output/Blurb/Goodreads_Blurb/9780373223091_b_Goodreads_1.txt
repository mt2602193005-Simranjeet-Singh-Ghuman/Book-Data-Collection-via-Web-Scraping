Sister, Sister Alexa Widmire's sister Mia made the fatal mistake of looking for love in the arms of a man other than her mob boss husband. Now Mia would have to pay the price...unless Alexa helped her. Alexa agreed to pose as her sister just long enough to get into the mansion, grab Mia's baby and run. Unfortunately, as she bolted for the door, Alexa ran straight into the arms of Damas Silva - Mia's new bodyguard, hired by her husband! Damas didn't give his client a chance to think, whisking her and her baby off to an isolated resort. But while he fought a dangerous attraction to "Mrs. Santini," Damas wondered if this smart mob bride knew his own secret.
