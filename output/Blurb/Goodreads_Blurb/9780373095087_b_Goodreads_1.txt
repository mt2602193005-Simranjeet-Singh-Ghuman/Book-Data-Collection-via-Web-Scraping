Ex-probation officer Nathan Brodie was man enough to fight for his ideals. He wouldn't pander to the investigator assessing his ranch for troubled juveniles. He knew Kristin Price was one tough woman, that she could make or break his dream-he never suspected he'd soon be asking her to share it! Unfortunately, Brodie's fondest dream was Kristin's worst nightmare. The boy's pranks, the ranch itself, resurrected terrifying memories, undermining her trademark courage. Though she found succor in Brodie's arms, not even his towering strength seemed equal to her inner fears. Could she ever brave the shadows of her past and stand tall in the sunlight of this man's love?
