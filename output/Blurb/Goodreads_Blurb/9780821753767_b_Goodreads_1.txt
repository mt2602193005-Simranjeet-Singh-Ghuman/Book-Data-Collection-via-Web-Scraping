In a tempestuous age of clashing steel and bold hearts... When Julianna Devereux, Countess of Rosmar, refuses to wed her young cousin, the willful beauty is banished to the court of England's Eleanor of Aquitaine. There, she is to await papal orders to marry. But Eleanor's is an enlightened court founded on the ideals of true love...and Julianna vows that she will settle for nothing less. Two lovers will be bound by their forbidden passion... One of King Henry's fiercest and most devoted knights, Stephen de Mandeville desires Julianna from the moment he beholds her. But he is a man tormented by a dark and unforgiving past...and Julianna has just been promised to another.
