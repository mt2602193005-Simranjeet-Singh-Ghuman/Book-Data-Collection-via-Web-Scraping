From the author of more than forty novels. Emma Vurney and her friend Lucy are excited by their career plans to run a riding school in Sussex. The girls' plans are thwarted byLucy's brother Philip, and to make matters worse her love life takes a blow when the elusive Nick Barbury does not return her feelings. Emma learns the hard way that her impulsive nature can cause both hurt and misjudgement, and when she stops rushing headlong at the world she finds real love from a surprising direction. A delightful new Rainwood story.
