As a nave college student, Rachel Tims realized too late that Daniel Gaines only married her to spite his wealthy family. Although she learned to dress impeccably, speak softly, and smile like a lady, she was never accepted as a true Georgia ""Gaines"". Now, widowed and alone in her forties, Rachel is finally ready to leave her world of dull charity work and lazy country club afternoons behind. In addition to a career of her own, she also wants something a bit more shocking... Celebrated ex-football star Quentin Rawls has returned to Augusta a hero. A national celebrity with two failed marriages behind him, Quentin is rich, rugged and years younger than Rachel. As he awakens a passion that sends her heart soaring, Rachel is faced with risking everything for a feeling she never thought to have.
