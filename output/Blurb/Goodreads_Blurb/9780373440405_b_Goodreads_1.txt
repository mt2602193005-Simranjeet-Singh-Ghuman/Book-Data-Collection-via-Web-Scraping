The ingredients for sexual chemistry... One feisty brunette Caterer Carly Westin's love life held no sizzle. Then while serving a romantic dinner for two, she witnessed a crime of passion. Suddenly her bland-as-tofu life was filled with spice when mouthwatering agent Jack Brannigan was assigned to protect her from the killer. But who would protect her from Jack? One hungry man... Jack had never had to protect a witness quite like Carly before. Opinionated, provocative and luscious, she was driving him crazy with desire. Determined to serve and protect, Jack went undercover in Carly's kitchen. That's when things really started to heat up....
