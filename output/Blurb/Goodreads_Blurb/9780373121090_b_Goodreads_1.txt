Revenge . . .Why has Nikolas Petronides offered Paige a job on his Greek island for the summer? Could it be that in Paige's precarious financial situation, he's finally found a means of revenge? Nikolas has obviously not forgiven her for walking out on him four years ago. But how could she have stayed after he'd so shockingly accused her of using her youthful beauty and innocence as a seductive means of extracting money from him? Once a welcome guest in his home, now she'd be a lowly paid employee. What, exactly, will Nikolas be expecting from her . . .?
