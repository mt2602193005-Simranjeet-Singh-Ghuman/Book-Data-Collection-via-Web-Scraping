She'd hired him as an escort... The wedding was to be a major social event - Georgina just couldn't face it on her own. How could she sit there and watch her ex-fiance marry her beautiful, shallow cousin? A desperate solution was needed: a male escort! But he'd become the father of her child! Callum Stewart was perfect. Gorgeous, dynamic - he certainly made heads turn at the wedding! And he made Goergina's heart turn over on the wedding night. But it was supposed to be a temporary arrangement. How could she tell Callum that their wedding-night affair had resulted in a baby?
