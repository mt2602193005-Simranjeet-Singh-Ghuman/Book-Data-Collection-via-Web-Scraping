Here comes the bride... Eliza Richards knew she had no business trying on that million-dollar wedding gown. It didn't look like it should cost a million bucks, so what was all the fuss about? But when she put it on, she knew. It shimmered, it sparkled - it made her look...well, gorgeous. And it made her see and image of a groom...her groom. There goes the groom... Five minutes before his own wedding, MacKenzie Courtland found himself literally attached - tuxedo button enmeshed in lace - to Eliza, the most beautiful bride he could ever imagine. Problem was, she wasn't his bride! How was he possibly going to explain two brides at the altar?
