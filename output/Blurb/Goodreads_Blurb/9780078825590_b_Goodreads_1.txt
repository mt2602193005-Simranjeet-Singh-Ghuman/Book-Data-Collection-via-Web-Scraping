HTML is the publishing language of the World Wide Web--HTML 4 adds enhancements in several areas to make publishing for the Web quick and easy. A comprehensive resource for all versions of HTML--elements covered include tables, frames, forms, and image maps, all of which continue to be supported by the browsers and the World Wide Web Consortium. Focus placed on listing elements, keywords, and other reference material--fewer pages are devoted to instructing readers on how to use HTML.
