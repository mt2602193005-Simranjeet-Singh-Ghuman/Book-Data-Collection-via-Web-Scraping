In the early 20th century, the field of aviation was dominated by men. Few women flew, the most notable of whom were Amelia Earhart and "Petite Laura" Ingalls. In 1936 the Bendix Cup Transcontinental Air Race was finally opened to women participants. On Wings of Love is the story of Maggie Rockwell, a beautiful and ambitious aeronautics student who dreams of competing in the famous Bendix Cup Air Race.
