A family held together by tradition - and torn apart by love! As beautiful and unique as the natural gem she was named for, Pearl Resnick always stood out in the crowded world of New York City's Lower East Side. And as she blossomed into womanhood, she caught the eye of more than one man - but she vowed to make a better life for herself. So she chose to marry someone who could fulfill her dreams of wealth and splendor, even though Pearl knew that his world was filled with danger and deception... In a journey that would take her from the squalor of a ghetto to the elegance of Central Park West and all of its finery, Pearl triumphs over the challenges of life - until she must face the cruel twisted fate of passion, and the betrayal of her own heart!
