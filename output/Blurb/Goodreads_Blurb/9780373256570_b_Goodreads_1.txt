In the summer of 1970, four young men—Ethan Roberts, Zeke Blackstone, and brothers Eric and Jack Shannon—shared apartment 1G at the Wilshire Arms. One night during a wild party, Eric Shannon committed suicide. Or so everyone thinks. Shortly after that fateful night at the Wilshire Arms, a young, single woman—mother to three-month-old Willow Ryan—was killed by a hit-and-run-driver. Now, 24 years later, Willow is still trying to untangle her past and find her biological father. In need of help, she hires hunky private detective Steve Hart. But when the trail leads them to the Wilshire Arms and the four young men who were at the party the night Eric Shannon died, they uncover more passion and scandal than either of them bargained for.
