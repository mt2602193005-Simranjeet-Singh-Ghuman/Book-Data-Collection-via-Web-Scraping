NOWHERE TO RUN Police detective Steven Savage knew the woman was trouble the moment he spotted her on the L.A.-bound plane. Beautiful, elegant California heiress Amanda Wainwright was a real class act--an irresistible risk a tough cop from the streets just couldn't afford to take. Then she became the leading suspect in a murder case ... and only he could prove her innocence. The privileges of wealth hadn't saved Amanda's heart from getting broken, and now caution was etched deep within her. Every instinct told her to flee from Steven, but her freedom was in jeopardy, and he was her only ally. Dare she trust the one man whose searing gaze aroused such dangerous desire? The heat was on to find a killer ... before Amanda and Steven got burned.
