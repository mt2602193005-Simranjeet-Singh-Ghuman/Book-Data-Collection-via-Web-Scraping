From the front endpaper: “This book is ‘… a cookery school for those who want to learn how to entertain with ease and elegance. From his extensive experience of running cookery courses, John takes you through the skills and methods which will build your confidence and inspire you to cook new and exciting dishes with aplomb. It’s all laid on: three sumptuous menus for each of the four seasons; complete plans for a vegetarian buffet and a brunch; advice on wine; shopping lists for each menu; and an invaluable countdown plan for telling you when to prepare and cook the various dishes. With full-colour photographs showing you just how the dishes should look.”
