Creating retirement income used to be simple: you would invest the nest egg you'd saved during your working years conservatively, and reap 10% return on your savings annually until you died seven years later. But the rules of retirement living have changed drastically: you can now expect to live for 20 years or more in retirement; at the same time, interest rates have fallen to new lows; and government pension programs (CPP and OAS) are no longer to be relied upon. This book does what You Can't Take It With You did for estate planning: provides easy-to-understand information on a complex subject to help you maximize your income and minimize tax. It explains what to do with your nest egg after you retire, how to invest it then, and how to make sure it can indeed last to carry you through the rest of your life.
