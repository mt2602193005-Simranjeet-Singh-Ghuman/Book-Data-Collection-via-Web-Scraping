This companion book to HYMNS OF FAITH AND INSPIRATION tells the stories behind the composition of America's best-loved hymns and includes the complete words and music to more than thirty-five hymns. Learn the origin of old favorites such as "Count Your Blessings," "Joyful, Joyful, We Adore Thee," and many more. Breathtaking, full-color scenic photographs complement the stories and hymns.
