Strange Angels tells the story of three siblings thrown together by the death of their father, Heywood Bennett. Forced to manage his sprawling prairie empire together, the Bennett children must find a way to get along despite their longstanding rivalries. "Connect to and enlarge upon the myth of the Wild West . . . and vividly portray cowboy life in all of its degradation, violence, and romance."-- Chicago Tribune A New York Times Notable Book, Strange Angels is a mesmerizing evocation of the contemporary American West.
