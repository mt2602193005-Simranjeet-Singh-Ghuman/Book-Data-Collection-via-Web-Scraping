Her heart chose to ignore her mind. The Burney household always bustled, what with a doctor father, three daughters, an aunt and one rather large dog. But life had never seemed hectic or confusing--until Daniel Elliot came to town. Rachel, the eldest Burney daughter, took an immediate dislike to the rugged Canadian, the new squire of Branford. But was her glamorous sister Carola falling in love with him? Of course, it made no difference to Rachel; she had Edward. . .
