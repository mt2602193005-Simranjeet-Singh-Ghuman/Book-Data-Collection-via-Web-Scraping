Beat the boring dinner blues with Betty Crocker's 1-2-3 Dinner! In this book you'll find all the answers to meet your quick cooking needs. The recipes are limited to three concise, easy-to-follow steps, so you won't find any elaborate or complicated techniques to follow. Even though these recipes cheat on time, they make up for with big flavor. Who says short recipes mean bland meals? You'll find mouthwatering chicken, fish, beef and pork dishes--plus sensational soups, salads, pasta, pizza and more. Choose from homestyle favorites such as Chicken Stew over Biscuits or Meatballs and Mashed Potatoes. Or why not try something fun and new such as Thai Turkey Burrito Sandwiches, Polenta with Italian Vegetables or Pork Cutlets and Apple, Walnut and Gongonzola Salsa? You'll love every bite of Linguine with Tuscan Vegetable Alfredo Sauce or Chicken Enchilada Pizzas. Each recipe has been approved by the Betty Crocker Kitchens, so you can count on each dish tasting absolutely delicious.
