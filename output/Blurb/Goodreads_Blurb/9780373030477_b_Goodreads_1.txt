Some fires burn out of control . . Firefighters call them "fully involved." And fully involved described Gina Lindsay's feelings for her ex-husband, Captain Grady Simpson of the Salt Lake City Fire Department. She'd never stopped loving him, even though Grady's passion for firefighting--and Gina's fear of it--had driven them apart three years earlier. Now she'd conquered her fear and become as fascinated with firefighting as Grady was himself. She returned to Salt Lake a qualified professional. hoping for a second chance with her ex-husband. Grady's love for her hadn't died out--Gina felt sure of that. Then why was he so reluctant to accept her presence at Station One?
