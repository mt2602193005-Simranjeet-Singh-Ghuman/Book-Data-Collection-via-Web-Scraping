A detailed, illustrated guide to the secrets of Wicca as practiced by thousands of followers, The Witch's Book of Magical Ritual provides complete information on how to design and perform specific rituals. The book helps any reader to understand the ingredients, the sequence, and the technique before performing any ritual.Gavin Frost and Yvonne Frost show readers how Wiccan ritual can be used for religion, healing, and life improvement, such as bringing good health and healing to others, summoning forth objects of desire, finding a life-long companion, and acquiring greater spiritual understanding. Throughout, the authors describe transformations that people have experienced and relate many real-life stories about people who have succeeded in reaching their objectives with the help of ritual.A perfect companion to The Witch's Magical Handbook and The Magic Power of White Witchcraft, The Witch's Book of Magical Ritual is a dynamic guide to achieving self-empowerment, health, happiness, and wealth through the power of magic.
