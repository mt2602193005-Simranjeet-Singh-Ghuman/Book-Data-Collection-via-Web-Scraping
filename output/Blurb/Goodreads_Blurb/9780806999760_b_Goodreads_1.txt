If you're ready to take your passion for rubber stamping to the next level and are looking for a new crafting challenge, try these innovative techniques-you'll use them again and again. They're easy to learn, yet produce sophisticated results on cards. Combining a brayered background with colored tissue paper or garden leaves can be spectacular, especially if you develop an attractive layout, color copy it, then stamp directly onto it. A resist method will have you working with embossing, markers, and dye ink. Or, fill in the open areas of your stamped image with dimensional products like colored glitter glue. With these exceptional ideas, you're only limited by your imagination. The author lives in Howell, NJ. 132 pages (all in color), 8 7/8 x 8 7/8. NEW IN PAPERBACK
