If only her aunt hadn't planned it this way Most women would have jumped at the opportunity to accompany powerful London art dealer Benet Saker on a business trip to Bermuda, so why was Kirsten reluctant? She knew her aunt had always intended her to become Benet's wife, and Bermuda was an island made for lovers.... Why couldn't she bring herself to attempt what was expected of her and seduce Benet into marriage?
