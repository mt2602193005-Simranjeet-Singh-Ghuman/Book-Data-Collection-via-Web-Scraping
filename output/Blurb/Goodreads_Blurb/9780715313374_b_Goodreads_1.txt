Sam Hawkins has used his considerable skill as a cross stitch designer and his love of the natural world to bring together a stunning collection of seasonal designs. Throughout the year there are many cross stitch occasions. Sam has taken these moments and created a seasonal collection of projects that stitchers of all skill levels will enjoy. Each of the four chapters has a sampler to show the beauty of the season and is packed with stunning designs, including a breakfast set in yellow and lavender for Spring, picnic napkins in strawberry red for Summer, cushions to celebrate the gold and orange of Autumn, festive designs in red and green for Winter. At the end of each seasonal chapter is a library of smaller designs that give stitchers a wonderful year-round resource for cards and keepsakes.
