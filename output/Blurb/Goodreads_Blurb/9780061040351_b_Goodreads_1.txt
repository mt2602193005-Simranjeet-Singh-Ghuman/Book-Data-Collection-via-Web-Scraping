16th CENTURY MEXICO A LAND OF SEETHIGN REBELLION, STORMY INTRIGUE AND SAVAGE DESIRE Forced to flee her dangerous Spanish homeland, sheltered Elena de la Rosa was not prepared for the sights that greeted her when she and her family arrived in primitive Cuitlapan. Yet in the land of fierce warriors, one man stood out above the rest. He was Tizoc Santiago, the Aztec prince whose smoldering gaze ignited a hunger impossible to deny. Consumed by her raging need for the vanquishing Eagle Knight, the headstrong noblewoman defied sacred tradition and dared to surrender her heart. Against a world of fiery splendor and exotic tribal customs, amid the perils and intrigue of the Mexican court, the two lovers risked their lives in the name of honor and freedom -- and triumphed in a love for all time.
