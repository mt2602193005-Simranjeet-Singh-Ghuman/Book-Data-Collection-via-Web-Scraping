Dr. James Webber examines the X ray, disguising the private terror that tortures his soul. It had been easier in Vietnam to fake his way out - Vietnam, where the crazy deception had begun. But now, he's living a lie. He is not really a surgeon - not a doctor of any kind. he has never been to medical school. And his name is not James Webber. There is no turning back now. He has lied to too many doctors. He has treated too many patients. So he wears the mask of a surgeon and holds the sharp, gleaming instrument in his trembling hands. And Dr. James Webber's "career" is about to explode into unthinkable terror.
