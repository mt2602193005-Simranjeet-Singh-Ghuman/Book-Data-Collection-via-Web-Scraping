Pip is born in an old army boot on an abandoned farm in the Prairies. Barely two months old, he finds himself alone in the world and trapped on a freight train heading east. He ends up in a field on the Atlantic coast, but soon discovers that his new home is dangerous and unfriendly. Then he meets Meg, an enchanting deer mouse who walks with a limp. Together they share many adventures and meet many animals--friend and foe alike! Written as a novel for 6 to 10 year olds. Beautifully illustrated.
