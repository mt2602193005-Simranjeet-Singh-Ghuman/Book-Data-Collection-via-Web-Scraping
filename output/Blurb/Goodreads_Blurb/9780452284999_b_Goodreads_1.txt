From the candidates' views on key issues such as foreign policy, the death penalty, health care, taxes, defense spending, trade, education, and energy to their worst flip-flops and favorite buzzwords, to their pet issues and personal quirks, Slate's Field Guide to the Candidates 2004 delves deep to answer the questions readers didn't even know they had. This is the sources for every American who hopes to be truly informed about the 2004 presidential election.
