"Change your thoughts and you change your world." Many books will tell you that you can achieve your goals; that you can find the work you love and flourish in it; that you can enjoy close supportive relationships; and that you can experience confidence and peace every day. But positive thinking is only half the battle. In the real world, it takes concrete suggestions and practical steps to make change happen. Organized into six broad categories for quick access and immediate insight, it covers 125 distinct personal goals.
