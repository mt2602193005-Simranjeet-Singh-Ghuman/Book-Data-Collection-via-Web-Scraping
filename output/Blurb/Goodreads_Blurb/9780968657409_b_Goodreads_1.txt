Val Virgo isn't an activist; he's an entertainer. So when he receives an on-air death threat from a self-described serial bomber the wise cracking talk show host laughs it off as a sick joke. When he wakes up in a hospital bed, having narrowly survived the bombing of his apartment, he is no longer amused. Why would an Aboriginal terrorist group go after a glorified disk jockey? Or was this a "copy cat" bomber trying to settle a personal score under cover of an "Indian" blanket? In search of the answer investigative reporter Virgo (a.k.a. Izzy Miller) takes a trip down memory lane. He revisits the mean streets of his mispent youth - finding love along the way - with the "mad bomber" one step behind. The race against time comes to an explosive conclusion as Virgo unmasks his long-lost enemy in the shadow of his doorstep - one hour to late.
