With 101 Science Projects you have all you need to bring the excitement of hands-on science into your classroom. From plants & animals to the solar system, from maghets to weather, you'll find these fascinating classroom projects & activities are just right to extend & enrich your science program. It includes special activities suitable for children of all ages, reproducible activity sheets & lab reports to reinforce science concepts & skills, clear & complete instruuctions for projects that require only simple, easy-to-find materials. Your students will have a ball as they make gears from bottle tops, build a volcano, & create crystal gardens. 101 Science Projects will turn your classroom into a living laboratory where students learn to think like scientists, explore fascinating new concepts, & experience the fun & excitement of scientific discovery!
