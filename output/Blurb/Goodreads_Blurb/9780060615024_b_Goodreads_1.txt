The message of this book is that the traditional way of meeting between women and men is neither the most satisfying not the most human way of meeting for either sex. The dominance-submission pattern, the one up - one down relationship, cannot provide the conditions for the most open, growing, sexually satisfying relationship a couple can develop. There is another way, a better way, and that is what this book is about. The "middle" is the place where both men and women can get their needs met, individually and together.
