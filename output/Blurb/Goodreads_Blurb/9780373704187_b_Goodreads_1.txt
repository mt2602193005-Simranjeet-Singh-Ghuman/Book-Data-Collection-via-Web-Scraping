Simon McKendrick had a mission Fifteen years after the fall of Saigon, his sister Rachel and his brother Micah were being held prisoner in Vietnam by a corrupt government minister. The ransom for their release: a flawless pearl necklace. Simon found the ideal courier to smuggle the pearls into Asia: Annie Simpson, a nurse practitioner whose trip to Vietnam in aid of Amerasian orphans made a perfect cover... Simon's request drew Annie into an exotic and dangerous adventure. And though he promised to protect her, Annie couldn't help but suspect that the mysterious Simon McKendrick could he pretty dangerous himself.
