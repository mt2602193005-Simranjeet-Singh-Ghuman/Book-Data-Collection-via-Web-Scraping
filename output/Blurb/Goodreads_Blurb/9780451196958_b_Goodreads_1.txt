New York Times bestselling authors Diana Gabaldon, Faye Kellerman, and Eileen Goudge have joined together with nine nationally recognized writers of women's fiction to create this poignant collection of original short fiction and non-fiction memoirs. This unique anthology features twelve tales celebrating the relationship between fathers and daughters, and includes family photographs of the authors with their fathers. Just in time for Father's Day, Fathers & Daughters is the perfect gift.
