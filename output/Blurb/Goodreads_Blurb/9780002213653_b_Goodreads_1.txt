Now that the stealing of art-treasures is Italy's growth industry what more probable than that political terrorists should get in on the act? The IRA for instance. And what happier hunting-ground than the Vatican collections? But even the most expertly planned operation can be thrown out of gear by the unforseen. The rock on which the throne of St Peter is founded proves more than a figure of speech. And the new pope, the first German to be elected since the Renaissance, stumbles innocently into the path of a gang of ruthless men. The alarming consequences are further bedevilled by a homicidal maniac bent on revenge.
