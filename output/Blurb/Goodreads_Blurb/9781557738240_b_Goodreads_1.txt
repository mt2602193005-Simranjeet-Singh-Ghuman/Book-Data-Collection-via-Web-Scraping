A former librarian, Emily left civilized Connecticut for the untamed West and discovered an untamed passion that burned inside her like the Arizona sun. Zack had been hired by Emily's uncle to keep order on his ranch. But controlling his desire for curvaceous newcomer was much more difficult. The well bred Eastern lady and the wild Western man had nothing in common except a searing, deeply felt longing for each other that would take them to the heights of ecstasy and beyond.
