Is the Bible a closed book...or is God still revealing Himself to us today? This is the question Catherine Marshall asked when she sought to confirm her faith. In a violent and turbulent world, it is tempting to doubt a loving God who would permit good people to suffer--healing some while forsaking others. Candid, relevant, insightful and affirmative. Something More provides sober, intelligent reflection on Divine will--and offers a comforting road back to the One to whom we can all turn in troubling times.
