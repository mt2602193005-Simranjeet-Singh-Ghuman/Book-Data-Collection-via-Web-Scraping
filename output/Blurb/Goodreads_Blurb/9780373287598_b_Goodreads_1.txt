A Woman's Place Driven to carve a piece of the wilderness for her own, Amelia Daniels would let no man deter her. Neither the straitlaced Elders of Puritan Boston nor a roving seafarer with hidebound ideas of a female's "proper place"! Even one as compelling as Captain Garrick Marlowe, who stirred the slumbering wanton within her.. . . Wedded to sun and sail, Garrick Marlowe knew no woman's charms could silence the siren call of the sea. Free to follow the wind, he was captain of his soul. Until Amelia Daniels braved society's censuring eye and struck a bargain for his business ... and his heart!
