INVITE YOUR BEST FRIEND TO TEA! Time to relax, time to be quiet, time to sit and sip from your favorite china cup--tea time will be the most important part of your day when your guest of honor is God! Each time you draw away to unwind with these refreshing devotionals you'll find gentle touches of love and comfort, moments of beauty, and renewed enthusiasm. Part of the Quiet Moments With God series of devotionals, the easy-to-read meditations in Tea Time With God include scriptures, poems, stories, quotes, and much more. They'll give you precious moments to refocus your priorities and gather strength for the rest of your day. Troubles will melt away, and you'll rediscover healing for your hurts and soothing remedies for the frayed edges of your life. While the tea is brewing, you can thoroughly steep yourself in the presence of your very best Friend. And after you try just one of these relaxing and edifying devotions, you'll want to make these quiet times a regular part of your daily agenda. Go ahead and put the kettle on!
