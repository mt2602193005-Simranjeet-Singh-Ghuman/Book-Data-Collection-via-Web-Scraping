In the first chapter of this haunting novel, 14-year-old Susie Salmon looks down from heaven and describes the horrifying events of her murder. As time goes on, Susie continues her curious observations while her family struggles to cope with the pain of her death. Her younger sister grows tougher and more mature, her mother goes to desperate lengths to ease the suffering, and her father begins a perilous quest to bring the killer to justice. Suspenseful, daring, and even humorous in just the right doses, The Lovely Bones is an utterly original and unforgettable tale. It transcends one family's overwhelming grief to deliver an astonishing vision of hope and love. From the CD 10 CDs/11.75 hours
