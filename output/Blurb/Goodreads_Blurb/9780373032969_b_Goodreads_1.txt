The Diamond B Ranch near Fawn Creek, Arizona. Seth Brody - All man. All cowboy. No nonsense. He's spent a lot of years trying to get back his family's ranch, and he isn't about to lose it again. Janna Whitley - Daughter of a millionaire and a movie star. She can't cook and she can't clean, but she needs a job as a housekeeper on the Diamond B - because it's the last place anyone will look for her. From the moment Seth sees Janna - with her matching luggage and her perfect manicure - he knows she's capital-T Trouble. If he had any sense at all he'd leave her at the bus stop without a backward glance...
