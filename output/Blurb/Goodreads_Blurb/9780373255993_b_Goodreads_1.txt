You'd Better Watch Out... Ali Paxton Bell had more than Christmas on her mind when Sam Cantrell came to town. Smooth, sexy and charming, Sam definitely had a twinkle in his eye. And one hot, steamy night he paid Ali a visit she wouldn't forget in a hurry.... Sam could make her feel naughty - or nice. His love was better than the most expensive Christmas present. But was it a gift that would last?
