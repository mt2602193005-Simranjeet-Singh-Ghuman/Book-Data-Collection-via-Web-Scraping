He was definitely the wrong man! For starters, he was much too young, far too attractive and a little too interested in Selby. Not that Selby Maitland was averse to male attention. But she was on a mission: to find and confront the Danny Forest who'd betrayed her grandmother. The "Mr. Danny" she'd eventually tracked down to his Newfoundland lighthouse was obviously the wrong member of the Forest family. Danny's insistence she'd found the right man--for her--wasn't helping. Neither would telling him the truth!
