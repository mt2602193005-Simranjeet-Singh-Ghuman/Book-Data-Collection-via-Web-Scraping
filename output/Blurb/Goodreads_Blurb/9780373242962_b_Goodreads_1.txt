"Gaby, please tell me you've been kissed before." The softly whispered words from such a hardened man as Luke Anderson bared Abigail Richards's painful secret: she was twenty-eight... and had never been kissed. Not kissed in a way that made her feel special, and desired. Not touched in a way that was tender. Having sworn off men, she'd dedicated her life to rescuing others, yet SHE was the one who now needed saving. And this Montana rancher who'd at first resented her intrusion in his life seemed a most unlikely hero. But after slaying her dragons and awakening her with just his kiss, Luke was the only man to whom Gaby wanted to risk offering everything: her innocence, and her heart....
