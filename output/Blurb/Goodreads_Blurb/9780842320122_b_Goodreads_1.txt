Series premiere special price! The Lost Colony recounts the life of Jocelyn Colman, whose faith is tested and refined when she follows the husband she barely knows to an unexplored land. Jocelyn struggles with her husband's bitterness and guilt until God's forgiveness becomes a lifesaving reality. Based upon original historical documents and the writings of John White, The Lost Colony is a thought-provoking exploration of what might have happened to the colonists who founded North America's first European colony.
