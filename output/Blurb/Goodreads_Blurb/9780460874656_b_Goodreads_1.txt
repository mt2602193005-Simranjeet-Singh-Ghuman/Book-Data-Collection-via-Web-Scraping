Now that he is rich, everyone wants to civilise Huck Finn But he decides that he cannot stand it; he stages his own murder and, with runaway slave Jim, goes booming down the Mississippi on a raft — free of clothes, school, money and other adult inventions — and the greatest adventure story in American literature begins. For as Huck and Jim drift further south into slave country, their life is complicated by encounters with con artists, feuding families and girls in distress, until it seems that the civilisation that Huck left behind will overtake the boy at last and force him to choose his manhood. Will he become a 'civilised' man, or a good one?
