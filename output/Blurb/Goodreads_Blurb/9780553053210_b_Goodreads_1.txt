Offers unprecedented access to behind-the-scenes work and profiles the glamorous yet driven world of the American Ballet Theater and the style and influence of its charismatic and risk-taking artistic director, Mikhail Baryshnikov. Presents a photographic account of the performers, chronicling the daily lives of the dancers & their director, how casting decisions are made, touring schedules are planned, choreography is selected, publicity is developed. The hectic, unpredictable, unstable, highly charged atmosphere of the company is revealed.
