The book is based on material left by an unusual missionary. It is about his observations of the way locals relate to the Life of Music. He finds himself in a unique situation- introducing locals to their own powers. The book is about your nature, which is the nature of music. It is about your powers and the way they influence Your Perception of Music. How can you be aware of your powers and consciously exercise their interplay? And then it is about the Source of Music - Silence, which belongs to every human being. How do you relate to the Source of Music? This book will help you discover yourself and find the answers.
