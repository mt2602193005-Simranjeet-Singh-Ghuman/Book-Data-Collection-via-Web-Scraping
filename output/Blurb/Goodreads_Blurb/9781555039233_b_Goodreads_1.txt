This is the heart-gripping story of three women and the men they loved, all living in a time of fear and hope. It is the time of the Savior's birth in Jerusalem and the domination of the Gadiantons in America. •The Beautiful and pampered Johiah, who will trade everything for wealth and luxury. •Her daughter, the innocent and vulnerable Rachel, who wants to believe in God, but has seen too little evidence of his hand in her life. •Hoshea, the proud Lamanite woman, whose life is changed by one good man’s death. •Gideon, who courageously risks his life to learn the truth about his cousin’s death. •Benjamin, his son, who spends a lifetime searching for the woman he loves. •Amulon, a man who knows no God but himself. Follow the journeys of each of these people as they learn of the certain power of God. A moving tale of sacrifice and love that combines adventure and romance with gospel values. Profound and poignant, touching and tender, stirring and sensitive-all these and more!
