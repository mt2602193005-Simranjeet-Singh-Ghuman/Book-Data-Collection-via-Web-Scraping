DREAMS OF DESTINY As the illegitimate son of a woman who had lost her ancestral home, Gavin has been raised with two obsessions: the burning need to reclaim Gallhiel, and an overwhelming hatred for the MacAmlaid, its present lord. To him, lovely young Rhea MacAmlaid was not an innocent, headstrong beauty, but the Highland heiress whose seduction would fulfill his dreams of revenge. Yet when he held Rhea in his arms, his heart whispered that he woman he meant to betray was worth more than any lands or title, that he was a fool to risk Rhea's love by making her captive to his dream...
