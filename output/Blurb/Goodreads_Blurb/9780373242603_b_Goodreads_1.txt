THERE WAS A MIXUP AT THE SPERM BANK.... And now Lisa Langdon was expecting millionaire Jared Steele's baby. The dashing executive was determined to be an exceptional father, and Lisa couldn't deny her unborn baby his love. However, she never anticipated the needs she'd feel for the devoted daddy-to-be. Lisa kept telling herself that her reasons for wanting to know this man had to do only with the baby--and not with how charming she found him. Jared was handsome, intelligent--everything she-desired in a man. But the once-burned bachelor didn't believe in romance. Now it was up to Lisa to show him she was more than the mother of his child--she was a desirable woman in need of love....
