Collection of nine short stories captures the American experience. Mostly humorous and written from a wide variety of experiences. Stories are about the rich, the poor, the educated, the street-smart. What if you found a suitcase full of money? What if you came face-to-face with someone you'd just vented your road rage upon? What if you were a prisoner in quarantine? Robert Voyles won a cash award for one of the stories in this collection.
