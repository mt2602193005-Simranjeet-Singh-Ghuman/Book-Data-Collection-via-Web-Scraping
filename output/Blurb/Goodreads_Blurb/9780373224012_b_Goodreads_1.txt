He'd been stripped of everything... Detective Sam Moore had lost his badge, his job, his dignity. Now he was a fugitive, headed both for Marly Kramer's horse farm and for Marly's young charge, an eleven-year-old eyewitness to the murder of which Sam had been accused... Marly had been looking for a strong, hardy horseman. Soon enough, the way Sam filled out a stetson and jeans convinced her she needed Sam. But then she began to suspect the horrible truth - that Sam had really come, not for her, but for the young child she'd sworn to protect.
