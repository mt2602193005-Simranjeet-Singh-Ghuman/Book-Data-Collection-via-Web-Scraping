RED ALERT!Pretty redhead Phoebe Donovan had just seen all of her things go up in smoke when her house burned down! And fainting in the arms of some brawny firefighter sure hadn't helped matters any. Where was her strength? Her cool-as-a-cucumber attitude? And where in the world was she supposed to live?Jackson Miller was a man with two fighting fires and being a decent dad to his sassy teenage daughter. But when he discovered his matchmaking offspring had pushed their upstairs apartment on none other than "Phoebe the Fainter," he realized he was about to tackle a third getting Phoebe out of the frying pan and into his own--very personal--fire!
