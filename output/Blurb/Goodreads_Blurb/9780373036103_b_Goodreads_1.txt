Two bundles of trouble... When Mac Weston found himself guardian of his sister's children, he knew he was in trouble. He was a man who worked so hard he barely had time to look after himself; let alone a pair of mischievous toddlers. Which was why he hired Paris Barbour as the new nanny.... Paris could see straightaway that Mac adored the children - he just didn't know how to show it. What he needed, she decided, was to learn how to be a daddy. But during his lessons, was Mac going to realize he was also ready to be a husband?
