This is the second book of what I have come to refer to as "The Deathwind Trilogy." I wrote a single massive novel, under the title DEATHWIND OF VEDUN, that Zebra Books decided to release in three parts. My original title for this part of my three-part novel was THE SOUL WITHIN THE STEEL. That's the title of the 2013 re-issue by Borgo Press, currently available in paper and soon as an e-book.
