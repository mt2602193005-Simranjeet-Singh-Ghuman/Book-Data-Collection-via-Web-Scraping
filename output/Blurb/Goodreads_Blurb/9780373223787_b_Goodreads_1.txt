She couldn't remember... She woke up in a hospital bed with a doctor to die for beside her - and no memory. And while her mind was blank, her arms were full - with a squirming infant they told her was now in her care. He couldn't forget... Dr. Jeffry Kendall had sworn a solemn oath that drew the line between doctor and patient. But he couldn't keep away from this new mother. Chelsea captured his heart - and he would do anything necessary to protect her and the baby she didn't know.
