When retired American espionage agent Peter Gandy dies in London of a sophisticated poison, it looks like someone has broken the rules of the racket. Spies kill but not each other. Alan Trosper, Gandy's ex-colleague in the Firm, wants to know why - and who? What he uncovers is a long-hatched plot to penetrate the highest echelons in Washington. While the Firm holds its breath, Trosper sets a trap that will take him through the back alleys of Europe and the shadows of Nazi horrors to a showdown with the brute force of Moscow Center.
