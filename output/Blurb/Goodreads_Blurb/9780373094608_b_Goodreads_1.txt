Shane Burke was a star, with counties adoring fans. But to Dr. Charity Wells, he was the sun--shining especially for her. The daily pain and tragedy of the emergency room had taught Charity life's darker side. Her own recent, chilling trauma made her long to bask in Shane's warmth. Still, their life-styles were light-years apart. As different as the moon and the sun, would they merely cross paths briefly, caress fleetingly, and spin off into lonely orbit again? Or, like night and day, could they make their lives revolve around each other?
