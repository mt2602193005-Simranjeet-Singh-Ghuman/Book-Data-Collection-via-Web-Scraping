Black sheep had a certain reputation. Gabe Updike, well-respected owner of a Denver construction company, successfully lived it down. Lynn Tresca, however, seemed to bask in her reputation as a black sheep and troublemaker. Why else would she have fought romance? Why else would she deny the special magic that happened between a man and a woman? As a fellow black sheep, Gabe understood Lynn's reaction, but her resistance was taking its toll on his nerves. Luckily, Gabe had an instinct for magic. He knew how--and when--to use it.
