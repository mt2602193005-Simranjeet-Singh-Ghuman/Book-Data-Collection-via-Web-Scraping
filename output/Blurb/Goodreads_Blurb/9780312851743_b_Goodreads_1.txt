It’s the early 21st century. Ed Stone says he’s been in suspended animation since the 1930s. He says he was kidnapped by aliens. He says they sent him on a mission: to convince the nations of the world to build a massive vault, a mile on each side, in which humanity’s billions will lie in suspension and survive the impending destruction of Earth. Ed Stone says all these things, and the strangest part is that people believe him—ordinary people and powerful people alike.
