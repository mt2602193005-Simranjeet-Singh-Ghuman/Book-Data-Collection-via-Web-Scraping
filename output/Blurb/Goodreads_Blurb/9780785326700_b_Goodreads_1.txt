Lovingly illustrated by Darrell Baker, this sound-book is a very effective teaching tool for connecting words with music to your child's mind. It is an 8 pages board book featuring "Play-a-song", a touch panel of sound effects harmonized to the melodies of Old MacDonald; Over in the barnyard; Mary had a little lamb; and Oats, Peas, Beans, And Barley Grow. You press the colorful, shaped buttons on the keypad to hear the favorite farm songs. The keypad is battery operated.
