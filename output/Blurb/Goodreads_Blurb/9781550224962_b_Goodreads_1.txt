Equal parts visionary fiction, self-help manual, and practical guide to magical technique, this novel takes readers on a journey through Ezmereld, the University of Esoteric Knowing. Drawing on the Celtic tradition of immrama , ballads in which the listeners participated in the stories as they were told, this narrative is a learning experience that provides spiritual insight and psychic wisdom. Those interested in the practice of magic and witchcraft will be aided by an index of techniques used in the story, which serves as a starting point for further study.
