You don't have to live very long to discover that life deals some heavy blows. How do we survive such things without losing faith or giving way to hopelessness or despair? How is it possible to find our way through the darkness that often obscures the gospel path we seek to follow? In connection with the annual Women's Conference sponsored by Brigham Young University and the Relief Society of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, women were asked what illuminating effect reading the holy scriptures has had in their lives. The responses are deeply personal and often poignant. Yet these essays are not disheartening. They are inspiring, filled with joyful declarations of faith and gratitude. Contributors Kathy D. Pullins, Vicki M. Huebner, Barbara Di Conza, Paula Carlson, Marie Lynn Hughes, Mary Wilson Umoren, Lauras Owen, April Smith Allen, and many others.
