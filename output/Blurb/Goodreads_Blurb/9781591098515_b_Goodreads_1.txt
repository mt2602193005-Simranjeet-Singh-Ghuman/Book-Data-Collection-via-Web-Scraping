This is one man's story, based on fact, of a young immigrant's experience in the United States. Herby came to the United States not speaking english, and totally dependent on his relatives living there. After taking all Herby's money, his relatives sent him on his way to fend for himself. This book chronicles Herby'ss adventures in love, and romance. It seems that Herby has finally met the love of his life and his life long soul mate, but her life is suddenly and tragically ended; leaving Herby to ask "What about us?"
