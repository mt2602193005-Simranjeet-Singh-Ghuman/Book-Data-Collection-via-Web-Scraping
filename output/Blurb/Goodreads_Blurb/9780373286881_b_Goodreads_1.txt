Had She Truly Lost Her Senses? After all, the idea of a prim store clerk trekking off to the Klondike disguised as can-can girl was a crazy notion, to say the least. But the moment Julia Ames discovered her long-lost father's whereabouts, she knew she'd have no peace till she found him. Duncan Adair was learning to accept the burden of carting five rowdy showgirls to his brother's playhouse in the gold-crazed Yukon. But when he discovered his suspiciously ladylike songstress was in fact no "entertainer" at all, Duncan was in a jam. How could he protect the woman he loved when she was hell-bent on looking for trouble?
