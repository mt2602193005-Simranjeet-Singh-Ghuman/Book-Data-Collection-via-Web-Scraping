The bachelor needs a wife.... As a teenager, Katie Deakins had caused Nick Kenton nothing but havoc and had even ruined his love life! Now, years later, she was coming to stay and Nick felt obliged to look after her.... Nick was shocked to discover that the wild child had turned into a woman! A beautiful, radiant woman who made him laugh--and drove him mad. And, while she breathed fire into his stuffy city life, he became aware of her devious plan--to win him and wed him! BACHELOR TERRITORY There are two sides to every story...and now it's his turn!
