Sixty-eight year old Kaitlyn Frost has received a letter in the mail. The instantly recognized handwriting on the envelope creates a whirlwind of emotions and memories that stretch fifty years into her past. Anxiously torn between what the letter says and her own dreams of what might-have-been, Kaitlyn carries the reader back to the innocent time of love's first bloom. Throughout the day, the letter remains unopened, as Kaitlyn struggles with the twilight fragility of her present and how it could all be swept away by chance, with a simple stroke of the pen!
