Every woman wanted Stephen Diamond ...except Tamara Hayes. Diamond was Hollywood's baddest bad boy-the reason for screaming female fans from coast to coast. But Tamara had fled Hollywood--and men like Diamond--years ago. Unfortunately, as activities director of the Diamond Queen, baby-sitting the sexy star was Tamara's job. Worse, the fool man fell in love with her at first sight. And then the proverbial other woman appeared, with outstretched claws.... The final blow was that Diamond was more man than myth. He kissed so softly that Tamara's resolve melted in his arms and she began cruisin' him! But could he love her if he found out about her past?
