IN LOVE WITH AN OUTLAW San Francisco cop Kristen Ford looses all the family she has left in the world when her father dies. In despair, she opens her door late one night to find a mysterious stranger with an unbelievable request. Suddenly, Kristen finds herself hurtled back through time to the Wild West - facing off against a band of outlaws led by the deadliest gunslinger west of the Rockies.Parrish is a rugged, dangerous bandit who trusts no woman. Yet, something about the tough-hearted desperado attracts Kristen - something reckless, seductive, and irresistible. Star-cross lovers from different worlds, they will discover a desire beyond their wildest dreams .
