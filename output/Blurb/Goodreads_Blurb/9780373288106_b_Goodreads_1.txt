Undercover Honor Richmond knew full well she could lose her life working as Patriot spy, but she'd never expected to lose her heart. In fact love was the farthest thing from her mind...until the arrival of mysterious Dylan Alden. Dylan Alden was the determined to infiltrate the Patriot spy network and expose the man responsible for passing on the king's secrets. Yet, the lovely Honor Richmond was becoming such a distraction that Dylan was finding it difficult to remember just whose side he was on.
