This helpful book combines our most popular cartooning titles in one handy resource where you'll find everything you ever wanted to know about this specialized art form! The Art of Cartooning includes personal advice from each of the contributing artists, all accomplished professionals in the world of cartooning. You will find easy-to-follow, step-by-step instructions for creating cartoons—from character construction and comic-strip illustration to the basics of animation. You'll also discover tips and tricks for creating your own original cartoons. Young or old, no aspiring cartoonist should be without this comprehensive reference!
