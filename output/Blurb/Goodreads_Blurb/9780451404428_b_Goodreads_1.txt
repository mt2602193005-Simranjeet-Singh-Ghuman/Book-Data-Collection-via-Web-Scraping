BOUND BE PASSION To Trevor Shelby, Leah Reese was a beauty forbidden to him by class barriers: he was a Southern aristocrat and she was an overseer's daughter. Denied his dream of being a doctor Trev's tormented past showed him nothing of tenderness, and he wasn't prepared for Leah's warmth and trust. His kisses taught her about passion, but she taught him about love. Together they dared to dream of a future as bright as their days of tender discovery. TORN APART BY LIES But Leah and Trey were torn apart by deception and a war between the states. In the ashes of the South and in the betrayals of others, Leah and Trev could see the death of their dreams. Yet if they dared to reach for the flame and embrace the fires of their true love, they would rediscover a passion strong enough to lead them to a new dawn....
