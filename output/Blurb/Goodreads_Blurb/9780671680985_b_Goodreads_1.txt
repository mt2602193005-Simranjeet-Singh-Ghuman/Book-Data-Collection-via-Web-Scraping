What are your chances of being happy together in your relationship? Will the chemistry between you last? This book presents insights from the ancient art of palmistry showing how to analyze and compare the features of your hand and those of a lover, spouse or would-be partner. Detailed chapters on the shape of your hand and fingers and the major and minor lines, end with compatibility profiles that compare your particular "type" to your partner. For example, the reader may find out how faithful their partner will be and whether he or she will be a good marriage partner. This enables the reader to rate their potential for love and compatibility.
