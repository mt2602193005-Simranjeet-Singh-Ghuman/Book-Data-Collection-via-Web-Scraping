When overworked, out-of-shape executive Sam Sarnoff arrived at Couchiching Spa in Orillia, Ontario, for a brief respite from his hectic business, he was shocked to find that the beautiful resident dietitian was someone he had once known very well indeed. But in high school, Beth Finlayson had been a shy wallflower, and Sam, a handsome, popular jock who had been ashamed to acknowledge their relationship. Now their roles were reversed, and Sam was decidedly uncomfortable. This new Beth could have any man she chose. And convincing her to choose him was proving to be a real challenge....
