She flew to him on broken wings.... Wounded and running for her life, photojournalist Jillian Brandt turns to the only person in the world she feels she can trust, rancher Trey Langden. Trey's her former love and the very same man she'd wronged more than once. Would he believe her tale? Would he even care? She gambled everything that he would. It wasn't the phone call itself that rocked Trey's equilibrium, but the familiar voice on the other end. Why now? Just when he was coming to terms over the bitter breakup with the only woman he'd ever loved? Still, he couldn't turn Jillian away, not with the desperation he sensed in her cryptic mutterings. This time would be different, he swore to himself. He'd be damned if he'd let her walk out on him again. He held the reins now.
