Not even a war between the states can keep apart a Southern aristocrat and the love of his life. From national bestselling romance author Raine Cantrell "Raine Cantrell showcases a new dimension of her astonishing talents that will leave listeners stunned and in awe. Intensely emotional, immensely satisfying." ( Romantic Times ). "A wonderfully compelling, honest and poignant love story with characters you won't forget. I couldn't put it down. Terrific listening." (Patricia Potter, author of the BELOVED trilogy). Tormented by his past and denied his dream of becoming a doctor, Southern aristocrat Trevor Shelby has little in his life to live for, until he meets the beautiful overseer's daughter, Leah Reese. Unused to the warmth and kindness Leah shows him, Trevor learns to love from the young woman, while his intensity teaches her about passion. Despite the difference in their social classes, they dared to dream of a future together, a fantasy that is shattered by lies, betrayal, and the outbreak of war.
